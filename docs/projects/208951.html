Project Summary

This project aligns with cluster objectives that aim to provide safe, timely, principled and gender-‎responsive life-saving humanitarian assistance to the most vulnerable groups (including those ‎displaced, directly impacted host communities, non-displaced people in conflict areas and refugees) to ‎reduce mortality and morbidity in areas with high severity of need or at risk of a further deterioration ‎of critical humanitarian needs., respond to the conflict-driven protection needs of crisis-affected ‎people through principled and conflict-sensitive assistance and advocacy as well as prevent and ‎mitigate emerging protection risks, with due regard to international norms and standards. Provide safe, ‎equitable, dignified and unhindered access to critical basic services (including primary and maternal ‎healthcare services, education, water supply and shelter) and livelihoods opportunities for the most ‎vulnerable groups to prevent further erosion of their coping abilities, strengthening community ‎resilience and enhance risk mitigation measures. The project will target 392,633 (92,347 boys, 100,043 ‎girls, 102,124 women, and 98,119 men) individuals in need living in 41 localities within 13 states ‎including Blue Nile, North Darfur, North Kordofan, South Kordofan, Central Darfur, West Darfur, Sennar, ‎Al Gazira, Khartoum, Gedaref, River Nile, White Nile and Red Sea states. The project will be focusing on ‎provision of water supply at IDPs gathering points/camps, community and institutional level including ‎water trucking in areas where supply is disrupted, provision of essential WASH supplies & NFI (Soap, ‎jeri-cans, aqua tabs and water purifications) and hygiene and menstrual hygiene items for female in the ‎reproductive age to meet their menstrual needs, establishment/ construction of emergency sanitation ‎and handwashing facilities in the current hotspot locations construction, light rehabilitation and ‎improvement for water sources, in addition to connection of water to schools, health facilities, and ‎child friendly spaces. The project will contribute towards the elimination of open defecation within our ‎operational areas through enhancing the capacity of local communities and mobilizing targeted ‎communities to construct their own latrines. This in line with the construction of emergency latrines ‎during emergencies for people affected by crises. The project will also contribute to solid and medical ‎management efforts through provision of garbage and collection tools, support to the disposal of solid ‎waste, construction of medical incinerators at health facilities to facilitate safe disposal of medical ‎wastes. The project will focus on improving the hygiene situation and status within the targeted ‎communities to mitigate and reduce the risk of diseases outbreaks as a result of poor WASH services ‎through general hygiene campaigns, awareness sessions, home visits as well as strengthening the ‎capacity of local community committees such as hygiene promotors, conduct training and establishing ‎hygiene clubs at schools and child friendly spaces, this will be accompanied with provision of hygiene ‎items such as soap to promote hand washing practices as mitigation measures to prevent disease ‎outbreaks as well as supporting female with basic menstrual hygiene items to facilitate safe and ‎dignified menstrual management for female at menstrual age. Moreover, the project will support ‎families who have malnourished children with hygiene items including soap, jerry-cans and aqua tabs ‎to address underlying causes of malnutrition. The project will create synergies, complement, and ‎integrate with Health, Nutrition, NFIs, Shelter and Food security interventions within the targeted areas ‎and worked with other sectors to address root causes of malnutrition and factors contributing to ‎diseases outbreaks. 3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

Through all programming, Save the Children aims to increase safety, uphold dignity,and ensure equitable access to services, including through addressing the concernsof individuals and groups with specific needs. Programme design and implementationis informed by context analysis including local power and conflict dynamics. Save theChildren will work closely with multiple stakeholders (community members,government partners, local NGOs) to avoid unintended negative effects on individualsor exacerbation of community tensions, and will seek programming which safeguards,protects, and capacitates vulnerable and marginalized individuals, and reducestensions. SC Sudan Protection staff support the effective mainstreaming of protectionacross all programming including though training on referral pathways, conductingprotection audits, and providing technical advice. A Gender Equality Specialistsupports with improving the gender sensitivity of programmes, and all projects areassessed through gender equality marker. Through community engagement activities,training of community-based structures, and training of service providers, informationwill be shared on protection risks and appropriate prevention and reporting actions.The WASH project interventions will ensure protection mainstreaming during thedesign, implementation and monitoring phases of the project and will ensuremaximum protection for beneficiaries specifically children, women, girls, people withdisabilities and other marginalized entities. WASH interventions will priorities safetyand dignity, and avoid doing harm through ensure the location of the facilities and theroute is away from actual or potential threats such as violence; especially the risk orthreat of gender-based violence (GBV), and attacks from armed groups, installinglights near to latrines as appropriate, infrastructure adaptations such as installingramps and railings to latrines, proper locks from inside, separate and label thelatrines“ male” and “female , also platforms and ramps at water points at appropriateheight to ensure safe manual lifting to all. Because women and children are the mostlikely primary responsible for collecting HHs water, special consideration will be givento the design of WASH facilities such as providing shades during sun hours, schedulewater collection times to limit overcrowding at the water points. Meaningful accesswill be ensured through discuss and consult with all representative samples of society(e.g., men, women, girls, boys, elderly, ethnic groups, persons with disabilities) thatshould have access to the service. Adapt the location to reduce the distance and toensure that the most vulnerable/marginalized have access.

4.1.1 - How will the project use community feedback to improve and adapt activities? (open-ended/free text, required)4

Save the Children will establish community feedback and compliant system in the project location using different mechanisms including hot lines, suggestions books, and through group meetings with different community entities. MEAL and accountability team receives the feedback and compliant and upload on the accountability system and create action trackers for all type of feedback based on the sensitivity and urgency, then shared with implementation team for actions, and with SMT for follow ups. As soon as the feedback actioned, the accountability team feedback the communities or the people who provided the feedback on the status of implementation, some of the actions could be implemented soon based on the availability of resources and information. Actions with no response either because of unavailability of resources or out of scope of the project will be recorded and kept with program and business development team to be consider in future projects.

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

Stakeholders’ participation and accountability to affected people is part of SCI’s corevalues. Save the Children will ensure that girls and boys, women and men (of all ages,abilities and backgrounds) are consulted in a meaningful way in programming at allstages of the project lifecycle and have regular opportunities to provide feedback andconcerns. SCI will ensure that targeted communities have been provided withopportunities to express their views and influence decision-making throughdiscussing together, deciding together and working together. Gender, age anddisability sensitive measures will be adopted, including through adaptingmeeting/service schedules and meeting venues to ensure equitable participation,using single sex discussion groups, considering ways to support the dignity andprivacy of beneficiaries, and ensuring facilities are physically accessible to all, andadopting child-friendly methodologies. Save the Children will provide timely andaccessible information (in local language sand culturally appropriate mediums) toaffected communities on proposed interventions and deliverables, expectations ofstaff behavior especially with respect to our Child Safeguarding policy and Code ofConduct, and how to raise concerns or provide feedback. SC Sudan has a well-establishedComplaints & Feedback Response Mechanism, including a telephonehotline, and multiple further modalities which provide affected populationsopportunities to give feedback anonymously and/or privately, and options for thoseliterate and illiterate. Field level MEAL staff are responsible for receiving complaints,managing responses, and reporting them to Country Office MEAL staff who report toSenior Management. Data is anonymized, analyzed and utilized for projectmanagement and planning. There are clear processes for responding to PSEA andother safeguarding concerns raised through any feedback channel. Save the Childrenhas safeguarding specialist staff at Country Office level, who will provide additional.support and conduct monitoring visits to project sites.
