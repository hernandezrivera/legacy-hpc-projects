Project Summary

South Sudan has experienced protracted political and economic challenges since December 2013, this has displaced a lot of populations and its estimated that there are more than 3 million populations who are in the neighboring countries and over 1 million internally displaced.
The signing of the revitalized peace agreement in August 2018, there has been a sign of stability and has also seen influx of returnees to the country with more than 64000 returnees seen in Nair county according to the IRNA conducted in March 2019. Magwi county has seen more than 24000 populations returning to the area according to the RRC and Multiagency assessment conducted in June 2019.
There have been heath challenge in all the mentioned counties with Nasir County being affected by floods and Magwi County at high risk of Ebola virus outbreak and other counties bearing a high disease burden due to un-functionality of health facilities and long distances to health facilities.
South Sudan has very bad health indicators with the maternal mortality rates standing at 789/100000 live births and child mortality rate of 69/1000 live births.
This project will wish to address these challenges by provision of 6 mobile health clinics and and 3 PHCUs to the vulnerable populations. this will be done by having 1 mobile health unit in each county and 1 PHCU in each of the following counties, Aweil East, Nasir and Ulang to ensure that there is essential health care provision to the vulnerable population which will include outpatient consultations in both 6mobile and static facilities, provision of BEmNOC services, reproductive health services, Health education, strengthening of referral systems and timely reporting.
TADO will use integrated approach to ensure that beneficiaries get the best services through referrals of the SAM cases to Nutrition centers, offering GBV services to the beneficiaries and IPC which entails WASH in Health facilities.
TADO targets 8302 beneficiaries out of which 4202 will be women and 4100 men that also includes the boy and girls.

1 - Needs

South Sudan has one of the worst health indicators in the world and also has a very weak health systems. This has greatly increased the morbidity and mortality among the vulnerable populations in south Sudan,Access to health services is highly limited with more than 56% of the population having no access to health services and even the 44% accessing health services having limited health package (SARA 2019)Health facility to the populations remains way far above the recommended ratio of 10000/ health facility and with only slightly above 1000 facilities against a population of 12 million individuals its highly above the recommended standards.South Sudan has a very low immunization coverage with only 49% coverage and with a very low cold chain coverage of below 50% (UNICEF 2019)There has been more than 17 counties in a total of 89 counties with active measles outbreaks. This is a major public health emergency which requires immediate health interventions to avert major outbreaks and reduce mortality and morbidity due to disease outbreaks,Due to its proximity with Democratic Republic of Congo, South Sudan remains at high risk of Ebola virus outbreak which is also a public health emergency and which calls for health interventions and robust behavior change and risk communication.According to the health service functionality bulleting, Magwi county has very many nonfunctional health facilities and that’s increases the vulnerability to contracting infections. Its also at a very high risk of EVD outbreak due to the proximity with Uganda and it’s a boarder point with increased movement of populations.Nasir county has a very low immunization coverage with the Health functionality bulletin showing a immunization coverage of 21%, and skilled birth attendants of 38%. There were 1329 cases of acute watery diarrhea and with the current flooding situation in the county the situation is likely to increase in the coming months. According to the IRNA report done in March 2019, Nasir has a returnee population of over 64000 and they are expected to increase with the signing of the peace agreement in august 2018.Aweil East and Tonj North counties have had measles outbreak in June 2019 and this can be attributed to the low immunization coverage and very limited availability of the cold chain facilities. Aweil east has had confirmed 19 cases of measles and more than 100 line listed. The county suffered great disease burden with more than 33000 cases of malaria and 4173 cases of acute watery diarrhea reported in 2019, (IDSR 2019) this calls for urgent interventions to reduce mortality and morbidity rates in the county.Maiwut County has very limited health service coverage and occasional community conflict in the county displaces the communities and therefore there is need for health service delivery in the county.Lack of essential medical supplies and health work force also poses a great need for emergency health services to reach the vulnerable populations in the aforementioned counties in the republic of south Sudan.Displacement of populations and returnees from the neighboring countries due to relative peace in the country increases the pressure on the existing health facilities and therefore calls for intervention

2 - Activities

• Outpatient consultations both in mobile and static health facilities• Establishment of 6 mobile units in the 6 counties• Support 3 PHCUs in Nasir, Ulang and Aweil East Counties• Management of malaria with ACT• Management of Acute watery diarrhea• ANC services• Skilled deliveries in the health facilities and in the community• PNC services• Mental health and psychosocial support services• Reproductive health services which includes distribution of condoms and family planning services to the vulnerable and affected populations • Referrals of complicated cases and cases of SGBV • Case Management of Rape services• Health education on behavior change communication• EPI services to ensure that the vulnerable and affected children are immunized against measles and polio• Growth monitoring, management and referral of children with MAM and SAM• Training on infection prevention and control, • Training on IDSR • Training CMR to ensure that the health workers in the counties have the capacity to respond to cases of Rape• Training on IPC• Training the health workers on skilled birth deliveries• Hire skilled health personnel to support health service delivery in the counties• Report any attacks on Health care• Weekly reporting on IDSR and EWARS and 5W
