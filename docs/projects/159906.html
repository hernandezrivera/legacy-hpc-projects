Project Summary

The objective of the project is to improve access to quality lifesaving nutrition services through integrated prevention, treatment and rehabilitation, services for acutely malnourished children (boys and girls 0-59 months of age) and pregnant and lactating women (PLW) among the most affected communities in addition to strengthening the capacity of national staff to respond

Project Activities

• Screening of under five children and Pregnant and Lactating mothers for acute malnutrition and referring to the treatment nutrition centers • Treatment of MAM and SAM cases directly in MOH health facilities and/or through mobile teams• Conduct Nutrition, health and IYCF promotion sessions in all supported static and outreach sites • Conduct one to one counselling for women with difficulties in adopting optimal infant and young child feeding practices • Provide micro nutrient supplementation to include Vitamin A, MMN and de-worming for children 6--59 months• Integrating nutrition with WASH and provision of Hygiene kits for mothers with children • Provision of Blanket Supplementary Feeding Programme for children and PLW• Training MOH nutrition staff on CMAM, IYCF, and BSFP • Conduct nutrition assessments: SMART surveys, coverage surveys, KAP survey• Treatment of SAM cases without complications in difficult to reach villages through the ICCM Plus approach• Training of Family Health workers on ICCM Plus as part of community resilience• Coordinate activities with the government and Nutrition Cluster• Referring mothers with cases of acute malnutrition, children less 24 months to be included unconditional cash transfer • Conduct SMART, SQEAUC , and KAP surveys to monitor nutrition situation in Somalia

Mitigation Measures

The mitigation measures will include 1) SC will work closely with community leaders’/clan leaders to ensure inclusion/representation of minority population. 2) Nutrition services will be provided without bias or discrimination according to the humanitarian principles 3) For activities requiring beneficiary selection/targeting, beneficiary selection will be conducted through project committees with adequate knowledge on the target population, their needs, conflict dynamics and vulnerability of different groups especially on the referral to the cash transfer programs available in the same locations where nutrition interventions are implemented 4) SC will ensure ensure that sites selected for health service delivery are located in a safe, well lit and accessible place for the beneficiaries including persons with impaired mobility 5) SC will carry out throrough background checks on service providers and any new staff 6) All SCI staff undergo mandatory Child Safeguarding and Sexual Harrasment training in addition to signing a legally binding code of conduct as part of their contract when they join the organization 7) SC will take every opportunity to directly encourage women’s participation through ensuring a gender balance in the community structures such as village committees and particular attention will be paid to the unique needs of women and girls and the contextual gender dynamics in Somalia 8) To ensure accountability to our beneficiaries, SC will utilise a beneficiary feedback mechanism that is convenient based on the context this will include an information sharing protocol for survivors of abuse that will avoid exposing her/him to further trauma and that all efforts are made to ensure her/his confidentiality and wishes concerning the care provided.

Please provide further explanation

- The Nutrition interventions will be linked with the food security and livelihood resilient interventions to include the KFW project in Mogadishu and the BRICS II project in Hiran region for sustainability purposes.- The Nutrition interventions to be provided to the beneficiaries are in line with the Somalia IMAM guidelines and protocols and also meet the international Nutrition standards, and it’s therefore legally recognized by the Ministry of Health, the Somalia authorities- The listed locations are relatively stable in terms of security and therefore access to the beneficiaries is viable.- All these Nutrition interventions are linked within the existing health systems in Somalia, and for areas within Somaliland and Puntland, MOH staff are in the forefront of the implementation of these interventions with Support from SCI- capacity building, technical, logistical. In the South Central Somalia area, the nutrition interventions are linked where possible with Ministry of Health, and has a lot of focus on the training and capacity building of the MOH/National staff. The perspectives of the displacement affected communities will be sought and incorporated at all stages of the project. Finally, this project is contributing the the first collective outcome that by 2022 the number of people in acute food insecurity decreases by 84 percent with GAM rates reduced by 5 percent and sustained below the emergency threshold.
