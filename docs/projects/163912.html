Project Summary

Strengthen preventive nutrition services for vulnerable groups in need of humanitarian response, focusing on appropriate infant and young child feeding practices, micro nutrient initiatives and optimal maternal nutrition. Improve access to quality curative nutrition services through systematic identification,
referral and treatment of acutely malnourished cases according to international standards.
Promote nutrition situation analysis using standard response standard tools and screening methodologies on children and women."
With regard to the deterioration of the situation in Idleb the project will integrate (RRT)
to provide immediate emergency nutrition services.

- Measuring Muac for pregnant, lactating women (PLWs).
- Conducting Individual and group sessions to raise awareness about the importance of breastfeeding.
- Giving Vitamins for children and PLWs.
- Giving consultations to PLWs.
- Distribution brochures that contain information on supplementary feeding breastfeeding.

4 - Needs

Continued conflict, displacement, shifts in control, and increasing displace together in one areas with depletion of livelihoods, limited financial and physical access to food, in addition, intensification of airstrikes and shelling since the beginning of the third week of December in southern Idleb the civilians are escape from Ma’arrat An-Nu’man area. The ground fighting between NSAGs and GoS forces resumed along the frontlines in southern Idleb governorate, amplifying this wave of displacement as civilians are fleeing in anticipation of fighting directly affecting their communities. The total IDPs estimated was 500,000. The recent wave of displacement has exacerbated the situation, adding an additional burden to nutrition activity, especially in some areas of Aleppo and parts of Idleb. The limited access to quality water and sanitation, as well as suboptimal hygiene practices, may contribute to a high prevalence of childhood disease and illness. These factors negatively influence the nutrition situation, especially in the context of disrupted health services and coverage that characterizes the situation faced by many people living intension areas, overburdened communities, newly displaced or ‘newly accessibleThrough four nutrition centre’s AL Resala Foundation (RF) provides nutrition services package for 89,393 beneficiaries in - Afrin town centre 76,500- Aleppo- Azaz- Shumarin- RF camp 2,093- Idleb- Harim- Babisqa- Al Manara camp 3.150- Aleppo- jabel saman- Atareb- Atareb centre 7,650 The percentage of the targeted beneficiaries’ nutrition services package as follows.- Children under 5 , % 49.8- PLWs % 38.8- Disabilities child under 5 % 0.87- Disabilities PLWs % 0.74- Children expected have GAM (SAM+ MAM) % 1.81- PLWs expected have GAM (SAM+ MAM) %1.18
