Project Summary

•Training of activists and APEs on a monthly basis, including training in monitoring the risk group (pregnant women, travellers, the elderly, children with basic disease, market vendors, domestic workers)".
•Transport and distribution of Nutrient Support for communities for suspect cases (supervised by health center) included in the project.
•Training of activists and APEs monthly, including training in monitoring the risk group (pregnant women, travelers, elderly, children with basic disease, market vendors, domestic workers)
• Screening and follow-up of malnourished patients and those at risk of covid-19 according to national protocols
• Distribution of supplements to patients at risk of malnutrition, malnourished and at risk of covid-19) with difficult access to health units
• Food education training , To every target group focusing on food waste in the community, using local language and megaphone.
• Education for correct Covid-19 hygiene and prevention standards and nutritional practices such as confession of food.
• Meetings with community leaders on a monthly basis, for preventive health and nutrition education, with the objective of spreading the message in the community,
• Replication of IEC material, with national and WHO standards for nutrition-related health unit and Covid-19 areas.
• IEC distribution in the most crowded places (markets, fountains, community sports fields)
