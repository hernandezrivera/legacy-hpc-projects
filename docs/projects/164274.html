Project Summary

IYD proposes this project to improve the shelter status for those are living in damaged and unfinished houses to meet the minimum standard of shelter provided by Shelter/NFI cluster and to enhance the daily living for new IDPs by distributing the requirements items NFIs to help them meet the daily basics needs, IYD will focus on a more durable solution, focused on shelter rehabilitation, to reduce protection risks for the most vulnerable, This project will target 400 damaged houses, and 200 unfinished houses(3500 individuals, 750 men, 878 women, 800 boys,1072 girls) and 1000 new IDPs individuals in three communities Al Bab, Aghtrin, and Suran located in Aleppo governorate, Albab district, and Azaz district. these communities belong to the Euphrates Shield area located in the north of Syria, these three communities are under local councils’ control in partnership with Turkish governmental where the local councils responsible for providing all services to the people and maintain and implement the infrastructure in the Areas. IYD has a complete and complementary plan in the shelter sector to rehabilitate both damaged houses and unfinished houses, this plan consists of 6 steps: Project Preparation, Coordination & Community Selection, Beneficiaries Selection & Technical Need Assessment, Tendering & Contracting, Implementation & Quality Management, and Documentation. These haphazard shelter solutions increase protection risks to women, young girls and children because they lack privacy and utility services like water and electricity often do not function. Aleppo reports the second-highest governorate for HHS reporting shelter inadequacy issues the most significant barrier to making shelter repairs was the expense; either that repairs required a professional, which households could not afford, or that households are able to make repairs but the materials are too expensive- this was more commonly reported than a general lack of shelter materials, Shelter/NFI cluster data clearly demonstrates that shelter units and more adequate shelter space are one of the most critical gaps with a need for increased support directed towards host communities to safeguard coping mechanisms and improve the standard and availability of shelter. Through this project IYD is planning to intervene in two locations at the district level, Aleppo governorate, Azaz, and Albab district, those two locations have a large number of damaged houses. Under this project, IYD will rehabilitate 400 partially damaged houses between 40 % – 70 %, 100 houses in Azaz, and 300 in Al-Bab, Aleppo governorate, and 200 unfinished houses in Al-Bab, by reconstruction the destroyed walls, install new doors, and windows, and rehabilitate the water services, the estimated total number of targeted beneficiaries 3,000 individuals, also, IYD under this project will construct wall to form separate rooms and provide new materials doors, windows, and lighting systems, in addition, the rehabilitation of the water services will be done by providing water tanks, toilets, and baths. IYD finds that residents are willing to contribute to improvement projects; specifically, to provide labor to support the repair and rebuilding of the accommodations. During conducting technical need assessment for shelter rehabilitation, IYD team will be focusing on the basic needs for the most vulnerable families, this is to identify the most required NFIs kits to be distributed for those families in the priority for new arrival families which they need, IYD planning to distribute new arrival kits, kitchen kit, and comprehensive kit, the distribution process will be through registration of the families and doing the verifications for the beneficiaries, the program team will prepare the distribution lists in coordination with the local council, each family will receive three types of NFIs kits.

4 - Needs

The number of people in need of shelter assistance has increased in overall Syria especially in Idleb, and Aleppo governorates. Overall, refer to the 2019 HNO report, the severity of need has increased by only 21 percent of sub-districts. Overall 300,000 more people now living in severity 4 and 5 areas over the last year with an overall increase in the number of people in need of 570,000. There has also been a significant worsening in overall shelter arrangements in Syria, with 36 percent of sub-districts reporting an increase in the proportion of communities living in damaged or temporary shelters. The acute humanitarian situation in the Northwest is driven by the high proportion of IDPs, overcrowding and the lack of shelter capacity, high rental prices and unaffordable items, and poor access to markets. A considerable number of people live in shelters with at least one adequacy issue, including lack of heat and insulation, lighting, necessary bathing or cooking facilities, inadequate space, lack of privacy and the inability to securely lock their home. The shelter adequacy issues mentioned by different age and gender groups were similar, with no notable difference observed. Approximately 680,000 households cannot carry out necessary repairs to their homes, most frequently because they cannot afford the materials and/or professional assistance required to carry out the rehabilitation, and due to a lack of skilled labor. As in previous years, the primary providers of shelter assistance and support are host households with an estimated 520,000 households hosting others. HLP issues remain a challenge on many fronts. Repair and rehabilitation of housing are only pursued following confirmation of HLP rights, however, many displaced people do not have sufficient documentation (many never had proper or formal documentation or lost them during displacement), and the processes and infrastructure to formalize or recuperate documents ar not uniformly functional or accessible.

5 - Implementation of activities

IYD planned to implement this project through the intervention in the following main activities to achieve the cluster objectives, Sector Objective 1: Provide life-saving and life-sustaining shelter and NFI support, IYD determined 200 unfinished houses to be rehabilitated in Albaband Azaz districts, and 1000 displaced families to be received 1000 NFIs kits(1000 new arrival kit, 1000 kitchen kits, and 1000 comprehensive kits the beneficiaries from this activity will be 2,200 individuals. Sector Objective 2: Reinforce an enabling protection environment and community cohesion by improving housing and related community/public infrastructure, IYD determined 400 damaged houses to be rehabilitated in Albab and Azaz districts, the beneficiaries from this activity will be 2,400 individuals, the methodology of implementing these activities will be as following: IYD will hire the required staff to implement the project. IYD team in coordination with the local authorities will publish an announcement in the targeted locations to allow the beneficiaries register to benefit from the project, this registration will be through criteria after the registration process completed, the verification list will be checked by MEAL team, • Needs Assessment: The technical engineer will conduct a technical assessment in the proposed areas,• BOQ and Estimates: based on the technical assessment the engineers will prepare the BoQs of the project's activities needs, this BoQs will contain the unit number of doors, windows, walls, and all accessories need to rehabilitate the water services. IYD through the local council and the representatives of the community will form a committee to review and ensure the property document of the houses, and in case of missing this document, this committee will lead the issue and sing an agreement to prove the house property.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

IYD has a security and safety team that conducts a security assessment for its projects by receiving information on security situations from its network of local councils and civil society activists, as well as by remaining continuously apprised of security situations through bulletins released by the international community. IYD has a good reputation as a non-profit organization and works side by side with the community to empower and encourage the community ownership of facilities. Accordingly. For emergency relief projects, when a security situation arises threatens the safety of staff, the staff is redirected to a nearby, safer location until the area is safe enough to conduct the aid or distribution project. IYD will coordinate together in the implementing process and both of the teams will work together to ensure the safety and security of the team in the field. 7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

The assumption of this project which IYD depend on is the security situation in the targeted areas will be stable during the project period due to the continues of the agreement between the main actors in the area, IYD in implementing the project will ensure the following important things: - protection of the beneficiaries while guaranteeing the safety of humanitarian staff. - the do no harm should adhere throughout the project. - the most vulnerable should be protected from violence and other human rights abuses. - affected people should be assisted in the recovery. The risk matrix for this project: Risk1: Lack or loss of civil documentation of the people in the targeted community, this will lead to no receive the assistance. Likelihood: medium, impact: medium Mitigation: During the registration process, IYD in coordination with the local authorities will verify from the people who missed the documentation. Risk2: HLP issue in the targeted areas, During the Syrian crisis 57% of the owners of the houses and lands lost their documentation, this will lead to don’t benefit from the project. Likelihood: high, Impact: high Mitigation: IYD in coordination with the local authorities will prepare declaration paper signed by the houses or lands' owners and approved by the local authorities. Risk3: Rental issue, the owners of the houses may be asking the families to pay house rent after the rehabilitation of the house. Likelihood: medium, Impact: high Mitigation: The local authorities in the project locations will ensure the sustainability of benefiting from the project for one year. Risk4: Land Attack by Syrian governorate, airstrikes by Russian Likelihood: high, impact: high Mitigation: IYD will suspend the project. IYD will provide full training on GBV, and PSEA to the project staff and all staff will sign the code of conduct. By using adapt communication messages, materials and channels IYD will Increases understanding, supporting and strengthening their systems on PSEA.
