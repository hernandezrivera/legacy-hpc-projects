Project Summary

COHESODEC’s response in 2023 will be realized in the following target communities of the North-West and South-West regions of Cameroon: Bali, Bamenda, Batibo, Kumbo East, Kumbo West, Wum, Kumba-North and Kumba-South. This response will ensure targeted implementation of activities to promote infant and young child feeding in this emergency for Complementary Feeding to support pregnant women and breastfeeding mothers registered in the baby corners/baby tents/dedicated counseling units (including breastfeeding and also ensuring that 5000 people (M/F) receive IYCF counseling/awareness).
Also, this project will support community engagement for behavior and social change by ensuring that 2000 mothers and other caretakers trained on family MUAC activity do effectively improve on the nutrition situation of their households and shall also ensure that 200 people (M/F) are attending participatory sessions on program design/evaluation.
Finally, the project shall also ensure that Integrated management of severe and acute malnutrition (PCIMA-S) involving at least 75% of children aged 6-59 months are admitted for SAM management and discharge as cured with a positive recovery rate meanwhile 1 mobile clinic will be established for Child comprehensive response in these target vulnerable communities.

1 - Besoins / Needs

COHESODEC recently conducted a need assessment where trained community health workers carried out nutrition screening in target vulnerable communities using the mid upper arm circumference (MUAC). Following the results obtained in this survey, several children were identified with acute malnutrition and referred to be supported in health centers for severe acutely malnourished children without complications on an outpatient basis. With regards to the national protocol, SAM cases with medical complications were also referred to the In Patient Facility (inPF) at hospital levels. Considering several parameters, COHESODEC select priority health districts in a bid to increase access, coverage and use of lifesaving nutrition services and enhance protection of the nutrition status In the implementation of this project, COHESODEC will be mobilizing and effectively counseling pregnant women and breastfeeding mothers registered in the baby corners/baby tents/dedicated counseling units. COHESODEC team will also be identifying, registering, counseling and disseminating educative handouts and posters for wider community sensitization and ensure targeted implementation of activities which promote infant and young child feeding in emergencies for complementary feeding including breastfeeding. Moreover, COHESODEC will have to identify, register and counsel 5000 mothers with infant and young children (M / F) and ensure they receive IYCF counseling / awareness. The project will as well support community engagement for behavior and social change by identifying, registering and training 2000 mothers and caretakers on family MUAC activity. Finally, this project will integrate the management of severe and acute malnutrition (PCIMA-S) involving children 6-59 months and ensure they are admitted for SAM management, properly treated and discharge with a positive recovery rate of at least 75% while equally ensuring that a mobile unit is established involving specialist for child comprehensive response in these target communities!
