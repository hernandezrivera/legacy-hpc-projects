Project Summary

In fact, SOS Sahel Sudan has significant experiences in WASH interventions, (water Sanitation and hygiene program) in the project areas through its partnership with different donors in the tow targeted States (North Kordofan and Kassala). Particularly in the two targeted localities of Shiekan in NK state and Telkok in Kassala state, the gap and community demands concerning WASH services are still huge which need more efforts and interventions. Thus SOS Sahel propose to continue tackling the problem of water, sanitation and hygiene promotion through WASH Response for vulnerable people Project. The proposed project interventions purpose is to address critical community needs through improve equitable access to safe and clean drinking water and supporting them to adopt and became more aware about sanitation and hygiene promotion issues.
In fact Telkok locality inhibited by 419,357 people (213872 Male and 205485 Female), out of the total population 100807 people are vulnerable residence, 30,886 people are vulnerable for water services, 88,505 people are vulnerable for sanitaion and 104,839 people are vulnerable for hygiene services. The project target is 15,000 people (7350 Female and 7650 Male), while Shiekan locality inhibited by 569,564 people (290,478 Male and 279,086 Female). Vulnerable residence is 142,391 and disability persons are 85,435. Its worth to be mentioned that 5087 south Sudanese refugee’s setteled at the locality. The project target is 18,000 people (8820 Female and 9180 Male)
The percentage of people have access to safe water in the Shiekan and Telkok localities is low and still the situation in some parts of the states is worse where people depend completely on water trucking or crossing long distance for more than 2 hours during the dry season. Water quality is generally a big concern as most people especially in rural areas prefer to use unsafe surface water due to shortage of safe water. Most of HH members of the two localities consumed less than 15 liters of water per person per day while sphere standard considered consumption should be 20 liters per day per capita.
One of the main challenges currently affecting people in the two localities including Host communities and Nomads is inequitable access to adequate humanitarian aid especially WASH services due to the general economic situation in Sudan and COVID19 epidemics which lead to limited allocated humanitarian services. Through this proposal SOS will work to reduce the vulnerability of the target communities and improve their living conditions by the provision of water, sanitation and hygiene promotion.
The main purpose is to address community WASH needs in Shiekan locality in North Kordofan state and Telkok locality in Kasala state through provision of water, sanitation and hygiene promotion services to the target communities. The main objective will be achieved through three outcomes by the end of December 2022:
1. People practice good hygiene measures to protect against key water related diseases
2. People access sustainable, adequate and equitable basic sanitation services
3. Improved access to safe, sustainable and affordable basic drinking water supply services
The implementation of the project will be done by SOS Sahel Sudan in collaboration with the governmental technical bodies like (WES project, sanitation department at state and local MoH). Involvement of the target community will be the key issue to ensure the sustainability and smooth implementation of the proposed activities. In delivering such activities, SOS will commit to the national and international specications and standards (Sphere, Sudanese standards, WHO standards etc).

1 - Needs

In Shiekan and Telkok localities there is a general limitation and shortage of basic water infrastructure and public services, which is related to the distribution of resources in the country and the historic marginalization of rural areas. Further, the limit of access to water to communities presents as a challenge. However, the most vulnerable groups are generally: women headed households with large family sizes, people lacking income sources, households hosting people with disabilities and families with children attending schools that lack sufcient water. The needs of these groups are varied, but generally include: critical community needs to safe drinking water, hygiene awareness, sanitation facilities to combate water related diseases and improve communiy health. Telkok locality inhibited by 419,357 people (213872 Male and 205485 Female), out of the total population 100807 people are vulnerable residence, 30,886 people are vulnerable for water services, 88,505 people are vulnerable for sanitaion and 104,839 people are vulnerable for hygiene services. Moreover people with disabilities are 62,904 people. Likewise Shiekan locality inhibited by 569,564 people (290,478 Male and 279,086 Female). 2 - Activities/Outputs

One of the strength points that SOS Sahel has the presence in Telkok and Shiekan localities. This presence enabled SOS Sahel to establish good relationship with different communities and other stakeholders. From the earlier beginning of the project implementation process, SOS Sahel will involve all people in the project areas and related government departments in the process of the project implementation. SOS Sahel in consultation with community representation will develop selection criteria for the most vulnerable people to ensure their full participation in the project implementation and managment. Following this, SOS Sahel will use the information provided by WASH sector to cross checked and verify the target. Likewise the project will focus on Interventions that foster the improvement of sanitation and hygienic practices associated with using hygiene facilities and the healthy use of water. To effectively implement this project SOS Sahel will focus on three main outcomes as detailed below: Outcome one: People practice good hygiene measures to protect against key water related diseases: Hygine promotion program as essential part of outcome one is intended to enhance hygiene practicies in the target locality through the following activities, 1. Provision of multi purpose bar soap (250gm/person/month/6month) 2. Build capacities, provide training, to enhance WASH of affected communities (state/locality level) 3. House hold hygiene messeging through home visits. 4. Hygiene campaigns in communal set ups (villages, schools, health facilities, markets and with media) Outcome two: People access sustainable, adequate and equitable basic sanitation services: The proposed Sanitation interventions aimed to enhance health situation in the target locality through implementing the following activities, 1. Construction of institutional latrines of four stances 2. Construction of fixed Hand washing facilities. 3 Disposal Solid waste and support its managment. Outcome three: Improved access to safe, sustainable and affordable basic drinking water supply services: Water proposed activities to satisfy such outcome are 1. Drill and install new hand pumps 2. Install new mini water yard (includes Hybird/solar). 3. Rehabilitation of broken hand pumps. 4. Rehabilitate water yards (includes solarisation) 5. Operation and management training for water committees at water point level. Water activities are aimed to increase equitable access to drinking water at the targeted locality

5.2 - Impact on environment/natural resources

The project integrates protective environmental measures as the improved access to water sources as mitigation of conflict over water sources. Further, by providing more water, sanitation and hygiene promotion services. The WASH intervention includes geophysical survey activity that ensures no unnecessary drilling will occur and water harvesting activities will be included. SOS Sahel is a natural resource management and environmental focused organization. The project partners will consider SOS Sahel methods for analysing environmental risks that will take part at different stages of the project cycle. The community participatory planning approach gives the community stakeholders the opportunity to discuss the environmental impact of community selected, interventions and ways of mitigating negative ones. For example, this may be the environmental impacts that a Hand Pumps could have an immediate environment impact if it is located next to a migratory route. Community led Monitoring & Evaluation processes will be used to monitor and assess the real impacts of certain inputs and interventions on the environment during the delivery services in particularly in case of rehabilitation of water sources. SOS Sahel will engage high quality competent staffs who are committed to ensuring adequate service and product delivery to the targeted, the conflict over natural resource and protection of water sources, environmental sanitation will also be part of the project through training workshop to enable community improve environment for their better health and life
