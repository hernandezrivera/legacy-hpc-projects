Project Summary

Protection and GBV services will be provided by Almanar aiming to address life-saving and life-sustaining protection needs among crisis affected families, while also enhancing the capacity of stakeholders to strengthen the protective environment. Through this project, men, women, girls and boys, older people, disable PSNs will be engaged ensuring the full and equal respect for the rights of all individuals, IDPs, Refugees and host communities, regardless of age, gender, ethnic, social, religious or other background in the activities.
Almanar will contribute to the general protection and GBV sub sectors objectives through implementing joint activities that response to GP & GBV sub sectors objectives summarized at the follow: Almanar will organize 12 orientation sessions to community leaders, local authorities, stakeholders and services providers to orient them about the project and will facilitate 24 network meetings with representatives of CBPCs to discuss protection and GBV related issues. Following to the meetings, 12 forums will be conducted between the CBPCs representatives and local and state government authorities after identifying the protection issues at the network meetings to find solutions.
Almanar will lead 6 participatory protection/vulnerability assessment survey in AbuJubaiha, ElReif Shargi, Talwdi in SK and Dein, Assalaya, Sheiria in ED and will establish 12 gender balanced CBPCs (20 members/committee, 50% female) and provide them with (5-days) capacity building training on protection essential functions, GBV and PSS. referral pathways will be Developed/strengthened in coordination with service providers i.e. hospitals, health centers, CMR support, legal aid challenging for survivors to refer 44 vulnerable individual to receive needed support. Distribution of 450 Direct Material Support (DMS) will be done to vulnerable individuals.
Almanar will raise the awareness of 1,400 community members on protection related issues and provide them with needed counseling through 28 awareness raising sessions. Also will provide 9 (5-days) ToT training on civic education for CBPCs and social workers targeting 30 participants/training and 9 professional, technical and practical advance 5-day capacity building training for staff representing line ministries on protection mainstreaming, GBV and PSS targeting 30 participants/training,
Rehabilitation of 6 women centers will be done to carry on protection/GBV activities and be utilized as a safe space and will provide 100 women with case management, consultation and psychosocial support. Awareness raising sessions of 2,000 women and girls survivors of violence will be conducted and will provide them with needed counseling through 40 awareness raising sessions on GBV & PSS related issues. Almanar will procure and distribute 1,100 dignity kits to vulnerable women and girls of reproductive age.
8 women empowerment/life-skills training will be conducted targeting 160 female/area. Almanar will participate on the 16 Days of Activism against SGBV Campaign. In addition, 8 CMR training will be held and attended by 120 health providers and 12 workshop training to project staff & suppliers will be conducted on PSEA basic concepts followed by 24 awareness session targeting 1,200 community members.

1 - Needs

According to SUDAN HUMANITARIAN NEEDS OVERVIEW 2021, the deepening economic crisis and scarcity of resources has impacted service delivery and resulted in a concerning increase in protection risks across the country. Access restrictions due to COVID-19 and damage caused by disasters have severely impacted livelihoods and mobility. Preliminary assessments have confirmed that IDPs and refugees do not live in visible and easily identifiable settlements. They have mingled in the local population, living with host families or squatting empty houses or other places. Community protection structures are not in place to ensure that vulnerable people are identified, provided with essential social care, and directed to the relevant services. The entry of displaced persons puts the host community under stress, and may cause pressure which lead to conflicts and fights. Breakdown in family and community structures leaves vulnerable people without adequate support to address their essential needs. Community safety nets are severely weakened, and social institutions are usually too stretched to provide adequate protection services to the affected population. Mechanisms and capacities contributing to conflict resolution and peaceful coexistence, as well as advocacy with authorities in displacement and return areas need support. Lack of awareness among communities makes it challenging to ensure prevention of GBV and to ensure access of survivors to the relevant multi-sectoral services. Vulnerable women and girls in areas of conflict need support to prevent risks of GBV, in particular to reduce their exposure. Women and girls continue to suffer disproportionately from GBV, poverty and violations of basic rights due to insecurity, low economic status and lack of livelihood opportunities, lack of community awareness on women’s rights due to cultural and societal norms. Violence against children is a moderate to extreme risk in 89% of operations; and Sudan is one of the acute countries, Children in Sudan have resorted to selling non-food items and shelter assistance materials to support their families (GLOBAL PROTECTION UPDATE NOVEMBER 2020). The IDPs and refugees are mixed within the host community. The main consequences of this conflict is disruption of basic services, exposure of women, girls, men and boys to further insecurity and violence, disruption of economic and agricultural activities added additional burden placed on host families. Almanar has a successful experience in implementing protection and GBV programs in targeted states with EU, SHF, UNFPA, UNHCR and other donors through working with well trained protection community based committees, social workers & LGAs through raising awareness on Human rights, women’s rights, child rights, citizenship, peace building & conflict resolution, cross cutting issues: (GBV, HIV/AIDS, environment), legal aid, protection materials support, PSS to GBV survivors and recreational and basic vocational skills as well as provision of protection support to the prisoners. Also Almanar is working in building the capacity of government authorities, community leaders and CSOs in the geographical areas where the proposed intervention. Almanar has a well-established main office in Khartoum state and 3 sub- offices in South Kordofan, west Kordofan, and east Darfur state with qualified and committed staff recruited in each office and good relation with the government and communities.

5.2 - Impact on environment/natural resources

Environmental mainstreaming will be integrated through the entire project management cycle and integrated in the assessments to ensure that environmental harm is reduced or eliminated and environmental benefits are maximized. Key environmental concerns will be built into the project in order to reduce deforestation in Sudan. Almanar will attempt to switch away from fire bricks to alternative constructive methods such as stabilized soil blocks (SSB) or concrete blocks (CSB) depending on the prerequisites of the region. Environmental mainstreaming will be included during monitoring and evaluation of the project outcomes of the response to ensure that the effects sought was indeed delivered, and to help identify possible negative environmental effects in time in order to adjust activities accordingly. As the main element of the project, the principle of do no harm is fulfilled. Furthermore, to help identify best practices and lessons learnt that offer valuable insight for making improvements in future aid efforts. Almanar will work with local NGOs, CSOs and communities as well as with the national and state governmental authorities, since Community consultation and participation is a key part of environmental management. Throughout the project cycle gender will be considered to access to, and management of natural resources to receive consultation from both men and women.

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

One of the most significant factors, which is increasingly recognized as an integral component of UN programming, is the gender equality and protection of girls and women. Inequalities between the sexes typically reflect, and can increase, vulnerabilities of young girls, teens, may face different protection risks, have different needs and choices, and possess different skills, knowledge and coping strategies. The project activities, will aim to ensure community participation through the project cycle, capacity and vulnerability analysis, reduce the level of exploitation to women and promote protection of girls in communities and families with the support of legal and traditional protection systems. The project will use and advocate for Minimum Standards of Protection package amongst the targeted communities throughout the project implementation process. This project through the referral pathway will ensure that women, girls and boys victims of S/GBV, elderly, disables and their families and guardians are fully aware of available services around them. Almanar will ensure that the service locations are safe and accessible to all without discrimination, and ensure that the beneficiaries receiving services in dignity and confidentiality.

7.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will inform and interact with inter-agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including SEA and abuse complaints?

Accountability will be ensured across the activities in this project through establishment of complaints and response mechanisms at project level aimed at ensuring that the voices of the affected populations will be heard. The existing community committees and community leaders will be actively engaged at all levels of the project including management, monitoring and evaluation. Complaints handling mechanisms will be established to ensure that complaints are comprehensively analyzed and responded to with the beneficiaries getting feedback on a monthly basis. These committees are expected to be gender and ability sensitive with the inclusion of both males, females, elderly and people with disabilities to ensure that all voices, including those of the marginalized are heard, equality and equity are realized with regards to access to services that will be provided by the project. Project staff will ensure adequate consultation with the communities to ensure that existing complaints handling mechanisms are incorporated into the complaints and response mechanisms to be established by the project. Almanar staff is trained and signed the sexual exploitation and abuse policy and committed to it with close monitoring by the supervisors.
