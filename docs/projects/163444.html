Project Summary

This project is a 2 years’ time frame project which consists of setting up an adolescent resource center in the North West region of Cameroon that will focus on meeting the health and social needs of children between the ages of 10 to 20 years. The center will comprise of a working office space, 4 health personals (a medical doctor, a nurse, a laboratory technician and a psychologist), 1 office manager, 4 capacity building coaches, 3 teachers and a network of volunteers. This center will work with the local community and volunteers to identify adolescents who have been victims of sexual harassments and/or rape, teenage sex workers, and street children. The specific need of each child will be assessed by the social workers and necessary interventions carried out.
