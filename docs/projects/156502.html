Project Summary

HIHFAD is currently operating two maternal and child hospitals; namely Atmeh hospital in Harim district and Balyun hospital in Ariha district, and will endeavor to continue operating them in order to provide the women of reproductive age (15-49 years of age) and children (0-18) within their scope areas with primary, secondary and tertiary healthcare services. Both hospitals are planned to operate 24/7 for the whole year of 2019. The services planned to be provided entail:
• Outpatient pediatric and reproductive health consultations.
• Outpatient pediatric specialized polyclinics (Neurology, hematology, cardiology and endocrinology)
• Outpatient mental health services (MhGAP).
• Facility and community based Psychosocial support services.
• Basic obstetric and neonatal care BEmONC services.
• Reproductive health-related surgery and comprehensive emergency obstetric and neonatal care CEmONC services.
• Inpatient pediatric and reproductive health hospitalization wards.
• Provision of secondary and tertiary healthcare services for children and neonates in need through a special care baby unit SCBU.
• Community-based vaccination services for children under 5 and women of reproductive age as per the expanded program on immunization.
• Outreach physical rehabilitation.
• Laboratory services.
• Provision of essential medicines.
• Imaging services (Plane X-ray, mammography and ultrasound imaging)
• Ambulance referral services.

Needs

According to the 2019 HNO draft for Syria, 37 per cent of the affected population requires routine reproductive, maternal, neonatal and child health (RMNCH) services.Pregnant adolescent girls and their babies are at high risk of medical complications and death. They constitute a substantial high-risk group in Syria, with early marriage reported to take place in 57 per cent of assessed communities143, and pregnant girls under 18 representing about 12 to 15 per cent of the deliveries taking place in health facilities.UNICEF and WHO estimate that in 2019, about 577,219 new-borns will require routine immunization across Syria, with an estimated 320,000 children between 13 and 59 months of age not fully vaccinated during their first years of life.Dana is a sub district located far most northwest to Idleb governorate and hosts some 496,911 population according to the latest data communicated by the health cluster/ Gaziantep hub. The healthcare severity is 4/6 across the sub district as per the latest HNO data for Syria.In the same context, Ehsem is a sub district located in the central part of Idleb governorate and hosts some 82,548 population according to the latest data communicated by the health cluster/ Gaziantep hub. The healthcare severity is 3/6 across the sub district as per the latest HNO data for Syria.All of the aforementioned details highly inform the necessity to support the provision of comprehensive healthcare interventions to cope with the mounting needs of the population.

Implementation of activities

• Provide essential primary and secondary health care services, including trauma care, EmONC and referral to specialist services: the healthcare services will be provided on daily basis and round the hour. The primary healthcare services will be provided in line with the essential primary healthcare package adopted by the health cluster- Gaziantep hub. Those services entail outpatient consultations for children (0-18 years of age) and women of reproductive age (15-49 years of age). In addition, those services entail the provision of specialized paediatric healthcare services and outreach physical rehabilitation PSS activities not to mention the provision of facility-based basic obstetric and neonatal care BEmONC services as well. As for the secondary healthcare services, they entail the inpatient hospitalization services for the paediatric and reproductive health cases in need, including the provision of comprehensive obstetric and neonatal care services. Also, those services entail the management of neonatal cases requiring hospitalization within the special care baby unit SCBU (Incubator department), where means of tertiary healthcare; like mechanical ventilators; are made available for the purpose of providing compressive healthcare services for paediatric cases in need. In addition, the facility is equipped with an ambulance for transporting the referral cases in need of further medical attention to the appropriate facility.• Provide routine immunization services to all children <5 years of age: All children under 1 year of age as well as any children under 5 years of age eligible for immunization within the scope area of the facility will be offered the vaccination services in line with the expanded program on immunization led by the Syrian immunization group SIG. The vaccination services include the administration of Penta3 and MR vaccines among all of the other adopted by the SIG and will be provided at the community levels.• Provide health facilities with essential medicines and medical supplies: HIHFAD will ensure that the facility consistently has adequate quantities of the essential medicines and medical supplies that are required for the implementation of the planned activities, where that will happen through the funding channels HIHFAD has established. Also, the provision of such items will be promoted through the coordination with WHO and UNFPA since they provide those items through their supply lines.• Strengthen the capacity of health care providers and community health care workers to provide essential health services: HIHFAD consistently assesses the gaps in terms of medical technical capacities of the staff and conduct the required trainings accordingly. Those trainings include and are not limited to integrated management of childhood illnesses, infant and young child feeding in emergencies, reproductive health-related trainings. In addition, HIHFAD will ensure that the community health workers attached to the facility are provided with the standardized trainings adopted by the community health working group- Gaziantep hub on the community health topics and modules.• Increase access to health services by establishing functional health facilities and mobile medical units and supporting referral: HIHFAD will provide the facility with a well-equipped ambulance to ensure timely transportation of any referral case that require further medical intervention to the appropriate nearby or remote facility. The ambulance will be operating 24/7 for the whole period of the project and will support the referral network in the scope area of the facility as needed. All services in the hospital are provided free of charge and the facility will be fully equipped to provide specialized health care. A health information system are in place in all HIHFAD's Facilities, using electronic and paper copies ,

Protection Risk Analysis

The list of the key protection risks includes and not limited to the followings:(Abbreviations: LCs: Local councils/ HDs: Health directorates/ L: Low/ M: Medium/H: High)1. Beneficiaries are affected by conflict related incidences while seeking healthcare at the Facility.• Likelihood: L/ Impact: H• Mitigation: Facility are based in areas included in the de-escalation zones agreement at adequate distances from gatherings of armed groups. Coordinate with the local authorities (Civilian LCs and HDs) to support the exclusion of the health facility from any possible clashes. Technical support by HIHFAD safety and security team. Training of staff on contingency plans. Ensure about the establishment of emergency exits within the facility.2. Patients are subject to harm due to low-quality healthcare services.• Likelihood: L/ Impact: H• Mitigation: HIHFAD follows strict HR recruitment procedures in terms of the qualifications and expertise required for medical personal. Also, HIHFAD consistently evaluate any gap of the technical capacities for its medical staff. Feedback mechanisms to be made available for the beneficiaries to ensure their participation in identifying any gap in the staff technical capacities that need to be addressed. In the same context, HIHFAD consistently seeks to ensure the standardization of the services provided in terms of medical equipment, medical supplies and pharmaceuticals.3. Children undergo vaccination induced side effects after receiving EPI vaccines.• Likelihood: L/ Impact: M to H• Mitigation: provide EPI staff with the necessary capacity building. The children vaccinated are observed for appropriate duration after receiving their scheduled vaccination. The vaccination services are introduced through a facility that can handle pediatric emergencies. In addition, HIHFAD to utilize HeRAMS to identify the required Facility and referral pathways required for promptly respond to emergencies related to vaccine administration.4. While at Facility, Beneficiaries are abused, harassed, undergo compromised privacy or dignity.• Likelihood: L/ Impact: H• Mitigation: Child safeguarding training provided for all staff. Establishment of feedback mechanisms with maintained anonymity. Ensure individuals of vulnerable groups (children, women, elderly and people with disabilities) are accompanied while at the Facility. Ensure adequate female staff representation. Application of privacy screens and separation of medical examination theatres. Establishment of breast-feeding corners. Separation of the bathrooms by sex. Label tags to be demonstrated within the Facility. Establishment of adequate waiting areas and ensure adequate lighting of the facility is in place.5. Confidentiality of the information provided by the Beneficiaries is compromised.• Likelihood: L/ Impact: H• Mitigation: staff of the Facility adapt HIHFAD code of conduct. Tags about confidentiality of patient records and information to be demonstrated within the Facility. Maintaining patient information through well secured and strictly monitored modalities inaccessible to public. Establishment of feedback mechanisms with maintained anonymity. 6. Communities are affected by medical wastes produced at the Facility.• Likelihood: L/ Impact: H• Mitigation: Securing the medical wastes prior to disposal. Application of medical waste management pathways for the purpose of proper disposal of them.7. Staff/ patients and their companions acquire nosocomial infections while at the facility.• Likelihood: M/ Impact: M to H• Mitigation: programmed use of disinfection measures. Programmed microbiological screening of the Facility. Establishment of clean and well-ventilated waiting areas. Proper handling of medical wastes. Use of personal protective equipment when indicated. Children presented at the Facility to receive EPI vaccines are offered the service within the outpatient department to ensure minimum contact with hospitalized patients.
