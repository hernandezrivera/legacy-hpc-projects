Project Summary

Alswaid Alkhadra through this project is intend to response to the humanitarian WASH needs to the most vulnerable IDPs, Returnees as well as vulnerable host communities in Sheirya, Yssin and Baher Al-Arab in East Darfur
The proposed activities are in response to ongoing and urgent humanitarian needs of IDPs and vulnerable host communities.
The interagency assessment conducted in East Darfur reveals that; water, Sanitation remain a priority need for all proposed areas for intervention. The poor infra-structure and the limited resources in addition to restriction on access to the areas all has exacerbated the situation and led to deterioration of the limited existing service.
AAO proposed WASH intervention will contribute to reduce suffering of affected targeted communities with aim to reduce diseases out break likely to be occurs as a result of poor environmental hygiene, unsafe water and poor sanitation.
IDPs recently arrived from Greada in South Darfur and settled in Malwi villages in Yassin locality will also be considered with this response.
Intervention will include:
• Rehabilitation/construction of 5 water supply system in Yassin (2), Baher Al-Arab (1) and Sheirya (2)
• Construction of 3 school latrines in Sheirya, Yassin and Bahr Al-Arab
• Construction of 500 family latrines
• Provision of 4000 hygiene kits
• Conduct of 9 environment cleaning campaigns
• Training and formation of community health volunteers (30 volunteers)
The project will target 21650 individuals in all targeted locations

1 - Needs

This intervention has been informed by several internal and external assessments carried out by different stockholders;A s per the HNO; Ayear after the start of the political transition in Sudan, humanitarian needs continue to grow across the country, despite progress made in the implementation of the objectives of the 39-month transition period and the peace process. The situation is driven by an economic crisis, exacerbated by COVID-19 containment measures, protracted internal displacement that is yet to find durable solutions.Lack of WASH infrastructure in schools across East Darfour, including hand washing stations and latrines, remains a challenge and increases the risk of disease outbreaks. Poor WASH conditions combined with a lack of knowledge about infant and young child nourishment, drive food insecurity and malnutrition, and according to WASH cluster meetings the information shows that Om Shejera school – Shearia locality, Om Kheranban School – Bahar Alarab locality and Malwie school – Yasssin locality is need urgent intervention in terms of construction of school VIP latrines and provision of hygiene dignity kits to the adolescent girls. The 2022 Humanitarian Response Plan (HRP) focuses on the following objectives, which AAO plans to contribute to through this project: • Provision and enabling affected peoples’ access to basic water services through operation, maintenance, and rehabilitation of water sources which inlueds and not limited to; hand pumps, water yards, protected wells, and hafir..etc• Provision and enabling affected peoples’ access to limited sanitation services through rehabilitating 30 percent of the existing damaged sanitation services (latrines) in target locations; construction of new latrines, installation of new latrines and rehabilitation of existing communal latrines;• Hygiene promotion messaging through household visits, campaigns, and media coverage. This is to be achieved by provision of soap and water containers for handwashing;• Provide outbreak affected and ‘at-risk’ population’s access to minimum WASH for Health package. Provision of WASH services are key for both the prevention and response to disease outbreaks. Water Supply: According to the school Needs Overview 2022, about 5.3 million people lack access to improved water sources, 6.2 million people lack access to improved sanitation, and 7.5 million people lack access to hygiene services in Sudan. In Yassin, Sheiria and Bahr Al-Arab water is a priority need for the population, although most of these places are located in a so called Al-bagara basin however the depth is far and the people are poor and mostly rely on water from unprotected sources as Hafirs.There are people with specific needs, including unaccompanied and separated children, survivors of gender-based violence (GBV), women-headed households, the elderly, persons with disabilities, and people with specific health concerns. These groups are highly vulnerable as they often lack coping mechanisms to mitigate the multiple risks they face and are often confronted with additional barriers to meeting their basic needs and accessing their rights. Access to WASH in institutions (schools and primary health centres) is limited. Fifty (50) percent of existing schools do not have improved sanitation facilities, and half of them do not have access to clean water. Due to the last assessment done by MOE along with MOH .there is clear recommendation to construct permanent latrines in all east Darfur schools , however the government started the plan but due some reason they did not manage to complete many schools. to fill the gab AAO planning to implement such project to construct latrine in some schools in the above mentioned areas particularly ,targeting the schools with huge number of children and which they are in dare need for latrines.
