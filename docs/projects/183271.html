Project Summary

In light of the current shortage in medicine and medical supplies, Anera plans to continue its support to hospitals and clinics to ensure sustaining their operational capacity, and providing the beneficiary with the needed health service. Anera which has initiated a prompt response to the Beirut Blast by shipping 48 medical shipments including essential medical supplies and PPEs, is now keen to respond to the urgent needs of the health sector in Lebanon. Thus, this project will ensure sustaining the operational capacity at selected public hospitals throughout Lebanon, ensure infection prevention and control measures in high-risk clinical and related settings, and enhance the protection environment in humanitarian health response. Anera plans to receive 6 shipments which are worth 1.5 million USD. To continue this type of assistance some support is required in supporting staff cost, shipping cost and transportation.
Some support is needed to ensure the arrival and distribution of the medical donations to health facilities in Lebanon. Support is needed in covering the shipping cost ($3,000 for every shipment), covering salaries of staff needed to facilitate the clearance and distribution, and the transportation of donations.
-The intervention will concentrate on provision of medications, medical supplies, & PPEs to vulnerable populations requiring tailored support, while ensuring that the medications are in line with Lebanon's essential drug list and are properly managed and dispensed to eligible beneficiaries.
- The donations will be tons of medicine, supplies, PPEs shipped in the form of international donations to Lebanon. No procurement will be done. The value of items is estimated to be 1.5M, and the focus will be on hospitals more than PHCs focusing mainly on where there is need.
Anera has offices all over Lebanon. Through its six offices in different governorates,. It employs over 60 local staff who are experienced in various sectors, and outreach volunteers who are mobilized to support our projects and ensure its proper implementation and monitoring, including overall beneficiary satisfaction with the project.
Anera's MIKP provides pharmaceuticals and medical supplies, offered by international donors, to local recipient institutions in order to help them maintain the quality of health services they provide, while relieving them of some of the costs to deliver these services. In doing so, the program also works to lower the cost of health care for vulnerable beneficiaries. Due to the increase in # of shipments by 4 folds in comparison to the last fiscal year, Operations/Logistics including shipping, handling, and clearance related charges of this medicine donation has drastically increased. This is in addition of the team expansion which now comprises experts in logistics, monitoring, distribution, pharmacy doctors, and inventory system expert.
The Medical In-Kind Program only accepts medicines that are needed by the recipient hospitals/health centers and approved by the Ministry of Public Health in Lebanon. The program adheres to standards and best practices of the World Health Organization (WHO) Guidelines for Medicine Donations and is in line with WHO and Lebanon’s Essential Drug Lists
Donations will include not only medications and PPEs, but also kits, relief items, and medical supplies to support hospital departments and consumables which have become in low availability, and at a high price in the Lebanese market. These fall under the category of IV, Surgery, Anesthesia/Respiratory, Drainage/Suction/Irrigation, Orthopedic, Patient Care, Urology and others.
Additionally Anera will distribute dignity kits to 5,000 women and girls in line with the subject objective "Enhance the protection environment in humanitarian health response".
Anera's programs and services reach out to migrants and vulnerable Lebanese through supporting public hospitals and PRCS which accepts migrants, and CLMC (Caritas).

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

1. Distribution of kits: Kits are standardized dignity kits, including sanitary items to women. Recipients are vulnerable women and girls benefiting from Anera education and wash projects such as those attending functional literacy courses, hygiene promotion sessions, and basic numeracy courses. Also vulnerable individuals in case of emergency. Anera will ship the kits from abroad each at a unit cost $20, and will need support with the clearance expenses. 2. Donations: Anera will not do international procurement. Anera DC office communicates with international pharma companies our needs and receives offers which are shared with local office in Lebanon and compared with needs list. For Example, Anera succeeded lately to receive from Elli Lilly a donation of various types of insulin worth around 8 Million dollars covering the national needs in Lebanon for vulnerable communities for two years. MD programs runs in Lebanon since over 20 years and supports health centers in Lebanon. Anera has been doing logistics and clearance since years and it's a crucial part of the MD team to have logistics pharmacist and monitoring and logistics officers following up with clearing agents, customs, MOPH, and IRI. Anera is an active member in the health working group and coordinates with UN agencies, NGOs , and MOPH not to duplicate efforts and confirms we are in alliance with sector requests.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Given increasing tensions and reports of increased incidences of domestic violence in Lebanon, Anera is planning to include protection-related elements into its projects for the next year. It will provide training for its partners and its own staff on detecting potential protection cases, providing psychosocial first aid. Anera will prioritize PSEA and child protection as mandatory training themes for partners. Anera uses RIMS system for protection referrals, and notifies inter-agency referral focal points immediately of any protection cases as a matter of priority. Anera has proven PSEA and child protection policies that are compliant with UNHCR and UNICEF requirements.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

Anera ensures to accept shipments which are of high quality, and are compatible with the needs of populations. Once received Anera has effective monitoring to ensure the beneficiaries receive quality services. These include: recipients undergo a certain vetting before any donation is distributed to them:1. OFAC’s port of sanction list 2. System for Award Management (SAM) 3. Assessment sheets. Tools & Monitoring Visits: A- Assessment sheet: Filled out after approaching or being approached by a potential partner, including: administrative details, type of centers, # of centers, the nationalities of their end beneficiaries, the partners’ vision & services provided. Monitoring Visits/year & Survey: After the distribution of a donation, partners are visited by an MD Team representative. During the visit, Anera checks handling/storage/dispensing, handling/storage practices. A survey is filled to allow us to derive several indicators (impact ratios, coverage, and needs).
