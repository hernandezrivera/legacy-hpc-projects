Project Summary

1. Strengthen an integrated Early Warning System (EWARS), monitor draught-related illnesses and response to disease outbreaks, identify children with severe acute malnutriton, monitor communicable potential disease outbreaks and institute early response to minimize morbidity, mortaltiy and spread contain the outbreaks. 2. Provide adequate medical services through exisitng health facilities for management of severe acute malnutrion with medical complication. 3. Strengthen the coordination and leadership for severe acute malnutrition in the health sector both at natioanl and provincial level and 4.
