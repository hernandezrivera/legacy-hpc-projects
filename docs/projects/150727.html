Project Summary

The project will provide integrated Health, Nutrition and WASH services to the most marginalized communities of Barawe, Qoryolei, Kunturwarey and Marka district. The project aims to increase the resilience of populations through enhancing their health, hygiene and nutrition status which are the most deteriorated in Lower Shabelle where people are facing massive displacement due to different types of disasters such as prolonged drought and recurring armed conflicts
Therefore, based on the urgent humanitarian intervention on the ground in Lower Shabelle region and much needed support to scale up and provide integrated health, nutrition and WASH services to most vulnerable and acute watery diarrhea most affected households in four districts,its highly recommended to have adequate integrated Nutrition,Health and WASH services , through provision of Nutrition supply to support SAM and MAM children and women, Provide vaccination of Measles,Penta1,Penta 2 and Penta 3, Provision of Vit A supplements,MMN,folic acid, conduct awareness sessions for WCBA, Conduct IYCF awareness sessions,Provision of vaccine to under 5 years children and tetanus toxoid to women of child bearing age (WCBA) in target areas,Establish and strengthen BEMOC services to pregnant and lactating women in lower shabelle, (Barawe, Qoryoley ,Mark and K/warey districts), Provision of basic life saving health care services, including detection and treatment of endemic and epidemic diseases through provision of full course treatment for uncomplicated cases treatable at community level,Provision of referral for complicated cases including severe dehydration of acute water diarrhea to nearest referral health facility, targeting children and PLW, Supporting our health facilities through drugs and different services provided,ensure Women and children access and utilization of basic nutrition services,screening and admission of severely acute malnourished children and PLW,as well as ensure access to safe water sources, rehabilitation of shallow wells and construction appropriate latrines, Hygiene promotion sessions in all targeted locations, establishment of chlorination of water sources as well as providing quality trainings to field staff who are responsible of implementing activities, distribute hygiene kits to communities, and raise capacity of staff through continuous and quality training.

Needs

The humanitarian situation in Somalia remains fragile with the latest FSNAU assessment estimating 6.2 million people in need of life-saving. An estimated 3.1 million people, 25 percent of the population, are expected to be in Crisis (IPC Phase 3) or Emergency (IPC Phase 4) through December. The Gu (April-June) cereal harvest was far below average, prices of local cereal remain well above average, and substantial livestock losses have occurred, all of which have lowered household access to food and income. Persistent drought has led to large-scale population displacement. Deyr (October-December) rains are expected to be average to below average, but levels of acute food insecurity in Somalia will remain high through the end of the year. Acute and widespread food insecurity and increased morbidity have contributed to further deterioration of the overall nutrition situation in Somalia (FSNAU, MAY , 2018).L. Shabelle Region faced recently insecurity resulted in increasing of displacement, this had exacerbated the needs of both displaced communities as well as host community, People left their lands, Home to completely unknown situations, with high probability of facing very bad circumstances in terms of Health, Nutrition and Water,Sanitation and Hygiene services.With construction of gender disaggregated toilets communities will learn to use the toilets. Hygiene awareness extended to community members will help them change behaviour thus preventing outbreak of cholera/AWD among target communities. training of youth on water treatment (chlorination) will help communities manage water at community and household level.

Activities

Based on the urgent humanitarian intervention on the ground in Lower Shabelle and Galgadud regions and much needed support to scale up and provide integrated health, nutrition and WASH services to most vulnerable people, the project plans to achieve project objectives through execution of the following activities:HealthStrengthen the capacity of health workforce and monitor the performance of health partners in order to improve the quality health service delivery. Ensure provision of maternal, child and reproductive health to crises affected and most communities in Somalia. Ensure provision of maximum vaccine coverage through enhancing routine immunization and launching of immunization mass campaigns.Strengthen and enhance disease surveillance, ensuring early detection and timely response to disease alerts/outbreaks. Provision of emergency life-saving health, nutrition, and wash supplies and equipment to health facilities.Nutirion:Staff will regular identification of acutely malnourished children and PLWs. Therapeutic feeding support for treatment of acute malnutrition cases. Micronutrient support for vulnerable groups ( PLW and U5 children ) e.g Vitamin A & MMN. Provision of Integrated multi-sectoral nutrition, health, hygiene (NHHP).. MCHN/IYCF support (promotional and preventative) especially support to caregivers for feeding the sick childWASHRehabilitate/construct and maintain WASH infrastructure at institutions (health centres, CTCs, schools, TLS, CFS) and make sure hygiene messages are coordinated with and support other clusters Emergency latrine construction and latrine desludging for communities & IDPs. Water treatment, chlorination and routine water quality testing programme, in the water point (systematic) and household level.CHWs and volunteers and WASH partners will implement community sensitization and hygiene promotion around drinking water collection and storage; hand washing; and hygienic sanitation

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

Displaced individuals and households faces critical problems from surrounded environment and host communities sometimes, this problems result in their exclusion or rejection from host communities, which could restrict or reduce their access to different services, this could sometimes rise to security issues and threats to their personal wellbeing,livelihoods or assets.this project is based on all the humanitarian principles, and aim to promote equality and equity in access to services.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

Risks related to gender and age like, excluding women and girls from some services based on their gender, also excluding or ignoring some categories based on their age whether they are elders or young children and not including them in our services planning .This will be mitigated through ensuring full access and inclusion of all categories of the community equally regardless of their age and gender to the services we are providing.

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

L.shabelle region is known with unstable situation and this could hinder the services flow as well as the security of the staff and community as well, Our organization make efforts to implement its activities in the safer areas, which are in the same time accessible to the beneficiaries. The project will closely work with all stakeholders including local authority, clan elders, youth (male and female) and women. All clans will be represented in relief committee and staffing to avoid conflicts arising from competition for scarce resources and opportunities.
