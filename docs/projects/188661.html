Project Summary

INTERVENTIONS AND PURPOSES: The proposed intervention has been designed to respond to the dynamic and recurrent crisis affecting vulnerable communities in Borno state IDP camps.
(1): Strengthen community governance structures at camps. This will be a continuous process over the life of the project.
(2): Effective Accountability through Communication and Community Engagement (CCE). Also to be continuous throughout the life of the project.
(3): Capacity development of the affected population, on protection, sanitation, hygiene and risks of fires, diseases, flood and wandering around the safe environment.
(4): Planting of fire stations.
(5): Preparedness culture development. Fire outbreaks preventive measures and also floods.
(6): Governance structure improvement. This is going to be achieved by involvement of all age group, for equality.
(7): Advocacy: Shelters, WASH, FOOD and other sectors where there are gaps.
(8): Manual registration of returnees, displacement, mortality and natality.
(9): Camp profiling updates.
(10): Provision of tools for sanitation and maintained the culture, and discourage open defecations
(11): Training of Staffs, volunteers, women and men community leaders and Bulamas,
(12): Contingency and emergency preparedness.
(13): CCCM support and activities proper throughout the life of the project.
TARGETS: returnees and Displaced communities around Nganzai/Gajiram, Jere and Maiduguri.

2 - Activities

In oder to to ensure a more qualitative, efficient and accurate respond, PPGW will implement a comprehensive CCCM intervention which will involved the following key activities:1- Effective Accountability through communication and community Engagement (CCE). For a guarantee unhindered access to direct feedback loops for all IDP residents, PPGW will continue to manage the complaints and Feedbacks Response Mechanism.2: Community led Improvement and Maintenance. PPGW CCCM team will closely monitor sites, and will conduct regular assessments on site conditions, in order to identify urgent site maintenance and upgrades required to protect the safety and well being of IDPs.3: Contingency planning and Emergency preparedness. To strengthen protection of IDPs against critical risks, PPGW CCCM team will implement mitigation, preparedness and response strategies against rapidly onset emergencies within the target IDP sites. Based on PPGW experience managing camps in Maiduguri, Jere and Nganzai, anticipated risks include fire outbreaks, Sanitation issues, diseases out break and flooding.4: Hygiene promotion. Through the WASH committee. In order to promote good hygiene practice and strengthen IDPs first line resilience against outbreaks of diseases PPGW will conduct hygiene promotion awareness campaign in all location we are covering5: Camp Coordination and Camp Management. Strengthening the community Governance structures.6: Capacity Building/ Training.
