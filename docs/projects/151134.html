Project Summary

Somali Children Development Association have developed this program document in order to continue responding to the life threatening nutrition situation in Buurdhuubo Town. The overall objective of this intervention is to reduce malnutrition related morbidity and mortality in children under five and women of childbearing age in buurdhuubo Town with community and facility based nutrition interventions.
The program expected results will be achieved through scaling-up the integrated management of acute malnutrition (IMAM) while also promoting adequate infant young child feeding practices

Needs

The FSNAU 2018 GU season and Nutrition Assessment specified Morbidity rates are high in all assessed But highest Among Population groups Surveyed in south Gedo riverine (Buurdhuubo and Baardheere) Currently Their is A gap of the nutrition Situation in Buurdhuubo Town and surrounding villages The Humanitarian NGO working in Buurdhuubo Town now reaches limited areas and their is need of nutrition interventions Existing in the area Therefore SOCDA organization Which is active member in the Gedo nutrition cluster recommends to be offered Partnership by Unicef so as to cover the gaps existing in the area. Malnutrition is one of the most persistent humanitarian challenges in Buurdhuubo despite concerted efforts to address it. Acute malnutrition in children between 6-59 months is a direct outcome indicator of recent changes in nutritional status which could be attributed to changes in food security, WASH and health situations among others. The significant deterioration of malnutrition situation seen among the IDPs can be attributed to high morbidity (disease incidence e.g. Malaria, measles …etc), low humanitarian support, poor child feeding and caring practices. Similarly, SOCDA generally felt that food insecurity, limited health service availability, like poor EPI coverage, and increased morbidity, poor health seeking behavior, and difficulty of accessing clean water supply are driving factors for the current situation. Malnutrition is an outcome indicator highlighting needs a number of sectors, and the vulnerability of communities In South Gedo riverine Mainly Buurdhuubo town is very high. Villages existing the gaps Surriyo, Malkahidda, Qeydo, Gardabeyle, Teysow, Laheley, Xadhuunley, Timacadde, Saabley, weeldheen, Uurqaad and Diidowbullaa

Activities

The proposed project targets severely, moderately malnourished children aged 6 to 59 months and pregnant and lactating women. The purpose of the proposed program will be to scale up the IMAM program and establish fixed 2 OTPs and 3 mobile TSFP sites in Buurdhubo Town. thereby decreasing malnutrition-related morbidity and mortality among children younger than five years in Buurdhubo Town
