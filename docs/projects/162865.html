Project Summary

In Kassala State, the recurrent and prolonged periods of drought, accompanied by sporadic torrential rains that cause flooding and destruction of the vegetation cover, increasingly deteriorate the subsoil, reducing its capacity to absorb water. The lack of adequate water structures for the supply of safe water for domestic use constantly exposes the population to various diseases that further worsen the harsh living conditions. Many water stations are unusable and require rehabilitation and training of local management and maintenance personnel to ensure their sustainability. This project aims to facilitate access to water for the most vulnerable populations in the four locations targeted.

1 - Needs

Following field visits and meetings with the institutional representatives of the Ministry of Health and Social Welfare, Ministry of Production and Economic Resources, SDWC and Ministry of Animal Resources and Fisheries (Ministry of Animal Resources and Fisheries), HACs and local communities, the following needs of the population of the target locations of the proposal have been identified: 1. Need to increase the amount of water available through rehabilitation and upgrading water structures including deep wells (MWY) and surface wells equipped with hand pumps. 2. Need to improve the management of the installations by the members of the WASH Committees and the SDWC; 3. Need to improve knowledge, attitudes and hygienic-sanitary practices (to increase the health and nutritional status of beneficiaries)

2 - Activities / Outputs

A1.1- Rehabilitation of six existing water yard A1.2 Rehabilitation of ten mini water yard A1.3 Upgrade seven hand dug well to mini water yard by installing solar pump system,water tank 10-15 cubic meter, distribution points, fence and any related facilities A.1.4 Rehabilitation of sex hand pump A2.1 Creation, formation of committees for the management, maintenance and maintenance of water structures, follow-up and distribution of small equipment A3.1 Implementation of twenty nine H & S awareness campaigns for beneficiaries of water schemes (including students from beneficiary communities' schools)

5 - Environment Marker

5.1 - Impact on environment/natural resources

This project aims to optimize water resources through the rehabilitation of water facilities. In fact, the repair of water structures implies the reduction of waste due to leaks. In addition, upgrading to solar-powered pumping systems ensures minimal environmental impact and increased sustainability of the system.

6 - Protection mainstreaming

3=Yes, room for improvement

6.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

The design of project activities was based on needs assessment that reached representatives from all gender groups. Along side technical requirements, the selection of sites for water points will consider the needs of women and girls. The project will also deliberately target the participation of women in all the stages of the project implementation including involvement in decisions making structures like water user committees.

7 - Accountability to Affected People and Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse
7.1 - How will the project share information and collect community feedback and use it to improve your activities? Please select as many as relevant.

Face to face with community leaders with aid workers

7.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will inform and interact with inter-agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including SEA and abuse complaints?

COOPI and partners will create awareness to the affected population on their rights and potential risks of safeguarding concerns. In addition, all gender and age groups within the affected population will be sensitized on the need and channels for reporting safeguarding concerns. The project partners will position themselves as willing and ready to welcome concerns from beneficiaries through regular interaction and soliciting for feedback. Key project staff involved in the project and in contact with the affected populations will be sensitized on their obligations in ensuring safeguarding of the beneficiaries.
