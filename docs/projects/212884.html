Project Summary

This project aims to address the needs of the affected population in Aleppo and Idleb, including widows' camps, through comprehensive protection (CP) services within Child-Friendly Spaces (CFS) and mobile teams. With full access in widows' camps facilitated by the GBV program, the project will target 2800 women, 3500 girls,7000 boys, and 2200 men, encompassing the elderly and people with disabilities.
There's a pressing need for protection services, particularly CP, in these areas, with a significant gap in CFS coverage due to funding constraints and capacity limitations. MF will focus on Idleb city - Harim and north Aleppo (Azaz, Al Bab), extending CP services through mobile teams and CFSs.
The project will unfold in stages:
1. Collaborating with camp administration to secure project locations, prioritizing confidentiality and safety.
2. Ensuring involvement of key stakeholders throughout the operations management cycle.
3. Establishing elected committees of Beneficiary Needs Focal points (BNFs), inclusive of people with disabilities and elderly women, for decision-making on activities and locations.
4. Selecting project staff tailored to beneficiary demographics and cultural context, striving for gender balance.
5. Implementing activities through mobile teams, each comprising a CP case manager, PSS worker, female facilitator, male facilitator, and involving local authorities.
6. Creating gender-sensitive safe spaces for boys and girls.
7. Providing secure interview and case management locations for highly vulnerable individuals.
8. Conducting training for field staff on PFA&PSS, CPIE, referral pathways, and case management.
9. Raising awareness and organizing age-appropriate recreational activities in safe spaces.
10. Offering case management and referrals for vulnerabilities among all demographics.
11. Delivering parenting curriculum to aid crisis management.
12. Targeting the community through capacity-building efforts for humanitarian workers.
13. Overseeing project stages from design to closing, managing operations, ensuring cluster cooperation, and maintaining quality outcomes through skilled programming specialists.
14. conduct program training on community-based child protection interventions.
