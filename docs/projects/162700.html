Project Summary

The project will be implemented by CAFOD in partnership with national NGO ( SOS) . The project will tragets host community - in White Nile state/ Alsalm locality in 04 areas communities - Alradis 1, Alradis 11, Jouri and Oum Sangour. The project overall objective is to improve food security and livelihood of the host community - The livelihood of the host community depends mainly on farming and non-farming activities. The project will focus on host community are either farmers or pastoralists.
Total targeted groups are1700 (700 HHs in Alradis 1and Alradis 11, 500 HH in Oumsangur and 500 in Juri HC) who will directly benefited from the project services.
The community from the selected areas are vulnerable and are hardly hit by current country economic situation and climate changes. Their livelihood is mainly farming, livestock and non-farming activities..
Strategy of selection of individual beneficiaries of the project will be based on the level the needs in terms of food security, socio-economic services and women headed household. Community consultation include community forums and the leadership of the villages will be involved in leading the processes of project design and identification of the HHs. Project partner staff and community leaders will trained in Save, Accessible, Dignify and inclusive program- SADI to support community in developing community complaint mechanism- and feedback.
Project activities include:
- Provision of agriculture services and inputs.
Provision of veterinary services, this will include, conduct refreshment training to 10 CAHWs, Vaccination and castration to 25,000 heads of livestock belonging to 1000.
- Formation 10 groups of 30 members each (300 persons) and training of field agents at village levels.
- Provision of cash assistance- to 10 saving and loan groups.
- Train partner staff and community members in Save, Accessible, Dignify and Inclusive program tools.
- Establish on Community action group (CAG) and carry out DRR training of 20 selected members from Juri HC in DRR , develop Community action plans CAPs. and Implement and monitor the CAP.
Project monitoring: At the outset of the project CAFOD and partner will carry out a baseline survey and gather relevant information based on the indicators identified in the log frame –
The project will monitor through field visits, partners update, and progress report .An internal mid-term review will also form part of the monitoring system and this will also provide an opportunity for the team and communities to reflect and adapt the adaptive monitoring system. The MEL Framework will be adapted at this stage to reflect changes.
The team will capture unanticipated as well as planned change and this will be planned methodically (each quarter) by the team and also involve community members, government officials and other key stakeholders. These will be captured in case studies and stories of change by using narrative photos and videos

1 - Needs

The influx of refugee in white Nile (mainly SS) , with an estimated 860,000 reported in the country as of 30 Sept. 2019( OCHA report),.made a lot of pressure to host community- interim of access and sharing limited resources. Host communities are also struggling under current Sudan’s economic situation- and there have been instances where they have disrupted response activities and requested compensation for access to refugee areas. The fragile and vulnerable capacity of existing basic services at host community areas in WNS, has been influenced by heavy presence of South Sudanese refugees who added more burden for it to meet the increasing needs of both refugees and host community, which subsequently led to competition and frustration among the host communities. An inter-Agency need assessment for the host community adjacent to the refugee camps in White Nile was conducted during 17 – 22 Feb, 2019, IN which CAFOD fully participated, objectively to identify the current gaps on basic services, priorities of host community, and to understand the peaceful co-existence context between refugees and host community. The assessment result show that in the areas Radis 1, Radis 11, Oumsangoour and Juri is in most needed of food security and livelihood intervention. The proposed activities targeted farming and non-farming , vocational training tragets youth and women income generating activities.
