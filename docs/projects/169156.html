Project Summary

Tabitha's offices are only situated 4km away from the blast and affected by the blast. Tabitha's team conducted a rapid needs assessment (RNA) in the districts of Rmeil and Bourj Hammoud together with Helpage and local partners, consulting more than 800 individuals, including key stakeholders. These districts are located close to the port, are partially destroyed and heavily affected. The acute needs identified include the needs for reconstruction, shelter repair, health support, food assistance, and protection. People whose home has been fully destroyed are staying with their family or host families. Houses with partial destruction are still inhabited, particularly elderly have not moved out. Based on the needs assessment and sectoral expertise, Tabitha and its partner MSD will respond in Rmeil, Medaawar, Achrafieh and Bourj Hammoud and focus for this proposal on food security. The needs for food assistance are expected to skyrocket (WFP) due to destroyed stocks and disrupted supply chains. In food security the support will initially include prepared meals for people that due to the effects of the blast lack facilities to cook. Later on food parcels will be provided, in line with sector requirements, for a period of 1 month.

Plan fields

1 - Needs

Through the RNA, Tabitha was able to identify vulnerable groups including elderly people who were left alone, Tabitha is planning to prioritize the multi-sectoral needs for at-risk groups, particularly female-headed households, older persons headed households and those living alone. This project will tackle food security, however Tabitha and its partner MSD will be able to refer the cases to other projects run by Tabitha or to other NGOs who are already providing support.Collaboration and coordination will be a priority to avoid overlapping and support as many beneficiaries as possible.

2 - Activities

Individuals and HH will receive ready to eat meals on a daily bases in the first month of the program. Households will receive 2x food baskets during this project period which will be compiled according to the cluster guidelines. Covid19 will be mainstreamed during the distributions with awareness sessions and a hygiene kit added to each food basket consisting out of soap and a mask.
