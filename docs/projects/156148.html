Needs

The Syrian crisis has severely limited food production, marketing, imports and reduced the availability of food causing prices to rise households across the country are struggling with a lack of food or money to buy food. Furthermore, high prices of food and fuel, depreciation of Syrian Pound, poor food availability and consumption, lower crop production/failure and negative coping strategies have increased the needs. Moreover, there are a few NGOs that are providing vocational training is inside Idleb and Aleppo Governorates and inside Zoghra camp according to Ataa MEAL need assessment no NGO is providing vocational training to the displaced people coming from Homs city. In this context Ataa field teams coordinated with the camp manager there to have statistics about the urgent needs in the targeted camp and area and the most urgent needs were as the table below, the following professions are the most necessary professions which are needed in the targeted areas.Tailor Fixing cars Carpenter Dairy factories2 10 3 3Barber Smith Filtering Diesel Electronics3 3 2 7 Moreover, there are too many young people are joining the armed groups because they can't go to schools and university and they don't know any profession. So if from implementing this project Ataa will teach them the most required professions in the targeted areas in order to help them to depend on themselves.
