Project Summary

The "Inclusive Learning Spaces for Conflict-Affected Children" intervention is designed to address the critical educational needs of children in conflict-affected regions, specifically targeting Borno and Yobe States in Northeast Nigeria. The project aims to establish and renovate temporary learning spaces, ensuring a safe and protective environment for boys, girls, and adolescents affected by crises, including those with disabilities.
The primary purpose of this project is to create inclusive learning environments that promote access to quality education for conflict-affected children aged 3-17. By renovating existing classrooms and establishing temporary learning spaces, the intervention aims to provide a conducive atmosphere for learning, safeguarding children from the various challenges posed by the protracted crisis in the region.
The target population includes conflict-affected boys, girls, and adolescents, with a specific focus on children with disabilities. The project aims to benefit internally displaced persons (IDPs), returnees, and host community children in Borno and Yobe States, where the impact of insecurity, attacks on educational facilities, and climate-related crises has disrupted access to education.
The intervention will be implemented in identified locations within Borno and Yobe States, Northeast Nigeria. These areas have been severely affected by the crisis, with a significant number of schools deemed inadequate or destroyed, exacerbating the educational challenges faced by children.

1 - Needs

The project's justification lies in the urgent need to address the complex challenges hindering access to education for conflict-affected children in Borno and Yobe States, Northeast Nigeria. The protracted crisis has resulted in poor and unsafe school infrastructure, inadequate learning spaces, and the destruction of numerous schools. These challenges, coupled with insecurity, attacks on educational facilities, and a shortage of teachers, have created a dire situation. The project strategically addresses these needs by renovating classrooms, establishing secure learning spaces, and providing essential educational supplies. Safety measures, including gender-segregated facilities and psychosocial support training, aim to protect vulnerable children, particularly girls and those with disabilities. By comprehensively tackling issues of infrastructure, safety, and access to resources, the project not only responds to the immediate needs but also contributes to the long-term goal of rebuilding and strengthening the education sector in the zone.

2 - Activities

The project will undertake the following activities to address the identified needs:Preposition contingency education supplies, including teaching and learning materials and distribution to schoolsRepair and renovate temporary learning spacesEstablish/rehabilitate gender-segregated latrines and water pointsTrain teachers and adolescents on psychosocial support and the referral pathwayProvide HH cash assistance to ensure children continue education during the conflict/climate change related crises/displacement

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

The project will employ a multi-faceted approach to ensure protection mainstreaming, prioritizing the safety and well-being of all beneficiaries. Gender-sensitive facilities, including segregated latrines and water points, will be established to address the specific needs of girls and prevent gender-based violence. Psychosocial support training for teachers and adolescents will create a protective learning environment, particularly benefiting children facing displacement and adversity. Collaboration with local protection agencies will inform the project's design, ensuring the inclusion of marginalized groups, including children with disabilities. Robust community engagement, feedback mechanisms, and regular consultations will be integral to identifying and addressing emerging protection concerns. The project will consistently align with international protection standards, promoting a rights-based approach that prioritizes the dignity and security of all individuals, fostering a safe and inclusive educational environment in line with humanitarian principles.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

The project will establish a comprehensive feedback mechanism to ensure the voices of the most vulnerable community members are heard and valued. This will include the implementation of suggestion boxes, community meetings, and dedicated hotlines accessible to all. Specifically, efforts will be made to engage marginalized groups, such as children, girls, and individuals with disabilities, through targeted consultations. Collaborative partnerships with local community leaders and organizations will facilitate inclusive discussions, ensuring representation from diverse perspectives. Regular outreach activities will be conducted to reach communities in remote and hard-to-reach locations, ensuring that feedback mechanisms are accessible to all. The project team will prioritize cultural sensitivity and adaptability in collecting feedback, creating a participatory approach that empowers the most vulnerable community members to express their concerns, preferences, and recommendations for continuous improvement and responsiveness.
