Project Summary

The project is designed to respond to the WASH needs of 160244 people (Men: 33564, Women: 36480, Girls: 44199, Boys: 46001),( 72109 IDPs, 88135 hosted people, and returnees 11583 ) with 1315 people of them are people with disabilities, to respond to their protracted needs by ensuring the good access to sustainable WASH service, The project is designed to respond to the needs of IDPs, Returnees, and hosted people, stayed in the north of Syria in the communities and the IDPs camps .to cover their needs by delivering humanitarian WASH supplies and services to most vulnerable people: men, women, boys, and girls will benefit from the activities which aim to increase access to sustainable water, sanitation, and environmental hygiene solutions, the project will be transferred the water station from working by fuel to work by a hygiene system most of it solar energy.
One of the causes of the spreading of Leishmania is the bad solid waste management in place and this is another factor that can increase the threat on the vulnerable people
SECD is aiming to solve this long-known problem by undertaking by supporting the local council to activate good solid waste management which is reducing and eliminating the Leishmania causes.
Through this project, SECD is planning to support the targeted communities with the following activities:
-Installation of the solar power system to turn the operating system of the water stations: 30 water stations. and conducting a water safety plan, and establishing a cost recovery system to ensure the suitability of the project. The solar system will be used for both wells operation and/or for pumping stations
-Distribution of 10000 consumable hygiene kits (second round) for the most vulnerable people the component of the HKs are linked with (WASH Cluster - Hygiene Kits v2 final - COVID 19).
-Conducting 39 hygiene promotion campaigns, SECD will target the entire community with at least COVID 19 awareness. The hygiene promotion campaign will focus on Coronavirus-2019, SECD will adhere to the recommendation of the WASH cluster in the implementation of the hygiene promotion and will focus on using the recommended methods, also during these campaigns SECD will distribute PSEA for beneficiaries to increase the knowledge and access to PSEA reporting hotline.
Supporting sanitation works which include but not limited to :
-Provision and installation of 12050 garbage bins 0.1m3 size for the small camps.
--Rehabilitation and or /providing of the solid waste truck.
-Support SWM unit with salaries and fuel for 6 months, and uniforms for the workers, masks, gloves to protect the workers of coronavirus-2019 in this service with Conducting solid waste campaigns to encourage people to participate in this humanitarian effort.
-Rehabilitation of the sewer system which includes but is not limited to Rehabilitation and extending of Sewer system network ( (HDPE 30cm diameter ):3800m,- (HDPE100 cm diameter ):9600m.
SECD will be in a close position with the local council, the beneficiaries, the IDPs, and the host community and will involve them in all the project stages from the planning till the closure of the project to adhere to the community engagement approach by inviting them to regular FGDs and open all the channels to receive their feedback, complaints, and opinions.
the proposed project ensure that the sanitation service would be provided to schools, hospitals, children friendly center, Women empowerment centers, and public bakeries.
SECD have regular access in Non-GoS controlled areas: in Aleppo governorate in the area controlled by Turkish Armed forces and National Army and In Idleb governorate in the area under controlled by non-state armed groups

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumptions:- Access to the targeted locations will be still available.- Community acceptance for the project.-The border between Turkey and Syria still open.-The targeted locations are secure and accessible.- The assumption is that humanitarian principles are applied and adhered to for all stakeholders.-The needed materials are available in the local market. Risks and mitigation (Mitigating Measure): Risk (1): Airplane shelling, Likelihood of Risk Occurring: Low, Effect on expected Results: Very High. Risk Management Strategy: The risk of Security situation (only heavy shelling) and The risk of the camps being targeted by airstrikes is a very low probability according to The historical information for 10 years ago. Risk (2): Outbreak of conflict prevents access to project locations, Likelihood of Risk Occurring: low, Effect on Expected Results: Very High, Risk Management Strategy/Mitigating Measure: Lesson learned from 2013 operations found that while conflict occurred, access could be restored within a few days. A cautious approach that plans for these types of delays is expressed in the project work plan. Risk (3): Application of humanitarian principles: The risk of interference and aid diversion by armed groups exists, Likelihood of Risk Occurring: LowEffect on expected Results: Very High.Risk Management Strategy: high coordination with the AWG, signing MOUs with the local council and As a mitigation measure, SECD will monitor and report any allegations or instances of interference or aid diversion. SECD also engages with AWG to share information on access- and risk-related developments in NWS.
