Project Summary

Global Communities will build on the current under implementation programs, where we provide the essential WASH services in Atmeh Camp, we also conducted a rapid needs assessment in the camps in the Euphrates Shield area, where we found the need for improving the roads conditions inside the camps is considered a priority.
We plan to 1)-Implement gravel and the related drainage infrastructure for 20 km of roads inside the camps. 2)-Promote participatory management structures, 10 sites with participatory management committees, including active participation by women 3)-Train cluster member NGOs on camp governance and/or protection mainstreaming for humanitarian actors, 10 % of cluster member NGOs trained in camp management components and cross-cutting issues 4)-Equip and train emergency responders and IDP committees in IDP sites, 25 sites with self-run emergency response staff capabilities, including first aid and fire response.
Where we will target the newly established camps in Atmeh and around Atmeh, and the existed camps in Azaz.
The intervention will require 470,800 $ for assisting 50,000 people.

4 - Needs

Due to the deteriorated security situation in NWS, almost 2.5 million people became IDPs in NWS, 50% of the IDPs are staying in the last resort shelter solution which is the tents/camps. The majority of newly arrived IDPs were preferring to seek refuge in Collective Centres, Unfinished Buildings, and Hosted in the community, but these accommodations were temporary and intentions were to move elsewhere when weather conditions improved. Based on the movement intention survey conducted by REACH in December 2019, the majority of the hosted families out of the camps settings are expected to move towards the last resort shelter solution in the border camp settings or in the relatively safe areas in Afrin and Azaz when the weather gets better. Which will put us in front of a real need for improving the camps conditions including the roads and the floods mitigation measures which include the drainage infrastructure. Global Communities is already existed in Atmeh site where we implemented similar activities, however the needs for graveling the roads in the camps is always a need due to the topography of the camp locations and the agricultural roads in and nearby the camps. During the period December 2019 and January 2020, almost 30,000 people arrived to Atmeh, they were either hosted by their relatives or located in the newly established camp in the agricultural lands. We also conducted a rapid assessment in Azaz camps, where we found the need for implementing the gravel is a priority in the camps.

5 - Implementation of activities

Global Communities is planning for rehabilitating the damaged roads affected with the flood and heavy raining , GC will utilize its experience in Atmeh Camp to improve the quality of camp management and promote the community participation in the targeted sites. GC will support the formation of community committees in the camp, these committees will represent all camp residents including the most vulnerable groups such as women, children, the elderly and people with disabilities. The camp committees would have substantial roles in planning, programming, monitoring and evaluating service provisions in the camps. Ensure sites with self-run emergency response staff capabilities, including first aid and fire response. 1.Community engagement: collecting detailed information about the specific roads and infrastructures require rehabilitation, through FGDs, KIIs, Observations with all the stakeholders. 2.Topographic surveys for the selected locations, developing the BoQs and the technical specification.3. HLP due diligence for the targeted locations with the proposed rehabilitation activities.3.Procurement process: through transparent procurement process, advertising and receiving offers from the bidders, opening bids, TEC, and signing the contract with the selected contractor.4.Implementation for the roads rehabilitation, drainage systems rehabilitation and construction, and supervising the implementation of the rehabilitation and construction works.5.Promote participatory management structures through establishing 10 sites with participatory management committees, including active participation by women.6.Train cluster member NGOs on camp governance and/or protection mainstreaming for humanitarian actors, through conducting training for 10 % of cluster member NGOs in camp management components and cross-cutting issues. 7.25 Sites with self-run emergency response.Along the implementation of the activities we will have an ongoing monitoring for the implementation.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

During the implementation of the planned activities, we expect the following risks; There is a high probability of risk of increased community tension due to different assistance packages, unclear location selection criteria, and another risk that selected roads are influenced by local authorities may contribute to nepotism, tension in the community and risk of most vulnerable being left out, this can be mitigatedthis can be mitigated by ensuring location selection criteria is uniform/agreed among sector members, establishing complaint mechanism at community level., and training to humanitarian staffs. There is also a high probability for the risk of c risk of creation of spontaneous settlement in location where no management system is set-up and/or services are not provided, this can be mitigated by;1. Carefully and periodically check and coordinate with CCCM Cluster to ensure that relevant management systems can be set-up2. Raise awareness of IP and staff on the dangers connected to incentivating (indirectly) the creation of unmanaged camps.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

The following risks and assumptions are expected while implementing the activities; Selecting service provider with low capacity (technical/financial/project management) 2.Implementing of unneeded rehabilitation works or over reporting. 3.Procuring with high price, Over budget, A contractor with low capacity/quality 4.Safety and Security risk during the implementation 5.Market failure - Limited supply and large demand The existing and planned mitigating measures; -Review assessments by senior staff and return to field team with correction action -Feedback mechanism -Beneficiary registration and verification process -Post Distribution Monitoring -Proper inventory management -Reviewing the data/ purchasing requests by senior technical team member, send correction notes to the field. -Precise market assessments -Negotiation process -Safety procedures.
