Project Summary

This project will respond to protection needs related to Gender Based Violence GBV of South Sudanese refugees and their hosting communities in who faced threats of physical violence, exploitation, abuse, neglect in particularly to the current economic and health deterioration in light of COVID 19 in Sharg Alneil locality in Khartoumstate , needs for food security and livelihood . BPWO embraces community based protection approach by building capacity of targeted communities through equipping them with necessary skills and knowledge through reflect methodology to implement community driven solutions to their local communities. The project will carry out extensive consultations with local community and line ministries as an entry point for smooth project implementation. The intervention will enhance local capacity on GBV through training of community based protection networks (CBPNs) and social workers ,establishment of referral mechanisms , provision of direct support including case management for GBV survivors and distribution of personal dignity hygiene kits (PHKs) .The project will link the protection, health and legal response needed by GBV survivors through provision of case management and referral servicesf women centers is core activity as lack of having women center with well-designed space for provision of PSS hindered the GBV survivors from accessing the urgent support /assistance. Project team will conduct services mapping and organize monthly meetings with relevant partners and services providers to ensure better access of GBV survivors to other services. Women and girls will receive direct support through provision of PHKs. The project team will organize round-tables forums with police, health cadre, community leaders regarding the coordination and strengthening the reporting and referral of cases. Project team will provide weekly awareness raising /PSS sessions through microphone in the women centers to ensure social distancing, individuals will be reached with similar messages through structured FGD sessions. Awareness raising messages on GBV and SGBV risks will be delivered through mobile public awareness campaigns. The project will consider APP through community participation on conducting needs assessments and implementation of activities as well as establishment of community based feedback and complaint mechanisms, composed of Community Volunteers (CVs). Process and time for response will clearly be communicated to beneficiaries. The intervention will follow GBV guidelines, GBV Standard operation procedures to response to the needs and the identified gaps and will use vulnerability criteria applied for protection sector.
BPWO will commit to collaborate and coordinate with the GBV Sub-Sector on the national level and the GBV Working Group in ND. BPWO will attend the coordination meetings and report to the 4Ws.

1 - Needs

The project is designed to provide multi-sectoral life-saving assistance to South Sudanese refugees through effective and sustainable, Protection, Livelihood and food security and COVID-19 impact targeting 655 SS Refugees. 80% women and girls in HH haj yousif Sharq Elneeil Locality khartoum who are at GBV risks including rape, sexual and physical violence and psychological abuse cited as frequently occurring types of GBV harmful coping mechanisms including early marriage, illicit alcohol brewing , survival sex, limited livelihood opportunities, high levels of poverty, food insecurity, Most of them earn their living by informal economy, workers, and some marginal work, besides the continued economic downturn high inflation, fuel shortage, currency depreciation, lack of income opportunities which is linked to large numbers of drop out of school exposing to child labour, early marriage/pregnancy There are huge protection needs, reporting of GBV is limited and the referral pathway are non-existent, physical safety and psychosocial well-being of girls and boys affected due to the protracted exposure of gender based violence, disruption of social support systems, increasing poverty, and the persistent lack of access to quality social services necessities and the continued impact of COVID-19 HRP food security sector 2021 has identified 7,979,722 PIN, targeting 6,561,201 - 55,972 vulnerable, which need food assistance and lack of tools

The project targeting 655 HH will provide lifesaving hand wash and masks to improve hygiene practices in times of COVID 19 pandemic, distribution of hygiene and menstrual health kits for 335 Persons Reflect methodology which support the community to study early warning and work together in solve the problems with their own resources. BPWVO will addressed GBV by establishing a temporary women’s safe space in selected accessible gathering points based on the consultation with women and girls as well as community leaders with child safe space, to provide different services to 335 GBV survivors in the locality, strengthen the referral pathway and enhance the self-reliance of women and youth by promoting activities targeting households and individuals through livelihood Vocational training to 200 women and 100 youth in to be economically empowered in a studied Protection component where FSL vocational training for women in soap making, leather products, Hair dressing and henna painting, , 100 youth will be provided within this component Vocational apprenticeship and distribution of CBI to 20 diasbles to mitigate GBV and to sustain food security and strengthen opportunities for resilience and self-reliance BPWVO will conduct 9 awareness advocacy sessions on GBV addressing the wider SGBV issues to train the public community on GBV topics, initiate 5 women committees, training 35 Volunteers workers to build their capacity on GBV topics,

Through this intervention, BPWO is committed safeguard Right holders from violence and discrimination and is aimed at obtaining full respect for the rights of the individuals. To achieve this, BPWO embraces community based protection approach, for building capacity of local communities, service providers and government officials. Their capacity will be enhanced in analyzing & finding solutions to protection problems, inclusive of reducing threats& risks, reducing vulnerability of affected population and increasing their capacity to cope with protection problems following exposure to shock and disaster.
