Project Summary

In line with Protection response strategies as mentioned in the HRP and HNO 2022- Wared in collaboration with the Protection sector will implement the protection project in Duma / Alzabadani / Dara’a through the Activities below:
1) Providing awareness-raising services on Child Rights and Child Protection, and Risk Education to IDPs, returnees, children, and engaged children with disability, children in the worst form of child labor (WFCL), children under risk of early marriage, unaccompanied or separated children, and children out of school - all through mobile activities, home visits, and/or center-based activities.
2) Providing Capacity Building Workshops on CP, and Safe identification and referral (SIR) to teachers, community volunteers, and key persons from the local community, to take their role and the needed knowledge to start identifying the most vulnerable cases within their community and refer them to Wared case management team.
3) Providing 10 PSS sessions for children (06-12), and 10 Life skills sessions for children (13-17) aiming to increase their capacity to deal with daily challenges they are facing and to enhance their wellbeing. The structured PSS sessions aiming to provide the children with sessions with tools and ways to enhance their different stages of development and to empower them to deal with the daily challenges which could may they face, in addition to increase their resilience and capacity to demonstrate positive methods of dealing with their problems, and the sessions topics were (Who I’m? - Me and the others - Collaboration - Team work - Creativity - Dealing with Problems - Stress Management - Emotions management).
Furthermore, the structured Life skills sessions aiming to provide the adolescence and youth with tools and ways to enhance their different stages of development and to empower them to deal with the daily challenges and difficulties which could may they face, in addition to increase their life skills, problem solving skills, and their capacity to demonstrate positive methods and ways of dealing with their problems, and the sessions topics were (Dealing with conflicts - Communication skills - Decisions making - Creative thinking - Critical thinking - Dealing with Problems - Stress Management - Emotions management).
4) Providing 10 positive parenting skills sessions for caregivers and increasing their self-health. in this session each caregiver describe about his strength resources and how we use it in our life - Reduce psychological stress, this session talked about how we can reduce our stress during work or our social life - Parent tension and the influence of parents, in this session we give some advice about the parent tension while dealing with their children - Parents stress and its impact on child: In this session we talked about how to deal with their stress, and the impact of the stress not deliver to their children - Positive Attention: This session talked about what is attention and how it should be positive and don’t have a negative impact, for example (what should we do in problems situations. - Active Control: In this session we talked about what are the tools that help us to control our children's behaviors - Positive children rearing: This session is very important because in this session we know each parent how they reared their children and give them some advices how to rear children - Increasing self-confidence: This session parents learnt how to increase their self-confidence to do their best role as a parent and how they deal with stress - Going back to the past and looking forward to the future: This session we made a small discussion about the whole program and what they want to do in the future - Some information about COVID-19 and how to protect ourselves and children from it) aiming to provide the caregivers with tools to deal positively with their children.
5) provide Child protection case management services and referral, with high focus on GBV cases .

4 - Needs

based on the coordination with the protection sector and the HNO 2021 Wared conducted a need assessment at the end of 2021 to collect the basic needs of the areas where Wared worked before, and the assessment was done with different tools ( FGDs - interviews...etc) with the stakeholders and the key people mentioned that the percentage of drop out of school increased in order to find work to gain enough money for their family especially males, and the percentage of the girls were getting married in early age and left the school increased with different reasons such as traditions and to reduce the cost of living for the main family, so based on the assessment the main points and needs were: •Verbal and physical abuse•Child Labor•Dropping out of schools’ issues•Explosive hazards risks•More than 15% of children are considered as PWDs•Domestic violence toward women reportedly increased.•Early marriage: A range between 40 to 50% of girls are getting married between the ages of 13 and 17 (reported through key informant interviews), and a range of 60% to 95% of adolescent girls stated that it is common to get married (reported through focus group discussions) at their age. The common reasons for early marriage reported were social norms and financial problems.The assessment identified the most vulnerable groups as persons with disabilities (PWDs), orphans, internally displaced persons (IDPs), households with no source of income, as well as women and child-headed households.

5 - Implementation of activities

All the sessions (PSS – Life skills – positive parenting skills – Explosive hazard risks) will be conducted with the following steps:Outreach and coordination with key community person activities to Identify and select the most vulnerable people to attend the sessions. the registration phase for all the participants and conduct the Consent form.Divide the participants to attend the needed sessions. During the sessions, the case management team will always be observed the cases to start the process of the case management strategy for CP and GBV cases.All the activities above will be conducted by building the capacity of volunteers from the local communities and providing them with The needed training, a % of those volunteers will be provided training to be a protection\GBV committee in the area to help Wared team reach and provide the service with high quality convenient to the context, those volunteers will have an observation and follow up by Wared team, Wared team will provide them with fundamentals training such as CP\ and GBV basics and SIR how to report or refer the cases with high level of confidentiality, They will help Wared team to refer Cases to the CP\GBV case management team, all the services will be observed from the Protection\ GBV committees from the local community. (All the activities will be conducted inside WARED safe spaces in Duma, Zabadany, and Daraa) with high level of resiliency to conduct the activities through home visits when required.
