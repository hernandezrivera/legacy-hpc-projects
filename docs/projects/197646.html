Project Summary

This project seeks to improve access to education and early recovery for persons with disabilities in 03 (Fako, Manyu and Meme) Divisions in South West Region of Cameroon. Planned activities aim to increase enrollment of at least 500 children with disabilities in schools and support with provision of learning materials to ease teaching and learning process.
Capacity of some 90 teachers in special and mainstream schools will be improve on inclusive education to enable teachers effective teach children with disabilities who enroll in schools. Training sessions on psychosocial support, life skills and risk/disaster management in school will be done to equip teachers to properly teach and care for children with disabilities during emergency situations.
More so, training sessions on alternative means of livelihood will be organized for 50 persons with disabilities on agriculture especially animal farming and market gardening, with provision of seed materials for startup.
Regular awareness and sensitization campaigns on SGBV will be done in local radio stations and other social media platforms. Psychosocial support will be provided to victims and referrals for specialist intervention done when necessary.
The project will ensure increase sensitization and participation of persons with disabilities in humanitarian program planning and functioning of humanitarian system. Interventions will be done while respecting barrier measure for prevention of COVID 19 and other diseases as well as sensitization of target group on the need to get vaccinated will be communicated to all persons with disabilities and ensure materials on COVID 19 prevention is available in accessible format.

1 - Besoins / Needs

The socio-political crisis in the South West and North West Regions of Cameroon has had adverse effect on the lives of persons with disabilities with increase in number of disabilities due to gun shoots and use of improvised explosive devices. People with disabilities find it difficult to access education and to survive due to limited or lack of means of livelihood. During the onset of the crisis, many children with disabilities were forced out of school and some moved to safer locations. Their displacement prevented easy access to education for those who live with relatives and caregivers who do not consider the education of children with disability a priority. A major cause of this low priority given to education of children with disabilities is the high cost involve in meeting school needs such as fees, writing materials, transportation among others. In addition, the safety of children with disabilities in school during confrontations between state security forces and non-state arm group is of importance to parents and care givers. School authorities need to demonstrate capacity in risk and disaster management to enable parents allow children with disabilities access education. The continuous killing of school children increases fear and trauma for many children as well as children with disabilities. School authorities need to be trained on psychosocial support as well as improve their capacity on inclusive education considering that children with disabilities need to access education were they are found. Furthermore, the hardship caused by crisis on the population has force many businesses close and others cut down on number of workers. This has increase rate of unemployment with increasing number of persons with disabilities (acquired due to crisis) lost means of livelihood thereby increase suffering. There is need for alternative means of living especially for IDPs such as engagement in agriculture as well as vocational training to improve on their living conditions.
