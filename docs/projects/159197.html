Project Summary

Our proposed plan is to provide education for the most vulnerable school dropouts and injured children of of GMR. our target is to improve and provide support for the quality of education and provide a safe environment for them. We want to contribute to the improvement of the mental well being and resilience of the affected children in the targeted area.project target is to support 950 vulnerable & ignored children along with 50 teachers by providing emergency education supplies/substitutes and psycho social support. It will include remedial education to school going vulnerable children as well as train teachers for implementing home based education. there will be activities where the children will take lead to boost their morale. Male & Female children who are disabled will be participating in advocacy led campaigns to create solutions for them in collaboration with the education ministry, schools and affected families). The selection criteria for students to be enrolled in the project will ensure that vulnerable students are prioritized, this will include gender balanced selection (600 girls/350 boys), inclusion of students with disabilities,
In addition it will provide logistic support for disabled targets who will have full access to ensure equal opportunity of education despite special needs.
we will be targeting the children injured in GMR as well as the most vulnerable communities in the chosen area.

1 - Needs

Due to the situation in Gaza where an ongoing siege, wars, socio- economic crisis as well as the Great March of Return event has worsened the ongoing scene. This gave rise to increase in the number of vulnerable school children whereby some are injured physically, some are having mental illnesses, financial problems and finally they leave schools at an early age, especially in the ARA of Khan yunis According to research by Save the Children , more than 70% children have sleeping problems and about all the children have depression, hypertension and feel aloof. the Israeli attacks along with siege has affected their daily life as well as attitude towards each other.power cuts also has affected their way of learning and performing whereby they lack concentration and skill achievements. children feel disappointment, annoyed and hostile due to power and water supply cuts. family heads have expressed concern about their children due to all these factors which piles up their daily problems. According to OCHA Report(2019) & UNICEF Report(2018) & TDH (2019), access to education has been affected whereby we see increase in dropouts.Services of UNRWA also decreased in a very large manner which has affected the children as well as their caretakers who are employed by the agency. addition of shifts has also added to their woes. children are being put under an unending pressure of being resilient thus risking their innocence and future. According to the SDR oPT Final 2019 Data, the country report of Out Of School (Unicef July 2018):8.8 of all boys & 11.9 of all girls at the age of five do not attend school. The sharp increase of dropping out occurs at the age of 14 with 11.6 for boys and 2.7 for girls. The increase continues for boys at 22-29% within the age of 15-16..while as girls follow at a lower rate of 5.6-13.3%.Adolescent boys, aged 14 and 15, make up nearly half of all children, up to the compulsory school age of 15, who are out of school, thus a gender-specific pattern of missing education affecting adolescent boys in particular. Children from vulnerable households, children with disabilities, and 14-15-year-old boys are all affected by this system-wide barrier related the inclusiveness, quality and equity of education services in Palestine as reported in the OOSC_SoP report.Among 10-15-year-old children in Palestine, an estimated 4.9 per cent are out of school. Of the 10-15-year-old children who are out of school, an estimated 87.2 per cent of them have dropped out of school, 12.5 per cent have never entered school, and 0.3 per cent have not yet entered school and are expected to enter at a later age.In terms of the characteristics of the child, as also discussed above, a higher percentage of boys (7.6 per cent) are out of school than girls (2.2 per cent). This gender difference becomes more acute as the child gets older. Children with disabilities face particularly high risks of exclusion. Using the 2011 Disability Survey data, an estimated 30.2 per cent of 10-15-year-old children who have a disability are out of school.Due to violent conflicts more than 30,000 children were affected educationally as schools, children and the staff were subject to violence in one way or the other. there is continuous increase in the number of GMR injuries due to which children dropped out of school.so we have a very high number of school dropouts due to GMR and serious financial reasons and to get them back into the school we need to remove barriers by promoting collective efforts for their better future. We will collect data from MoEHE and conduct verification through field surveys for proper results and delivery of services(to avoid duplication/conflict of sorts). We will coordinate with concerned clusters as well as cbos to work together. this will strengthen the network locally and provide better quality of work on field.

2 - Activities

Main activities:-identification and collection of data/ no's of school dropouts through surveys/ field visits.-provide emergency aid to vulnerable children(drop outs,out of school & GMR injured children who cannot access the school) and staff-provide logistic support to vulnerable school children with special needs-target will be 950 school going children(600 boys and 350 girls) in the age group of 13 to 16 yrs whose education outcomes have been affected by hypertension, violence, abuse, destruction of schools as well as overcrowded classrooms with poor electricity and water supplies. these children will be provided with home based learning services .-train 50 teachers (50% Males & 50% Females) whereby 60% teachers will be from ARAs for teaching in homes & 40% teachers will be from the rest of Gaza.-provision of school kits to 300 most vulnerable students whose family cannot provide for them-HCCD will work in coordination with Education cluster, its members , Ministry of Higher Education as well as UNRWA wider reach and effectiveness as well as local CBOS.
