Project Summary

Establishing a support center (and its mobile team) in Babbella for people with disabilities that includes the following activities:
Mental health and psychosocial support
Linguistic and educational support including speech therapy
Technical and social support and training for life skills
Support for those with hearing impairment
Support for those with physical impairment including physical therapy
Support for victims of war
Support for families of people with disabilities including mental support and teaching them how to behave with and treat those in their care
Target beneficiaries will be people with disabilities (regardless of gender and age), however a large focus will be placed on ages 3-15

3 - Needs

People with disabilities (PWD) make up at least 28% of the Syrian population (aged 2+) according to UNDP.The organization currently has a total of 520 registered PWDs (329 males and 191 females), all of whom are also registered as PWDs within the Ministry of Social Affairs and Labor. Their disabilities include mental disabilities (30%), hearing disabilities (35%), visual impairments (2%), loss of limb and/or lack of mobility from war or birth defects (30%), among others (3%). The services will be offered to the PWDs themselves, as well as their families/caretakers, in order to allow them to improve their capacities and skills when caring for their loved ones.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

1) beneficiaries' inability to access the center or not knowing about it and its services, which will be mitigated through the mobile team by bringing people to the center if they are unable to go on their own, as well as advertising in neighborhoods and cooperating with community elders in spreading awareness regarding the center's services;2) humanitarian workers lacking capacities to work with the unique cases that might visit the center, which will be mitigated by hiring highly qualified personnel and ensuring they receive routine trainings from specialists, as well as having regular monitoring and evaluation of the services to ensure the beneficiaries are being well-treated;3) lack of permission from families in regards to equal access of services due to gender, this will be mitigated through the mobile teams delivering services at PWD's homes when it is of utmost urgency, as well as reaching out to community elders and building a trust-based relationship that extends throughout the local community


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

PWDs and females with disabilities in particular face higher risk of sexual exploitation and abuse, thus the center will offer awareness sessions, as well as trainings to build livelihood capacities;additionally, codes of conducts will be put in place in order to ensure a safe environment amongst workers, as well as ensuring that if workers are not predominantly female, there is at least an equal ratio of females to male workers.


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1) insufficient fuel for the mobile teams, which will be circumvented by scheduling routine visits ahead of time and allocating a contingency margin in the budget to be able to access various sources of fuel if absolutely necessary, and paying for PWD's taxi services in emergency cases during absence of fuel;2) inflation and the increase in prices of supplies, will be solved by allocating a contingency margin in the project's budget, as well as looking for local sources for as many of necessary supplies as possible.
