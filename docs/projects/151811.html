Project Summary

The project will be used to fill critical Shelter-NFI gaps in Banadir, Bay and Bekool regions and surrounding locations following the displacement of at least 200,000 people resulting from significant increase in forced evictions in urban centers, ongoing conflict and insecurity, and the continued lack of livelihood opportunity. The ES/NFI response will be consolidated by establishing a team that will provide assistance on a regular basis to the displaced populations in the proposed locations. The project will address the needs of affected population through provision of cluster standardized ESOKs and BNFI kits as well as improve access to adequate and dignified shelter solutions

Needs

Somalia has been in an emergency situation for the last two and half decades. There are almost 11,322 people who are new IDPs that have flocked into Banadir and Baidoa town and the surrounding areas (Human Appeal recent RNA in Banadir and Baidao) which are in desperate need of immediate emergency response due to the SNF and AMISON offense against Al-shabab in lower shabella, middle shabella and Bakool regions. According to Human Appeal's last assessment in Banadir town, 72% of the total IDPs living in Kaxda and Daynille IDP camps reported that the cause of their displacement is due to ongoing conflict in the areas of origin. Many IDPs Settlements lack access to basic household items or materials for proper shelter. Most of the displaced people are women and children who have fled conflict zones or been affected by drought. Newly displaced people are most vulnerable if they do not receive shelter assistance. Many IDP women, girls, boys and men are forced to share makeshift shelters that provide little privacy or protection from extreme weather conditions and pose protection risks for women and girls, particularly in relation to sexual and gender based violence. The most vulnerable IDP households in areas of new or recent arrivals will be prioritized for emergency shelters and NFI kits, while protracted IDPs may be provided with transitional shelters determined by the land tenure obtained. Vulnerable households (often headed by women, including elderly members, minorities, the displaced and those with young children) will be prioritized.Forced evictions from both private and government-owned land and buildings have uprooted over 10,000 people as stated above, mostly in Mogadishu. Findings of recent Human Appeal Rapid Protection Assessment (October 16-18, 2018) in several Banadir IDPs camps demonstrate that living conditions are extremely sub-standard.

Activities

1. Human Appeal will consult with Ministry of Humanitarian and disaster management to install 1,000 transitional shelters with 40 shared WATSAN complexes (with separate stances for male and female) benefiting 6,000 people in Banadir, Bay and Bekool regions. Constructions will be done a plot of land donated by local municipality of Banadir, Bay and Bekool. The certificate of the land will be obtained before the implementation. Beneficiary choice is of primary importance and Human Appeal will present them a first choice of prototype shelter, which they will be able to evaluate and prioritize. 2. Provision of household basic NFI kits (kitchen set, jerry can, solar light, ropes, plastic sheets) to 900 families3. Human Appeal will conduct a PDM to ensure accountability, appropriateness of materials and effectiveness of distribution. Data will be disaggregated sex and age to evaluate whether specific needs of women, girls, boys and men are met. The results will be shared with the cluster and the donor for them to evaluate also4. Distribution of 1,500 ESOKs and 1,000 BNFI kits

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

1. Potential eviction from the areas they displaced to at any time2. Potential rape and sexual exploitation, theft and other potential risks linked to the poor conditions of the existing makeshift shelters3. Highly fire risk due to the random installation/construction of the makeshift shelters

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

The project will ensure equal opportunity to the affected population without any discrimination to gender, age, background, ....etc. Human Appeal will develop the beneficiary selection criteria in consultation with shelter/NFI cluster as well as the gender experts from protection clusters to avid doing harm to the targeted population and prevent risks of exclusion based on gender and age

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

The impact of the armed conflict and violence will be mitigated through immediate shelter/NFI response to the most affected population in the proposed locations, provision of better shelter solutions with equal access to all categories in a well prevented and secured areas
