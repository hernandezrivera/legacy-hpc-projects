Project Summary

As part of World Vision Lebanon's (WVL) response to the Beirut Explosion, WVL will support the rehabilitation of 15 public, private and semi-private schools affected by the Beirut Explosion in the areas of Achrafieh, Gemmayze, Mar Mkhayel, Rmeil, Geitawi, Bourj Hammoud, Sin El Fil, Mazraa, Karantina, Basta, Khandaa al Ghamee and Bachoura. Rehabilitation will be conducted based on a detailed BoQ that will be developed following the assessment of each school.

Associated response plan

Lebanon Flash Appeal 2020

Plan fields

1 - Needs

As a result of the Beirut Port explosions access to learning and education support for more than 55,000children and youth in Beirut will be obstructed. The widespread destruction created multi-level challengesfor children and their families, which will have lasting consequences on enrollment and continuation ofthe learning paths. Severe-to-medium damages to an estimated 120 schools, all of which requires rehabilitation and basic learning equipment and materials. Further assessments will be needed to assess the exact need in each school affected by the blast.

2 - Activities

Rehabilitation of the damaged schools by the Beirut Explosion is necessary to avoid further disruption to children's access to education opportunities. This would be relevant when schools open fully or even should blended learning would be adopted as a modality. Replacement of damaged equipment will not be part of the planned activities for WVL's response. WVL will aim to rehabilitate a total of 15 schools in the affected area. Rehabilitation will be conducted based on a detailed BoQ on each school in the form of direct rehabilitation or cash for rehabilitation modality. The cash for rehabilitation modality will only apply to private and semi-private schools whereby WVL will agree on a BoQ with schools and will provide cash based on receipts for the rehabilitation.
