Project Summary

These results are reflected in nutrition HNO for 2024 with 1.48 million PLWs, adolescent girls, and children under 5 years considered people in need (PIN) for nutrition services in NW Syria with increased rates across malnutrition parameters compared with 2023; the acute malnourished children under 5 years in need increased by36% ( from 1 10,967in 2023 to 151,759 in 2024), the children in need for preventive activities also increased by 12% ( from 812,858 in 2023 to 933,177 in 2024), in addition to 35,061 PLWs in need for treatment and 1 75,375 PLWs in need for BSFP and IFA supplements. Moreover, approximately 153,000 children under five in Syria live with disabilities, facing heightened risks of malnutrition. PWD are 34 percent more likely to be stunted and 53 percent more likely to have symptoms of acute respiratory infection. One in five teenage girls (15-19 years old) either has children, is pregnant, or lactating. Teenage mothers exhibit a higher likelihood of having children aged 6–59 months suffering from wasting, with a higher prevalence of wasting themselves. Amal will establish two Mother baby areas in Albab City and Aldana district at Albab health center 2 under HeRAMS code "B0202051! Albab Health Center 2" and (Hezreh community– Alhanan camps) under HeRAMS code "Hezreh Center G0703207" besides three (Mobile teams) to conduct the following activities, active case finding, referral & treatment of acutely malnourished children under 5 and PLW Community-level screening and referral for treatment of acute malnutrition will be conducted. Children with Moderate Acute Malnutrition and Pregnant and lactating women with a MUAC of less than 23.0cm will be referred to available supplementary treatment programs. Severe acutely malnourished children with complications will be referred for inpatient care at the existing stabilization centers. The project will provide training in nutrition screening and referral services. The project will also ensure that HFs /EPI centers are equipped with minimum supplies needed for nutrition screening activities, (e.g. MUAC tapes, registration and reporting format, referral forms, guidelines etc.), promoting and supporting community mobilization and sensitization activities towards nutrition screening program. The PHC and Health Facilities will be equipped with SAM/MAM cases to be treated. he Project will improve children feeding practices to contribute to the reduction of
morbidity and mortality among infants and young children in NWS by ensuring access to safe, adequate and appropriate nutrition services in 10 months

Protection Risk Analysis 1.The likelihood of Safety and security risks is high as there is always a geopolitical, and economic downturn sometimes. Amal will continue monitoring the situation and adjust the programs based on the change in context. 2.Unequal access to services for women, girls and other at-risk groups like children with disabilities. A high likelihood of low decision-making capacity in the family leads to child marriage and adolescent pregnancy leads to malnutrition. Amal will emphasize gender mainstreaming in the programs and men's engagement for some interventions. 3. Increased risk of GBV due to lack of national sector standards related to protection, rights and GBV risk. Although a medium likelihood Amal can build awareness on GBV with supportive linkages to GVB case management. 4.Lack of participation by vulnerable group in project in age, gender & culture appropriate way. This is low likelihood as Amal plans to engage community at all stage of project cycle management. Amal will initiate program accountability to listen to the community. 5.The need to advocate for and support the deployment of GBV specialists within the nutrition sector. Amal will keep special eyes on the equity of feeding practices, complementary feeding, adolescent (girls) nutrition, vaccination, etc. to ensure the wellbeing of women & girls. Another focus can be given to sensitizing the education sector to equity for nutritional aspects. PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy Amal adopts a zero-tolerance approach towards Sexual Exploitation & Abuse. Support will be provided for the PSEA mechanisms to protect beneficiaries and their caregivers, which will include technical support related to safeguarding and PSEA-available policy review and development with partners. The Tot training will be provided for first-line workers, assigning field focal points with partners in addition to Amal field and national focal points, raising the awareness of the community in a proper way, including child-friendly material, regular reporting on all related activities and incidents in coordination with other clusters in addition of PSEA network. Child safety concerns are identified and monitored through regular child/beneficiary checklists, which are used by Amal field staff, which are constantly monitored throughout the project lifecycle. Furthermore, Amal has its own safeguarding incident preparedness plan which is understood by all staff and partners and is summarized by the procedures and actions related to proper responding to safeguarding and PSEA incidents in multiple levels and categories, in addition, Amal uses a standard online platform named Ethics Point that ensures the proper and timely response. Amal will ensure this hotline information portal is available to every location to ensure quick complaints and that a third-party investigator will respond to them to maintain unbiased and fair management of the complaints.
