Project Summary


The conflict between the Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF) and the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) has caused tremendous displacement and exacerbated pre-existing humanitarian needs across Sudan. As of end 2023, over 7 million Sudanese families are displaced. In response to identified needs within the Sudan HRP 2024, GOAL proposes to undertake an integrated multi-sector project targeting 337642individuals, including:66178 men, 68879 women, 99267 boys and 103318 girls in North Darfur and South Kor
from Abu Jubayhah, Ar Rashad, Gadeer, Habila & Talawdi localities in South Kordofan State:
The overall goal of the program is to provide timely multi-sectoral life-saving assistance to crisis affected people to reduce mortality & morbidity by providing life-sustaining basic services while mitigating protection risks and responding to protection needs. This program will; provide safe, equitable and dignified access to critical Health (with strong emphasis and integration of reproductive health (RH) services), Nutrition, food security and WASH as well as GBV to people in need of humanitarian assistance in the targeted areas. GOAL has an operational presence, expertise, and experience across the 13 localities in SK and ND targeted and will leverage these in addition to their existing programing to complement and maximize overall program impact. GOAL will provide integrated health and nutrition services with outpatient therapeutic program (OTP) services including the Nutrition Impact and Positive Practice (NIPP) intervention which will directly address the multi-sectoral causes of malnutrition, such as inadequate diet diversity, poor WASH behaviors, and unsafe food preparation and conservation methods from 54 health facilities and 4 stabilization centers.
. This will involve an assessment in all target localities, establishing and maintaining community-based protection networks, and strengthening case identification, referral, and management.
GOAL will continue to support the provision of comprenhensive health & nutrition services in 27 Primary health facilities across South Kordofan (Habila, Abukarshola, Rashad, Dilling, Abujebeha, Gadeer and Talodi localities including support for Stabilization Center in Talodi, Rashad and Habila) and 27 clinics in North Darfur (Kutum, Al Waha, Umbaru, Seref Omra, Kekabiya and El Sireaf localities including support for Stabilization Center in Kutum Hospital), The targeted areas have high numbers of confict-affected households (including newly displaced persons, long-term IDPs, vulnerable host communities, and some returnees) GOAL will conitnue support to rural and hard-to-reach facilities in these Localities.
Services include Reproductive Health, EPI, early warning and outbreak response, support and training for health staff, clinic running costs, provision of drugs, medical supplies and equipment, and support for the Locality Health Department, including the Health Information System, supportive supervision and training and deployment of Rapid Response Teams. GOAL also provides community health through Care Groups, community health promotion, defaulter tracing for RH and OTP services, and the Nutrition Impact and Positive Practice (NIPP) approach to prevent and treat Moderate Acute malnutrition. GOAL will ensure comprehensive care in supported facilities, and GOAL will continue to
closely coordinate through the Ministry of Health and Health and Nutrition Sectors in El Fashir and Kadugli and will also engage in ongoing coordination with other partners in each locality to ensure no overlap. GOAL provides a package of lifesaving primary healthcare services in all supported facilities. PHC services include curative consultation, provision and dispensing of essential drugs, managing injuries, EPI to increase the immunization rates in children and pregnant mothers, nutrition screening for children under 5, OTP for management of SAM children
