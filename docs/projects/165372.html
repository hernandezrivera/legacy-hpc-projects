4 - Needs

After eight years of conflict in Syria, although a considerable reduction of violence observed over the past year, still there are 11.7 million of people in need for various forms of humanitarian assistance by the end of 2018 (2019 HNO-Syria), including 5 million people in acute need. Out of this number of total people in need in Syria, 6.2 million remained internally displaced with 25 percent estimated to be women of reproductive age and 4 percent pregnant are women in need for sustained maternal health services. Risks related to protection may continue in 2020 since still there are tense fighting the regime against the opposition group, turkey against Kurdish forces and as well the tension between Turkey and the regime. In Ar-Raqqa, it has been estimated that 0.22 million people are in acute humanitarian needs while 0.51 are in Deir-ez-Zor. Water, Sanitation and Hygiene promotion has been impacted adversely by this long crises, reference to the humanitarian needs overview, the number of people in need for WASH services is increasing since 2019, in 2019 15.5 million people estimated in need for these services. The water, Sanitation and Hygiene assessment that conducted in 2018 has indicated that around 10 percent garbage is left in public places that might contribute into increasing related diseases such as Leishmaniosis which observed since the beginning of the year, this is beside the poor management of the garbage dumping areas that might increase vectors. Damage of water facilities also has impacted safe water for consumption, for example, in Deir-ez-Zor, only 22 percent of the population are served with piped water. With regards to sanitation facilities, estimated at least 70 percent of sewages are untreated and at least half of the sewage systems are either partially function or non-function. All gaps in services and severe impact mentioned above necessitates a humanitarian intervention.

5 - Implementation of activities

1-Solid waste management: Under the solid waste component, arche nova will improve the environment of the targeted community and increase local council’s capacity. a. Rehabilitation of the landfill. b. sprinklers with pesticides. c. Distribution of bins capacity 100 L. d. Provision Tractor with trailer. e. Provision Uniform and basic tools. f. Stipends and diesel. 2- Water Networks Rehabilitation of water facilities by equipping boreholes and the water distribution network. a. Equipping boreholes b. Replacing/maintaining of water valves. c. Construction of new water pipelines to connect houses with main water network; d. Providing new generator set with fuel. e. Building room for electrical equipment. 3- Sewerage networks Rehabilitation of Sewerage a. Replacing the broken pipes b. Cleaning manholes and storm drains. c. Construction storm drains d. Provision and installation of cast iron covers. e.Provision sewage tanker trailer.With aim of ensuring a coordinated intervention without unnecessary duplication of efforts and as well increasing the impact, Arche nova will coordinate closely with sector represented by the WWG IN NES, coordination will include and not limited to attend regular coordination meetings as possible, planning and reporting using identified reporting channels. Coordination also will extend to sister agencies working in the same operational areas for the sake of learning and exchanging experiences that improved the quality of intervention.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Safe access to public water and sanitation facilities, especially girls, women and children: this risk is mostly high in camps settings, females and children could be exposed to GBV violence while accessing water and sanitation public facilities. The organization will ensure locating the facilities in secure and accessible locations, also will continue coordinating with the existing community structures like camp management committees on reporting cases in a timely manner and seeking mitigation measures for further protection. Also emphasis will be put on ensuring communities, especially women, girls and children have sufficient awareness to keep safe. Health issues Risk linked to consumption of untreated water collected from water sources: collecting of untreated water is common in the operational areas and that’s contributes into spreading of water-borne diseases among communities. The organization is committed to rehabilitate water sources including installation or rehabilitation of chlorine units. Also authority water operators will be provided trainings on chlorination within the planned operation and maintenance trainings. Linking the authorities responsible for operation of water systems to the WASH working group is another measure to ensure gaining more knowledge and as well accessing supply of spare parts and that’s contributes into sustainability of water quality monitoring aspect. Risk related to use of harmful pesticides during spraying households against mosquitos, flies and Leishmaniosis: spraying community households against disease transmitting agents such as mosquitos, flies and Leishmaniosis is important to prevent communities from related diseases. Using of untested and approved pesticides might bring a round another health burden, therefore, the organization will ensure using approved types of pesticides from authority and cluster sides, expert people will engaged in implementing spray activity to ensure no harm caused.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1- Fighting between opposition groups: AN will suspend activities until fighting stops and the situation becomes clearer. AN staff will hibernate until further notice. 2- Government forces shelling: AN will freeze all activities immediately. All staff will hibernate at the safest and nearest location until further notice. 3- Local hostility towards INGO’s: AN will immediately suspend activities and hold discussions with local actors and authorities on the ground. 4- the Specific targeting of Arche nova staff members: AN will suspend activities immediately and discuss the situation within the organization and with the local actors on the ground.
