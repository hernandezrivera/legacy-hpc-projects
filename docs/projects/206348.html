4.1.1. Provea brevemente información al respecto - máximo 1.000 carácteres

Geneva Call seeks to integrate a gender perspective in all its activities and at all stages of its project cycle, in accordance with International Humanitarian Standards on best practice gender policy, and in collaboration with its partner organizations and donors. Geneva Call is committed to gender-sensitive programming integrating gender analysis; and to consulting, involving and engaging women and men wherever possible in its activities and promoting their full participation. Furthermore, Geneva Call seeks to achieve gender balance in its operational activities as well as its internal structure. Girls and boys are affected both similarly and uniquely by armed conflict. For example, boys may be more susceptible to use in hostilities, and girls may be more susceptible to sexual violence. Programmatic responses consider the special vulnerabilities of children as children as well as the special vulnerabilities of girls and boys.
