Project Summary

This proposed intervention will be implemented in Tilaberi region maily regions mainly the communes of Inates, Banibangu, and Tiloa. Interventions will be around child protection and gender based violance in emergencies and will focus on (1) Reinforcing the community child protection mechanisms and system as a way tp prevent and respond to abuses against children both girls and boys , (2) provide pyschosocial support to children affected by the conflict (3) contribute to prevention and response to separated and unaccompanied children and (4) reinforcing positive foster paring skills

Needs

By last October 2018, a total of 75 565 individuals were displaced in Tilaberi Region due to the conflicts of insurgents and most of them are young girls, adolescents and women. According several protection reports and Plan needs assessment conducted in 2 communes out of the 3 affected are in need of protection services. Children and young adolescents mainly the girls are exposed to sexual abuses, exploitation and other forms of negative practices. The yougn boys are exposed to armed groups utilisation and rectrutment. In these areas, though the situation is deteriorating day by day, less humanitarian actors have provided services and this is making the IDPs population more vulnerable. Schools are closed in the areas of conflicts, no safe spaces and protection infrastructures. Affected boys and girls are exposed to risks of pyschosocial distress and community based structures not working or ineffective to prevent and respond to these issues.
