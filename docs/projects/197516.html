Project Summary

ForAfrika will provide food assistance to IDPs and host community in Montepuez (20,581people) and Meluco(5,685 people) as part of the HRP 2023. ForAfrika targets to provide food assistance to IDPs in fleeing from attacks in districts surrounding Montepuez, such as Ancuabe, Nangage and Namuno. Targetting will done on collaboration with the food security cluster to ensure that the most vulnerable are served and that duplication of efforts is avoided. The composition of the food basket will also be defined in consultation with the food security cluster. The food baskets will be provided to new arrival IDP HHs as a one-off distributions.
The main activities planned in the interventions are:
a. Beneficiary Identification and Selection through community consultation
b. Training of project staff and key stakeholders on the project activities
c. Coordination meetings with government officials
d. Community mobilization and sensitization on project activities.
e. Procurement and distribution of food baskets
f. Monitoring and reporting
g. Complementary livelihood activities such home gardens

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project will conduct security risk assessments to identify potential risks. Social and Environmental Responsibilty assessment will be contacted to assess any possible negative impact of the project. Through community consultations, possible sources of conflict and risk will be identified.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

Beneficiary identification and selection will be done in consultation with community leaders and target community to identify the most vulnerable persons. This will done in order to make sure that no one is left out. The project will design a vulnerability assessment tool.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Persons with disabilities will be identified through consultation with affected population and community leaders. These will be prioritized to getting assistance. Distribution plans will be design to cater for the needs of these, making sure that they easily get access to their entitlements.
