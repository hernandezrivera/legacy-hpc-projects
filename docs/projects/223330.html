1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The proposal aims to respond to the nutritional needs of a total of 48,000 people identified by the Nutrition Cluster in the districts of Ibo and Quissanga. The actions include screening and treatment of malnutrition in children under 5 and LGM, preventive actions as an activity of the Nutrition Intervention Package and training of community agents.This project aims to implement and improve approaches to preventing malnutrition in children under 5, pregnant and lactating women and to improve nutritional health status, taking into account food accessibility and availability in the implementing communities, promoting breastfeeding and good feeding practices for pregnant and lactating women and children. The approach is based on strengthening the capacity of community actors (APEs, activists, local leaders, health technicians, health committee members, etc.) to identify cases of acute malnutrition among children under 5 and pregnant and lactating women to be addressed in the community, outpatient (without clinical complications) and inpatient (with clinical complications), as well as to disseminate the key messages of the IYCF. The reference policies for implementing the project will be the NRP (Nutritional Rehabilitation Programme with a focus on CDT (Treatment of Malnutrition in the Community)), and the NIP (Nutritional Intervention Package). The activities will take place in the communities where the project will be implemented. As for the key activities, training aimed at community actors to identify and monitor and treat children under 5 with malnutrition in the community, as this activity will be carried out in close coordination with the local health authorities and in accordance with the MoH guidelines. Its main objective is to reinforce nutritional screening and identification of cases of malnutrition, ensuring quality follow-up treatment, as well as the prevention of chronic malnutrition through NIP interventions and other general health sensitisation, taking into account the involvement and coordination with District services, local leadership, community actors, creating a close link with field officers. They will also be able to provide and support the services of Integrated Mobile Brigades. AVSI will draw on its long experience to support the implementation of interventions and offer these services to the local community population. This approach aims to improve and bring basic health services to the community by sharing the work with the local authorities, in order to support them and allow them to continue after the end of the project. Logistical support will also be provided for quarterly discussions and debriefings at district level to ensure quality data triangulation. At the end of the project, the trained APSs and activists will continue to support their communities and can be supported by the health services at district level

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Restore and expand the capacity for life-saving nutritional interventions through health units and community outreach activities in communities far from the health unit.In coordination with SDSMAS, provide technical support for CDT and NIP activities, ensuring the availability of materials and supplies for their execution;Provide logistical and technical support for integrated Mobile Brigades, depending on the needs of the implementing districts in communities far from the health unit, covering the general population of the community;Strengthen the capacity of CHWs (APEs and ACS) to treat acute malnutrition at community level;Implement active case finding and referral of malnourished children and monitor nutritional status through MUAC tests;Strengthen CHWs (APEs and ACS) in the provision of micronutrient supplementation at community level;Promote breastfeeding and infant feeding practices, and general infant and young child feeding (IYCF) best practice messages for carers of children under two, either through awareness sessions and cooking demonstrations in communities;Training CHWs (APEs and ACS) in thematic areas related to IYCF and HIV to provide individual sessions at the household level.Providing support in carrying out data discussions at district level to ensure quality in the triangulation of information;

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project will ensure protection mainstreaming process in order to esure DO NO HARM approach, promoting meaningful access, safety and dignigy in all actions. In order to guarantee this principles all the staff will be trained on the principles and procedures in place and will sign the reference documents such as the Internal Code of Conduct, the Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse Policy (PSEA) and the AVSI Children Safeguarding Policy documents as well as the AVSI lead protection mainstreaming checklists. In order to avoid conflicts between IDPs (new or extended) and host communities, the specific selection criteria (nutritional status) will be well explained and widespread to local communities, institutions, in order to ensure the use of the principles of impartiality and neutrality and at the same time guartantee that specific need of U 5 years old Protection monitoring will be garuatee through routinely monitoring access to services by women, girls, boys and men through spot checks and discussions with communities, and promptly address the obstacles to equal access

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

This project will follow AVSI Foundation SOP described in the AVSI FOUNDATION ACCOUNTABILITY POLICY (DIGE 11/2020) on ‘feedback and accountability mechanism’. All staff are always oriented and re-oriented on the need to seek the views of communities concerning program implementation. Beneficiaries and the population at large is also made aware of the forms available to provide suggestions and complaints. AVSI Foundation will carry out household surveys and ad hoc studies to shed lights on the condition of special and vulnerable groups. The results will be presented and validated with all communities in the camp and host communities, local government, as a means to establish a productive fact-based conversation with affected populations. In AVSI’s experience, this has proven time and again to be particularly productive, as not only people’ concerns and priorities are taken into account, but, more importantly, solutions in line with the specific contexts and people capable to support such initiatives are identified. Example of these practices could be found at: https://assessments.hpc.tools/assessment/c891c32a-7c60-4aa9-bf6d-a8b48a84730a https://reliefweb.int/report/south-sudan/multi-sector-household-survey-rumbek-north-county-lakes-state-june-2021 The capillary and continuous presence that AVSI has in the communities targeted by the intervention (AVSI has more than 80% of field based staff) ensures that such fruitful cooperation with project beneficiaries continues throughout the project’s implementation.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

In the present project proposal screening, surveillance and participation of people with disabilities have been included as key activities and indicators. During their implementation, the detection of people with disabilities will be reported (disaggregated by gender and age) and given special attention and priority to inclusion as beneficiaires, with a focus on HIV. AVSI Foundation will, when possible, organize specific studies on the inclusion and conditions of persons living with a disability and will utilize such studies to devise inclusive strategies on round table with PwD as well as local communities. Examples of such work in similar context can be found at: https://assessments.hpc.tools/assessment/e3f17126-d1f5-4a78-80a4-99dea7c46e69. AVSI Foundation will ensure the implementation of sensitization sessions throughout its activities to ensure that these people are supported within the community and given special attention when coordinating and planning activities with local SDSMAS and partners.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

AVSI aims to promote a sustainable and healthy environment in order to improve the wellbeing of the community. Given the experience of AVSI Mozambique in energy effciency-related projects, method to save charcoal and wood during cooking demonstration will be also promoted so that to sensitize the target population on preventing deforestation that in turn impact positively on family finances by saving wood and charcoal. In addition of that, it will be prioritazed the use of local materials in order to adopt an environment-friendly approach by trying to increase awareness in the affected communities. In order to guarantee protection Environment, AVSI can rely to an internal Policy on environment on May 11th 2020.
