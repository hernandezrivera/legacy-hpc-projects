Project Summary

The project helps to find quick and urgent solutions to the problem of (IDPs, returnees, affected persons, women with families and university graduates). Those people who have not found employment opportunities and whose livelihoods have been cut off in Aleppo due to the current crisis. By creating 98 income generating jobs through this project by implement vegetable and fruit conversion industries to be suitable for non-seasonal uses especially because of the high prices of foodstuffs.
The place of the project / Aleppo / one of the most suitable places to implement this type of projects generating jobs, where there are thousands of families returning to Aleppo, and the chances of finding work are often hard to existent. The proportion of women who support their families in the city and its rural areas is very high; The Aleppo countryside is famous for its multi agricultural products.
Such projects create a chance of hope for the affected and returnees within the city and the surrounding villages in the possibility of access to income generating jobs, and to benefit from the investment of these products. Also training of many women who were unable to complete their studies and became dependents of their families to have a good job with a good salary. The society also ensures the continuity of the project's investment as a food conversion plant through a long-term contract to ensure the use of infrastructure equipment and to ensure sustainability. Considering that, the storage of food is the profession of all women in the community and returnees and all of them have sufficient experience to do this work. A rustic restaurant serving handmade products will be opened. The restaurant provides a suitable environment for products to be tested and it will be a direct selling point. It also provides job opportunities for different age groups from all segments of the youth, men and women where work requires only simple experiences.
The marketing of the products of this project is ensured through an integrated marketing plan that includes market study with the possibility of opening a shop for the products of the project at a later stage, in addition to local marketing in shops, shopping malls and restaurants. for market these products manually converted by the ladies in the city to ensure that the production is in accordance with the needs of markets and the task of sales representatives.
Following the sale of the products, the capital and profit earned will be recycled to cover the future operation costs of the project, ensure the maintenance of updated employment opportunities and increase the number of beneficiaries of the project services.
There will also be a distribution of kits containing transforming industries tools and the project will ensure the provision of raw materials for domestic manufacturing industries within their homes and the marketing of their products.

Needs

The observer notes the repercussions of the current crisis in Syria, which negatively affected the various aspects of social, economic, health and psychological life.Any programs and projects to respond to the humanitarian challenges of this crisis must first focus on the basic needs that have become lost or threatened by many of the affected population, perhaps the need for an appropriate source of income is a top priority for many stakeholders, such as international organizations and NGOs,The statistics indicate that a large percentage of citizens (40% to 50%) lost their sources of income. in addition, to the large rise in the average prices of basic goods and services, especially food and logistics.It is estimated that the rise in the average food supply reached 700% between 2011 and 2019, which led to a very large increase in the number of job seekers. In contrast, there is also a significant decline in the number of jobs offered by economic activity.This is seen clearly in the city of Aleppo, where the proportion of women who have to support their families in this city and the surrounding villages increased because of the crisis and its negative consequences on the population. In addition to the displacement of hundreds of families returning and coming from the countryside. The problem of unemployment is also evident in young university graduates because of the scarcity of jobs and even their absence, These groups and families are primarily dependent on the services provided by international organizations and NGOs.Therefore, one of the most important challenges at the current stage is to restore the livelihoods of these people through such projects, which support the local economy and are based on securing permanent and income-generating jobs for those marginalized and affected by the crisis.

Implementation of activities

1. Establishment of a factory for the manufacture and storage of foodstuffs to provide (65) job opportunities for people who are cut off from their livelihoods:1.1. Establishment of a food production and storage plant: The location of the plant is located in the densely populated city of Aleppo and the largest concentration of displaced persons, returnees and people affected by the crisis, which lost a large part of its people their livelihoods as a result of the crisis, benefiting from the existence of a large diversity of agricultural products and the presence of good experience of a large number of women to provision and manufacture food products.This factory is equipped with the basic equipment for the work and the industrial security considerations are taken into account in the processing of this plant so that it is provided with the required means of protection and safety (such as fire extinguishers and others).1.2. Attracting, operating and training a number of displaced persons and those their livelihoods affected :Where the targeting criteria are determined in a precise and clear manner by agreement between all partners and a committee is keen to oversee the commitment to implement these standards.Accordingly, 83 people (taking into account age, gender and social diversity) will be attracted to those who supervise the recipes and mixes in the manufacture of food. They are experts in this field to ensure high quality, they also training at least 50 people to master the profession.2. Provide emergency solutions to help alleviate the suffering of the population in obtaining food and stocked food at reasonable prices:2.1. Insurance of orders for ready-made foodstuffs from jams, dryers and manually manufactured foodstuffs:Raw materials are purchased from the local market through the implementing partner (Syrian Society for Social Development). These raw materials are stored in suitable warehouses (all raw materials are available). The manufacture of final products is guaranteed according to the quality standards approved and suitable for market demands. Specialized experts carry out all stages of manufacturing under continuous supervision.2.2. Distribution and marketing of project products, considering humanitarian considerations:The factory products of food preserves, converted food and dryers are the main commodities and distributable within the local market and restaurants in Aleppo,The marketing team of the project is also working on the implementation of an integrated marketing plan including the study of food markets and prices of these materials to ensure that the production is according to the needs of markets the mission is carried out by sales representatives.In addition to the possibility of opening a shop for the products of the project at a later stage.Humanitarian considerations will also be considered as part of the plant's products per month for poor families living near the project.3. Establishment of a rustic restaurant:3.1. Where the place will be chosen to be in densely populated areas and ensure the diversity of all strata of society in Aleppo, thus helping to increase social cohesion and population relations in Aleppo.The restaurant will also provide 15 jobs that are not limited to a particular population, so they can include a large segment of the community who do not have enough experience to work in other areas because of current circumstances and scarce jobs.4. Distribution of household industry toolkits:4.1. In some cases, some women have to be the dependents of their families who can not join any work outside their homes.The project will be distributing household toolkits to them. The project will ensure that raw materials are provided for them to carry out some domestic industries that do not require large equipment. The role of the project will also be to store and market household materials.

Protection Risk Analysis

1. In some cases, some women have to be the dependents of their families who cannot join any work outside their homes.2. Work on the project depends mainly on women who have roles in the housework and therefore there is a possibility of non-compliance within the long working hours.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1. In order to ensure that women who can not work outside their homes benefit, household equipment will be distributed so that the project provides them with a job at home.2. To ensure that women comply with working hours and not to interfere with their housework, eight hours a day for work will be determined.
