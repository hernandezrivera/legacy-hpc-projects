Project Summary

Hope Revival Organization (HRO), aims to continue providing GBV services in the WGSS continuing and established in Idleb(Harim-Jisr Ash-Darkoush Maaret Tamsrin )Aleppo (azaz-Afrin)Ar-Raqqa(Tell Abiad-Suluk)and Al-Hasakeh(Ras Al Ain District),to ensure that a greater number of Syrians have access to information and GBV services in their community,the total number of WGSSs proposed in this project is 9 and will be located in: Darkosh- Jisr-Ash-Shugur- Qourqeena- Salqin- Armanaz - Kafr Jales- Afrin- Azaz-Tell abiad, four WGSSs are currently operating in the following areas, but the fund will end soon:Darkosh and Jisr-Ash-Shugur, Kafr Jales (June 2020)- Afrin (March 2020)two WGSSs are currently run by HRO and funded by UNFPA until the end of 2020. They are located in Bdama.
HRO is currently providing GBV case management inside health facilities run by SAMS and funded by DRL. Locations: Kafr Deryan, Afrin, Idleb city, Hazano. And Maretmasrin.
HRO aim to Promote GBV risk mitigation, Through this project also, HRO And continuing to provide GBV response services with a particular focus on most at risk groups (for ex. adolescent girls, divorced and widowed women and girls, women and girls with disabilities and older women ,the WGSS will Continue Providing lifesaving activities including case management, referrals, PSS for survivors focusing on vulnerable groups as in divorced, widows, women and girls with disabilities as well as for men & boys especially with sexual violence, conducting Training of GBV actors to respond to GBV through a survivor-centred and inclusive approach( social workers, facilitators; lawyers, healthcare workers; case workers) theTrainings may include( Case management- GBV basic concepts-identification and referral..), Life Skills Activities for young girls through the program (Girls dreams) with concern to the consequences of early marriage and what are the alternatives that those girls could have instead ,Free space for women and girls Focus on (adolescent girls, divorced and widowed women and girls,women and girls with disabilities and older women) to socialize and re-build their social networks; enjoy non-stigmatizing Women and girls in WGSSs 5-young mother club program ,Vocational Training:Including first aid,hairdressing, computer skills,English and Turkish languages, and sewing, Awareness raising structured by using GBV Tool kits inside center and through outreach to address negative attitudes and believes that foster harmful social norms ,GBV emergency response plan will be activated during new displacement, evacuation 8-provide Information/sensitization sessions about GBV related services for one time and continuing to distribution GBV IEC materials ,Menstrual Hygiene Management MHM for raising awareness of women and adolescent girls about correcting the traditional beliefs and myths about menstrual hygiene, training of Humanitarian actors (non-specialist) to reduce risks of GBV and refer to specialised services and safety audit:Outreach teams will conduct participatory safety audits in camps/communities for assess the GBV risks in different sectors,There will be10 WGSS each WGSS consists of the following staff :6 facilitators 2 case managers,1 Technical Field Supervisor1 Data Entry1 Cleaner1 Child Attendent1 Guard1 M&E Assistant1 logistic1 Finance Officer 1center manager

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

1-Risk: The lack of quality of services, especially referral, the survivors expose to more harm while implementing the referral, stigmatizing the survivors and putting them at risk, Mitigation: CM is provided by case managers who trained to implement safe referrals, training the staff on the GBV SC referral pathways and SOPs 2-The case management service providers are exposed to stress and secondary trauma as a result of dealing with highly sensitive humanitarian casesMitigation: Including a self-care strategy within the program, especially for case management staff.3-The lack of case managers' ability to manage information on cases of GBV leads to violations of case confidentiality. Mitigation: Train the case management team on the information management system and determining data access only to persons who need to be reached, 4-The community didn't accept the messages of GBV, thus creating a state of resistance or a backlash against the program, Mitigation: Community participation from project design to closure, conduct consultations with beneficiaries (WMGB) and adapt messages to be more context-appropriate. 5- Causing more harm and stigmatization for PWD and elderly women because of the place doesn't suitable for their needs, Mitigation: adapt the space to be more able to receive and participate with PWD, by conducting consultations with these people and training the team on integrating of PWD into GBV programs, 6- Expose girls to the risk of sexual harassment and violence while coming to attend activities, Mitigation : Providing transportation for girls and the possibility of escorting mothers to girls,7-Commit violations of sexual content by the team against the beneficiaries during the implementation of the program, leads to harm to the beneficiaries and the loss of their confidence in the program, mitigation: Staff sign Code of Conduct of PSEA and attend relevant training, provide a complaints’ mechanism for SEA and train the beneficiaries on it

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumption 1:The host community will welcome our intervention as the project staff is from the host community, and the organization is well known there. Risk: The local authorities decide to prevent us from implementing the project or part of it. Therefore, we will not be able to access high-risk GBV cases and we willnot be able to follow up on the GBV survivors’ cases whose files are open. Mitigation: We will coordinate with the technical local councils and raise their awareness on the importance of the project and the impact of it, and will coordinate also with the Access Group.Assumption 2: Persons with disabilities will be able to attend the session and participate in the activities, Risk: lack of access for persons with disabilities, or access with being exposed to more harm due to the fact that the spaces are not appropriate for their needs. Mitigation: provide the needed transportation and tools to ease the persons with disabilities access to our WGSSs, and training the team on how to include PWD in the provided protection activities. Assumption 3: GBV survivors will have safe and easy access to our GBV specialized services, Risk: Stigma toward the GBV survivors. Mitigation: The case management room is located within WGSSs, easy to reach by Survivors, and enjoys privacy.Assumption 4 :The WGSSs are located in relatively safe areas, Risk: These places being bombed and subjected to attack and displacement, thus, closing a center is recommended, Mitigation: to set up Plan B in advance, follow the steps for ethical closer of program and ensure continuous updating of the plan to close the program as needed.
