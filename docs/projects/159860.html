Project Summary


Provision of Integrated Emergency education and Child Protection for Drought-affected IDPs and vulnerable host community school- age boys and girls in Bari,and Sool and Regions is a 12 month integrated Basic Education and Child Protection with life-saving project. The primary objective of the project is to retain and support 1,972 (1,080 boys and 892 girls) drought and displacement-affected
Children by providing essential and life-saving assistance. The project will target 8 schools (3 IDP schools, 2 rural schools and 3 community schools) in Bosaso, Qardo, Qandala, Bocame, Talex, and Xudun Districts
The proposed project intends to improve the capacity of existing public schools to cater to the educational needs of drought-affected children as well as vulnerable children from host communities. Through provision of teaching and learning material, 1,972 (1,080 boys and 892 girls) learners will be targeted. TASS will provide School feeding through take-home food (5Kgs white Flour and 1 litre of oil) for each of 1,972 (1,080 boys and 892 girls) targeted students. In addition 48 teachers will be supported through monthly incentives as well as trained on child centered teaching methodologies for emergency settings. 40 CECs members from the 8 targeted schools will also be trained on overall school management with a focus on (psycho-social support for learners, child rights and protection in schools clean and safe drinking water and school feeding) management of the target schools. To protect children from AWD/ Cholera and any other communicable diseases TASS will install water storage tank in all the targeted schools to provide safe and clean drinking water for the pupils.

Furthermore TASS will train 40 teachers (5 per school) the training will include training the teachers on psycho-social support for learners, child rights and protection in schools as per the minimum standards for child protection in Humanitarian action. 500 adolescent girls will be provided with sanitary kits to maintain hygienic condition as well as to attend school without any disruption. TASS will provide sanitary kits comprised of 8 packs of 10 pads and 6 pairs of underwear to each enrolled adolescent girl to last 8 months duration during the academic year including vacation period. The proposed project will also construct PVC water Tanks for the 8 targeted schools. In addition the project will appoint Child Protection focal points at school (1 male/1female) with additional PSS First Aid training and knowledge of referral pathways to specific child protection services hence TASS will Establish community dialogues to raise awareness on mitigating risks and all kinds of child abuse practices to enhance children’s access and retention in education

Project Activities

EDUCATION CLUSTER: Activity 1.1.1: Learning and Teaching Material for students: TLM will be distributed to 1772 students in accordance with Education Cluster Minimum Standards Activity 1.1.2: School feeding: 1,972 students will benefit from take-home food (5Kgs White flour and 1 liter of cooking oil. Activity 1.1.3: Water distribution in schools: Water will be supplied to 22 schools through water trucking at a rate of 3 liters/day/student for a period of 8 months during school days and excluding the vacation period furthermore TASS, will form and train school clubs: 20 members per school (10 girls and 10 boys), to improve and sustain the participation of learners in hygiene and sanitation promotion activities in school. The strategy of adapting the child-to –child approach has been proven to be effective in spreading and attracting buy-in of knowledge, information, practices and skills among children, as they easily emulate each other. Activity 1.1.4: Water and sanitation infrastructure construction/refurbishment: in order to improved access to safe drinking water and personal hygiene practice among students in the drought affected schools, TASS will provide 8 PVC tanks (2,000 liter capacity each) for the use of drinking and hygiene. Activity: 1.1.5: Community Education Committee training: TASS aims to train selected 40 (20 male, 20 female) Community Education Committees (CECs) of the 8 targeted schools for three days on managing education in crises to ensure that learning is not interrupted during conflict or in times of crises, including child rights, child protection, water management, hygiene and sanitation and psycho-social support. Activity 1.1.6: Teacher training pedagogy: TASS in collaboration with the local ministry of education will train 40 school teachers for three days on teachers preparation,(lesson planning/scheming/record keeping) classroom instruction and also train them on psycho-social support for learners, child rights and protection in schools as per the minimum standards for child protection in Humanitarian action. Activity 1.1.7: appoint Child Protection focal points at school (1 male/1female) with additional PSS First Aid training and knowledge of referral pathways to specific child protection services 1.1.8: provision of sanitary kits to adolescent girls. 1.1.9: Establish community dialogues to raise awareness on mitigating risks and all kinds of child abuse practices to enhance children’s access and retention in education. 1.1.10: Facilitate structured gender sensitive recreational and PSS activities at the schools for both boys and girls
