Project Summary

• To ensure the quick movement of life-saving humanitarian cargo to impacted communities and project implementation sites for the humanitarian community responding to the Cyclone Idai crisis in Mozambique.
• To support the overall humanitarian logistics response by augmenting logistics capacity through enhanced coordination and information sharing mechanisms.
