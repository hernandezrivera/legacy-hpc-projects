Project Summary

The enormous blast that ripped through Beirut has disrupted families’ access to basic water and sanitation services. Initial assessments done by Anera’s shelter team reveal that households affected with mild to moderate damages in the areas of Burj Hammoud, Medawar, and Rmeil have suffered from damages to their water tanks, which are connected to the primary domestic water source. These families have been already suffering from economic challenges, due to the 89% depreciation of the LBP since October 2019 and the increased unemployment rates due to this economic crisis as well as COVID-19 restrictions. This puts a high burden on the families who cannot set priorities related to paying for house repairs, ensuring access to safe water, and buying hygiene supplies along with baby needs. The disruption of access to clean domestic water, poor hygiene practices, and inadequate sanitation play an important role in increasing the risk of transmission of COVID-19 and other communicable diseases, especially that it is difficult to abide by physical distancing after the displacement of families.
Anera has followed a holistic approach in responding to identified priority areas, in which it has initiated its response through planning a short term level response which includes addressing the WASH needs of families. This response addresses access to safe water through installation of water tanks, to the distribution of hygiene kits, and planning hygiene and COVID-19 specific promotional campaigns.
Through the “Provision of Hygiene Kits and Water Tanks to affected families in the areas of Burj Hammoud, Medawar, and Rmeil” relief project, Anera will deliver a 3-month, integrated WASH program. A total number of 500 households out of 4,000 shelters assessed by Anera’s shelter teams, will be provided with water tanks connected to the networks of water supply, and a total of 17,000 individuals (5,000 HH) and 3,000 infants from the identified HHs will benefit from the distribution of hygiene kits. The hygiene kits comply with the set standards for the “post blast hygiene kit”. This intervention is particularly essential for maintaining the dignity of affected families, particularly women and girls, through ensuring access to safe domestic water, and hygiene supplies. To ensure the sustainability and the effectiveness of the planned activities, Anera’s WASH team will ensure delivering door-to-door hygiene promotion, and COVID-19 awareness on topics related to handwashing and environmental hygiene. In light of the ongoing economic crisis, Anera will provide 32 youth with livelihood opportunities through the project’s Cash for Work component. The youths will support in on the ground distribution and awareness activities.
Anera’s planned WASH activities are guided by the SPHERE standards, will be guided by UN interagency planned meetings, and will be implemented while taking into consideration all the needed COVID precaution measures. In every stage of the project, Anera will provide workers and laborers with the needed PPE. Anera’s monitoring and evaluation team will be highly involved in preparing assessments, analyzing data, and ensuring the effective delivery of planned activities.

1 - Needs

The explosion that hit Beirut has come at a time where Lebanon faces multiple and interlocking crisis. The economic crisis, with the currency inflation, has compounded the needs among the Lebanese community. Families affected by the blast have been pushed to the brink. The families in the areas of Burj Hammoud, Medawar, and Rmeil, whose houses have been affected at different scales, cannot afford to pay for repairs due to the inflation of prices. Some suffer from substantial structural damage, and others from minor to moderate damages including destructions in windows, and doors, along with damaged water tanks which are connected to the main domestic water supply source. The water tanks are used to collect and store water for later use and timely access. The damage in the water tanks, which disrupted the access of the families to the main source of domestic water supply, forces the families to rely on short term alternatives which include buying water gallons, and other hygienic necessities to satisfy their daily needs. This exacerbates the economic burden on the families, who cannot afford to constantly cover the high cost of supplies which are far from enough in covering the short and long term needs. Families with children under 5 years are disproportionally impacted with the current conditions mainly because they cannot afford buying the children necessities, along with the hygiene supplies in these difficult times.The disruption of access to clean water, sanitation, and hygiene plays an important role in increasing the risk of transmission of COVID-19 and other communicable diseases. The number of COVID-19 cases have dramatically increased in a period of 1 month from 84 per day on July 20 to 605 cases per day on August 20. This sharp increase in number of cases is expected after the blast, especially because it is difficult to maintain social distancing practices with these displaced persons living with families and friends. The intersection between COVID-19, the emergency situation, and the economic crisis have left the affected desperate families in a high vulnerability state. Accordingly, the specified needs among families affected by the Beirut blast in the areas of Medawar, Burj Hammoud, and Rmeil include: a) The need of sustainable access to water sanitation and hygiene. b) The need for continued awareness on COVID-19.c) The need for improved personal hygiene practices aimed at reducing the incidence and risk of widespread transmission of infectious diseases. Anera has planned to respond to the needs in the WASH sector in coordination with the shelter team which has been assessing the mild and moderate damages in affected houses. The WASH assessment will be done by the experienced WASH team in the areas of Burj Hammoud, Rmeil, and Medawar to: 1) identify the damages in the water tanks and access to water, 2) assess the hygiene practices at the household level, 3) assess the awareness level of families on COVID-19 transmission, 4) and identify the vulnerable groups including girls and children. Accordingly, context specific hygiene promotion and COVID transmission messages will be planned by the experienced WASH team, which has been successfully delivering RCCE activities in response to COVID-19, and community based hygiene promotion in different contexts. Baby and hygiene kits will comply with the standards of “post Beirut blast hygiene kit” and will include a various range of hygiene and sanitary supplies, including dental hygiene supplies, cleaning and disinfection agents, and necessary PPEs. As for the baby kit , it will include thermometer, clothing, baby detergents, and diapers.

2 - Activities

Activity 1: Procurement and Logistics The efficiency of response to the WASH needs of the damaged households is highly important to embark the path to recovery and access to domestic water. Anera ensures the application of adequate frameworks to procure the needed material and recruit the teams in an efficient manner. Anera’s access to a high number of suppliers, and its previous experience in responding to the needs of communities in Beirut, South, and North Lebanon through the procurement of more than 10,000 hygiene kits along with PPEs under projects funded by UN-OCHA and UNHCR in response to the COVID-19 pandemic, keeps it ready to efficiently finalize the procurement procedures. Activity two: Recruitment of Youths and Consultants- Anera will choose the 32 youths based on selection criteria in cash-for-work activities related to this project. Gender mainstreaming is taken in consideration in the selection of youths, and every attempt at gender parity among youth is made. Anera’s emergency unit has been in contact with contractors assessing shelters and providing adequate supplies few days after the occurrence of the blast. This will highly support the timely response to the family’s needs. - Refresher trainings will be provided to all the field staff on topics related to data collection, PPE and field precaution measures, code of conduct, and PSEA, especially in a COVID-19 environment. Activity 3: Assessment - The monitoring and evaluation team has prepared concise and clear assessment tools target and identify damages at the households and is well prepared to create a WASH assessment form in coordination with the WASH team and consultants to identify the exact WASH needs of families. The assessment will be done comprehensively with the shelter team, which has already identified the zones, and will selected the optimum modality of work for data collection. This will take place over a one-week duration with the distribution of 10 volunteers per area. The monitoring and evaluation team will also plan the needed tools to ensure delivery of services. Activity 4: Distribution of Hygiene Kits and Hygiene Promotion - Priority will be given to women-headed families and families with children. Hygiene kits will be distributed over two months to cover the needs of each family, with each kit intended to last 2.5 to 3 weeks. Anera will ensure covering the needs of the families over a scope of 2 months throughout the project implementation to ensure complying with the “Do No Harm” principle to beneficiaries. Thus, a total of 10,000 hygiene kits will be distributed to 5,000 families over the 3 selected areas. Each family will be receiving 2 kits over a 2 months’ duration. The trained youths will be mobilized- 10 youths per area- over a 16 days’ duration/ month to cover reaching out to the identified 5,000 families. The efficient implementation of the distribution will be covered by 3 field coordinators distributed over the 3 areas. - From the 5,000 Identified families with children, 3,000 will also receive baby kits. Every kit serves the needs of a 1-month duration, thus the identified families with children will receive 2 kits over the project duration. - The hygiene campaign will focus on COVID-19 precaution measures and personal hygiene messages implemented as follows during a two- times door to door visit while receiving the hygiene kits: o 5,000 individuals will receive awareness on COVID-19 and personal hygiene through door-to-door visits (key messages); in addition to printed flyers on COVID-19 precaution measures. o 3000 children will be receiving awareness on personal hygiene/ handwashing through printed coloring booklets.Activity 5: Installation of Water tanks - Assessment: Held by Anera’s shelter emergency team, and will be referred to the WASH team. - BoQs: 10 qualified preselected contractors will prepare them - Installation: By the contractors; monitored by 2 field supervisors over 2 months
