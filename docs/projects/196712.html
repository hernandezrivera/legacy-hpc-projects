Project Summary

Open a carpentry workshop in the area for the following reasons :
- The furniture is considered one of the most crafts that the people of Eastern Ghouta relied on to earn their livelihood in the past.
- the great destruction on workshops of artisan in the area
-the presence of expert manpower and creative craftsmen, with no suitable place for their expertise.
- the inability to work far from their area due to the high cost and scarcity of public transportation and the high cost of the fuel.
Most of the region's population lives below the poverty line and in disastrous situation in terms of food security , education and health care.
Most of the children suffer from malnutrition due to the high cost of living and the unemployment of the parents.

3 - Needs

Before the start of the crisis in the region, Eastern Ghouta, specifically the city of Saqba, was known as the most prominent furniture factory in Syria, as it had carpentry workshops that used to send its products to Damascus and the entire countryIt was visited by merchants from Lebanon, Iraq, Jordan, the Gulf, and Cyprus, as the furniture industry and its accompanying professions such as carving, dyeing and upholstery extended from Saqba towards the neighboring towns, Hamouriyeh, Irbin, Kafr Batna, and Hazza, for thousands of people to work in it, and the street became Extending from Kafr Batna Square to Hamouria, with a length of 2.5 km, it was a large market for displaying furniture, with more than 400 showrooms.due to the crisis many carpenters to closed their workshops, with the lack of all materials, the electricity shortage and the high prices, some residents, including the owners of carpentry workshops, burned their furniture or sold them to keep warm in winterit is recommended to open a carpentry workshop in this area for the reasons:The furniture industry is the main profession of the people of EG for livelihoodThe presence of expert manpower and creative craftsmen, with no suitable place for their expertise.The inability to work far from their area due to the high cost of public transportation and fuelMost of the region's population lives below the poverty lineMost of the children suffer from malnutrition

4 - Implementation of activities

Al Nada will rent a place for the carpentry and buy all the required items and select the personnel the carpenters that lost their jobs during the crisis according to some specific criteria that will jointly developed with organization Also Al Nada will install solar panel to generate the electricity so the carpentry will not suspend the work especially with the lack of fuel.Al Nada will do follow-up visits to check the quality of the furniture and the consistency of the work and ask for periodic inventory of the items and figure out the challenges and difficulties in terms of resources and utilities.Al Nada will also demand a financial report on a periodic basis.

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

Criteria of selecting carpenters might become controversial since they are only 10% (50 out of 500) of the total target who work in this profession. likelihood high -the worker might get injured while using the tools if not restricted to the safety rules. likelihood high Al Nada will conduct a workshop for the safety of the laborsIt might involve in the carpentry children below 18 but Al Nada will insist of applying the law of ministry of social affair which is not allowed to hire children below 18


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Al Nada has zero tolerance toward the sexual exploitation and abuse, also all the staff, ORV’s and contractors are committed to the 6 rules of IASC. Risk 1 (low): Women beneficiaries and children are threaten to suffer from sexual exploitation and abuse because only men worked in carpentry. Mitigation measures Al Nada will conduct a training workshop before initiating the project and will let the PoCs who work in the carpentry sign the protection from sexual exploitation and abuse formRisk 2 (moderate) the beneficiaries are afraid of complaint and the feeling of stigmaMitigation measure : Al Nada will conduct awareness session to beneficiaries in coordintation with Al Nada SCs in Maliha and also we will distribute flyers indicating the hotline number to receive complaints.
