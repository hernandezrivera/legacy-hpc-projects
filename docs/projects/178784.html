Project Summary

The project is designed to respond to the early recovery needs of 990people (Men: 850, Women: 140),( 350 IDPs, 530 hosted people, and returnees 110 ) with 27 people of them are people with disabilities, to respond to their severe needs by capacity building and improve their skills to be able to operate and maintain the solar energy systems through vocational training sessions, the project is designed to respond to the needs of IDPs, Returnees, and hosted people, located in the north of Syria in the communities and the camps, to cover their needs about operating solar energy systems and environmental hygiene solutions, also, support the local councils by equipment and cash for work to the technical workers for two months.
The project aims to teach the beneficiaries the basic information related to testing and monitoring the whole equipment of the solar power systems such as testing the PV panels, cables, inverters, …etc. and how to clean the panels periodically, how to use the fire extinguisher if need, and how to behave in emergency cases to keep on the equipment.
SECD will be in a close position with the local council, the beneficiaries, the IDPs, and the host community and will involve them in all the project stages from the planning till the closure of the project to adhere to the community engagement approach by inviting them to regular FGDs and open all the channels to receive their feedback, complaints, and opinions. The proposed project ensures the enhancement the skills of the trainees to be more able to involve in the labor market and providing services for operating and maintenance of solar power systems which used in the different sectors such as irrigation, water station, and that which used to supply the homes with electrical energy.

4 - Needs

ERL needs remain considerable in scale, severity, and complexity across the country, with an estimated 11.6 million people needing early recovery and livelihood support. While precautionary measures against the spread of the virus were crucial to contain transmission, they exacerbated pre-existing and underlying socio-economic vulnerabilities, by reducing availability and access to basic services and employment opportunities. COVID-19 restrictions further and heavily impacted employment opportunities across the country, with unemployment estimated closer to 50 % of the working-age population at the end of 2020. pushing up prices and further increasing dependence on negative coping mechanisms. The regional economic downturn also resulted in reduced economic flows into Syria. The estimated $1.6 billion of remittances sent to Syria each year are estimated to have reduced by up to 50%. The impact of this is expected to be the hardest for 86% of the Syrian population who were living below the poverty line even before the COVID-19 crisis. As a result, safety nets and livelihood resources are more strained than ever before, and unaffordability is now reported across sectors as the main obstacle to accessing goods and services. A period of further economic contraction is to be expected, with reduced production, increased poverty rates and further socio-economic deterioration. 5 - Implementation of activities

The activities will be conducted by the technical staff of SECD, including the volunteers. Regarding the procurement process, SECD will commit to the procurement policy to select the vendors who will only supply the required materials from the local markets if available. The vocational training sessions will be performed by the technical staff of SECD, and cash for work will be provided to the workers for two months. SECD's approach is conducting consultation with beneficiaries through meetings with them in local council's offices and public meetings will be done regularly before the start of training finish. Since the inception of SECD, the organization has used a two-pronged approach for "role missing permitted action. As a major part of the SECD strategy is to support the empowerment and institutionalization of local civil society organizations, city councils, and other coordinating bodies, SECD has implemented projects through these bodies on the ground. The second approach for project implementation has been the use of direct implementation, working through the SECD team inside, but in close coordination with the aforementioned actors. For those reasons, SECD has a proven track record of successful project implementation employing both approaches. Implementation for this project will be completed through SECD. The MEAL team will remain present throughout the various activities of the project. Also, SECD will be responsible for the technical, project management, and determine project-specific work plans, ensuring transparent and clear procurement processes. M&E field staff will be charged with collecting information regarding beneficiaries, benchmarks, and baseline data.

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA) policies are in place, practiced and monitored. Policies will cover standards of behavior for the organization’s staff and representatives, specifically forbidding sexual exploitation and abuse, and include accountability measures. Policies will address risks at the organizational level and the program level. Trainings and refreshers on PSEA will be done systematically. The targeted communities and learning facilities should be aware of SECD policies and procedures. SECD staff and targeted communities will have access to safe and effective reporting systems including knowing when they will get a response to their report and having the right to report anonymously. SECD will ensure a survivor centered approach that ensures the safety and confidentiality of the affected person. The survivor will be an active participant in the processes. A poor handling of allegations of sexual explanation and abuse can put the accuser/survivor, alleged perpetrator and others at risk.
