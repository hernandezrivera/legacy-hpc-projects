Project Summary

The purpose of the project is to enhance the coordination and management of IDP camps in Mogadishu/Afgoye corridor. This project will target 67,200 beneficiaries all the beneficiaries will be drawn from the camps in Mogadishu and the Afgoye corridor Most of the IDP camps are manned by gate keepers. The relationship to and with the IDP community is similarly diverse but in the long absence of stable Government providing IDPs with assistance and protection, informal managers have often become the primary go to person and provider of support and, to a certain extent, protection, in particular since communities of IDPs are often marginalized and discriminated against because they belong to minorities or are separated from the protection of their clans. Aid diversion has been allegedly high in informal settlements, but the extent of it remains unclear. SSWC intends to establish new community structures and strengthen the existing structures in collaboration with the local authorities in Mogadishu/Afgoye . SSWC will focus on creating coordination structures and mechanisms that help the beneficiaries’ access different services in their settlements. Communication channels will be enhanced to foster information sharing in the camps with other service providers in the targeted areas. The project will develop service mapping mechanisms to address the population movements, trends of new arrivals, exiting individuals and returnees in the IDP camps. This will be enhanced by engaging focal points in the camps, the focal points will conduct service mapping and record all the essential needs/gaps in the camps.
SSWC will conduct safety audits in the camps and share with the cluster on quarterly basis, Moreso there will be strong measures in place to collect feedback from the community within the project areas so as to know the what are the complaints and how to address them amicably.
The project will take into account all the beneficiaries irrespective of their colour, tribe and the ability. This is to enhance inclusivity of all the communities in the project area.
On the other hand Populations in Banadir continue to be affected by climatic shocks and insecurity, often leading to their displacement. Urban areas receive newly displaced people, who move into private IDP sites with poor living conditions and little access to basic services. IDP returns to home are extremely limited as many displaced families have lost livelihoods and are reliant on humanitarian services. People displaced to informal sites are living in precarious conditions and are not having their basic needs met due to inconsistent service provision or exclusion from accessing humanitarian support. There are over 2,400 recorded IDP sites across Somalia.

Project Activities

The project will have varied activities in the camps. These activities will be site management, capacity building of the camp leaders, conducting community forums, service mapping in the targeted IDP camps and providing effective structure for practical collaboration and coordination of humanitarian interventions. SSWC will conduct site improvements including road improvement, drainages, site improvement kit distributions, public light improvements benefitting IDPs living in IDP sites. SSWC will strengthen the existing coordination mechanisms and in sites where there no existing structures SSWC will establish active structures which will be governed by Standard Operating Procedures(SOPs). The structures will be identified through camp leaders and existing projects staffs.This will help in the identifying needs and duplication of services. SSWC will ensure at-least 20 community structures exist in the vast IDP settelements in Mogadishu /Afgoye corridor with specific sector task assigned to each structure. 20 groups comprising of 8 members will be trained and strengthened as part of the camp structures establishment. Establish/reinforce community mechanisms for communication channels (centres of information/centres of communication/centres of coordination) SSWC will develop communication centres in all the targeted IDP camps for essential information recording and sharing. The centres will allow the existing and new IDP arrivals to access information of the various services offered at the camps.The centres will also provide a linkage between the SSWC and the IDP residents whereby SSWC will further coordinate with other services providers in the IDPs. Its expected 5 communication centres be established in different IDP settlements in Mogadishu/Afgoye. Site information on availability of services-Creation of IM product providing information on population movement and numbers (new arrivals, exits or returns, population by site, identification of gaps, monitoring of services, mapping of or establishment of focal points) after Inventories existing tools and identify gaps in information SSWC will conduct to service mapping and monitoring and safety audits on quarterly bases. On the the hand the will be stakeholder monthly meetings that will be chaired by SSWC, deliberations will be made on where there are challenges, gaps and make decisions on the enhancement of implementation of interventions for all actors in Mogadishu/Afgoye.

Mitigation Measures

Exclusion and Marginalization. -Risk of exclusion and Marginalization: To mitigate this, SSWC uses community based participatory targeting and selection to ensure the most vulnerable are selected and that these are community-acknowledged vulnerable people, which helps with acceptance. - Access and staff safety: if security deteriorate and staff are unable to access communities for targeting, registration, verification, distribution and monitoring, SSWC will immediately inform SHF to determine the best course of action. - Acceptance: Communities and local authorities do not accept the program, and local disputes cause reputation damage for SSWC. -SSWC is already mitigating this risk by having a good relationship with local authorities and communities and ensuring community involvement in beneficiary selection. SSWCalso employs extensive community consultation and feedback mechanisms to mitigate this risk. Exclusion and Marrginalization - SSWC will consider women, girls, men and boys in the project. The will be part of decision making during the process of project design, implementation and monitoring the project activities. - SSWC will seek all the beneficiaries voices by setting up feedback and complaints desks this will help in collecting feedback irrespective feedback irrespective of age and gender. -SSWC will inculcate the attitude of engaging all the gender during the sensitization campaigns so as to avoid exclusion and marginalization Protection Mainstreaming: -SSWC will collect data that is disaggregated by sex and gender and different groups in order to ensure protection for all particularly the less disadvantaged. -SSWC Make sure that a comprehensive registration system is in place, enabling women to be registered in their own name and provisions are made for child-headed households. Ensure that the purpose of registration is properly communicated to all groups and that effective data protection measures are applied. -SSWC will emphasis the safety of all the beneficiaries in the camps by distributing items in a humane manner, SSWC will distribute wheelbarrows, spades,rakes that will be used in site maintenance during this activity the staff will be present to avoid overcrowding of beneficiaries in one spot - SSWC will give special consideration to the lactating mothers, pregnant women, old men and people living with disabilities to be served first. - The project will ensure that service providers consider the needs of different ethnic, racial, national or social groups and ensure that the quality of their services is equitable. - SSWC will put into account, all persons have equal access to work opportunities in the camp particularly the CFW activities. Consult with all actors operating in the camp to determine whether monetary compensation will be offered for work, and ensure consistency in agreed approach. If compensation is offered, ensure equal payment for all persons without discrimination.

Please provide further explanation

SSWC understands that a gradual, often long-term process of reducingdisplacement-specific needs and ensuring a coordinated and timelyengagement of different actors within IDP camps is vital for safety, liberty orhealth of the IDPs. It`s on this basis that SSWC through partners will supportstrengthening of CMCs to play a greater role in day to day running of thecamps. The interventions will also support early recovery for pastoralists thatlost their existence and displaced. The durable solutions will ensure that IDPslive in safe and secure camps, help CMCs solve housing, land and propertyissues; support post-conflict reconstruction; and come up with workableremedies for violations suffered. To achieve these, SSWC and the partners shallset up effective feedback mechanisms to monitor the process of supportingdurable solutions and determining what remains to be done to achieve durablesolution. Assessments conducted will include a gender analysis and take intoIDPs who have special needs or might be marginalized within the displacedpopulation. Credible complaint mechanisms for IDPs and other affectedpopulations will ensure that concerns are brought to the immediate attention ofthe responsible national or local authorities through the relevant channels.
