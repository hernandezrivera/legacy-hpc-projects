Project Summary

According to HNO Syria and HRP highlight in 2024, 16.7 million people (8.4 million female, 8.3 million male) are assessed to need humanitarian assistance across Syria, up from 15.3 million in 2023. Of the 16.7 million people in need, 5.5 million are displaced, including over 2 million who live in last resort sites.
After 12 years of hostilities and persistent economic crisis, people in Syria saw their situation worsen when a series of earthquakes hit northern Syria and Türkiye on 6 and 20 February 2023. The earthquakes uprooted hundreds of thousands of families, resulted in almost 5,900 deaths and more than 12,800 people injured in Syria, and severely damaged infrastructures already weakened by the conflict. Many families lost their main breadwinner due to death or injury, at a time when the economic situation was already dire, increasing the vulnerability of millions of people previously unable to meet their basic needs.
The Food Security Project contributes improvement of food security for the most vulnerable and the cholera spread in NWS affects the case and takes attention to don't infection it.
Syrian IDPs population, to meet the basic and food security-related needs of the 1620 vulnerable Syrian IDPs families ( 12000 BNFs) in Dana Sub-district and Ariha district through targeted Food Baskets and bread support
Space Of Peace will provide support at the household level. The most vulnerable households are being targeted, selected among “Gender of the HH, Marital Status of HHF, the total number of children, pregnant women, lactating (breastfeeding), widows, orphans (under 18 years old and without mother and father), Chronic Illness, Disability, Shelter, Income, Livelihood, new IDP & old IDP”
The Local councils will be informed prior to the distribution process and SMSs will be sent to the beneficiaries who have mobile phone numbers if applicable in some locations, or WhatsApp messages or any other announcement method can be set up in locations where no full access for the mobile
network, two days before each distribution.
The Food Basket will Contain the main needs of food as ( Rice, sugar, Oil, Butter, Tomato sauce, Pasta, Tea, and Bread bundle weight of 800 grams) will survive 12000 IDPs in Ariha and Al Dana Camps especially our target IDPs are Women, Children, Widows, and Old men and will distribute in-Kind home -to-home to avoid them the infectious from diseases, and the team will apply the ruler of Covide-19. the distribution will be monthly in the last week of the Month.
