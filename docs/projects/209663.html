Project Summary

Bonyan built its educational projects and plans for 2024-25 to effectively contributes to reducing the educational gap in Syria and reaching the largest number of children of school age, and in a manner that complies with the objectives of both the HRP and ED Cluster by improving methods and procedures to reach a suitable educational environment for children and youth.
[*] Starting with the needs, the protracted crisis in Syria, further compounded by the aftermath of the earthquake continues to negatively impact the education system. Where there is a need to support up to 7.1M school aged children and teachers, including CwD, of which 6.9M are children (48% female and 52% male). There are more than 2.45 4million Out of School Children (46% girls, 54% boys) and over a million who are at risk of dropping out. Idleb tops the governorates with the highest percentages of OoSC children reaching 69% and Aleppo rate is 38%. the main driver reducing access to education is economic, affecting both the households and teaching personnel.
[*] As for 2024 Bonyan plans to reach up to 35,000 children, youth, teachers, education actors and caregivers (14,000 Boys 14,400 Girls 3,100 Men 3,500 Women – 1,750 CwD) in which 20,000 children and youth are in the classrooms (9,800 Boys 10,200 Girls – 3-24 years old and includes 900 CwD and 12,000 IDPs) in 43 learning centers (4 ECCE centers, 20 formal schools, 15 non-formal learning centers, and 4 non-formal TVET learning centers for youth)
[*] For 2025 Bonyan plan to increase its intervention by 30% to reach total of 45,000 children, teachers, education actors and caregivers with total budget around 3M USD.
[***] The details of education activities are included in the extended summary paragraph.
[*] Brief on PSEAH prevention measures: In Bonyan, PSEA Focal Persons are appointed to address allegations promptly, ensuring investigation and reporting follow standard procedures. Persons with disabilities receive PSEA awareness and direct communication access. Call center numbers and PSEA-related materials are promoted in project areas. Survivor-centered approaches guarantee information access and immediate assistance.
Risk: SEA Violate from one of the staff, Mitigation includes staff training, community feedback, and safe community centers for domestic violence. Mandatory PSEA training, CoC, and complaint mechanisms.
[*] Key Risks and Mitigations: Bonyan is following the cluster risk matrix as well as developing its own risk matrix when operating in certain areas.
(1) Risk: Security risks may hinder the safe implementation of activities. - Mitigation: Collaborating with local authorities and humanitarian agencies, conducting thorough security assessments, and maintaining flexibility in project timelines to adapt to changing security situations.
(2) Risk: Vulnerable groups may be overlooked - Mitigation: Regular review and updating of vulnerability criteria, extensive training for community committees, and the establishment of a robust feedback mechanism to ensure continual refinement and inclusivity in beneficiary selection.
(3) Risk: Inadequate coordination may lead to duplication of efforts or gaps in service delivery. -Mitigation: Establishing clear communication channels, active participation in cluster meetings, and regular sharing of project updates to ensure effective collaboration with relevant stakeholders, minimizing the risk of duplication or service delivery gaps.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The goal of the Bunyan project will be to return school-age students who have dropped out of education to formal education, through various activities that ensure a good quality of education for these students, with a focus on children with special needs who face greater problems than other students, activities will be implemented and project interventions in coordination with local authorities from the directorates of education in Aleppo and IdlebThe project will begin with minor rehabilitation of schools and education centers, such as replacing doors, windows, whiteboards, and fixing walls, based on a technical assessment. BTL activities will be conducted to raise awareness about the importance of education and gain local support for schools and education projects. These activities will target 35,000 individuals, including children, parents, and teachers, and will lead to increased enrollment in available education services.ECE will be provided in 4 centers targeting 400 children. The project will link formal and non-formal education by providing support to 20 formal schools and 15 non-formal learning centers, targeting a total of 20,000 children to attend classes in 2024 and providing incentives for 1035 teachers and education actors.The project will also focus on inclusion for children with disabilities by increasing access through rehabilitation, dedicating inclusion officers, and raising awareness about the needs of CWDs.As a main activity, Bonyan will conduct the following: [1] Provision pf children with Early Childhood Care and Education – 4 centers – 2 learning-periods (each 6 months) – target: 800 children (3-5 years, 400 Girls / 400 Boys) [2] Provision of formal education to children and youth – 20 formal schools using SiG curriculum – target: 8,200 children (6-17 years, 4182 Girls / 4018 Boys) [3] Provision of children and youth with non-formal education programs:[3.1] 15 non-formal education centers using either curriculum B or UNICEF SLP curriculum – 2 learning-periods - target: 9,000 children (5-17 years, 4610 Girls / 4390 Boys) [[Literacy and Numeracy Classes and SLP will be provided for children who are already dropped out of schools meanwhile Remedial Classes will be provided to children with low achievement and for children who are at risk of dropping out]][3.2] 4 non-formal TVET centers – 2 learning-periods – target: 2,000 youth (15-17 years, 1000 Girls / 1000 Boys) [4] The centers will be provided. All centers and formal schools will be provided with supplies, heating fuel, school bags, children’s school supplies, textbooks, teaching resources, kits and guides, and recreational materials. The children will be provided with school-based psychosocial support, PTA, Children councils and life skills and citizenship education programs. MEAL team of Bonyan will conduct ongoing monitoring of project activities to verify achievement of indicators and to correct any deviation.
