Project Summary

This CSF allocation will be used to scale up our response in Arauca and Valle del Cauca. The allocation will be used to cover a small percentage of technical staff time (CP Technical Advisor and MHPSS Technical Advisor) to train or build staff capacity. CSF allocation will also be used to purchase protective PPE equipment for key response office staff to maintain staff safety and comply with biosecurity protocols for the COVID-19 pandemic. Supplies, transport and implementation (capacity building, planning and strategy activities) will also be provided.
With regards to Programme Activities, CSF allocation will be used to implement the following activities:
1. Perform a conflict sensitivity needs analysis – CSF funds will be used to work with a Colombian think-tank Fundación Ideas para la Paz specialised in conflict analysis to support conflict sensitivity analysis and interview key stakeholders. This analysis will support us to better understand our context, anticipate and plan for future events, and ensure our response strategy adequately and appropriately addresses the needs of affected boys, girls and families.
2. Emergency assistance for displaced families – CSF funds will be used to scale up emergency response to purchase emergency shelter and family hygiene kits to meet the immediate needs of displaced families and vulnerable families affected by conflict. Project assistants will also provide immediate Psychosocial First Aid to displaced boys, girls and families.
3. Child Protection awareness-raising activities in communities– As part of an integrated Education and Child Protection response, CSF funds will be used to raise awareness of Child Protection risks (with a particular focus on recruitment and use of children including sexual exploitation) amongst boys, girls, parents, caregivers and community members and relevant CP referral pathways. CSF funds will be used to purchase material for these activities (including printing of relevant information in accessible and friendly language and local radio campaigns). Early activities will focus on Arauca and Valle del Cauca targeting internally displaced Colombian populations and irregular Venezuelan migrant populations who are often most at-risk from recruitment, use and/or sexual exploitation by non-state armed actors. SC Colombia field offices which will be involved in these early activities will be: Arauca (Arauca) and Cali (Valle del Cauca).
