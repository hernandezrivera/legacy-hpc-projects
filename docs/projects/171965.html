Project Summary

The project supposes assistance in providing food and goods necessary for agriculture, the population that has become vulnerable due to the ongoing military conflict, and the decline in economic indicators in the region. The economic and humanitarian situation in NGCA is also expected to worsen further as a result of additional COVID-19 restrictions.
Firstly, it is planned to promote the medical system by providing food vouchers to medical workers involved in the response to COVID-19. Taking into account the isolation of the local health system and the lack of qualified personnel, it is planned to provide resource support to medical workers in order to increase their motivation by providing vouchers of $ 100 for food products. Such assistance helps to maintain the health system's efficiency and will allow health workers who are engaged in a critical public service and receive an average of $ 80 to $ 200 to meet their food needs.
Secondly, support for the population that take care of themselves through agriculture: provision of feed, greenhouses, seeds, poultry and agricultural equipment, etc.to improve and stabilize them in winter, especially in the context of the COVID-19 pandemic (increased isolation, loss of sources of income).
The results of the project are expected, firstly, to increase the sustainability of the local health system, reduce the outflow of doctors, due to the support of doctors responding to COVID-19; secondly, to increase food security due to resource assistance to households engaged in agriculture in the winter period.

1 - Needs

The economic downturn caused by years of conflict has increased the vulnerability of the population of NGCA, which has become dependent on receiving social benefits. As a result of measures taken to reduce the risk of the spread of COVID-19, the possibility of crossing the demarcation line was blocked. This has had a serious impact on the socio-economic security of hundreds of thousands of people who rely on pensions and social benefits as their main source of income. At the moment, their ability to receive social benefits on the GCA is limited by their inability to cross the contact line. According to data from a research of socio-economic situation in the region conducted by the organization in collaboration with UHF, most participants of focus group discussions noted that the total income of their family members practically does not cover the basic needs of households. Thus, according to respondents, in order to provide the household with everything necessary, the minimum income per family member should be, according to various estimates, from 3500 UAH to 5860 UAH. At the same time, according to data from local employment centers, the average salary in Donetsk does not exceed 5800 UAH, in other cities and rural areas of NGCA of Donetsk oblast it is lower. Thus, for households that have members wich are unable to work and / or receive social benefits (due to restrictions on crossing the line of contact), the issue of food security is acute. It should also take into account the unemployment rate at the beginning of the year reached 33%, as well as the closure of small businesses due to decline in economic indicators while COVID-19 pandemic. Providing 500 FSL grants for the purchase of seeds, poultry, agricultural equipment (including incubators), and the construction of greenhouses in the amount of $ 500 will strengthen the food security of local communities. The COVID-19 pandemic has also negatively affected the local health system. The authorities have de facto stopped publishing information on the number of medical workers infected with COVID-19 since July 11. While this information was published, the percentage of medical staff among those who infected reached from 25% at the beginning of the pandemic to 18% in the first decade of July. Thus, the threat of COVID-19 aggravates the existing problems of lack of medical personnel. Recently, the number of medical workers has been falling: from 01.01.2018 to 01.01.2020, the number of doctors decreased from 9245 to 8901 people, and the number of secondary medical personnel – from 18296 to 17936 people. According to the report of the head of the local health regulator, the lack of doctors at NGCA is more than 5,000 people, secondary health workers – more than 7,500 people. This situation is facilitated by the low social security of doctors and medical staff, so according to information, the minimum salary of health workers varies between 3700 UAH for junior medical workers and 9400 UAH for doctors with high categories. At the same time, only the cost of a food basket in the area reaches 2300 UAH per person. Such an imbalance between wages and the cost of basic needs meeting leads not only to a threat to food security, but also provokes a change of employment to more profitable, but not specialized, which exacerbates the above-mentioned shortage of medical workers. Providing 2,000 food vouchers equivalent to $ 100 for food purchases for three months will support and stimulate the work of medical personnel involved in the response to COVID-19. The voucher will have restrictions on the purchase of exclusively food products, including restrictions on the purchase of low-alcohol, alcoholic and tobacco products.

2 - Activities & Outcomes

As part of the project, it is planned to: 1) Selection of 500 participants of the FSL grant program among 500 households ($500 per household) that have members corresponding to one of the categories: unemployed, elderly, people with disabilities, single parents, as well as among large families. Priority is given to these categories due to the discrepancy between the average salary and the needs of more than one person. The FSL grant program will provide participating households with a variety of agricultural products (seeds, feed), poultry, or agricultural equipment (such as greenhouses) of their own choice. The project's planned provision of appropriate varieties of various crop seeds and agricultural resources, the provision of livestock feed, and the distribution of poultry. This will allow vulnerable populations affected by the conflict to obtain funds for self-sufficiency.2) Verification of participants and their needs, consultancy on current areas of household-based agricultural activities.3) Purchase and provision of requested goods and equipment4) Monitoring the targeted use of the provided assistance after 6 months5) Selection of a retail network for the implementation of the voucher program according to the criteria of presence in the project locations, competitive cost of the product basket, compliance with the quality of products sold, the possibility of implementing the voucher component, and the availability of a wide range6) Development of an electronic database for the implementation of the voucher component (for monitoring and monitoring the use of the voucher), printing of vouchers7) Selection and verification of 2000 participants of the voucher program among doctors and medical staff involved in the response to COVID-19, providing them with food vouchers equivalent to $1008) Providing advice on the operation of the voucher component for beneficiaries and employees of the selected retail network on the assortment, operation of the electronic database, etc. In the framework of the project will take into account gender and age factors. Both FSL grants and the voucher component is designed to be individualized and will allow beneficiaries to independently choose the purpose of the allocated assistance. This approach will also take into account the needs of people who have a special diet (including due to health conditions) or other restrictions. Assistance will be provided impartially in accordance with the project's target categories. The project plans to conduct post-distribution monitoring to assess its effectiveness and achievement of indicators. All mechanisms of accountability to the affected population will also be observed, a hotline, e-mail will function for receiving complaints and suggestions, messages will also be received through the organization's social networks and an anonymous mailbox in the organization's office, etc.

3 - Indicators & targets

Cluster goals within HRP:1) Ensure immediate access to food for the most vulnerable people affected by conflict and or СOVID-19. Activity: Provision of food assistance (food, cash or voucher) to meet the immediate needs of the conflict affected population in Eastern Ukraine —will be achieved by providing food vouchers to doctors and medical staff involved in the response to COVID-19.2) Improve food security through the provision of emergency and time-critical agriculture assistance. Activity: 1) Timely provision of appropriate varieties of assorted crop seeds and agricultural inputs that allow conflict affected vulnerable populations restore their livelihoods. 2) Provision of animal (livestock and poultry) feed and fodder production. 3) Distribution of livestock (including honeybees) and poultry/domestic fowl 4) Distribution of agricultural equipment and support to backyard kitchen gardening activities to improve access to nutritious food —will be achieved by providing FSL grants for the purchase of seeds, feed, poultry, agricultural equipment, and greenhouses, depending on the needs of the household and the choice of its representative. In order not to limit the beneficiaries, a single indicator of 500 households — 1250 beneficiaries will be applied for the three activities of this goal Indicators:1) #2000 of affected population who receive adequate food/cash responses, in a timely manner2) #1250 of conflict affected people assisted on time with appropriate agricultural livelihood assistance
