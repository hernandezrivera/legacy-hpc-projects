Project Summary

This project is mainly designed to address the most urgent lifesaving ES NFI needs and critical gaps to save the lives of the extremely vulnerable affected people that include men, women, boys and girls in camps and gatherings and protect them from the protection risks, violence, neglects and exploitations and reduce the impacts of the crisis. This will be done by ensuring providing needs-based and timely emergency shelter, NFIs to most vulnerable population including protracted IDPs and returnees in areas affected by emergencies. Through provision of timely qualitative and protective ESNFI services, the most affected populations including newly displaced after 15th. April 2023 will be more effectively protected from violence, risks and exploitations and will be able to mitigate external shocks. This will be achieved through implementation of several activities that to 20124 HRP project sheet. The proposed activities under ESNFI intervention are summarized as follows: distribution of shelter and Non-Food Items targeting 2000 vulnerable families , each package includes plastic sheeting, plastic mat, cooking sets, jerry can, blanket) , provision of cash/vouchers for NFIs, establishment of communal shelters, rehabilitation of old collective centers in the targeted localities, tent distribution during winter weather conditions, rainfall and wind in particular, many cannot protect children and the elderly or buy other basic items that help protect them from the cold and rainfall. Distribution of Emergency Shelter Kit (ESK) that includes local materials such as bamboo sticks, wooden poles, robes, plastic sheet etc.., provision of cash/vouchers for shelter repair or emergency shelter, provision of cash for renting houses and general site development.
This project is expected to cover large number of beneficiaries in the targeted areas in North Darfur State ( Alfasher, Um Baru, Kornoi, Tawila and At Tina localities ) and South Darfur State ( Alradoum, Baleil, Gereida , Kass and Um Dafoug localities), this can be imagined as well through the following activities , the direct recipients of environment conservation, shelter and risk reduction skills ,the HIV/AID and Gender messages will be disseminated among large number of population to raise their awareness , etc however the large numbers of the populations in the targeted areas will benefit from the project. HDPO has the current operational presence, capacity, and technical expertise to continue and scale up interventions in targeted conflict/ affected locations. HDPO is actively contributes to the coordination and implementation of rapid responses to population needs in IDPs camps, gatherings and returnees undeserved areas affected by current emergencies.
4350 households (HHs) are expected to benefit from this project activities directly (3000 IDPs &1350 host communities) as follow: Females (1500), Males (1350), Children heading households (800), Adults (200), Elderly (300) and Disabilities (200) with total estimated budget of 1,712,000 $ to ensure the above identified critical needs been addressed in effectively and timely manner.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

HDPO field trained staff on DO NO HARM principal will work closely with the beneficiaries to identify any abuse, exploitation, violence and report it to the respective authority for further investigation and community leaders' women, men, boys and girls jointly will prioritize the appropriate and safe place for distribution of the project services to the beneficiaries so as to reduce the numbers of harm cases that may happen in that distance as well as HDPO jointly will work with the community leaders and other stakeholders if possible to ensure the targeted beneficiaries have equal access to provided services in timely transparently manner. and the most vulnerable populations (conflict affected children and person with specific needs) will be given special attention during the distribution of NFIs, Improved Emergency shelter ,training and awareness sessions .

The project will put in place avenues such as suggestion boxes, use of aid workers to conduct interviews- and surveys, including meetings and social media platforms. The information obtained from beneficiaries and stakeholders will be used to take corrective action, ensure quality assurance and inform decisions to be taken by the project steering team.

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

In order to ensure the targeted beneficiaries number is reached and the intended services are provided equally among the most vulnerable populations to save their lives in timely and effectively manner, HDPO will adopt all the safety, protection and accountability to the affected population related measures including DO NO HARM policies and procedure at all levels of the project implementations.:- 1- In order to ensure that the communities are aware of the project activities, the communities (women, men, boys and girls) were involved from the very beginning of the project that include needs assessments, project designs and will be involved fully in implementation of the project. 2- HDPO will work closely with the affected populations during selection of beneficiaries so as to ensure fairness, equal distribution of services by considering gender needs and sensitivity and to avoid any conflict of interest that might occur among the affected populations. 3- Joint monitoring team will be formed by HDPO with involvement of key IDPs community leaders, local authorities at state levels. The team will meet on quarterly basis to review the effective implementation of the project activities. This mechanism will enable the target populations to discuss and evaluate the performance of HDPO and provide feedback, complaints and recommendation where necessarily needed. Their feedback, complaints and recommendations will enable the organization management to take effective steps toward the implementation of the project and its impact of the target beneficiaries. HDPO will follow up the guidelines and procedures that provide instructions on how to set up and run interagency-based mechanism to handle reports of abuse and to provide victim assistance and scaling up protection from sexual exploitation and abuse and focusing on encouraging victims to come forward by ensuring that safe, trusted and accessible mechanisms for reporting and promoting a culture of zero tolerance for SEA.
