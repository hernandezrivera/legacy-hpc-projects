Project Activities

The goal of Community Based Demining (CBD) is to clear landmines and explosive remnants of war (ERW), and create employment in vulnerable communities. These activities will help to promote stability through: - Destruction of landmines and ERW, which kill/injure civilians - Clearance of contaminated land, ready to be handed back to communities for productive use- Employment of women and men vulnerable to economic shocks Landmines and ERWs impact pastoral communities in Hiraan and Bakool. Pressure from drought, famine, and the return of displaced persons forces local populations to venture into hazardous areas where they are likely to be maimed or killed by landmines and ammunition. These explosive hazards also denies land from productive use. Clearance by mine action NGOs alone is not enough. Impacted communities need training and resources to clear contaminated land. Local leaders need direct engagement with the mine action community to understand appropriate responses to contamination. Through CBD, HALO will empower women and men to clear hazardous areas within their own communities. Dangerous items will be cleared, safe land will be returned to communities, and employment will build resilience through steady incomes and upskilling. Support from other donors to cover overheads means HALO can deliver value for money and target a higher percentage of funds directly to clearance, employment, and local procurement. Area Clearance: HALO will recruit women and men from communities impacted by landmines and ERW, and will train them as deminers. HALO will deploy the deminers to clear minefields in their communities. The deminers will use the full excavation method of landmine clearance, as opposed clearance using metal detectors. Experienced section commanders and supervisors will manage the safe conduct of the demining activities. Reintegration and victim assistance:As part of the preparation for clearance HALO will do extensive survey of each task, including any victim and accident data. Strengthening of community-based protection mechanisms:By hiring staff directly from mine-impacted communities HALO will strengthen community based protection mechanisms by:• Dignified employment and training, leading to steady income and useful skill sets to communities vulnerable to economic shocks• Return of useful land to productive use Training is a key part of HALOs method of operations. All staff learn about mines and ERW, the dangers they pose, and how to safely respond when an item is found. Under CBD HALO will be hiring and training community members en mass, increasing knowledge of safe behavior relating to ERW, strengthening the community level protection, and resulting in fewer deaths and accidents. Many protection issues are rooted in vulnerability to economic shocks. CBD insulates communities from economic downturns by providing employment opportunities. The inflow of cash to the community will help protect at risk households from economic instability. CDB provides an ideal opportunity for hardworking young people to gain regular employment with HALO, even after their home communities are cleared, ensuring a continued stream of income to their communities. Additionaly, valuable land will be made safe from mines and ERW. Once the land is cleared it can be put to productive use, for agriculture or grazing, further insulating communities from economic shocks. Provision of protection-oriented support to people with specific needs or heightened vulnerability:CBD provides an opportunity to employ women. Under CBD, women will be working near home in their own communities, bypassing many standard cultural concerns related to women working. Ideally, when female employees become accustomed to working with HALO (and see that it is a safe and reputable place to work) and benefit from a steady income, many will continue working with HALO even after their communities are cleared, and inspire other women to do the same.

Mitigation Measures

Risk 1: Exposure to landmines or explosive remnants of warMeasures:- By the nature of this work staff will be exposed to landmines or explosive remnants of war, and the project will target beneficiaries who are at high risk of mines and ERW. The objective of this project is to decrease this risk- By clearing and destroying ERW and mines we will be actively mitigating the risks they pose- All staff who will work in clearance will be trained on proper procedures and safety measures. All staff will be closely managed by experienced and trained HALO managers to ensure all safety procedures are followed- All confirmed hazards areas in the community will be communicated to local leaders, clearly delineating danger areas from safe areas. This will help communities avoid mined areas.- HALO will liaise closely with local leaders and community members to ensure that as many community members as possible know what to do if an ERW is found Risk 2: Delivery of humanitarian assistance affects peaceful coexistence and spark intercommunity conflict over access to resourcesMeasures:- HALO will liaise closely with community leaders to explain HALO’s policies related to potentially contentious resources i.e. vehicle rental, local purchases, employment- HALO will follow strict and transparent rules for employment and local procurement, that endeavor to ensure that the benefits of the NGO are spread fairly throughout the community.- HALO will work with local authorities to ensure that valuable income sources, in particular the revenue earned from car rental, will rotate between different vehicle owners of different clans, so that the benefits are distributed fairly.- A HALO community liaison will maintain close communication with the local community throughout the life cycle of the project to ensure close cooperation with the community, and offer an avenue for quick and clear communication. Risk 3: Risk of dispute on land ownership/land tenure in targeted project areasMeasures:- HALO will research the task before clearance starts to ensure the land's ownership is not disputed- If land is disputed HALO will work with the local community to ascertain who it belongs to and what they intend to do with it once it is cleared.

Please provide further explanation

Security:In both Bakool and Hiran HALO will be working primarily in the border region, which is largely secured by Ethiopian forces and has proven stable enough for long term projects. As CBD is designed to work with and within local communities other security concerns such as road movements will be minimal. Links to Government/Community Systems or long-term Interventions:The Government of Somalia has state level Somali Explosive Management Authorities (SEMA) responsible for the coordination and implementation of mine action in line with the Badbado 3 year plan. This project fits directly into this plan and offers a foundation for other actors to run long-term projects (agriculture, water management, education) on land once it is clear of explosive hazards. Alignment with National Objectives for the Sector:The Government of Somalia is working to fulfill its obligations under the Convention on the Prohibition of the Use, Stockpiling, Production and Transfer of Anti-Personnel Mines and on Their Destruction (Ottawa Landmine Ban Treaty) in 2012. This project will direct contribute to assisting the government of Somalia meet its treaty obligations. Sustainability and Handover Components: When an area is cleared of mines HALO will handover the land back to the local community. The goal of mine clearance is finite; once an area is cleared of mines the explosive hazards element of this project will be complete. HALO will work with other actors to ensure that the newly cleared land is put to productive use. Additionally, staff that proved themselves to be hardworking and bright can be recruited into other HALO activities, leading to long term capacity development within HALO. Incorporation of Perspectives and Challenges of Displacement Affected Communities:This project is not directed to displaced communities as the majority of explosive hazards are found along the border regions. However, this project will assist in preventing future displacement by providing incomes and by making land useable for economic activity.
