Needs

Labena is one of local NGOs working in Central Darfur, Blue Nile, Kassala , South Kordofan and Khartoum supporting the people in need and working with WASH partners to response to the emergencies in its operation areas. Central Darfur Azum locality have been identified as gap area in access to WASH services due to the limited number of agencies working there. 2600 IDPs and 2500 vulnerable resident are targeted .In Nertiti locality 3000 IDPs are targeted. concerning Rokoro locality, 3000 IDPs and 5000 vulnerable resident are targeted. In Kassala state ,7000, 8000 and 8432 vulnerable residents are targeted from Kassala, Rural Kassala and Wad El Helew localities respectively.In South Kordofan , project targete ,Abu jubaiha(1400 IDPs and 1400 vulnerable residents), Al Qoz locality(161 vulnerable resident), Kadugli(600 IDPs and 1400 vulnerable residents). In Blue Nile state ,five localities are targeted,Bau(1400 IDPs),El Damazine (3600 IDPs and 7000 vulnerable residents),El Kurmuk(800 IDPs and 2000 vulnerable residents),El Roseires(2000 IDPs and 2000 vulnerable residents), Geissan (1400 IDPs and 2000 vulnerable residents). As food security situation is a key determinant for the WASH intervention, According to Integrated food security phase classification (IPC) projection for January to March 2019., 4,6 million people in Blue Nile, White Nile, South Kordofan, Kassala, Gedarif, Red Sea and Darfur are in IPC phase 3( 20% food insecure). Households used unprotected water sources with a high risk of contamination due to open defecation practices. Hygiene practices like hand-washing, sanitary collection and storage of drinking water were rarely practiced. In these villages, agricultural and health interventions were threatened by lack of improved water resources. Labena proposes to address sanitation gaps through CLTS campaign. Labena will focus on providing a full package of WASH interventions and will adopt a participatory approach, mobilizing communities with different ages and gender in the design and planning of WASH activities. The project will take into account the specific needs of each individual of the community women,men,boys ,girls and elders. while giving special consideration to the most vulnerable, including women and children.The needs, roles and dynamics of women, girls, boys and men in different age groups are understood.In the target states, women and vulnerable persons have to go far from the homestead to defecate. This poses great challenges to them in terms to their personal security and makes them susceptible to sexual abuse. With improved sanitation, they shall be able to access latrines even at night within the homestead. Bringing WASH facilities closer to homesteads ensures that the old and sick are able to access these facilities without straining themselves by walking far distances. When drinking water become available for all we can ensure that ,Women, girls, boys and men in different age groups get the right resources.
