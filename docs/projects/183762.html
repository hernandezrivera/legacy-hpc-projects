Project Summary

Libya’s economy has been decimated by conflict and instability over the past decade, with many years witnessing a contraction of the economy. While there was positive growth between 2017 and 2019, the impact of COVID-19 and the 11-month blockade of oil facilities resulted in negative growth of -31.3 per cent in 2020 to US$ 37.8 billion. The annual growth rate of GDP employed person also declined from 24 per cent in 2017 to -20.7 per cent in 2019. Poor economic performance has also had negative consequences for employment opportunities. Total labour force participation (ages 15—64) has been steadily increasing over the past decades, reaching 52.8 per cent in 2019, although the rate of increase has slowed in recent years. However, the participation rate for women stood at just 36.5 per cent in 2019 compared to 68.8 per cent for men.
With Libya in a state of protracted conflict and in a fragile context, WFP aims to expand the life-changing programmes that would support the resilience of vulnerable communities across Libya, while addressing the immediate humanitarian needs with the life-saving assistance.
The reduction in available migrant labour, both skilled and unskilled, combined with the instability of Government salaries and the impact of COVID-19 pandemics, has left many Libyan households reliant on coping mechanisms in order to meet their basic food needs. In this context, WFP Libya will continuously scale up the Food Assistance for Training projects, matching people in need with livelihoods and business skills and eventually decent works in the local job markets.
Conditional food assistance will be provided to the project participants and their household members as long as minimum attendance requirements are met, giving an immediate boost to food security. After the training, if successful, food security levels should be improved, with less reliance on coping strategies. Household resilience to future shocks should also be improved due to an improved asset base through greater skills, tools, and savings.
