Project Summary

This project is aligning with cluster objectives that aiming to support newly displaced.‎
individuals in open spaces, public buildings, and camps, as well as those residing with
friends/relatives, including populations in transit at border points or within the country.‎
with emergency shelter and basic NFIs to mitigate the negative impact of the war, and other ‎crises and ensure preparedness to people affected by crisis, ensure people affected by crisis
receives timely shelter solutions and NFI assistance to reduce mortality and morbidity,‎
mitigate health threats, and improve living conditions, and to improve access to shelter.‎
solutions for volunerable people to support the transition to recovery. The project
will target 130,960 (33,369 girls, 30,802 boys, 35,399 women, and 31,391 men) individuals
including 1911 elderly and 490 people with disabilities in need living in 29 localities within
‎12 Save the Children priority states including North Kordofan, North Darfur, South
Kordofan, Centeral Darfur, West Darfur, Khartoum, Gazira, Gedaref, River Nile,‎
White Nile, Sennar, and Blue Nile. The project will be focusing in‎
mitigating the negative impact of displacement associated with the conflict and the fighting ‎through community sensitization, capacity building and training for affected communities to ‎reduce the impact of the war, provision of timely Shelter solutions and NFI assistance for new ‎and protracted displacement people in addition to hosting communities to reduce mortality ‎and morbidity, mitigate health threats, and improve living conditions through provision and ‎distribution of essential NFI items, and distribution of life-saving emergency shelter items and ‎materials in addition to life-saving non-food items, shelter repairs, as well as provision of ‎cash/voucher for NFIs, and Shelter,‎
in addition to cash for rent. The project will also focus on supporting transition, recovery
and reintegration of returnees, volunerable and protracted households, and protracted.‎
IDPs through provision of transitional shelter items, cash for shelter, cash for non-food
items, more over the project will be supporting prepositioning of NFIs and shelter items
and materials to support timely response for anticipated and potential hazards. The
project will work to ensure cross cutting issues have been addressed through ensuring
households have sufficient living space, in addition to ensure as part of accountability to
affected populations; complains and feedback mechanisms are in place. The project will
be implemented in 12 months with estimated budget at $ $15,923,333.‎

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

The project designed to consider protection issues, concerns and the needs ofwomen, men, girls and boys, in addition to Persons with Special Needs (PSN) andother vulnerable groups. Shelters will be designs in consultation with women aswomen in most cases are responsible for erecting and taking down traditionalshelters. Shelters should therefore be designed in consultation with women, inaddition to assessing transport of materials from distribution site to site of shelters.‎The project will assess households with disabled persons or pregnant women andidentify any need for adjustment of shelter materials. Exploitation and S/GBV can beserious threats in IDP settlements; SCI will advocate for or provide fixed lighting,‎flashlights and/or whistles to increase personal safety. Save the Children will work toraise the awareness of community leaders on the importance of includingmarginalized groups, including unaccompanied children, the chronically ill or disabled,‎and child/elderly/disabled and vulnerable single headed households. Work withcommunity structures to identify these people within the community and ensure theirinclusion. During data collection, registration and verification process, SCI will becollecting data on age and sex of household head, presence of unrelated children orindividuals with chronic illness or disability, in addition to analyzing the collected datato ensure whether or not particularly vulnerable children and households are beingreached. The project will ensure adherance to child safe guarding protection, and corehumanitarian principles during implementation of the project to ensure all communitymembers and especially children were protected, not harmed by our actions. Theproject considers conflict sensitivity by understanding the situation, examining howeach activity may influence the conflict and work to maximize the positive andminimize the negative impacts of the proposed interventions. Both the newly IDPs andhost communities will benefit from the project interventions. Save the Children willstrive to ensure that the proposed interventions will minimize unforeseen orunintended negative effects on the targeted population which could potentiallyexpose them to further physical and psychosocial risks. Save the Children will workclosely with its various stakeholders (community members, government partners,‎local NGOs) to analyze the security situation and to avoid tensions between hostcommunities and IDPs through its holistic approach to projects whereby both old andnew IDPs as well as host communities are targeted based on the prioritization ofneeds.‎

4.1.1 - How will the project use community feedback to improve and adapt activities? (open-ended/free text, required)4

Save the Children will establish community feedback and compliant system in the project location using different mechanisms including hot lines, suggestions books, and through group meetings with different community entities. MEAL and accountability team receives the feedback and compliant and upload on the accountability system and create action trackers for all type of feedback based on the sensitivity and urgency, then shared with implementation team for actions, and with SMT for follow ups. As soon as the feedback actioned, the accountability team feedback the communities or the people who provided the feedback on the status of implementation, some of the actions could be implemented soon based on the availability of resources and information. Actions with no response either because of unavailability of resources or out of scope of the project will be recorded and kept with program and business development team to be consider in future projects.

Thisinformation will be communicated in the local language and medium that are easilyunderstood, respectful and culturally appropriate for different members of thetargeted communities, especially vulnerable and marginalized groups.
