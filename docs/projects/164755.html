Project Summary

The primary objective of the IMRC is to provide critical information management support
and capacity building to responding actors to facilitate the enhancement of the
coordination mechanism and further develop information management capabilities of
response actors leading to better identification of the needs and gaps of humanitarian
assistance inside Syria.
