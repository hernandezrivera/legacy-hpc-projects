Project Summary

According to Food Security and Nutrition Analysis FSNAU report in September 2019, 2.1 million people are expected to face food consumption gaps, high acute malnutrition, or depletion of assets indicative of Crisis (IPC Phase 3). The Gu (April-June) cereal harvest was far below average, prices of local cereal remain well above average, and substantial livestock losses have occurred, all of which have lowered household access to food and income. Persistent drought has led to large-scale population displacement. Garbaharey and Elwak districts were identified in Emergency (IPC3/Phase 4)..The drought and poor rains have contributed to severe water shortages, poor livestock performance, reduced access to milk, increasing malnutrition, surging food prices and water. Hence Severe shortage of water, poor hygiene and sanitation practices are wide spread among the rural villages in the two districts. Dry weather conditions are expected to continue to worsen the situation as we enter the hot and dry Jilaal (January-March) season 2020.Scaled up humanitarian assistance must be sustained in order to prevent further deterioration of food security and nutrition situation of the affected population.. ARD has conducted assessment in the regions of Gedo of south central somalia. The available water sources of surveyed areas are water catchments and view unprotected shallow wells, thus target HH don’t have access to clean water. the findings of the assessment included that the functioning boreholes could not cover the scale of water needs of the community and more than 70% of the affected people cannot afford the cost of the water as cost of water prices increased 70% s from approximately (0.7 dollars) per drum to almost 1.19 dollars (26 Sh Somali ). 75% of the shallow wells need rehabilitation, and 68% of the people practice open defecation and have limited knowledge on AWD transmission and prevention. Majority of the households are female headed. Thus, Women and girls have the primary responsibility for water collection. often take on a journey’s of more than 40 minutes walks for water collection and become exposed and risk for gender-based violence instances and acts. The lack of access to clean water and sanitation facilities will likely result in health and high levels of waterborne diseases may likely increase the prevalence of morbidity in children. Moreover, women and children have been hardest hit, with women shouldering increased care burdens and girls dropping out of school and facing early marriage, Outbreaks Acute Watery Diarrhea (AWD), Sexual Transmitted Infection(STI) are a major concern. To improve the health and well-being of affected communities in Garbaharey and Elwak districts, ARD proposes the following interventions

Project Activities

1. Rehabilitate/construct 8 flood-proof shallow wells with animal troughs benefiting 4000 affected people at target districts (4 in Elwak and 4 in Garbaharey districts). The action include deepening the well 3m, to increase the water yield at minimum water head of 3m, purchasing and lining concrete rings for the well walls, Construction of Reinforced concrete apron of 1.5m radius, construction 5m long drainage channel Provision of 1m3 soakage pit filled with large stones, where applicable (permeable soils), Construction of 1.5 deep sanitary seal made of cement grout, Construction 50cm high head wall with stone masonry and construction of animal trough. All the concrete works should be 1:2:4 cured for 7 days, and finally Shock chlorination after works as completed with at least 50mg/land. Women and girls will be consulted the design and place of the water source rehabilitation for the safe protection of women and girls2. Improve accessibility of safe drinking water through upgrading one boreholes (including a communal borehole) benefiting in Gedo region districts 6,000 persons. the actions include installation of water tank (e.g.18 cubic meter), water kiosk with 6 taps, generator and store rooms.ARD will ensure that affected persons should be satisfied the design of the project and improve ownership and utilization of safe drinking water3. ARD will carry out comprehensive hygiene and sanitation promotion awareness campaigns to promote the adaption of good hygiene and sanitation practices to reach 10,000 affected men, women and children. ARD will train 8 community hygiene promotion members on best practices of hygiene and sanitation to continue the awareness campaigns after the face out of the project. Trained hygiene promotors consisting women and men will work closely with the community members to highlight their important role in preventing the spread of diarrhea disease at the household level.4. ARD will distribute HP kits to 1500 households who benefited from the water sources rehabilitated and at risk of AWD/cholera diseases. 5. Increase access to sanitation facilities to the population benefited (IDPs and host communities) by the water services through rehabilitation of 40 flood proof desludging latrines in IDP camps (benefiting 1200 people) and construct twin latrine in schools benefiting 300 school children(200 boys, 100 girls)

Mitigation Measures

mitigation measures : and security measures were taken into account by close consultation with women in the program design like latrines and water supply to be constructed close to their settlements. This project is totally Addresses the needs of rehabilitating water points at reasonable distance from the houses for easily accessible to safe and reliable water, instead of women and girls walk long distance for searching safe and reliable water (women and girls are responsible for fetching water to their households, where sometimes they meet with GBV incidences) and constructing gender sensitive latrines with lockable doors, instead of practicing open defecation during night time; this will reduce the exposure of women and girls to SGBV, The overall goal of the proposed project is to reduce the burden of women and time spent in search of WASH services regarding dangers of long distance or secluded places. Womens’ participation in planning, implementation and evaluation will be ensured significantly in water, hygiene and sanitation projects. Women will be encouraged to actively get involved in WASH committee. Toilets/latrines with lockable doors and ensures privacy • Toilets/latrines lighted during night • Toilets/latrines equipped with menstrual hygiene management facilities.
