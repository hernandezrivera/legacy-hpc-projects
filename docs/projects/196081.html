Project Summary

DRC WASH intervention includes both lives–saving WASH assistance to better resist the threats posed by conflicts and further impacts if utilities have stronger resilience capacities.
The conflict-affected population is assisted in compliance with the Sector and MoLWR standards. DRC aims to address overburdened communities with a high ratio of returnees and IDPs in the rehabilitation of sewage and water systems. Water supply will better resist the threats posed by conflicts and further impacts if utilities have stronger resilience capacities. Protecting a system against damage to social, environmental and economic components is a prerequisite for resilience. Supplying everyone equitably with water services also helps build a stronger service system.
A total of 160,000 beneficiaries (Men 18-49 years 20%, Women 18-49 years 25% , Boys 0-17 years 25% , Girls 0-17 years 25%, Elderlis above 60 5%) will be reached out in different locations with a 3 and above severity scale during 2023 and will allow beneficiaries to have access to access to improved sanitation and drinking water in 2023.DRC will achieve this through the following outcome: Newly displaced IDP s and returnees living in life-threatening emergency conditions and/or impacted by covid-19 or cholera Response have access to basic WASH assistance in Idleb, Aleppo, Dara'a, Rural Damascus, Hama and Homs. DRC will prevent, mitigate, and respond to risks aggravated by the Cholera Outbreak , contributing to a strengthened protective environment for conflict and displacement affected individuals that are facing a sudden onset, a recent emergency that gives rise to an urgent WASH risk that they are unable to overcome.
DRC will respond to emergencies where sudden population movements cause conflict-affected populations to be living in conditions posing immediate life-threatening risks as well as address needs arising due to damages in the WASH infrastructure .
IDPs assistance following WASH Sector Standards will be provided in private unfinished/demolished buildings in both, urban and rural areas. Newly displaced populations get prioritized as an emergency response including Cholera Outbreak response, according to the Sector’s directive. Prioritization is set on water supply, water storage, and provision of sanitary facilities also considering the additional needs of disabled populations. The Identified locations are in huge need of borehole rehab, pump stations, clean water networks, and sewage network rehab for the long term, and during emergencies, the response will be water tanks, water trucking HK distribution, repair of the partially damaged water system and hygiene promotion.
DRC will undertake the repair of conflict-damaged WASH infrastructure, enabling access of the conflict-affected population to safe water for drinking and domestic use, and safe excreta disposal. Advocacy efforts will be mainstreamed through this action.
DRC will be implementing a resilience project to address the root causes of agriculture under performance. Focusing on sustainable natural resources and farmers’ access to water for production in order to support local food production and generate regular incomes to enhance access to basic education, health services and other needs.
Furthermore, working towards sustainability and equity goals will help build stronger resilience. As per sustainable objectives, protecting a system against damage to social, environmental and economic components is a pre-requisite for resilience. Supplying everyone equitably with water services also helps build a stronger service system.
Estimated % of secured budget is 60% during 2023.

3 - Needs

The conflict in Syria is now entering its second decade, and the number of people relying on humanitarian aid for survival continues to grow, a decade of conflict in Syria has displaced a total of 6,845,888 individuals, averaging around 1.4 million displaced per year inside and outside of the country. People in Need are 12.2 million of WASH assistance, Out of Which 5,000,443 are in need (In Rural Damascus 1,740,081 M, In Daraa 680,951 K, In Der Ez Zour 622,431 K, In Idleb 2,637,931 M).According to the comprehensive Shelter and WASH assessments that were conducted in January 2022 by the DRC team, the results of the conducted needs assessment shows the following:- Residents in targeted areas are depending on water trucking, which turns out not only expensive but also unsafe due to the absence of water tests and water quality control, exposing households to the risk of disease. - WASH needs including Waste management and availability of safe water in schools, remain very high and could be linked with dysfunctional public water and sanitation systems the facilities are connected to.- Individuals are in need of Early recovery assistance including community infrastructure rehabilitation and in need to improve agricultural production via the improvement of irrigation canals.

4 - Implementation of activities

DRC will repair conflict-damaged WASH infrastructure, enabling access of the conflict-affected population to safe water for drinking and domestic use, and safe excreta disposal. -Sanitation: Rehabilitation of sewage system: Encompasses repair of manhole and replacing damaged sections of piping in areas of intervention.-Water supply; Rehabilitation of water network Includes repair of water infrastructure, damaged sections of water piping, fixing leaking in the network pipes inside and outside buildings. Generators for the water systems that will be rehabilitated will be provided in coordination with local authorities and other actors in the area such as ICRC and UNICEF.-Infrastructure for agriculture: Repair of the partially damaged water system and infrastructure of the agriculture projects at the community levels. -Repair, rehabilitation and operational support to sanitation/sewage and solid waste management systems.-Rehabilitation of WASH facilities at schools, and providing essential WASH items to the students through a hygiene promotion campaign on AWD/ Cholera key messages related to symptoms, transmission and prevention through direct awareness sessions and leaflet distribution.procurement rules.- install solar energy for boreholes as alternative source of power.- Debris Removal at construction and operations sitesImplementation will be done by DRC directly through selected suppliers in line with DRC’s global

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

- Increased risk of inter or intra-community tension due to varied assistance packages, unclear beneficiary selection criteria, or poorly timed distributions leading to a feeling of discrimination. LH: HIGH, IMPACT: MIDUM, Mitigating Measure: Ensure beneficiary selection criteria are uniform/agreed upon among sector members and with the government and are communicated widely within a community and amongst different groups. Engage with community leaders, to raise awareness in the community. Establish complaint mechanisms at the community level- Available means of transporting water do not support equality and accessibility of water distribution for vulnerable groups. Likelihood: Medium. Impact: High. Mitigation: Ensure agreed context/SPHERE standards and closely monitor these aspects during implementation; Consult separately girls, boys, women and men, including older people and those with disabilities in the assessment, design and implementation phases; Distribute smaller water containers more convenient for women and elderly people; Consider specific support to people with disabilities to ensure they access sufficient quantity of water;


8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Water distribution not accessible, especially for women, girls (exposure to GBV risk) and children elderly and people with disabilities: LH: high, Impact: high, mitigation measures: Ensuring agreed context/SPHERE standards and closely monitor these aspects during implementation; Consulting separately girls, boys, women, and men, including older people and those with disabilities in the assessment, design implementation phases; Establish complaint & feedback mechanism; Monitor safe and equitable access and use of water distribution points. DRC planed to do HK or water distribution at the Household level .Toilets present GBV and/or health, safety risk; Access to toilet is not available to all 24/7: LH: high, Impact: high - mitigation measures: Ensuring agreed context/SPHERE standards and closely monitor these provisions/aspects during implementation. Ensuring consulting women and adolescent girls during the design, implementation, and monitoring phase of projects, SWM: Movements of garbage trucks in congested, camp like settings; Unreliability of services. Sector staff protect the beneficiaries dignity by taking actions through the implementation, Separating the distribution lines (dignity basket), latrines…,etc, Female and Male staff presence in the field while interacting directly with beneficiaries;Oblige the contractors to follow WASH sector partners’ Code of Conduct by always including it in Sector contracts; Install rewarding system.


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1. Non-availability of good performing contractors - Maintaining a good relationship to good performing contractors, contractor evaluation for procurement processes 2. Expanding sanctions of key construction material to Syria - Discussing the new development within the TWG of both Sectors 3. The long approval process for work execution - Considering the long approval process in the work plan, advocacy, and awareness-raising in the sector coordination mechanism, maintaining a good relationship with the ministries in the field 4. Increasing inflation and high depreciation of Syrian Pound with consequent increase of prices in the market. Contractors may be affected by the constant increase of price and withdraw from their contractual obligation, creating a delay in the implementation of the activities. 5. Non-acceptation of DRC delivered work by beneficiaries - Remaining in compliance with Sector’s guidelines, informing in advance local authorities and beneficiaries on the planned intervention, referring to MEAL findings of previous similar projects, feedback mechanism.
