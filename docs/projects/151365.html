Project Summary

FSNAU report indicates that food security situation in Somalia in worsening, still acute food insecurity, severe malnutrition and livelihood needs remain unchanged, 2.6 million people are displaced persons. The seasonal assessment indicates that severe level of food insecurity and malnutrition withing the IDP camps. However, Green Hope conducted assessments to identify the nature and needs within the IDP camps in Mogadishu and the outskirt most of the displaced persons are traditional farmers fled from Qoryoley, Kurtunwarey, Bariire, Awdegle and Afgoye districts in Lower shabelle, and Burhakaba and Baidoa districts in Bay region. Majority of the IDPs are destitute Women, young females, disables and children, and found that 48 persons are women-led households, malnutrition rate is honestly high, yet NO services provided. Thus, Green Hope targets a number of 3,800 households in both Zona K and Tabellaha with unconditional support, and prioritize safety and dignity and avoid causing harm, and mainstream all protection document

Needs

Green Hope together with the regional administration conducted rapid needs assessment conducted rapid assessment in Mogadishu and the outskirt at 6 to 8 of October targeting two main areas one in zona K under hodan district and another one in Tabellaha area under the Dharkinley district with a total IDP camps of 18 al of them are newly arrived people from Qoryoley, Kurtunwarey, Bariire, Awdhegle and Afgoye districts in Lower shabelle, women, young girls, disables, elderly and children are the most affected ones \, they are all poor farmers but faced problems and shocking food insecurity and left behind their livelihood assets, the assessment indicates that women-led households and households with malnourished children is considerable number, there are no Health and Nutrition, Food security, WASH activities in both areas our NGO raised the issue at the Banadir Food security cluster, given the fact that their situation is life threatening, women use small job for a living for washing clothes to get very small amount of money and sometime they are threatened simply because of leaving their pay, they live in an IDP camps with a damaging morale. assorted humanitarian problems continue as to undermine opportunities for food security improvements. However, Green Hope proposes to provide livelihood assistance for a number of 3,500 households with unconditional transfer modality (food and cash) to mitigate the effects of man made disaster. According to FSNAU report indicates that 2.7M are in severe levels of acute food insecurity and malnutrition .

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

Beneficiaries will be assisted any risks associated and damages relates to the diminished capacity of individuals, groups, or households to anticipate with, and recover from the impact of a disaster. Individuals, groups and households differ in their exposure to and ability to cope with or recover from disasters. Social characteristics such as gender, ethnic identity, age, disability, sexual orientation, and other factors sometimes expose people to different risks, or affect the nature of coping options available to them.

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

Societies affected by, or recovering from, conflict often suffer a normalization of violence that needs to be addressed in order to secure a stable and peaceful path towards recovery and development. The longer conflicts run, the more complex they become and the harder they are to stop, This helps people identify and relieve tension, isolate key factors in conflict and employ successful mediation techniques.
