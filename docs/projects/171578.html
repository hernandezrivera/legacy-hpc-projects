Project Summary

The project aims to address the humanitarian challenges of a classified affected hard to reach populations in El-Afwein of Sanaag Region. The emergency nutrition proposal is intended to avert the present nutrition situation which is serious to critical with GAM rate of Malnutrition of 13.8%, meaning nearly one million children (boys and girls) are moderately malnourished. In East-Golis pastoral the GAM rate is 16.2% which is in IPC4 (Crisis). According to 30 Sept 2020 FSNAU Post Gu’ TR, 83,000 persons living in Sanaag region are projected to be in IPC4 (Crisis). This situation has been exacerbated due increased prizes of food and HH items such as medicines as a result of COVID-19 outbreak. There are 34.9 childhood morbidity rate in East Golis pastoral of Sanaag Region. The populations living in this region has been affected by several medium scale conflicts causing displacement of the already weakened and drought affected communities. The proposed intervention is combined emergency curative and preventive nutrition services to be in access to respond to the needs of malnutrition affected women, children, youth, and men along with pregnant and lactating women, elderly and persons with disabilities. This will be achieved through recruiting and training Emergency nutrition teams on standard Somalia IMAM and IYCF tools with the purpose of early detection and treatment of under 5 children with moderate and severe acute malnutrition. Thereafter, the project will help and give integrated services including screening, identifying, and treating and referring children with moderate and severe acute malnutrition. The pregnant/lactating women will receive multiple micro-nutrient supplementation, while benefiting Infant Young Child Feeding (IYCF) counseling services. Breastfeeding promotion is provided throughout the course of the project. Children and mothers by referring to the nearest Health facility. In protection mainstreaming, HEAL fully involves IDPs, including from marginalized communities in the design, implementation, monitoring and evaluation of the projects.
1. The project will support 4 mobile teams in El-Afwein District with linkage to existing health facilities of El-Afwein MCH, Garadag MCH, Huluul MCH.
2. The identification, referral and treatment of 5,605(6-59 months) children suffering from acute malnutrition through OTP/TSFP and community based approaches.
3. The project will strengthen local technical capacity for integration of Integrated Management of Acute Malnutrition (IMAM) into the Primary Health Care system through utilization of Basic Nutrition Services Package (BNSP). 3. Strengthening supply chain management, well-trained health workers, financing, and use of existing community based nutrition programs in order to scale-up prevention, early identification and treatment of acute malnutrition of children and Pregnant and Lactating (PLW).
4. Service delivered will be Maternal and child nutrition components including IMAM, IYCF, NHHP, referral system, Ready to Use Therapeutic Food (RUTF) supplies, mitigation of COVID-19, nutrition surveillance and as well as mobilizing, educating and creating demand for service uptake.
5. Preventive measures are, de-worming, counseling and promotion on infant and young child feeding including Infant and Young Child Feeding (IYCF) in emergencies. Community mobilization, screening and referral, micro-nutrient supplementation for PLW (multiple micro-nutrients and Iron Folate), Expanded Program on Immunization (EPI) and U5 (Vitamin A supplements) for children reinforced with Communication for development (C4D). Also Personal protection of staff using PPE.
6. Prevention services will include COVID-19 include Dissemination of key information to parents, dispelling misconceptions during infant and young child nutrition (IYCN) due to COVID-19, and the national recommendations on preventing and controlling COVID-19 (the 6 signs and the 6 prevention measures)
