Project Summary

Purpose: Humanitarian coordination, information sharing, and INGO collective advocacy are enhanced through the work of the Libya INGO forum.
The INGO Forum in Libya was formed in late 2016 to develop a collaborative platform for effective and principled INGO interaction, engagement and coordination for humanitarian interventions in Libya.
Since inception, the INGO Forum is growing to become a critical platform for ensuring effective information sharing and analysis; leadership on advocacy and policy engagement; and liaison with the Libyan authorities, the United Nations and the international community and engagement ensuring the perspectives of INGOs and affected populations are ultimately included in decision-making processes.
The INGO Forum is governed by a core membership of 18 members (and 5 observers) with new member applications pending approval on a regular basis. The INGO Forum is governed by an elected Steering Committee of five Country Directors that works alongside a full-term Director. Since February 2020, the INGO Forum is administratively hosted by NRC in Tunis, Tripoli and Benghazi.

1 - Needs

The initial motive behind the creation of Libya INGO forum (LIF), beyond the need of improved inter-INGO coordination, information sharing and sought for synergies, was to create a mechanism that could also convey common advocacy messages and collective action at INGO level and ensure a strong, principled and vocal representation vis à vis all stakeholders. Members of the LIF have expressed a need for increased INGO coordination on operational matters, administrative-related issues, security matters, and advocacy for improved humanitarian space and response. As the operating environment remains in Libya extremely challenging, the partially remote management of the humanitarian response is likely to continue. In a context of shrinking humanitarian space, exacerbated by the COVID-19 crisis, a collective approach to humanitarian access is needed more than ever in Libya. As some agencies start obtaining the necessary authorizations and clearances to return to Libya, they need support to undertake the administrative procedures needed for registration, bank accounts, office venues, etc. This is important as procedures are continuously changing with little accountability from the authorities and no clearly identified interlocutors given the fragmentation of the institutions and political landscape in Libya. It is of utmost importance to continue to improve coordination between all INGO interventions to guarantee their efficiency, draw synergies between actions and renew efforts to collectively carry and advocate for a principled intervention. LIF is in a strong position to continue to facilitate linkages, coordination and information sharing among INGOs in Libya which will directly serve vulnerable affected population in Libya.

2 - Activities

The Forum Secretariat facilitates information sharing, mutual understanding and common positioning among all members with the aim of promoting effective intervention and for the betterment of serving populations in need in Libya. The Forum focuses its efforts through four main areas: coordination, representation, advocacy, and information-sharing. CoordinationThe Forum Secretariat facilitates information sharing and mutual understanding among our members and organises regular meetings between member country directors, HR, admin and finance teams, security focal points and advocacy leads. We also act as a focal point for external actors to develop effective relationships between INGOs and different stakeholders. RepresentationWe consistently represent our members in external meetings and coordination mechanisms, and ensure their inclusion in critical humanitarian decision-making platforms. We aim at being an interface between the LIF members and all actors involved in the Libya response, starting with the Libyan authorities, the members of the international community, and all agencies mandated to support crisis-affected populations in Libya. AdvocacyWe mobilise the LIF members on collective positioning on critical humanitarian issues to support engagement with donors, UN agencies, policy makers and other relevant stakeholders at national, regional and international levels. We strive to create an enabling environment for INGOs to work collectively and with a unified voice. Information sharing and knowledge managementWe ensure an effective and transparent sharing of essential information on humanitarian needs, priorities and coordination, relevant to both our members and external stakeholders, and generate data and analysis on NGOs’ work in Libya.
