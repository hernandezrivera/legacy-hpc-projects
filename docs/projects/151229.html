Project Summary

The project will be implemented directly HIRDA organization in Bardhere, Garbaharey and Belet Hawa district in Gedo region Somalia. Through this project, HIRDA will be able to prove emergency primary health care services both MCH/HC facility as well as the mobile clinic, HIRDA will ensure provide quality treatment on communicable and non communicable diseases as well as immunization of children and mothers as well as provision of vaccine-preventable disease,Through this project HIRDA will ensure capacity development of staff on integrated management of childhoods illness,community awareness and EPI services.HIRDA will ensure to coordinate with MOH Jubaland regional state as well as MOH federal government as well health cluster and other humanitarian actors.However, this project will contribute towards the cluster strategic objective to ensure equal and integrated lifesaving assistance to children and people living in humanitarian emergency and crisis to reduce mortality and destitution. The project seeks to strengthen and provide support the vulnerable children, women and men to enable ensure all gender inclusivity within the program. The program shall also coordinates with other humanitarian actors in the region and more so the Garbaharey and Bardhere district in which HIRDA remain commitant in delivering coordinated health, nutrition and livelihood response. The project will ensure coordination with other clusters and agencies with similar activities and also ensure gender is mainstreamed throughout the implementation

Needs

Somalia is experiencing protracted emergency leading towards a deteriorating humanitarian due to long term conflict,drought and other natural hazards, More than 3.1 million people in drought/flash floods affected areas in need of health services, including 500,000 children under 5 and 575,000 women of child bearing age. In 2018, Over 78,000 of Acute Watery Diarrhea (AWD) cases were reported and 535 of these died with case fatality of 3.5%. 58% of the cases were children under 5 years of age. In southern regions of Somalia in particular Gedo, more than 687 Acute Watery Diarrhea AWD/cholera cases have been reported (Health Cluster Bulletin, 2018).According to the Somali Health Cluster, Somalia's health sector remains in critical condition with one of the worst health indicators in the world and the 7th largest internally displaced people. The under five mortality rate is 137 per 1,000 live births while approximately 732 women per 100,000 live births die from pregnancy or childbirth related complications. An estimated 3.3 million people are in need of health services throughout 2018.The country has significant high maternal mortality 700 per 100,000 live births, high newborn mortality 94 per 1000 live births.Key challenges facing the Somali health sector include persistently high burden of disease, limited institutional capacity and stewardship role of ministries of health, inadequate, unpredictable and unsustainable level of financing, with a high share of out of pocket spending on health, absence of balanced, motivated, well distributed and well managed health workforce with the appropriate skills mix; limited and unequal access to essential health services, and poor quality and safety of services across all levels of care, weak births and deaths registration, limited operational research and disease surveillance, lack of synergy of humanitarian response to health; and inadequate action on social determinants of health,therefore, this project will address that gap.

Activities

1. Provision of three emergency health clinics to vulnerable women and children in Bardhere, Balad Hawa and Garbaharey district Gedo region. 2. Provision of immunization services to children under five and pregnant and lactating women attending MCH, mobile teams and hard to reach areas campaigns. 3. Accelerated response to epidemics and communicable diseases outbreaks by conducting rapid assessments; implementation of lifesaving services; joint field monitoring, coordination, and reporting

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

In implementing the health service provision in highly risk and unpredictable areas there might be different factors that might led the risk of exclusion based on age and gender in circumstance such exclusion may include sexual exploitation among the female health employee, exclusion on job opportunities, discrimination against minorities, physically challenge person, poor representation among the affected people, poor feedback mechanism as well as lack of code of conduct, monitoring and reporting channel, such risk factors might hinder the actual program implementation purpose thus led it to ineffectiveness in reaching the affected people.To mitigate this HIRDA intends to established clear feedback mechanism for complaints during such instance on sexual exploitation as well as poor health staff conduct that calls for unprofessional behaviors in doing so HIRDA we provide the project beneficiaries with hotline/call center that shall be responsible by a compliant officers who is going to directly report to executive director for immediate action, it shall also create reporting and monitoring system that shall ensure properly documented project information as well as ensure all heath staff signed code of conduct that will be used against any such eventuality.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

The following risk of exclusion based on gender or age such factors are limited capacity among the trained female nurse, within the health and nutrition sector might hampered the inclusion of women in the nutrition and health staffing as well as management role hence the might provide room for men dominant. To mitigate this HIRDA shall ensure to capacitate the female nurses and community health workers and provide them equal chance on both management, community health committee as well as ensure their dignity and safety are preserved.Information gap, discrimination, gender disparities and infrastructural design of nutrition and health facilities environment further contribute the exclusion risk factors facing the target population to mitigate this HIRDA will embark on social mobilization and information dissemination through public Barraza radio show for larger coverage on community enlighten, hence this will provide better avenue for both behavior change communication and increasing the services provision for the affected population in both host communities and IDPs. HIRDA will also ensure to identify the most needy group and provide more responsive intervention by providing life-saving health and nutrition intervention/needs for both girls, boys, children with disabilities, minority communities, children belonging to disadvantaged or marginalized groups and work with community leaders to support equal and safe access to nutrition basic services package without discrimination.it will provide adequate community sensitization and monitoring and coordinating with stakeholders that will improve both facility based and outreach/mobile intervention, HIRDA however, intends to provide friendly OTP facility toilet as well as easy adaptation of these facilities by disable and physically impaired persons hence provide better facility that shall enable to provide adequate access and safe for the disadvantage group.

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

The following will be mitigation methods for identified armed conflict and violence that is:Landmine and explosive remnant of war – OTP facilities both static and outreach are susceptible for the risk related to landmines since children are the main beneficiaries in these centers, however, Somalia having many sites of such explosive due to its porous security situation but HIRDA will ensure safety and dignity is prioritized and provided with all necessary prevention of such landmine by ensuring it works closely with mine action agency in closer coordination with community leaders in mapping out the areas proposed for construction of static outpatient therapeutic sites as well ensure any such sites is free from an explosive areas Busy roads – this will also increase the risk of fatal accident among the already vulnerable malnourished children if it is not well taking care of, however, HIRDA will ensure its nutrition facilities are far from such areas for the safety of its beneficiaries to be more responsive for their safety by providing all inclusive conducive environment Within military camp – This areas are susceptible for attack and counter attack and hence might endanger the safety of beneficiaries, however, HIRDA will ensure its health and nutrition facilities are in far areas from any military base that would have otherwise endanger the life of already vulnerable population in needs, through the implementation of this project HIRDA will also ensure proper awareness are done with help of community leaders.
