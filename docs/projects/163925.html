Project Summary

Bonyan designed its inclusive education interventions to contributes in addressing the actual education needs in Syria and bridges the gaps in the education sector and comply with the strategic objectives of both the HRP and Education Cluster guided by an analysis of the severity of needs in accordance to assessment findings and key indicators.
the priority for Education intervention will respond to the education needs of children and youth in those areas facing the highest severity of needs.
Likewise, Bonyan 2020 Education program will focus to bring children back to formal education pathways through variety of interventions ensuring that they have access to lifelong learning. The response prioritizes vulnerable children including adolescents/youth, girls and boys living with disabilities, especially in hard-to-reach locations targeting total of 69,340 children (35840G & 33500B – 5000 CwD, 5-17 years old) with strong coordination with relative authorities (Turkish MONE, LC, ED) in Aleppo, and Idleb Gov
** Obj.1: Scale up safe and equitable access to formal and non-formal education for crisis-affected children and youth aged 5-17 years
Starting with back-to-learning campaigns targeting children with their parents to raise awareness of importance of education, address parents’ concerns, and outreach for the established education services including distribution of School kits for children, teacher, and schools including hygiene and recreational kits. Total target is 39,340 children (19474G /19866B, 5-17 years old) and their parents and surrounded communities. Distributing supportive items plans which includes Schools’ kits, teachers’ kits, students’ kits
Schools and desk maintenance activity will be conducted in order to expand the capacity of the educational facilities to host more children, provide equity services for both females and males, and children with disabilities and provide better education, targeting 62 schools~29500 children and 10,000 desks~30000 children
Providing operation costs for 27 formal education school targeting 11,340 children (6340G/5000B, 5-17 years old). non-formal education is tailored by age and needs in order to fulfill the gaps for each targeted age and gender, 25 NFE centers will target 15,000 children (8000G/7000B, 8-12 years old) to provide literacy and numeracy along with PSS sessions. And 10 NFE centers will target 3,000 children (1500G/1500B, 12-15 years old) to provide Remedial program. Both FE and NFE centers will be provided with equipment, student and teachers kits, learning materials, operational consumables, and heating during winter season
As education is one of the very basic children’s rights, Bonyan is planning to provide access to education for children with disabilities by provide targeted assistance within the schools in order to encourage these children to continue their education and integrate them with their peers
** Obj.2: Enhance the quality of formal and non-formal education for children and youth aged 5-17 years within a protective environment
Building the capacity of 1100 teachers and educational actors on child centered, gender-sensitive inclusive education, codes of conduct, anti-bullying PSS, referral mechanisms, EiE, and INEE. Targeted training will be conducted for the in-school protection supervisors, and coordinators to improve project quality and compliance with protection principals and improve the established referral system
Bonyan will provide the schools incentives for the teachers and education actors along with teaching resources and supplementary kits during the whole academic year. And will integrate structured PSS (I Deal curriculum), Children’s rights promotion and life skills and citizenship programs within school activities. (refer for project 163930 for the detailed Protection intervention)

4 - Needs

According to HNO 2020, There is a total of 7-million people in need of education support; The total people in need that Whole of Syria aims to support is 6.8 million people most; 86% of PiN have acute and immediate need of humanitarian education assistance. The total number of people in need of education support in Aleppo 1.4M, and 940K in Idlib Bonyan participated in Joint Education Needs Assessment For Out of School Children along with 13 other NGOs. Targeting 112 communities in (86 city, and 26 camp) non-governmental areas of the Northwest of Syria. Bonyan Organization has conducted an educational needs assessment in October 2019, the survey targeted several main areas in Idleb governorate (Idleb – Ariha – Harim), as well as areas in western Aleppo countryside (Al-Atareb – Daret Azza-Afrin)The key ﬁndings:# of displaced children in all targeted areas increased by 41% of the total number of displaced children. The survey found that the total number of displaced and resident children is 249,537The number of displaced children before the last military campaign is 33,698 children divided as follows: 20,198 in Idleb (Idleb - Maaret Tamsrin - Bennsh- Harim) and 13,500 children in Aleppo (Daret Azza and Atareb)After the recent military campaign, the number of displaced children increased to 57,178 children divided as follows: 33,178 children in the schools targeted by the survey in Idleb and 24,000 in the schools targeted by the survey in Aleppo governorateThe dropout rate reached to 35% (24% girls & 11% boys) of the total number of children in Idleb assembly schools, this is highest dropout rate in the assessed areas, while the lowest dropout rate was 7% in Atareb assembly in Aleppo governorateThe top reasons for all genders and ages are child labor, early marriage, recruitment by armed groups, and displacement In Idleb and connected countryside 35% of the assessed schools considered provision of maintenance is the ﬁrst priority

5 - Implementation of activities

New modality will be applied in this project by linking non-formal centers with formal school. This will be achieved through the scaling up of equitable learning opportunities, enhancing the quality of teaching and learning, ensuring safer learning environments, and strengthening education systems at the community level 35 NFE center targeting 18,000 children (9500G/8500B, 8-17 years old) will operate on cycle bases three learning cycles (3 month each). Learning cycle includes BTL events at the beginning, and children transition to formal school at the end. months for one cycle can achieve a notable progress on children education status. if there is a child still require additional courses, will be reenrolled in the next cycle automatically 37 FE will be supported targeting 11,340 children (6340G/5000B, 5-17 years old) (SiG curriculum) this schools will be able to accept the new enrolment of children transitioned from the NFE center. Schools will undergo Inclusion program for children with disabilities by increasing schools’ capabilities through assigning inclusion supervisor, and structural modifications to school building. FE and NFE will include Daily structured PSS sessions following curriculum with life skills and citizenship programs 678 classrooms in addition to disability and gender sensitive WASH facilities in 62 schools will be rehabilitated in order to increase school capacities and provided more access to children BTLs will be conducted to increase awareness among parents on the importance of education. outreach teams will conduct awareness-raising activities to support enrolment, and sustain community support to education services in and around schools and build the resilience of communities to find solutions to local needs Provide professional development for 1100 teachers (550M/550W) on child-centered pedagogy, active learning, PSS, referrals, integrating CwD, EiE and INEE Conduct Monitoring and Quality control activities
