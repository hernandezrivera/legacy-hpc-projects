Project Summary

Water pollution and waterborne diseases are major obstacles to the protection of human health in lower Shabelle, Bay and Galgadud Regions. Widespread poverty, shortage of water and the precarious hygiene and sanitation situation are major factors of limitation for the improvements of livelihoods of both local communities and the IDPS. More sustainable solutions for water accessibility are needed for both displaced and non-displaced populations, given that it is also a key to combating the spread of diseases such as cholera and acute watery Diarrhea (AWD). The proximity of water sources and the availability of latrines is also a critical issue for women and girls to avert sexual and gender-based violence.
The project aims at improving living conditions for a significant number of vulnerable communities through improving access to safe drinking water for both human and livestock consumption, particularly during the long dry seasons. By providing sufficient and safe water, adequate water storage facilities and initiating behavioral change; the project will help to improve human health and livelihood sustainability.
NWO will hold at the inception of the project, community mobilization, and sensitization meetings with all stakeholders including the local authorities to officially launch the project. During the sensitization meetings, the project objectives, implementation strategies, scope, beneficiary selection criteria, beneficiary entitlement and roles and duties of each stake holders will be discussed. NWO will involve the target community through its leaders and engendered committees in the first stage of planning of the project to ensure community participation. NWO will ensure the location and access routes to WASH facilities and distribution points are safe. NWO will locate water sources in visible, central locations and not more than 500 meters from settlement; Latrines will be separated between female and male and will be lockable inside and outside to keep the privacy of women and girls.
The project will provide temporary safe water to 7200 (1200 HH); men (2419), women (3341) and children (720 boys and 720 girls) affected IDPs for 90 days (15 l/p/d) , will rehabilitate: 1 boreholes equipped with hybrid complete with 3 water tanks, 2 generator and storerooms benefiting 4800 men, women and children including people in disabilities and IDPs. The solar powered system will ensure cheaper and reliable access to safe water to the IDPs and host communities; Construct 1 water kiosk supplied water through piping system at reliable distance to 1 school benefiting 600 boys, girls, men and women. Will Construct 6 flood proof shallow wells equipped with solar power pumps complete with 6 elevated water tanks and 6 water kiosks, benefitting 12800 men, women, boys and girls including people with disabilities and IDPs. The project will chlorinate water sources in the area of implementation including the rehabilitated water sources on daily basis. The project will also train WASH committees’ members (28 men, 20 women) on better water source management and 6 Well operators (3 men, 3 women) will be trained on O&M and well chlorination. NWO will conduct DRR and conflict resolution mechanisms workshop to 100 community members (50 men and 50 women) NWO will build 6 twin block institutional latrines in 6 schools and 60 flood proof and gender sensitive latrines with lockable doors and hand washing facilities to IDPs and low income HHs to reduce open defecation and risk of GBV against women/girls in Galgudud. Bay and Lower Shabelle regions. In this project, NWO will provide 2750 Hygiene Promotion kits to 2750 families and IDPs who are at risk for AWD / cholera and malnutrition, NWO will monitor the quality and quantity of the WASH intervention during the implementation of the project and its achievement according to the plan.

Mitigation Measures

New ways will collect and use data dis-aggregated by sex, age and diversity to effectively inform programming. - New ways will ensure that assistance and services are reaching the most vulnerable - Identify and prioritize the most vulnerable groups in the community and prevent discrimination or exclusion of marginalized groups - New ways will ensure lack of documentation does not exclude individuals from accessing services - New ways will ensure the location, distance and access routes to the WASH facilities and services are accessible to all members of the community, including the most vulnerable groups. New ways will ensure that beneficiaries know of available WASH services and assistance where and how to obtain it, through various means to ensure communication reaches the broader community and consider literacy levels (eg. health community workers, door-to-door, poster, radio, social media, use of pictograms). - New ways will set up mechanisms for submitting feedback that do not require the beneficiary exposing themselves to project staff. - New ways will respond to complaints, regardless of whether corrective measures can/need to be put in place. - New ways will make sure staff and WASH committees will be representative of all groups within the community (e.g. gender, age, ethnicity, socioeconomic group, persons with disabilities) and all staff and members will receive protection mainstreaming training. - New ways will ensure all staff and volunteers working with affected populations understand, sign and adhere to a Code of Conduct stating their commitment to respect and foster humanitarian standards and the rights of beneficiaries, including the confidentiality of patients. NWO will Ensure the location and access routes to WASH facilities and distribution points are safe. NWO will Locate water sources in visible, central locations and not more than 500 metres from settlement; - Install lights near communal facilities. If lighting is not possible, NWO will consider coordinating with NFI partners for the provision of solar lamps / torches for each household. - Avoid placing facilities or distribution points near military installations or threats such as attacks stray bullets, crossfire or violence. Weapon bearers should not be present inside distribution points. - Introduce accompaniment arrangements to reduce threats to the safety and dignity of women and girls e.g. fetching water in groups. - Identify central locations for distribution points - beneficiaries should not have to traverse long distances or pass through conflict zones to access distribution points; Latrines will be separate between female and male and will be lockable inside and outside to keep the privacy of women and girls.

Please provide further explanation

NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS will work the Government in order to build a resilient and responsive system infrastructure. NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS will focus both on the everyday routine challenges that front line staff face on a regular basis as well as focus on sudden shocks such as disease outbreaks. NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS will work to build the leadership capacity of district and regional state representatives and in this regard will target Government key managers, field staff, community members as well as Community Resource Persons so as to empower them to solve problems at their level and build relationships and social networks. NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS will focus on strengthening existing social networks to provide access to multiple resources available in the community and forms of value such as community knowledge and reputation that can build and restore resilience to the existing systems. NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS will use the model of everyday resilience building in the systems of the Government through a combination of absorptive, adaptive and transformative strategies that authorities, staff and community members can use to adopt the system in responding to strains and shocks. This will help in producing a strengthened and more resourceful resilient system infrastructure. NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS will train the Local Authorities, field staffs, resource persons and community members on how to deal with sudden shocks such as outbreak of diseases. All the different strata of the community system will be taught how to manage such disease outbreaks at different levels in order to build a resilient and responsive infrastructure into every level. NEW WAYS ORGANIZATIONS will use the funding from donors as well as its own funds to ensure adequate resources and strong government system infrastructure is built to contribute to generating a resilient and responsive system in Galgaduud, Lower Shabelle and Bay regions.
