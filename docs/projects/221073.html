Project Summary

In response to the growing humanitarian crisis in Lakes State. WAV Gender Based Violence project plan will directly target 5600 vulnerable IDPs and host communities in CueIbet and Rumbek North counties with a total of 2600 females, 1,000 males,500 boys and 1500 girls 5% of the target population will include people living with disability, female headed households, pregnant and lactating women and the elderly .The project aims at providing quality GBV services to GBV survivors and reduce suffering of women, girls, men and boys by providing timely and integrated multi-sector assistance and services to reduce acute needs and promote and sustain their resilience to recurrent shocks. WAV proposes to implement comprehensive, lifesaving SGBV prevention and response activities by providing quality and timely, age appropriate, case management and psychosocial support to GBV survivors.
The prioritized objectives to achieve the overall goal will include:
Cluster Objective 1:Ensure mobile or static provision of critical protection assistance and services (lifesaving), including specialized response services. Prioritize a collaborative, child-focused approach, with the engagement of local communities, government, and partners to enhance effectiveness. Responses are tailored to address the specific needs of the individuals, considering age, gender, and disability.
Cluster Objective 2:Ensuring that human rights are upheld and promoted for the persons of concern by strengthening coordination, advancing centrality of protection among humanitarian actors to reduce protection risks, and addressing coordination gaps/issues through close dialogue with partners and effective protection monitoring to inform programming, advocacy, and response incorporates the voices of affected communities, particularly children, youth, and GBV survivors, at all Programme stages.
Cluster Objective 3: Promoting nexus to development and solutions as the transition between the delivery of emergency humanitarian assistance, systems building and the provision of long-term development assistance to meet peoples’ needs, mitigate risks and vulnerabilities, exacerbated by systemic and socially reinforced exclusion of women and girls and persons with disabilities.
This projects is designed to improve responses to cases of GBV and enhance the ability of
survivors to recover from GBV incidences.
The main activities to be carried out to realize these objectives and achieve the overall goal will include :
1. Provision of survivor-centered psychosocial support focused on healing, empowerment and recovery
2. Provision of appropriate GBV case management services including coordinated care and support
3. Establish and / or strengthen GBV referral systems and linking GBV survivors to available response services and support
4.Implement Women and Girls Friendly Space (WGFS) programming to provide services, information and activities that promote healing, well-being and empowerment
5.Conduct dignity kit distribution and programming targeting vulnerable women and girls as part of GBV risk mitigation
6. Conduct community awareness raising activities on GBV risk factors, mitigation measures and on how survivors can access support and services in line with the “do no harm” principle

1. Needs

In Lakes State Cuiebet and Rumbek North counties have an estimated 669,000 people (53% of the state’s population) were facing Crisis (cluster priority 2) ) (IPC September 2023- June 2024)or worse acute food insecurity between September and November 2023. 167,000 people are in (IPC priority Phase 2) acute food insecurity, and 502,000 people are in Crisis (IPC priority Phase 2) acute food insecurity. The drivers of food insecurity are high food prices, prolonged dry spells that contributed to poor agricultural production, depleted stocks from previous harvests, and low household incomes because of limited income-earning opportunities due to past floods that continue to hit Cuiebet and Rumbek North counties and leading to severe economic hardship as a result increasing the risk GBV cases among the IDPs, returnees and host communities. Some of the mitigating factors during this period are harvesting and sale of crops such as groundnuts, vegetables, green grams, and early maturing sorghum. During this period, all the counties are classified in (IPC priority Phase 2) acute food insecurity with Cuiebet and Rumbek North Counties also featured at IPC priority phase 2 due to GBV rising cases. Some of the mitigating factors during this period are harvesting and sale of crops such as groundnuts, vegetables, green grams, and early maturing sorghum which have dwindled in production resulting in poor or no harvest in the in Rumbek North and Cuiebet counties hence pushing the county to IPC level 2 Emergency. The joint agencies IRNA mission to Rumbek North and Cuiebet Counties took place in July 30 – 3, 2024. The key findings confirmed the displacement caused by drought coupled with series of floods in the previous years as the population moves in search of food .Those displaced were found to be in immediate need of food, educational services and protection particularly GBV services provision for women and adolescent girls who have become vulnerable during this crisis as they work hard to look for food for their families. Women Aid Vision (WAV) noted increase and targeted attacks against women and girls with growing levels of Sexual Gender-Based violence perpetrated towards the IDPs, returnees, and host community members in Cuibet and Rumbek North counties . Women and girls walk for long distances in search of food and wild fruits exposing them to the risks of GBV and a few households with food women reported encountering intimate partner violence (IPV) due to heightened pressure on men to provide food for their families. In the three Counties, women and girls walk for long distances in search of wild fruits exposing them to risks GBV(Removed this part) and giving into sexual exploitation in exchange for food. Based on WAV’s programming experience in Cuiebet and Rumbek North counties most of the adolescent girls forced out of school, and subjected to early and forced marriages in order for the families to be able to access food within the households. This has led to growing levels of sexual violence, abductions, sexual harassment, and sexual exploitation and abuse. At least 70% of the beneficiaries targeted by WAV for PSS and psychological first aid (PFA) and services at the women and girls’ friendly spaces will be women and adolescent girls. The current assessment shows heightened vulnerabilities for female-headed households, unmarried adolescent girls, elderly women, disabled women and girls, pregnant and lactating mothers. The findings from the assessment indicate high need for a GBV response in the area of Rumbek North and Cuiebet Counties.
