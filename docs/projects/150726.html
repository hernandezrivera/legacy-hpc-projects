Project Summary

The main objectives of this project is seeking accountability by providing legal interventions on behalf of victims of Israeli attacks occurred in the context of the great march of return that is taking place in the access restricted areas and also to strengthen monitoring, verification and documentation of the possible IHR/IHRL violations including attacks on health staff and other protected and/or vulnerable groups in the Gaza borders in addition to enhance access to medical care for the victims outside Gaza and in the special context of peaceful demonstrations in the Gaza Borders.
Those objectives are directly linked to Humanitarian Response Plan Country Level Strategic Objective 1: The rights of Palestinians under occupation are protected in accordance with International Humanitarian Law, IHL, and International Human Rights Law, IHRL, and linked to strategic result statement 1 of the Protection Cluster: 1. Increased respect for International Humanitarian Law and Human Rights Law and Accountability for Violations
The project will be implemented by the Palestinian Center for Human Rights –PCHR, and it will last for 12 months starting from 1st January, 2019.
The activities of the project will vary between legal aid for the victims of IHR/IHRL violations in ARA, Monitoring and documentation, reporting of the violations in the border areas and also providing legal aid to address the freedom of movement restrictions impeding injuries and patients from receiving treatment outside Gaza.

Needs

As a result of the great march of return started in 30 march 2018, about 154 civilians including 33 children (1 girl), 3 paramedics, two journalists, 5 person with disabilities and a woman lost their lives. More than 8431 were subjected to injuries, including 1435 children, 225 women, 101 journalists, and 119 paramedics. Among those wounded, 463 in serious condition and 76 had their lower or upper limbs amputated.Israel use of lethal force has been condemned by UN special coordinator for the Middle East Peace Process, Nickolay Mladenov, who described Israel shooting at children as ‘outrageous’ and called for investigations. According to these rising circumstances at the borders, PCHR have had to intensify its presence and work on documentation of violations and provision of legal aid. This triples the expected number of field visits and eyewitness/victims interviews, and raises transportation and communication costs considerably.Furthermore, based on the numbers of victims who raised cases or complaints through PCHR to the Israeli ministry of defense in the aftermath of these peaceful demonstrations, it is estimated that a case load of 137 individuals will require legal support including counselling, assistance and representation. As the nature of this intervention is of timely manner, it is estimated that the 137 cases for victims/individuals will require to continue their proceedings and will continue to be in need of legal support in 2019 and 2020, this ensures the engagement of the beneficiaries across the whole project cycle where they asked for the service, updated about the whole progress during the implementation and five groups discussions will be conducted to measure their satisfaction at the end of the service.. Israel not only used lethal force against Palestinian protesters in the field but also denied passage for medical treatment to critically wounded protestors. The punitive policy denied the wounded protesters from travelling via Erez to the Westbank to receive urgent medical care unavailable in Gaza in order to save their wounded legs from amputation. The state asserted that it did not allow the wounded young men to leave Gaza because they were wounded as a result of their participation “in a violent riot organized by Hamas.”, this increases the number of the patients asking for the medical treatment outside Gaza where the number of the patients who asked for permits through PCHR during 2018 is 295, 40% of them will continue to be in need of legal assistance to get the permits in the project period and new 295 patients is expected to ask for the legal aid in 2019 .PCHR emphasizes that continuously inflicting casualties, either killed or wounded, and also denying injuries from receiving the medical treatment outside Gaza is unjustified, and using lethal force to target and kill civilians, who exercise their right to peaceful assembly or while carrying out their humanitarian duty, is a serious violation of the rules of international law, international humanitarian law, the ICC Rome Statute and Fourth Geneva Convention. Thus, PCHR proposed through this project to hold accountable all those applying or involved in issuing orders within the Israeli Forces at the security and political echelons , It is so important to continue and pursue accountability avenues for Israeli violations within the Israeli mechanism of justice and within the accessible international avenues in order to break the cycle of Israeli impunity, and thus deterring the IOF from committing further violations, PCHR is exerting efforts in this regard through Legal aid and representation and Civil and criminal complaints which are sent to the Israeli Compensation Office in the Defense Ministry and to the Israeli Military Advocate General (MAG) respectively , PCHR always ensure the periodic coordination with Al-Mezan Center for human rights in monitoring work and casualty tracking.
