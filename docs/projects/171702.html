Project Summary

The objective of the project is to strengthen the protection of most vulnerable girls, boys, women and men affected by conflict and climatic shocks by reducing risks and vulnerabilities and increasing community capacities. Key to this action will be a focus on improving capacity for immediate response to violations and remedial action as well as strengthening the protective environment in Somalia.
On immediate response, the action will aim to address acute protection needs of girls, boys women and men, stemming from violence, including GBV, child rights violations, as well as coercion, and abuse. Using a survivor centered multi-sectoral approach ASAL, vulnerable women and girls will receive GBV services through Women and girls safe spaces and case management.
To enable recovery and restore dignity of individual and community victims of violence, neglect and abuse to improve positive coping strategies and prevent further violations. In addition, material assistance and livelihood skills training for GBV will also be provided.
On environment building, ASAL will reinforce formal and community institutions for better legal and community-based protection of vulnerable groups from violence, coercion and abuse. Key to achieve this objective, activities will include awareness-raising and outreach among communities on protection issues, capacity building and service providers and humanitarian workers on GBV.
On advocacy, ASAL will strengthen coordination of protection services and enhance advocacy on protection. Key activities will include strengthening of referral pathways, mapping of service providers and Protection Monitoring of rights violations. ASAL will further identify and provide protection-oriented support to people with disabilities in the target locations.
The proposed action will reach 1500 HHs and project beneficiaries will 9000 persons in Somaliland (3000 girls, 2500 boys, 2000 women and 1500 men).

Project Activities

ASAL will undertake the following activities in line with the objectives of the Somalia Protection Cluster Strategy: 1.Provision of material assistance as part of GBV response and prevention will include distribution of dignity kits, solar lanterns and installation of street lights in selected locations 2. Social- Economic reintegration and empowerment for GBV survivors through livelihood skills training, legal support and cash based assistance. 3. Conduct awareness-raising and outreach sessions for child protection among communities through prevention messages on recruitment of children, SGBV, harmful practices, family separation and Children's rights 4. Capacity building and training non-institutional service providers, this will include training of community based child protection structures, duty bearers and humanitarian workers and on child protection approaches in emergencies 5. Child protection case management. Girls and boys with child protection risks will be identified and referred through case management. Those with special needs such as disabilities and medical care will receive specialized services 6. Awareness raising advocacy and public outreach campaigns on GBV prevention, response and mitigation. Information on GBV prevention and response as well as services will be shared through various means include one on one and group campaigns and leaflets. 7. Provide post-eviction protection focused assistance to girls, boys, women and men 8. To strengthen GBV and CP referral pathways and monitoring, 9. Protection monitoring of protection violations. ASAL will work with other partners and the Protection Cluster on the Protection Monitoring system to support regular monitoring of protection violations to inform prioritization and targeting.

Mitigation Measures

Mitigating the risk of Exclusion and marginalization of marginalized community’s and minority groups Minority groups in Somalia are rarely targeted by key interventions or considered in policy design. Minorities as a whole are marginalized and invisible. Women and children are among minority groups are even more sidelined and excluded and do not benefit from structures that affords others some degree of protection. Recurrent insecurity caused by conflict creates an environment where minority groups are vulnerable and abnormally displaced from their homes. In addition, at least 15% of the population are people with disabilities who face heightened risks and exclusion from services. To mitigate on this risks, ASAL will target and empower, protect and promote the rights of minority communities, with a focus on women and children who experience multiple-discrimination on account of their gender and minority status. ASAL will follow a selection criterion for beneficiaries that include members of minorities as vulnerable groups that require special attention. Selection of beneficiaries will be through participatory methods in collaboration with trained community representatives. Through protection monitoring, ASAL will identify trends of exclusion and marginalization. Data collected will be used for targeting and prioritization, ensuring that no one is left out. ASAL will consult people with disabilities and/or their representatives during the design and Identify and address barriers to participation for people with disabilities. Mitigating the risk of Exclusion and marginalization of women, girls and boys Women, girls and boys remain vulnerable to protection risks and harmful cultural practices, GBV, child recruitment and hazardous child labor. Their ability to access justice and remedies and to enjoy rights is often limited and further weakened in case of displacement.

Please provide further explanation

Durable Solutions: Recognizing that the project is set in a protracted and complex emergency context, the project will partially contribute to durable solutions. The project will aim at addressing all 3 dimensions of safety, key to the achievement of durable solutions – physical, material and legal. Material safety will be addressed through integration with ASAL response in other sectors in which protection will be mainstreamed. The program is designed taking into account the needs and perspectives of the communities that ensuring that the intervention will contribute to physical, material and legal safety of the targeted communities. ASAL will conduct conflict analysis before and during project implementation and identify among other things, root cause of conflicts and other factors that may lead to conflicts. The information from conflict analyses studies will inform program design and re-design. Social cohesion and peaceful coexistence will be supported analyzing how targeting can minimize inter-community conflicts.
