Project Summary

It is a 12-month responsive project, which aims to improve the quality and accessibility to health care and rehabilitation services for 400 Children with cerebral palsy to 14 years old (CwCP) (50% female, 50% male), and their careers\care givers living in North Gaza Governorate and Gaza Governorate. This project will respond mainly to the essential health needs of the target group through holistic rehabilitation activities, which include provision of assistive devices and diapers also provision of rehabilitation services including physiotherapy (PT), occupational therapy (OT), speech therapy (ST), and psychosocial support (PSS).

Needs

Cerebral Palsy “CP” described by loss or impairment of motor function, caused by brain injury or abnormal development of the brain that occurs while a child’s brain is still developing. It can also impact fine motor skills, gross motor skills and oral motor functioning. Globally the CP is the most common motor disability in childhood ( ) ( ). Palestinian Central Bureau of Statistics (PCBS) showed that " mobility is the most prevalent disability among population in the Gaza Strip – (1.4% among children 0-17 years, 1.8% for males and 1.3% for females), as their needs and rights which provided by official service providers and civil societies are inadequate, and does not meet their deferent actual needs. The PCBS 2017 also emphasized that about 85.3% of persons with mobility disability face difficulties in moving around local environment, and they are the most who suffer in utilizing and access to public services at 74.4% . The last need assessments conducted by Baitona on 19, July 2017 for 50 caregivers of CwCP who were discharged from the center based and/or outreach rehabilitation services to address their difficulties and it were:1. The rehabilitation services were inadequate because it were limited to few sessions compared it with actual needs.2. Lack of proper skills on how to provide basic rehabilitation for their CwCP, and no access to equipment needed for rehabilitation therapy at home.3. Lack of quality and accessibility to essential health care and rehabilitation services due to the lack of pediatric stroller, which increase the health complication resulted from the CP conditions, such as high risk of pressure sores, respiratory infection, bowel and bladder problems (Constipation) and the weakness of the immune system. On the other hand, they confirmed that they do not use the regular pediatric wheelchairs because of the social stigma, and its size and weight not appropriate with sandy roads and general and private vehicles.4. They rarely have proper referral to rehabilitation service providers and other mainstreaming stakeholders. 5. Only few of them got some kind of psychosocial support, despite their overwhelming of psychosocial support. The Handicap International Gaza, emphasized that the absent of psychological support can lead to the rejection of the disability or apathy, which is lead to lack of the involvement of the caregiver of rehabilitation . Over the last 15 years, Baitona has been providing rehabilitation services for CwCP in the North Gaza governorate. Recently, Baitona database indicated that 900 Children with cerebral palsy or/and multiple disabilities (CwCP) in North Gaza. This project coincides with the Goal number 3 and meet the specific objectives number 2, 4, and 6 of Baitona’s Strategic plan. Noting that Baitona has a comprehensive therapy center (PT,OT, ST) and has a excellence experience with CwCP with partnership with HI. In addition. This project relates to health and nutrition cluster objective number ONE and related to strategic objective number TWO.

Activities

Activity1: Provision 9600 outreach rehabilitation sessions for CwCP. (400 CwCP * 24 sessions of PT, PT, ST)Activity2: Distribution of 300 assistive devices for the CwCP.(50 F & 50M)Activity3: Referral of 40 of CwCP and their caregivers to social empowerment, health & nutrition, educational services and others. Activity4: Provision of 4699 individual practical training sessions on "how to implement independence rehabilitation activities based-family, (PT, OT, ST) for 400 caregivers of CwCP. Activity5: Provision of 200 rehabilitation home equipment to CwCP and their caregivers to be used in rehabilitation home activities.Activity6: Provision of diapers to 400 CwCP. (6 packs for each one).Activity7: Distribution of 400 "Illustrated Guide for the Families of CwCP" caregivers on "how to practice healthy habits on daily life including: nutrition, dressing, safety sleeping, showering, feeding positions and toilet training.Activity8: Provide 1600 individual psychosocial support sessions to 400 caregivers (50%F & 50%M) using the psycoeducation approach at home.Activity9: Provide 240 caregivers with group psychosocial support sessions using the Mind Body Medicine approach (24 groups, each group received ten sessions, two sessions a week, 2 hours each session)Task/Activity 10: organizing five workshops to enhance the mechanisms of coordinating among the NGOs, CBOs, and service providers in the local referral network, where the CwCP afford accessible and qualified health and social services.
