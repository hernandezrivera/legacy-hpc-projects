Project Summary

As a result of the Beirut Port explosions, access to learning and education support will be obstructed in Beirut. 120 schools reported some sort of damage from the explosion. This affects 55,000 Lebanese and non-Lebanese children who are due to resume classes in September. The widespread destruction created multi-level challenges for children and their families, which will have lasting consequences on the enrolment and learning continuation. Loss of education can lead to permanent drop-out and increase of child labour. School-aged children (Lebanese and Syrian refugees) living in Beirut will not be able to resume education in the affected schools from the private and public sector (morning/afternoon shifts).
According to the Minister of Education, schools will start in the last week of September and learning modality will be blended, which combines online educational with traditional place-based classroom methods. However, it is obvious that schools, which encountered severe damages in the building structure or in the educational equipment and material, will not be able to start normally in the short run. Challenges are greater with vulnerable Syrian children whom attend afternoon shift in public schools, that are damaged. Those children are prone to drop out from school. Education sector will face challenge to support education to these children for the coming academic year 20/21.
Through this project, Islamic Relief will conduct needs assessment for 10 educational institution; Rehabilitation for 6 schools and education centers; provide educational supplies for 4000 children; and provide PSS training for 185 teachers and education personnel in close coordination with CP partners.

Plan fields

1 - Needs

As a result of the Beirut Port explosions access to learning and education support for more than 55,000children and youth in Beirut will be obstructed. The widespread destruction created multi-level challengesfor children and their families, which will have lasting consequences on enrollment and continuation ofthe learning paths.• Severe-to-medium damages to an estimated 120 schools, all of which requires rehabilitation andbasic learning equipment and materials.• Students displaced from their homes are particularly vulnerable to withdrawing from school due totransportation difficulties and insufficient support.• Families who lost their homes and jobs need additional support to cover costs for public, private andsemi-private schools.

2 - Activities

By the end of the 3 months' project:- IR will conduct Rapid Needs Assessment for Six education institutions (4 private/semi-private, 2 NFE centers) and assess the impact on infrastructure and program.- Based on Islamic relief experience in the past couple of years, constructing Hilani Public School (مدرسة حيلان الرسمية) and repairing a number of education centers, in addition to planning for rehabilitation of Baasir Public School, IR will address the flash appeal needs through the rehabilitation of three public schools, two semi private/private schools, and one NFE center all of which encountered light to moderate damages. In later phases and recovery stages, IR will address the need to rehabilitate public schools with major damages. - Provision of education supplies for 4,000 children enrolled in affected educational institutions including public, private and semi-private schools. 2,500 (two thousand five hundred) students in ECE and elementary will receive student kit and 1,500 (one thousand five hundred) secondary students will receive student kit. (kindly check below for the student kit components)- Psychosocial Support (PSS) training for 185 teachers and education personnel in close coordination with the Child Protection partners. Throughout the education projects IR was implementing in different areas of Lebanon including South, Mount Lebanon and Bekaa, we provided PSS sessions for children from FE/NFE and schools in addition to providing PSS training sessions and child protection induction. During this response, IR will coordinate with MEHE to assign a combined training programme (online and physical training) to schools that are supported and to schools with children that have been traumatized by the blast. *Student Education Kit: School bag, copybooks (math ,Ara, Eng.), pencil case, pens, pencils, ruler, colorings(KG-elementary), geometry set and scientific calculator (secondary).
