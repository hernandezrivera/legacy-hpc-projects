Project Name

Start Date

01/01/2019

End Date

Project Summary

The project aims to provide Nigerian refugees affected by displacement with adequate shelter solutions. The target will be 50% female and 50% male. However, women heads of households will be taken into as such and prioritise when possible as they are more vulnerable.

Needs

The Lake Chad crisis has been having spill over effect on populations displacement in the Far North of Cameroon. According to the DTM Round 15 report: -The most affected departments are those of the Logone and Chari (37%), the Mayo Sava (29%) and the Mayo Tsanaga (12%)-Out of camp refugees represent 6% of displacement, an increase of 6% over round 14.In the NFI/Shelter sector, the lack of shelter and NFI is one of the main needs since the beginning of the conflict facing out of camp refugees.In August-September 2018, following the multi-sector assessments carried out by UNOCHA, the NFI/shelter requirements were estimated as follows:-NFI: 68% of out of camp refugees;-Shelter requirement: 51% reported having their shelters overcrowded, in very poor condition or uninhabitable, or without shelter. -The most vulnerable population is elderly persons without family support, single women heads of households, persons with physical or mental handicaps, minors in charge of minorsIn addition, the Lake Chad Basin crisis in the Far North region has led to the destruction, abandonment and deterioration of schools in many villagesNRC's response will be in line with those of the NFI/Shelter cluster in order to meet the above mentioned needs for out of camp refugees in the departments of the Logone and Chari, the Mayo Sava and the Mayo Tsanaga.
