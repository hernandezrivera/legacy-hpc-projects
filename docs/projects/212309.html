Project Summary

According to HNO 2024, the protracted crisis in Syria, further compounded by the aftermath of the earthquake continues to negatively impact the education system. There has been a 4% increase in the People in Need (PiN), compared to the previous year. This emphasizes the urgent need for emergency education services to support up to 7.1 1million school aged children and teachers, including children with disabilities. The PiN, comprises 6.9 million children 5 – 17 years (46% girls, 54% boys) and also includes 230 education personnel, with (48% female and 52% male). The PiN consists of 10% 2of children with Disabilities (CwD), highlighting the need for specialized education services.
There are more than 2.45 4million Out of School Children (OoSC) (46% girls, 54% boys) and over a million who are at risk of dropping out. "HNO 2024". The project aims to support the educational process in several schools in Jarablus, Jandairis and Idleb.
The main objective of the project is ensuring children’s right to access to education and the basis for further and higher education. Scaling up safe and equitable access for 200students, 105 girls & 95 boys, 5% of them are CwDs, (7-13 years old) to a regular formal protective education environment. Payments of monthly incentives for the teachers in non-formal settings.
The project will mainly contribute to mainstreaming child protection mechanisms with in the schools through the PSS (Psychosocial Support) activities, support, and provision of activities that will reduce child protection risks.
There will be leisure activities for the students (sports activities, music activities, the activities, etc.) in addition to recreational trips if the circumstances permit.
Provision of non-formal education will be supported by providing teachers with monthly incentives.
in addition, the project will provide 8 schools with heating materials in Idleb and Jandairis.
Risks: Students are not sufficiently protected:
Mitigation:
Learning facilities should have Child Safeguarding polices and systems in place will be regularly, monitored and corrective action taken when needed.
Child Safeguarding will cover all people who may interact with childrenAs HIHFAD staff, teachers, other school personnel, school bus drivers, contactors, etc.
Risk: Insufficiently safe learning facilities
Mitigation: Learning facilities will be improved to ensure that buildings and grounds are safe and protected. This may include stairs, walls, roofs, flooring, electric, water, etc. School grounds should have fencing. All works should be supervised and monitored by technical personnel. The team will ensure Child Safeguarding measures are in place before work start.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

HIHFAD aims to support and supervise all education activities in primary and medical education under direct supervision for two schools in Jarablus, in addition to provision of stationery and heating materials to schools in Idleb and Jandairis. Signing contracts with teachers and education personnel to provide remedial classes after coordination with ED in Gaziantep and Jarablus to provide incentives for teachers. Education Supervisor will provide regular training for teachers. PSS facilitator will provide training for teachers and will provide PSS sessions to students and caregivers in coordination with Protection department in HIHFAD. Provision of formal education will be supported by HIHFAD by providing running cost materials for schools (stationery, fuel for heating, cleaning materials, …, etc.) in addition to provide needed technical support for teachers and education personnel. Provision of non-formal education is supported by providing teachers with incentives for 24 teachers, providing needed materials for school in addition to technical support by education team. No transition will be applied, non-formal education activities are to strengthen students' levels and knowledge. PSS and recreational activities will be provided to students, caregivers as well as teachers and education personnel by PSS facilitator. HIHFAD will provide transportation for female students in secondary school 76 students. Stationery and school bags will be provided to students both primary and secondary (320+76), heating materials including fuel and heating stoves to 9 schools in Jarablus, Jandairis and Idleb. Schools rehabilitation will also be conducted to improve the access of student and increase the capacity of the schools.
