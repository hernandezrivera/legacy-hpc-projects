Project Summary

1 Access to appropriate sanitation and hygiene facilities, including management of solid waste.
1.1 Construction of temporary low-cost latrines in new school and public space on relocation areas
1.2 Solid waste management / cleanup and drainage for relocation areas and institutions
1.3 Support returning families for the (self) construction of latrines in their relocation areas
1.4 Distribution of water treatment products for use at household level, including training of for recipient households.
2 Dissemination of information on safe water, sanitation and hygiene and non-food items to prevent spread of wash related diseases
2.1 Distribution of hygiene kits (standard cluster kit)
2.2 Community level hygiene promotion & awareness campaigns (including development and distribution of IEC materials)
2.3 Media campaign with radio spots and theater shows and film projections in public areas to disseminate hygiene messages
3 Primary-flood-affected girls and boys have access to safe and inclusive learning environments
3.1 Establish of Temporary Learning Spaces (TLS)
3.2 Establish and activate School Committees (SCs)
3.3 Establish and conduct student groups in learning spaces
3.4 Conduct Psycho Social Support (PSS) & Child Protection (CP) activities.
