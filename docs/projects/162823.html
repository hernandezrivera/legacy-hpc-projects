Project Summary

The project will be a 12 months project in Zalingei in Central Darfur State targeting 54, 355 direct beneficiaries and 31,541 indirect beneficiaries who have been affected by conflict, yearly flooding with poor agricultural practices, poor access to quality nutrition services and poor health seeking behavior and dilapidated wash, hygiene and sanitation services. The sites of the project include Korolay, Tamar, Kotirgi and Dankouj which are prone to problems of food insecurity, poverty and environmental degradation. IRW will carry out the following activities; mobilization and sensitization of community through dialogue in the project area to create awareness of what project activities to be implemented, time frame and importance of the project to the community, identification and registration of beneficiaries, securing the agricultural inputs in Partnership with UN agencies recipient of 2019 CERF, state ministry of Agriculture, Health and Water and IRW in partnership with the ministries, and provision of nutrition services, construction and rehabilitation of WASH facilities, delivery to the distribution points, distribution of agriculture inputs to ensure access to agriculture inputs by the most vulnerable groups as a startup package for increased crop production, Technical guidance/training on use of irrigation in vegetable production during dry season, technical guidance on agronomic practices including sustainable land use practices, environmental awareness, provision of high quality preventive and promotive nutrition services including PLWs and promoting hygiene and sanitation. The community where this project will be implemented will be identified through consulting HAC, the local authorities and other stake holders on ground on the most vulnerable groups (IDPS, Women headed Household, Returnees and host community) who will be prioritized in the selection. The project will also address the precarious situation of shortage of sufficient agricultural inputs, limited appropriate farming knowledge and poor health seeking behavior, damaged WASH facilities, dilapidated nutrition facilities and hygiene and sanitation facilities.
In the end, IRW will have supervise and monitor the project activities to ensure quality, value for money and submit reports and any other relevant documentation as required.

1 - Needs

Based on the needs assessments conducted by IR, Humanitarian needs overview 2018, the 2018 HNO states; of total 685,278 individuals, 53% of those being children, are in urgent need of humanitarian assistance in Central Darfur, including 395,329 unregistered IDPs - displaced populations living in rural settlements and urban areas. These estimates may differ as there is no systematic registration of displacement outside of camps. In Zalingei locality where most of the IDPs choose to reside, 69% out of 112,632 inhabitants are IDPs.According to the most recent returnee populations from HAC, in Central Darfur, Zalingei is currently hosting a total of 54,335 (21,762M and 32,573F) returnees and with the current prevailing peace and reduced incidences of insecurity more are expected to return. On the contrary, IDPs are willing to relocate to their original homes but this will not happen in the near future unless permanent peace and tranquility is fully restored in the greater Darfur Region.The inflation experienced in the recent past has made the cost of living to be very high and prices of basic commodities still remains high and on the onward trend. This is in agreement with the WFP Monthly Market Update Report (February, 2019) that reported a steady increase in commodity prices in 2019 as compared to 2018 the same periods. The report further states that Central Darfur commodity prices as compared to same period 2018 has shown higher prices with Sorghum, goat and groundnuts having shown 26%, 13% and 9.3% increase respectively in February 2019 as compared to February 2018 with sorghum, goat and groundnuts having increased by 48%, 110% and 22% respectively in February 2019 as compared to their prices in February 2018. These high prices is similar to several HH commodities in Central Darfur that has thus made cost of living to be extremely high hence a precursor to high rates of malnutrition amongst children U5 and PLWs and high morbidity rates amongst the populations. Economic vulnerability remains one of the major reasons behind household food insecurity with 77 percent of the households economically vulnerable. Based on the need assessments conducted by WFP in Zaligei locality, food insecurity of the households in 2018 is as high as in 2017 despite of a significant increase in the harvest compared to 2017 where 31 percent of the households are food insecure (CARI module). The households in Darfur states that predominantly relied on agriculture as the primary source of livelihood have improved food security outcomes in 2018 compared to 2017, likely due to increased agriculture activities and a good harvest in the area (Source: CFSAM 2018).

2 - Activities / Outputs

Responding to situation needs, the project intends to provide integrated FSL, Wash and Nutrition activities to build resilience for targeted 54, 355 direct beneficiaries (26,090 male, 28,265 female) and 31,541 indirect beneficiaries (15,140 male, 16401 female), the project activities are categorized under three outputs: Under output 1: Increased Access to and Improved WASH services for conflict affected populations of Zalingi in Central Darfur, the activities are: install 4 new boreholes, construct 4 new solar powered Mini water yards, select and form 20 WUC, conduct awareness sessions on hygiene issues, construct 4 common latrines and HH latrines, Rehabilitation of 16 existing latrines in schools and health centers.Under output 2: Increased access to and utilization of improved and diversified food security and livelihood (FSL) opportunities for conflict affected households of Zalingei in Central Darfur, the activities are: Distribute food and cash crop seeds and tools to 800** vulnerable farmers, distribute vegetable seeds, tools and simple irrigation kits, vaccination of 15,000 animal heads, select and train 24 extension Service Agents (12 Female and 12 Male).Under output 3: Improved and sustained access to quality CMAM and IYCF care practices for children < five years and PLW’s the activities are: Conduct community awareness campaign on CMAM, IYCF, Provision of CMAM services for SAM and MAM cases of U5 children and PLW, Procurement of RUTF, RUSF and other nutritional supplies, rehabilitation of storage facilities at 4 PHCs/Health facilities, organize Quarterly MUAC screening campaigns at the 4 targeted locations (4 in HFs and 4 in community), Conduct training for nutrition workers, community volunteer on CMAM, IYCF, Establish mother support group for promotion of IYCF.

5.1 - Impact on environment/natural resources

Conserving natural resources is a basic requirement for sustainable development and improving the quality of human life. To assure this principle is adopted to target project activities, the project will aim to not reverse the trend towards resource degradation, and will consider giving greater attention and priority to ecological aspects. The project activities were designed in a way as not to impact the environment and will not affect the geographical and context of both the problem statement and nature of target groups. The activities are planned to dig deep into the roots of the problem and search for solutions. This trend support inclusion of positive environmental aspects and sustainable change.

6 - Protection mainstreaming

3=Yes, room for improvement

6.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

The protection will be a key feature within the intervention. In all the awareness session woman and girls will be sensitized about the protection issues and to reduce the risk of such incidents. For example, facilities and water points will be constructed in safe locations that minimize risks of various types of GBV. Pitching water in most part of remote areas in Sudan is the responsibility of women and young girls. However, this project will spare women an ample time and give them the opportunity to carry out other activities and participate in the development process of their communities, state and country. Hygiene promotion will be also be gender sensitive, with topics applicable for girls and for boys.To deal with GBV and other cases in the field IR will establish a referral mechanism with the health facility at the locality level to treat the GBV patients, referral mechanism with the social welfare department to lead if there is any protection issue reported. IR field teams with the support of the CHPs/volunteers will refer the victims to the health facilities or the social welfare.
