Project Summary

Assistance and durable solutions for Gender Based Violence with forced evictions by supporting with conditional grants through involving with Micro Enterprises Based in Family Entrepreneur, in order to get self-reliance
First and foremost Baseline data and finding needs assessment will be conducted in order to produce analysis Report and address needs of the target beneficiaries the target number of beneficiaries will 200 Households Through Social-economic reintegration, access to justice, and empowerment (livelihood; legal support; cash-based assistance) (GBV)
through Safe, dignified and meaningful access to life-saving assistance (provided directly or through referral) is ensured including persons with disability
Gender based violence survivors and child victims of violations (including children associated with armed groups/forces) reached with social-economic reintegration
We provide the targets business plan training workshops in order to build up their capacities of engagement for different kinds of Micro enterprises
also build up those who have some kind of skills are also eligible to support their livelihoods
The grants will provided in conditional basis
1) must be family returnees led by Woman
2) Must engage some kind of work
3) Must settled target IDPs settlement
4) Must be fair Returns and bring Guarantor like IDPs Elder/Host community or local authorities
5) Each family returnees entrepreneur must work 3000 Hrs in Six months
6) each entrepreneur must be indicating their business as profitable enterprise, Must reinvest at least 20% of the profits
7) each family returnee will get $300 as first Installment
8) after six months of profitable and entrepreneur will be eligible the second and last $300 Grants per each successful business
9 ) fulfillment of all above mentioned mentioned conditions are acceptable for grants
