Project Summary


Establishing CFS at Several areas inside ruler Aleppo Syria city to empower, children, and families through awareness raising and training.
The project justification:
Children Approximately 50% of the total number of displaced children in all Syria city's
This caused many results.
- The spread of the phenomenon of incomplete education
- Increase domestic violence and social violence.
-Early marriage phenomenon.
-Child labor phenomenon.
children are the most vulnerable among the displaced, where the war has resolved in poor families and children's education.
Due to difficult living conditions, many cases of leakage from schools, gender-based violence, many early marriages and the lack of women's participation in social life have deprived them of their rights (because women do not know these rights)
The main objective of the project is to support CFS by increase capacity to deal with these cases and alleviate them and identify them on a sound basis to deal with the psychological conditions of the family.
The project also aims at empowering families with rights and duties, dealing with children and Secure child-friendly spaces for children for "Boys and girls affected by the crisis and exposed to neglect, abuse and violence are protected through the access for them and their caregivers to groups and individual psychosocial support."
and "Strengthen the capacity of teachers to address child protection risks and needs and to enhance access to quality specialized child protection interventions in targeted locations in line with the Child Protection Minimum Standards in Humanitarian Action.
and Most vulnerable boys and girls exposed or at risk of neglect, abuse and violence such as child labor, early marriage or other forms of child protection issues have access to specialized services and are able to respond to their basic needs.""

Needs

According to HRP 2019, the findings of the available assessments, the protection environment in Syria remains characterized by the occurrence of several protection issues selected for the analysis, with varying frequency of occurrence across locations and population groups, as well as some constant patterns. in 48% of assessed communities, the occurrence of at least four protection issues was reported by the consulted population. In parallel, qualitative protection sources are a crucial source of information to indicate how different protection issues are inter-linked and should, therefore, be addressed in a holistic manner.The current crisis has interrupted community routeen and destroyed many services hence creating a huge humanitarian need. These conditions are reflecting the suffering and psychological trauma of the IDPs in these areas, especially children aged from(6-14). The Jana Watan team collected information from the residents through our direct contact with them where we found that the IDPs in Aleppo are facing harsh conditions and nothing available to protect them from cold and heat inundation to the lack to the minimum basic requirements like protection, privacy, and other non-food items and the children are facing severe psychological stress, symptoms of trauma and disorder. Some of the key findings were that Children are often facing learning difficulties, school behavioral problems related to scenes of violence as well as problems in the relationship with parents and are spread symptoms of multiple psychological problems and stress caused by the effects of scenes of war and bombing in addition to mental disorders resulting from their experience of conflict.The main factor that drives our organization to pay attention to this region is our knowledge of needs of this area where there is a few organization that provides psychological protection and support services in the whole region leaving them overstretched and unable to offer the required services to these families. and of course not enough.According to the (need assessment/focus group decisions/ Questionnaires) conducted by our team, the Children suffer from psychological problems and because of the sudden shelling in the communities addition to that, there is a large percentage of them exposed to child labor and early marriages, families, also there are different psychological problems and they need an internal balance. (It has also been an assessment on the Kobo program(For 3 levels: The family of beneficiaries / And local councils / And teachersruler Aleppo city, and the number of people is about 5740 girls and 2466 boys out of them in need where the protection severity scale is 5

Implementation of activities

1-Preparatory activities for the project - Arrangements and Preparations 2-Establish mobile clinic a Child Friendly Space (CFS). 3- Provide access to a CFS and conduct PSS activities for boys and girls (6-14 years) 4- Provide trainings for families to improve their capacity to conduct and facilitate psychosocial support services and to deal with children in crisis.15 teacher per training 5- Conduct awareness raising campaigns with the community and stakeholders6- Develop a localized service mapping system 7- Support the most vulnerable boys and girls to access specialized services through a localized referral system 8- Provide individual follow-up and counseling to the most affected boys and girls 9-open one CFS center in Dayr samaan which will prove the service for 800 children Our Organization Monitoring and Evaluation plan will be predicated on the project log-frame and M&E plan focusing specifically on indicator measurement and reporting. At project inception, a light touch joint pre-implementation assessment (pre-assessment) will be conducted to refine and finalize overall project targets. Also, regular site visits will be conducted by the M&E field staff for documentation of project implementation progress by routine meetings with key informants at local level and committee to generate qualitative data on the effectiveness of project interventions. Depending on beneficiary consent, visual evidence that includes photos and videos of project activities will be documented. Exit interview questionnaires will be administered to know the perceptions and satisfaction of beneficiaries with services being delivered. Monitoring data will be shared routinely with project management through kobo Toolbox for informed decision making and necessary adjustments, and determine volunteers to supervise on the process in all the details in each location. Towards the end of the project, a post-implementation assessment (post-assessment) will be used to review performance indicators progress and level of achievement but this will be determined. A lesson learning event will be conducted at the end of the project lifecycle to draw some lessons and best practices from the project. The complaints mechanism, through complaints boxes, it has also been allocated telephone number to receive any complaints without boxes. the beneficiary can put his complaint in the box, monitoring and evaluation team receives complaints through his weekly visits to the boxes, after that he filters the complaints according to type, then transferred to the organization management and the administration discuss it with responsible department. Also, we coordinate with the local council to replay on complaints and explain any reasons behind inquires. For the telephone number, the MEAL team receive complaints, discuss, explain and reply on beneficiary if there were any proposed solution or idea to reach the best type of help. Also our staff shall work to ensure accountability to the affected population especially through participatory approaches and routinely consult and engage with the communitiesThe monitoring plan will include the date and implementation for activity, and Age categories of beneficiaries and Identify sex. Also Monitoring staff schedules and the extent to which activities are committed.
