Project Summary

Tabitha's office is situated only four kilometers away from the Beirut blast site, enabling the the office to provide a fast emergency response. Tabitha staff conducted a rapid needs assessment (RNA) in the districts of Rmeil and Bourj Hammoud together with Helpage and local partners, consulting more than 800 individuals, including key stakeholders, immediately following the emergency. These districts are located close to the port, and are partially destroyed and/or heavily affected. The acute needs identified include the needs for reconstruction, shelter repair, health support, food assistance, WASH, and protection. One year later, houses with partial destruction are still inhabited, and in particular, the elderly have not moved out. Based on the needs assessment and sectoral expertise, Tabitha and its partner MSD will respond in Rmeil, Medaawar, Achrafieh and Bourj Hammoud providing hygiene kits as per sector standards joint with ongoing food distributions. The hygiene kits will reach 150 households. This project has already received funding through the Lebanese Humanitarian Fund to cover part of this project, and is linked to Tabitha’s food security project.
Hygiene kit contents fall in-line with sector requirements. In addition, Tabitha will provide baby kits that include diapers, detergent, baby bathing soap, baby underwear, pajamas, and thermometers. Kits will also be distributed for the elderly which include diapers, hand soap, shower gel, underwear, pajamas, slippers, medical masks, and towels. Distribution will be done through Tabitha's community center in Achrafieh; beneficiary selection is conducted by community outreach workers and social workers.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

This activity falls under WASH sector objective 2, "Provision of customized hygiene kits for girls, boys, women, men and elderly." Under this project, Tabitha will distribute hygiene kits standardized by the sector not only for households, but also specialized kits for the elderly and babies. This project is linked in Tabitha's ongoing food parcel distribution activity, providing food for the most critically vulnerable affected by the Beirut blast. By engaging in both food parcel and hygiene kit distribution, Tabitha endeavors to support communities to have access to a wider range of services in one location. Beneficiary selection criteria and beneficiary outreach is conducted through Tabitha's community outreach and social workers in Achrafieh who are trusted and well known within the community. This project will reach a total of 150 households. Within this, the project, elderly individuals and babies will received specialized kits. Tabitha will also include awareness raising materials with the kits on issues like COVID-19, mental health, and reproductive health.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Tabitha has immense experience working on inclusion and gender mainstreaming, particularly under its protection program. Tabitha utilizes social work methodology to ensure that beneficiaries receive individual vulnerability/needs assessments through a network of outreach workers (selected among vulnerable host communities) to facilitate trust building and access to isolated groups. Tabitha mainstreams protection in all its activities which allows maximization of the protective impact of the project through ensuring that the activities target the most vulnerable and enhance safety, dignity, and promote and protect the human rights of beneficiaries without contributing/ perpetuating discrimination, abuse, violence, neglect and exploitation. Tabitha also engages in social cohesion activities that are designed to bring together individuals of different backgrounds. Tabitha staff have been sensitized to gender & diversity and should recognize that vulnerabilities differ from one to another.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

Community engagement is at the core of Tabitha's humanitarian work across Lebanon. For this reason, Tabitha has created a robust two-way feedback mechanism, operational through various channels including calls, emails, written boxes, and verbal feedback. Tabitha builds many of its projects based on the feedback received at its community centers which are trusted, longstanding entities. Tabitha also maintains high monitoring and evaluation standards, and ensures that staff are well-trained on the code of conduct and humanitarian standards. Tabitha has successfully participated in a series of coaching/mentoring sessions on PSEA & safeguarding integration done by ABAAD in partnership with OCHA, LHF and the Lebanese National PSEA Network. A training on integrity is followed by all staff of Tabitha and it is mandatory.
