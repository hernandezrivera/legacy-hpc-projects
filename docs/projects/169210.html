Project Summary

For the shelter response, UNOPS proposes to contribute to the rehabilitation of lightly-to-moderately damaged housing units. See details in "Activities".
UNOPS will follow the Shelter Cluster proposed methodology and utilise the standardized Household Assessment Form. UNOPS did not took part in the initial needs assessment conducted and therefore remains flexible to implement its response in any non-covered area which would be identified by the Shelter Cluster.
All procurement are to be carried out under emergency procedure to ensure speedy delivery under 3 months.
Estimated costs and target values are based on the available needs assessment results, and costs of similar projects implemented by UNOPs in Lebanon and the region.
UNOPS is submitting a multi-sectoral response, with proposals in HEALTH, SHELTER and EDUCATION. The final budget is contingent of the confirmation of funding for all projects.

1 - Needs

On the 4th August, the city of Beirut was hit by a devastating explosion which flattened most of the city's port, damaging neighborhoods and buidlings across the capital. According to the Rapid Damages and Needs Assessment (RDNA), the explosion is estimated to have caused US$ 2.08 billion in damage to the Housing sector, adversely affecting an estimated 351,000 residents of Beirut. The housing sector represents 14% of the total needs. According to a UNDP economic brief, a total of 200,000 housing units were affected in Beirut; 40,000 buildings were damaged; and 3,000 housing structures received serious damage as a result of the explosions. The LRC’s coordinated multi-sectoral needs assessment and UNHCR report that 7% of the properties assessed have no damage, 42% sustained light damages (level 1), 28% moderate damages (level 2) and 23% severe damages (level 3). More specifically, the damage assessment analysis framework, which was established to determine the needs according to 3 different levels related to the conditions of three elements (doors, windows and toilets), reveal that:- 29,771 apartments (89,313 individuals) were reported with at least one element damaged (level 1). - 17,100 apartments (51, 300 individuals) were reported having all 3 elements with sustained damage

2 - Activities

This brief project proposal focuses on the rehabilitation of lightly and moderately damaged housing units (level 1 and 2) Activity 1.1.) Minor rehabilitation of 200 housing units (level 1).This activity includes technical assessment and minor repairs such as fixing brokenglass, broken doors, locks, collapse of false ceiling. This activity will target 800 individuals.Activity 1.2.) Minor rehabilitation of 100 housing units (level 2)This activity includes technical assessment and minor repairs such as fixing brokenglass, broken doors, locks, collapse of false ceiling, obviously of quantity more than Level 1,but with no structural damage. Services may include fixing of water, sanitation and electricity.This activity will target 400 individuals.
