Project Summary

The project aims to address the problems of high morbidity and mortality rate and common diseases: Malaria, Diarrhea, Pneumonia, Measles and cholera, a situation brought about by limited number of functional health facilities, scarcity of supplies and flooding seasons, not only that, it will also reduce excessive morbidity mortality by timely detection and coordinate response to epidemic prone diseases as well as improve equitable to life-saving essential, provision of quality comprehensive package of basic healthcare
The beneficiaries will be provided with essential primary healthcare services in static, outreaches and mobile health facilities using qualified, Clinical officers, Midwives, Nurses and other health care providers. They will also be provided with mosquito nets, blankets and water chlorination tabs that will increase drinking safe and clean water which is favorable for human use
This project will take into account the humanitarian – coordination and referral pathway linkages to strengthen SGBV services provision resilient and coping mechanism.
It will also take into consideration issues of gender disparity and try to improve accessibility to services for the most disadvantaged groups in the communities such mental health and people living with disabilities. Stakeholders are to be sufficiently sensitized and kept engaged throughout the lifespan of the project with support from WHO, Health cluster, MoH and County health department (CHD) and community leaders, this project shall target a population of 10,000 people which be assisted in Ayod county as disaggregated as follows; men 1500, women 2500, boys 2700 and girls 3300, people living with disabilities 15% and elderly people age 60 years above 5%) all with be provided with healthcare services.
The project will have supplies from Core pipe lines WHO UNFPA and MoH respectively.
This project will positively contribute to health cluster objectives 1. Improve equitable access to life-saving essential quality health care services including maternal, child and adolescent health, sexual and reproductive health, treatment common illnesses, disability, MHPSS and GBV health services to crisis affected populations
2. Provide access to life-saving essential healthcare, including mental health to women, men, girls and boys, including ability challenged persons
3. Enhance resilience and promote humanitarian-development linkages to strengthen health system recovery and coping mechanisms
EDA will monitor and evaluate the project and will report to health cluster, CHD and state MoH through 5Ws, EDSR and DHIS2. This report will be collected and analyze by our M & E focal person and data will be shared in weekly and monthly basis. EDA will coordinate with state health cluster and involve in health needs assessment. We will coordinate with other partners from different clusters (nutrition, FSL,WASH protection so that there will be strong effort and synergy to improve the livelihood of affected population in Ayod. EDA will coordinate and maintain AAP strategy to keep local community and beneficiaries in the implementation process.

1 - Needs

This year more people are anticipated to suffer due to prevalence of diseases cause by persistent flooding and other multiple shocks, the like of intercommunal fights amongst (up to 36%), poor quality and diversity of food, elevated levels of food insecurity in most counties, hard access to health and nutrition services had also triggered the services accessibility, Across the country, more than ten 10 Counties are classified under IPC 5 in either inter-sectoral severity and health severity. The majority of these counties are in Greater Upper Nile region followed by Greater Bahr el Ghazal region and lastly Greater Equatoria region with classified in Emergency (IPC Phase 4) acute food insecurity.Ayod county is depending on humanitarian aids provision since 2013 and this has been worsened by the ongoing flooding that has destroyed the livelihood of people and their properties, There is lack of infrastructures of health facility and the main health system is compromised greatly by flooding. Penta 3 coverage is (<30% DHIS-2) and malaria keeping increasing during rainy seasons and displacement is far from existing health facilities. This left a lot of gaps unclose due to insufficient resources cause by the huge needs across the world since the influx of refugees and IDPs is a global concerns in almost all the world continents, As South Sudan is amongst the countries with huge humanitarian needs, health remained the great concerns, living as health cluster PiN indicate, 5, 467, 710 are seeking health services across the country, and HNO report indicate that, 5.5m, 50% representing women, 52% for children and 15% are people living with physical impairments, all are in critical needs of health services, therefore provisions of quality essential healthcare services shall be the top priority of this project, in doing so, qualify health professional shall be recruited and deployed across the healthcare units and center to reduce maternal mortality, this will be achieve through professional midwives to deliver mothers, EPI services to be intensify to help reduce childhood diseases, Episcopal Development Aid -EDA, will mobilize medicine from MoH, WHO, Health cluster and other stakeholders to be preposition at the sites, there shall also be intensive awareness on infection preventions and diseases control, health educations sessions at the health facilities, coordinate with other actors to strengthen the referral pathway with GBV victims for further management, nutrition providers for nutrition cases management, we will integrate our existing health project with SSHF project and coordinate with other actors in the areas. South Sudan has continued to experience worse humanitarian disasters since its independency, brought about by the following factors:• Climatic shocks - Floods and droughts, led to displacements, destructions of health facilities, cut off road linkages due to fear on highways, destruction of homestead, wash off of roads by either flooding or rainy water. • Sub-national and inter - communal conflicts, coupled with widespread insecurity, affected livelihoods and loss of productive assets as well as reduction in ownership of livestock amongst the counties. • Macroeconomic crisis, causing continuous depreciation of the South Sudan Pound and corresponding skyrocketing of prices and thereby eroding the purchasing power of vulnerable households who rely on markets to purchase food and other basic needs. • The COVID-19 pandemic disrupted and continues to disrupt supply chains, as it the pandemic remained as global threat, South Sudan is still battling the virus as death ceases increase to 133, with a stand of 12,600 cases while 24,680 people fully vaccinated with either J&J or AstraZeneca vaccines, this project will focus on advocacy and sensitize the communities to turn up for vaccinations once services arrive at their areas since the uptake of the vaccines is low compare to number of doses supply in the country.

2 - Project activities

1. Recruitment and deployment of healthcare workers i.e. Clinical Officers, Nurses, Midwives and field highly technical team to supervise the junior clinicians. 2. Early detection of epidemic diseases, carry out intensive surveillance, IPC practices, investigation and timely reporting to the next level of care. 3. Intensifying expanded activities on immunization by administering all the childhood diseases antigens, procurement and prepositioning emergency items to areas of interventions. 4. Prepositioning of life-saving healthcare services on the target of managing communicable and non-communicable diseases throughout the lifespan of the project in either static, mobile and outreaches at (Hospitals, PHCCs, and PHCUs) supplying other significant medical equipment that may include, BeMONC equipment, midwifery kits amongst other. 5. Enhance humanitarian-peace-development by building truth between aid actors, communities and the governing authorities, this will help the aids providers to operates smoothly without fear. 6. Ensuring quality provision of health services throughout the lifespan of the project, this will be achieved through quality supportive supervision with clear objectives with special focus on specific areas in the hospitals, PHCC/Us. 7. Defaulters tracing through community health-based services, the like of Boma health initiative, this will be achieved through coordinating with other actors and existing of our health project funded by the world bank in Ayod, this will help to identify and report the children who missed their interval doses to receive full coverage. 8. Requesting medicines and other medical supplies, the like of vaccines from; WHO, mosquito nets from PSI, UNICEF, MoH, UNFPA, Health Clusters as well as finding transportation through Logistics cluster WFP for emergency transport that will support the project. 9. Health education and promotion through hygiene and keys health messages. 10. Training of healthcare providers through on job and special training sessions on CMR, HIV, COVID-19, IPC, and on how to report suspected outbreaks and how they can response to.
