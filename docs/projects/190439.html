Project Summary

Ethiopia’s Education and Training Policy, and many education sector development programs (ESDPs) and strategies Clearly State equal education opportunity for all children including Children in Emergency Situation with emphasis for those Children affected by drought , conflict and other humanitarian Crisis .
Regardless of ample efforts by government of Ethiopia national and international developmental Partners Efforts to ensure access to education for all children the challenges and gaps with in Ethiopian Education System continued last five years crisis of drought, conflicts and other natural as well as mana made crisis make the problem more worth . Evidence to these recent humanitarian updates and reports shows According Ethiopian Education in Emergency response Strategic document for 2021/2023 the Ethiopian education system is still undermined by persistent challenges to access, retention/transition, equity, inclusion, and quality. Ethiopia has experienced a rapid expansion of primary education services, but this has had a significant impact on quality: suboptimal learning outcomes and slow development of foundational as well as transferable skills remain a significant concern. Particularly low efficiency and extremely high dropout rates, with low completion rates of primary and even fewer transferring to secondary is of high concern. In terms of equity, especially children living in poverty, rural areas, girls, children with disabilities, and pastoralist children have less access to education, as well as progress
and achievement. The Situation become Worth in 2019-2022 due added conflict and Drought in Various Parts of the country. recently sever in oromia regional State .The Oromia DRMO reported that the assessment identified a total population of 3,605,304 due to the current drought related emergency. East Bale Zone has the highest number of drought affected proportion (51.2% of the rural population) The Education Bureau has identified 1,765 primary, 671 pre-primary and 50 secondary schools are at risk of closure and students at risk of dropping out due to the impact of the drought. According to Regional Education Bureau statement, about 847,163 school-aged children (6-17 years) are affected by the emergency . With population movement and lack of community support for teachers’ remuneration, there will be high probability of school closures. Children who drop out of school are at risk of recruitment into daily laborer, early marriages for girls and are vulnerable to exploitation and abuse. Imagine 1day designed Education can not Wait ( ECW ) with the aim of ensuring access to quality ,resilient and relevant Primary and pre Primary Education opportunity for childrenAffected by Emergency in Ethiopian Oromia regional State .The Life of the Project Will be from 2022 to 2023. imagine 1day international Organization have years Experience and Experts of Implementation similar Education Projects With Ethiopia and Impacts thousands life children and Community affected by Emergency Crisis through designing and implementation of education and Child Protection programs both with in development and conflicts , Drought crisis and addressed the needs of IDPs ,returnees and host Community . This project will be implemented in East Haraghe Zone Babile Woreda and East Bale Zone Dawekachen Woreda With in the Two Targeted Woreda it Planned to create access to quality ,resilient and relevant Preprimary and Primary Education opportunity for 6993(50% Girls ) including children with disability , Idps, returnees and host community .the target include at risk of drop out children to be supported in conventional Class/Schools and Children enrolled to Non Formal Education ( ALP and ASR). will work on four results improving: access and learning environment : ,quality and relevance and ,retention and transition of emergency affected including children with disability in pre primary and primary education .
