1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project consists of creating spaces dedicated to the promotion of good child feeding practices in 8 accommodation centers in the district of Metuge and in the community of Impire.The growth in the number of PIN in Cabo Delgado region (which amounts to 712,000 - OCHA) highlights the risk exposure of the most vulnerable groups, including PLW.In Metuge district, there are 12,877 IDP families, totaling 55,981 people: 15,096 women, 12,588 men and 28,290 children (Rapid Response Plan OCHA).These spaces are intended to strengthen a vulnerable group - PLW and children up to 5 years of age - by clarifying, promoting and training the PLW population among internally displaced persons on issues related to good dietary and nutritional practices since pregnancy, using:1. nutritional screening;2. training of activists for active case identification and signaling for outpatient treatment;3. promotion of breastfeeding;4. promotion of proper food diversification;5. enrichment of infant feeding through resource to native products;6. culinary and tasting demonstrations in the context of training;7. IEC campaign within the scope of good practices in maternal and child nutrition.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

- Capacity building of activists through training with specific modules of PSEA (Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) so that they can contribute to the identification of possible situations of abuse;- Close articulation with the official services, namely with the District Services for Women and Social Action, for quick referral of detected situations of abuse;- Articulation with OCHA members for signaling and eventual referral of trauma or abuse situations within the beneficiary population;- Referral and signaling of malnutrition cases to health services;- IEC campaign accessible to the most vulnerable population on good feeding practices;- Early identification of risk situations in the household or generalized in the region in relation to severe hunger situations.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

The project aims to improve the nutritional health of the maternal and child population, through (among other activities) the involvement of women activists, residing in the accommodation centers themselves. It is intended to involve the community itself and the leadership in the selection process of the activists, so that it is a process that encourages the relationship and contact between “activist - maternal and child population”.Regarding the themes to be addressed in the sessions, mothers and caregivers will be consulted in an earlier stage to determine which topics raise the most interest and relevance. Also, at the end of each session, an assessment of the methodology and the topic addressed will be done, aiming at a continuous improvement and involvement of the target group with the project activities. Also, in relation to the communication activity, which involves painting murals and banners, the intention is to involve the community regarding the final choice of messages, phrases and images to be used, so that there is greater identification with the project's communication.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The project foresees, through the inherent proximity factor, to identify all situations of people (women or children) who present some degree of disability, through the local leaders who will be called upon to collaborate in this identification. These leaders will receive the established action protocol for the inclusion of people with disabilities, which will be specifically prepared for the context in question. When these situations are identified, strategies will be defined that allow all people to access the activities, either by adapting the activities or the places where they are carried out, or by prioritizing the service, or even through the relocation of the team to the locations where these people are, if they cannot move. Other measures will be considered if those planned do not allow access for some people.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project does not provide for impact assessments or measures to compensate for environmental implications, since the impacts are not considered significant (the project does not foresee constructions with soil mobilizations, for example).There will be few travels, since the population will not travel and the team of nutritionists, who will travel to the places on a regular basis, will prioritize public transportation in their travels. The communication campaign designed here will not use widespread distribution materials so that no waste is produced, using only banners and mural painting.
