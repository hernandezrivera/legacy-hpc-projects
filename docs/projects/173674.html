Project Summary

The project aims at providing livelihoods opportunities to the most vulnerable IDPs in host communities, returnees and members of host communities including women, men and youths in Mafa LGA of Borno State. This will be done through multiple livelihoods interventions, such as provision of skill and vocational training women, men and youths, setting up of small scale business with start-up kits to the participants, in order to support their income-generating activities. In addition, the project will carry out community discussions with relevant stakeholders as well as carry out awareness and sensitization on peace and social co-existence, GBV and human rights to ensure and enhance socio-economic cohesion among IDPs, returnees and members of host communities in project location

1 - Needs

North-East Nigeria has experienced violence and armed conﬂict since 2009 due to the Boko Haram insurgency and operations by the security forces in Nigeria. The multi-year Humanitarian Response Strategy (HRS) (2019-2021), stated that the violent conﬂict has resulted in deaths, displacement of about 1.8 million people (440,000 women, 364,000 men, 614,000 girls, and 516,000 boys) loss, and destruction of property, deterioration of living conditions, disruption of livelihood, accumulated stress, weakened resilience, and heightened vulnerabilities. The conﬂict has had disproportionate impacts along gender lines as in other armed conﬂicts, casualties in NE Nigeria are predominantly young men. Similarly, the separation of families and the systematic migration of men towards larger and safer cities to be able to provide for their families back home has translated into a signiﬁcant increase in female-headed households. Also, both social and economic structures, livelihoods have been destroyed, women, men, boys and girls including persons with disability have been affected the most. This has caused many to become more vulnerable to sexual exploitation and abuse and could resort to negative coping mechanisms. Social cohesion is weakened due to mistrust and lack of respect for each other or socio-cultural and religious beliefs, thus threatening communities’ effective socio-economic participation and their overall development. Although the displacement has witnessed some level of life saving interventions by existing Development and humanitarian partner, there is still an urgent need for early recovery interventions, including Livelihoods stabilization and income recovery, especially for female headed households, for returnees, IDPs in Host Communities, along with members from the Host Communities as well as restoration and or enhancement of social cohesion among these groups.

2 - Activities

The project will focus on two sector objectives. The first aspect will be addressing Objective one, Livelihood stabilized and income recovered. The second component of the project will focus on objective two, Inclusiveness & peaceful co-existence among IDPs, returnees and host communities. it will encourage, engagement and participation of women men and youths including persons with disabilities in peace processes These will be achieved through the following activities: Act1. Carry out assessments to determine preferred and innovative context-demand-driven vocational and income-generating activities for women, men and youth, including persons with disabilities. Act2. Provision of skills and vocational training with startup kit/capital for 100 women, men youths especially female headed households and link them to have access to IGAs to boost their socio-economic capital and self-reliance. Act3 support 100 women, men youths especially female headed households shall be provided with start-up kits to establish small-scale businesses. Act4. Organize and support 200 women, men and youths to form 4 groups of Village loans and saving associations (VLSA). (25 members per group) made up of those provided with startup kits/funds. Act5. Provide 100 women, men youths with 2 cycles of 3 days training course in basic business skills, including bookkeeping, saving, and basic business management skills. Act6. Carry out 8 discussion sessions on Peace and gender-based violence (GBV) prevention including community leaders, community security officials, returnees, IDPs and members of the host communities. Act 7. Sensitize 1000 community members on gender equality, peaceful co-existence and human rights. Act8. dissemination of peace and reconciliation messages within target communities.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

The proposed project is designed such that gender is mainstreamed at all stages of the project (design, implementation and monitoring and evaluation). It will enhance women empowerment as the project targets female beneficiaries including persons living with disabilities to promote gender equality. Also, project will ensure compliance with COVID-19 principles and also carry out sensitization on Prevention of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse, ensure the strict implementation of Do No Harm Principle and child safeguarding.

9.5 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members?

Sirri Care will work with community members including women, men, girls, and boys, particularly female-headed households, and people with disabilities to establish gender-sensitive and appropriate compliant mechanisms in project target communities. The established complaint channels will provide for women and girls, particularly female-headed households and people with disabilities that are directly affected by the project a means to raise complaints about the quality of program and staff behavior. Also, there will be complaint and feedback desks and boxes at strategic areas in the project communities to ensure that community members are able to provide feedback and complaints about the project.
