Project Summary

This project is planned to target 3 localities mainly Dilling, Dalami and Rashad locality in South Kordofan State. It aims at contributing to HRP 2019 outcome 2 through provision of NFIs and Essential shelter materials for 10000 households (6000 female and 4000 male), and training of 2000 IDPs and returnees vulnerable households in transitional shelter and fuel efficiency environmentally friendly.
For gender, youth and protection mainstreaming in the project, we have considered the traditional context in many women, especially among the IDPs and returnees have very limited participation. We will make every effort to promote the involvement of women and youth female and male as well as provide them with opportunities to gain skills. We will conduct gender mobilization, sensitization and awareness rising at community level as part of the assessment, training and provision of NFIs packages which will continue to be built into training programmes, and all employers involved with the project must obey employment laws related to gender and protection of women, girls and children with social security risk at project sites.
Accountability to affected populations (AAP); we will make information available on the need for participation, consultation and use feedback mechanisms to determine beneficiary preference for project interventions and feedback mechanism will be developed. AAP will be mandatorily and incorporate with measures to ensure that the voices of the beneficiaries at all levels are integrated from the design phase, implementation and through to the evaluation phase. Grievance mechanisms are an integral part of this system and will be incorporated along with checks and balance to ensure accountability with follow up focus group discussion, spot check on implementation and key informant interviews.

Needs

Inter-agency assessments conducted in South Kordofan State mainly in Dilling, Dalami, and Rashad locality between 2017 and later 2018. The assessment findings indicate; 1303 IDPs Households (9611 persons) and 1153 returnees Households (8400 persons) were assessed in Dilling locality, majority of IDPs and returnees settled in a muddy and flood prone area surrounded by bushed which may exposed them to many safety and protection risks of which 40% Households are headed by women and 2% headed by children. Protracted IDPs caseload in Dilling locality had received NFIs package before 4 years and are in need of urgent replenishment of NFIs as most of the IDPs were forced to move to new areas by landlords. 8088 IDPs and 8082 returnees in Dalami locality settled in 4 villages and Dalami of which around 1511 Households were verified by IOM and some 834 new IDPs Households (2024 persons) were not verified by HAC/IOM. 80% of the IDPs and returnees require NFIs to renew/rehabilitate shelter for winter and rainy season. Their existing shelter condition is very poor to harsh weather during the winter and rainy season, it was recommended that, there should be NFIs full package distribution for the protracted IDPs and new returnees arrivals such as utensils, tarpaulins and blankest. The assessment team from 12-26 October 2018 assessed 22324 protracted IDPs (3576 households) in 14 villages and 3000 returnees in 3 villages in Rashad locality who told team that their immediate need is ES-NFIs for rehabilitation/renewal of shelter. Most protracted IDPs them had received NFIs since 2015 while new arrived returned did not receive any ES-NFI Package. Secondly, the devaluation of local currency and high inflation rate >70% have led to dramatic increase in prices (between 200% and 300%) which have affected the protracted IDPs, and returnees persons to access basic ES-NFIs needs which have increased vulnerability of poor households among IDPs and returnees.

Output 3. Impact on environment/natural resources

The project has neutral impact on the environment the project is part of HRP 2019 responding to the protracted IDPs situation and returnees we expect excessive tree cutting for Shelter construction and wood, worsen by the depletion of forestry resources will make it difficult for IDPs and returnees to access building materials for shelter, and firewood for heating and lighting in the night. In the project activities we proposed providing NFIs and Essential shelter materials, for IDPs and returnees and vulnerable households and support them to participated in transitional shelter and fuel efficiency environmentally friendly trainings which will cover SSB technology instead of cooked burned bricks for shelter construction. To reduce use of high flammable construction materials for shelter, and impact of cooking sites, we will conduct awareness rising on risk of fire outbreak and promote use of fuel efficiency stove (FES) alternative to use 3 stones and firewood, butane gas instead of firewood and promote an integrated protection, EN-NFIs, vocational and life skills livelihoods support and use of soil stabilize block and minimize use of wood and grasses.
