Needs

Conflict in Ukraine has had a destabilizing impact on the country as a whole and has also had differing effects on women and men.The United Nations Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs (OCHA) estimates that 70% of those in need of assistance are elderly, women and children. Households have had to use up their savings on basic goods, and female-headed households, especially those with children, are thought to be the most vulnerable and at risk for adopting “negative coping strategies”. While women in the general population of Ukraine face differing forms of discrimination, in conflict setting some women groups (elderly women or women with disabilities) face human rights abuses, intolerance and particularly severe discrimination in access to employment, health care, education and other basic services. Among the many vital humanitarian needs in Shelter/NFI sector, the following two Winterization needs should be highlighted: 1. Critical infrastructure damage (disruption of centralized heating systems). According to the “Humanitarian Needs Overview” for the year 2018, the disruption of critical infrastructure in settlements along the ‘contact line’ is becoming the daily ‘normal’ for millions of people. While hostilities continue near critical infrastructure, damage to supply systems and – as a knock-on effect – the collapse of inter-dependent heating systems may be inevitable, leading to increased humanitarian needs across areas of critical concern during the winter months where temperatures routinely drop to negative 25 degrees centigrade. 2. Depleted people’s capacity to prepare and winterize their homes. According to the “Humanitarian Needs Overview” for the year 2018, the protracted conflict has dramatically depleted the affected population’s capacity to winterize their homes and prepare for winter. Alarmingly, REACH has found in an ongoing assessment in the GCA that 58 per cent of households in rural Donetska Oblast, 34 per cent in urban Donetska Oblast, 43 per cent of households in rural Luhanska Oblast, and 29 per cent in urban Luhanska Oblast stated that they do not have enough supplies for the winter. Between 2017 and 2018, Emmanuel Charity Association replaced heating systems in 2211 households in the towns Maryinka and Krasnohorivka and distributed 2847 tons of solid fuel. However, these efforts have not covered the existing need. We have been continuously receiving new applications from the locals requesting humanitarian assistance in form of solid-fuel/electric heaters and solid fuel for the winter. Working in cooperation with local Military-Civil administration, Emmanuel received applications from the locals requesting humanitarian assistance:- 10054 HHs in need of solid fuel; - 1408 HHs in need of solid fuel heaters;- 2830 HHs in need of electric heaters.Increasing numbers of vulnerable families eligible for assistance has forced Emmanuel to apply even stricter prioritization criteria in the selection of target groups. Our main target group will be non-displaced people who are unable to access basic winterization means, with priority to families living within 0-5 km to the Line of Contact who - or whose members - present one or more of the following situations or vulnerabilities:- Older person (60+) unable to care for self- Disable (1-2 group invalids) or serious medical conditions- Single parent- Large family- Pregnant woman- Persons in hardships (preferably families with children) When working out our humanitarian response plan, Emmanuel was forced to drastically reduce the number of recipients, but to assure the supply of sufficient quantities to cover the entire cold season, as follows:- 3200 vulnerable HHs to be provided with solid fuel (3 tons of solid fuel per HH)- 300 vulnerable HHs to receive solid fuel heaters and will be provided with solid fuel (3 tons of solid fuel per HH)- 700 vulnerable HHs to receive electric heaters

Activities & outcomes

ACTIVITY 1. Distribution of solid fuel In order to reach Shelter/NFI Objective 1 (address essential shelter and NFI need of the most vulnerable IDPs and conflict affected population through monetized/in-kind assistance and contingency), Emmanuel Charity Association plans to provide the vulnerable population with solid fuel. OUTCOME: As a result, 3200 vulnerable households will receive solid fuel for the winter (3 tons of solid fuel per household).300 vulnerable households will receive solid fuel heaters and solid fuel for winter (3 tons of solid fuel per household) ACTIVITY 2: Distribution of electric heaters and utility network repairs and connection (electric heaters with installation)In order to reach Shelter/NFI Objective 3 (provide/upgrade permanent shelter solutions for the most vulnerable conflict affected population), Emmanuel Charity Association plans to distribute and install electric heaters in apartments. OUTCOME: As a result 700 vulnerable households will receive electric heaters and will benefit from utility network repairs and connections.

Indicators & targets

INDICATORS:• Number of vulnerable households receiving solid fuel support for winter 3200• Number of vulnerable households receiving solid fuel heaters (with installation) and solid fuel - 300• Number of vulnerable households receiving electric heaters (with installation) - 700; Emmanuel has also designed multiple indicators to measure distinct benefits for people in need. The influence of the project as to delivery of solid fuel and installation of boilers will be measured with the help of indexes and markers of project evaluation, namely, with the help of number of beneficiaries that received help and the index of decreased tension in the society. The index of tension level in the society shall be measured with the help of questionnaire containing the following indexes (the questionnaire will be filled out by every head of beneficiary household): - Index of promptness of works implementation (delivery of solid fuel and/or installation of heaters) (0 to 10 points)- Index of quality of the works (0 to 10 points)- Fullness of received information as to the safety rules (0 to 10 points)- Fullness of received information as to performance characteristics of the installed equipment (0 to 10 points)- Index of satisfaction (0 to 10)- Suggestions and proposals. TARGETS:We plan to distribute 10500 tons of solid fuel among 3500 households; replace and install 300 solid fuel heaters and 700 electric heaters.
