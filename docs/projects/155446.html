Project Summary

The effects of drought leads to many challenges experienced by the communities in general as a result of food insecurity and caregivers tend to look up to adolescent boys and girls to contribute to household needs especially food. Negative coping mechanisms including selling of assets, transactional sex, marrying off children which leads to increased teenage pregnancies and the risk of contracting STIs and HIV. This places adolescent girls from poor households at risk of sexual exploitation as they engage in transaction sex which often leads to unwanted pregnancies and motherhood. . These adolescents further face rejection, stigma, discrimination and abuse when they fall pregnant and often lack the most needed support nurturing their babies and dealing with the social issues that are consequential to child pregnancy and early motherhood.
This project aims to strengthen the protection of adolescent and young mothers in 15 districts by integrating PSS in Nutrition response and Health response for CMAM and Maternal Health.
The outcome of the project would be empowered and resilient adolescent and young mothers. Secondly, the project would lead to capacitated Health workers, nutritionists, social workers, CCWs and VHW in integrated approach in delivering humanitarian assistance. It will also facilitate health facilities with effective referral systems to psycho-social and child protection services.

Needs

The needs of adolescents experiencing drought induced challenges resulting in negative coping mechanisms such as selling of assets, transactional sex, marrying off children which leads to increased teenage pregnancies and the risk of contracting STIs and HIV include, among other things:• Lack of safe space for adolescents to share and articulate their experiences • Lack of support in assuming new role as a mother/daughter-in-law, single mother. School drop out as they face rejection usually by both families (their own family as well as the family of the person responsible for their pregnancy/father of the child)

Activities

• 15 000 pregnant adolescents and young mothers reached with Psychosocial and Child Protection Services • 750 village health workers and CCWs trained on child protection early childhood development and parenting. • 450 New mother to mother support groups in 15 districts to reach 4 500 caregivers (Males & Females) with face to face orientation on parenting on early childhood development with a focus on child protection and development • 450 support group leaders trained in-group management, PSS tools and parenting in 15 districts 15,000 mothers/ caregivers (Males& Females) participating in the Community based ECD activities
