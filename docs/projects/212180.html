Project Summary

This project is designed to cover part of the gap in the Shelter sector in IDP sites and communities in two approaches: dignified shelter with infrastructure and houses rehabilitations in Salqeen, Jisr Ash Shougur, Dana, Darkosh, Maaret Tamsrin, Daret Azzaa, Atareb, Jarablus and Mare’e subdistricts, through improving and enhancing the living conditions in the camps and communities, provide more dignity and sustainability for the families living in poor conditions against the harsh weather and provide the sustainability of shelter approach integrated and interlinked to WASH, ERL, protection, and GBV activities. IYD will target 1,750 tents in three subdistricts, Dana, Darkoush, and Maret Tamsrin, with site planning and site preparation to improve the living conditions inside the camps, rehabilitation of the roads to increase the access in the camp and facilitate the movement of residence. Installing 1,750 dignified shelter units to provide privacy, dignity, and protection to the IDPs, addressing the PWD needs.
This project is designed for community engagement during the cycle of life. HLP; IYD will conserve the ownership of individuals and groups, is preserved, and cannot be infringed on in any case. The construction and installation process of dignified units will be determined as a result of FGDs; one of two modalities will be followed: 1st will be carried out by local suppliers and under the supervision of a trained and qualified engineering staff; 2nd Through cash for shelter modality, IYD will follow the guidance of the shelter cluster(cash and voucher for shelter basic guidance-NW Syria), Cash Working Group, and IYD cash policies, and the implementation of dignified shelter through cash modality will be on three transactions. The First stage: after IYD finalizes the Beneficiaries list and verification process, and technical assessment for the existing tents, 40% out of 3,000 USD will be distributed through cash vouchers. The second stage will be during the implementation by the beneficiaries themselves. When the progress is achieved reaches 50% of the work, the second patch will be released, which is 30%. Third Stage: the remaining 30% will be released after 100% completion of the activity and quality checks have been done by the IYD team.
Following cluster reports, IYD will target 700 HH for moderate repair works with shelter rehabilitation activities in the host communities; this activity will be implemented in Salqin and Darkosh, subdistricts in Idlib governorate. And Suran and Mare subdistricts in Aleppo governorate. This will be implemented by Cash for Shelter modality by doing the medium and moderate repair works for 700 HHs (3,850 beneficiaries) in the host communities by providing 1500 USD for each HH for the rehabilitation works. Following the cluster reports, IYD will provide NFI kits and winterization assistance (180 USD), to 6,600 households. IYD will target sites with critical needs and a high percentage of vulnerable groups in Dana, Darkosh, and Maaret Tamsrin subdistricts in Idlib governorate and Atareb, Mare', Daret Azza, and Jarablus subdistricts in Aleppo governorate to cover the needs of IDPs.
