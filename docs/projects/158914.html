Project Summary

The intervention seeks to provide water accessibility instruments in significance of efficient and effective water utilization and support the bedouin community’s resilience portrayed in protection against settlers abuse, better animal productivity and enhanced economic situation. In this context, the project targets the inhabitants of Fera'a community comprised of 13 households with a total of 104 individuals mainly considered as herders in addition to targeting their livestock through providing water inputs. The activities proposed are summarized as follows (The increased investment per individual is due to the fact that the beneficiaries are a small bedouin community that faces Israeli violations):
• Water quality testing of six samples of the main water source of Ain Feraa and the provision of a filter
• Spring rehabilitation including cleaning and maintenance, fencing against settlers, construction of retaining walls, closing of water cement pipes, reparation of the road leading to the spring through stairs construction
• Supplying water tanks and the installation of transmission lines.
• Conducting training on water management and transmission lines maintenance and Filter Maintenance.
• Providing drinkers and semiautomatic tanks.
The methodology of selecting the beneficiaries is derived from a field visit to the community, it was examined through sight, observation, interviews and questionnaires in terms of accessibility to basic means of life and it was chosen on the basis of being the most vulnerable community compared to nearby areas.
UAWC centralizes its projects within Area C and strives at promoting the steadfastness and sustainability of small farmers’ livelihoods and protecting and defending the farmers’ national and democratic rights, not to mention that one of the strategic objectives within its strategic plan 2018-2020, UAWC seeks at responding to emergencies, different variables and instability, having worked extensively with the most marginalized and vulnerable areas in Palestine and being established in correspondence to the continuous Israeli abuses in Palestine.

1 - Needs

The community of Fera’a is an extremely vulnerable community. The settlers of thesurrounding settlements keep abusing the community members and exploiting the naturalspring of Ein Fera’a; which is the only water source for the community, for their bathingrituals. Access to the spring is females' and children's responsibility and herding. Sheepbreeders depend on their source of income mainly on sheep breeding with an averagemonthly income of NIS 1,670 below the national poverty line. This is considered a greatchallenge for these families due to the difficulty of transporting fodder and water, and thepossibility of transporting them to farmers in this area due to the lack of water lines ortankers, more than 300 cups of water are required for these herds monthly (ie, 10 cups perday for 1,200 heads of sheep raised in this area). Lack of water will weaken thephysiological capacity of these sheep to productivity and disease resistance. The lack ofclean water also limits the consumption of water for sheep In addition to that the region isone of the areas with high temperatures. In numbers, and as per UAWC assessment, 59%of households rely on livestock only and 41% rely on agricultural and livestock.Furthermore, 75% of agricultural production goes to personal consumption and animalfeeding. The average number of sheep and goats owned by household is 87 heads.

Activity 5. Installation of a SolarWater Pump: One Unit of water pump and one valve. Activity 6. Supplying and Installationof Transmission Lines: 2 Pipes of 1,200 Meters. Activity 7. Conduct Trainings in watermanagement and the transmission line maintenance water to reduce water losses withinformation about the amount of water supply from the spring, and to acquire theminimum knowledge of maintenance in case of emergencies (clogging, faults in the pipe)Activity 8. the provision of tanks and semi – automatick drinkers and plastic tanks foreach farmer in his area and linked water network to the water network Activity 9.Installation of a Biogas unit utilizing the animal waste that is available in large quantitiesand which can be used to generate electricity through a reactor or for providing the houseswith gas for cooking, in addition, to be exploited to make organic fertilizer for agriculture.Activity 10. Providing therapeutic activities for women and children such asproviding a safe play area for children( swing, slides, seesaw with fences for protection) inaddition to nurturing and psychological rehabilitation (character, family, decisions),behavior modification.
