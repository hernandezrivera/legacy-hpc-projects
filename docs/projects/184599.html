Project Summary

Mercy-USA is proposing a project that includes immediate response to the urgent humanitarian needs while simultaneously rolling out a livelihood recovery for 27,960 people and 79,200 livestock in Galmudug and Hiraan regions of Somalia. The purpose of this project is to improve households’ immediate access to food for crisis-affected people through provision of unconditional food for cash based transfers to meet their basic needs during times of crisis and to support stability, protect and restore livelihoods, and build resilience through provision of seasonally appropriate livelihood inputs and technical support in rural and peri-urban settings. The program seeks to carry out relief assistance, veterinary support through equipped and trained CAHWs, support fodder farming and seed preparation for the crisis-affected population.
The population of Hobyo district, one of the largest districts in Somalia, primarily rely on pastoralism and fishery as their economic activities while those in Abudwak, and Buloburte districts rely on agriculture and pastoralism. Pastoralists and agro-pastoralists in these locations have less and less access to food as their herds reduce due to distress, sale and death; consequently, milk production has dropped, livestock prices are diminishing and water and grain prices are rising. Women-headed households are the worst hit by the drought because mothers have to make a choice between caring for their household and protecting their livelihoods; the former usually prevails.
Farmers in Buloburte district have experienced extensive crop failure and loss of livestock due to the poor performance of the rains. These rural farmers practice irrigation farming and due to the reduction in the economic value of their farms, most farmers are unable to afford the fuel prices to power irrigation systems for watering their crops as well as the cost of purchasing farming inputs. Mercy-USA has worked with these farmers through WFP-supported projects that rehabilitated irrigation canals conveying water to their farms through gravity, which has enhanced Mercy-USA’s understanding of their livelihoods in order to design activities that reduce the vulnerability of agro-pastoral communities, including the importance of teaching farmers positive coping mechanisms to recurrent drought through fodder production. Currently agro-pastoralists practice migration, minimize food consumption or slaughter weak and aging animals as coping mechanisms during drought periods. Drought resistant fodder farming will perform better in the dry periods as compared to conventional cash crops thus supplying vulnerable farmers with fodder to feed their animals.
According to FSNAU-FEWSNET-2021-Post-Gu-Assessment. Nearly 3.5 million people across Somalia are expected to face food consumption gaps or depletion of livelihood assets indicative of Crisis (IPC Phase 3)1 or worse outcomes through the end of the year, in the absence of humanitarian assistance. Out of the total 3.5 million people in need of urgent food assistance, 640,000 people face significant food consumption gaps. Mercy-USA is therefore proposing relief intervention reaching 230 vulnerable households in Hobyo district, through unconditional cash based transfer to purchase food items. The relief activity is designed to provide poor and vulnerable households with reliable, predictable, and adequate food items that meet their immediate food consumption gaps and protect against food insecurity and malnutrition risks expected as a result of the humanitarian crisis. The project will pay attention to household targeting, the process of enrolment and timely top-ups.
Mercy-USA will ensure that people with disabilities participate in the implementation of project activities by ensuring that they are represented in all community meetings. During routine and regular program monitoring and evaluation, Mercy-USA staff will collect disaggregated data by disability, age and gender.

1. - Projects activities

1. Relief distribution while targeting 230 households in Hobyo district. Mercy-USA will explain through the relief community selection committee (CSC) the modality i.e. Relief distribution through Cash/voucher based transfer. Mercy-USA will provide entitlement of 55.00 USD per person per month for 3 months.2. Train 20 CAHWs in Abudwak district, 20 CAHWs in Buloburte district and 20 CAHWs in Hobyo district for (10-day training). 3. Distribute veterinary starter kits to the 60 newly trained CAHWs. 4. Train 20 lead farmers on fodder production, seed bulking and marketing for both communal consumption and commercial purposes in Buloburte district (5-day training).5. Provide drought-resilient fodder grass seeds and farming inputs to 20 fodder production training beneficiaries in Hiiraan Region (Bulaburte), including baling boxes, rope, sickles and knives.6. Distribute fodder grass seeds to 40 secondary beneficiaries. Mercy-USA aims to reach 230 vulnerable households in Hobyo district, through unconditional cash based transfer to purchase food items. The project will pay attention to household targeting, the process of enrolment and timely top-ups. Selection process will be through community based approach which is through the CSCs. This program will focus on closing the existing gaps between the demands of the ultimate consumers (livestock keepers) and the supply of services offered by the CAHWs. Mercy-USA will target CAHW activities through supporting 108 CAHWs in Abudwak, Buloburte and Hobyo districts to reach vulnerable pastoralists in the vast geographical area. Mercy-USA proposes to build the capacity of agro-pastoralists to recover from the effects of prolonged drought and to improve pasture availability during times of stress through fodder farming targeting 20 lead farmers and 40 mentored farmers through farmer to farmer capacity building. Mercy-USA will seek to deliver interventions in areas where the drought has hit the hardest and livestock are affected by opportunistic diseases. All site locations and activities will be determined based on community priorities. Borrowing from lessons learned in previous CAHW activities, it is crucial to provide an adequate number of days for CAHW training due to high rates of illiteracy. Therefore, Mercy-USA will carry out a 10-day training for the 60 new CAHWs using FAO’s Community Animal Health Workers training module in Somalia and guidance from LEGS. Upon completion of the training, each CAHW will receive one veterinary starter kit to start their business.A starter kit includes broad spectrum antibiotics, antihelminthes, gloves, syringes, drenching guns and castration equipment. A nominal fee will be charged for the provision of animal health care services to community members. This practice helps to ensure project sustainability and the replenishment of kits to continually support livestock owners. For restocking of the kits, CBAHWs will be linked to nearby veterinary stores available in Somalia, where there is market access to key supplies. Mercy-USA will provide technical skills and good agricultural practices for household fodder production, fodder seed propagation, crop production process, harvesting, management and marketing for 20 small-scale farmers in Buloburte district in Somalia. Mercy-USA will promote small-scale fodder production to benefit vulnerable pastoralist families settled along the River Shabelle through the distribution of seeds for fodder, capacity building and provision of irrigation subsidy for poor farmers. This will mitigate the extent of livestock losses during the next crisis. The 5-day training package will include fodder propagation, harvesting, seed bulking and storage. Mercy-USA will emphasize conservation by use of simple hay baling technologies to ensure that the farmers can harvest more fodder and store it in a manner that is easily transported by the livestock keepers.

2.1.1 - What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

The voucher system allows beneficiaries to choose from a list of food items approved by the Food Security Cluster. This increased autonomy enhances the dignity of beneficiaries and enables them to select food items in line with household needs. Moreover, Mercy-USA favors the voucher transfer modality as it: • Enhances beneficiary dignity: making choices for themselves empowers the beneficiaries and can help restore a sense of dignity that is often disrupted by forced displacement as HHs able to prioritize based on HH needs; • supports local markets through cash injection for small/medium size vendors and therefore create multiplier effects for the benefits of the humanitarian intervention; • increases the cost-effectiveness of Mercy-USA's response.

2.1.2 - Explain how the project design considered market functionality?

Through Mercy-USA's field offices, Mercy-USA has carried out an assessment of the local market and determined the proposed food items are available through vendors in the markets which remain functional and accessible. Mercy-USA will continuously conduct market surveys in the target areas to asses markets and vendors` capacities and to finalize the list of suppliers where the beneficiaries could redeem the vouchers. Mercy-USA will support markets through providing technical support or training, small grants/loans to food security service providers and generating demand for the products/services among consumers.
