Project Summary

The purpose of this proposal is to support public health responses to contain the spread of the COVID-19 pandemic by reducing morbidity and mortality through targeted WASH interventions.
Mvuramanzi Trust (MMT) proposes to work in Bulawayo Metropolitan, Hwange Urban and Murehwa
In Bulawayo Metropolitan MMT intends to support 10 Health care facilities with WASH enabling infrastructure , supply 6,000 Hygiene Kits to vulnerable community members, reach 40,000 people with hygiene messages and installing 138 hand washing facilities in COVID-19 high risk areas.
In Hwange urban MMT intends to support 6 health care facilities with WASH enabling infrastructure, supply 566 hygiene kits to vulnerable community members, reach 1,800 people with hygiene messages and installing176 hand washing facilities in COVID-19 high risk areas.
In Murehwa, Mvuramanzi Trust intends to support 7 health care facilities with WASH enabling infrastructure, supply 1,500 hygiene kits to vulnerable members of the community, reach 4,000 people with hygiene messages and installing 112 hand washing facilities in COVID-19 high risk areas.

Plan fields

1 - Activities

1. Improving awareness among the population on the importance of hand washing with soap and ‘respiratory hygiene’ through the use of sms messages, loud hailers, megaphones and banners. 2. Providing access to critical hand washing facilities including soap and alcohol-based sanitizers. This will be done through the installation of tippy taps and provision of PVC water urns at strategic areas. Liquid soap will also be provided to the hand washing facilities care takers and hygiene promoters to replenish the water urns and tippy taps.3. Distribution of WASH Hygiene kits to vulnerable members of the community. Selection of the vulnerable members will be done through the Ministry responsible for social welfare, community leaders and church leaders. A verification process will be carried out by a team comprising District Officers, Implementing Partners and the Local Authority. The hygiene kits will be comprising of bars of soap and buckets. 4. Supporting the repair/ rehabilitation of WASH infrastructure for Health Care Facilities (HCFs) responding to COVID-19. This activity will be implemented at Rural HCFs where MMT proposes to construct double BVIPs for both males and females with disability and women friendly infrastructure. 5. Supporting emergency rehabilitation of water points in health care facilities, isolation centers and or surrounding communities to increase water availability. In Rural HCFs, MMT proposes to rehabilitate a total of 10 boreholes to improve water availability. In urban HCFs, MMT intends to install water tanks and stand pipes to augment availability of water.
