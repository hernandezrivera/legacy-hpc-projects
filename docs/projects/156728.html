Project Summary

Two centers in two locations { Northern of Hama CFS in Ziarah village, and reach to ( Al ziyadiah, kastoon, al mansorah, zayzon ) Western of Idleb rural ( jisr alshogour ) CFS in ( Zarzor town and outreach in Qunayya / Yaqoubia / Al hamamah),} will focus on reducing risks and strengthening protective factors for children to ensure their physical, social and emotional development that will contribute to the cluster priority of protecting children and mitigating the effects of violence and displacement. The proposed interventions are aimed to reduce/mitigate the impact of threats to children’s physical and psychosocial security in Given the seriousness of the protection threats experienced by children affected by conflict and displacement, the child protection interventions proposed are one of the frontline services that are critical to the physical and psychosocial survival of conflict-affected and displaced children. This project will promote girls’ participation in the CFSs and provide them with opportunities to safely talk about sexual violence and other gender-specific CP concerns. MF will also enhance the ability of CP staff to detect and support specific vulnerabilities amongst girls and boys through comprehensive case management and referral. CAAFAG (will be approached by MARAM case managers), and children with complicated mental health problems will be referred to Other service providers. Children involved or at risk of hazardous labor and in the same line, the center will provide parenting programs to the parents and caregivers. According to HNO In 81 percent of assessed communities, respondents reported child labor preventing school attendance was an issue of concern. This issue will be approached by MF case managers to comprehensively be managed by working with children and their caregivers to promote minimal standards and involve children in MF CFSs. The activities will aim at increasing boys’ and girls' knowledge and awareness of possible threats, allowing them to better protect themselves. MF will also be involved in raising awareness on the prevention of recruitment of children by armed groups, children involved in hazardous labor and prevention of family separation and will provide cash in case management to who needs regarding set criteria for ( labor, exploitation, recruitment, and who needs some special equipment). The main target will be parents, caregivers and community stakeholders such as religious leaders, local council members, teachers, medical point’s directors, and etc. MF will train caseworkers to identify and respond to the needs of such children. MF will use the CP sub-cluster SOPs and referral pathway to provide the needed services through case management. In the child-friendly space (CFS), dedicated time and space will be created for adolescent girls where they can be together and where they do not have to compete with boys. MF will ensure that a female facilitator is available to work with the girls, who can offer confidentiality, warmth, and advice. MF will seek also to link CFS to other services such as education, health, and social services to enhance referral of children in need of special assistance. MF seek to build capacities of child protection staff and community stakeholders to provide protection services for children according to CPMS. These Interventions will aim at addressing vulnerabilities related to child labor, child recruitment, child marriage, and family separation. This project will work with leaders and Community Based Protection to prevent labor, exploitation, and recruitment.

Needs

The seven years of conflict in Syria have increased the suffering of civilians including the suffering of armed conflict, blockade, homelessness, lack of basic needs in addition to multiple and severe protection risks. In spite of the surrounding circumstances and the poor security situation in Syria, the humanitarian actors proceed to respond to the enormous scale of basic humanitarian needs, especially those related to protection issues. And after the displacement that occurred toward Northern states of Syria has exacerbated family stresses, disrupted normal patterns of living and crumbled social support systems exposing children to protection threats and psychosocial distress. these children will be forced to process their conflict experience alone, which may prevent them from being able to fully engage in daily activities such as playing, learning, or basic social interaction. According to the report of HNO 2019, the most important concerns of child protection within the Syrian context during the current crisis include the child labor with its various causes, intensity, and consequences, along with the concern of separated and unaccompanied children with all the services offered for this category of children. In addition to the psychological disorders and distress of both children and caregivers. Child labor section focuses on the proportions of communities that have the phenomenon of child labor, including boys and girls labor, which restricts them from going to school. Focus was on two special types of cases, child-headed household and the families that contain people of special needs, The psychosocial needs section focused on measuring the intensity of the mental distress for both children and caregivers and the consequent type of services required for each level of the psychological distress and The category of separated and unaccompanied children is considered as the most affected by the present conflict. Separation is associated with many other risks that threaten this category including poverty, exploitation, violence, labor, and recruitment. Therefore, (the plain of Al gab) in the northern Hama countryside Ziarah village, and in Al ziyadiah, kastoon, al mansorah, zayzon subject to the Syrian opposition had priority, Where the local people began to return from the camps to their areas of residence, especially after the signing of the agreement between the countries on the cessation of hostilities and the withdrawal of heavy weapons between the parties to the conflict, The area and around was almost empty, so there are a lot of risk factors surrounding them and also there are several villages surrounding the visiting area will be targeted, Local councils in the region have called for a meeting with all organizations working close to that area to support the services, calling for the establishment of centers for children and women, empowerment and education ., In the western countryside of Idlib, adjacent to the Jisr al-Shughour area, Zerzur, and the surrounding area of Qunayya / Yaqoubia / Al hamamah especialy the random camps, The data indicate that there are no child-friendly spaces in spite of population inflation there from IDPs and host communities

Implementation of activities

- The project will Increase access for boys and girls to quality CP interventions in line with the CPMS according to the principles of CP sub-cluster to establish CFS, according to SOPs and CPMS, and based on HRP and HNO 2019. all activities will take into consideration the balance and gender between children and disabilities we will secure services either outreach or vehicle which transport them to/from the center. Firstly Establishing children and youth safe spaces. Activities will focus on mitigating the risks of child labor and encouraging them to return to school, through awareness sessions about the risks of child labor and help them to learn a job that does not affect on their life, development, and education. It will also target caregivers at the same line through awareness sessions parenting skills on the dangers of child labor. It will also provide structured psychosocial support for groups of children. And activating the program of the resilience of children and youth to measure their adaptation in new situations ( Save the children curriculums) and psycho-drama activities using puppets theatre to introduce some solutions to their problems and all of these activities will conduct under supervision ( psychologist ). Grandma home here will provide a small library and telling stories or novels. Some recreational activities, Origami and provide some mind games to develop the capacity of thinking and some mind skills. The instant challenge regarding the curriculum of Destination Imagination for children and youth, which improve their thinking that, focuses on creative problem-solving skills. Awareness session focus on sexual harassment risks, abuse, ERW and neglect and how to mitigate them. specialized child protection services (case management) for girls and boys are available in targeted locations. - Case managers will be trained on CPCM, supervision skills and FTR in emergencies and some specialized training related to focused PSS and PFA for children. Provide individual or case-based services, care plan and follow-up to "at-risk" children including those recruited by armed groups, child laborers, children with disabilities and children without appropriate care. It also will provide 6% of the budget, as cash through case management services during the project to children families to return to their education, normal life and to leave any work that affected their lives. it seeks reunification of separated and unaccompanied children and to try to secure appropriate alternative care arrangements by building relationships with local councils and local authorities to help provide temporary and permanent shelters for children and to link with other service providers in the region. Strengthen human resources capacity to respond to child protection concerns in Syria. it will provide training on core CP and some related training to the local community and form social networks in local authorities to respond to any child protection concerns and to focus on disabilities. Project performance and operational monitoring systems will be crucial to ensure that project activities are being implemented as planned and achieving the expected results. Evidence-based process monitoring supported with documentation will be conducted by the M&E department to track activity implementation progress and quality assurance. Pre and post-evaluation will be conducted to measure the impact of activities provided on the beneficiaries. interviews will be administered with randomly selected IDPs and host communities monthly to determine service satisfaction and perceptions about the services. Train accountability teams of 2 staff on information provision means and modalities, Accountability staff are assigned for collecting and responding to feedback ( complaints and suggestions), conduct consultation FGDs.
