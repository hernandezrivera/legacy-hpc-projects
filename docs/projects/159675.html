Project Summary

Arab Ar-Rashayida is a village located in eastern of Bethlehem governorate and away from the center of Bethlehem city about 20 km.
The population is approximately 2,132 capita (1060 under 18 years old, 54 over 65 years old, 520 men and 498 women) distributed on housing within the community as well as in the surrounding areas as livestock breeders residing in the eastern slopes where they live in permanent homes (not tents), this will ensure sustainability and the maximum benefit of the water network services. They own over 25,000 sheep and about 600 camels as well as several chicken farms (the largest number of sheep in the governorate).
The project will aim to provide Ar-Rashayida village the access to safe and affordable drinking water and to end the suffering of the Bedouins by ensuring a proper water network services, through the renewal of the available water network (established since 1980 to serve the Israeli army camps) and linking it with a new water source which is provided by the Palestinian Water Authority(PWA) in a way that ensures the improvement of social, economic and health conditions in the targeted community.
This will be done through the renewal of the existing water network by installing 5760 meters of pipelines of different diameters, and through the extension of an additional 1250 meters of the network pipelines in order to serve the whole targeted community.
The Bedouins of Arab Ar-Rashayida are the most vulnerable and marginalized communities in the governorate according to the latest statistics by PCSB, which are represented by harsh and unstable conditions, which put more burdens on the community in general and on women specifically. Women play vital roles in dealing with WASH issues in the households traditionally, they are usually responsible for providing the drinking water to their houses from different water sources which are sometimes far away and dangerous due to the risks of settler attacks or wild animals.
Recently, increased numbers of Kidney disease cases were documented, this is mainly due to the contaminated water caused by the damages in the existing distribution network, this raised the need for renewing the existing water network in addition to the high percentage of water loss.
The Council is also forced to close the main water valves to reduce the amount of lost water that reaches more than 50%. Because the water supply service is not available through the water networks, the village council is unable to collect water bills from local community.

1 - Needs

AAA has conducted comprehensive needs assessment for the community, the result revealed that Ar- Rashaiyda suffers from a significant shortage of infrastructure and basic services. AAA found that the core need of the targeted community is the provision of safe, affordable and good quality drinking water implemented to serve the community in a sufficient and sustainable way.Ar-Rashaiyda village is provided with water by the West Bank Water Department, through the public water network established in 1983. About 67.6 percent of the housing units are connected to the water network, 31.5 percent are dependent on the purchase of water tanks, and 0.9 percent are dependent on rainwater harvesting cisterns. The quantity of water supplied to Ar-Rashaiyda in 2008 was about 0.0297 million cubic meters/year therefore, the estimated rate of water supply per capita is about 80 liters/day. Here it should be noted that many of Ar-Rashaiyda citizens do not, in fact, consume this amount of water due to water losses, which are about 50 percent. The losses happen at the main source, major transport lines, distribution network, and at the household level (Ar-Rashaiyda village council), thus the rate of water consumption per capita in Ar-Rashaiyda is 45 liters per day. This is a low rate compared with the minimum quantity proposed by the World Health Organization, which is 100 liters per capita per day. Water and sanitation is a pressing need for the community, hence AAA propose to renew the water network and expand it to cover more people, in particular, that the PWA showed interest to connect the community with an additional good source of water if the water network rehabilitated.

2 - Activities

The Palestinian Water Authority (PWA) has provided a new source of water to supply the area with water recently. - Rehabilitation and installation 7010 m of pipelines with different sizes 3",2" and 1", as the following: Arab Ar-Rashayida (1900M of 3", 4110M of 2", 1000M of 1"). Installing those pipelines will enhance water supply for human use and will improve their livelihoods. This will decrease the water loss, cost, time, effort, and water-related diseases, this will also guarantee better quality water. Hence AAA conducted several meetings with relevant stakeholders, in Oct. 2019, covered water supply and needs in the community around Arab Ar-Rashayida to have a better vision about their needs and burdens. The project will decrease water loss (which is about 50% in the village nowadays).People in area C are 2132 capita. The council will be able to collect the price of water, connect the network with houses and managing the necessary maintenance for the network. Output 1: 2132 individuals will benefit from access to safe and affordable drinking water through the pipelines in the targeted community.Output 2: No water losses in the carrier line and in the installed pipelines.Output 3: capacity building and improved awareness level for different groups within the community, especially for women.Output 4: Individual daily water use increased by 45% getting closer to the global average of water use by WHO.Output 5: The income of the Arab Ar-Rashayida council will increase, this means less debt and better services for people. Output 6: No water physical contamination, thus high water pressure in the pipelines, so better water supply mainly to remote areas.This project will foster future expansion to serve the Walidah, Khalat Hamad and Al Rawaen communities where they will be direct beneficiaries

3 - Indicators

Monitoring and evaluation: • A baseline survey will be conducted at the initial phase of the project, which will be updated until the final evaluation. Information will be collected through semi-structured interviews, household visits, and field observations. Regular monitoring will allow AAA to document the impact of the activities, document challenges and constraints on a monthly basis, and suggest the right solutions. Attention will be paid to the quality of implementation and will ensure close liaison with PWA as well as Arab Ar-Rashayida council, community leaders, and local protection committees. AAA will consult prior to the implementation with the protection cluster and NRC to ensure legal advice, second legal opinion will be consulted to ensure minimum risks on the implementation is likely to happen. Accountability will be ensured through ongoing follow up by AAA with field teams and beneficiaries. The expected indicators and targets of the project are: - A total of 2132 direct (1060 under 18 years old, 54 over 65 years old, 520 men and 498 women) beneficiaries will benefit from enhanced access to drinking clear water. ِIncreasing of 20% of the population (men and women) who have access to water. - Expenditure on drinking water will be decreased by 70%. - Reduced time and effort required by women and men by 50%. - Reduced the lost water in the pipeline to 50%. - Decrease the community municipality water bill deficit by 40%. - Gender aspects in the project: The identified indicators will be disaggregated by gender to ensure inclusion of women, and other groups as well as involve men, girls, and girls at schools, youth, PwDs and elderly. Women and girls have considerable responsibilities for water bringing and use at home, as well as ensure adequate hygiene is maintained for children. The intervention will ensure this role is respected and enhanced by reducing the pressure on women in the physical labor of bringing water on a daily basis. The implemented activities will take into consideration the needs of diverse groups of women and girls such as the hygienic aspects that comes more essential with better availability of drinking water that is close to the community, and that bringing water will be of much less burden on women who usually do this responsibility of work. Reports gathered on women's roles will be capitalized and used in AAA women empowerment as well as used to support local AAA initiatives on policy change towards marginalized areas. M&E Indicators will focus on 2 categories: 1 Women Health (women Access and Utilization of water, Disease and Prevention, Environmental Health, water and fertility, Health Care Management, Health Expenditures, Maternal, and Infant Health, Mental Health and Risk Behaviors, Nutrition and water), 2 Economic Contribution (Resource Equity) related to water availability.

9 - Protection mainstreaming
9.1 - Do No Harm

This project will help in mitigating the violations affecting human rights by providing people their right to get clean and affordable drinking water. Overall AAA aims to take a high level of humanitarian action with attention to risk mitigation thereby avoiding doing harm to the greatest extent possible. Indeed, this project will implement 7010 meters in the densest sites affected by the water crisis, through the committee set up to design the project after ensuring that the proposed pipelines will meet the urgent need to stop the deterioration water crisis in the region. Moreover, 85% of the implementation place is somewhat far from direct contact with the occupation forces because it will be between the neighborhoods, and therefore there is no risk or damage to the population or project during the implementation period. In determining the project's activities and specifications, the future development was taken into consideration so as to ensure the continuity of the supply of healthy water in the appropriate quantity and quality in case of extending. AAA will conduct a risk analysis for proposed activity in the targeted location to identify potential risk and propose solves like a community legal status: to assess if there are military orders or other legal concerns and to ensure that the proposed intervention will not trigger the community legal case in a harmful way. By including different stakeholders in the community and other actors from outside the community, including population and owners, AAA believes they have taken concrete steps in considering Do No Harm policy. AAA will keep an eye on any new issues in the area to avoid any negative impact as a result of the project activities.AAA will hold a monthly meeting with the stakeholders ensuring a guaranteed complaint mechanism in order to raise the voice of the most vulnerable groups of the local community especially women, children and people with disabilities. Moreover, a suggestion box will be placed in the village council to receive complains, suggestions and any notes regarding the implementation and selection of beneficiaries.

9.2 - Equal and impartial access to aid

The project aims mainly to connect households to the potential water distribution network. Thus, all HHs who will be connected to the network will benefit from the service without any discrimination according to AGD measures globally. AAA, will ensure the equal chances for widows, people with disabilities, elderly people and the most vulnerable groups to benefit from the project.There is an old water network in the town that will renewal, that has implemented as a population spread and the real need. The project will select the parts that will affect the efficiency and operation of the network as a whole and in general. Therefore, there is no bias for any site, especially since the selection was made by AAA, the Municipality and the PWA. The basis was to take into account the population density and the nature of the problem existing in the region and its unity, this is the approach of AAA in all its projects where the bias is in favor of the most marginalized groups commensurate with the number and density.AAA will hold launching event, awareness sessions and capacity building campaigns including all people groups and will ensure separated sessions for women and mothers to release them from pressure and to guarantee effective participation and discussions, making sure giving them maximum benefit and improving their ability in dealing with WASH issues.
