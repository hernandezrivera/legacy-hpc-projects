Project Summary

The Programme seeks to support and protect 417 000 highly food insecure, vulnerable, and at-risk urban and peri-urban households in Mzilikazi, Reigate, Epworth, Hatcliff, Harare South, Mabvuku/Tafara,Chitungiwiza and Dzivarasekwa districts with monthly multi-purpose cash transfers.

8 - Accountability to Affected People
8.1 - Describe how your project was designed in consultation with communities? How will you incorporate communities in project implementation and what feedback mechanisms do you have in place?

DCA will set up a robust Community Feedback Mechanism which encompasses the use of toll free numbers to bridge the gap between the organization and people assisted. Project banners are also displayed whenever beneficiaries are gathered displaying the toll free number which beneficiaries can use whenever they have feedback/complaints to provide. Dedicated staff member will be employed to attend to beneficiary complaints or feedback. Help desks will be used during pre and post distribution meetings, however because of COVID-19, these will rarely be used since public meetings will be minimized. Bulk SMSs will be widely used to communicate with beneficiaries and each message will have the toll free number. In addition, e-vouchers will also have toll free number printed on them to maximize its use whenever necessary.

9 - Environmental considerations
9.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project through the adoption of a humanitarian-development-peace nexus lens will implement climate and environmentally sensitive activities such as fungiculture, recycling and waste management and horticulture aimed at promoting adaptive capacity for project beneficiaries for continuity and averting food insecurity beyond the cash transfer lifespan. Fungiculture uses hub based mushroom production which is designed to minimize negative environmental impacts for instance by an increased absorption rate of toxins, use of waste material as primary ingredients for the production cycle and the waste material from the mushroom cycle is used for horticulture thereby reducing use of fertilizers which destroy the natural ecosystem. The project will rely on environmental assessments conducted by other partners like Dialogue on Shelter Trust to inform resilience building activities that beneficiaries can embark on.
