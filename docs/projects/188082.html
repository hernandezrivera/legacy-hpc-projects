Project Summary

Silk Road proposes 20 months direct ERL response to continue and expand its humanitarian services to the vulnerable population in different communities in the target communitie.
The 3 ERL packages will work to help vulnerable people to overcome the negative economic and social results left by the spread of the Covid 19 besides the Syrian crisis through :
1- the provision of short-term labour opportunities to skilled and unskilled workers (Women) supporting 360 HHs who will mainly be responsible for producing around 421200 reusable masks through the cash for work that will be implemented (Silk Road has already the basic equipment like sewing machines to produce reusable masks in addition to the experience we got in a previous project).
2- Cash-for-work (CFW) activities will include wastes and garbage collection activities Operation of water stations and Performing the necessary periodic maintenance through recruiting skilled technicians and operators in the water stations we will operate, consequently, Silk Road will secure short-term labour opportunities to 240 skilled and unskilled workers (CFW for both gender) plus 120 technicians and operators under the direct supervision of our team ( CFW for both gender). All WASH and ERL activities will be implemented and provided according to WASH & ERL standards as well as they will provide timely equitable access to the project services.
3- Morover, Silk Road will train and equip men, women and youth volunteers or through the CFW activity (Slight support) to provide temporary employment for public projects to the most vulnerable segments of a population targeted through this project. Furthermore, the training will include best practices for each specific training subject, and explain what safety risks they may face in their work and how to mitigate them. Silk Road will target 900 beneficiaries of both genders including youth above 18 years old. Silk Road will request those 900 beneficiaries to integrate themselves in small volunteer separate groups which will implement community initiatives in the targeted locations which include.
1- Rehabilitation or repair of basic, local economic infrastructure
2- Removal of debris and waste
3- Rehabilitation of education facilities
4- Rehabilitation of health facilities
5- Rehabilitation of access to basic utilities (electricity, gas, water, sewage) for around 1300 HHs.
6- Basic housing repair support
7- Community initiatives support
One of the ultimate goals of this project is to provide the targeted beneficiaries with the tools and resources to work towards self-empowerment and sustainability by providing each trainee with, Knowledge, expertise and a full equipment kit to enable him/her strongly enter the labour market. This could be through handing over some of the assets and equipment of the project such as sewing machines and could be through the distribution of the kit.
Silk Road expect that the local community will facilitate project activities and from our side, we will motivate them to implement similar projects and activities, attract their attention to the importance of vocational training and involve them in counselling as well as we strive to support trainees and providing favourable conditions for them and encourage them to ensure the success of the project depending on the extent of their interaction, commitment to the program, and seriousness and commitment of qualified trainers.
Silk Road will strive to target all affected people applying aligned and reliable selection criteria to benefit from the ERL activities in addition to the priorities that will be given for IDPs, vulnerable groups such as the elderly, people with disabilities, and women taking into consideration the women and children exposed to violence based on Gender. The project will adhere to the "Do No Harm approach" & "Cash for Work Minimum Recommended Actions Guidance for NWS" especially on daily wage rate, th number of work hours per day, and project duration.

4 - Needs

According to the need assessmen conducted by Silk Road we found the followin results:All people are suffering from the lack of job opportunities and lack of agricultural production inputs and the high prices, the high cost of agricultural and fodder inputs, and the weak purchasing power of the population in general, whether the displaced or residents.There is a lack of financial resources, high prices, and limited purchasing power, which places additional burdens on the population, especially with the onset of winter and the high price of heating and cooking materials such as gas and fuel.In light of the spread of COVID 19 which increased people's suffering, Syrians in the covered areas find themselves with very low incomes barely covering their existence. Insecurity is further challenged through decreased income, overstretched capacities, unemployment or displacement. A large number of families are food insecure or at risk of insecurity.Local councils have reported the need for rehabilitation of infrastructures, such as water and sewage networks, the lack of livelihood opportunities, small business projects, vocational training and restoration of agricultural equipment and assets have been reported as priorities. In agricultural areas needs are huge for the provision of livelihood kits inclusive of tools, seasonal crops and rehabilitation of wells, powered by solar panels. Furthermore, water, sewage and energy systems in the villages need repair, restoration or upgrade.

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

Risk 1-Security issues related to cash transfer. Mitigations measures: 1- The frequency of payments will be weekly or monthly (This will be agreed upon with women and men). 2- Payment mechanism: The delivery instrument that will be used to make the payment is a direct cash distribution (We will encourage all women and men before signing the agreement to open a PTT account where they can receive all payments on their accounts put if it is too hard or inapplicable then we will deliver it in direct cash in the workshop. 3- Payments must be made in the presence of the workshop supervisor and one of the monitoring and evaluation teams. Risk 2-Risks related to the limitation of the participation of the disabled people in the program cycle.Set up community channels for communication (e.g. focal points and committees) to ensure that the information goes through and reaches marginalized groups, including persons with disabilities.Look for people with disabilities and include them in the lists of beneficiaries and consult all of them.Risk 3-GBV survivors are easily identified by external parties / Lack of clear data protection protocols. Mitigations measures: GBV survivors should not be labelled as such(to keep confidentiality)- eligibility criteria will not be limited to GBV survivors. GBV survivors will be included as 'individual recipients' or another non-specific category name. Institutionalize/incentivize data protection policy/protocols in agreements and MoUs.


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

1- Risks of the violation of the conduct of Silk Road or/and Misconduct or violence in the workplace. (Likelihood medium - Impact high). GBV & PSEA related issues including rape, assault or sexual exploitation- This applies to employees, beneficiaries, workers, suppliers, partners and any party that may be related to Silk Road work. Mitigating Measure: - Silk Road has zero tolerance for some special cases such as any acts related to terrorist or radical groups, PSEA related issues including rape, assault or sexual exploitation, blackmail, possessing a weapon, threatening or assaulting another person or corruption. - Empower children, women and other vulnerable groups- discuss with them their rights, what is acceptable and unacceptable and what they can do if there is a problem. - Bullying, harassment (including sexual, gender and racial harassment) and discrimination are not tolerated, along with any unwelcome comment or behaviour that is offensive, demeaning, humiliating, derogatory, or any other inappropriate behaviour that fails to respect the dignity of an individual. - Code of conduct of Silk Road automatically forms part of all contracts of employment and contractual conditions of service including any procurement). - Breaches of the standards of the Code of Conduct will be subject to investigation and possible disciplinary action in line with Silk Road’s Disciplinary Procedure stated in the Human resources and Management Responsibility and Accountability Policy.


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk 1-Increased armed conflict due to increased tensions between different groups. Mitigations measures: 1.Consider conflict patterns and contextual analysis as part of decisions for establishing a fixed site or mobile service provision. 2. Engage with diverse members of the community on safety and security in the community 3. If this reaches Silk Road project towns, they will pause activities and wait until the situation on the ground becomes safer. Risk 2-inflation,especially the cost of fuel and construction materials, may result in higher than planned prices. If prices spike too high, then SRO may not have the budget to complete the project. Mitigations measures: 1. Conduct a strong and constant market monitoring before starting any local procurement 2. Keep the prices of the market monitoring with short and periodic frequency 3. Explore alternative supply lines through local procurement 4. Provide logistics capacity 5. All contracts will be in USD Risk 3-Risk of market distortion or and risk of market volatilityConduct a strong and constant market mMonitoring before starting any local procurement to have early warning of potential lack of supplies, and also to map out alternative supply. Consultation with stakeholders in the marketsActivate channels for sharing information and updatesConducting a periodic market assessmentTaking into consideration the prices in the market and the availability of materials and labour, the wages are in line with the market.
