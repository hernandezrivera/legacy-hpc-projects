Project Summary


Our project will alleviate the suffering of 495 families of widows, orphans, and special needs in NW Syria in Sarmada city, and camps too, so we will providing them a $ 50 voucher monthly for 8 months that helps them to get their food needs, and emergency needs, especially for families with children suffering from malnutrition or milk Shortage. The cash idea was chosen to give more flexibility to families to get their needs.
MATTAR organization will target 495 families according to the eligibility criteria according MATTAR policies, who are widows, orphans, and special needs, the elderly and vulnerable families.
The beneficiary selection process will be in coordination with civil society representatives, and based on several criteria, Including duration of displacement, vulnerability and eligibility, priority will be given to female-headed households, as the case of Sarmada widows and people with special needs, coordination will be at a high level between MATTAR organization and representatives of humanitarian organizations and coordination bodies to avoid any possible interference..
MATTAR organization has a PTT account in banks in NW Syria, where MATTAR organization transfers funds by Turkish post, which will be used within the framework of this project to transfer funds and distribute them to the beneficiaries.
Before distributing the cash, a quick market assessment will be conducted to ensure availability of supplies, prices and access to local markets.
Cash vouchers will be printed in a way that prevents fraud and forgery through thermal stickers, purchasing process will be from Turkey, which include serial number and Barcode for each beneficiary name, as they will be distributed to the beneficiary families. When the cash amounts are distributed and Include their information, and its recovered at the end of the project.
The number of widow’s families in SARMADA is 355 HHs and the number of individuals is 1273, there are database of names, where MATTAR organization has implemented a special project of bread distribution for them, in addition to the distribution of dignity baskets and provision of sacrificial meat. These families live in Sarmada, and their living situation is very difficult. There is no donor provides them with support, and most women work to cover the needs of their children.
special needs number is 140 HHs and the number of their family members is 700, they were previously targeted by MATTAR organization through distributing bread, food baskets and sacrificial meat too. They were injured due to the war and most of them have bad living situation, previously they were working, but the COVID-19 19 pandemic affected them greatly and stopped their work

4 - Needs

Days later, the war in Syria enters its tenth year and brings with it suffering, displacement and removal, with dangerous indications that describe it as a disaster.The Syrian crisis is considered the most dangerous, after the Second World War, and described as the biggest disaster due to food insecurity and the lack of foodstuffs.where several reports were issued by HNO and the FSL sector indicating that 11.7 million Syrians suffer from food insecurity, 6 million of them need an urgent response to cover their need for food that causes severe malnutrition among children in northwestern Syria,These numbers are constantly increasing.In addition to, the Covid 19 pandemic, which affected life in general and in northwest Syria in particular, where those, who used to depend on work to meet their daily needs, their work is stopped due to the Covid 19 pandemic and the rapid spread of the disease and its impact on all aspects of life. Thousands families of widows and orphans who their work have stopped which increase the suffering more. This leads to limited economic opportunities that forced many to resort to negative confrontation strategies, Therefore, displacement and the complex security situation have limited Syrians' access to the correct food systems which due to their poor availability and high price also.

5 - Implementation of activities

1- Rapid Market Assessment and to ensure the availability of foodstuffs and the ease with which the beneficiaries can access them2- Choosing the target area, Idlib -Sarmada, because most of the displaced move to it and because the need is great there3- Selecting the beneficiaries and verifying their names based on the policy criteria of the MATTAR Organization for the Most Needed and with the participation of the local community in the process of selecting the beneficiaries.4- Establishing the distribution plan, accountability mechanism, and distribution monitoring plan5- Contracting with a service provider in Sarmada, and he must be Exchange in order for the money to be delivered through him6- Printing the vouchers in Turkey, including a barcode, serial number, and a thermal sticker to prevent forgery7- Distribute cash vouchers and inform beneficiaries of the complaint mechanism, and include a phone number and email to report any problem8- Monitor the distribution process and ensure that beneficiaries receive vouchers9- Monitoring the process of delivering funds by cashiers to beneficiaries

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk 1: Implementation of the durable activities is stopped by regular clashes, violence, or an upsurge in criminality including theft or kidnappingMitigation 1: MATTAR will monitor the security developments and reported criminal violence along its main delivery routes and in its areas of operation. If an increase in violence is noted, alternative routes and distribution points will be used to mitigate against the possibility of an armed group putting any pressure on the beneficiaries or consider alternative areas/activities under the planned intervention with the approval from HF and Cluster Risks 2: A fees might be collected by the local council in return for receiving humanitarian assistance leading for Aid diversion. Moreover. Conflict and tension in the community might happen between the project team and non-beneficiaries since they believe they are marginalizedMitigation 2: MATTAR will ensure the humanitarian nature of the project activities by communicating the project scope and selection criteria with the beneficiaries through verbal communication and distributed brochures that the humanitarian assistance is free of charge. In case the aid diversion happened, MATTAR will immediately suspend the activity and report the incident to OCHA as per MATTAR policy. Furthermore, The project team will involve the stakeholders from the beginning of the project to facilitate the activities and getting their support
