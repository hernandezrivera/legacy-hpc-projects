Project Summary


ARD is intending to sustain and expand the provision of basic WASH services to drought, flood, AWD and conflict affected populations in Bakol and Lower Shabelle regions in Somalia through the provision of water, sanitation and hygiene services in communities, schools and health/nutrition facilities. The proposed project is also designed to ensure equitable access to Water, sanitation and hygiene promotion services considering people with disabilities and elders, safety of children as well as dignity and privacy for women and girls. Coordination will be maintained with WASH clusters at national and state levels, in addition to coordination with key actors in target areas. The project will contribute to emergency lifesaving and live sustaining of WASH as well as recovery and resilience of the target communities and institutions by ensuring participation of beneficiaries in the project cycle. The project will address gender issues and collect sex and age disaggregated data which will be analyzed to identify gaps, provide gender sensitive interventions and ensure participation and empowerment of women and people with disability The accountability to affected populations will be ensured by involving communities, information sharing and engaging them in the complaints and feedback system special consideration will be given to people with disability . Protection of beneficiaries will be ensured during access and utilization of services reducing further risks. The interventions also fit under the sector specific objective to sustain and expand WASH services for IDPs and the affected population.
The proposed project activities will include construction of strategic water sources, provision of operation and maintenance tools, construction/rehabilitation of sanitation facilities, emergency water supply as well as provision of comprehensive hygiene promotion targeting the most vulnerable household in emergency in the target villages, IDPs and institutions, trainings and dissemination of hygiene best practice through IECs with and distribution of hygiene kits to most vulnerable people while special consideration will be given to people with disability, ARD will ensure 10% of the proposed activity intervention is reserved for people with disability while also ensuring their contribution is incorporated in project design, implementation and monitoring phase hence enhance inclusion in decision making process

Project Activities

1.1 Rehabilitation and upgrading of 10 strategic boreholes in 10 droughts, flood and AWD prone areas through replacement of electro-mechanical systems into installation of hybrid solar systems (PV+Generator), construction of elevated tanks, extension of distribution system, construction of new water kiosks hence benefiting a total of 6000 men,women,boys,girls and PLWD1.2 Construction and extension of 15 communal water points including water kiosks in IDPs in Merka (7), Bardale (5), Wajid (3) and 15 rainwater harvesting systems with storage facilities at health/nutrition sites and schools.1.3 Rehabilitate 20 community and institutional water points including roof water catchments in schools, CFS and health/nutrition facilities in both rural and IDPs benefiting a total of 10,000 people including people with disability 1.4 Create/reactivate and strengthen the capacity of 140 water point management committees and 140 community based hygiene promotion committees in target communities including IDPs, schools, health/nutrition facilities with a focus on gender parity and 10% inclusion of people with disability 1.5 Train pump technicians and preposition pump maintenance kits for 32 water points including 15 strategic boreholes1.6 Construction of 50 HH emergency shared latrines 10% reserved for people living with disability in IDPs and host communities, AWD/Cholera hotspot areas and drought affected villages with portable hand washing facilities thus benefiting a total of 12,000 men,women,boys,girls and women in the project target location 1.7 Rehabilitate/Desludge 100 flood affected latrines in Bardale, Marka and Wajid district hence improving the sanitation of 12,000 men,women,boys and girls including PLWD in the target district 1.8 Construction of 100 gender segregated latrine blocs with hand washing stations in health/nutrition facilities and public schools with attention to ensuring proper sanitary facilities for girls, in line with SPHERE standards1.9 Distribution of 50 sanitation tools to 50 drought affected communities, schools and health/nutrition facilities i.e. wheel borrow, rakes, spades, pick axes for debris clean up1.10 Conduct 12 community hygiene promotion sessions to promote good hygiene and sanitation practices in targeted communities, health/nutrition facilities and schools thus reaching a total of 3,000 men,women,boys,girls and PLWD with improved knowledge and practices1.11 Conduct community meetings to sensitize people on women and PLWD inclusion in the management of community WASH committees 1.12 Conduct woman to woman 12 sessions on hygiene promotion focusing on menstrual hygiene management (MHM) and safe drinking water within the family.
