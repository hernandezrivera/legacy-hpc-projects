Project Summary

The project comprises on a broad-based market assessment of the trades and skills which are in sever need in the north of Syria after the long years of conflict, delivering technical and vocational training for young women and men, offering support to establish and run small shops and home-based businesses and linking young entrepreneurs with potential markets. Potential entrepreneurs will be selected through detailed assessment. After completion of the training, QC will execute a small grant scheme including provision of vocational kits. This will enable the trainees to start their income generating ventures. QC will thoroughly monitor the business and job efficiency status of the participants in order to ensure project effectiveness.

Needs

After 7 years of conflict and the destruction of the economic activites anf the deterioration of the Syrian currency, Syrian male and female youth have now very limited opportunities to gain useful skills which would enable them to have access to the labour market and provide respectable living for themselves. These opportunities have greatly decreased due to a lack of assistance with skills training and micro-credit schemes. Consequently a lot of youth are often seen as disappiointed people as they are not able to provide for their dependents. The UN reports said that 83% of the Syrian People are under the poverty line and 11,606,585 persons are in need to work opportunities created . The unemployment rate reached 53%, according to Syrian Center for Policy Research. Our need assessment focused on Idleb and Aleppo Governorates, since they have a large number of IDPs. Our meetings with youth, both male and females, has revealed that the youth is willing to learn vocations skills, they need support and facilitation to become self-relient. The idea of vocational trainings was also appreciated from the member of the local councils and some of the parents which we could meet during the course of need assessments. As a result Qatar Charity proposes to launch this project of vocational training for male and female youth, which may focus on trades like mechanics, carpentry, electrical work, plumbing, welding, tailoring , computer and other fields of training. However the final list of trades for vocational training will be finalized only after the detailed market analysis, market needs and the Training Need assessments of the potential trainees. The duration of each training may vary depending on the type of each one. These skills will benefit 4000 female and male youth. The project will support two categories: unskilled entrepreneur, who will get training and the required equipment once the courses are completed, and skilled ones who will get the equipment directly. The eligibility criteria will include vulnerable people: unemployed, Female-Headed Families, IDPs, young- headed orphans. This is to be noted that Qatar Charity, in partnership with QUEST, is already implementing 2 projects of TVET training in Northern Syria (Aleppo and Idleb) targeting female and male youth and planning to extend the spread of the interventions.
