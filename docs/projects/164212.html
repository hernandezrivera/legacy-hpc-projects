Project Summary

This project aims to support 2500 farmers in six locations with agriculture inputs as the following:
1- Support farmers in Atareb, Al-Bab, and A'zaz to plant vegetable crops as following
I) Support 500 local farmers who can plant one donum agriculture lands but they are in need for agricultural inputs through supporting them to plant one donum with seeds, fertilizer, pesticide, fungicide, modern irrigation network, and fuel to plant summer vegetables (tomato, eggplant, and cucumber
II) Support 500 potato farmers in Atareb and A'zaz with (tubers, fertilizers, pesticide, fungicide, modern irrigation network, and fuel) to plant one donum with potato crop in Autumn season. It is worth mentioning that IYD will divide the farmers into groups; each group will share in covering the operational costs of the agricultural services such as irrigation.
2 -Support 500 olive farmers in Bennsh and Kafr Takharim, Idleb province with agriculture services regarding pre-harvesting practices (supplementary irrigation to increase the resistance to high temperature and improve the composition of olive oil inside the olive fruit), harvesting process, extraction process, marketing such as branding and labelling) and post-harvesting process (post-harvesting tillage, intercropping, pruning, and fertilization. Here we should mention that pruning and harvesting will be conducted as CFW modality. Olive farmers will be targeted in an average of 3-4 donum for each which mean almost 50 olive trees for each.
3- Support 500 wheat farmers with seed and fertilizers to plant one Hectare for each farmer in Atareb.
4- Support 500 farmers in Jarablus to plant one hectare with rainfed barley crop through providing them with barley kits which contain (barley seeds, fertilizers, herbicide).
5- Rehabilitation of the irrigation canals of Jarablus by recruiting 100 workers as Cash for Work (CFW) to clean and maintain the canal edges. The workers will work for twenty-two days per month, and each skilled worker will be paid for 7.5 USD per day and 6 USD for the unskilled ones. It is worth mentioning that IYD will provide these workers with agricultural equipment set so that they will make use of it after the end of the project.

4 - Needs

According to HNO 2020, an estimated 7.9 million people in Syria are food insecure and an additional 1.9 million people are at risk of food insecurity, therefore, supporting the rehabilitation of critical agriculture infrastructure for boosting household food production will further comprise one of the core responses under agriculture and livelihoods. More than 37 per cent of food-insecure HHs reported having outstanding debt and 57 per cent of food insecure people had adopted negative coping strategies. Potato production in the country is now down to about one-third of its pre-crisis levels, largely because of the reduced availability and high cost of seed potatoes and required inputs. According to the technical needs assessments conducted by IYD in 2019: 1- Atareb, in general, contains large tracts of fertile agricultural land and farmers with small agriculture land.The percentage of cultivable land in the targeted area is 65 %. 2- The rainfall rate in the Atareb subdistrict is about 350-500 millimetres annually and the percentage of irrigated land is 42 %. 3-The farmers of Jarablus suffer from lack of water for irrigation and the percentage of irrigated land decreased significantly especially in the last three years because the main irrigation canal has been out of service for seven years. This issue alone led to drop-in cultivation by 25% in Jarablus. Cultivation decreased throughout the area, which has had a negative impact on the local economy. In other side, After nearly 9 years of the conflict in Syria affected negatively the olive sector in Idlib governorate, especially the areas surrounding the military sites and points where there was fierce fighting because of its strategic location such as Bennesh city due to their location near Foah city, this has led to the fact that a wide range of olive lands is not invested.

5 - Implementation of activities

Summer vegetables and potato: 500 farmers who have a small scale will be supported to plant one donum with summer vegetables and another 500 farmers to plant one donum with the potato by providing agriculture inputs (seeds, fertilizers, fungicide, pesticide, fuel, and drip irrigation networks). Concerning olive farmers,500 farmers will be supported as follow: a. Pre and Harvesting processes: the following activities will be implemented: i. supplementary irrigation, ii Field workshop on proper harvesting practices.iii. Supervising the harvesting process by scheduling the process to have all BNFs harvesting their field within 1-month, olives will be collected using plastic boxes, contracting with an olive mill to extract the olive oil, labelling the products, and providing harvesting tools. b. Post-harvesting process: the following activities will be implemented: i. Post-harvesting tillage for all BNFs will be carried out. ii. pruning tools will be provided to the BNFs and they will be trained on pruning. iii. Intercropping activity by providing legume seeds to the farmers to be cultivated under olive trees. Cash for work: 1000 workers will be targeted by cash for work activities, where 500 workers will work for a month in manual harvesting activity and 500 workers will work for one month in the pruning process. This project aims to upgrade the irrigation status throughout 1- Rehabilitation of the irrigation canals of Jarablus by recruiting 100 workers as Cash for Work to clean and maintain the edges of the canal. Vehicles’ costs to implement this activity will be covered. In this way, large space of agricultural spaces will be watered and irrigated. 2- The farmer's awareness about the water-saving concept will be promoted through conducting raising awareness sessions, as well as distributing modern irrigation system to farmers enough to cover one donum per farmer.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

- The risk of sexual exploitation by humanitarian workers. IYD will mitigate this by establishing a confidential complaint mechanism and referral mechanisms, addressing the SEA complaints, training staff on PSEA and GBV, asking all staff to sign code of conduct, creating females and males’ queues separately, having female staff. Behaving with beneficiaries as do no harm and respecting their dignity. - Increased risk of inter-community tension between community residents and host communities due in part to unequal level of assistance given to community residents in comparison to host communities. IYD will mitigate this by ensuring that the community are all needs based, and that community residents are amongst the most vulnerable. - To avoid Gender exploitation. IYD will mitigate this by including all the age and gender needs accordingly within the project designing phase. Clarifying all the project objectives and beneficiaries’ selection criteria to all the local authorities. Asking the local authorities’ members, vendors and services’ providers to sign the PSEA code of conduct. - To avoid the gender discrimination, IYD will provide suitable and safe distribution environment such as: accessible ramps for people with special needs, drinking water, waiting seats, umbrellas, secure distribution center and placing it away from military installations or threats such as attacks. - Child labor risk, the mitigation is to make the service provider committing to the listed conditions which provided to not make any work with him if he was breaking the signed contract with IYD by practicing the child labor. - Child food ration receiving risk. The mitigation is by not delivering the food ration to the child and having a mobile distribution team to deliver the ration to this child house in case the householder is not able to come to the distribution center.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Assumption, The agriculture inputs have high quality. Risk Description, Low productivity per donum because of weak plant resistance to the surrounding environmental conditions. Mitigation, coordinate with technical team to check seeds after that supply seeds of high productivity and resistant to environmental conditions. Assumption, The climatic conditions will be stable. Risk Description, External climatic risks (drought, diseases, physiological impacts…) Mitigation, Technical supervision by prevention and suitable treatments procedures. Assumption, All the agriculture inputs are available with stable price. Risk Description, Lack of availability of certain materials or changes in prices during the project period. Mitigation, The financial study of the project took into consideration the change in the exchange rate as well as the writing of long-term contracts with suppliers will mitigate the damage resulting from price change. Assumption, The exchange rate will be stable during the project period. Risk Description, Change in the exchange rate for the Syrian Pound against the US Dollar. Mitigation, The financial study of the project took into consideration the change in the exchange rate as well as the writing of long-term contracts with suppliers will mitigate the damage resulting from the price change. Assumption, The security situation will be stable during the project period. Risk Description, Change in the security situation. Mitigation, IYD will coordinate with the active actors in the ground to pick the safest area to implement the project, additionally, IYD safety and security officer will follow the security situation and send reports to the field team to ensure their safety.
