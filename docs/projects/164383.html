Project Summary

The project aims to assist /46800/ of the affected people include (13572: The project aims to assist /46800/ of the affected people include (13572: men, 15444: women, 8424: boys and 9360: girls) on northern of Aleppo governorate (Afrin- Al Bab- Aghtrin and A’zaz Sub-districts) as following:
1. Provision 36000 cooking gas cylinders for 3000 households (18000 individuals include 5220: men, 5940: women, 3240: boys and 3600: girls), where each household will get one cooking gas cylinder on a monthly base for 12 months.
2. Provision 792000 liters of heating fuel (diesel) for 3300 households (19800 individuals include 5742: men, 6534: women, 3564: boys and 3960: girls), where each household will get 240 liters of diesel during the winter.
3. Repairing 1500 shelter units for 1500 households (9000 individuals include 2610: men, 2970: women, 1620: boys and 1800: girls) depending (HLP) and Shelter guideline.
The activities 1 and 2 are relevant to Sector Objective 1, while activity 3 is relevant to Sector Objective.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

Prioritize safety & dignity and avoid causing harm:  Ensure the location of and access routes to distribution points and services are safe. Ensure the ration size is appropriate for beneficiaries to be able to carry. Design and construction of shelters must preserve the safety and dignity of the affected populations: Take into account the land tenure arrangements of IDPs, including statutory/legislative and customary access rights to land and inheritance rights. Provide solar lamps/torches for each household in settings where the lighting of communal areas is not available. Avoid community tensions, by providing the local/host community with access to assistance. Keep lists of beneficiaries and personal data confidential and safe from unintended use. Work in coordination with protection actors to put in place measures to prevent and respond to physical/sexual violence arising due to crowded shelter conditions. Promote equitable and impartial access for all  Ensure that NFI packages include suitable items to cover the specific needs of women, girls, older persons. Make infrastructure adaptions to shelter structures to make them user-friendly to persons with reduced mobility. Issue ration cards to female-headed households, child-headed households and unaccompanied children in their own name.  Establish mechanisms to give priority to those who cannot stand in line for long periods. Ensure that beneficiaries know how to obtain assistance. Ensure accountability to affected populations Be transparent with the affected populations by providing them with accessible and timely information on selection criteria for targeted assistance. Set up accessible, confidential and well-understood feedback mechanisms for suggestions and complaints with a view to improving programming. Shelter/NFI staff and committees should be representative of all groups within the community and all staff and members should be given protection mainstreaming training.

7 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

1. Provision cooking gas cylinders:Risks and Assumptions:1.1 SRD plans to procure the gas from local market vendors, but the source of gas will be from Turkey, the closure of the border, if it happens will deplete the market and inﬂate the prices.Mitigating Measures:- Monitoring the situation of cross border by coordination with logistic cluster. - Ensuring that there is reserve stock of gas cylinders in SRD warehouses. - Find alternative sources of cooking fuel in local markets.1.2 Abuse of Authority by distribution actors (GBV, discrimination, monetary coercion)Mitigation:-Training on GBV concept and PSEA for all staff members and ensuring they sign on the Code of Conduct.- Increased number of monitoring staff-Activate Strong Complaint Mechanism system. 2. Provision heating fuel-diesel:Risks and Assumptions:2.1 Diesel fuel available in local markets is poor quality, and its combustion, when used in heating, causes the emission of toxic and harmful gases. Mitigating Measures: - Contracting with reputable, well-known suppliers in local markets. - Our ﬁeld team will conduct periodic and surprise tests prior to purchase. 2.2 The indirect purchase of conﬂict associated resources as diesel fuel might contribute to conﬂict.Mitigating Measures:- Conducting (Anti-Terrorism) check for suppliers before contracting them. - Monitor the lines of supply of the fuel and track the source down and ensure fuel is not procured from parties involved in the conﬂict 3. Repairing shelter units: The Risks and Assumptions: Eviction from shelter due to lack of approval from property owner and/or exposing beneﬁciaries at risk due to lack of HLP documentation for the shelter rehabilitated.Mitigating Measures:- Abide by the HLP guidance document that was developed by the SNFI Cluster- Do not proceed with the Shelter rehabilitation unless with the consensus of the owner, proof of registered HLP ownership, verified ownership claims.
