Project Summary

The project will upgradation and strengthening of existing shelter at Rohingya and Host community. Frienddhip have provided 211 shelter in the year of 2018 and 2019, that all has installed in camp 8W. By this time monsoon have over, so project team will have targeted that some of shelters will be needed repairing, especially the previous project have not use treated bamboo for piller and roofing. thats why the new project in 2020 has targeted to replace existing bamboo pilar, roofing and other materials as required.
Project has also targeted about 200 shelters in host community for repairing and strengthening, especialy those who are at risk of strong wind and landslide. project team will conduct focuse group discussion to identify the most vulnarable shelters and will prepare a priority list. Based on assessment, project will provide materials and engage community people for reparing works. In addition, Rohingya beneficiaries will be received awarness training on shelter maintenance and keep safe shelter. Both os caces Freindship will be used PASA tools in capacity building initiatives.

Needs

Friendship have provided 211 shelter in 2018 and 2019, by this time Rohingya families have faced monsoon and cyclone situartion. Resulting, thats create weak condition of shelter iteams like bamboo piller, fence, lickage roof, inside tie in rafter or perlin etc. So now Friendship will take initiatives for providing support to 211 Rohingya families for reparing and strengthening shelter by using improved items like treated bamboo, replace of roofinfg materials like thatch or tarpolin, lickages fence, re installtion of footing with pilar and tie-down. Project have exparienced that many of host community shelter need to reparing and strengthning againest heavy wind like. The shelter roof, pilar and fence not used treated bamboo, no tie down, bracing, weak fences almost fragile condition of entire shelter. So the project will identify them most 200 vulnarable shelter in host community and provided materails and fixing cost accordingly. Especially the families who have limited income and unable to strengthening house or recostruction will get priority first.

Activities or outputs

The list of Activities:1) Considering shelter cluster guideline and suggested materials will be used for repairing and maintenance of previously provided 211 shelter, those all will work in camp 8W and block is A 35, A 36, A 39 and A 40. Friendship have provided housing facilities in Rohingya camp, in total 211 housing has been installed in 4 blocks of camp 8W. Now the time to provide reparing and manitenance works. So all 211 shelter already have selected, but required iteam will be identified by assessment. Project team will be visited every individual housing and meet with owners to find their needs. approximate 352 to 588 USD will be allocated for per housing repair and maintenance. In every cases, Friendship will provided treated bamboo and replace what has provided before. 2) Awarness raising on strengthening shelter of 211 Rohingya families who received shelter in year of 2018 and 2019 from Friendship. Friendship will take initiatives to aware Rohingya people to use their shelter and some management technique to safe their shelter and will engage them to reparing works. So number of orentation 3) 200 shelter will be identified at host community which need to strengthen for reducing disaster risk from cyclone and land slide etc. The most vulnarable housing will be identified through focus group discussion and house hold assessment. Friendship team will maintain coordination with Local govt. for avoiding overlap and ensure quality work. However, Friendship will look forward support from DRC or BRAC or IOM for treating bamboo that will be provided to Rohingya community for replacment.
