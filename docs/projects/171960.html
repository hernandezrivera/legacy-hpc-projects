Project Summary

The project provides for the restoration of the thermal circuit of households affected by the fighting, through the repair of roofing.
It also provides for the provision of winter kits (NFI) to residents of villages and towns that were affected by the fighting.

1 - Needs

The ongoing conflict in Eastern Ukraine has been destroying lives for 6 years. Deep economic and social crises, lack of jobs due to the shutdown of key industrial enterprises, low wages, social and pension benefits insufficient to meet even basic needs, housing and infrastructure destroyed as a result of shelling, de facto isolation from the world community due to the economic blockade, the presence of roadblocks, as well as restrictive measures in connection with the COVID-19 pandemic make life extremely difficult for the population. In fact, people are put on the edge of survival.In addition, due to the migration of the working-age population, a significant part of people living in non-government controlled areas of Donetsk oblast are representatives of vulnerable categories-pensioners, disabled people, families with people with disabilities, people with serious illnesses, lonely elderly people, children who are unable to travel to safer and more economically developed locations.In winter, the situation is further complicated by the lack of central water supply and heating in rural localities along the contact line, the destruction of infrastructure (in particular, damage to gas pipes that cannot be repaired due to the risk of constant shelling), the inability to provide rapid assistance due to the risk of shelling, the presence of roadblocks and the lack of high-quality road surface and regular transport links.During numerous monitoring visits, it was revealed that the most urgent need for residents living along the contact line is still assistance in restoring housing damaged during the fighting, as well as providing non-food essential kits (a blanket, a water canister, an electric heater, a solar-powered lantern, etc.). Such assistance is critical in winter.It is planned to restore the roof for 1000 households (including the purchase of materials and work): the average case amount will be $ 750 per household.It is also planned to transfer non-food kits for vulnerable categories of the population (the final composition of the kit will be formed after clarifying the needs of target groups).

2 - Activities & Outcomes

The current project includes the following activities: 1. Identification and verification of families most in need of roof restoration;2. Visit locations to determine the amount of work performed and the necessary materials;3. Purchase of materials (for the restoration of roofs will be used environmentally friendly materials: eco-slate, ondulin, roofing material, etc.) and execution of works to 1000 HHs;4. Identification and verification of families most in need of winter kits;5. Procurement of goods and formation of sets for 1000 HHs;6. The distribution of NFI sets.

3 - Indicators & targets

Cluster goals within HRP:1) Address critical gaps in essential shelter and NFI needs of the most vulnerable IDPs and conflict affected population. Activity: NFI distribution.2) Contribute to adequate transitional shelter solutions (monetised or in-kind). Activity: Light and medium repairs. Indicators:1) #2850 individuals (1000 HHs) receiving NFIs (clothing sets, bedding sets, and other general NFIs)2) #1000 HHs supported with light and medium repairs
