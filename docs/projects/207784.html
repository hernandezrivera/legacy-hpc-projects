Project Summary

Families in Memba district will be enrolled into a cash transfer program to support them with basic monthly subsidies for two years to support food security, increase consumption, and stimulate the local economy. Meanwhile, youth aged 18-24 will be offered business skills training combined with a lump-sum one-time cash transfer to support entrepreneurial activities and strengthen their economic resilience. The intervention will target vulnerable communities in Memba district with high poverty levels and youth unemployment.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project is a cash transfer program with complementary cash plus activities. The overall aim is to promote food security, economic resilience and stability in Memba. Communities with high vulnerability to food insecurity, youth unemployment, and poverty will be targeted and all families living in these communities will be eligible for cash assistance. The project will encourage but not demand families to select a female household member as the principal cash transfer recipient. While all families will be eligible for two years of basic monthly subsidies, youth aged 18-24 will be eligible for a one-time lump sum cash transfer combined with business skills training to incentivize entrepreneurial activities and build their economic resilience.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The project will target specific communities in Memba using a geotargeting approach based on AI-enabled analysis including satellite imagery and relative wealth indices. Communities will be enrolled into the program in a multi-stage process to confirm their eligibility and take a baseline of their socio-economic characteristics. Beneficiaries will also be equipped with a mobile phone and mobile money account at this stage. A sample of enrolled recipients will be audited by a separate team to check for quality, integrity and ensure proper safeguarding. Once each community has passed through the audit, monthly cash transfers will be sent using mobile money. Program beneficiaries can report challenges via a multi-lingual call center and all cash recipients will be followed up with after each transfer to confirm receipt and track spending choices. Business skills training will be offered to youth living in the communities after 9 months of cash transfers and a one-time lump-sum transfer sent to all youth after this course is delivered.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Comprehensive safeguarding, gender and risk assessments are carried out at the beginning of the project. Risks are identified, controlled and monitored throughout implementation. The project maintains channels for proactive and reactive risk identification via follow-up field teams and a hot line. A separate and anonymous audit team is also tasked with identifying potential issues and resolving cases, including freezing transfers or making households ineligible if harm may be caused.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

All households in the targeted communities are eligible, fostering inclusiveness. Households are encouraged to select women as their principal cash recipient at enrolment and experience shows between 70-90% of households tend to do this. Elderly people are supported to enrol using helper models to ensure access to digital payments even if not literate in mobile phone use.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Disabled people are supported to enrol using helper models in which they select a trusted person to receive cash on their behalf. Households opting for this model receive more regular and detailed monitoring from the field teams.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project is delivered in coordination with partners working on sustainable fisheries management and fostering sustainable livelihoods in the area.
