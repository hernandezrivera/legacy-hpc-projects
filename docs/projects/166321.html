Project Summary

The objective of the project is to empower youth and women through providing technical
and financial toolkits to build a sustainable fuel-efficient stove value chain and through
providing access for vulnerable household to improved cooking solutions. The expected
outcome of the project is access to sustainable energy, protection and resilience among
vulnerable households in Borno State through the empowerment of IDPs, returnees and
host communities with focus on youth and women.

2 - Activities

1. Procurement and deployment of thermometers, handwashing station (bucket with tap, stand, soap, paper towels), hand sanitisers, masks, surgical gloves and water carriers in training centres, stoves/briquettes distribution points and venues for public awareness campaigns. 2. Extended capacity building programmes. The capacity building on production of stoves and briquettes will be staggered. only 10 persons will be allowed per session making a total of 20 sessions for the 200 trainees. 3. Extended distribution of stoves and briquettes by Adhoc staff. Stoves and briquettes distribution will be staggered longer than initially planned. Only 10 persons will be admitted to the distribution points at any given moment and social distancing and other health protective measures will be strictly enforced. 3. Extended public awareness campaigns. The awareness programmes will be staggered to avoid large crowds. Only 20 persons will be allowed in the venues at any given time and social distancing and other health protective measures will be strictly enforced.
