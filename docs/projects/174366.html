Project Summary

Over 12 months, Oxfam will provide life-saving and life-sustaining support to 23,100 of the most vulnerable people in two disaster affected districts of Sindh province. The project will focus on to improved food security through provision of cash grants and livelihood support to most vulnerable communities affected by multiple shocks and disasters.
This will be achieved through two inter-linked work streams:
1. Assistance for Food and other needs through Multipurpose unconditional cash grants to the most vulnerable households.
Oxfam will provide unconditional cash transfers (13,200 PKR per HH) to 2600 HHs. In addition, Oxfam in Pakistan will extend Micro enterprise support to disaster affected businesses (4,200 Individuals in 600 HH) and provide them seed money to improve their livelihoods. This will benefit a total of 22,400 individuals (based on average family size of 7) in acute need.
2. Rehabilitation/revival of disaster affected agricultural land
This project will focus on restoration of disaster affected agricultural land in consultation with district Agriculture Department and the affected communities. This will benefit 700 individuals and improve their food security and agricultural livelihood.
Key stakeholders for this project will be community members, relevant government ministries and agencies at national, provincial, and district level, and other humanitarian actors.
The project will ensure enhanced food security and livelihood of disaster affected communities through its interventions.
