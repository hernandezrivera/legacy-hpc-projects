Project Summary

Since 2017, Cabo Delgado province has been facing insecurity caused by non state armed groups/insurgents. This has resulted in the destruction of individual houses, disruption of basic social services such health, education, and displacement of large groups of the population to safe areas and abandonment of farms and water projects under construction. Currently most of the districts in the north of the province are affected by the insecurity situation. Recently the security situation deteriorated significantly with many attacks recorded between January – October 2020 and now they seem to be daily.
UNICEF through this proposal is planning to implement life facilitating activities in the Education area focusing on the IDPs children (3-15 yrs) and teachers.
Since classes were suspended in March 2020, UNICEF has been supporting DPE through CERF on learning supplies for schools in hosting communities and on PSS for teachers to provide access by children to continuing education by distance with a special focus on vulnerable children such as girls and children with disabilities and IDPs children. Also, personal hygiene kits will be provided to help children prepare for a safe and gradual return to schools.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

To respond to the needs of education sector, UNICEF plans to provide:1. Continuation of trainings for teachers on Psychosocial Support as many children were forced to flee from their homes. Some are now separated from their parents and caregivers along the way. During this conflict, they have endured staggering violence, severe injuries, stress, trauma and the constant threat of recruitment by armed groups. Many are cut off from fundamental services like clean water, health and mental care and education, especially as their communities become militarized and divided and isolated from basic social services. The training will target teachers for them to reach the children in small groups in the accommodation and/or relocation centres and follow up will be done in their host families or through professional child care services in close collaboration with the child protection cluster and organizations.2. Basic learning supplies (school in a box, recreation kits, learners’ and specific ECE kits, mobile chalk boards) to at least help children to start some basic activities that help to reduce stress, trauma and create a haven where they can play and socialize and do some basic learning under the lead of accommodation centre, education and protection staff. If permitted by the authorities, also child friendly spaces will be provided for the different age groups (3-5 and 5-15) including some non-formal skills trainings for the young adolescents. 3. Monitoring- the monitoring process will be jointly done with DPE and DPGCAS and selected NGOs to ensure quality implementation of all activities planned.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

A risk assessment process will be carried out respecting the origin of all communities, their habits and customs to avoid sensitivities or conflicts that may arise in the accommodation or relocation camps. For all activities to be carried out in the field, such as volunteering for the children enrollment, children's animators, for cleaning and preparing the child friendly spaces UNICEF will prioritize the involvement of the local speople who are in these camps. In this way, UNICEF aims to reduce the risks caused by hazards and anything else like social or cultural aspects that can harm children. Because many children and families are hosted by their families in the targeted districts in our activities we will also involve these members of their families. In the places for children that are identified and permitted by the authorities to play and to do other activities, UNICEF will adhere to all regulations set by the government and MINEDH in preventing infection and transmission of COVID 19. Therefore teachers and other facilitators of these activities for children will be provided with training on personal hygiene, WASH and materials such as soap, buckets with taps for washing hands, meanwhile obeying physicall distance and reduced number of children per activity.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

The project emerges as a need to urgently respond to the needs of the communities, children and parents during our interaction when they arrive at accommodation and relocation centers. The communities understand the need to organize activities for children so that they can play, relax, forget a little about painful and stressfull events that they have experienced and at the same time can educate their children on some basic skills for personal hygiene, reading, writing. In the monitoring of the process, UNICEF ensures that children are found comfortable, and wel listend to, so the activities firstly to address their stress and traumas rather than learning and studying. In this monitoring the communities, parents or caretakers of these children will be as much as involved as possible through creation of reference groups, discussion groups of different sex and age and also using the complaint boxes that will help us improve our implementation.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The registration process for children will be carried out in the accommodation and relocation centers and in the families that have received the IDPs. Community mobilization in the fields and in the host families in collaboration with local community leaders and selected NGOs will deliver a message of inclusion, namely that all children regardless of disability, boys and girls can participate. UNICEF will work with NGOs and staff experienced in delivery specific services and support for these children so that they can fully participate in the activities. Therefore as explained above, these activities are organized in good collaboration with the protection, WASH and health cluster and their selected NGOs in order to provide an intersectoral service for all these children. A specific process of screening children to discover invisible deficiencies wil be set up to increase the adaptation of our activities and ensure full participation of the children with disabilities . All will be done to give these children the social and emotional tools to overcome their challenges. As these challenges are also caused by general misunderstanding and underestimation of what these children are capable of, the parents, authorities and community will be fully involved in the promotion of inclusive actitivies and education. In the long run, facing and overcoming challenges and reducing the prejudices from the general public and increasing their chances to actively participate in society will help them to grow stronger and more resilient.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The selected location of the temporary activity and learning spaces inside a tent or a classroom or outside will be preceded by an evaluation of the location in collaboration with the local authorities , so that the location can be considered safe with materials that are safe for children to use, free of natural hazards (floods, storms) and clean with basic WASH conditions, and with confirmation on responsibilities for maintaining security, WASH and protection. At the same time the activities will aim to raise environmental awareness and protection of natural resources and avoiding erosion for instance by planting fruit and shade trees, creating living fences, grass planting and directing and harvesting rainwater etc.
