Project Summary

After influx of Rohingya refugee in Bangladesh, Nabolok has been addressing lifesaving services since June, 2018 to till today and we have been planning to continue for the coming year of 2021 at camp-23, 24, 26 as well as surrounding host communities under Teknaf upazila in Cox’s Bazar. Already, Nabolok had implemented few projects in camp 23, 24 and 26 for Rohingya and host community in Teknaf upazila on WASH, LPG distribution and Solar Street Light installation. Under this project we have been focusing to improve Site Management and Site Development (SMSD) as a consequence, Nabolok had already installed 362 solar street lights at camp-23, 24 & 26 for both Rohingya community and surrounding host community to consider ensuring safety and security issues at night with ensuring regular maintenance and operation of this Solar Street Light. Farther more, based on identifying gap Nabolok will install 70 nos of solar based street light to cover the existing need for the Rohingya refugee in the camp-24 & 26. In the early stage for the installation of new SSL at first we have plan to discuss with the beneficiaries especially women & adolescent girls for primary place selection considering their need so that this lighting facility will help them to use WASH facility and their daily movement at night more safely without any sexual harassment or abusement as well as it reduce rubbery also. Besides this, we will also have a plan 30 nos of solar street light for host community at Nhilla union. We have plan to focus on DRR issue during site selection just as: selecting a site near narrow bridge, Selecting a site near a safe place so that during natural disaster beneficiary can easily reach that place with lighting facility etc. The project will also ensures repairing & maintenance works on a regular basis by collecting feedback from direct beneficiaries along with regular field monitoring both the field level staff as well as a full time monitoring officer as well as considering the feedback from site management and other stakeholder for upcoming five years. This site development activity will increase the level of feeling safe during night at the roadside and at the community.

1 - Identified Needs

Considering the camp context, several field experiences, identified gap along with assessing the needs of beneficiaries regarding safety issue especially at night, we found that around 58% beneficiaries aren’t getting enough lighting facility during night. As a consequences, beneficiary doesn’t feel safe during night especially for using WASH and facilities and there always a possibility of harassment, sexual abusement and robbery. In this project we have plan to minimize that problem at camp and host community level by installing 70 nos of solar based street light at camps-24 & 26 and 30 nos for nearer host community under Nhilla union. Along with, we will ensure the proper use, regular operation & maintenance of total 462 nos of installation which we had installed during previous couple of years including newly 100 nos of installation. As well as we can ensure maximum number of beneficiaries will get lighting facility by our installation, meet up safety issue during at night besides we need to address the new challenges or demands regarding this issue. This project will help to improve site management and site development activities, to be more specific it will ensure safety, security during night and protection issues. The all above mentioned activity will be included in our project to ensure the 50% of targeted beneficiaries have proper lighting facility at night as well as their involvement during site selection and regular feedback throughout the whole project duration.

1. Installation of 70 nos of solar based street light for Rohingya refugee after assessing the gaps at the camp-24 & 26.2.
