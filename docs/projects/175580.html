Project Summary

The project plans to assist the most vulnerable population displaced by violence in Cabo Delgado province in four districts, through actions aimed at ensuring the provision of water through the construction of new water points and rehabilitation of some existent, and sanitation through the latrines construction, in Metuge, Pemba, Montepuez and Ancuabe districts. 60.000 IDP’s direct target. // Indirect target 145.500.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project will guarantee access to basic water and sanitation services to the population displaced by violence in the districts of Pemba city, Metuge, Montepuez and Ancuabe, by implementing two kind of actions:The first type are those activities to offer a quality and safe service of a) sanitation, through the construction of latrines in adequate places avoid contaminating the aquifers, and at the same time guarantee the safety especially of women and children and considering gender issues, and b) water supply through the rehabilitation of some existing sources and the construction of some new sources in some communities, IDP's site camps, and health centers, whenever possible, including the formation and supervision of water committees where necessary. The second kind of actions are aimed at strengthening the capacities of the people targeted, and improving their hygiene and health conditions including management of water for consumption, maintenance of personal hygiene, and adoption of healthy and safe behaviors, with the NFI WASH related distribution (ex. chlorine, hygiene kits) and mass media WASH message diffusion, and finally, hygiene promotion campaigns aiming to prevent some diseases, including covid19, focusing on hand washing and hygiene measures adoption, water management, and correct use of latrines. 2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

R1 - CLEAN WATER DISTRIBUTION A1.1. Rehabilitation and disinfection of existing water points in host communities, accommodation centers, and resettlement areas, including support for water committees. A1.2. Construction of new water sources for host communities and resettlement areas A1.3. Distribution of point-of-use water treatment materials (Certeza©), including training and monitoring on proper use at household level. A1.4. Creation and training of water committees for development of promotion activities and maintenance of waterpoints. R2 - SANITATION SERVICES PROVISION A2.1. Installation and management of temporary sanitation and bathing facilities in accommodation centres / transit camps and / or resettlement sites (first phase) A2.2. Promotion activities for self-construction of household latrines and garbage pits in resettlement sites and hosting communities (including the provision of sanitation supplies for most vulnerable households) R3 – HYGIENE COMMUNITY PROMOTION ACTIVITIES A3.1. Dissemination of IEC WASH related messages through mass communication media A3.2. Procurement and distribution of WASH related Non-Food items, with focus on most vulnerable families / households: ex. Hygiene kits A3.3. handwashing promotion and covid19 prevention measures regular campaigns A3.4. promotion campaigns at waterborne diseases prevention level: Cholera and AWD outbreaks prevention, malaria prevention, including water management at household level.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The joint and coordinated work between local authorities, Cluster Leading international Agencies (UN) and the beneficiaries themselves is essential for a successful, flexible and continuously developing risk analysis and management and screening it. For this reason, will be carried out a previous identification of potential risks , which will also include a risk and management analysis , through the implementation of a collaborative coordinated work system between the beneficiaries, district and provincial authorities and with the different clusters involved in the project (CCCM, WASH Cluster, Protection Cluster (UNICEF)). These results will be contrasted with the previous risk analyses identified by the Mozambican authorities in charge for the assistance to the displaced people (INGC), as well as the district administrators. Above all, it will be taken maximun precautions to ensure the safety of the women and prevent any kind of harassment or assault. The empowerment of people will be one of the crosscutting goals that the project aims through individual and familiar WASH hygiene related promotion activities, and involvement of people within the activities themselves, making them part of the decision making process, and not just recipients of the activities, making people the protagonists of their own well-being.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

The base of this project is the care of people's health through a combination of active participation and service provision, so it is vital to encourage and empower people to be the engine of their own improvement of access to WASH services, and taking the control of the activities filling the project as own. Taking this into account from the beginning and maintaining it throughout the project. This is the reason for the formation, training and supervision of the water committees in each of the locations and their articulation with the district and provincial authorities and CCCM in every sitecamp.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

People with disabilities will be located and incorporated into the activities as priority beneficiaries through the implementation of a baseline census of the beneficiaries at the beginnig of the project conducted among project promoters , community leaders, and the collaboration of other national and international partners directly related to the work with vulnerable people. will be placed an special emphasis on the active incorporation of these persons in the activities, being prioritized to receive WASH items and join the promotion activities. At the same time, will be developed a map of existing barriers to identify and eliminate these, or at least to manage it correctly to allow a full and fair access to WASH services for all. Finally, will be carried out a permanent screening of people with disabilities and special vulnerability during the visits of the promoters and with the collaboration of community leaders and water committees.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Respect for the environment will be ensured throughout the project, avoiding any type of environmental pollution, such as forest degradation, and the contamination of aquifers specially regarding to latrine use and water management, and avoiding public health problems as open-air fecalism that could lead to a deterioration in the quality of life of the people who will benefit from the activities of this project. The collection and adequate treatment of home/familiar waste materials will be ensured, following the public health most elemental regulations. Likewise, the development of activities that promote an adequate environmental sanitation of the sites camps and homes that they host will be encouraged, aiming to guarantee a healthy, clean, and hygienic environment. That is the ultimate goal of all promotion activities related with encouraging families to build and manage properly their own latrines and waste pits. It will be included in all health promotion activities as a cross-cutting element of environmental conservation and environmental health, highlighting the impact that environmental degradation has on people, and why it is so important to avoid deforestation, preserve water sources and what measures can be taken to respect in daily life.
