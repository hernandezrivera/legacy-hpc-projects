Project Summary

Orange, based on its experience implementing several evidence-based education, livelihoods, protection, and civil society capacity development programming in northern Syria, its well-established relationship with relevant stakeholders, including the project proposed beneficiaries, propose to implement a project titled, Strengthening Resilience of Conflict-Affected Youth in Northern Syria as a continues intervention with reaching out to the number of targeted beneficiaries and expanding into further geographical locations. The project's main objective is to strengthen the resilience of youth to be engaging and participating actors in their communities. The project will include several activities and outputs which will contribute towards achieving the project objective, as further detailed below

4 - Needs

The ten years of war and displacement changed every aspects of the lives of millions of Syrian women and men, girls and boys. The livelihood conditions of the population in northern Syria has been hugely affected by the ongoing crisis mainly because of the economic situation, including the deflation of the Syrian pound, the breakdown of the government subsidies, breakdown of supply routes and sources of raw materials and the closure of factories, and massive rise in the prices of fuel and raw materials. This resulted in increasing competition for decreasing job opportunities. The displaced people did flee to other areas with little or no assets and were forced to sell assets, which have minimized their long-term coping abilities, and reduced income-generating opportunities. The ongoing conflict not only caused also led to the destruction of infrastructure, including the destruction of public facilities, markets and factories. Rehabilitation of infrastructures such as quick repairs of electricity and water, jobs, and employment opportunities, are also urgently required. Agricultural production is severely diminished due to the collapse of the farming sector. Both staple crop and horticultural production are affected, with the proportion of rural families engaged in horticultural production from almost 100% to an estimated 30%. The main causes are damaged irrigation, drought, lack of money for key inputs, and insecurity. Seed prices have doubled, and the proportion of families taking loans to buy seeds has. Decreases in harvest yields are further depleting families’ already largely exhausted stocks and pushing more HH into negative coping strategies. Nonetheless, agriculture remains a core source of income sources.

5 - Implementation of activities

This project aims to contribute empowering conflict affected youth and strengthening their resilience through technical and vocational trainings and start-up opportunities. Orange will identify beneficiaries’ needs and develop appropriate care plans and support these with service mapping services. After completing career counselling and providing, and conducting a labor market assessment; Orange will make a technical and vocational training plan which will improve the beneficiaries skills and make them eligible candidates for employment or entrepreneurship in respective markets. These technical and vocational trainings will qualify them to be reach out to the new jobs as well as it qualifies for them to start-up their own business. Beneficiaries will be encourages and trained to provide a business start-up plan and they will have chance to receive grants for their start-up according to the results of a competitive selection process. Supporting the start-up plans with small grants (in-kind productive assets) and counselling will be the next step of the project and this will enable youth to establish sustainable income generating businesses and increase their living conditions and resilience.
