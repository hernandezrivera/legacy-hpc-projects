Project Summary

Explosive Ordnance Risk Education ( EORE) is aiming to address short term emergency needs as well as to contribute to long term sustainable response in line with the thematic areas of the national plan for protection of civilians . NADA used to deliver EORE projects since its establishment in 2014 and currently NADA is among few national actors whom are NADA is the only organization executing EORE and it is paramount to continue with EORE activities in support of UNMAS program especially after Juba Peace Agreement (JPA) and return of armed groups without systematic storages of weapons. On the other side this will encourage voluntary return and as thus big influx of IDPs and Sudanese refugees will be at higher risk in moving back to their home villages and towns without comprehensive knowledge about the present of E NADA strategy aims to provide EORE services that meet short-and long-term needs with special focus in the remote areas, IDPs camps, new settlements, rural villages and among nomads. NADA plans the EORE program following the International Mine Action Standards (IMAS) and SNMAS. In the short term, emergency EORE will be provided to at-risk population such as IDPs, returnees, nomads and local communities in the form of direct presentation, Community based EORE training, safety orientation public information campaigns (PICs), and radio program broadcasting. Affected communities including EO victims and Persons with Disabilities (PWDs) will be given awareness raising on the rights and issues of victims and PWDs. The long-term strategy will focus on integrating EORE into the existing community services and structures through provision of Community based CB EORE targeting of teachers, health /social workers, community leaders, members of the community based protection networks (CBPNs) and community volunteers including children. This will contribute to better access of communities to information through the presence of trained community members This EORE project will consider raising landmines/ERW victims’ awareness in terms of prevention of incidence; victim-related rights and available information on services providers. The project is designed to response to the objectives of Sudan Mine Action Multi-year plan 2020-2023 which aim to have Sudan free of landmine/ERW by 2023. The project with be implemented in collaboration and coordination of UN Integrated Transitional Assistance Mission in Sudan (UNITAMS), for that the project will be delivered through immediate EORE and community based responses. In all its EORE projects; NADA considered two aspects a) age and gender – appropriate methods of communication will be used. Messages for drama, songs, and public campaigns were developed for different age group with full consideration of culture. NMAC and UNMAS will be consulted prior to printing /production and/or distribution of messages. b) New innovation NADA will pilot new innovation such as silent theater methodology. Also the project will consider delivery of EORE to school children during the school year, while focusing on the other children out of school . During limitation of EORE teams ‘movement, the project team will focus on CB EORE, radio and PIC and media channels to ensure wider coverage in case of road locks due to insecurity or diseases outbreak.

1 - Needs

Long history of conflict in Sudan affected vast land with Explosive Ordnance (EO) including land mines and Explosive Remnants of War (ERW) which have resulted in people displacement and led to severe restrictions on emergency and developmental aid as well livelihood activities as .Darfur states are among the heavily contaminated states as indicated in HRP 2023 report . Approximately 2.07 million people living or moving through EO affected areas are in need of improved and safe life conditions including humanitarian assistance which could be hindered or limited because of the presence of ERW. In Darfur, according to the Information IMSMA, EO contamination resulted in the casualty of 16 individuals in the year 2022. 100 % of victims’ case load are children. As of September 2022 IMSMA report; 2,539 victims were killed and /or injured 6,632,914 square kilometers were released in the five Darfur States. In Darfur context; EORE should be implemented at short term and long term strategies in light of the new UN political integrated transition Assistance Mission in Sudan and the political and security dynamic, government and humanitarian actors are planning to support the returnees in their home villages since the voluntary return and protection of civilian program are already launched in the five states. Populations in risk and need an urgent EORE program prior to their trip back home as their areas are known for wars and battles and might be contaminated of EO. In Darfur women and children have additional social roles such as brining water and fire wood from far distances, this explore them to higher risks of being injured or killed due to the present of EO and/ or UXOs. Women and girls some time reach to 5- 9 Km away from their residence searching for fire wood. Newly arrived returnees especially those who were Sudanese refugees in other countries are lacking any information of EO /UXOs present risks, impact and prevention ways. Children in particularly from nomads’ groups moved for long distance with their catties and they are facing daily risk of getting injured or maimed due to the contamination of the EO or /and UXOs. EORE will be promoted through different interventions including direct EORE, community-based approach, radio broadcasting, public information campaigns and distribution of awareness materials.

Output: Community members including individuals from social workers, community leaders and volunteers are trained on CB EORE on community driven approach and gain knowledge on the right and issues of PWDs including EO victims. Activity: • Compose CB EORE volunteer criteria and select them according to it with consideration to gender, age, and culture balance. • Train community volunteers including individuals from teachers, health and social workers, community leaders in CBEORE. Output: Communities of the targeted states within Darfur received audio EORE messages through radio program broadcasting including drama in simple local Arabic.
