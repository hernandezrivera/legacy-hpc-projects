Project Summary

Adverse impacts of the 2016-2017 severe drought combined with protracted armed conflicts continue to persist in Marka district,Lower Shabelle region especially among agro pastoral people who became destitute and got displaced due to the drought and the protracted armed clan conflicts and among them are agro pastoralists who lost most of their productive assets and animals.
Drought and armed conflict affected agro pastoral people in Marka district need urgent assistance to purchase and produce food. Cash transfers offer a vital safety net to families that lost production, assets and income, and cannot otherwise meet their food needs. Providing cash to agro pastoral communities affected by droughts and armed clan conflicts will not only fights hunger, but minimizes displacement and the sale of productive assets that ultimately feed people.
The objective of this project is to increase access to food security and restore livelihoods for 2500 drought affected agro pastoral households in Marka district Lower Shabelle region saving them from further succumbing into emergency (IPC4) through integrated unconditional cash transfer and agriculture inputs distribution.
In this regard, Action for Women and Children Concern(AWCC) is targeting vulnerable 2500 (1250 female and 1250 male) headed drought and armed conflict affected agro pastoral households through integrated cash and agriculture inputs distribution project which combines unconditional cash transfers with agricultural inputs to ensure families meet their immediate food needs while restoring their own food production.
The target vulnerable 2500 farming households will be provided seeds (somtux maize/Maize), pulses (Cowpea), vegetable seeds, and unconditional cash transfers.
The project will support the target beneficiaries for a period of three and half (3.5) months which constitutes direct support to affected agro pastoral target communities for a period of one cropping season.
AWCC will incorporate project component on training target beneficiaries on Good Agriculture Practices(GAP) and promote sustainable agriculture in the face of growing climate change effects in Somalia.

Needs

Acute food insecurity, malnutrition and livelihood needs still remain critical throughout Somalia. Two in five Somalis remain acutely food insecure, more than 2.6 million people are internally displaced.According to FSNAU/FEWSNET Post Gu seasonal assessment over 1.5 million people face acute food security Crisis or worse (IPC Phases 3 or higher) between now and December 2018. Approximately 3.1 million people are Stressed (IPC Phase 2). This brings the total number of people in need (IPC Phases 2 or higher) through the end of 2018 to 4.6 million.The last 3 to 5 cereal harvests of 2016-2018 have been very poor, ranging 37-70 percent below average, and the Deyr harvest in December is also expected to be low. The adverse impacts of the 2016-2017 severe droughts continue to persist, especially agro pastoral people who became destitute and got displaced due to drought and armed clan conflicts in Marka district. The affected people lost most of their productive and animals and are not able to purchase agriculture inputs in the event they should restart their livelihood. Families are also facing price shocks as tightened food supplies drive prices up. As of September in Marka, price of somtux maize was 50 percent above average. This translates into one-third less food for the same money – or insufficient food for 2 out of 6 people in a household in Marka. As prices rise, poor families reduce portion sizes, cut nutritious food from their diet and skip meals. People are deep in debt and cannot access further loans. Much of their meager production goes to repay past loans.Rural people need urgent assistance to purchase and produce food. Cash transfers offer a vital safety net to families that lost production, assets and income, and cannot otherwise meet their food needs.With the provision of integrated unconditional cash and agric inputs, target households will have increased access to food security and agric inputs to grow crops to become resilient.

Activities

• Mobilization and sensitization of communities at regional, district and village level;• Beneficiary (2500 HH heads of whom 50% female) selection and registration • Procure seeds(50 MT maize and 20 MT sesame seeds) and Assess the quality of seeds in traders stores;• Preparation of Distribution plan; and distribution of inputs and cash vouchers; • Linking the beneficiaries to the Money vendor (MV);• Identification and registration of willing money vendor for the unconditional cash transfer• Coordination of unconditional cash payments to beneficairies by the MV;• Distribution and supervision of unconditional cash transfer activity on monthly basis• Monitor exchange of vouchers for seeds by the beneficiary HHs; • Facilitation of the post distribution review and Crop Harvest Assessment; • Reports according to format provided .

How is the project linked to durable solutions/resilience?

The project is linked to durable solutions as it strengthens the communities capacities to increase their coping mechanisms and become resilient to climatic shocks through restoration of their productive assets.The project will be providing agriculture inputs as start up capital with sustained support of unconditional cash transfer for one cropping season which encourages the beneficiaries to grow crops with the unconditional cash transfer support they receive and after the cropping season and project phase out they will be able to sell the surplus of their produce to meet their other needs while also saving some of their crops for food while also working to continue producing different crops.There will be a project component where the beneficiaries are to be trained on crop diversification,income generation and community disaster risk reduction to increase their resilience and make the project outcomes more sustainable.Generally AWCC will ensure that the project contribute to Core Humanitarian Standar(CHS) pillar 3 strengthening the capacities of the target communities to make them more prepared,resilient and less vulnerable to aftershocks with the achievement of sustainable project outcomes particularly increased capacities through restoration of their livelihoods particularly revitalizing agriculture/production,value addition of their produce and increased access to markets through establishing trader linkages.

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

AWCC,in line with CHS commitments, brings all communities on board for inclusive participation and prevention of exclusion based on displacement and risks.AWCC realizes and considers existing threats and risks,it would also identify risks in consultation with target groups to explore mitigation measures. Currently fragile security owes to existential threat in the Somalia with this regard, AWCC ensures that distribution and beneficiaries routes from distribution points to their homes are maximum safe.AWCC ensures all stakeholders including local authorities and different militias leaders respect humanitarian norms for safe project delivery. Given that there is a fast change in terms of the fragility of the security situation, AWCC is optimistic chances of incidents in relation to security are minimal though. AWCC engages target communities in all decisions that affect them in line with CHS pillare 4 making them know their rights and entitlements, have access to information and participate in decisions that affect them. AWCC works closely with community leaders, traditional groups, women groups and the local authority to introduce and strengthen DRR, community coexistence and respect to human rights and protection of vulnerable groups in the community. Displacement related to natural crisis would be reduced through DRR and provision of cash to affected agro pastoral communities will not only fight hunger, but minimizes displacement as once beneficiaries have cash to meet their immediate needs and agric inputs they will resume farming.As part of mobilization and sensitization exercises, AWCC will target traders to ensure they adequately supply essential commodities to address rising food prices contributing food security pillar of "AVAILABILTY” and mitigating risks of rising food prices affecting the beneficiaries. AWCC works with all stakeholders to ensure that traders address commodity supply bottlenecks that affect food "AVAILABILTIY” and keep food prices stable.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

Marka district,Lower Shabelle region is an areas recovering from protracted armed clan conflicts and there are undeniable risks particularly in association with clan rivalry that may have the propensity to contribute to marginalization and exclusion,however,Action for Women and Children Concern (AWCC) will ensure to identify groups at risk and in collaboration with community stakeholders to prioritize their needs in very inclusive approach promoting equitable and impartial access to support inline with beneficiaries targeting criteria depending on the needs of the affected people irrespective of their gender,caste and clan.Action for Women and Children Concern (AWCC) will also organize and hold respective sensitization meeting for male and female groups in specific gender and age sessions to identify and learn their needs to increase prevention of risks and exclusion based on gender and age.For complaints and grievances AWCC will ensure that project target communities have access to safe and responsive mechanisms to handle complaints particularly setting up hotline phone for complaints and response.

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

Action for Women and Children Concern(AWCC) will strongly avoid all forms of avenue that may lead to armed conflicts and accordingly form inclusive village relief committees in open and transparent participatory sessions in the presence of different clan groups,community leaders and the local authorities and AWCC field officers will play an active role in convincing the communities that targeting criteria will strictly remain guiding principles of the project implementation particularly in the identification and selection of beneficiaries in the course of the project.As part of cross cutting issue,Action for Women and Children Concern(AWCC) will promote peaceful coexistence and strengthen reconciliation through increased inclusive meetings with concern to project activities particularly continuing sustained sensitization throughout the project time frame.
