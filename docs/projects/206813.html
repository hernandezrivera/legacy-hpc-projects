Project Summary

The implementation of PEBE consists on a practical training for school staff, including director, teachers, parents and students on how to implement this basic emergency plan. In addition to School Disaster Management Committee, the plan includes participation of key people from community. To achieve this, there are several manuals approved by Ministry and kits that must be distributed in each school. During an emergency situation, PEBE is activated to ensure efficient management of school, including protection of staff and materials.
The training will be carried out in schools located in conflict zones in the north of the country and natural disasters. According to Education Cluster prioritization and aligned with UN-Habitat plan, in North Zone were selected districts from provinces of Cabo Delgado (conflict) and Nampula (conflict); in Central Zone were selected districts from provinces of Zambézia, Tete, Manica and Sofala; in South Zone were selected districts from provinces of Inhambane, Gaza, Maputo Province and Maputo City. The target group is made up of 157,893 children from host community.
One of the sustainable development objectives is quality education, inclusion of everyone, in addition to make communities safe, resilient and sustainable.
Each school needs a specific plan to deal with natural events such as cyclones, earthquakes and floods. For this reason, plan will include useful information to be used whenever a natural disaster occurs, such as: emergency contacts, person responsible for kits, evacuation routes, meeting points, shelter areas, accessibility to the terrain, simulation to test the capacity of the school in relation to an emergency, etc. It is also necessary to have useful information such as: number and type of school infrastructures, material used to construction and state of conservation, identification of vulnerable points of buildings for reinforcement and knowledge of most frequent risks and threats in the past.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The potential risk identified is as follows:- Poor preparedness for a natural disaster such as floods or cyclones caused by changes in the members of the management committee or other reasons leading to human, economic and material losses. In order to avoid this situation, PEBE will be monitored during all stages of implementation, which is a fundamental control measure for the success and fulfillment of disaster management.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The group of students who will participate in the PEBE training must be made up of girls and boys in order to guarantee the inclusion of all genders, and in accordance with the objectives of sustainable development, which are quality education and inclusion of everybody. The disaster risk management committee will include female teachers, students and some parents. Together, students will participate in training, mapping and first aid simulations. As every school belongs to a community, the plan designed not only influences the students, but also members who live in the surrounding area. Despite focus being school community, the elderly people have an important role in the community as more experienced residents, that's why is important their involvement and consultation in reducing the risk of calamities in the environment where the school is located.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

One of the objectives of PEB training is the identification of safe areas and elevated shelters, so in the event of an emergency, people with special needs can have access through appropriate routes presented in the specific plan of each school as well as risk points such trash holes.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project does not negatively impact the environment, on the contrary it can modify it in some cases through the creation of physical barriers to protect against winds and control erosion, such as through planting trees and placing sandbags.
