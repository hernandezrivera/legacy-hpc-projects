Project Summary

Silk Road will tackle the challenges identified in its needs assessment. This will be achieved through multi-faceted awareness-raising interventions targeting conflict-affected groups in urgent need of humanitarian assistance. We aim to increase knowledge of Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (SEA) within targeted communities, including Harim, Bsanya, Kafr Hum, Ariba, Salkin, Azaz, Suran, Mare, Harbal, Salama, Sejar, Maarin, Karamil, Kafra, Qazal, and Yahmul.
Experienced outreach staff will conduct field sessions with beneficiary groups. These sessions will highlight SEA issues with practical examples drawn from real-life experiences within Syria, always adhering to relevant humanitarian standards. Additionally, awareness brochures with key SEA messages and illustrations will be distributed, ensuring accessibility for illiterate individuals within the targeted communities. Silk Road will work to resolve all gaps/risks related to the organizational management by utilizing the WoS PSEA Network Checklists. As it will be utilized by management and HR to review existing organizational policies and procedures related to SEA. The field checklist also will be utilized in the field to assess the organizational situation and fill the gaps accordingly. The organization team will be attend SEA training to enhance the importance of complying with SEA prohibitions and reporting any incidents. New staff should always be briefed when starting work. The organizational PSEA Code of Conduct is signed by all staff. The Code of Conduct is displayed in prominent places in offices. Silk Road will ensure designate a staff members as a PSEA Focal points with providing them with the needed support. Silk Road will comply to establish a complaints mechanism and publicize how staff can report concerns or suspicions of SEA. Together with the PSEA Network and local populations, establish how beneficiaries / local populations can report SEA. As this complaints mechanisms will be in the same line with other organizations operating in a given location, rather than the Silk Road own system. Giving the ability of giving feedback on a variety of issues, and not just SEA, in order to encourage people to use the complaints mechanism and to avoid stigmatization of the complainant. Silk Road will establish clear procedures regarding how to handle and direct complaints.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Silk Road will start Key activities preparation including announcing the project for the targeted areas and explain the roles of each party, the purpose of the project, its objectives and activities . Mainly, Silk Road will target the people who are living in camps with targeting the vulnerable people inside communities. As per our Rapid assessment, no PSEA activities have been implemented during the last year in the targeted areas with the existence of a big number of IDPs live in the camps there. So we will do a quick registration for the people inside the communities to assess the vulnerability and prepare the final lists of the participants inside communities, while no need for the registration to those people who are living in the camps, as the people in the camps considered a vulnerable people who are basically in need for the human aid. Mainly the awareness sessions will be provided to the head of the family who will be able to transfer the knowledge to his family members. The awareness sessions will be provided by the Silk Road reach out personnel who have an experience to do this kind of activities. The awareness sessions will be provided in the location by gathering small groups of people to receive the sessions. The attendees for the sessions will be provided with brochures as well.
