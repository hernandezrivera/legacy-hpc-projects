Project Summary

NRC will provide provide the much-needed emergency response to the needs of young people affected by conflict in northeast Nigeria. The role of NRC is to provide an education intervention that aligns with the principles of humans rights through youth programming. Convention Rights of the Children will also be taken into consideration since children are also targeted in the education response. Youth programming and support of peer to peer education on technical skills will address the youth needs.
NRC will support vulnerable conflict-affected adolescents and youth (aged 14 to 25 years) and children through a non formal community-based education intervention that applies the concept of functional literacy by provision of Accelerated Education Programme that allows transition into the formal schools

Needs

The humanitarian needs overview shared by OCHA in 2018 reported that education gaps in north-east Nigeria are as a result of ongoing conflict. An estimated 2.9 million children are deprived from quality education or have no access to education at all. An estimated 1.8 million people are displaced in Borno, Adamawa and Yobe and around 40 percent (0.8 million) of that population is comprised of school aged children between three and seventeen years displaced across the three statesSome programmes require at least the completion of primary education in order to enrol in any of the vocational training courses. As a result, illiterate youth are excluded from such programmes. Furthermore, there are no other kinds of opportunities that cover different aspects of youth and human development such as citizenship or positive social engagement. The situation remains bleaker for women, who have no social network that can support young mothers. In addition, are there less female teachers who can provide the much needed motivation to enrol in any form of education. The situation is such that there is a specific risk of continuous cycles of negative coping mechanisms and the creation of a culture of dependency. Existing low levels of literacy and the decrease in livelihood opportunities due to the conflict are putting youths in a hopeless situation.

Activities

NRC will provide entrepreneurship and vocational trainings to out of school adolescent and youths to create opportunities of self employment to augment their living. Many children had their education disrupted due to conflict and had never had access to any form of education. To reach out to education needs of these adolescents and youths and in Borno state, NRC plans to provide non-formal education, training and skills development tailored to employment or livelihoods opportunities for out of school adolescents and youths. NRC’s education strategy focuses on youth because they face a social and political vacuum encompassed by lack of social services, group identity, and economic opportunities. Existing low levels of literacy and the decrease in livelihood opportunities due to the conflict are putting youth behind and in a hopeless situation. For youths who have never attended school or not completed the first three years of primary education, it is much more challenging to be involved in income generating activities or to secure a job. The project objective is to reduce vulnerability of youth and adolescents is through provison of livelihoods and vocational skill to create self employment opportunities.
