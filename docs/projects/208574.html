Project Summary

Through this project NRM seeks to provide support to vulnerable conflict affected households with Micro Enterprise and vocational skills trainings, and IGAs cash grant for Economic Recovery and Market System in Michika and Madagali LGAs, Adamawa State. The project targets vulnerable conflict affected HHs including IDPs, Host Communities and Returnees. The intervention aims to provide livelihood, promote the local market, and ensure self-reliance by the project participants. Selected households will undergo vocational and non-vocational training by NRM in collaboration with Government MDAs and be provided IGA assistance through cash grant.

1 - Needs

The over a decade conflict in the Northeastern Nigeria, particularly Michika and Madagli LGAs, many Households have lost their means of Livelihoods/productive assets and as such having difficulty to meet their daily needs. There is need for IGA cash grant and capacity building for the affected people on entrepreneurship and vocational skills to improve in their income generating activities to be able to withstand feature shocks. This will strengthened their resilience and self-reliant if they are empowered.

2 - Activities

NRM under this project will carry out these activities: training of 1000 participants on Micro Enterprise and vocational skills to equip them with skills and knowledge to adequately run their income generating activities smoothly for growth and profit, formation of 10 VSLA groups with 10 members in each group and support with VSLA kits to encourage saving culture and access to financial services such as loan by the participants and also to promote social cohesion among the participants, provision of IGA cash grant to 1000HHs to support them with IGA startup capital which will contribute to the stimulation of the local market/economy and vocational skills training to provide them with basic knowledge of business.
