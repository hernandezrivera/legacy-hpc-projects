Project Summary

The rehabilitation approach of about 30 endangered schools affected by the conflict and long aging provide a real time assessment of needs based on the priority of people and communities with regard to vulnerable educational process. when long age of ignorance and conflict occur, coupled with neglecting routine maintenance over years and rapid increase in population, schools in the affected areas may suffer damages and disruptions to their operations. Students may be forced to miss classes or move to alternative learning environments, such as makeshift classrooms or online learning platforms. The extent of the impact on schools would depend on the schools’ age and type of construction. Most of aged schools experienced different types of cracks and disruption of school environment. More than 12 years of conflict and tens-years of neglecting have left a heavy, devastated and trifling burden on the education process. When these painful situations continued without any proper remediation and rehabilitation, the educational situation and the kids’ lives inside the schools become more complicated and devastated.
Needs based on HNO-2024
Learning environments are unconducive due to overcrowding, lack of school furniture/ school supplies, and operational WASH facilities. Lack of electricity is affecting education supply, as schools lack heating, cooling, and lighting to operate in multiple shifts.
The education infrastructure was already insufficient prior to the earthquakes. An estimated 3,700 schools are in need of rehabilitation/repairs. There are 1,054 schools damaged in north-west Syria. Availability of schools is further reduced with sudden onsets of displacements when schools are used as temporary shelters in some areas.
Current status of the schools
Classroom and corridors: Cracked walls with peeling paints and worn plaster, old wooden doors, old worn and chipped tiles, broken windows, bad security screen mesh, in addition to aged discs. Latrine units: often suffer from deteriorating infrastructure, including plumbing and fixtures that are outdated, damaged, or malfunctioning. This can lead to issues such as leaking pipes, broken toilets, faulty faucets, or inadequate drainage systems. External facades and boundary walls: worn plastering and peeling external finish.
The intervention will be implemented in the conflict affected areas; the exact locations for Rehabilitation of schools will be in Idlib district. The schools in this area are most properly marginalized, ignored and at risk. The list of schools assessed by MIDMAR and coordinated with Education departments, will be shared with concerned cluster once the project is approved to avoid any overlapping with other actors.
Rehabilitation of these schools will give them lives and sustainability and readiness for their original functions to accommodate emerging generation's educational process. Consequently, more than 13,000 students; (6,630 males & 6,370 females), 600 children with disabilities, aging from 6 to 17 years will have access to proper education.
Moreover, the intervention includes internal and external maintenance for classrooms, laboratories, multi-purpose rooms, other admin rooms, and corridors as well. it includes also all WASH facilities and latrine units, in addition to provide access to water. it includes also environmental adaptation of all schools' facilities for children with disabilities, like rails, ramps, access roads, bathrooms, etc. The intervention includes providing access to creational areas, playgrounds, and green staff. It includes providing access to solar energy system and necessary electronic equipment like computers and printers. The intervention includes maintenance for school's furniture and providing new furniture when needed.
Regarding PSEA: this rehabilitation project has no direct influence on PSEA as the project provides technical renovations to schools' buildings. The labors have no direct contact with children or teachers.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

MIDMAR prioritizes reaching those schools which are most marginalized and at risk. This will be done primarily through a comprehensive maintenance approach. A key component of our programming in Syria is responding where needs are most high, where others are not responding, and in line with ours, and concerned parties’ missions and visions. To this end MIDMAR will provide support to endangered schools affected by the long aging and conflict to enable decent educational environment. Rehabilitation can involve these steps:Assessment and planning: The first step is to conduct a thorough and detailed assessment of the condition of the school, including the buildings, infrastructure, WASH facilities, and equipment. This will help to identify the specific areas that need attention and prioritize the rehabilitation efforts. (this step has been completed since April 2023, where tender documents and bills of quantities are ready). It should be mentioned that these schools have no maintenance up to date. Once the assessment is complete, a comprehensive rehabilitation plan can be developed.Implementation: The actual rehabilitation work can then begin, which may involve repairing or replacing damaged facilities, upgrading infrastructure and equipment, and improving the learning environment (if possible). For these maintenance type projects that require special technical capacities, the local contractors registered with contractor’s union as maintenance contractor’s category-A in NWS, will be awarded the works based on competitive quotations. The field site-supervision will be provided by local consultant as well to ensure best complying to specifications.Protection Risk Analysis and mitigation measure: during the execution of the project, “Do no harm” principal shall govern the performance of contractors to use guide signs, fences and special pathways to prevent contact with children. The dusty works; like hacking paints, removing plastering, repairing/ welding steel components, demolishing works, and rubble removing should be executed in the off-time where children are out of schools.Moreover, the rehabilitation includes construction protection steel sheds in the school yards, and environmental adaptation works to facilitate access of CWD to school settings.Monitoring and Evaluation: Throughout the rehabilitation process, progress should be regularly monitored and evaluated to ensure that the project is on track and achieving its intended goals. Any necessary adjustments should be made to the plan as needed.Overall, the approach for rehabilitating endangered schools should be collaborative, involving all stakeholders, including school administrators, teachers, students, parents, and community members. By working together, it is possible to create a safe and supportive learning environment that promotes academic success for all students.
