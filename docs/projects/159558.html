Project Summary

The proposed action aims at contributing towards the reduction of mortalities and morbidities associated with Malnutrition among under-five children and pregnant and lactating women by providing preventive and curative nutrition interventions and enhancing accessibility to nutrition services at select districts of Somaliland, Puntland and South Central. The action is geared towards reducing the vulnerability among target populations thus preventing or reducing levels of malnutrition within the chronic humanitarian context of Somalia. In order to achieve sustainable impact, the Project will be integrated with other health care services for enhanced provision of basic life-saving interventions and standard Health, Nutrition and WASH services at community level, health facilities and IDP camps in urban areas. Through the proposed action, WVSOM envisages to strengthen preventive and curative services and enhance community mobilization and sensitization for increased awareness on available services leading to increased up-take of Nutrition services and other integrated Health and WASH interventions at community level. Community engagement and participation will be crucial throughout the project cycle for ownership and active involvement during implementation and enhanced sustainability after project phase-out. WVSOM will work with community health workers (CHWs) and volunteer mothers at community level to support in the implementation of the project for early identification, referral and prompt treatment of malnourished children and PLWs for better health outcomes in addition to securing positive behaviour change on infant and young child feeding (IYCF) practices. To enhance effective implementation of the project, WVSOM will actively participate in nutrition cluster coordination mechanisms both at national and Zonal level. The proposed action intends to support early warning mechanisms, alert and response systems/surveillance with close linkages to the respective sectors to ensure prompt and early response to prevent excess mortality and morbidity. The action will endeavor to strengthen some aspects of health system strengthening (HSS) by building the capacity of MOH staff, provision of essential drugs and other essential supplies, support the support supervision field visits to target health facilities in order to improve quality service delivery to the target communities.

Mitigation Measures

Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacementWVSOM will ensure equal access for women and girls to integrated Health and Nutrition services, Health and Nutrition promotion activities, training programs and equal chance to linkage to other livelihood programs. The program will include both host communities, IDP and refugees so as to mitigate discrimination and resentment. WVSOM will put in place regular monitoring and feedback mechanisms to ensure that women and Girls are not violated and taken advantage for them to access services. The project will also work towards providing a platform and Negotiating for the participation of women and youth in leadership and decision-making both at community and household level on factors affecting their health and nutrition status. During project implementation WVSOM will also advocate for the linkage and inclusion of displaced and locally integrating populations to prevent discrimination.Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and ageGender inequalities in economic participation and opportunity. Despite high labour force participation by men and women, women are more disproportionately represented in unpaid subsistence and domestic work (reproductive gender roles), which is not accounted for in Labour Force Participation. Gender inequalities in economic opportunity are also reflected in high wage inequality for similar work. Women occupy less technical and professional positions than men. These economic factors when aggregated make women earn considerably lower annual incomes compared with men. Disability limits physical access to health care, education, employment, and justice. It also leads to economic, political, and social exclusion of vulnerable groups. Women and girls with disabilities face double discrimination and are more vulnerable to poverty and social exclusion. WVSOM will work towards ensuring universal inclusion of the vulnerable populations within the project.Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violenceWVSOM will focus on women and youth – to encourage interventions that are targeted to their particular needs, while acknowledging the crucial roles in household nutrition. The design and implementation of services will be based on a thorough analysis of local conflict dynamics so as to ensure that interventions, at a minimum, “do no harm”. WVSOM during the project implementation will ensure there is an inclusive community dialogue and start-up workshop including all community members and stakeholders aimed at enhancing ownership and programme effectiveness, thereby contributing to conflict mitigation. By ensuring equitable access to program services at the same time respecting the fundamental rights of the government and community members, and social protection will be used as a powerful tool in maintaining calm. WVSOM aims during the project implementation period to build the capacity the MOH and community to be able to participate in the program activities hence being used as influencers in achieving the Do No Harm principle. World Vision Somalia through its implementation and collaboration with MOH will work towards safeguarding the vulnerable groups and invest in social dialogue during the implementation of the project to understand key issues to address that contribute to conflicts hence have clear feedback mechanisms in place.
