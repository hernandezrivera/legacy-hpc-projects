Project Summary

EPDC plans to respond to the winter needs in Rural Damascus, Dara, and Aleppo with shelter rehabilitation and winter NFI kits to vulnerable households. Given easy access and pre-existing presence in the proposed locations, EPDC will ensure a quick and efficient launch of the project. Shelter: In 2022, EPDC plans to rehabilitate 50 apartments in Harasta, Rural Damascus 50 HHs/ 250 individuals (50 W, 50 M, 85 G, 65 B), and 50 apartments in Ne’meh, Dar’a 50 HHs/ 250 individuals (50 W, 50 M, 85 G, 65 B), and, in 2023, EPDC plans to rehabilitate 50 apartments in Harasta, Rural Damascus 50 HHs/ 250 individuals (50 W, 50 M, 85 G, 65 B), and 50 apartments in Ne’meh, Dar’a 50 HHs/ 250 individuals (50 W, 50 M, 85 G, 65 B). EPDC team has conducted visits to the local community and met with the heads of municipalities, mukhtars, and other key figures for consultation regarding the suggested intervention and gathered information about the extent of the damage and what is required for the IDPs to return to their homes. EPDC team conducted visits to several houses in the proposed locations to assess the extent of damage and what neighborhoods were most damaged. After the field visits, an approximate detailed BOQ was prepared based on the primary assessment of the apartments and necessary works to protect against the winter weather. A market study was conducted on the targeted areas that have most of the materials required for rehabilitation works. As a result of the existing visits, and based on a previous communication with the Shelter sector, EPDC will intervene with the amount of 2,500 USD (±500 USD) per apartment leaving a margin of ±500 USD because some apartments may need more or less rehabilitation works than others. EPDC will start the implementation with the registration process. Then, the MEAL team will conduct the verification and the eligible beneficiaries will be selected. EPDC will announce two tenders one for Dar’a and one for Rural Damascus and the winning contractors will conduct the rehabilitation works in the selected apartments. EPDC engineers will supervise the works and follow up. EPDC will receive the apartments and hand them over to their owners. EPDC MEAL team will monitor the redeeming process and conduct PDM.
NFI: in 2022, EPDC plans to distribute winter NFI to 800 vulnerable households in Hanano, Aleppo using voucher methodology i.e. 800 HHs/ 4,000 individuals (800 W, 800 M, 1,000 G, 1,400 B). EPDC conducted a recent needs assessment in several areas of Aleppo, which showed that there is a mass need for winter items. The survey showed that many people are more taken with securing the basic daily need of food items and due to the expensive cost of winter items they became unable to afford them. The need becomes urgent to provide supplies that protect the families from the cold of winter, in light of the lack of heating sources such as fuel and other methods. EPDC will obtain the necessary and required approvals to carry out the activity within the proposed area. EPDC will start implementation of the project with the registration process using the KoBo application and the MEAL team will conduct the verification according to selection and vulnerability criteria. The beneficiaries will be selected accordingly. EPDC will start the procurement process to contract with vendors and shops in the targeted location. Each NFI voucher will be redeemed for 5 winter jackets, 5 winter single quilts, and 5 pairs of socks and BNFs are free to choose the size and color. EPDC team will allocate certain days for redeeming the voucher and coordinate with the vendors to ensure they have the materials ahead of time. During the redeeming days, EPDC program and MEAL team will monitor the process and ensure that the materials that the beneficiaries are selecting are within the agreed specifications. EPDC MEAL team will monitor the redeeming process and conduct PDM.

4 - Needs

11 years into the Syrian conflict, an estimated 14 million people currently remain in need of humanitarian assistance, (HNO 2022). The situation remains volatile, especially in Dar’a and Aleppo where security, political, economic, and contextual factors contribute to the instability of the area. For this EPDC conducted needs assessments in Dar’a, Harasta, and Aleppo. In Dar’a and Rural Damascus, EPDC conducted a shelter needs assessment, and in Aleppo an NFI needs assessment. According to the results of the assessment people in Dar’a and Rural Damascus are in need of light shelter rehabilitation to their houses especially since the targeted locations were heavily affected by the crisis in order to protect them against the harsh weather of winter. Some of the apartments are partially damaged. The partially damaged shelters have been damaged due to the violent events but are structurally safe. The works include fixing walls, rehabilitation of wash facility in the house, and installing doors and windows. According to EPDC needs assessment in Aleppo, people are in need of winter items such as jackets, and quilts because their prices are expensive, and with the limited purchase power of the people in affected locations, the HHs are unable to purchase them. The modality of voucher proved its usefulness as the BNFs are free to select suitable sizes and colors and this reduces challenges of on-field distribution, in addition, has experience in implementing this type of modality.

5 - Implementation of activities

Shelter: EPDC will start the implementation with the registration process and apply for the approvals. The MEAL team will conduct the verification and eligible BNFs will be selected. EPDC will announce two tenders one for Dara and one for Rural Damascus and the BoQs with the type of required work, and the winning contractors will conduct the rehabilitation works in the selected apartments. EPDC will hand over the apartments to the contractors as per the contracts. EPDC engineers will supervise the works and follow up. EPDC will receive the apartments and hand them over to their owners. MEAL team will monitor the rehabilitation process and receive complaints, and conduct PDM. NFI: EPDC will start the implementation with the registration process and apply for the approvals. The MEAL team will conduct the verification and the eligible beneficiaries will be selected. Accordingly, the eligible BNFs will be selected and contacted to receive the vouchers. EPDC will contract with vendors and shops in the targeted location. Each NFI voucher will be redeemed for 5 winter jackets, 5 winter single quilts, and 5 pairs of socks valuing 400 $ and BNF are free to choose the size. EPDC team will allocate certain days for redeeming the vouchers and coordinate with the vendors to ensure they have the materials ahead of time. EPDC team will ensure the number and quality of the materials are according to the agreed specifications. MEAL team will monitor the process and conduct PDM.

11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Risk:1.Planned activities pose unintentional spread of COVID-19.2.Security situation in targeted areas deteriorates, preventing access and the implementation of activities 3.The value of the Syrian Pound deteriorates causing activities cannot be implemented within the planned budget.4.Permissions are unobtained within a reasonable period of time for the planned project.5.Potential for corruption or fraud on the side of the vendors Mitigation Measures:1.All activities will be planned and monitored closely in coordination with the local community to ensure no harm. All EPDC staff will be required to use adequate PPE while in the field, practice social distancing and receive awareness training on COVID-19 and prevention measures, including handwashing.2.Closely monitor the security situation in all targeted areas from a variety of sources. If needed, temporarily pause of the operations and re-evaluate the situation. If the situation continues, the activity will be shifted to another accessible area or scale-up in an existing target area.3.Monitor the exchange rates, both official and unofficial to keep updated and use open tenders to ensure large number of potential suppliers and increased likelihood of obtaining the most accurate and best prices in the tenders.4. Apply for permissions well in advance of planned implementation and follow-up continually with relevant line ministries.5. Maintain strict controls according to EPDC Procurement and Finance Policies and CRM in place.
