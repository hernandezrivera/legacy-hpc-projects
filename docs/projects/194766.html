Project Summary

The project aims to contribute to reducing violence, exploitation and abuse of children, the process of protecting children from violations requires raising children & local community awareness, advocating stakeholders, actors with key interventions in this project to further collaborate and pursue new opportunities to cover current gaps in child protection services and to implement a comprehensive community approach and a full range of CCP activities for children, represented in: providing self-protection and life skills for children, ensuring participation Children in protection activities; utilizing of the already existing structures (protection networks) or established (school protection clubs) for providing psychosocial support services, raising awareness and strengthening family support, training people works with children (teachers in basic schools, kindergarten teachers and Sheikhs of Al-Khalawi) and Raising awareness at the level of neighborhoods.
The project targets children (3000, age group from 8-14 years), teachers (150, age group above 20 years) in basic schools, children (400, age group of 8-17 years) and Sheikhs (25, age group above 20 years) Al-Khalawi Quran memorization, teachers (25, age group above 20 years) kindergartens and mothers (960, age group above 20 years) in neighborhoods and host communities – Nyala south locality - East administrative unit
The need to protect children in the city of Nyala is more than in the countryside, due to the quality of the social structure in the countryside, which considers the society as one extended family, and the city is linked to the phenomena of violations more than in the villages for a number of factors, including overcrowding and high population density and other factors that help to cause violations that make It is difficult to address them completely and reduce their occurrence.

1 - Needs

This project addresses the issues of protecting children from the dangers of violations in the state of South Darfur, in the city of Nyala, the administrative unit, east, in a residential environment surrounded by displacement camps, host communities and adjacent villages, where illiteracy and poverty prevail, and this is closely related to children facing a set of challenges related to protection from violations, child labor Exploitation, violence. In such an environment, children are deprived of the necessary knowledge and life skills that they desperately need in order to protect and protect themselves from all forms of violations surrounding them. Children also need to build self-capacity, enhance confidence, and protect themselves from violence in order to survive in the future.To respond to the above-mentioned situation, it is necessary to enhance the security and protection of children as a result of their exposure to those risks that affect their health and psychological condition, with an emphasis on not being repeatedly exposed to them.Child protection requires concerted efforts between institutions working in protecting children and working with local communities that need education for a life free of child abuse. The consequences of these violations on the individual, family and society are very serious due to their recurrence and development from time to time, and thus require concerted efforts by all to reduce them. The process of protecting children from violations requires raising children’s awareness, educating the local community, advocating for the authorities responsible for protection, providing psychological and social support, and enhancing participation through empowering and building children’s capacities and providing them with the necessary life skills to maintain security, safety and life.

2 - Activities/Outputs

Outputs and activities:Output 1: The knowledge and skills of basic school teachers, kindergarten teachers, Sheikhs of Khalawi, parents and communities have improved to contribute to providing adequate protection in a safe and protective school environment:1. Activating or creating a community protection mechanism for children - converting 8 PTAs into protection networks in basic schools.2. Training of 125 (kindergarten teachers, Khalawi sheikhs and basic school teachers) on protection, violence, exploitation and abuse and psychosocial support.3. Implementation of 5 awareness campaigns to support community participation to combat FGM, protection and child marriage.Output 2: The level of children's participation in protection activities improved and positive attitudes and practices were strengthened at the local community level.1. Establishment of 8 school clubs for protection (self-protection, life skills, psychosocial support, drama and competitive sports activities).2. Organizing 48 awareness sessions for mothers on protection, violence, exploitation and abuse.3. Organizing advocacy meetings with stakeholders and the local community.
