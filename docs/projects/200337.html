Project Summary

The project aims to ensure that LHIF continues as an independent coordinating body for International NGOs operating in Lebanon, facilitating the work of its members to address the needs of the most vulnerable through response coordination and leadership, collective advocacy and operational support.
Through this project, LHIF will ensure that it is:
• Representing LHIF members with a common position in response leadership and facilitating improved representation of NGOs throughout response leadership, planning and coordination mechanisms.
• Facilitating coordination, information sharing and agreement on common positions amongst LHIF members to ensure humanitarian operational space is maintained.
• Working together with LNNGO platforms to develop and deliver against the broad localisation agenda through strengthening collective work across advocacy, operations and coordination; and
• Delivering LHIF advocacy interventions around issues of common interest, developing common positions and strategies/approaches to advocate for policy and practice changes necessary to improve the quality and relevance of the response in Lebanon.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

LHIF will maintain strategic representation as the INGO coordination entity in relevant humanitarian leadership bodies as well as engaging in working level groups in support of the Humanitarian Country Team. LHIF will closely coordinate with LNNGO coordination entities to ensure a collective NGO voice, where possible, on coordination and advocacy issues across the response. LHIF will also continue to coordinate with the donor community and the Government of Lebanon on key issues of concern. To ensure effective representation of the full LHIF membership, LHIF will consult with members to inform input into strategic bodies and will ensure timely feedback. LHIF will support various technical working groups, including finance, HR, and advocacy, as well as localization work streams and ad hoc working groups upon request. LHIF holds a monthly plenary meeting at Country Director level and supports four regional INGO coordination groups across the country.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

While LHIF is not an operational organization, it will continue to support its members through sharing resources, best practices and training opportunities, to ensure its members have the tools necessary to integrate protection, gender, and social inclusion into their activities and prevent sexual exploitation and abuse. LHIF’s membership criteria also requires all new members to become PSEA network members.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

While LHIF is not an operational organization, it will continue to support its members through sharing resources, best practices and training opportunities, to ensure its members prioritize accountability for affected populations, including through active participation in the HCT AAP workstream.
