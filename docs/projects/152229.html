Needs

Since Boko Haram established its control over Mafa, formal schooling was banned and schools were closed and school facilities could resume their activities only in September 2016, after Nigerian army recaptured the town. Children lost almost 3 years of education. Currently, the schools are partially occupied by IDPs, many of the teachers active in the town prior to the crisis still did not return, most of school materials have been lost, destroyed in a recent fire outbreak and still not replaced, and Tdh estimates the children daily enrolment rate of children from 4 to 17 as low as 18%.

Activities

The project has at its heart an interconnectedness between education, child protection and psychosocial support addressed in an integrated, mutually-beneficial manner to effectively and efficiently address the multiple needs of conflict -affected school-aged boys and girls.Through the project, children will have access to improved education services. Schools and teachers will have their capacity strengthened resulting in improved school accommodation capacity, increased number of education staff and quality education opportunities through distribution of teaching material and provision of training in health, PSS and CP.The project is in a full alignment with the Nigeria Education in Emergencies Working Group 2018 Response Framework responding to all three objectives identified at the national and regional levels, mainly:O1: Increase education access to school-aged boys and girls through the provision of immediate protective, inclusive and learning environments offering quality education with a focus on areas recently retaken by the GovernmentO2: improve the ability of school-aged children, teachers and education personnel to cope with the psycho-social and environmental impact of the conflict and rebuild individual and communal psychosocial well-beingO3: Support and enhance the community mechanisms for resilience in the restoration of basic education services which provide formal and alternative education pathways for school-aged conflict-affected girls and boys. Activities include;- Construction/rehabilitation of 10 classrooms- Recruitment, training, supervision and incentivisation of 10 new volunteers- Distribution of learning and teaching material- CPMS, Hygiene and health trainings- Education and Child protection promotion campaign- Provision of PSS support to children and adolescents through establishment and support of Child Clubs and Youth Clubs
