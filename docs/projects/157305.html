Project Summary

Under UNDP initiative of "Enhancing Conducive Environment for Returnees and Host Community Members in Um Dukhum Locality", IRDS constructed five (5) women and youth Community Centers (CCs). IRDS also organized the communities and established five (5) Community Based Organizations (CBOs) and provided psych-social support to the women addressing social ills such as; adolescent girls marriages, young wifes abandonment, young girls education ban etc.
In consultation with the communities as a whole and women in particular, during the past 18 months, IRDS identified real social causes inhibiting the recovery of women, and confirmed with the communities the need for the creation of community led safety and security networks (SSNs) for women. IRDS would like to propose a nine (9) months project, aiming to establish and strengthen women and youth support groups in the five targeted communities and link them together to form support networks to champion the efforts of creating social change to rid the communities of social illnesses and provide protection to all group members. The project will train women groups in how to pool resources and act as a unit in formulating and arguing their cases to their community elders and leaders, the women groups will propose common solutions to their issues and advocate for the implementation of these solutions. The project will also create support groups to cater for the needs of young mothers in providing childcare and child protection environments / spaces, for all community members to benefit from. The project will also construct fences around the five (5) Community Centers (CCs), constructed by IRDS with support from UNDP, for child protection and women security. In addition to the fences IRDS will install two solar systems in Abujaradil and Surey CCs.
The project will also establish stitching for dignity groups and provide them with materials to enable them to provide female and babies sanitary kits, in their respective communities, at nominal costs.
The project will target women in five communities where IRDS is present and have constructed a women and youth Community Centers, namely in; Abujaradil, Beltebi, Magen, Marrari and Surey. The project will focus in addressing all social illnesses and issues facing women in general and young women and adolescent girls in particular. Some of the main identified issues are; Girls Early Marriages, young mothers childcare, widows and women headed household.
This project will complement the work of UNDP and operationalize the five (5) constructed CCs to provide critical support services for vulnerable women to enable them to realize their early recovery and furnish the path for durable solutions in all five targeted communities.

Needs

IRDS has been working in the five targeted villages under UNDP RRR initiative, IRDS constructed five (5) community centers for the provision of safe and secure environment for women and youth to socialize and use for recreational activities. IRDS conducts regular perception surveys on community security and focus group discussions with women regarding their wellness and challenges. IRDS identified, from the perception surveys and focused group discussions, the need for the creation of women social protection groups to address women issues and social illnesses dictated by tradition and poor economic conditions. As it stands, women feel alone within their household and family setting, they are unable to push-back when their male partners and family members make live changing decisions on their behalves, they tend to accept, as their fates, so they are not singled out and regarded as renegades or rebellions. IRDS also found that in groups women can defend their peers and reverse some decisions however, this is seldom done as the opportunities, and the community's setup does not lend itself to the women to practice such solidarity. Also it is found that there is minimal interaction when it comes to what is considered private household matter, such as domestic violence or missing husbands who been away for years. The women said they will confide in one another and raise their issues for resolution if they are sure the matter will not be returned to them individually. IRDS found that at least one of every two women (50%) have serious issues such as; divorced with no child support, abandoned for more than two years and no contact with husbands, husband married a younger wife and neglected her etc, but all women do not have ways to resolve or speak about their issues. At least 60% of the women said they would like to have a women only forum where they can voice their concerns in confidence to other women and get a resolution. All women with children under two (<2) and no family support said they will utilize a safe baby-siting service and are willing to pay a nominal fee for the services if they managed to have a good paying job or profitable micro-enterprise "Micro-business". All women participants in the community thanked IRDS for constructing the CCs and requested a play-grounds for the children and a periphery wall or fence to protect the CC and its users. The women also requested the installation of lights to expand the hours and operate an electric food mixer. There were additional request made for the establishment of micro-enterprises such as bread bakery and cheese making equipment. IRDS will convey these type of requests to other organizations working on the food security and livelihoods "FSL" sector.
