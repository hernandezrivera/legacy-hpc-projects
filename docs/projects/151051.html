Project Summary

The main purpose of this project is to access primary schools in Lower Shabelle region particularly Merka, Qoryooley, Kurtunwarey, Wanlaweyn and Awdhegle districts, the objective is to enable vulnerable schools aged children and youth with immediate access of learning opportunities, support learners retention, improvement of learning environment and strengthening and improvement of quality of effective service delivery. In addition more focus will be paid on system strengthening to ensure sustainability and enhancing education capacity particularly with a focus on strengthening education in emergencies preparedness, response planning of the education system, AYUUB will get advantage on the new liberated areas and provide free access to primary schools, through this AYUUB will collaborate with the Ministry of Education of South West State.
The community is the primary source, the owners of the schools, they will co-lead the implementation of the program, the CECs will be trained on the management of the schools, water management and school feeding administration.
Special care and priority will be given the vulnerable and marginalized girls and boys for the internally displaced persons and food insecure communities in Merka, Qoryooley, Kurtunwarey, Wanlaweyn and Awdhegle districts in Lower Shabelle region.
AYUUB will target 3,500 persons (1600 fem) 80 teachers(30 fem), 120 CEC members (60 female) by providing incentives & quality trainings to teachers & CECs, provide emergency incentives for teachers, establish new TLS & ensure learners have access to learning materials & provide safe drinking water as well as school feeding in Merka, Qoryooley, Kurtunwarey, Awdhegle & Wanlaweyn districts.
AYUUB, being also the Education leading cluster in the region will work closely with the line ministries and Education cluster for a coordinated integrated response approach, AYUUB will continue to work closely with WASH, protection, Food Security and Nutrition to expand the provision of services to children in schools. This will ensure and improve the teaching quality and retention of learners.

Needs

Somalia Education cluster (Education HNO) states that 1.8 million school aged children in need of education interventions, Lower Shabelle is the 2nd populated region in Somali after Benadir region, AYUUB estimated that more than 700,000 children are out of school in the region. These children are affected mainly by conflicts & droughts.Merka, Qoryooley, Kurtunwarey, Awdhegle & Wanlaweyn districts are the most vulnerable districts due to the clan conflicts & repeated droughts leading to an influx of IDPs who lack essential services of Education, Protection, Shelter, WASH, Health & Nutrition. Education is one the most affected sector because most of the children remain out of school & enrollment figures are the lowest in most districts. Most of the schools remain closed hence depriving children access to learning opportunities. This exposes young boys & girls to child labor to help their families in getting the daily subsidies, recruitment to armed militia groups & early or forced marriages. The 40 schools run by AYUUB & few others are operational because the teachers are working on voluntary basis, hence susceptible to leave the schools & seek employment elsewhere for better payment & this has a negative impact on education.A multi-sectoral assessment conducted by AYUUB NGO (Sep 2018) in Lower Shabelle region shows that main gaps are Inadequate classrooms for pupils, lack of teacher incentives & motivation, lack of capacity building training for the teachers & CECs, Lack of pure water & WASH facilities and services, poor collaboration with the MoE, in order to intervene deeply AYUUB will target 3,500 persons (1600 fem) 80 teachers(30 fem), 120 CEC members (60 female) by providing incentives & quality trainings to teachers & CECs, provide emergency incentives for teachers, establish new TLS & ensure learners have access to learning materials & provide safe drinking water as well as school feeding in Merka, Qoryooley, Kurtunwarey, Awdhegle & Wanlaweyn districts.

The ministry of Education has produced its ESSP 2018-2020 that guides the sector in long term goals while linking the same to the MDG and united Annual work plans.For AYUUB’s policy in terms of sustainability and resilience is to build and fully equip the CEC which will be durable solution for the schools, the Community is the ownership of the schools, they will run directly and look after the implementation of each project, AYUUB has practical experiences on this matter (imagine the primary schools opened AYUUB in 1993 are still functional, thanks to the community and CECs), AYUUB will seek collaboration with the ministry of Education both Federal and state level and focus will be paid on system strengthening to ensure sustainability and enhancing education capacity particularly with focus on strengthening education in emergencies preparedness, response planning of the education system. Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

Women and girls are discriminated or prioritized on issues of social economic development, this has made more discouraged the exclusion of certain activities, more worse girls are abused and violated in different occasions by different actors. In Somali, women are rarely represented in political movement. In some occasions many are forced to move from their communities and some also move pre-emptively for fear of violence being deliberately imposed on them.Schools serves as a safe space for children and is critical to ensuring that children displaced by or affected by drought and con ict do not only have access to education but are better able to cope, survive and recover from the crisis impacts and contribute to longer term peaceful development of Somalia.AYUUB organization has operational manual protecting gender right, all staff will be signed code of conduct, at the sametime AYUUB will ensure all people for all ages and gender are involved in all project process, girls and women will be prioritized considering the circumstance they posses.

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

AYUUB has a risk and Mitigation Policy in place that will be applied during Project Implementation.The area where the project is planned to deliver the services is full control of SFG, there is no other actors can create armed clash, there can be small intra- clashes between SNA forces, but this will not be target to the beneficiaries of the project and AYUUB in applying its Risk assessment and mitigation policy will do mobilization to both beneficiaries and project staff in the respective area when need calls.
