Integrated response for inclusive and fair access to quality protection and education for children, in urban cities hosting internally displaced people (IDP).

Start Date

01/01/2024

End Date

31/12/2024

Project Summary

The project’s main objective is to ensure that at least 236,880 children and adolescents, that are affected by the armed conflict and displacements, will have access to formal and non-formal education opportunities, psychosocial support, and vocational training through an integrated and comprehensive set of activities designed to build learning skills quickly among at-risk communities in five regions of Burkina Faso. The components of the project are based on three outputs:
Output 1: 500 schools in cities and their peripheries hosting IDPs in the regions of Sahel, Centre-Nord, Est, Nord, and Boucle du Mouhoun are introduced to or reinforced in the Quality Child Friendly School approach
Output 2: Increase to at least 34,380 the number of out-of-school children aged 3-18 years old, including girls and children with disabilities in five regions, who have access to inclusive and equal access to quality education (demand and provision)
