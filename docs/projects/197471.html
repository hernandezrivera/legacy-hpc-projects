Project Summary

The main project objective is to assist 15,540 people IPDPs affected by violence in Cabo Delgado in Mueda and Chiure district through food assistance and the implementation of Livelihood Restoration activities which include promotion of family vegetable gardens and food productiuoin.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Dorcas intends to partner to provide food assistance to 15,540 (60% women) IDPs households families in Mueda and Chiüre District over six month and implement asset creation activities with the involvement of the beneficiaries and local authorities. Dorcas is targeting Mueda and Chiúre Districts which has one of the largest number of IDPs in Cabo Delgado.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The key activities are: Activity 1: Distribution of basic emergency food packages to 15,540 IDPs in both districts (Chiure and Mueda); Activity 2: Distribution of basic production seed kits and promotion of farming activities, to help IDS to produce their basic food to daily diet and market; Activity 3: Training, capacity being to IDP farmers on proper use of the seeds including providing knowledge on farming management and linkage no market.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Through direct face-to-face meetings with project staff and volunteers (steering committee members and sub-units), in writing, and also virtually (directly or through steering committee members). The steering committee will consider and choose from such alternative complaint handling methods as mobile hotline, complaint box, and posters (with information how to fill out a complaint).

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

Vulnerability criteria will be applied to identify and select the most vulnerable and needy beneficiaries and avoid inclusion and/or exclusion errors. A participatory process will be applied involving community leaders and local government authorities. The final master beneficiary list will be endorsed by community leaders. Host community members will be explained the benefits of the programme and especially the interventions that extend benefits to the community at large in Livelihood Restoration and mainstreamed interventions

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The preferences of the target population, that includes disabilities, will be adequately considered and decisions concerning which methods/channels to use will be jointly decided, considering international standards and safety issues. The targeted IDPs are all poor and include vulnerable women, children, the elderly and people living with disability

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project will be implemented in such way that will not harm the environment. All staff and partners of this project will be mobilized to respect and fulfill local culture and protect environment. The type of tolls and seeds for farming are very familiar to the target groups and all will be environmental friendly.
