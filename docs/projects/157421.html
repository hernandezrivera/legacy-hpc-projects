Project Summary

IRDS is pleased to present this 12 month project entitled;Nutritional Support System to Improve Mother and Child health (NSSMAH) in Ad Deain locality. The project goal is to reduce malnutrition among pregnant and lactating women and children less than two years of age, by establishing a locally managed and operated supplemental nutritional assistance program (SNAP). The project will collaborate and support the efforts of the Mother and Child Health (M&CH) center in Ad Deain town.
Ad Deain locality/town is the capital of the newly formed state of East Darfur with a total population of 400,264 . The project will serve the most vulnerable population living in IDP settlement as indicated below;

Taking into account the nascent stage of the formation of East Darfur State (EDS), this project is designed to support the establishment or strengthening of multiple local initiatives leading to the formation of a supplemental nutritional assistance program “network”to provide continued and sustainable services to the most vulnerable families in Ad Deain locality. The project identified four (4) local health centers, M&CHC (Main), Al Neem(a), Al Neem(b) and Khor Omar,which areall well positioned, in the project target area andare providing health services to the targeted population. The project will provide refresher training to improve these centers staffs’ skills in diagnosing and referring malnourished pregnant women, lactating mothers and their children less than two years of age, and provide necessary tools to for the selected centers to perform this duty. The project will develop comprehensive referral system to identified beneficiaries to receive supplementary feeding services at one of the food processing centers.
The project will support selection of 116 participating women and engage 100 of them in the establishment of one central FARM for food production. The remaining 16 women participants will be engaged in the establishment of four community women owned food processing centers (FPCs) to ensure nutritionally balanced meals are prepared and provided to eligible program beneficiaries at specified daily time schedules.The FPCs will be supplied with meals ingredients purchased from the local market for the first six (6) months of the program;this will be phased out gradually for most items once the central FARM starts production.Excess food will be used to prepare regular meals to be sold to the general public in a commercial “restaurant type” settings to generate income to the participating women “workers” and sustainability of the program

Needs

Malnutrition among young children in Darfur is only one aspect of a broader and long-standing challenge in Sudan. Each year, hundreds of thousands of Sudanese children suffer from severe acute malnutrition . Yet interventions that save lives reach only a fraction of that number. At 16.4%, the Global Acute Malnutrition rates among under-five children in Sudan are above global emergency levels . Although the Mother and Child Health Center (M&CHC) is providing vital support to the pregnant women and lactating mothers and their children less than 2 years of age, the M&CH center lacks the resources to identify all needy women within its jurisdictions, it also lack the resources to provide the services and treatment to its current beneficiaries. The center receives a nominal annual budget from the State Ministry of Health only sufficient to cover its essential (chapter 1) costs. According to WFP vulnerability analysis and mapping (WFP-VAM) 25.9% of households in Darfur are food insecure, the causes are attributed to four (4) main factors, which are largely conflict related;i. Asset poor householdsii. Female headed householdiii. IDP householdiv. Households experiencing multiple shocksAccordingly the Global Acute Malnutrition Rates which fluctuated seasonally ranged between 10% in February “post harvest season” to 28% in June, while the Severe Acute Malnutrition rates seasonal fluctuation ranged between 1 to 4%. Food availability and pricing are the main drivers.The project idea is to establish a community based, women for women, technically supported, and economically viable, sustainable comprehensive supplementary feeding services to eligible beneficiaries.In Ad Deain, EDS a Mother and Child Health Center is created to address malnutrition and other mother and child health issues. The M&CHC center was established by the Federal Ministry of Health (MoH) in the mid 1970’s,to provide targeted mother and child health services such as; Screening of pregnant women including (blood pressure, urine and stool tests, tetanus) Providing supplemental vitamins and fefol tablets to pregnant women Registration of newborns,  Vaccination and child growth follow-up services. In 1994 a midwifery school and a unit of reproductive health was added to the center. To date the unit trained; Five (5) groups of traditional midwifes, 20 women per group Three (3) groups of senior-nurses, 8 nurses per group Six (6) groups of registered professional midwife. 5 participants per group Additionally the M&CH center organizes pre-birth sessions (for 8 weeks, twice a week)for an average of (70) pregnant woman. For 2013 the center provided basic care service to 2,152 beneficiaries, assisted 345 women with child birth and provide reproductive health sessions to 150 women.

Activities

IRDS developed this project to assist the M&CHC of Ad Deain. The project will add to the center’s capability and enable it to;i. Capture malnourished pregnant and lactating women and children less than 2 years, from within the most vulnerable population of IDP settlements in Ad Deain localityii. Increase food production and distribution in the project areaiii. Ensure consumption balanced nutritional meals by eligible beneficiariesSpecifically the project will support the establishment of;- Collection and processing of proper and comprehensive mother and child nutritional / health records in the target area by;- Identify and select four local health facilities - Coordinate with all health facilities in the project area and sensitize their responsible staff in detecting undernourishment in pregnant women, lactating mother and their children (< 2) years old.-Refer all detected cases to one of the four local health facility-Provide refresher training to medical staff in the selected heath facilities and provide equipment to enable them to identify malnutrition cases and intruding them to the program-Development of comprehensive identification and referral system that detects all malnourished pregnant and lactating women and children < 2 years of age and refer them to the nearest SNAP center-Establishment of the Supplemental Nutrition Assistant Program (SNAP), this will be achieved by; -Selection of beneficiariesDevelopment of a selection criteria for the participating women in direct collaboration with the M&CH center, IDP community leaders and IRD program staffInformation session to all potential participating women regarding the program activities and the available jobs opportunitiesScreening of interested women applicants regarding skills, social conditions, willingness and ability to participateSelection of participantsTraining of participants and development of SoW, standard rules and procedures - Establishment of food processing centers
