Project Summary

The project is aimed at responding to impact of the 2016/2017 drought affected persons who are still in desperate situation in need of food security through provision of food through vouchers to the most vulnerable 25302- (4217HH)- (men 8097, women 12145, boys 2024 ,girls 3036) IDPs and the host communities in Nimcaturabi, Gunsore, malble and doorawera IDP camps in Daynille, Banadir District. Currently, Banadir District has 456000 people who are stressed (IPC 2), 189,000 in crisis (IPC3), 30,000 emergency (IPC 4) and 5000 (Mogadishu idp camps) in catastrophe (IPC 5). By providing food through vouchers from selected vendors who will undergo a vetting process, the beneficiaries will be able to have immediate access to food. Included will be returnees and IDPs living in deplorable conditions with severe food insecurity and completely eroded purchasing powers. The most vulnerable men, women, boys ,girls and the elderly will be prioritized during the beneficiary selection process. There will be transparency with the affected populations by providing them with accessible and timely information on selection criteria for targeted assistance, organisational procedures and processes that affect them. For the purposes of security and safety , the location, distance and access routes to the distribution points will be accessible to all members of the community, including the most vulnerable groups. During the distribution sessions, priority will be prearranged to those who cannot stand in line for long periods, such as older persons, persons with disability, unaccompanied children, pregnant and lactating women. Save Somali Women and Children will Introduce special arrangements for persons who have difficulty accessing the distribution point or unable to carry the food item.Save Somali Women and Children will directly implement the activities.

Activities

1. Community sensitisation. Save Somali Women and Children will arrange for a meeting in advance with the IDPs and the host community separately. These sensitisation is meant to enable the target participants to play an effective role in implementation of project activities and enhance community involvement in implementation. It will also create awareness on project strategy, objectives and expected results. This will enable the selected beneficiaries to understand their roles in project implementation2. Beneficiary selection and registration. The proposed project will target the most drought affected vulnerable households in the selected IDP camps and host community as per SSWC and cluster selection criteria. Priority will be given to households with malnourished children, Female Headed HH (widowed divorcees, separated and without adequate support from extended family members), Elderly Headed HH with no economically active members of the HH looking after young children (parents have died), Child Headed HH and Socially excluded groups (abandoned women, intellectual/mental disability. Vendors will be selected from within the selected target areas. 3. Selection of vendors Vendors will be selected in a public and transparent process in which small traders will be encouraged to participate. 4.Printing of vouchers. Vouchers will be printed with features (i.e. watermarks) to make it difficult to reproduce locally. Voucher distributions will take place in the target communities, ensuring that the elderly, pregnant women and women with young children all have easy access to the selected distribution points.5. Distribution of food items.The food items will then be distributed to the selected 25302 (4217HH) newly displaced individuals (men 8097, women 12145, boys 2024 ,girls 3036) beneficiaries. The safety procedures will be followed as explained in the project summery.

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

There is expected risk of conflicts between the host communities and the selected vulnerable beneficiaries from the IDP community. The distribution of food items may trigger tensions between IDPs and host communities and increase risks of exclusion and marginalization, or even trigger violence against IDPs.Save Somali Women and Children will include the vulnerable host community members as part of their beneficiaries and stakeholders in the implementation of the project. The host community will also be emphasised in the sensitization and planning meetings.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

There is a risk of insecurity which may result to Gender Based Violence in the process of accessing distribution points and returning to the IDP camps.Save Somali Women and Children will ensure the location of and access routes to distribution points and services are safe with enough space for orderly distribution. The distribution centres will be central and the distribution will take place in daylight hours giving beneficiaries sufficient time to travel home before nightfall.People living with disabilities may have difficulty accessing the distribution point or unable to carry the food items.Save Somali Women and Children will ensure it will work vendors that have special arrangements for persons who have difficulty accessing the distribution point or unable to carry the food items.
