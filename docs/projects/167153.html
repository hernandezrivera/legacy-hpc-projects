Project Summary

The present project is part of the ARC program which is a multi-year initiative financed by USAID/Food for Peace since September 2016, up to 5,949,880 dollars for the first year, 8,000,000 dollars for the second year, and 8,540,000 dollars for the third round of funding which is being implemented between May 1st 2019 and April 30th 2020 . The project has been supporting food security activities for very poor households in northern Mali and is co-financed by EUTF for an amount of 14,940,000 Euros, through a resilience-building component running from December 2016 to March 2020). ARC is a consortium led by Humanity and Inclusion (HI – lead NGO), in partnership with ACTED, Action Contre la Faim (ACF), Danish Refugee Council (DRC), International Rescue Committee (IRC), Norwegian Refugee Council (NRC) and Solidarités International (SI). All the partners excepted NRC implement cash transfers; in addition, ACTED implements a disaster risk reduction component across the program while NRC manages activities related to civil documentation and land legal documentation. The project’s general objective is to contribute to reinforcing of the resilience of very poor households and communities in northern Mali who are at risk of economic, food and nutritional insecurity. So far, this HI-led project has reached 14,561 very poor households in 17 communes of Timbuktu, Gao, Mopti and Menaka regions through unconditional cash distributions and/or livelihood support measures.
