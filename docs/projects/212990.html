Project Summary

The intervention aims to enhance Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH) services in conflict-affected communities of North-West Syria (NWS) through a comprehensive approach integrated with Early Recovery activities. Key objectives include improving access to clean water by rehabilitating and expanding water networks, maintaining existing water stations and connecting them with solar power systems. Additionally, the project will address sanitation issues by rehabilitating and expanding sewage networks and supporting operational costs. An advanced anaerobic baffled reactor (ABR) wastewater treatment plant will be established, with a solar-powered pumping station to provide water for irrigation to local farmers. solid waste management and awareness campaigns on hygiene practices, water conservation, disease prevention, and cost recovery will be conducted, alongside the distribution of hygiene kits. The intervention targets communities and internally displaced persons (IDPs) in camps in Afrin, Raju, Jandairis, Azaz, Harem, Maaret Tmisrin, subdistricts.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The project seeks to significantly improve the Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH) services in conflict-affected communities of North-West Syria (NWS), integrating comprehensive strategies with Early Recovery initiatives. By addressing critical needs and challenges, the intervention aims to create side to side emergency and sustainable intervention lasting positive impacts on the lives and resilience of vulnerable populations. Here's a detailed overview: Enhancing Water Supply: Access to clean water is fundamental to human well-being. The project focuses on rehabilitating and expanding water networks to ensure reliable access to clean water for communities in NWS. This includes maintaining existing water stations and integrating them with solar power systems for sustainable operation. By bolstering water infrastructure, the project aims to alleviate water access issues and improve overall community health and hygiene standards. Improving Sanitation Infrastructure: Sanitation is essential for preventing the spread of diseases and maintaining public health. The project addresses sanitation challenges by rehabilitating and expanding sewage networks in the target areas. By upgrading infrastructure and supporting operational costs, the project aims to enhance sanitation conditions, reduce environmental health risks, and promote community resilience. Establishing Advanced Wastewater Treatment: Effective wastewater treatment is crucial for environmental conservation and public health. The project includes the establishment of an advanced anaerobic baffled reactor (ABR) wastewater treatment plant. This innovative technology efficiently treats wastewater, reduces pollution, and conserves natural resources. The solar-powered pumping station linked to the ABR facility further supports local farmers with irrigation water, fostering agricultural sustainability and economic resilience. Conducting Awareness Campaigns: Community engagement and awareness play a vital role in promoting hygiene practices, water conservation, and disease prevention. The project conducts comprehensive awareness campaigns targeting communities and internally displaced persons (IDPs) in camps across Afrin, Ma'btali, and Raju subdistricts. Through various communication channels and the distribution of hygiene kits, the campaigns aim to empower individuals with knowledge and skills to adopt healthy behaviors and contribute to the sustainability of WASH services.Solid Waste management include garbage bins distribution and waste collection in IDPs sites. In summary, the project's integrated approach combines infrastructure development, capacity-building, and community mobilization efforts to address the multifaceted challenges faced by conflict-affected populations in NWS.
