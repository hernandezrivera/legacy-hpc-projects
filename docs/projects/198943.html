Project Summary

The proposed action aims to support the affected IDPs and host community with life- saving humanitarian WASH interventions. The action will focus on Mafa of Borno state and Michika, Madagali and Fufure of Adamawa. The response will target gaps in water supply, sanitation and hygiene practices. The WASH activities will improve protection and dignity of vulnerable groups, and mitigate the risk of communicable diseases

1 - Needs

IDPs face critical challenges in accessing WASH services due to overcrowding at water points and latrines, long walking distances to WASH facilities, dilapidated sanitation facilities, and inadequate water sources across several camps. WASH non-food items (NFIs) remain a critical need, with the majority of women and girls lacking access to menstrual hygiene management kits and services. In most IDP camps, access to safe water remains a challenge due to low groundwater capacity/yield which restricts the construction of new boreholes. This has resulted in overcrowding and long queues at the few operational boreholes that exist. While most IDPs in camps have received hygiene awareness messages, the lack of behavioural change calls for continuous, intensive, multilingual, multi-channel hygiene promotion programmes to ensure sustainable awareness of safe hygiene practices. The increased attacks on water systems have disrupted supplies in several LGAs and delayed the rehabilitation or disinfection of water systems. In addition, women and girls have expressed security concerns while accessing water points. (Humanitarian Needs Overview 2022)

2 - Activities

Christian Aid will provide access to either communal or Household latrines, at both out-patients therapeutic sites for nutrition activities and households, depending on the community setting, to provide for safe excreta disposal and promote the affected population’s dignity. Access will be provided through construction, rehabilitation and dislodgement, while all latrines will be fitted doors, locks and lights to mitigate against protection risks. Christian aid is aware that securing suitable land to build additional facilities is a persistent challenge, and for which land agreements would be signed in collaboration with relevant local authorities. To ease latrine cleaning, each representative from the households that will be using the latrines will each receive a latrine cleaning kit and training on latrine use and maintenance. While for solid waste management, garbage bins, as well as environmental sanitation kits, will be provided to communities through the WASH Committees and other community nutrition mobilizers.
