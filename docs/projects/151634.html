Project Summary

The project will provide holistic life-saving and life sustaining nutrition intervention to scale up current nutrition intervention through establishing static and mobile outpatient therapeutic program sites in Badahde,Kismayo and Afmadow district hence provide active community case finding, screening, admission and treatment of severe acute malnutrition cases boys and girl <5 children in the targeted internally displaced person . Southern aid shall enhance referral services to the affective and most complicated cases to the nearest stabilization center in Kismayo and Badadhe town to enable provide it adequate attention to the severely malnourished children in the internally displaced person. Southern aid shall also provide routine immunization, deworming and vitamin A supplementation services to <5 children boys and girls as well as multiple micronutrient to the target beneficiaries. It shall provide integrated management of acute malnutrition and infant and young child feeding program and basic service for nutrition packages training packages to its technical nutrition staff as well as community health workers to enable them deliver effective management of the IMAM programs in the target Internally displaced person in the target district. Southern Aid (SA) will be able to provide integrated management of acute malnutrition training package training packages to its technical nutrition staff as well as community health workers to enable them deliver effective management of the integrated management acute malnutrition programs in the target internally displaced person in the target district .The project will integrated with food security cluster in which Southern aid is also intending to undertake the project in the same area hence improving the life of the vulnerable population both Internally displaced person returnees and drought affected pastoralist in the target area

Needs

Somalia is one of the top ten countries with the highest prevalence of malnutrition in the world, and the third highest in the eastern and southern Africa region, at 17.4 percent Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) amongst children under five years, with 3.2 percent being severely malnourished. Despite the large-scale humanitarian assistance delivered, the Food Security and Nutrition Assessment Unit Somalia - Famine Early Warning Systems Network (FSNAU-FEWSNET) post Gu assessment indicates sustained high-level risk of famine in some parts of Somalia by the end of the year. This is due to a combination of severe food insecurity, high acute malnutrition and high burden of disease. The projected number of children who are, or who will be acutely malnourished, has increased since the beginning of the year to 1.4 million , including over 231,829 (including IDPs) who have or will suffer life-threatening severe acute malnutrition (SAM) over the next one-year period from Sep 2018-Sep 2019. Severely malnourished children are nine times more likely to die of killer diseases such as acute watery diarrhoea (AWD)/cholera and measles. Predictably the drought impact combined with the ongoing conflict is a manifestation of the rising malnutrition outcomes. Recent HMIS data collected from Kismayo and Badhaade Southern aid nutrition facilities for the month of January to March 2018, showing high trends of malnutrition where it has shown GAM rate of 12.9, SAM 2.9 and MUAC of 11.9 among <5 children and MUAC of 17.9 among the PLWs in Kismayo IDPs among under-five children seen at the existing OTPs in Kismayo districts. This increase could also be attributed to the increased outreach activities in the district which attracted more admission with early detection of SAM cases before further deterioration leading to better treatment coverage in the newly accessible sites. Southern aid through facility and outreach OTP program for effective services delivery to vulnerable boys,girls and plws.

Activities

1.Provide therapeutic and supplementary treatment to 4,000 severely acute malnutrition to vulnerable boys and girls in Kismayo IDPs, returnees and Host community as well as Badhaade district.2.Provide treatment to 2,000 moderately malnourished pregnant and lactating women in Kismayo and Badhaade district Lower Juba regions.3.Provide basket supplementary to 2,000 pregnant and lactating mothers and 4,000<2 years boys and girls in Kismayo and Badhaade district.4.Improve project staff capacity through provision of integrated management of acute malnutrition (IMAM) and basic EPI training to 30 staff (20 male and 10 Female).5.Improve referral of severely acute malnutrition for effective an urgent management of severe cases with medical complication targeting vulnerable under five boys and girls in Kismayo and Badhaade district

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

The project targets to intervene in Lower Juba more specifically Kismayo IDps,Badhade and Afmadow district where there is large number of internally displaced person as well as returnees from the boarder Dadaab refugee in Kenya however, this different diversity in the target areas may create conflicts or community tension among the already weak IDPs, returnees and host communities on resources sharing, to mitigate this Southern aid will enable to provide equal and transparent project information and target beneficiaries hence reducing the community tension.Also lack legalize land for constructing permanent health facilities is a big challenges and may create animosity between the various stakeholders, where such issues raise Southern aid will ensure closer coordination and information sharing as well as government guidelines on the best way possible as well engage the community for durable solution.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

Poor beneficiary data dis-aggregated on needs may challenge to enable reach the actual people in needs, to mitigate this Southern aid will strive to conduct pre-intervention assessment as well as provide mobile clinic to the far rural areas with limited access to enable save the life of the vulnerable groups.Location and distance of the health facility may be challenge for the boys,girls and pregnant and lactating women in emergency , SA shall be able to ensure the health facility is closer to the beneficiary to avoid any risk related such as sexual violence/abuse and explosion of war remnant.Unfriendly health facilities infrastructure will discourage the disable and physically challenge members of the community, to mitigate this SA will ensure all health facility are up to date and health cluster standard required ensuring further on gender sensitivity twin latrine hence friendly to all user including boys and girls.

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

Project staff capacity will built on prevention of sexual exploitation among the vulnerable women, girls and boys as well the staff will sign code of conducts, Southern aid will ensure to promote all inclusive consultation and feedback within the project staff as well as the community to avoid any gender base violence among the displaced and returnees. It will also ensure to prioritize the most vulnerable among the target population giving more consideration on women, boys and girls.
