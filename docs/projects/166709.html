Project Summary

Organise trainings for health professionals through MOQI Platform to increase knowledge about prevention, diagnosis and proper treatment of malnutrition; prevention, diagnosis and treatment and follow-up of nutritional deficiencies, such as pellagra, in line with the social distance measurement; strengthen infection prevention and control procedures in nutrition programs, ensuring adequate protection of staff and beneficiaries; provision of home health care to vulnerable groups; adapt guidelines on Infant and Young Child Feeding Practices to incorporate preventive measures against the risk of COVID-19 transmission and protect and promote breastfeeding and adequate complementary feeding.
increase knowledge regarding nutritional education for residents in context of COVID-19; nutritional education and healthy eating habits, risks of poisoning, water pollution and hygiene; production of information, education and communication (IEC) materials : examples such as jobaids and posters for daily use at the health center; and pamphlets, brochures or short stories; distribution of information leaflets through tents to prevent group formations.
