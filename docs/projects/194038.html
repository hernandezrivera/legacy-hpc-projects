Project Summary

Korma Administrative Unit (AU) of El Fasher locality , is West of El Fasher town 79Km
distance. It population 109,241 .It is inhabited by several tribes: Fur, Arabs, Zaghawa,
Tunjor, Barty, etc. The main activities of the people are Agriculture, Trade and Animal
breeding. Korma is one of the most affected areas by Darfur crisis since 2004 and
Continuous insecurity situation. This situation was complicated by the armed conflicts
That took place on 24th of March 2014, 15 villages were attacked, burnt and looted.
People were terrified and displaced in mass populations to the existing IDP camps in El-
Fasher seeking protection, while others rushed to UNAMID Team Site in Korma,
Creating a new IDPs settlement in and around UNAMID Team Site.
According to the Sudan S3M 2 survey (2019), North Darfur state (where this project is
Proposed is one of five states that carry high prevalence of the national burden of SAM
(Second highest after Red Sea, North Darfur, River Nil , Kassala and South Kordofan) with GAM (Weight For Height) prevalence of 22.68% and Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM using MUAC) of 1.39% and 3.28 % WHZ . Within the state El Fasher Locality is one of the four Localities in the state with high SAM burden out of 18 localities. The Nutrition survey (2019 S3M 2) showed El Fasher locality average GAM (Weight For Height) prevalence of16.13% and Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM using MUAC) of 2.95%. These are above the WHO emergency thresholds of 15% and 2% for GAM and SAM respectively. And only21.97% of mothers reported good Infant and Young Child Feeding practices (IYCF) and 78.48% of mothers were exclusively breastfeed their children. Despite the benefit of extended breast feeding beyond 6 months only 73.95% mothers practiced continued breastfeeding. Similarly, sanitation which is one of aggravation factor for child malnutrition reported to be very poor: only 43.04% households reported access to Improved sanitation facility and 10.06 % aware about the five-critical hand washing points (mean). Access to primary health care services in the selected areas is poor. There are 10 PHCs in Korma out of which (6PHCs) don’t provide the minimal essential package of health care Services. With the recent S3M survey in North Darfur state 30.01% of children have had diarrhea at the time of the survey and among them, only 62.54 % of children visited health facility for treatment. Antenatal care utilization during the most recent pregnancy is only 40.19% and similarly postnatal care coverage is 39.49 % for this state. Vaccination coverage for the state is very low, first does measles coverage is (26.96) and full immunization converges is 23.45%. In El Fasher locality measles 1 coverage is 34.61% and full vaccination coverage is 28.96 %. This resulted repeated measles outbreak reported in El Fasher locality which needs essential vaccination services to improve vaccination coverage and protect children from prevent vaccine preventable diseases. The exciting Facilities are lacking of qualified manpower with almost 80% of the existing health staffs are volunteers, insufficient of essential drugs particularly for treatment of under 5 children is insufficient, facilities are not monitored and supervised regularly.
UNICEF has been supporting ZPDO since 2013 to deliver preventive and treatment
Nutrition intervention in Korma (AU) in Korma IDPs. In 2019, ZPDO admitted 2,054 SAM Children for treatment program and supported 12 OTPs. Around 1674 Mothers received Counseling services on infant and young child feeding practices. This current project will implement both health nutrition interventions. Treatment of SAM children in 12 OTPs, provision of IYCF counseling to care givers with 60 Mother support groups. Provision of essential PHCs services by supporting 2PHC facilities in Korma IDP camp and Gobor Alkhanam including health promotion activities and Vaccination services through mobile Program as well as maternal and newborn.
