Project Summary

The Anglophone crisis that has been going on for the past 4 years in Cameroon has caused more harm than good to both North West and South West Region. Over 15,780 individuals from 2,625 households were reportedly displaced in the North West (NW) and South West (SW) regions in June 2019 due to ongoing crisis/violence. Protection continues to be a major humanitarian concern in the NWSW. Civilians continue to be caught in crossfire while summary execution of civilians by both the military and NSAGs is on the rise. Temporary displacements continue to be recorded as civilians flee for safety.
Some have lost the possibility of schooling, parents cannot afford school fees, and girls are exposed to prostitution, early marriages meanwhile young boys exposed to drugs, abduction into NSAGs and other mal-practices. Capacity building training to young boys and girls shall go a long way to enhance a fall in unemployment, increase literacy level and a sense of responsibility to the society as a whole. We intend to carry on this project in Fako and some hard-to-reach areas of Kupe-muanenguba not leaving out Mezam, Boyo and Momo division. This will involve management, business and IT skills to better placed them in the society.
Total target of 1500 with projected budget of 47,900USD inclusive of direct cost, logistics, operational and staff cost. This will last for a period of 4 months beginning June 2020.

1 - Besoins / Needs

In Cameroon, over 15,780 individuals from 2,625 households were reportedly displaced in the North West (NW) and South West (SW) regions in June 2019 due to ongoing crisis/violence. Population in the Northwest and Southwest continue to suffer the severe humanitarian consequences of the socio-political crisis in both regions ongoing since 2016. Parties to the conflict have failed to heed to repeated global calls for a ceasefire as a result of the outbreak of COVID-19. Lives continue to be impacted by the effects of continuous displacement, confrontations between the military and non-state armed groups (NSAGs) as well as the threats posed by the COVID-19 outbreak.Protection continues to be a major humanitarian concern in the NWSW. Civilians continue to be caught in crossfire while summary execution of civilians by both the military and NSAGs is on the rise. Temporary displacements continue to be recorded as civilians flee for safety. In the course of running, many people tend to abandon their daily activities, young boys and girls get involve in similar effect. Some have lost the possibility of schooling, parents cannot afford school fees, and girls are exposed to prostitution, early marriages meanwhile young boys exposed to drugs, abduction into NSAGs and other mal-practices. Apart from the issue generated from the crisis, there is increase unemployment rate in Cameroon, young graduate both male and female have a little or no jobs. It will be of great importance for young boys and girls to have the possibility of undergoing capacity building trainings in order for them to obtain some management, business and IT skills .In return, they will be able to invest in small scale businesses, applying skills obtained and be able to manage their investments successfully. This will reduce the rate of illiteracy, unemployment and give room for some IDPs to regain their businesses and source of income.In order to get the right vulnerable groups, we shall conduct a need assessment at a preliminary stage. Ones data is analyzed, communication department shall equally contact beneficiaries to take part in the training. Areas of concern are Fako,Kupe-muanenguba, Boyo, Mezam and Momo division with total target of 1500 with projected budget 47,900USD. Budget is inclusive of direct cost, logistics, operational and staff cost. This will last for a period of 4 months beginning June 2020.
