Project Summary

The project will be conducting activities for both IYCF and CMAM in three governorate, Al-Hasakeh, Deir-ez-Zor and Ar-Raqqa.
In Deir-ez-Zur Gov.
Covering Bokamal and Al Mayadeen districts, covering four sub districts (Beban , Bseirah , Sur and Hajin ). The actions in Deir ez-Zur will target about 48 communities
In Al-Hasakeh Gov.
Covering Ras Al Ain district, covering two sub districts (Ras A ain and Darbassiah ). The actions in Al-Hasakeh will target about 24 communities
In Ar-Raqqa Gov.
Ar-Raqqa and Al Thawra districts, covering six sub districts (Mansura, Ein Isa, Solouk, Karama, Raqqa and Tel Abyed). The actions in Ar-Raqqa will target about 52 communities
The project will have a wide range of activities that will target children under 5 in addition to pregnant and lactating women, for both the IDPs and host communities,through the project both IYCF and CMAM programs will be implemented, with different activities,like screening, referring and treating malnutrition case, in both children and PLWs, provide IYCF-E counselling, BMS in line with BMS SOP, and dilver IYCF messages through group sessions, in addition to adequate training for providers,
The project will implemented through 3 OTP center, and 6 mobile unites and 20 CHWs team, in the three governorate, with establishing a good referral network between the whole implemented system.
The project will be also strengthened with a continuous coordination with both NES forum and WoS nutrion sector, by attending NES meeting, providing detailed map on activity, sharing and receiving information from other forum partners, presenting the ongoing activities, reporting through 4Ws and all needed tools on a monthly basis for NES and WoS nutrition sector.

Implementation of activities

Through the project, a comprehensive IYCF + CMAM program will be implemented. The CMAM program will be implemented by screening for acute malnourished children under 5 cases by both MUAC and Z-score, through outreach teams in addition to those who will visit the fixed center directly, according to the screening children will be provided by either received nutritious food (fortified food, high energy biscuits and LNS) or micro-nutrient powder, or refered for treatment if it's MAM or SAM cases, in the OTP MAM and SAM cases will be treated and they will be followed up with the help of the outreach teams, SAM cases with complication will be referred to SC centers. Through IYCF program will pregnant and lactating women, to increase the awarness and support them with the optimal feeding practices, through one-to-one counseling, group support sessions, providing BMS if needed in line with BMS OPS, IEC materials distribution on awareness raising on IYCF-E and optimal maternal nutrition for pregnant and lactating women. In addition to screening for malnourished PLWs, and distribute of preventative nutritious food and treatable nutrition supplies for them according to the screening results. The project will be implemented through 6 mobile clinic, 3 OTP centers and 20 CHWs teams, in each governorate there will be 1 OTP center and 2 mobile clinic, and 6 CHWs team except of Der Ezzor there will be 7 mobile teams. Through the project all the recruited staff will have multiple on-site and online training on IYCF and CMAM programs, and will be linked for referral with each other and other facilities that already exist in the region.

Protection Risk Analysis

Different Protection risks are available during through this project it can be summarized with as the following: 1). The risk from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse PSEA which is referred to measures taken to protect vulnerable people from sexual exploitation and abuse by staff and associated personnel. During the course of their work, aid providers come into contact with vulnerable people. Humanitarian workers have access to goods and services that put them in a position of power over the community. Unfortunately, some humanitarian workers have used this imbalance of power to exploit and abuse vulnerable members of the community. 2). Gender-Based Violence GBV: health and nutrition outreach services are often the first and in many occasions the only point of contact for GBV survivors seeking assistance, without necessarily disclose the abuse due to shame or fear. In order to facilitate the required care, and refer the case to the right position, survivors must have safe access to facilities, and it is critical that health & Nutrition teams have the right knowledge to handle the case and deal with it.

Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

The mitigation measures for protection risk are: -Effective community-based complaints mechanisms, including victim assistance.-Awareness-raising on SEA amongst personnel, with continuous training on the topic. -Provide information to communities and people affected by crisis about the organization, the principles it adheres to, how it expects its staff to behave.- adopting a flexible delivery modality with adjustable distribution schedules.- Specialized staff should be trained on providing first-line support, clinical management of sexual assault and rape, identification of GBV cases, and safe referrals.- Identification of safe referral pathways to ensure comprehensive management of GBV cases connecting health services and clinical management with social services, mental health and psychosocial support (MHPSS), in a manner guarantees safety, confidentiality, and privacy of the survivor.- Presence of female staff in outreach teams and fixed facilities increase access of women and girls to health services. If a risk other than protection risk arises like risks beyond the project's control like attacking a facility or team, then we will also have different mitigation measures like: - Set up centers in safe and accessible locations that can be easily reached by beneficiaries.- Relocate nutrition centers in relatively safe areas- Reporting on attacks against healthcare.
