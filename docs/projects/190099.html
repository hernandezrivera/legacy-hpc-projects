Project Summary

The purpose of this project is to protect and sustain core-agricultural livelihoods and enhance vulnerable households’ ability to withstand recurrent and future shocks in Somali region . A total of 15,000HHs across selected Woredas of Somali region will be benefited from this project. A total of 8 woredas are selected. These woredas are prioritized for this project due to the magnitude of draught impact on livelihoods, SCI interventions areas on other projects and less interventions on other organizations . The purpose of the project will be achieved by implementing the following major activity:
Provision of multi-purpose cash assistance (MPCA) to the most vulnerable pastoral households.
MPCA in unconditional direct cash support that gives more flexibility to the HH on how to spend on basic needs. It respects their decision power and dignity. Multipurpose Cash Transfer estimated at $40 per household. The amount of the unconditional MPCA will be calculated to cover the minimum costs to purchase food(50% of the HH food basket), basic WASH items and other HH items and transport to support a family of five. MPCA transfer values be will be calculated based on the Somali region multi-sector MEB developed by the CWG Group.
Beneficiary targeting will be carried out closely with local authorities and communities. Awareness creation will be done with families to avoid diversion of the money on items that may not benefit the family and concentrate on food and non-food items which can contribute for nutritional and HH livelihood improvement. Cash distribution sites will be selected with active community participation at nearby or short walking distances. Elders, PLWs, and PwDs will get priority services and special support will be provided by CVs. SC will work with the bank which have corporate agreement for cash transfer program to improve services and facilitate for the beneficiaries to access cash from nearby points/cashiers. Market assessment will be done before the project implementation.
PDM assessment will be conducted jointly with local government to understand how the process went and address issues. A complaint and feedback mechanism will be established by SC in the target villages, through beneficiaries can confidentially share their concerns and complaints related to the program as well as report on the behavior of our staff. The proposed intervention is environmentally friendly intervention. SC will teach the community not to harm the environment during the program implementation. MPCA do not have any negative impact on the environment rather it will have positive impact to protect the natural resources. If the affected HHs received money for their basic needs, the vulnerable HHs will not damage the natural resources to secure their basic needs,

3.6 - How will your project ensure feedback is collected from the most vulnerable community members using age and gender appropriate, safe and confidential channels?

SC will establish accessible Complaints and Feedback Mechanism (CFM) with multiple community friendly channels that take into account age, ability, and the literacy level. This will include both reactive (hotlines, suggestions box) and proactive (community satisfaction consultations, focus group discussions [FGDs]) channels and posters/advertisements in local languages to ensure that participants understand their entitlements and know how to raise concerns as needed. The CFM channels introduced in project locations will allow targeted communities, including children, women and marginalized people, to safely and confidentially report serious complaints (including child safeguarding concerns, sexual abuse and exploitation concerns and breaches of the code of conduct). SC will manage complaints in a timely, fair and appropriate manner that prioritizes the safety of the complainant and those affected at all stages with confidentiality. SC’s MEAL Team will analyze and present the information from CFM to Senior Management to inform decision making. SC’s MEAL team will work with MEAL and Accountability focal points in areas of operation to close the feedback loop, communicating with communities and children about the actions that have been taken based on their feedback.
