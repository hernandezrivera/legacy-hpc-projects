Project Summary

The project is aimed at ensuring the operation of the “hotline” and conducting the Information Campaign on obtaining recognition certificates of learning attainment for secondary education and admission to the “Donbas-Ukraine” Educational Centers for children/young people from NGCA and “contact line” (CL). The project envisages the continuation of the “hotline” operation in Kyiv together with MoES (in 2018, almost twice as many calls were received through the “Open Policy Fundation” hotline, which has worked for three years with the support of UNICEF and together with the Ministry of Education and Science. In January-September 2018, there were than 11,970 calls - including 1,282 calls from NGCA, CL and 399 from Crimea. The record number of calls, 559, was registered on July 18, 2018) and the opening of the “hotline” in the Department of Education of Luhansk Oblast; the education of consultants and employees of regional, city education ofices, educators from “Donbas- Ukraine” Educational Centers, colleges, vocational schools, from 25 schools, which provide distance education/external studies in the GCA.
The 2019 Information Campaign will be extended at the national and local levels. Posters, banners, flyers, audio clips, step- by-step instructions will be placed on the checkpoints, in the educational institutions in GCA, CL, in the trains of “Ukrzaliznytsya”, on Internet resources and will be broadcasted on the radio, local TV.

Needs

According to the Ukraine Education Cluster (https://tinyurl.com/ya36728n), in NGCA in 2018, 99,000 children and young people (including approximately 49% of girls and 51% of boys) need recognition of learning attainment, since the documents on education they receive in NGCA are not recognaized. In 2019, there will be 120,000 and in 2020 – 141,000 children of such kind. This creates barriers to obtaining a profession and employment outside the NGCA. In order to ensure the equal access for children to education, the simplified entry system to vocational schools and higher education institutions has been implemented for enrollees from NGCA, which was used in 2016-2017 by 2,201 residents from NGCA, CL (in 2016 – 855, in 2017 – 1,346). About 4,000 applied for consultations. In 2018, the number of applications and those who became students has grown – 1,522 people from NGCA, CL. More than 35 Educational Centers operate). The number of persons from NGCA interested in obtaining secondary education in schools, colleges, vocational schools of GCA has increased. As of September 1, 2018, more than 12,000 students from NGCA, CL are studying at schools of GCA, CL, Donetsk, Luhansk oblasts (http://openpolicy.org.ua/donbass-crimea/news/228/). This was facilitated by the Information Campaign of the NGO “Open Policy Foundation”, which was held in 2016 cooperation with MoES under the support of UNICEF. In the framework of the Information Campaign, the operation of the “hotline” was organized (in 2016 - almost 6,000 calls were accepted, they included more than 1,000 appeals from the East of Ukraine and Crimea, in 2017 – respectively 8,000/2,970, within 9 months in 2018 – 11,970/1,681). The information products (audio, anima videos, roadmaps, leaflets etc.) are produced and distributed, they are placed on oficial websites of MoES and the Regional State Administration, Dostupna Osvita (https://dostupnaosvita.com.ua/), in “Ukrzaliznytsia”, chekpoints, as well as on radio and television channels, in city transport in the GCA, CL. The dissemination of information on educational opportunities should be provided at the national, regional, and local levels during the entire year. The dissemination of information products with the simultaneous education consulting on the “hotline” will enable children and young people on equal access and wider recognition of learning attainment and continue their studies at vocational schools and universities in the Donetsk and Luhansk oblasts in GCA. However, the impoverishment of the inhabitants leads to the lack of funds to finance the transfer and other expenses. Therefore, according to the Ukraine Education Cluster, only 4% of enrollees from NGCA enter the vocational schools and higher education institutions via the simplified procedure through the “Donbas-Ukraine” Educational Centers. According to the calls to the hotline of the Open Policy Foundation, there is a lack of information in the NGCA regarding the opportunities of receiving education for free. In addition, the simplified entry system is constantly being improved, new regulatory documents are being adopted to remove barriers to equal access to education for children and young people from NGCA. In 2018-2019, 15 new schools that provide distance education/external studies in GCA were added. The optimization, reducing of the number of vocational schools in the region take place. This necessitates the provision of additional clarification and information support. There is a lack of information in local communities about the mechanism of implementing a simplified procedure for obtaining recognition certificates on education and entry to vocational schools. Therefore, it is extremely important to teach educators of new institutions with distance education and vocational schools regarding regulatory and legal framework for access to education for children, youth from NGCA, CL.

Activities & outcomes

• work of the “hotline” in Kyiv cooperation with MoES and in Severodonetsk together with the Department of Education and Science of Luhansk Oblast State Administration on education for сhildren, youth, and their parents from NGCA, CL. Result: 12,000 appeals (calls) to the “hotline”.• conducting in cooperation with MoES 3 webinars for 250 representatives of the “Donbas-Ukraine” Educational Centers, vocational schools, schools in GCA, CL. Result: 250 educators have been trained.• create and distribution of 2 types of posters and leaflets, 1 banner (on the 5 checkpoints), 1 anima and 2 audio clips on equal access to education for children, youth, their parents from NGCA and CL. Result: 10,000 students and about 20,000 of their parents, relatives, acquaintances from NGCA, CL have learned about educational opportunities and simplified entry system.• holding 2 press briefings (in Kramatorsk, Severodonetsk) on educational opportunities for schoolchildren and enrollees to vocational schools and higher educational institutions. Result: 30,000 residents from Donetsk and Luhansk oblasts have learned about educational opportunities and simplified procedure because it will be shown on local TV (channel auditory) and published on local media sites, Donetsk and Luhansk Regional State Administration.• holding 2 trainings on the simplified entry procedure and equal educational opportunities for 50 representatives of “Donbas-Ukraine” Educational Centers, vocational schools, schools, ofices and divisions of education of GCA, CL. Result: 50 educators have been trained. • website administration ‘’Dostupna Osvita” https://dostupnaosvita.com.ua. Result: 25,000 site visitors. In the all information materials, social advertising (videos) of the Open Policy Foundation, there is a link to the website ‘’Dostupna Osvita”, where located hotline phones, regulatory documents and free video lessons. The site traffic counter, installed on the site - records about 3-5 thousand visitors in month.
