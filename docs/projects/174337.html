Project Summary

Armed conflict in northeastern Nigeria – Adamawa, Borno and Yobe States –
has driven millions from their homes and uprooted agriculture-based
livelihoods. In worst affected Borno State, poor energy access has exposed
vulnerable people to a number of challenges linked to food insecurity
and malnutrition (e.g. insufficient fuel to cook food), deforestation
(e.g. unsustainable felling of trees for fuel), protection risks (e.g. harassment,
assault, physical and sexual violence when collecting wood fuel) and health
risks (smoke inhalation leading to respiratory illnesses).
The high demand for firewood and charcoal has caused deforestation, which contributed to desertification that is fast approaching the northeast region of Nigeria and has affected the volume of water level in Lake Chad, as well as affected biodiversity.
Also, within the affected conflict population suffers some forms of violence while in search of firewood, or sleeping without a source of lighting in their rooms at night, while some skip meals because of no firewood.
In view of the above challenge, GREENCODE is proposing to implement SAFE project to help improve access to SAFE and reduce some of the actions that contribute to climate change and carbon emission.
The project is aimed at achieving the following objectives
(i) reducing
energy demand by promoting fuel-efficient technologies; (ii) increasing
energy supply through sustainable forestry management practices; and
(iii) promoting safe and sustainable livelihoods.
In achieving this GREENCODE will be procuring and distributing 10,000 fuel-efficient stoves, 3, 000 solar lamps for home, plant 7,000 trees seedlings, as well as mainstream protection and climate change into the project in Borno and Yobe states and will be targeting 25, 000 beneficiaries.

1 - Needs

In mid-2020, GREENCODE conducted a baseline assessment on SAFE in 2 locations (Konduga in Bonro and Fune LGA in Yobe state), the finding shows high demand for firewood and charcoal as over 97% of households assessed depend on firewood and charcoal for their daily domestic cooling, while trade-off other items just get money to buy firewood and charcoal. In the area of household lighting, few depend on kerosine for their lighting at night, while others depend on battery-powered torchlight, while some sleep for days without lighting at night.The assessment shows some protection issues arising from the search for firewood/charcoal, while some on the danger of sleeping or moving within the house at night without any source of lighting.
