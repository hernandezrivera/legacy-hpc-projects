Project Summary

The intervention is to build resilience of the target local government areas (LGA) of Bade,
Nguru and Yunusari in Yobe state, Nigeria. Bene􀂦ciaries will perform task in the
communities of the 3 LGA rehabilitating communal assets and get payment after the work
each month, bene􀂦ciaries with existing small scale business or newly established will be
supported with cash to boost their business. The bene􀂦ciaries will be attached with
master Trainers for skills training and be supported with in-kind start-up tools, 84 VSLA
will be established covering all the 2,100 people on cask for work, small scale business
and skill acquisition. To strengthen the local government system, 25 staff each of the 3
LGAs will be trained to improve on their capacity in the areas of management,
accountability, e􀂨ciency and planning, this will also include topics on GBV, PSHEA and
fraud prevention. To enhance the functionality of the LGA, the project will support each
LGA with 50 chairs, 50 tables, 10 computers set, 1 projector, 3 printers, 250KVA generator,
3 projector screen, 10 pair of air conditioners and 3 mobile communication sets. This
project will be an integrated one, the different sections of the project will be running at the
same time. It will be for 12 months, the cash for work and small scale business project
bene􀂦ciaries will be mobilised to join the Village Savings Loan Association (VSLA), this
will help them to have access to saving and loan facilities. The groups of bene􀂦ciaries in
the VSLA will have access to training on leadership, business development and 􀂦nancial
management. The project target majorly the adult for direct support with a special focus
on 70% female due to their high vulnerability. Households headed by women, widow,
children and people living with disability will be given priority in participants’ selection.
Household with pregnant and lactating women will be given preference in targeting. Plan
has strong mechanism for Accountability to Affected Population (AAP), Plan International
will work with community structure in the project location, this community structure will be
carried along during project planning, implementation and evaluation. Responses from
stakeholders will be received through a dedicated phone line and response will be given
while issues will be resolved as soon as possible, issues that may need referral will be
referred to other partners for support. Plan International has zero tolerance to GBV, there
will be a Gender and Inclusion staff who will ensure prevention of gender discrimination in
all interventions and monitor the prevention of gender based violence that may come up
in the project. All stakeholders will pass through PSEA training, sign the organization
PSEA policy. All contract will have PSEA policy as part of the document to sign. All cases
of SEA are to be reported immediately. All training session will have a short PSEA session
as part of the agenda This project is part of our Plan Emergency Response Plan strategy,
all the interventions will be continued for long impact even after the end of the project
period.

1 - Needs

The project will address the needs on community assets and social facilities which weredestroyed during the con􀂧ict in Yobe state. Some of the infrastructure are in dilapidatedstate, some are completely un-useable, such as roads, bridges, schools, hospitals andtown halls. Nguru and Bade Local Government Areas (LGA) has blocked drainage thatoften lead to 􀂧ood, there is high sanitation and refuse disposal challenges in thecommunities that can be addressed through cash for work. In Yunusari LGA, the localgovernment facility is in pathetic state that need urgent support, the effect of con􀂧ict frominsurgency attack has affected many social infrastructures such as schools building,hospitals and others. Other needs identi􀂦ed in the 3 LGAs are the inadequate skills of theyoung women and men in vocation that will assist in improving their livelihood, mostaffected people are not having gainful source of income to sustain their livelihood. Somehave skills but not enough to help them in competing in the trade and needed to beupgraded with more knowledge. May are using sub-standard tools for their vocation.Those in business have inadequate capital to boost their trade to get enough pro􀂦t. Thebusiness cannot generate enough pro􀂦t for re- investment, many are not having good􀂦nancial management capacity to maintain the business, majority have lost all theirbusiness tools and working materials. The people in the areas are not having access tosaving and loan scheme to help them in their business. They have no collateral for formalbank loans, many are not comfortable to patronize formal 􀂦nancial institutions. The localgovernment systems are having gaps in resources, the human resources are notadequately trained to be highly e􀂨cient in their duty, the female staff are specially are notso much at advantage for training opportunities due to traditional household tasks; theknowledge base for staff to perform at expected level is low and needs to be upgraded.Training on the most important tasks of each staff are not been provided as expected.The local government funding for facility, equipment, furniture, o􀂨ce tools are very poor.Majority of the staff are working with obsolete tools and sub- standard facilities. There isepileptic supply of electricity from the national grid which affects the performance of thelocal government services. There is no high voltage electricity generator for o􀂨ce use,those in use are not well maintained for good output of needed electricity supply. Theo􀂨ce computer for staff use is below standard, there are inadequate personal laptops forstaff use.

2 - Activities

These activities were chosen based on gaps identi􀂦ed in the areas by Plan Internationalduring our 􀂦eld surveys. Plan International has presence in Yobe state and have beenrunning projects in these communities, we are part of the community and we feel the gapsand also have passion to help in 􀂦lling the gaps when possible. During our discussionwith the community, we have reported cases of the sub-standard communal socialfacilities that need to be rehabilitated, there are able body men and women that can befacilitated through cash for work approach to rehabilitate these infrastructures, theopportunity for cash support will also reduce the negative coping strategies, provide cashto meet households basic needs and address other areas of life like health, hygiene andeducation for their children. The unemployed young women and men are many in thesecommunities, some have no skill for gainful engagement in a pro􀂦table business whilesome in business has low capital for re-investment, the project will therefore support withskill development and business management training. The project participants will besupported with necessary tools after the training, the bene􀂦ciaries will be given follow upand facilitated to join the Village and savings association (VSLA). The VSLA groups willbe supported with kits to have successful operations, through the groups, more trainingwill be given to the members, the groups will be supported with seed money to boost thefund for loaning the members, this will support the members business and lead to incomefor sustaining their livelihood. The government at local level has not been able to deliverexpected mandate as the needs arises, this is due to low staff capacity and working tools.The project will focus on the staff capacity development, the women will be targeted morethan men due to past disadvantage position they have been in the past. This project willprovide training on accountability, sexual abuse, fraud prevention and standardmanagement practices. This training will be facilitated by competent trainers who areexperienced in these areas, this will improve the delivery approach of the staff, increasetheir competence and will lead to high outcomes in the community. The local governmentworking tools are not adequate, this project will support the system by strengthening thefacilitie; equipment such as generators, working tables, more comfortable chairs,environmental friendly air conditioners, multi media computers projectors, projectorscreen, personal laptops and communication sets will be provided as support through thisproject. The provision of needed working tools and equipment will help to make the staffto be more e􀂨cient and effective in carrying out their daily work. This will translate tomore development in the target community.
