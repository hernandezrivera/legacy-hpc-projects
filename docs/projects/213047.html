Project Summary

Insan Charity Organization (ICO) will target the most vulnerable IDPs in Jandairis and Maaret Tamsrin sub-districts with winter assistance as follow:
1000 HH will be provided cash assistance with transfer value 180 USD for one time (during December 2024) as per SNFI cluster guidance.
500 HH will be provided with 750kg in-kind heating fuel called "pirine" along with appropriate stove (This will be distributed in three rounds during October, November, December 2024)
Total Beneficiaries 1500 HH (7500 individual's, 3750 males and 3750 females)
All targeted beneficiaries will be in IDPs in camps.
