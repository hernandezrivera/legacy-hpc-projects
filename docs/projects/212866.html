Project Summary

After what Syria witnessed during the harsh years of the scourge of wars, the imposed
siege, and the drought that occurred during the past three years in Al-Hasakah
Governorate, which affected Al-Yarubiyah, its countryside, and the countrysides of Al-
Jawadiyah, Ma’bada, and Jazaa, where these problems had negative effects on the
people and the spread of poverty greatly. The rise in prices and lack of financial resources
led Due to the decrease in spending on health and the frequent increase in drug prices,
more than seventy percent of people are at risk of not being able to cover the required
treatment expenses, such as medications, tests, doctor’s visits, and the high costs of
surgical operations. The spread of poverty, ignorance, and lack of health awareness in
areas reaches Its area is 100 square kilometers and the lack of any center to provide free
health services leads to a society infected with diseases and epidemics, which makes the
most vulnerable groups in society such as children, women, girls, the elderly, and people
with special needs vulnerable to serious health conditions, which causes them
complications and health complications that affect their health. Their lives may lead to
death, as these health conditions are accompanied by food insecurity, as these
aforementioned areas suffer, according to the assessment of the World Food Program,
from unstable food security, as they are rural areas far from city centers and no type of
health care is provided. This has led to a large number of deaths and miscarriages.
Among women and the spread of chronic diseases, all of these conditions require
* Opening a fixed health center in the town of Yarubiyah to provide primary health care for
children, people with special needs, and chronic diseases.
* Establishing three mobile teams in the countryside of Al-Yarubiyah, Ma’bada, Al-
Jawadiyah, and Jaza’a to provide medical services, consultations, health education, and
awareness sessions.
* Performing surgical operations for all specialties through contracting with the National
Hospital in Qamishli and one or two private hospitals.
* Providing medicines to beneficiaries in all fields.
*Holding health education courses in the center and mobile teams.
* Referrals to provide analysis and x-ray services.
* Referrals to specialist doctors for patients who require referral according to the field of
specialization.
