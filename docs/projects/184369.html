Project Summary

The project aims at ensuring equitable access to quality lifesaving curative nutrition services among boys and girls (6-59) months & PLW through systematic identification through screening, treatment of acutely malnourished caseloads and referral for untreated caseloads as well as strengthen preventive nutrition services for vulnerable population groups (marginalized women, girls and children as well as people with disabilities ) focusing on improving nutritional status through provision of supplementary nutrition products, appropriate infant and young child feeding practices in emergency and micronutrient interventions through the most the most appropriate selective feeding and nutrition treatment for 7560 children (3780 boys and 3780girls) aged between 6-59 months and 3541 Pregnant Lactating Women (PLWs) through appropriate selective feeding and nutrition treatment and further increase evidence based BNSP services linked to Nutrition, Health, WASH, Food security and protection in the targeted locations in
Combined effects of poor and erratic Gu 2021 rainfall compounded by impacts of past successive seasons of below normal rains, flooding and the problem of desert locust that has significantly impacted to chronic food insecurity, limited access to safe water and sustained high malnutrition rates among boys and girls aged 6-59 months and PLWs in Galgadud, Lower Shabelle, Middle Shabelle,Bay and Banadir Regions Somalia. Approximately 1.2 million children under the age of five are likely to be acutely malnourished in Somalia including nearly 213400 who are likely to be severely malnourished. (FSNAU-FEWS NET 2021 Post Gu Technical Release of September 9, 2021). Current forecasts indicate an increased likelihood of below-average rainfall during the 2021 Deyr season which will adversely affect food security and nutrition outcomes while current GAM rates are worsening at >15%.
Escalating malnutrition rates is exacerbated by poor breast feeding patterns among pregnant lactating women (PLWs), poor diet diversity and complimentary feeding practices among boys and girls aged 6-59 months as well as limited access to safe water and poor hygiene conditions and practices attributed to frequent cases of water borne diseases that worsen already precarious malnutrition status of Pregnant Lactating Women (PLWs) and boys and girls aged 6-59 months as well as limited access to nutrition and health services coupled impacts of conflict and insecurity in Lower Shabelle and Middle Shabelle and Bay region disrupted trade and productive activities.
The above plight calls for emergency Nutrition services through identification and treatment of acute and moderate malnutrition and preventive therapeutic nutrition services as well as very strong community support for sustained impacts. The project will in addition focus on the needs of malnourished women, girls and and children under the 6 years with disabilities that are affected by common cases of violations and discrimination when accessing humanitarian assistance to enable for their increased protection by ensuring their comprehensive consultation, participation and inclusion in the project planning process as well as in the entire project implementation stages including continuous monitoring to ensure that their needs are fully covered and met by the project.

1. - Projects activities

1. Screening of 7560 children (3780 boys and 3780 girls) aged 6-59 months and 3541 PLWs and treatment through OTP/SFP program and referral of untreated OTP cases to SC at the project targeted locations in Dinsoor and Adan Yabal, Bay and Middle Shabelle Regions, Somalia2. Promotion of optimum IYCF targeting 3541 PLWs in project locations in Dinsoor and Adan Yabal, Bay and Middle Shabelle Regions, Somalia3. Prevention and management of common diseases (anemia, diarrhea, malaria etc) through Vitamin A supplementation, deworming, multiple micronutrients, supplements, iron/folate supplements, and dissemination of hygiene promotion messages and practices to the targeted 7560 children (3780 boys and 3780 girls) aged between 6-59 months and 3541 Pregnant Lactating Women at the project targeted locations in Dinsoor and Adan Yabal, Bay and Middle Shabelle Regions, Somalia4. Training of 80 OTP/SFP staff (40 men and 40 women) as well as volunteers in case detection throughMUAC measurements and weight measures at the project targeted locations in Dinsoor and Adan Yabal, Bay and Middle Shabelle Regions, Somalia5. Training of 60 Community health workers (30 female and 30 male) on nutrition education and to enhance their knowledge in Malnutrition to mitigate increasing malnutrition cases among children aged 6-59 months and PLW at the project targeted locations in Dinsoor and Adan Yabal, Bay and Middle Shabelle Regions, Somalia6. Conducting 12 Community education sessions for 180 community nutrition volunteers (90 female and 90 male) to increase awareness on malnutrition at the project targeted locations in Dinsoor and Adan Yabal, Bay and Middle Shabelle Regions, Somalia
