Project Summary

The project will be implemented in all the five districts as the effects of COVIS-19 are widespread. The project will support approximately 50 235 people from the target districts of Beitbridge, Chimanimani, Chipinge, Chiredzi and Nyanga.
Focus of support to be provided by the International Rescue Committee (IRC) will be on inclusive access to humanitarian assistance for women, girls, boys and men in the targeted districts with COVID-19 prevention and control measures. The IRC will carry out the following activities:
1) Support community focal persons to conduct GBV and COVID-19 awareness raising and sensitization in target communities
2)Train Community focal persons to provide basic psychosocial support to GBV survivors and provide information on available clinical care services, COVID-19 and specialized support services
3) Provide education on sexual reproductive health, awareness and education on GBV and COVID-19 prevention for in and out of schoolgirls.
