Project Summary

The project aims to enhance resilience, empowerment and outreach of vulnerable people and communities facing protection related risks and in need of services under multiple emergencies both natural, disease outbreaks and human made as well as humanitarian crisis in high risk areas of Pakistan. According to Protection Sector HNO 2020-21 the recent COVID-19 crisis has major impact on the protection of multiple vulnerable groups, including refugees, TDPs, people with disabilities; elderly individuals and LGBTI individuals, together with women, children and adolescents in Pakistan. In this scenario the proposed project focusing right based holistic approach through integrated Risk Communication and Community Engagement (RCCE) interventions would contribute to address or mitigate risks, vulnerabilities and protection and prevention related life saving health care and psycho-social support needs of these population categories in project target areas. According to IASC guidelines on RCCE protection lens to RCCE intervention contribute to address and minimize marginalization, exclusion, multiple intersecting forms of discriminations, human right based violations and associated risks against vulnerable groups. The proposed project interventions would focus to minimize and address prevention and protection from Gender based Violence including Sexual violence; abuse; neglect; stigma; multiple intersecting forms of discrimination across diverse factors - including gender, age, disability, migrant or refugee status, nationality, ethnicity, health conditions, geography and socioeconomic status in emergencies; right based violations and barriers to accessibility towards information and services. The services include protection related health care services; psycho-social support services as well as information and referral services. Moreover, the proposed project interventions included rapid protection risk and vulnerability analysis in target humanitarian areas for evidence based RCCE activities ; community based safe home campaigns through the network of community health workers as well as through digital solutions in target areas; Development and dissemination of Risk Communication messages and awareness material ; print, electronic and Social media campaigns with digital solutions; development of RCCE strategic standards and plans based on adapted IASC RCCE guidelines; pilot digital community feedback mechanism on protection related concerns and violations in humanitarian settings to bring accountability in terms of service delivery; information pack including digital version on available protection related health care, psycho-social support and other multi-sectoral socio-economic services in target areas to address accessibility barriers due to lack of information; Interagency coordination and linkages.
