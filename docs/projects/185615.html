Project Summary

The project includes a comprehensive family-based economic empowerment approach for ensuring technical skills development of the young people, with a special focus on girls and young women. After capacity development (short and long term), the young people, girls and young women from the will be supported for enterprise development or job placement. The project included demand specific employment and entrepreneurship strategies considering the sustainability aspects. The target people will also be linked with the Government extension departments, training institutions and other scopes in the private sectors. Inside the camps, the household will be supported for effective self-reliance activities that will prepare them to return back to Myanmar.
GAM: The project will reach to the 10,000 women and 2300 men both in the host and refugee communities. The target people are selected from the bottom layer of the population considering their economical vulnerability and as well as deprived for gender negative stereotypes in the society. The gender transformative approach in the project will contribute for their food security, control over resources, decision making process and finally in the role sharing at households’ level. This strategy will ensure male engagement and diversity also.
In addition, specific needs of women and people with disabilities have been highlighted in the design and linked to the proposed interventions, that will help reduce access barriers for girls, boys, adolescents, women and men awareness raising and sensitization activities that target negative gender-based norms and values in the project area.
