Project Summary

The project aims at an effective reduction of SAM and MAM rates among both IDP, as well as vulnerable host communities in Metuge, Ancuabe, Montepuez and Ibo.
Most vulnerable areas where food supply has shown to be intermittent will be prioritized (such as Ibo island and Messanja locality of Metuge).

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project aims to improve the nutritional situation of people displaced by violence in the province of Cabo Delgado, as well as vulnerable host communities by implementing two types of actions: 1. Facilitate access to treatment services for Acute Malnutrition and micronutrient deficiencies, such as mobile brigades foused in nutrititional screening and treatments, detecting new cases, distribution of supplement feeding to health units and IDP sites/hosting communities, and continuous monitoring in the targeted districts. (limited target)2. Awareness raising activities on improved nutrition to the targeted and at risk populations, by increasing their knowledge and improving their general health status through Nutrition focused hygiene promotion activities. Here, CHWs (APEs and ACS will be trained on topics related to IYCF and HIV to provide one-on-one sessions at household level, cooking demonstrations will be carried out with the aim of demonstrating good practices on the use of local nutritious, cheap and locally available products to at risk families to improve their feeding and that of their children, training on the correct handling of food, or the creation of health committees that among other responsibilities will develop a continuous screening in search of new cases of malnutrition to report to the health agents during the visits. (Higher target), in addition it will broadcast radio spots, novels and other media products in programmes related to IYCF and HIV, in local radios.Likewise, control and prevention measures against covid19 will be implemented in all planned sites

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

1) Screening of malnutrition cases in health units in the project area, as well as other conditions related to poor nutrition.2) Implement active case finding and referral of malnourished children and monitor the nutrition situation through MUAC screenings; 3) Breats feeding promotion and screening.4) Logistic and technical support to mobile brigades linked to the nutrition activities in project areas.5) Trainings on good infant and young child feeding (IYCF) practices.6) Cooking demonstrations and training on correct food handling. 7) Support logistic chain: transport and distribution of supplementation of Vitamin A and deworming, and other nutritional supplements in health units of targeted districs.8) Training of CHWs (APEs and ACS) on topics related to IYCF and HIV to provide one-on-one sessions at household level 9) Communication campaign on nutritional issues around IYCF, food hygiene, breastfeeding and HIV

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

A conflict-sensitivity assessment will be carried out previously to the implementation phase in order to avoid inflicting harm through the action. In order to decrease ground for tensions and conflicts, both, host and IDP community will be participating in the project activites.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

Protection will be mainstreamed throughout the entire project, which itself aims to reduce the exsposure to protection threats that arises from the lack of access to health services. Meaningful access is assured on the basis of a vulnerability assessment among the IDP population, which will be carried out prior to the implementation phase and which will inform the beneficiary selection. Targeting will thus be need-based, not status based and the project will consider the specific needs that arise from different vulnerabilities.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

Effective disability inclusion will be assured through identification of persons with disabilities including different disability status in an exhaustive vulnerability assessment and using the fundamental tools (Washington Group Questions). Persons with disabilities will be prioritized as especially vulnerable group.All project elements will adehere to the standards for physical accessibility.Furthermore, FGD will we conducted with PWD or with their families in order to understand specific needs and barriers in accessing health and hygiene and nutritional services.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project aims too reduce the environmental footprint wherever possible. As far as quality permits, project materials are locally purchased in order to reduce transport and the related emissions and plastic is avoided wherever possible.
