Project Summary

The project aims to improve access to food and help improve social cohesion, support early recovery of vulnerable and food-insecure communities affected by the internal socio-political crisis in the North-West and South-West regions. This project will focus on the rapid deployment of food aid through the distribution of food conditional on participation in activities. And it will address the immediate food needs of vulnerable people (women, men, children), including internally displaced persons (IDPs), returnees and local populations severely affected by the crisis and suffering from food insecurity disrupting economic, agricultural, and pastoral activities in these regions. A total 20,000 beneficiaries (60% women) will be covered, i,e. 10,000 per region. Food distributions will be linked to participation in economic empowerment activities. To implement the activities, Community-Based Participatory Planning (CBPP) sessions will be carried out in collaboration with local community members (women and men), decentralized technical services of key ministries and partners to identify the real needs and priorities of local communities that will help affected populations develop their own response strategies and strengthen their resilience. This people-centred approach ensures the provision of effective solutions with beneficiaries and reduces reliance on relief. Assistance through for FFA, will be implemented using in-kind Food for six (06) months under the FFA-early recovery. The transfer value is calculated based on the identified food gap and is designed to assist beneficiaries to reach the recommended minimum calories intake of 2,100 Kcal per person per day.
WFP will collaborate closely with Government technical ministries, cooperating partners and communities to ensure project implementation and sustainability. Nutrition, Gender and protection issues are taken into consideration at all the stages of the project elaboration, implementation, monitoring and evaluation. Gender and protection risk analysis will be carried out and the most vulnerable will be targeted, including female-headed households, pregnant and lactating women, persons with disabilities, people suffering from chronic diseases, etc. Project sites will be in secure areas and at convenient distance from the homes of the beneficiaries. To support smooth project implementation and ensure sustainability, capacity strengthening sessions will be organized for all stakeholders on gender and protection policy, project management, monitoring and nutrition.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

This project is one of a series of interventions that WFP Cameroon is implementing as part of its Country Strategic Plan (CSP) 2022 - 2026, in collaboration with partners to support the Government of Cameroon in achieving Sustainable Development Goal ODD 2 "Zero Hunger". The project will adopt an approach focused on the implementation of livelihood activities aimed at providing safe access to adequate and nutritious food during and after crises to crisis-affected populations in Cameroon, including refugees, internally displaced persons, returnees and host populations. The aim of these activities is to meet the immediate food needs of vulnerable individuals and households, while helping them to strengthen their livelihoods, reduce the risk of natural disasters, and build resilience to present and future shocks. Interventions will focus on supporting vulnerable households to set up small agricultural and livestock production units, supporting the development of income-generating activities (IGAs) and promoting gender and nutrition-sensitive activities. The assets created will enable households affected by climate shocks, conflict-related displacement, or economic shocks (e.g., COVID or the war in Ukraine) to cope with the disruptions created by these shocks, diversify their livelihoods, and return to their previous or better situation.To ensure the implementation of this intervention, WFP will sign agreements with partners (International and local NGOs) for the implementation of activities on the ground. Food assistance (food distributions or cash transfers) as well as inputs, materials/tools and equipment will be provided to beneficiaries for the proper execution of activities. WFP will support solutions to address bottlenecks to make food systems and value chains more productive and inclusive for women, youth and marginalized populations, as well as more environmentally sustainable and better able to provide healthy and nutritious diets for all, especially in the event of shocks.
