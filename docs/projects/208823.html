3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

Healthcare Foundation Organization (HFO) will ensure that all the persons engaged by the project will adhere to PSEA policy. Therefore, mandatory PSEA related cases will be observed throughout the project implementation cycle. All staff, associates and volunteers should receive regular and refresher PSEA and child safeguarding, do no harm, psychological first aid, and risk assessment. The sexual exploitation and abuse are critical issues facing south Sudanese communities; through this project specific measures and activities will be tailored. Through our child safeguarding policy and other related policies such as PSEA, will be maintained and always will be referred to for guidance. All staff and anyone engaged MUST sign a code of conduct before providing frontline services and engagement with beneficiaries. Through awareness of other services and referrals will all contribute to protection from sexual exploitation and abuse.
