Project Summary

Ensure access to and continuity of essential health/MHPSS services for vulnerable displaced people and host communities, provision of emergency referrals, and support outbreak preparedness and response, including at points of entry. This will be implemented through an integrated community-based approach to affected populations in Cabo Delgado & Nampula provinces.

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

This project will build on IOM’s experience and capacity (since cyclone Kenneth in 2019) providing integrated Health, Protection and MHPSS essential services in four districts of Cabo Delgado (Pemba, Ibo, Chiure and Montepuez) and one district in Nampula (Memba) to IDPs and host communities. IOM mobile teams work through a community-based approach with a network of activists within the target communities.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Objectives 1 & 3: community awareness-raising on health, protection, and mental health, active case finding and referrals of HIV/TB and MH patients for continuity of care. Support outreach health care services including integration of chronic conditions (HIV, TB, and COVID-19 related co-morbidities), in the package of outreach services and effective delivery of quality services responsive to people’s needs. The project will also support effective referrals of patients to next level of care, including emergency medical referrals, which is a major gap in the province, as well as support to treatment initiation/adherence for improved health outcomes. Objective 2: community engagement on COVID-19/communicable diseases, support to uptake of prevention & control measures, support to integrated community-based diseases surveillance; monitoring of disease-related rumors to tailor response (initially COVID-19, the monitoring system will be adjusted and tailored to relevant disease in case of outbreak, e/g/ cholera). At Points of Entry: Systematic risk communication and screening, Procure PPE contingency stock and upgrade border facilities and training for improved preparedness and response to health threats.Under cluster Objective 3, key activities will include provision of community based psychosocial activities with particular attention to the adolescent and young people, direct MHPSS services and referrals of cases, as well as capacity building of humanitarian and Government partners on Mental Health and Psychosocial Support (MHPSS) and Psychological First Aid (PFA) to ensure standards as established by the Inter-Agency Standing Committee (IASC) and the IOM Manual on Community-based Mental Health and Psychosocial Support in Emergencies and Displacement.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

IOM has an integrated protection-MHPSS approach which ensures intersectional considerations of protection and MHPSS needs as well linkages with health programming in a whole-of-community approach. IOM Health Teams who support mobile brigades and COVID-19 response are trained on protection principles. IOM identifies individual and structural-level protection risks by conducting the protection/safety audits in the districts. Also, the individual cases are identified and referred to specialized protection services in order not to cause further harm.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

IOM conducts community based rapid assessments in targeted communities and settlements, to map and assess populations main needs, vulnerabilities, resources, expectations and capacities. This approach ensures that the project activities are tailored and respond to the needs of the communities and that community members can participate in project design and implementation.As part of these assessments, IOM also conducts mapping of available services for MHPSS, Protection and Health essential services, their respective capacities and gaps, to ensure that the project strengthens overall capacity of services providers to ensure continuity and sustainability of services.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

IOM Mobile Teams identify people with special needs including individuals with physical and mental disabilities under the integrated health-protection-MHPSS activities. Also, IOM uses a diverse range of COVID-19 information dissemination materials and channels (including door-to-door, radio, and other community-based channels) to reach individuals with impairments.
