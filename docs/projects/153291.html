Project Summary

Great commission Operation Mercy is a faith-based national non-profit, humanitarian and development organization, operating in the former Unity and Jonglei states of South Sudan. GCOM’s mission is to support peace and development help grass-root communities and vulnerable groups in the society to get equal access to basic needs and develop their resilience against the effects of conflict, illiteracy, diseases and discrimination. GCOM has operated in Unity states since 2017 Education in Emergency interventions and supported lifesaving assistances to persons will special needs and significant vulnerability assisted. In anticipation for longer-term solutions needs to elevate suffering of the IDPs, returnees, and host communities. This project aims to contribute to the South Sudan strategic objectives ‘’ support at-risk communities to promote and sustain their resilience to recurrent shocks’’ that support recovery and resilient of the affected communities to rebuild and mitigate shocks that associate with lack of essential education supports. The targeted area for this project as per recent findings and reports experience influx of the IDPS and returnees to various payams to start a new lives and livelihoods. GCOM is well positioned to implement the project as per the cluster guidelines and procedures. The organization has trained staff and institutional experience which will give strong leadership to the program and avoid the flaws that fail interventions. Through the experience of the previous projects, GCOM has learnt the importance of closely monitoring the adoption of the key education practices and policies to contribute directly into community development. GCOM presence in the targeted communities will increase the likelihood of success of the project because of the knowledge of the region and experience working in the area. This project is aimed to attract and support most vulnerable returnees and host communities with essential education programmes to rebuild resilience and recovery programs.

Needs

Five years’ conflict in South Sudan, the altruistic crisis has continued to intensify and now paused on signed celebrated peace deal, on a costly flight for the country’s population and their viewpoint on the future. The compounding effects of widespread violence and tenacious economic decline have further impacted and diminished the capacity of people to face threats and shocks. The number of people uprooted since the start of the conflict in 2013 has reached more than 4 million, including 1.9 million internally displaced people, with up to 85 per cent estimated to be children and women. Children continue to suffer the effect of conflict and economic pressures. The situation for children has deteriorated over 2018 in Leer, with continued incidents of displacement, recruitment, abuse, exploitation and other grave violations have directly affected children since the beginning of the conflict. 9200 out of 1900 children associated with conflict are estimated to have been recruited by armed actors and destruction of schools and the departure of teachers from many affected areas severely obstructed access to quality education, with over 2 million reported - children out of schools. In Leer counties, most schools are closed due to lack of education support and programmes and insecurity caused by armed conflict in the state. The population around Leer has been adversely affected and the education system is interrupted and many schools are closed, destroyed and looted. Children in Leer County are not attending schools and many teachers displaced seek refuge in safe place rendered schools closed. Girl’s child education in Leer counties also remain a challenge since girls are subject to domestic and cultural practices which does not promote girl child education. In the light of negotiated peace, Great Commission Operation Mercy is expecting to support education system in Leer counties to attract return of the displaced population and children to access education. To ensure disrupted education system restored and the existing challenges and gaps need to be filled so that affected children enrolled and attend schools. Great Commission Operation Mercy would like to assist and help the affected community to re-open and mobilize stakeholders to support children education access. To ensure schools are re-opened and function to increase enrollment and retain of children to schools, Great Commission Operation Mercy through this project seek to work and focus on following areas of interventions to support education programs in Leer Counties; • Community engagement, social mobilization, and advocacy through Back-to-school campaign and awareness. • Establishment of inclusive temporary Learning Space (TLS) and rehabilitation and renovation of re-opened and functional schools. • Provision of EiE suppliers (scholastic materials) to the re-opened and functional supported schools through cluster pipeline.• Training of teachers/PTAs/SMCs/education supervisors for re-opened and functional schools supported.These planned activities will increase enrollment rate of the affected children to schools and strengthen education system in the counties and states levels. Great Commission Operation Mercy will work hand to hand with state and national education clusters to ensure all planned activities are implemented to meet the overall objectives of education cluster target in South Sudan to ‘’improve the quality of education to ensure continuity of relevant education services for children and youth in emergencies’’ and in line with humanitarian specific objectives to ‘’Support at-risk communities to promote and sustain their resilience to recurrent shocks’’. This project will create knotted recovery result-based achievements that directly link to education and psycho-social support programs for the children affected by armed conflict in Unity state.

Activities

• Establish and rehabilitate gender segregated latrines and water points and hygiene promotion, Number of children with access to gender-sensitive WASH facilities (in line with INEE and sphere standards).• Provide emergency-affected children with quality education and recreational material, Number of children and youth (6-18 yrs.) benefitting from teaching-learning, and recreational materials.• Provide trainings to equip teachers with EiE skills that improved teaching and learning experience for crisis-affected children on EiE, basic pedagogy and learner-cantered methodologies.• Provide and undertake training for Educators, community members and authorities on psychosocial support skills.• To equip SMCs/PTAs and education authorities on school management and governance through trainings on school management, monitoring and record keeping.• Establish and rehabilitate temporary learning spaces and classrooms, Number of temporary learning spaces established/rehabilitated in affected areas (in line with INEE - Minimum Standards for Education in Emergencies).• Provision of reports and continuous presentation of the needs of education support to the affected communities.
