Project Summary

Tearfund proposes to support 1,200 individuals from most vulnerable IDPs and host communities in Kala Balge to cope with the devastating economic impact of COVID-19 pandemic. This support will be provided in form of multipurpose cash grant of NGN 4,010 per person per round for a total of 4 rounds. The cash grants will support the beneficiaries to meet their basic needs including food. Community volunteers will create awareness on COVID-19 within the community to reduce spread of the virus. The community will be sensitised on basic prevention hygiene practices such as hand washing with soap and using face masks to prevent community infections.

1 - Needs

Since the first case of Coronavirus was reported in Borno State on 21st April 2020, the number of confirmed cases has since risen to 100 with 14 confirmed deaths as at 4th May 2020 according to the reports from NCDC. In order to reduce community transmission of the virus, the Borno State Government has declared 14 days of total lockdown in the whole state including all citizens staying at home, from 22:30 on Wednesday, 22 April 2020. The order also includes restriction on all public gatherings and full closure of all offices and businesses. Only providers of essential services have been exempted.The total lockdown has come at a heavy cost to the already vulnerable conflicte-affected population that has suffered as a result of high poverty, lack of access to food, nutrition and basic social services. Kala Balge is one of the high-risk LGAs identified by the government given that it is at the border between Nigeria and neighbouring Cameroon, which has a high rate of transmission of Coronavirus. The porous border poses high risk of cross-border movement of people, which is likely to increase transmission of the virus. In addition, the displacement of populations in the LGA because of frequent and vicious Boko Haram attacks has pushed people out of their villages into densely populated camps which poses high risk of faster spread of the virus. In addition, high food insecurity in the LGA as result of lack of access to farmlands poses a big threat to the communities which is likely to lead to negative coping mechanisms such as transactional sex, child labour and child marriages.This project proposes to support 1,200 most vulnerable individuals in Kala Balge with unrestricted multipurpose cash grants to enable them cope with negative impact of COVID-19 including access to food.

2 - Activities

Multipurpose Cash Grant: The project will provide 4 rounds of multipurpose cash grants per individual beneficiary. Each beneficiary will receive NGN 4,010 per round. The disbursement will be done through a Micro-Finance Bank. The frequency of the disbursement will be determined by the frequency of approvals from cash movement received from the Economic and Financial Crimes Commission. Tearfund will implement the project through COCIN Community Development Programme, a local organisation with a working experience in the target region. Tearfund will support the implementing partners to ensure that social distancing and other prevention of infection measures and guidelines are followed during implementation of the project. Monitoring will be conducted through Post Distribution Monitoring to gather feedback from the beneficiaries on project activities and to receive any recommendations on how to improve the project.
