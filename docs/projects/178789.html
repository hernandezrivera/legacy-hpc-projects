Project Summary

Recent reports, including the Humanitarian Response Plan, prioritize the need to scale up interventions to contain the economic decline affecting livelihoods.
Al-Sham Foundation conducted a needs assessment in 6 areas in Idlib countryside and reported the need to create job opportunities, improve livelihoods and support self-reliance among the affected IDPs, especially in the flood-affected camps during the period from January to March 2021, and we designed a project for 8 months In the ERL sector, with the aim of contributing to filling some of the gaps in the camps, where assistance will be provided through ERL intervention through: vocational training, cash for work, followed by an in-kind grant.
The integrated intervention proposed by AHF will consist of the following pillars.
300 IDPs will be selected, and vocational training will be provided to move from dependence on aid to production, and the beneficiaries will be selected according to predetermined selection criteria and from the most vulnerable IDPs in the Idlib countryside camps that were flooded (CCCM Cluster report). The labor market needs according to a detailed need assessment that AHF will undertake in the preparatory phase of the project, the project period is 8 months divided into one month, half a preparatory phase, and 15 days is the stage of final exams and the distribution of attendance certificates. As for the actual training period, it is 6 months. Personal, on-the-job training in various occupations and training topics.
Cash-for-work intervention for 300 beneficiaries who will be linked to the labor market and workshops and will receive cash-for-work support during the on-the-job training period that will last for 6 months.
275 beneficiaries will be provided with an in-kind grant in the form of toolkits (average of $ 600 / set) after completing the training period to help them start their small businesses, pursue their careers, and enter the job market.
The ERL component is linked to the Group 1 objectives and will contribute to enhancing the resilience of the affected communities according to the needs assessment conducted by AHF in the IDP communities, especially those affected by floods in Idlib countryside, the project aims to improve the livelihoods of 300 of the most vulnerable IDPs through vocational training including: This on-the-job training is supported by money-for-work, and an in-kind grant will be provided at the end of the training period to 275 beneficiaries.
AHF has been working in the targeted area for 8 years and will build on its previous experience in implementing similar interventions, including the Karim Microfinance Project that was implemented in Al-Bab City from the beginning of 2019 until now, which provides easy and beneficial - free lending services. 300 beneficiaries and benefited from training in personal skills in accounting and marketing. In addition, AHF implemented the Miraas program to rehabilitate youth and train them in soft skills, where more than 950 trainees were trained in 3 regions (Azaz, Al-Bab, and Afrin).
Accordingly, it is highly recommended to implement the proposed intervention. AHF will take all precautions in accordance with the recommendations of groups related to COVID-19 including avoiding physical contact between employees and beneficiaries when providing assistance, promoting employee hygiene, minimizing personal interactions, monitoring self-protection measures and rational use of personal protective equipment, and using alternative methods such as contacting about distance. And telephone interviews for some monitoring and evaluation activities.
