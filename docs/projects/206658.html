Project Summary

The project, "Psychosocial Support for women/girls, Orphans and Vulnerable children affected by the sociopolitical crisis in the South-West Region of Cameroon," aims to provide comprehensive psychosocial support and protection to individuals affected by the sociopolitical crisis. With a target of 2000 participants distributed across various target beneficiaries, the project intends to address the urgent needs of women, girls, orphans, and vulnerable children by restoring emotional well-being, empowering individuals, and creating a safer and more inclusive environment.
The project will implement a range of activities to address the assessed needs of the target beneficiaries. Trauma counseling and support will be provided to help individuals heal from the emotional trauma caused by violence, displacement, and loss. Safe spaces and support networks will be established to foster empowerment, resilience, and a sense of belonging among women, girls, and children.
To ensure access to quality education and skill development, the project will provide education support through document catch-up classes, scholarships, and vocational training. Concurrently, awareness programs on child rights, protection, and gender equality will be conducted to promote the well-being and development of vulnerable children.
To ensure that the target enjoyed their citizen right, the project will assist in the establishment of civil document like Birth certificate and ID Card.
To tackle gender-based violence, the project will offer medical and psychological support for survivors, conduct awareness campaigns, and provide legal assistance. Dignity kits containing essential hygiene and personal care items will be distributed to promote the well-being and dignity of survivors. Economic empowerment opportunities, such as microfinance initiatives and vocational training, will be provided to help survivors regain independence and rebuild their lives.
The project will also address housing, land, and property issues by advocating for secure HLP rights, providing legal assistance, and conducting sensitization programs. Efforts will be made to facilitate access to interim land for farming for internally displaced persons (IDPs) and ensure their rights in housing and land tenure.
A participatory and community-based approach will be adopted, engaging key stakeholders, community leaders, and local organizations in the planning, implementation, and monitoring of activities. A robust monitoring and evaluation framework will be established to track progress and assess the effectiveness of interventions. The project's sustainability will be ensured through capacity-building activities, collaboration with government agencies, and income-generating initiatives.
By implementing this project, we aim to make a significant impact on the lives of those affected by the crisis, fostering their recovery, development, and overall well-being. Through psychosocial support, protection measures, and empowerment opportunities, we aspire to create a more resilient and inclusive community in the South-West Region of Cameroon

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

1. Emotional Trauma: The project will address the emotional trauma experienced by individuals due to violence, displacement, and loss caused by the crisis. Trauma counseling and support will be provided to help them heal and recover.2. Lack of Support Systems: Many affected individuals lack stable support systems and face isolation and loss of identity. The project will establish safe spaces and support networks to foster empowerment, resilience, and a sense of belonging.3. Education Disruption: The ongoing crisis has disrupted education, limiting opportunities for children's development. The project will provide education support through catch-up classes, scholarships, and vocational training to ensure access to quality education and skill development.4. Civil documentation: Many affected individual don’t have their civil documentation and are unable to establish it. The project will assist to establish civil documentation like ID card and birth certificate.5. Sensitization on GBV issues: For most of the time, women face aggravated GBV issues such physical abuse, rape, loss of children and deprivation of resources. The project will organize sensitization campaign and will also form and train communities relay to better prevent, identify and intervene in suck case before competent authority.
