Project Summary

Garbaharey district, in Gedo Region, continues to face a number of food insecurty related challenges. There are 49,394.32 people in need (PiN) falling within the IPC 3 & 4 according to Food Security Cluster. The eratic weather patterns and repeated crop failures have routinely depleted assets and contributed to lack of sustainable livelihood opportunities effectively affecting the resilience programs.
Food security is an important element in the stability and health of the people in Garbaharey, Gedo Region. Food insecurity has thus been consistered a worrying priority by different actors within the Gedo riverine and pastorals areas. This adds to the insecurity challenges which straddles the district. As such, the project seeks to increase food security for the at risk communities in Garbahare host and IDPs by integrated food-related multipronged interventions including screening, provision of food referrals, and awareness. The project aim is to reduce malnutrition, ameliorate severe effects, or fatalities associated with lack of adequate and nutritious food to the at-risk population especially Pregnant and Lactating Women and their children between 6 - 59 months.
A very important element of the project is building resilience through provision of relevant skills to increases self-reliance, enhance self-sustenance and provide cutionary livelihood activities against shocks. The targeted are the IDPs and those whose assets and sources of livelihood were destroyed affected by prolonged drought and demonstrate susceptibility to food deficiency diseases. Identification is done through standard screening processes and capacity building to PLW to undertake MUAC screening methodologies. Periodic screening, coordination with the local actors including the local administration.

Project Activities

The project will constitute the following milestones;1. Conduct social communication, mobilization and community awareness of the project. 2. Do basic screening to determine the level of food insecurity needs among the targeted at-risk population using standrad screening standards. 3. Routinely provide food vouchers to 3300 HHs with over 50% going to women-headed households. 4. Asses and increase capacity to food production through provision of farm input to 4200 households. 5. Training and capacity building on food-safe storage for 150 households 6. Engage 1000 households through rehabilitation of assets and provision of food. 7. Creation of short-term job opportunities through food for work activities targeting 1,500 people.. 8. Enhance resilience by provision of marketable vocational skills to 50 youths. 9. Provide basic awareness and capacity building concerning the Coronavirus pandemic and provision of awareness materials and essential sanitation programs including facemasks.

Project Indicators

1. The number of families accessing the requisite, at least 3 meals a day in sustained daily frequency reached through integrated food security programs. 2. Number of HHs with an acceptable level of Food Consumption Score. 3. The number of families accessing, and adopting, diet diversity as a result of training and capacity building sessions conducted to HHs to increase food consumption knowledge. 4. The number of families that have adopted better, acceptable Coping Strategy Index during the lean period. 5. Number of HHs with an acceptable Food Consumption Score. 6. The number of covid-19 prevention and awareness activities and programs conducted including face masks disributed, handwashing stations and sanitizers/soaps distributed.

Mitigation Measures

GEWDO's programming, and aid activities, are meant to promote safety, dignity and integrity of the beneficiaries. The organization has placed the beneficiaries' involvement at all levels of decision-making process including addressing the prevalent needs to include their opinion in daily programming. Inclusion of all the beneficiaries including the people with disabilities and the minorities are structured under a targeting approach that harness the input and opinion of all the stakeholders. Inclusive and targeted beneficiries' selection will ensure only needy persons are equitably, and collectively, selected, harness community support and eliminate any social tension. Safe access is also a fundamental pillar in GEWDO programming. Planning for the distribution programs will be structured such that most of the activities will be conducted in the morning hours to reduce exposure to protection risks. Additionally, the project will support efforts towards ensuring that beneficiaries are not exposed to high risk through moving thr distribution activities closer to the community. The facilities will have safe planning including separate washroom and saintation facilities. GEWDO will work with other community stakeholdersto ensure the project brings about unity and progress rather than any form of division; clear communications and transparent engagaement will be a qualitative approach at all levels of project planning. The organization's management will be a strictler to its own policies including PSEA, Child Protection, Code of conduct and HRM which will support quality maintenance and cover protection needs around project management. WHile protection risk can adopt different dynamics, the organization will fully engagae with the community, the local administration and make efficient feedbcak mechanism in the project prioritization. The organization will share a public feedbcak number and notices in local language for Complaints and Feedback.
