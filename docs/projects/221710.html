Project Summary

The project aims to provide lifesaving access to safe sanitation and hygiene (WASH) to reduced prevalence of water-related diseases, and enhance dignity to 3600 affected people by the insurgence, living in Metuge District - Cabo Delago province.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

In all activities, the project includes do no harm to planned activities considering the impacts of displacement affect women, men, girls, and boys in different ways. Thus, different actions were lined up to reach the needs of each participant group. Amongst the affected, whose majority are children, they have seen their welfare compromised. Protection interventions aim to mainstream Gender Equality and Social Inclusion (GESI) to attend the needs of the most vulnerable groups.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

The project will encourage participation of both women and men in all aspects, i.e. at least 50% representation of women in Hygiene promotion and Water and sanitation committees.For elderly person the project will ensure that their specific needs will be built into the response according to local context.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The Project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance conducting community assessments. In these assessment people with disabilities will be determined in order to ensure that their specific needs will be built into the response.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project impacts on the environment adopting some mitigation measures.These measures are essential for managing the risks associated with environmental degradation, promoting sustainable practices.For this project we have taken care of environmental education sessions (solid waste management) and distribution of waste management kits.
