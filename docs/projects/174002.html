Project Summary

RGLSA seeks to support, protect, and empower adolescent girls in Monguno and Dikwa LGAs in Borno State. The project will contribute to improving the prevention and response to violence against adolescent girls in the project locations by providing them with the skills and knowledge to identify various forms of GBV and seek support services if they experience or are at risk of Violence.

1 - Needs

Insurgency that have been ravaging the northern eastern part of Nigeria for the past 10 years have a great impact of the development of children and adolescent who have witnessed violence, abuse, exploitation and neglect. Have been in condition or situation where local/community based protection structured is no longer functioning. Children especially adolescent girls are left behind in development with appropriate fundamental skills and mentoring.This is a great risk to the sustainably development Goal we are all fighting to achieve.While girls need to see their daily realities reflected in program content, they also need to be able to envision themselves as empowered individuals. In many places, adolescent girls are not encouraged to have ambitions, so life skills programs can be an opportunity for them to discover new aspirations for their present and future personal and professional lives. Women and girls should be portrayed in life skills programs as confident, capable and empowered to handle the difficult situations that life brings them and to pursue their goals. Contents related to human rights, gender equality and power relations are critical to transformative programs.The project module will focus on six topic areas to improve/develop the basic skills girls need to survival, and assets necessary to decrease risk and increase safety for girls:Trust - This will lay the foundation for the group to be able to move on to more challenging topic areas, while also addressing some immediate issues related to safety. Social & Emotional Skills – This will provides the foundation for building girls’ positive sense of self and their ability to successfully interact with each other and their communities.Health & Hygiene - This will give girls the opportunity to learn critical information related to their bodies, during a period when they are going through significant changes. They can explore issues related to hygiene, puberty, and sexual health in a safe space, where they are provided accurate information. Safety - This will focuses on the concepts and skills girls need for identifying, preventing, mitigating, and responding to GBV. Solidarity - This will help girls to start preparing for the end of the program cycle, and strengthen their support networks so that they will continue to nurture the skills they have learned and their support network once the life skills curriculum finishes.Visioning - This will strengthen the social and emotional skills related to perseverance, as well as to create hope and support girls in concrete planning for their futures.

8.6 - Please describe briefly the modalities your project will put in place to ensure protection mainstreaming (particularly referring to the above questions).

The organization will address this risk by offering detailed training on dignity and respect, organizational Code of Conduct, mandatory reporting, to all program staff and (any community volunteers, community leaders, community-based committee leaders) and will provide an overview of punitive measures. Protection teams will also work to ensure that beneficiaries understand their right to be free from all forms of exploitation, including especially preventing sexual exploitation and abuse (PSEA), and will provide them with the information they need to be able to report such harms and seek assistance if such harms do occur. The Big Smile Foundation have a feedback mechanism which will be deployed in all locations of project implementation
