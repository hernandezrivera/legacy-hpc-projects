Project Summary

The main goal of the project (saving lives) is to alleviate, as much as possible, people’s suffering from the high cost of living, especially after the Corona pandemic and the escalation and increase in the prices of all goods, such as food, health, clothing, education, and others, which are among the basics of families, and because food is considered the most important need in an individual’s life in order to maintain... His health. Therefore, the project will target the most vulnerable groups and vulnerable families. The association has a database of families living in the city of Al-Tall.
Needs:
Due to the crisis that the Syrian country has gone through, including wars, economic crises, and a very oppressive cost of living, 80% of families have become fragile and weak, unable to secure their various needs. Hence, it is necessary to alleviate the suffering of these families by providing food aid with the aim of avoiding the families from falling into situations of poverty. Nutritional.
The association has different accountability systems, through which affected populations can increasingly influence the type, delivery and quality of assistance. The association maintains accountability and engages people through several ways, such as social media. Direct and transparent dialogue with the community, and rapid response to their needs. Inquire, listen to their suggestions, follow up on their complaints, and take the necessary amendment measures if necessary. There are also complaint boxes and a help desk. The association has ready centers serviced with everything necessary to provide services to needy families. There are dedicated offices that were and still are for food aid and nutrition projects, which are considered close to residents in the heart of the city. Therefore, there is no difficulty for families in arriving or a security risk to them, and the association has a policy, rules, and code of conduct for cases Harassment or exploitation
Project Implemantion
The association, in cooperation with the funding organization, will provide food baskets to the families registered with it. The baskets will be stored in warehouses belonging to the association and distributed according to a distribution plan to the families, documenting the necessary reports for the entire project.
Risks
Failure to obtain the required approvals to start the project from any concerned authorities (without specifying).
implementation:
Delay in delivering materials to the association, which in turn will affect the beneficiary family who needs that food support (families are currently in a state of danger due to the narrow living situation and high prices).
Attempting to exploit power to harass, violate, or exploit beneficiaries of services (from any party), which is considered unacceptable in accordance with the policies of the association or funding organization.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The project's goal is to help families suffering from foodlessness or near foodlessness by providing food rations (food insurance for families). In cooperation between the association and the partner organization, the association receives the food rations and stores them in its warehouses, and then distributes the rations to families according to weekly distribution plans (monthly distribution).The association has distribution centers through which the delivery receipt is distributed to the family who will go to the warehouse to receive their share. The shares distributed are recorded daily through storage tables, and monthly reports are prepared on those distributions, as well as quarterly and annual reports.There is also a storage table for all materials to record the number distributed and received daily.Supervision and follow-up are carried out by managers appointed in each departmentProject manager who supervises all departments with department managersEach department manager supervises and follows up on daily negligence while providing the project manager with daily and monthly reports on the workThere will be a department responsible for complaints and assisting families, to receive families’ complaints, opinions, and suggestions and record them in paper and electronic records, which will be submitted to the project manager, some of which will be processed immediately, and some of them will be submitted to higher departments (project manager, then the executive director, then the board of directors, in order to implement the procedures. necessary), complaints and suggestions with answers are scheduled on a monthly scheduleAwareness sessions are also conducted on gender, protection, and related matters once a week (4 sessions per month). A certain number of beneficiary families are contacted to come to the association’s center and conduct the sessions.Routine visits are made on a monthly basis to the families benefiting from the basket for the purposes of follow-up and verification, and to re-apply for the study if necessary. Each family has a record of its data and the studies that were conducted on it...etc.
