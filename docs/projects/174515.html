Project Summary

The proposed project aims to prevent, protect and respond to sexual/gender-based violence issues of GBV Survivors and at-risk groups through a mix of prevention and response services at individual, family, community and district level. The proposed action will target at risks women and girls, survivors of GBV, women and girls with disabilities and gender and sexual minorities through the range of programing approaches which include static or mobile delivery approaches, standalone and/or integrated programing approaches, and direct or in-partnership implementation approaches. IRC will establish women and girls’ safe spaces which will serve as an entry point for the provision of safe and confidential GBV services to GBV survivors and at-risk groups. In addition to this IRC will strengthen community-based mechanisms by mobilizing community-based women led groups and putting in place safe and confidential GBV case management services through health facilities and WGFS. The awareness raising and men and boy’s engagement in a systematic manner will remain and integral part of project implementation to reduce risks of GBV at domestic and community level.
The project will reach an estimated total 74,520 individuals (75% women and girls and 25% men and boys) in 6 targeted districts (3 in KP and 3 in Balochistan province). The proposed services provided through this project will be in line with the HRP protection (GBV) sector strategy and IASC GBV and IASC MPHSS guidelines.

1- Project Activities

Objective 1: Prevent, protect and respond to sexual/gender-based violence issues of GBV Survivors and at-risk groups.Key Activities:1. Establish integrated GBV/SRH services in health facilities to address issue of S/GBV including CMR – a. Identify gaps in GBV/SRH services for survivors of GBV using a minimum standards checklist in targeted Health Facilities (HFs) and provide support to establish safe and confidential SRH and GBV services. HFs will be supported to have availability of short- and long-term Family Planning (FP) methods and safe delivery kits.b. Establish GBV referral pathways from communities to supported HFs via LHWs and social mobilizers for SRH/GBV services and uptake of essential healthcare services. c. Conduct training on Clinical Management of Rape (CMR) as well Health Timing and Spacing Pregnancy (HTSP) for health care workers jointly by women’s protection & health staff. 2. Provision of psycho-social support services to GBV survivors and at-risk groups (in person or tele-psychosocial support services) – a. Establish Women and Girls’ Friendly Spaces (WGFS) for provision of PSS services (at level 2 and 3 of IASC intervention pyramid) for women and girls in a safe and supportive environment, and, in line with COVID-19 IPC protocols. PSS activities will range from informal recreational activities (e.g. sewing groups, tea sessions) to structured group psychosocial using IRC curriculums (1) Girl Shine and 2) Women Rise. The WGFS will also serve as an entry point for specialized services for GBV survivors and a safe and confidential space to heal. b. Offer mobile outreach services including PFA, life skills session and information about services to women, girls and transgender (TGs) persons a their doorsteps. c. Pay attention to protection and psychosocial needs of women and girls and TGs in home isolation and offer tele-counselling through a toll-free hotline (already set up in Balochistan and KP) through qualified psychologists. d. Distribute dignity kits and conduct sessions on Menstrual hygiene management and safe disposal of sanitary napkins. 3. Prevent and protect S/GBV issues through community-based protection mechanism – a. Establish community-based women led protection committees and build their capacity in GBV prevention and referral. These will comprised of women of different age groups and abilities but not limited to teachers, LHWs, women leaders etc. b. The women led protection committees will be trained to conduct safety audits and advocate to address identified risks and barriers to women and girls' safe and equitable access to humanitarian aid.4. Establish GBV multi sectoral coordination and referral pathways and case management services;a. IRC in coordination with Social Welfare Department will conduct mapping of key GBV service providers including health, police, legal aid, MHPSS etc., and establish and/or update GBV referral pathways in targeted districts. b. Provide case management as per GBV case management protocols through service providers and trained and skilled case workers.c. A GBV case management training will be organized for the key service providers. 5. Increased awareness among local population on available GBV/SRH services – a. Joint awareness raising messaging on COVID-19 prevention, GBV and SRH response services, among the women and girls.b. Conduct sessions with men and boys including family head and religious leaders to promote acceptance of women and girl centered programming such as GBV response and SRH. 6. Frontline health workers and auxiliary staff received PPEs and training on IPC – a. Provide adequate PPEs i.e., masks, gloves, hand sanitizer, soap and menstrual hygiene supplies for female staff on duty etc. b. An orientation for front line workers on IPC measures, Covid-19 mitigation during their routine activities to minimize the risk of COVID-19 transmission.c. Disinfect HFs and WGFSs on as per needs and clients’ load.
