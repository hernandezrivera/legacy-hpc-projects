Project Summary

HOPE is planning to work on recovering access to WASH services for the 160,000 vulnerable affected population in localities of Jebel Awlia & Karari in KH, Zalingei & Golo in CD, Al Idia & An Nuhud in WK, and Madeinat Al Gedaref, Wasat Al Gedaref in Gadaref, that will contribute to 3 HRP WASH sector objectives for 2024.
In this project, HOPE will (1) provision access to lifesaving water services include (i) operationalization of water treatment plants in Khartoum, (ii) water distribution (water trucking) to health facilities and gathering points, (2) provision of essential WASH supplies and NFIs to support emergency sanitation services and compliment hygiene promotion activities – particularly in flood prone areas affected people and (3) Rehabilitation and operation and maintenance (O&M) water supply schemes includes supply pipeline support and technical and management capacity for sustainable WASH services.
The main activities will be delivered by HOPE in this project are: Rehabilitate of Mini Water Yard (MWY); Rehabilitate hand pumps driven water points; Upgrade high yielding hand pump system to Mini Water Yard; Support operation and maintenance of motorized mini water yard; Establish and train water quality volunteers on surveillance, treatment, and reporting; Conduct chlorination treatment at the household, and water point; Train Water Management Committee (WMCs) members on the O&M, and sustainability; Conduct locality wide CLTS approach; Construction of gender sensitive latrines in schools; Train and support women on PHAST and hygiene promoters; and Train and support Solid Waste Management Committees and hygiene worker to conduct clean-up campaign at weekly basis.
HOPE will consider accountability to affected people through full community participation, strengthen the establish / refresh the community mechanism/ committee such as WASH committees and hygiene promotors as well as do follow up for all benefit targeted separately to assess the project, identify challenges and provide recommendations to improve the project outcomes.
