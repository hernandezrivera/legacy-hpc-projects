Project Summary

COVID-19 Emergency Risk Mitigation project is designed to support public health responses to contain the spread of the COVID-19 pandemic by decreasing morbidity and mortality. It is aimed at Creating an enabling environment for the COVID-19 response through immediate interventions to improve governance, human rights and gender equality, coordination, social cohesion and service provision at district level. The project is targeting Masvingo and Matabeleland south provinces in the following districts; Gutu, Masvingo rural, Masvingo urban and Zaka; Insiza, Mangwe and Matobo.
