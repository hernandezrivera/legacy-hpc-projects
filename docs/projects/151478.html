Project Summary

The overall objective of the project is to ensure comprehensive humanitarian response to the Water sanitation and hygiene needs as well as the protection concerns of the drought and conflict affected populations (Internally displaced persons, Pastoralists communities and host communities). The project seeks to mainstream protection measures in the provision of WASH services and will maximize on the positive impacts of WASH interventions on people’s safety and dignity and support the affected people to enjoy and access their rights.
The community mobilisation processes, the community members will participate effectively in the implementation of the project as they had been part of the gap identification process. Communities will be briefed regarding Cash for work interventions and use of vouchers to redeem hygiene materials. They also helped select the drought committee that developed the criteria for beneficiary selection that included;
The WASH facilities constructed or rehabilitated (boreholes, extension of water pipelines, water tanks shallow wells, berkards, support operation and maintenance, hygiene services through use of vouchers and sanitation facilities in communities, introduction of prepaid water metering and institutions etc) will be done in accordance to the SPHERE guides and responses conducted in relation to the core humanitarian standards. The project will achieve this by improving the prevention strategies through public educations and awareness campaigns, establishing and supporting community patrols and media campaigns against the harmful traditional practices.
The project will also enhance access service and livelihood support to Gender based Violence-GBV survivors by providing comprehensive GBV survivors services including; Medical, Psycho-social, referral, Legal support and livelihood assistances through provision of dignity kits and by offering life skills/ vocational skills training for economic empowerment and independency. Proposed interventions focus on provision of timely, effective and quality lifesaving protection response and service provision to prevent or respond to Gender-Based Violence in the context of the drought in Somalia. Activities will be implemented with full knowledge of government line ministries that will support implementation of the sexual offenses act.
Cash programing will be integrated in our responses when constructing and rehabilitating WASH facilities as well us strengthening livelihood conditions for GBV affected families.
The project will target Garbaharey and Dollow districts in Gedo region and Dangorayo, Garowe , and Eyl districts in Puntland state and Bosaso district in Bari region.

Needs

Acute food insecurity remains prevalent among pastoral populations who lost most of their livestock and became destitute during the 2016/2017 drought & Cyclone Sagar; riverine populations affected by flooding in April & May 2018; and others experiencing protracted displacement due to conflict & natural hazards. Assessments by NCA & HADMA in 2018 show high water prices with pastoralists & IDPS buying water at unaffordable exorbitant prices.Assessments by NCA in Gedo, Puntland& communities indicate that women, girls & children lack access to water, have poor hygiene & sanitation conditions. The burden of fetching water falls squarely on them. Safe, lockable & well-lit latrines are not available, making women & girls prisoners of day light. They wait for darkness to relieve themselves increasing health related concerns. Night trips to fields for defecation, bathing & walking long distances to collect water puts them at risk of physical attacks & sexual violence. They lack basic hygiene items (sanitary cloth, soap & sanitary clothes) to enable them lead a dignified life Insecurity in IDP camps, increases risks of sexual violence for women & girls. Many of them live in shelters made of cloth & plastic sheeting, which are easily accessible to an intruder. Some of the IDP settlements are physically isolated, situated on the outskirts of the towns which worsens their situation. This is aggravated by lack of justice for survivors and GBV remains a norm in Somalia; because traditional Somali society does not openly discuss GBV issues. Traditional /customary law overrides the state judiciary as they respond quickly & are more accessible than formal legal mechanisms.As a result, women & children are forced to adopt risky harmful coping strategies, including collection and sale of firewood and payment of debt through forced marriage. Pressure for survival intensifies the potential for intimate partner violence. Lack of Income causes dependency increasing vulnerability to SG

Enhance safer access to sustainable safe basic water supply for men, women, boys and girls through; Construction of 7 Masonry Water tanks 14mx7mx4m in Garbaharey &Dollow; Construction of water tank 25m3-Qansahaley camp; Pipeline extension/water points - Qansahaley camp; rehabilitate two community berkads in Dangorayo for pastoralists; Installation of prepaid water meters in Qansanley Camps; expand pipeline extension with water kiosk in Garowe IDPs & Strengthen the capacity of water user committees.Improve access to sanitation facilities for Women, men, girls and boys at communal & institutional level; Construct 2 gender segregated & lockable IDP latrines blocks in Garowe with hand wash facilities; Communal latrines constructed for the health facility in Garowe with hand washing facility; construct 2 garbage pit & conduct Clean-up campaigns in Garowe IDPs; construct 4 ventilated improved pit latrine in schools in Garowe & 150 communal latrines for IDPs in Garbaharey & Dollow Ensure women, men, girls & boys have enough knowledge to protect themselves against health related risks by: Establish & train hygiene clubs in Garowe school; Engage 220 students in Competitive hygiene & sanitation learning; Distribution of Community life straw, to increase safe drinking water in schools; Training of 48 teachers & CECs in schools; establish school hygiene clubs; Hygiene campaigns in Garbaharey, Dollow &Dangorayo & Provision 1000 Hygiene kits in Garowe and Qansanley IDPS GBV activities will improve GBV services for women and girls ; Establish & support 15 joint patrols; Conduct 4 public information & education campaigns on GBV; Conduct 2 media public messaging & awareness campaigns ; Conduct GBV prevention & response training for 200 people; Provide 1500 solar lamps vulnerable groups ; support Maatokal Bosaso Centre & Establish a one stop referral centre in Eyl ; provide training for 40 healthcare & social service providers & Create awareness on the sexual offenses bill.

How is the project linked to durable solutions/resilience?

The project is designed to address both the immediate needs of the crisis affected households and support recovery and resilience building. Construction and rehabilitation of WASH facilities is geared towards long term. The project will ensure communities are involved in the project from the planning stage and throughout the whole project implementation cycle. This is a deliberate effort to ensure the community owns and takes responsibility for the project. These actions are expected to lead to sustainability of the project even after the end of the intervention. The project has a neutral impact on the environment and WASH facilities will be done to ensure that water is used maximally and there is not wastage and that it drains with low negative impact on target population. sanitation facilities will be done in accordance to approved designs to limit ground water contaminationThe involvement of the communities strengthens the communities ability to manage the project and ensure that needs prioritized locally are responded to. Besides this, empowering/capacity development of the different committees will ensure increased management and sustained access to WASH and protection services. Through the partnerships, the different and complementary roles of local authorities, committees and local population will be strengthened. This together with building the capacity of the committees will enhance community development in a manner that communities are responsible or their own needs. At the end of the project, community capacities and coping mechanisms are expected to be strengthened on different fronts, constructed/rehabilitated infrastructure will remain in place, and hopefully, capacities for peace and stability will be enhanced. NCA will work closely with the government and community structures. The proposed project will educate the stakeholders and community policies and laws existing and their rights as fas as the water policy and the sexual offenses bill are concerned thus a step towards enhancing the capacity of both community and government representative. The constructed or rehabilitated community facilities after completion will be managed by the community and supervised/ rehabilitated by the local government as well as the private sector.NCA will engage rights holders and stakeholders at all levels and stages of project implementation to ensure their participation. During community mobilization and sensitization, NCA will provide information to the targeted communities about their entitlement /benefits. NCA will conduct consultative meetings with the relevant government officials and work closely with existing community leaders and representative bodies (Village Committees) especially in providing the criteria for beneficiary selection which will be translated into the local language

Protection risk analysis & Mitigating measures
Preventing risks of exclusion based on displacement

Drought, conflict and floods are the main recurring risks facing the target population, when these calamities occur they trigger massive displacement of people and further exposes the community to serious protection threats and negative coping mechanism. This leads to deterioration of communities’ resilience and social fragmentation. In order to address this the proposed plan will continue to involve target beneficiaries in the project cycle where project planning, implementation and monitoring of activities and results will be done with concerted efforts of the target beneficiaries, NCA and local authorities in target locations. Through community mobilization and awareness sessions, Target communities will be given the platform to influence decisions about the services and the interventions they get through community consultations, gender sensitivity and though community participation and involvement.NCA will work closely with the existing local administration and community structures such as religious leaders, clan and community elders, minorities’ women group and youth networks, men, women, girls and boys from IDPs community to accommodate the most vulnerable communities and will ensure inclusiveness, accountability and transparency. NCA will adhere to the internal control procedures that ensure value for money is achieved through out the procurement processes. NCA to ensure improvement in the quality of interventions being undertake. NCA will be responsible for back donor communication including reporting. NCA and partners will activity participate and report on project activities during the regional cluster meetings as well as the Nairobi meetings. Through the NCA complains handling mechanisms. Beneficiaries will at will be able to share any concerns or provide feedback on areas of improvement to the organization for improved project management and coordination.

Preventing risks of exclusion based on gender and age

Women and children are the worst affected by displacement caused by natural and man-made disasters. The Somalia customary system is based on a clear gender and age division, women and youth have unequal access to resources, opportunities, power and decision. During emergencies women and youth are not involved. NCA will be gender and age sensitive in its interventions. The reason been during crisis times, family, community and social structures breakdown and that girls, women, boys and men are affect differently and In efforts to resist violence, survive and support their dependents, women and men act differently (e.g. by selling off assets essential for continuing traditional livelihoods, men may migrate for work, young men may feel there is no other option but to join armed groups, women and girls may resort to sex work, there may be an increase in early marriages, etc. The old and the infants become the most vulnerable. With all these into consideration NCA will provide gender and age sensitive interventions that will address the different needs of all these various groups in an inclusive manner- equal opportunities for all.The project will make deliberate effort to maintain a protective environment at community level so that girls and women in particular are not exposed to harm such as sexual and gender based violence while undertaking their daily chores. Further, gender equity will be observed while implementing all project activities. Men, women, Boys and Girls will be treated equally with their different needs being targeted and support provided accordingly. Women will be fronted to participate in decision making structures like the community project committees where they will be involved in project monitoring efforts.

Mitigating the impact of armed conflict and violence

Given the constraints faced by IDPS and Host communities when it comes to sharing resources, this project will aid in eliminating this tensions as it targets a wide array of drought and conflict affected populations. The project will provide water hygiene and sanitation support as well as improve the protection environment of men, women, boys and girls in target locations. With increasing GBV cases in IDP camps, the project will provide lighting and ensure that all WASH interventions are strategically located and accessible in areas secure for the target population. Patrol groups will be established within the camps to offer an additional protection layer for vulnerable groups within the camps. Women and girls will get relief as WASH facilities will be constructed in close proximity to their shelters as outlined in the SPHERE thus limiting GBV related risksThe impact of armed conflict and violence will be in minimal, the project intervention will focus on areas where there is no security threat, currently the regions security is relative calm but in case of worst scenario NCA has a security plan in place so that its staff are not exposed to exaggerated risks and can quickly be moved to safer areas. Security focal points at field level and proper communication strategy have been established. Also, NCA regularly follows the security updates provided by the NGO Security Program (NSP)
