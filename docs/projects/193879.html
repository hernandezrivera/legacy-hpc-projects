Project Summary

The project aims to mitigate the vulnerable farmer's struggles and suffering resulting from
Israel's occupation and settlers' attacks to contribute to the sustainable income of small-
scale women and men by increasing the strength of the most vulnerable families
resilience in the northeast and northwest of Jerusalem( Beit Duqqu, Al Qubeiba,Biddu, Al
Isawiya, Azzayyem, As Sawahira, Beit Iksa, Beit Surik, An Nabi Samwil, Aljudeira, Jaba,
AlEizariya, Al Jib, Mikhmas, Beit Anan, Abu Dis, Arab al Jahilin,Wadi Abu Hindi, Abu Nwar,
Wadi Jiml), and northwest Ramallah (Umm safa, Shabtin, Qibya, Deir Nidam, An
Nabisalih) in area C by targeting vulnerable farmers who are highly vulnerable and lack
access to basic services, the specifc objective is to increase the well-being and income of
women and men engaged in small-scale agriculture in vulnerable communities through
improved agricultural and access to land and market.
The project is directly linked with FSS Objective (SO3) achieved enhancing in resilience farmers, herders, livestock holders of vulnerable households, and their agriculture-based livelihoods, income generating, and protect them, low-income households who are facing limited access to food to be able to meet their basic food needs by interventions to support livelihoods and the economic empowerment of food-insecure households and restore their agriculture production to safeguard livelihoods, through rehabilitating damaged productive assets; and provide time-critical productive inputs. The project will support and enhance the resilience and protect the agricultural livelihoods of 3455 individual beneficiaries (1762 M, 1693 F) of the communities and families to build their capacity to absorb shocks, including access to the land and improving access to food (132 individuals) by (CFW) activities and create manually work for unplowed people (women and men) through these activities:

1- Rehabilitation of 750 dunums of agricultural land which serve 809 (223 M, 222 W, 178 B, 162 G, 24 E) individual
beneficiaries and create 400 direct working days (cash for work) for 27 direct unemployed workers by implementing this activities in Jerusalem and Ramallah .. (Note: some land will get full rehabilitating and others just get what they need of retaining walls or fencing or manually cleaning).
2- Construct 8 kilos of agricultural roads that serve 240 farmers who own 1600 dunums. 3- Rehabilitate 10 water cisterns that serve 10 farmers who own 50 dunums.
4- Rehabilitate 30 barns that serve 147 individual (41 M, 40 W, 32 B, 29 G, and 4 E elderlies).
5- Distribution of 130 cutter machines, that serve 130 farmers who own agricultural land to serve 1300 Dunums.
6- Distribution of 130 Olive picker machines, that serve 130 farmers who own olives trees to serve 1300 Dunums.
To ensure threats of food security, income, and life, in general, are minimized. This project
Cash for work will be in this project as the following:
- Land rehabilitation for cleaning the land manually will serve 132 direct individuals and 2024 in direct individuals ( in Ramallah and Jerusalem targeted areas), The opportunity to benefit from this activity will be given to the unemployed young men or women or farmers affected by the bad conditions, and since the land is cleaned manually, the opportunity will be given to women farmers to continue the land cultivation.
- 113 indirect beneficiaries (CfW) in Jerusalem and Ramallah will benefit from Agricultural cisterns rehabilitation.
- Road rehabilitation will benefit 78 individuals (CfW) (22M, 21 W, 17 B, 16G, 2 E) indirectly in Jerusalem and Ramallah.
- Barn rehabilitation will benefit 69 individuals (CfW) (19M, 19 W, 15 B, 14G, 2 E) indirectly in Ramallah and Jerusalem.

1 - Needs

To understand the causes behind deteriorating livelihood conditions in Jerusalem' villages, and the targeted Ramallah areas in area C, various economic, demographic, agricultural, nutritional, health, environmental and food security issues must be considered. AAA did a comprehensive needs assessment for the targeted villages in early Oct. We noticed, through group meetings, the extent of the suffering of farmers affected by the attacks of the occupation of their lands and their source of livelihood as they are prevented from using the bypass road 443 which shortens the space between the lands and villages and these bypass roads have consumed a lot of agricultural lands, Israeli illegal settler's violence against farmers and their farms, consequently, the farmer's access to his land takes a lot of time and effort, as the infrastructure available for movement and mobility suffers from wear and tear due to the dredging and planned destruction of the occupation and by the weather factors, and the eastern regions are characterized by a low rate of rainwater, which burdens the farmer in summer in the process of irrigation of crops, confiscation notices against the possession of their land, the release of wild Deers (which become an extra risk for farmers' trees mainly when attending their lands), confiscation of lands and scraping of agricultural lands by the occupation, as happened in the village of Mikhmas, where 300 olive trees over 30 years old, waste recycling plant in Al-Eizariya poses a threat to the environment are attempts to force the farmer to leave of the the remaining land, the farmers suffer from strangling and restrictions on movement by the the separation wall, bypass roads that are not allowed to use, the continuous expansion of Israeli settlements which lead to confiscating of lands from the Palestinian people, continues violence that occurs by the settlers live in that area including destroying agricultural lands, roads, and infrastructure, limited access to water, and climatic change (less rainwater and high temperature), all these practices deteriorated life in the targeted locations, so the suggested interventions like rehabilitating agricultural road, land rehabilitation, rehabilitate barns, distribute olive pickings machines and cutter grass, and rehabilitation of cisterns wells, will facilitate/ease farmers’ movement, and work on their adherence to land and plant them. That will support farmers' livelihoods and resilience to stand in the face of land confiscation and therefore their survival in the profession of agriculture, which keeps their livelihood source secure, profitable, and sustainable. The the idea falls in cluster objective (SO3) To support the resilience and the productive capacity of farmers, particularly small-scale ones are restored/enhanced and livelihoods protected. So it was agreed with the need of local authorities and farmers on the need to open new agricultural roads to facilitate access Farms to new lands and facilitate the transfer of crops in addition to the need to rehabilitate reclaimed lands in the past to expand the area To replace the areas swallowed by the settlements, facing various levels of drops in Agricultural crops productivity, and building the necessary retaining walls to protect the soil from erosion. The assessment reported a sharp increase in the unemployment rate in those villages mainly among farmers and especially young people and women, thus they are demanding to work in agriculture as a source to secure their livelihood. The project is targeting a total of 3455 direct beneficiaries ((953 M, 947 W, 760 B, 691G, and 104 elderlies)) individual beneficiaries (disaggregated by gender) beneficiaries will benefit from increased access to their land to ensure the inclusion of women, and other groups as well as involve men, girls, and girls at schools, youth, and elderly. Where women are considered strong participants in the rural agriculture sector The assessment revealed that women are more vulnerable to limited access to land and low income, as they bear agricultural burdens of up to 65%, so the provision of qualified agricultural roads is an important factor in facilitating their agricultural work 15% and providing the burdens of transportation on bumpy roads that may cause them more pain and physical problems, and the children of retaining walls work To provide a safe environment that protects her and her children from falling while moving between the agricultural terraces and provides her with more time to take care of her family. These are the main issues that farmers are currently facing farmers from obtaining the optimal use of their main source of income that contributes significantly to their families' food security. AAA offers to work on the most badly eroded 8 km of agricultural roads and rehabilitate 750 dunum, also distributing 130 olive pickers machines and 130 grass cutters will reduce the effort during the picking process and will speed up the process, especially for the lands behind the wall that need security coordination, rehabilitation of 30 barns, and 10 cisterns for the most vulnerable farmers. This will enhance farmers' access to their agricultural lands, stability, and better access to livelihoods source. AAA will implement this project in close coordination with the MoA and other actors in the area to avoid any duplication and overlapping. The intervention objectives are to facilitate the access of farmers to their lands, facilitate the transportation of the inputs and outputs to and from the land without any extra cost or effort, better access to water and other inputs, and a more sustainable income. It will reinforce farmers' resilience on their lands.

2 - Activities

Prior to the project implementation, AAA will strictly coordinate the final definition and selection of localities and beneficiaries with line ministries offices, and with any other organization working in the same area and sector of activities. AAA will be targeting farmers in the northeast and northwest of Jerusalem( Beit Duqqu, Al Qubeiba,Biddu, Al Isawiya, Azzayyem, As Sawahira, Beit Iksa, Beit Surik, An Nabi Samwil, Aljudeira, Jaba, AlEizariya, Abu Dis, Arab al Jahilin,Wadi Abu Hindi, Abu Nwar, Wadi Jiml), and northwest Ramallah (Umm safa, Shabtin, Qibya, Deir Nidam, An Nabisalih) in area C by main activities (rehabilitation of agricultural roads, rehabilitation of agricultural lands, cistern rehabilitation, rehabilitation barns, and distributing olives pickers machines and grass cutter). Through agricultural roads, farmers' mainly women access their agricultural the land will be facilitated, with less time and more cost-effective. Farmers will perform farming practices more effectively and easily and securely, this will increase productivity and quality of the produce and easy access to the marketing of high-quality agricultural products. Furthermore, through the implementation of collective fencing of agricultural areas trees and humans including women and children will be safer from wild animals and deer attacks. AAA in coordination with local CBOs, related ministries, and village councils will formulate a steering committee in each community, which will create clear beneficiary selection criteria with clear marks that will help in the selection of the poorest farmers. The criteria should include technical standards, household status, and income, 15% extra marks will be given to each household headed by women, a number of families members, disability members in the family, chronic diseases in the family, having university students in the family, the benefited land space, the type of crop, where we will target crops with higher viable economically, and land location if its near settlements and bypass roads, military camps... etc. AAA. will discuss the applications with the participation of the selected committee to each location with the implementation of field visits to verify the information written in the applications specifically (the technical standards, the economic situation) AAA will make sure to enhance women's roles through participation in the local committees and will give priority to women-headed households and youth. Rehabilitation roads will facilitate farmers mainly women access to cultivated lands, in addition, to transferring the product or production materials to the cultivated land. The agricultural road will ease access to tools and machines on the agricultural land, which decreases women's workload, noting that 65% of the agricultural work is on the shoulders of women (Oxfam’s olive value chain report). AAA will make sure to have women representatives on the selection committee. Also, they will receive positive discrimination during the process of evaluating the applications for the benefit, as the a woman who is the breadwinner for her family or the widow will be awarded 5 additional points and the same is for the woman who applies to benefit from the activities who owns the land, and families who have 3 or more female children will receive two additional points. It will also take into account the cases of disabilities and university students. The activities will be in Jerusalems' villages and Ramallah areas as the following: - Land rehabilitation 750 dunum as the following: ( in Jerusalem (Beit Duqqu, Al Qubeiba,Biddu, Al Isawiya, Azzayyem, As Sawahira, Beit Iksa, Beit Surik, Aljudeira, Jaba, an Nabi Samwil), in Ramallah (Umm safa, Shabtin, Qibya, Deir Nidam, An Nabisalih) . -Agricultural Road 8 KM as the following: (Beit Duqqu, Al Qubeiba,Biddu, Al Isawiya, Azzayyem, As Sawahira, Beit Iksa, Beit Surik) - Rehabilitate 10 Cisterns as the following: (Beit Duqqu, Al Qubeiba,Biddu, Al Isawiya, Azzayyem, As Sawahira, Beit Iksa, Beit Surik, Al Eizariya, and Abu Dis). - Rehabilitate 30 barns in Arab al Jahilin,Wadi Abu Hindi, Abu Nwar, Wadi Jiml. - Distribute 130 olives pickers and grass cutter machines in (AlJib, Mikhmas, Beit Anan, Biddu, Abu Dis).
