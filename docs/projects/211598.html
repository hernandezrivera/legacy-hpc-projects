Project Summary

The intervention aims to 1) Saves lives: Provide lifesaving and life-sustaining EORE through Community Focal Points (CFPs), in areas with a high severity of needs, to women, girls, boys and men, and by safely removing explosive hazards from contaminated communities. 2) Enhance protection: Enhance the prevention and mitigation of protection risks and respond to protection needs through DCA’s systems for internal referrals through CL activity (namely to psychosocial support and Cash for Food) as well as external referrals (for victim assistance, namely prosthetics and assistive device provision). 3) Increase resilience and access to services: By clearing EO from buildings, agricultural land, and key infrastructure, increase the resilience of affected communities by improving access to livelihood opportunities and basic services, especially among the most vulnerable households and communities in areas with a high severity of needs.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

Survey and clearance of EO contaminated communities will be the focus of this project, and through this activity as well as continuous engagements between DCA and communities, suitable infrastructure for potential rehabilitation by DCA will be identified and/or prioritised through local authorities and engagement with technical working groups (TWG) at NES level. Based on coordination efforts as well as data gathered previously through needs assessments all proposed infrastructure will be selected for rehabilitation based on community priorities, cost effectiveness and long-term impact.DCA will support targeted communities across multiple sectors through the implementation of integrated programming and deployment of multi-skilled CL teams, conducting Non-Technical Survey (NTS) and Explosive Ordinance Risk Education (EORE), DCA's local partner RMCO will deploy to accurately map confirmed/suspected hazardous areas, select Community Focal Points (CFPs) to deliver EORE in their own communities, signpost individuals and vulnerable groups to relevant Victim Assistance (VA) services, and deliver emergency EORE where needed. The CL teams, through direct contact with communities, identify potential redevelopment opportunities during NTS and through Explosive Ordnance Risk Education EORE activities, signpost individuals, families, and communities to specialist Victim Assistance (VA) and support mechanisms. This project further aims to enable vulnerable families to safely return and live a dignified life with access to proper living conditions and basic services. The aim is to reduce the threat presented by EO in populated areas to pave the way for rehabilitation, return unused infrastructure, public spaces, and residential homes to communities for redevelopment and safe and productive use, and enhance safer access for humanitarian interventions. Output 1: Key community / public, socio-economic infrastructure is rendered safe from Explosive Ordnance (EO), Output 2: Key community/public infrastructure identified, assessed, and rehabilitated for redevelopment by HMA and DCAs Shelter Team, Output 3: IDPs, returnees and host communities receive safety messaging and training to better protect themselves, their friends, and families from EO.
