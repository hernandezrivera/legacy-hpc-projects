1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The overall objective is to reduce morbidity and mortality through an integrated multisectoral response addressing the critical lifesaving and life-sustaining needs of most vulnerable people of Cabo Delgado affected by the conflict and climate crisis ensuring meaningful access to comprehensive, safe, equitable and dignified essential care services in hard-to-reach, IDP and returnees concentrated areas of Cabo Delgado, with a particular focus on women, girls and children. The project has two main components: 1. Lifesaving through mobile essential services provision. Component implemented by Integrated Mobile Clinics and Brigades of doctors, nurses, midwives, nutritionists, psychologist workers, behavior change officers.2. Life sustaining through Health Facility and Community Network strengthening. Component also implemented by multi-disciplinary teams.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Both of the project components are deployed overall the areas of intervention with different intensity depending on the context (both intensified in case of high crisis): mobile approach privileged for hard-to-reach and after a displacement; static approach intensified to respond to an epidemic outbreak (cholera for instance). 1. Lifesaving through mobile essential services provision.Through both a direct substitution (Mobile Clinics) and support (Mobile Brigades), in order to offer a comprehensive package of care. This package is implemented by multi-disciplinary teams specialized in Primary Health Care (including sexual and reproductive health as well as mental health and psychosocial support) and Community Management of Acute Malnutrition for all types of care of beneficiaries (adult, adolescent, pregnant and lactating women). Pharmaceutical support as well. 2. Life sustaining through Care Facilities and Community Network strengthening as well as StudiesThrough multi-disciplinary teams implementing both sustainable and emergency approaches among the existing health and nutrition systems. Sub-activities being small rehabilitation, capacity building (trainings, on-the-job coaching and surge approach support as well as to respond to sanitary diseases) for health workers and community health actors for community case management. Nutritional studies as well.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The project will mainstream protection, addressing the protection risks and needs of affected people with the aim of safeguarding their dignity while strengthening the protection environment through collective and multi-sectoral action in line with commitments to gender equality principles, the centrality of protection, and PSEA across the humanitarian response. ACF will promote inter-agency collective action on the centrality of protection by ensuring implementation of mitigating measures, provision of complaint and feedback mechanisms, PSEA and child safeguarding focal points and disability inclusion. Moreover, ACF has already started the preliminary steps for conducting a gender and protection analysis to highlight specific gender issues in the intervention area and strengthen gender and protection activities, with an analysis of protection risks and existing vulnerabilities, distribution of roles and responsibilities including empowerment and decision-making power within the household, etc. ACF will complete the gender analysis and protection risks assessment in the initial phase of the project implementation and will disseminate main results and observations to inform project design and intervention activities. ACF will also closely work with Protection Cluster to be supported on protection referral.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

ACF will ensure that at beneficiary targeting stage, community committees with equal representation of all community groups (including women, elderly and people with disability) will be formed for the initial self-targeting process. ACF will also advocate and propose to the community committees to utilize beneficiary selection criteria that includes gender and age vulnerabilities. In addition, during the needs assessment, ACF will assess also the specific needs of the different groups and accordingly tailor the assistance. The time, location and implementation approach of the project activities will be defined in consultation with the beneficiaries and according to their needs, particularly of women, elderly and persons with disability, to ensure their participation and access to assistance. The Mobile Approach includes not only mobile clinics and brigades proposing child friendly spaces but also outreach teams for door-to-door approaches in link with the community actors to reach everyone.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The project will identify people with disabilities since its targeting phase. Community committees will be formed and have equal representation of all community groups including people with disability. The communities, in all their different groups thus including people with disabilities, will be consulted to define appropriate strategies and implementation approaches for removing any barrier for participating to the project and access its assistance.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

Rehabilitation activities will promote the use of local materials and sustainable structures. Medical waste management will be carefully discussed and monitored with the health facilities and mobile teams. The Health and Nutrition teams work in close collaboration with the WASH team to ensure that aspect.
