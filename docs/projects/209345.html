Project Summary

UNHCR site management interventions will aim to (i) enhance coordination and assistance delivery in sites and site-like settings, (ii) reinforce community engagement by mapping /establishing site governance structures that are inclusive and representative of the age, gender, and diversity of the population, (iii) facilitate capacity-development through a capacity-sharing initiative on best practice site management and protection mainstreaming and (iv) enhance site protective environment through small-scale care infrastructure repairs of IDP hosting sites to facilitate dignified living conditions. UNHCR site management interventions will adapt an Area-Based Approach (ABA) targeting the whole of society in assistance delivery in targeted locations. The approach will mitigate sites becoming pull factors while facilitating assistance to the most in need in a wide geographical location. The project will target 1,040,480 IDPs in different states in Sudan.

3 - Protection mainstreaming
3.1 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

UNHCR maintains zero tolerance towards sexual exploitation and abuse. The project teams will undertake mandatory training on the code of conduct and be continuously sensitized on the Prevention from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA). Further, the project staff and partners will be trained on the humanitarian and protection principles, including observing the elements of neutrality, impartiality, operational independence, and DO-NO-HARM, and ensuring the highest standards of dignity in assistance delivery. UNHCR will undertake risk mapping and analysis to ensure proactive and reactive measures effectively mitigate the impact. UNHCR will use its capacities available in country and its long-standing partnership with national NGOs in Sudan to facilitate localization in programming, foster local ownership of the response, and pave the way for an exit strategy. UNHCR will ensure that due diligence on HLP-related issues is considered in site planning and development interventions. UNHCR will facilitate community consultation throughout the site development processes.

4.1.1 - How will the project use community feedback to improve and adapt activities? (open-ended/free text, required)4

. Project will deploy complaint and feedback mechanisms (CFM) including static (information desks) and mobile teams to reach out to wide groups of persons of concern, record complaints and provide timely feedback. Household level and or assessment survey on services will be conducted to identify gaps at household level and provide tailored response. Surveys will aim to reach out to all groups including the elderly, persons with disability, women, men, and children. Project will ensure that persons with disabilities have access to all relevant information by preparing gender responsive key messages in multiple and accessible formats

4.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will support Inter-Agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including Sexual exploitation and abuse complaints?

Through this project, UNHCR will aim for a more robust Accountability to the Affected Population (AAP) mechanism that places the affected population at the center of programming. UNHCR will adapt mobile SM approaches that involve deploying roving teams, setting up information desks, and providing feedback to the community. Where feasible, UNHCR will deploy static community resource centers that provide a one-stop information hub to the affected population in the gathering sites and the host communities, and in close coordination with the protection partners to ensure coherence and non-duplication. Further, UNHCR will ensure that persons with disabilities have access to all relevant information by preparing gender-responsive key messages in multiple and accessible formats. Key messaging and information dissemination will consider using local languages for a wider outreach.
