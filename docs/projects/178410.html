6 - Protection Risk Analysis

SGBV and Child labour are the main risks that beneficiaries or humanitarian workers may be exposed to through the implementation of the project’s activities. The mitigation measures would be providing technical and in-kind support for the beneficiaries till the income generating activities are well established, providing PSS to children and create a referral path for those suffering from SGBV and child protection issues. Humanitarian workers will be protected by our Duty of Care Policy.

7.1 - Please list any risks related to sexual exploitation and abuse that have been identified during the project design phase and any risk mitigation and prevention measures that have been adopted and built into the project

Local staff was trained on PSEA and singed a Code of Conduct integrated with Ai.Bi. PSEA Policies. Any reported breach in the code will be investigated following the Complain Policies and the HR Manager will evaluate the step to follow.

8 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Given the volatility of the context the security situation may change in any moment. New IDPs arrivals are a concrete risk. The project would mitigate the risks if they were to materialize readapting the budget structure up to a 15% as per donor regulation and will reconvert the functions of the field staff to support PIN.
