Project Summary

The situation has been made worse by the Covid 19 whereby parents and children were forced to stay at home due to restricted movements and schools closure. There have been increased GBV cases including, rape, FGM and early marriages. IDPs vulnerability increases the likelihood of them experiencing GBV. Somalia is a challenging environment for the protection and especially for people living with disability. Support of GBV survivors due to cultural sensitivity, fear of stigmatization and reprisals . Hence, access to service providers is limited for GBV survivors. People with disability are the most vulnerable and are at risk of exposure to GBV. GBV remains significantly under reported and undocumented. A legitimate concern is the lack of prosecution of the GBV perpetrators which has contributed to a climate of impunity. This is due a weakened rule of law and lack of legislation. The main protection risks to GBV survivors are: limited access to health services, lack of information and insecurity in the camps Leaflet.The proposed project is aimed at strengthening and improving protection activities in Kismayo, Baidoa and Afmadow Districts. The proposed project aims at one provide timely GBV awareness, effective and quality response. The project will also aim at strengthening case management and referral services of GBV and unaccompanied and separated children. This includes providing training and mentoring to the GBV and CP staffs of WRRS. The project will conduct community safety audits in the target areas of Afmadow, Kismayo and Baidoa, Complaint and feedback mechanism will be established in the three locations whereby beneficiaries will be able to access, even those living with disabilities. The project provides immediate assistance to vulnerable populations. GBV survivors will receive case management and psycho-social support (PSS), including emergency care, referral to medical teams trained in case management of rape (CMR), tailored material assistance, basic emotional support, and follow up on the services will be provided. Capacity building with formal and informal authorities will be strengthened and improved. Further, the intervention provides training and technical support for health workers engaged in CMR, as well as for additional health and protection staff, in order to equip participants with the skills to provide compassionate, confidential, and competent clinical care to survivors of GBV. Persons with disabilities will be prioritized, and especially for children living in the streets with disabilities. The project will also provide material support to the victims of GBV, GBV survivors will be provided with dignity kits, street solar lights will be installed in the most congested IDP sites to enhance protection of women and girls. The proposed project also aims to protect child rights and safety, prevent critical deterioration of their physical and mental health and promote their short and longer-term well being. The activities to achieve the above includes: Recreational activities/Counseling and medical child support, case workers capacity building, material support to enhance provision and access to essential services for child survivors, Prevention and Response services for unaccompanied and Separated Children(IDTR ). awareness and community engagement, supporting coordination, CP assessment and Monitoring of CP situation. WRRS will conduct training for monitors to conduct monitoring on displacements in the two target regions. WRRS will conduct safety audits/mapping exercise in the two target regions. The organization will also establish a complaint and feedback mechanisms that will be used by the beneficiaries to report on any protection concerns during the project implementation.

1. - Projects activities

Clinical Management of Rape (CMR) cases targeting the affected population (800 women & 200 girls) in Lower Juba and Bay regions.Women, girls, men and boys will be supported to access and benefit from age appropriate and quality services of clinical management of rape in GBV one stop centres that meet the minimum standards. •Provide psychosocial support for FGM/GBV/Rape and sexual assault survivors in existing GBV one stop centres and women and girls' safe spaces that meet the minimum standards •Provide GBV/FGM/Sexual Assaults and rape survivors with key GBV/FGM information on service availability, prevention and mitigation of GBV/FGM/Sexual Assaults and rapes cases. •GBV - Training of duty bearers, medical personnel, and humanitarian staff. WRRS will also mobilize PSS counsellor for in Lower Juba and Bay Regions to deliver one on one and group psychosocial support and counselling for traumatized women and girls in the IDP camps. The counsellor together with community mobilizers will sensitize the community and provide basic psychological support to the GBV survivors, early or forced marriage and vulnerable girls. •Provision of material assistance (dignity kits; solar lanterns). Sustaining dignity is necessary for maintaining self-esteem and confidence, all of which are necessary for coping in stressful and possibly overwhelming situations. Women and girls’ self-esteem and confidence can be boosted, which makes it easier for them to care for and protect themselves and their children. Hence, WRRS aims at having GBV survivors and especially women and girls) and vulnerable women and girls receive the dignity kits. The kit will include, Dress (Dirac) Shawl (Garbasar), Sanitary Cloth, Panties (Sizes), Bar Soap (250 gms), Pack of Power Soap (100 gms) Petticoats (Gorgorat), Head Scarves (Shash). •Survivor-centered multi-sectoral support (medical, legal, PSS, and safety) and case management. WRRS will cover the costs of transportation for GBV survivors women and girls to get compassionate, competent, and confidential clinical care and follow up on services in all of the project's target regions. •WRRS will offer funds for protection to women and girls GBV survivors and other vulnerable women in order to reduce GBV risks and contribute to their safety and dignity of women, girls, and anyone at danger of GBV. The Cash Voucher Assistance to target beneficiaries in Lower Juba and Bay regions will be based on case-by-case approach. This is designed to prevent a further risk to the victims and survivors of GBV, Rape and Sexual assault. The initiative is further anticipated to offer social-economic reintegration, access to justice, and empowerment (livelihood; legal support and cash-based assistance) •Case management and psychosocial support of vulnerable groups(women, girls, boys and men) Lower Juba and Bay regions. The target populations will be provided with access to comprehensive case management and psychosocial support (PSS), which includes emergency care and referral to medical teams trained in clinical rape management (CMR). WRRS will recruit additional case workers to support the current personnel to ensure that there is maximum utilization of the synergies. With the survivor's cooperation, follow-up will be undertaken to ensure that survivors of GBV are progressing well and that they are receiving excellent assistance from other service providers, as well as sending them to medical health centres for further treatment if necessary.WRRS will conduct training for monitors to conduct monitoring on displacements in the two target regions. WRRS will conduct safety audits/mapping exercise in the two target regions. The organization will also establish a complaint and feedback mechanisms that will be used by the beneficiaries to report on any protection concerns during the project implementation.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

WRRS will work with the minority groups to ensure that they are not excluded from the project implementation, they will be involved in project decision making from the onset people living with disabilities will not be left behind in the project implementation.The organization is well versed with the risks associated with SEA and therefore will work closely with all the stakeholders to ensure that the beneficiaries are not exposed to any form of sexual exploitation and especially during the delivery of project activities. Awareness raising on how to report the incidences of SEA will be done to all the organization stakeholders that is the staff, contractors and otherstakeholders involved with the activities of the organization. A reporting mechanism for PSEA will be established to look into any issues related to Sexual exploitation during and even after the end of the project. WRRS will ensure that all groups of beneficiaries are involved from the onset of projects, normally in Somalia people from marginalized clans and women have been left out in key decision making process and mostly they are not consulted. This is not going to happen for this project as WRRS will ensure thatall the minority and marginalized persons will be involved in major decision making of the project. WRRS will establish a complaint and feedback mechanism that will be accessible to all the beneficiaries in the project target area. WRRS work cases of explosives are rare although measures will be put in place to prevent the risk of explosive hazards in the target area.
