Project Summary

Somalia is facing a Triple Threat of Desert Locust, flooding’s and the global pandemic COVID-19 contributing towards the deterioration of production outputs, social economics increasing vulnerabilities of households. These culminations result in an impact on the states of acute malnutrition situations affecting children and women. Already, vulnerable communities due to persistent climatic shocks, locust infestation, and the COVID-19 pandemic, are facing severe food and water scarcity and are at risk of deadly communicable diseases such as cholera outbreak and acute watery diarrhea (AWD), With limited resources and funding, the humanitarian agencies in Somalia are overstretched and in need of urgent support to provide a timely and deliberate response. 2021 Nutrition cluster needs have been estimated 2.3 million U5 children and PLW of whom 1.14M need treatment services while 1.16M needs prevention services. The purposed activities of the nutrition project meant to address and meet the dire nutrition needs of the host and IDP households of Dhusamreeb communities. The project activities will prioritize lifesaving preventive nutrition services for vulnerable population groups focusing on improving nutritional status through the provision of supplementary nutrition products, appropriate infant and young child feeding practices in an emergency, and micronutrient interventions. The project will also focus on the provision of equitable access to quality lifesaving curative nutrition services among boys and girls (06-59) months & PLW through systematic identification, referral, and treatment of acutely malnourished cases. The project will be integrated and work closely with others clusters such as health, WASH and protection, by mainstreaming protection into the nutrition programs. This project will be working closely by protection sectors through information sharing, referral and community awareness campaigns, this project will consistently report to protection cluster and participate meetings conduct by protection cluster. The proposed project will target IDP and host communities of Dhusamareeb district of Galgaduud region of GAMUDUG state.

Mitigation Measures

Mitigation measures for the Risk of women, girls, and boys exposed to sexual violence including SEA when accessing humanitarian assistance include:-1. Project will ensure the location and access routes to health/nutrition facilities and services are safe.2. Project will ensure that the health/nutrition services are respectful and inclusive of cultural and religious practice 3. Project will Ensure the location, distance and access routes to the health/nutrition facilities and services are accessible to all members of the community, including the most vulnerable groups. Mitigation for the Risk that marginalized groups, persons with disabilities, women, boys, and girls do not participate in consultation or decision making process for the project or are excluded from humanitarian services/assistance/activities include:- 1. Project will Identify and prioritize the most vulnerable groups in the community and prevent discrimination or exclusion of marginalized groups.2. Project will ensure Introducing special arrangements for persons unable to access health/nutrition facilities eg. mobile health services, transportation services, facilitate accompaniment arrangements (e.g. youth groups assisting the elderly/persons with a disability; community networks).3. Ensure that beneficiaries know of available nutrition services and assistance where/how to obtain it. Mitigation of Risk of interference from external stakeholders in the impartial & needs-based selection of beneficiaries thus negatively affecting social cohesion and sparking intercommunity conflict over access to resources:-1. Project will be transparent with the affected populations by providing them with accessible and timely information on selection criteria for targeted assistance, organizational procedures, and processes that affect them.2. Project will engage the community and committee representative to play an active role in identifying solutions and in the decision-making processes that affect them, so as to promote a sense of ownership, build their self-esteem and improve the relevance and sustainability of the response.3. Project will prioritize consulting with women, men, boys, girls, persons with disabilities, chronically ill, older persons, pregnant and lactating women, and marginalized persons to collect accurate information about their specific needs and preferences for health center location, design, and services
