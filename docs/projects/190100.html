1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project focuses on out-of-school children and those at risk of education disruption, forcibly displaced children (among internally displaced people), children within their host communities, and children with disabilities. It also focuses on internally displaced and host community teachers affected by the conflict. • A total of 80,428 (39,410 girls; 41,018 boys) conflict affected IDPs and host community children will be targeted. 5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

SC takes child protection and child safeguarding very seriously, and installs robust training and reporting systems wherever we operate. All individuals engaged with the project will be trained on child safeguarding and child rights. The teachers, and local leadership will be trained on how to identify cases of child rights abuses and resolve cases locally, and report serious abuse cases to their respective district referral systems. Feedback and complaints boxes will also be installed in all communities and schools for children and adults to report cases anonymously. The boxes will be opened twice monthly by trained committees, whose membership will include an equal mix of sex and age brackets (adolescents and adults), and if available, also include those with disabilities.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

The project was designed based on assessments conducted by SC and other partners including IOM in preparation for the 2022 HPC cycle. SC during project implementation of other projects in the targeted districts has been having discussions with the provincial and district government, local leaders, and with the provincial clusters to ensure that the project interventions are appropriately tailored to the target groups. Regular planning meetings will be used to report back on results of interventions, and feedback and complaints boxes, which will be installed in all schools and communities and will be opened and issues addressed and feedback given back to the communities on scheduled intervals. Boys and girls will actively, meaningfully and ethically participate in project activities through giving feedback during feedback meetings and feedback and complaints boxes. The feedback and complaints boxes will also serve to anonymously report any concerns related to project implementation (for example, concerns related to exploitation, favoritism in beneficiary selection, fraud), and including any concerns relating to the performance or behavior of SC staff.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

SC will collaborate with Forum for Disabled Persons of Mozambique (already an implementing partner under ECW) to share the already identified the average number and types of disabled individuals in the communities under the ECW project. As part of the project’s initiation, the team will also identify the families that have disabled individuals and ensure that they are routinely checked up on. The project will ensure that disabled school-aged children, to the extent possible, are supported and facilitated so that they can attend schools, and these will be checked on specifically during mobile brigades to ensure that they are receiving adequate food and health support at home. Issues concerning disabled individuals will be raised with the local leadership to help provide additional support to the families.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project will ensure that all masks and other related medical commodity waste were applicable will be disposed of according to Ministry of Health protocols.SC will work closely with the government line ministry and ensure adherence to environment considerations minimum standards.
