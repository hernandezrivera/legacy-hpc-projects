Project Summary

The Overall Objective is to address and reduce economic vulnerability of women and other marginalized groups in the crisis situation with linkages to social protection schemes, improving household food security and income replacement opportunities. The focus of interventions by UN Women will be on livelihood support and economic recovery. The women for these activities will be selected according to a set of criteria, from six districts namely Peshawar, Swabi, Khyber, Pishin, Nushki, Thatta, and Umerkot, across three provinces of Pakistan which includes both urban and rural areas. The government’s Ehsaas Emergency Cash program was launched by the Prime Minister on April 1, 2020 in the context of the economic hardship being experienced by vulnerable people as a result of covid-19 crisis. The financial assistance under the program is meant to help them buy food rations so that they don’t go hungry. The project will link up with this program. Beneficiaries who were not reached through the Ehsaas program will be selected, or those who need additional immediate support. • Based on existing socioeconomic impact of COVID-19, identify and link 10,000 vulnerable and affected women with programmes implemented by the Government at federal and provincial levels. They will be reached through SMS, Messaging and Direct Contacts in the field. Based on existing programmes under the Women’s Economic Empowerment Portfolio, identify 6000 vulnerable HBWs women and provide cash transfers of PKR 17,000 each equivalent to the minimum wage established by the government. Facilitate 500 micro businesses owned by women, to mitigate the impact of the COVID-19.

Identify 6000 vulnerable HBWs women and provide PKR 17,000 equivalent to the minimum wage established by the government.• Selection of responsible party in each of the targeted districts• Design a criterion for the selection of women HBWs• Selection/identification of women HBWs through a designed criterion through responsible party• Identify the disbursement mechanism (would mainly be through UNWFP LTAs). However, direct disbursement will be done where LTAs are not effective.• Disbursement of the funds to women HBWs in targeted districts Facilitate 500 micro businesses owned by women, to mitigate the impact of Covid-19 • Selection/identification of women home businesses through a designed criterion through responsible party• Support in development of business plans for the women businesses through responsible party.• Technical evaluation and review of the proposed businesses through advisory group/firm (mainly a third party) Identify cash-based interventions and disbursement mechanisms (would mainly be through UNWFP LTAs). However, direct disbursement will be done where LTAs are not effective.• Conduct rapid analysis to identify gender responsive cash-based interventions and mechanisms• Disbursement of the funds to women businesses in targeted districts Work with government service providers and communities to target digital messaging for women to fully access government schemes targeting the economic impacts of COVID-19.• Identify government service providers who would be linked with women• Develop material/messages to be disseminated among the women• Disseminate the information among women regarding the government schemes for their economic, social and political empowerment, and access to justice.
