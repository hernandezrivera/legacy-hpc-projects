Project Summary

The project will provide safe spaces for women ‘s psychosocial support.The project will also include GBVie case management to ensure quality, survivor centred, psychosocial support The action will also promote legal actors to support GBVie survivors to access survivor-centred legal services that protect their rights and promote their access to justice through mobile legal aid clinics for women to seek legal advice on GBV issues. Support for dignity kits will also be provided. Also key to this emergency response is ensuring the availability of quality GBV services for women by strengthening GBV referral systems and strengthening the relationships between the various GBV actors in the districts such as Victim Friendly Unit, Ministry of Health, Judiciary Service Commission and Social Welfare. Community based information kiosks will be set up to serve as referral pathway information points as well as spaces for women and men to access GBVie services information. The intervention will support men and boys and the wider community on GBV risk and mitigation in emergencies.
Theimplementation of this project will conduted in partnership and collaboration with other cluster projects and activities such as the Food and Security Cluster. Some of the implementing partners that will work on this project that include Caritas Masvingo are also implementing projects for the food and security cluster in some of the targeted areas.

1 - Activities

8 - Accountability to Affected People
8.1 - Describe how your project was designed in consultation with communities? How will you incorporate communities in project implementation and what feedback mechanisms do you have in place?

In the areas where Trocaire and partners are already, implementing communities play an active role in project design through developing of project initiatives as well as inputting into needs assessments conducted by Trocaire and partners. The communities are continuously engaged through ongoing monitoring on project implementation. Additionally community members are the key information providers for project evaluations. As such, their input into the intervention was incorporated through these community engagements. Trocaire and partners also have set complaints handling and feedback mechanisms. The partners and Trocaire had trained and designated Safeguarding focal persons who are responsible for handling community members feedback and complaints. Toll free numbers, mobile phones and community based safeguarding focal persons are some of the mechanisms that are used in already existing project areas and these will be replicated in the project.

9 - Environmental considerations
9.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

To counter the impact this project may have on the environment, Trocaire will adopt the following measures:• For dignity kits, Trocaire will explore environmental friendly consumables such as soap, re-usable sanitary-ware and bags and avoid use of plastic packaging for products. • Purchase of Covid-19 personal protective equipment will comprise for re-usable face masks, communal hand sanitisers among other consumables. • Monitoring mileage as part of monitoringTrocaire’s carbon footprint and making adaptations to transportation aspects related to programming where feasible such as car-pooling with partners. • Introducing waste management bins in safe spaces so as to encourage beneficiaries to segregate waste into- kitchen/food waste that can be used for composting (ii) recycling papers and cardboard and (iii) recycling cans/bottles/glass/metal and so on. • Promotion of use of water bottles for water during capacity-building sessions to reduce purchases of bottled water. • Explore the use of mobile and online platforms to distribute IEC materials to reduce the printing of paper. • Working with service providers such as printers to use recyclable paper as well as collection of recyclable waste from the segregated bins where possible.
