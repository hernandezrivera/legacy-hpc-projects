Project Summary

WFP’s programme focuses on preventing malnutrition amongst the most vulnerable populations through strengthening supplementary feeding with focus on the fi rst1000days. The project also targets People living with HIV:AIDS for prevention and treatment of acute malnutrition; The programme activities include: Screening for malnutrition and referral of severe cases for treatment, Social and behavioral change communication in favor of appropriate infant and young child feeding practices and other essential Family practices, distribution of specialized nutritious food to children aged 6-23months and PLHIV, promotion of the use of the blanket feeding platform for provision of complementary services including immunization, micro-nutrient supplementation, deworming, malaria and other childhood illnesses prevention, family planning, etc. WFP in 2023 intends to target about 44000 beneficiaries across the North, East, and Adamawa regions

1 - Besoins / Needs

The results of the latest nutrition survey (with the SMART method) conducted in February 2021 by UNICEF and the Government of Cameroon showed GAM rates of 4.8 percent in the North, 3.8 percent in the Adamawa, and 4.5 percent in the East with 1 percent, 0.6 percent and 0.1 percent of SAM for the same regions respectively. For chronic malnutrition, prevalence rates were in the alert phase with rates of 32.8 percent in the East, 34.6 percent in Adamawa, and 40.2 percent in the North.The nutrition situation of the CAR refugees living in camps and within host communities is alarming with a GAM rate of 12.5 percent and SAM rate of 2.6 and 3.4 percent respectively, indicating a significant degradation since the last survey in 2016 (8 percent and 7.6 percent GAM rate). In the East, Adamawa, and North regions affected by the Central African crisis, close to 82,000 people, mainly girls and boys under the age of 5 and pregnant or lactating women and girls, will need nutrition assistance. It is estimated that 76,000 children would need treatment for acute malnutrition in 2023 amongst whom 23,500 boys and girls will suffer from severe acute malnutrition. WFP intends to assist about 4400 beneficiaries with nutrition prevention and treatment services across the regions affected by the CAR crisis in 2023.
