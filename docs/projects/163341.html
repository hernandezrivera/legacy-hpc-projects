Project Summary

Activities:
Addressing malnutrition in 3 Health Areas of the Kumba Health District (South West) through screening, referral, and prevention as key elements at community level and by referring complicated cases of severe malnutrition to higher healthcare facilities (feeding centers, health facilities and hospitals), as well as by preventing a deterioration of the nutritional status of the population. In this respect, Solidarités International/Mentor Initiative will monitor and improve the nutrition status for children under five years and pregnant women, each out of their own competence.
A total of 25 CHW’s and 6 health facility staff will be trained in the use of Mid Upper Arm Circumference (MUAC) and supplied with Reddy to Use Therapeutic Food (RUTF).
Solidarités/Mentor’s aim is a target population of 50000 with in the Meme district, operating in three health facilities through a network of twenty-five community health workers and six trained health facility staff

Besoins / Needs

The crisis has led to a significant population movement, with 450,268 internally displaced and 203,634 returned according to IOM’s latest estimate (October 2019). On the Kumba-Mbonge axis, that hosts the Kumba Health District targeted by the project, it is estimated that more than 120,000 are IDPs. The movements are in progress, with 5,938 having left their home in July 2019, primarily because of clashes with armed groups and the military, but also because they lack access to basic services. Many have moved to the surrounding bush to their places of origin, making them especially difficult to reach, and most have not yet shown any intention to return. Their needs range from farm tools to protection, with health, education and drinking water prioritized by most when asked about their top-3 priorities (67%, 52% and 49%, respectively). Food comes a close fourth at 47%.According to MOH the target population in Meme district is 218967 people including 50% women and girls, and 16% of children under five. The nutrition situation of displaced persons and host communities affected by the conflict is of concern. The latest on data on nutrition from WFP’s Emergency Food Assessment (Jan 2019), revealed that proxy global acute malnutrition rates for the South West are 5.6%.Solidarités/Mentor’s aim is a target population of 50000 with in the Meme district, operating in three health facilities through a network of twenty-five community health workers and six trained health facility staff.
