Project Summary

This Early Recovery Project aims to strengthen resilience and improve the livelihoods of refugees from the Central African Republic (CAR) and Nigeria, as well as their host communities in the East, Adamawa, and Far North regions of Cameroon. These regions face prolonged displacement challenges due to conflict, insecurity, and climate-induced disasters, which strain local resources and limit opportunities for sustainable development.
The intervention will focus on enhancing food security, supporting livelihood restoration, improving access to basic services, and fostering peaceful coexistence between refugees and host populations. Key activities include agricultural support, vocational training, provision of startup kits for small businesses, infrastructure rehabilitation, and psychosocial support services.
By prioritizing capacity building, community engagement, and gender-sensitive approaches, the project seeks to address immediate recovery needs while laying a foundation for long-term development and self-reliance. The target beneficiaries include 25,000 individuals, comprising refugees, internally displaced persons (IDPs), and vulnerable host community members, with a particular focus on women, youth, and persons with disabilities.

1- BESOINS / NEEDS
1.1 Décrivez le projet avec les activités et leur mise en oeuvre, le lien avec les besoins ainsi que les résultats attendus / Please describe the assessed needs the project will address through its activities.

The regions of East, Adamawa, and Far North Cameroon are hosting a significant number of refugees from the Central African Republic (CAR) and Nigeria, alongside internally displaced persons (IDPs). These regions face critical challenges, including: Food Insecurity and Livelihood Challenges: Refugees and host communities struggle with limited access to agricultural resources, tools, and inputs, compounded by climate shocks and depleted natural resources.Lack of Economic Opportunities: Unemployment and lack of vocational skills hinder the economic self-reliance of refugees and host populations, particularly among women and youth.Limited Access to Basic Services: Overburdened health, education, and water systems exacerbate the vulnerability of displaced populations and host communities.Social Tensions: Competition over resources and economic opportunities has led to friction between refugees and host communities, threatening social cohesion.Project Activities and Implementation The project will address these needs through the following activities: Enhancing Food Security and Agricultural Livelihoods: Distribution of agricultural inputs and tools to 5,000 households.Training in climate-smart farming techniques and post-harvest management.Establishment of community-based storage facilities to reduce food losses.Promoting Economic Recovery and Livelihoods: Vocational training for 1,000 individuals in income-generating activities (e.g., tailoring, carpentry, and mechanics).Provision of start-up kits for small business ventures.Creation of savings and loan groups to enhance financial inclusion.Rehabilitation of Basic Infrastructure: Repair and construction of water points to improve access to clean drinking water.Renovation of schools and health centers to restore essential services.Strengthening Social Cohesion and Psychosocial Support: Organizing community dialogues and conflict resolution workshops.Providing psychosocial support and mental health services to vulnerable groups, including women and children.Expected Results Improved food security for 25,000 beneficiaries through increased agricultural productivity.Enhanced economic self-reliance for refugees and host community members through vocational skills and small business opportunities.Increased access to essential services, benefiting over 10,000 individuals.Strengthened social cohesion and reduced tensions between refugees and host communities.The project is designed with a participatory approach, ensuring beneficiaries are actively involved in planning and implementation, and fostering sustainable, community-led solutions to address identified needs.
