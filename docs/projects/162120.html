Project Summary

WFP intends to develop the following activities:
• Lead the dedicated coordination cell to strengthen horizontal cooperation among organisations and to enable synchronization of logistics efforts.
• Co-lead sector-specific logistics operations and facilitate access to technical expertise in order to improve existing processes.
• Maintain a preparedness and support cell to strengthen their operational capacity through capacity building such as training and simulations or direct advisory.
• Strengthen the support to ISCG and the Government of Bangladesh with advisory on logistics-related implications of cross-cutting issues and advocate for streamlined policies and processes that improve logistics performance.
• Establish a Information Management Cell (IM) and design tools to collect, consolidate and make available timely information on logistics intelligence such as market capacity, assets capacity and access constraints.
• Manage common service provision whenever resulting from a systemic constraint;
• Augment organization support by reinforcing operational resilience and prepositioning standby operational capacity.
