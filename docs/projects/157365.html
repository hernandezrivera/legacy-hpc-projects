Project Summary

The Darfur region has faced acute humanitarian needs gap, due to protracted conflict, inter-tribal tension. The Darfur conflict has continued to displace populations, which has caused many households to be food insecurity category (phase3) the humanitarian situation exacerbated by harsh climatic conditions, with limited rainfall that negatively affects household food production. The population of North Darfur is constrained by a harsh environment, low access to services, protracted conflict, sporadic inter-clan violence, and an influx of refugees from South Sudan to Eastern localities namely El Laait and El tweish.
Kalamindo is located south east of El Fasher Locality. It inhabited by several tribes: It inhabited by several tribes: Fur, medob,Hamar, Tunjor, Barty,Flata and many others. The main activities of the people are Agriculture, Trade and Animal breeding. Kalamindo is the most affected areas by Darfur crisis in 2004 and continuous insecurity situation. There is a huge gap in many service such as health ,WASH, nutrition, education, peace building and protection. According to the assessment result, Education is the one of the sector which need to be adressd. Therefore, NOBEL with collaboration of UNICEF is designed this project address the Education gap in Kalamindo locality through construction of class rooms, fences, offices, latrines, , capacity building for teachers, PTAs & At-Risk youth, provide livelihoods opportunities and provision of seats & benches , water tanks, nursery and recreational kits for pupils.
. The project is targeting 2637 individuals dis-aggregated to 835 male and 1,802 female.

Needs

Kalamindo locality is the one of the most affected areas in Darfur by Darfur Crisis in 2004. The war has breakdown and the result of war was several villages are attacked, burnt, looted and tens of soles killed, and people forced to displace to El Fasher and other neighbor localities .. In September 2018,a joint humanitarian Needs Assessment conducted by NOBEL, Save the Children –S,Oxfam America,Children care council SMOH, and HAC.The result of assessment showed that there is a huge gap all sectors specially in Education and it need to be addressed.This project will address the need. The needs including status of schools construction, education supplies, capacity building for teachers/students, PTAs and At-Risk youth, livelihood support gaps including pupils with special needs, water, sanitation and hygiene promotion gaps particularly for girls, recreational activities and awareness gaps including HIV/AIDs and disaster risk reduction.

Activities

Activities of this project include construction of class rooms D5x9meters, offices D4,5x5meters, stores D4x5meters, kitchens D3x3m, fences 60x100m and nurseries 3x3meters. capacity building for 100 teachers in teaching methodologies and EIE including psychological support, health and hygiene, mine risk education, disaster risk reduction and child protection and PTAs on school management, child protection, psychological support and disaster risk reduction and At-Risk youth in manufacturing of solid soil bricks (SSB) and construction skills. The provision of school supplies including benches & seats, teaching aid, water tanks (Tiger), hand washing tab, water pots, jugs, soap, pupil kits and recreational kits. The project will provide livelihood opportunities for pupils with special needs e.g. restocking of chicken, Cows,goats including provision of different seeds to product seedlings for vegetables, decoration, shadow and desertification reduction. The project will also pay education fees for the poor and pupils of special needs specially girls.Nobel will provide space for the staff, furniture (tables, chairs), assets (computers, printers, photocopy machines and scanners) for the project staff during the implementation phase. It will also use its good relations with the communities in Korma to ensure smooth implementation & community participation. NOBEL will coordinate with State Ministry of Education to render technical support to the PTA. The locality has committed to offer land to construct the school, pay the teachers & will continue to address any administrative problems that may occur in the future.

Impact on environment/natural resources

The project will use solid soil bricks for the construction activities to completely avoid cutting of trees and pollution of environment. Preserving of trees will contribute in natural conservation, climate change and combat drought & desertification. The nurseries will produce seedlings & provide tree seeds for sowing and seedlings to be used as belts to combat desertification. It is also providing latrines for the schools to avoid open defecation and reduce increment of flies and other insects that spread diseases.
