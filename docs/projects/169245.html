Project Summary

The proposed response is in line with the priorities set by the Shelter Core Group which includes UN agencies and the biggest Shelter actors in the country. The intervention includes provision of immediate relief efforts, rehabilitation of residential shelters, cash for rent to support relocations alongside referrals of affected children to SCI’s child protection team. Our proposed intervention builds on existing shelter experience in Beirut Mount Lebanon, SCI has been one of the main shelter actor in both Governorates Beirut and Mount Lebanon since 2016 responding to the Syrian refugee crisis and lately the Covid-19 response.

1 - Needs

Save the Children conducted a rapid needs assessment and some of the report findings include severe shelter damage leaving families living in unstable buildings, insufficient materials for repairs and lack of financial capacity to procure shelter materials urgently needed for renovations. Save the Children’s rapid needs assessment report notes that extensive destruction of homes, essential public services, schools, hospitals and private business will require huge reconstruction efforts. Therefore ensuring immediate and safe shelter for families and those who are most vulnerable, is essential, as well as starting immediate rehabilitation and reconstruction efforts.

2 - Activities

1. Immediate Relief Support:A. Debris Removal: SCI supports the Lebanese Red Cross in their ongoing relief efforts. LRC established a community volunteering network in collaboration with Lebanese University, local NGO’s and youth Initiatives, SC provides LRC with cleaning and protection material to be used by the volunteers such as wheel barrels, helmets, goggles, safety gloves, large brooms, shovels with handles, sanitizer and N95 masks to ensure COVID-19 protocols are in place. (confirmed funding/activities)B. Temporary Shelters: SCI provides LRC with 20 Emergency tent establishment (HH level) to be used on lands identified by LRC or government entities for HHs to temporarily reside for short term displacement (72 hours); or for the purposes of acting as a central hub for aid assistance and/ or distributions. (confirmed funding/activities)C. Sealing Off Kit: SCI provides in-kind assistance to the most vulnerable which includes labor support (through SCI local shelter Partners Delta and SADA) during installation. 400 Standardized assistance packages include a combination of plastic sheeting, different sizes of timber sections, plywood and ironmonger and tools to allow weatherproofing of external openings (door – windows) and installation of light partitions. SCI will target the most impacted area while flagging the vulnerabilities based on the Vulnerability Mapping conducted by Unicef, UN Habitat and UNHCR. (confirmed funding/activities)2. Rehabilitation of Residential SheltersA. Minor Repair: This activity is implemented partly directly by SCI existing shelter team and through our two local shelter partners (Delta and SADA). Assistance packages costs an average of 1000 USD per shelter and includes installation of aluminum windows, internal aluminum doors, external wooden doors, and light improvements to household-level access to water and sanitation conditions. Target: 210 H.H – 1050 beneficiaries (confirmed funding/activities)B. Major Rehabilitation: This activity is implemented partly directly by SCI existing shelter team and through our two local shelter partners (Delta and SADA). Assistance packages costs an average of 4000 USD per shelter and includes installation of aluminum windows, internal aluminum doors, external wooden doors, Electrical works, painting works and improvements to household-level access to water and sanitation conditions. Target: 5 H.H – 25 beneficiaries (confirmed funding/activities)3. Cash for Rent integrated with Child ProtectionCash for Rent: Provision of Cash for Rent for children and their families is based on thorough assessment and support provided in line with case plan and best interest of the child. A total of 30 households will be provided with a standard amount of $200 per month for a total of 3 months. This integrated CP and Shelter programme aims to support vulnerable children and their families who’s shelters are severely damaged and unlivable. SCI supports these families by providing Cash for rent support over 3 months, repairs to their damaged shelter or major rehabilitation if the shelter do not offer any structural damage, in addition to referral of cases (by SCI’s case management team) of families with children affected by this crisis to specialized services as needed. Target: 73 H.H – 365 beneficiaries (confirmed funding/activities)
