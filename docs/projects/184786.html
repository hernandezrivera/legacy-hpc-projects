Project Summary

The COVID-19 epidemic is also exacerbating gender based violence (GBV). COVID-19-related restrictions led to the termination of several GBV services. Women, adolescents, girls, and children account for 95 per cent of survivors who reported GBV occurrences in 2020, as per the 2022 Somalia Humanitarian Overview with 75 per cent coming from displaced populations.
Since 2009, NUSOJ has been leveraging on media and media resources to expand communication, advocacy and lobbying programs that tackle difficult socio-economic and human rights issues in the country, Female Genital Mutilation (FGM), being one of the issues. NUSOJ is a strong advocate of in coordination with partners working on sexual and gender-based violence in Somalia.
This project intend to continue addressing the Awareness , mobilization and advocacy, by supporting radio discussions and talk shows on the abandonment of FGM with; journalists, policy makers, religious leaders, parliamentarians and young persons in Somalia
NUSOJ will also ensure participation of people living with disabilities in panels (estimating up 10% of direct beneficiary) and also publicly seeking feedback from people living with disabilities. NUSOJ will directly engage up to 20,000 participants (50% female) directly.
