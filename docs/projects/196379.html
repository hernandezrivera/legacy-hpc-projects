Project Summary

The scale, severity and complexity of humanitarian needs have become even more extensive due to
the economic downturn, the rising cost of commodities, the devaluation of local currency; all
compounded by the socio-economic impact of COVID-19 and ongoing hostilities. This has created devastating consequences for already-vulnerable populations across the country, particularly displaced populations and returnees.
This situation has left many families unable to provide for the basic needs of the children, particularly during the critical wintertime. This is particularly true for families who have been displaced several times As well as families who returned to their homes
and continue to live in dire conditions.
To respond to this situation, SYCA will protect children and their families affected by the crisis and
displacement from harsh weather conditions through the provision
In-kind assistance (baskets of winter clothes for children)
The project is in line with the NFIs Sector Winterization Response Strategy 2021- 2022;
(NFI): Provide life-saving and life-sustaining non-food items (NFI) support; Eligible action
Winterization the largest life-saving activity in the country, this includes like fuel, heating fuel, stoves, and children's winter clothing
Jackets, thermal blankets,
The suffering of the targeted people in the proposed intervention areas in Al Sabkha and Maadan towns
and its countryside in last harsh winter showed that poor vulnerable groups especially returnees who
lost their livelihoods resorted, to meet their needs to adapt their practices to improve resilience of health
and health care to extreme weather, to use unconventional methods like burning plastics to warm
themselves. where indoor pollution led to several acute infections and negative impacts on patients.
infection of an asthma attack, irritation of the airways with acute respiratory. Indeed, the effects have been
varying by age, gender, geography, and socioeconomic status.
Moreover, the compound impacts of extreme cold weather events and COVID-19, vulnerable people
The project aims to provide winterization assistance to the most poor families to contribute to help vulnerable
With a focus on children who are more susceptible to the cold, especially those who attend school
people stay healthy despite cold waves will prevail the area of intervention as well contribute to
minimize the spread of infectious diseases.
Overall project objective
The project t aims to reduce vulnerabilities, enhance the resilience of the populations of concern to cope with the effects of the current crisis,
Baskets of winter clothes will be distributed to children from the age of /4/ years to the age of /14/ years, equally between males and females, as /7500/ baskets are distributed in Sabkha and /7500/ baskets in Maadan
The population will be targeted according to the following (10% IDPs, 60% Returnees, 20% and Host communities)
