Project Summary

According to the 2024 HNO, over 14.9 million people in Syria are estimated to need life-saving primary and secondary care health assistance in 2024, this includes 5.1 million displaced persons, 2.3 million children under five years – including 532,595 live births expected, 4.1 million women of reproductive age (15-49 years), more than 700,000 older persons, and 2.4 million persons with disabilities. Districts classified as severe (3) & and extreme (4) in the health sector severity have increased from 89 and 113 in 2023 to 122 and 118 in 2024, respectively. The devastating 7.8 magnitude earthquake hit Türkiye/Northern Syria in early 2023, with a devastating impact on the health system. In Northwest Syria, 67 health facilities were damaged by the earthquakes, of which six were destroyed. other regions were also impacted, it is estimated that the short- and medium-term recovery requirements total is 935.5 million $
The project aims to contribute to improve the functionality of health system and the availability of quality health services in Northwest Syria through covering the gaps in funding key health facilities and improve the capacity of the local health staff in providing protocol-based interventions. the project will improve the diagnostic and curative capacity in the targeted health facility through securing advanced health technologies and access to more stable medical supply line of standardized medication and consumable list.
The project will cover a variety of health facilities, including general hospital, specialized maternity and pediatric hospitals, surgical and internal medicine hospitals, primary health care centers, BEmONC Centers and specialized centers such as tuberculosis treatment centers, Blood Banks, Cholera Treatment Units integrated with hospitals, vaccination centers. IDA will enhance the networking approach of health facilities to reach more effective referral pathways and mechanism by maintaining the coordination efforts at the cluster level and field level, the networking will help to minimize the fragmentation of the service provision within the health sector.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The project will improve access of people with disabilities to health facilities through assessment of all supported health facilities for inclusion criteria agreed by trauma and disabilities technical working group, and through providing access to physical rehabilitation services in Azaz and Afrin districts. the project will also include cash and voucher for health targeted vulnerable groups with specific needs including but not limited to cancer patients, patient who need medical diagnostic and curative interventions available only in the private sector, people with tuberculosis and people with chronic disease. IDA will be providing C-Section in both of: Azaz National Hospital and Al Mahabbah Hospital, BEmONC services will be provided for 24 hours in Bulbul Center, including:Administration of parenteral antibiotics, Administration of parenteral anticonvulsant, Administration of parenteral uterotonic agents, Removal of retained products (MVA), Assisted vaginal delivery, Manual removal of placenta, Resuscitation of the newborn.
