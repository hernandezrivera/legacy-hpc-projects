Project Summary

To provide regular, reliable, and predictable monitoring of special multi-sectorial needs and conditions of non-displaced populations, IDPs, and returnees in both accessible and hard-to-reach areas of Borno, Adamawa, and Yobe (BAY) States.
This project aims to inform the five focus themes (armed conflict/displacement, camp closure and resettlement, climate change (flood), disease outbreak (cholera), and food insecurity/malnutrition) and the impact on the planning and delivery of a humanitarian response to crisis-affected populations in the BAY states.
Through the Information Management and Analysis Cell (IMAC) in Nigeria, the proposed activities are complementary in nature and draw on strong collaboration with coordination mechanisms as highlighted below:
A. Analysis support to inform strategic response plans and their updates.
B. Interagency coordination to support monitoring and analyzing the situation, needs, impact, and response:
• Displacement, and population movement monitoring
• Overview of crisis Impact: Drivers and aggravating factors, including impacts on people, systems, and basic services
• Conflict and access monitoring
• Emerging risks and vulnerable groups
• Current and forecasted priority needs
C. Establishing baseline and monitoring mechanisms such as updating and managing Common Operational Data Sets (CODs), 3W (Who, What and Where) response data, and situation and impact monitoring.
D. Interagency data preparedness initiatives to help adaptation as the situation evolves.
E. Situation awareness to support decision-makers with analysis of the humanitarian situation, impact, needs, and response.
To implement this project, MERCY CORPS NIGERIA will continue to work in close partnership with OCHA and the established Sector coordination mechanisms, and other platforms, to ensure that data and findings are shared as widely as possible, thus contributing to an informed, and effective multi-sectoral response to meet the most urgent needs of conflict-affected populations.
The proposed project will involve fact-finding assessments focusing on five thematic areas in year 2023:
• Armed Conflict/Displacement
• Camp closure and resettlement,
• Climate change (flood),
• Disease outbreak (cholera), and
• Food insecurity/malnutrition
Cognizant of the fluid context, MERCY CORPS NIGERIA will also provide rapid assessments capacity, using appropriate survey and data gathering methods to inform rapid response mechanisms and actors responding to newly displaced populations, and to contribute to informing evidence-based decision making and programming in relation to durable solutions.
Findings from the above-mentioned assessments will be shared in the regular information products such as Factsheets, Situation Overviews, Monthly reports, and Maps, as well as through presentations at sector and coordination meetings, whilst ensuring sectors’ buy-in into assessment tools and analysis, and the various products benefitting the wider humanitarian community working on the Northeast response.

1 - Needs

Since the beginning of the violent conflict that erupted in 2009 across Northeast Nigeria, the mass movement of people led to significant losses of livelihoods and fields laid for several years. 8.3 million people in Borno, Adamawa, and Yobe (BAY) States are estimated to need humanitarian assistance in 2023 (according to the 2023 HNO). The dilapidated environment has led to the eroded living conditions of the affected people, especially women and girls. As a result of access constraints rendering sections of Borno state inaccessible, shifting context dynamics, as well as limited assessment capacity of operational actors, currently, the Northeast response faces the following primary information gaps. Most especially on comparable data across all relevant sectors, crisis-affected areas, and population groups to inform humanitarian planning. MERCY CORPS NIGERIA aims to continue to contribute to filling these information gaps and extend to new areas of interest to humanitarian response actors, using appropriate survey and other data gathering methods.

2 - Activities

In the bid to support and address the information gap outlined above and establish the intention of internally displaced persons to no longer having any specific need that is linked to their displacement and further enable them to enjoy their human rights without discrimination, the proposed assessments is expected to provide a data-driven analysis output to support high-level decisions centered around durable solutions. The assessments and services will be designed and conducted in close coordination with OCHA, Sector coordination mechanisms, and other platforms. These will have a structured timeline including activities overview, and systematized partners’ review alongside core discussion around the methodology and sampling frame. MERCY CORPS NIGERIA will: • highlight thematic areas that are ready for a durable solution. • provide adequate clustering of thematic areas in accordance with the most likely scenario themes. • established document repository for future analysis.
