Project Summary

The project aims to improve access to quality health services to people in need specifically IDPs, Returnees and Host communities in White Nile ,North Darfur and Kassala .The project will support provision of Primary Health Services in 11 clinics across 3 states ,in Kassala state( Reifi Nahr Atbara, Reifi Wad Elhilaiw and Reifi Gharb Kassala), White Nile State(Ad Diwaim Jabalain, Guli,Um Rimta,Tendalti ) North Darfur(Melit,Saraf Omra,Tawila)The essential package of health services which will be implement includes the treatment of non-communicable diseases, ensuring the availability of essential medicines, provision and
expansion of the Minimum Initiated Service Package (MISP) for reproductive , neonatal, and
child health; and strengthening the Expanded Programme on Immunization (EPI). The COVID-19 response and mitigation measures will be integrated within planned activities, with emphasis on strengthening surveillance and reporting systems, testing and laboratory capacities, infection prevention and control case management, and Risk Communication and Community Engagement (RCCE).The project targets 2000 HH in White Nile Kassala & North Darfur .Among them is 1000 above 18 years old female 1000 male above 18 years old , 1500 Female age below 18 years old and 1300 male under 18 years old .

1 - Needs

According to MSNA (Key health finding ) report, in 2020 57% of households (HHs) selected health in the top of 7 self-reported priority needs. HHs that attempted to access health care in the 3 months prior to data collection reported experienced barriers to accessing this health care including: Lack of medicines at the health facility (70%),cost of services and/or medicine was too high/cannot afford to pay (66%), High cost of transportation to health facilities (26%), health facility is overcrowded (25%), and absence/shortage of qualified health workers at the health facility (25%). Further, COVID-19 affected all states. In all targeted 3 states White Nile, North Darfur & Kassala the worsening economy and protracted health emergencies have resulted in an increase of the number of people who do not have access to basic health services, and a decrease in the health system capacity to deliver essential healthcare to people. IDPs residing in and outside camps need sustainable access to basic health services. Attention to people living with disabilities, services for GBV survivors, and outreach services for remote communities and displaced populationneed to be strengthened.

2 - Activities/Outputs

The project will link to the health sector Strategic Objective Support essential public health functions with focus on strong primary health care. and Strategic Objective Strengthen emergency preparedness, response, and all-hazards emergency risk management& Address the needs of vulnerable groups who are disproportionally affected by health emergencies .The project will support provision of Primary Health Services in 11 clinics across 3 states, in Kassala state (Reifi Nahr Atbara, Reifi Wad Elhilaiw and Reifi Gharb Kassala), White Nile State (Ad Diwaim Jabalain, Guli,Um Rimta,Tendalti) North Darfur (Melit,Saraf Omra,Tawila) the services will be comprehensive include: Outpatients consultation services, provision of antenatal care, cleans & safe delivery attended by trained midwife, post-natal care and family planning, treatment and counselling for STIs, counselling for survivors (victims) of GBV, follow up of nutrition status of PLW and provide referral service (GBV & NUTRITION) whenever it is needed. All activities will be conducted with follow up the precaution measures from COVID-19, and during the implementation of each activities, Plan will distribute PPE. Capacity building of health cadre is crucial to ensure provision of high quality services , this will be core components through all the period of the project by conducting training on important health topics as well on job training .
