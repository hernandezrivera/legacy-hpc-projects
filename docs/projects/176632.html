Project Summary

This funding will support Burundian returnees from the United Republic of Tanzania and the republic of Rwanda through reintegration projects including, Housing Land and Property (HLP) and Shelter interventions. A community-based approach, including cash-based interventions (CBI), will shape the methodology to support returnees in Cankuzo, Ruyigi, Muyinga, and Kirundo provinces, based on needs assessments. To ensure that social cohesion is reinforced during the reintegration process, the returnees and host community members in the local communities of return in Burundi will be provided with critical HLP support and shelter solutions, contributing to reintegrate returnees and improving social and economic conditions.
The Project will focus particularly on the Housing, Land and Property (HLP) component, as a critical need to address in country, to ensure security of tenure and mitigate conflict level linked to land. This aspect will be covered through sensitization of communities to land right and land registration, and support in the process of obtaining a valid land certificate.
