Project Summary

Hajin is a sub-district that located in Abu Kamal district in Deir Ez Zor Governorate to the east of Syria. The area is currently controlled by The Syrian Democratic Forces SDF since 2017 after kicking out The Islamic State IS from the area after intensive military conflicts. The area, even before the start of the crisis, in 2011, was neglected by the government of Syria, and the public health and service indicators were relatively low comparing to other regions and provinces in Syria. The crisis aggravated the humanitarian situation and deteriorated the infrastructure and service delivery. The area of Hajin witnessed intensified and prolonged military operations that strained the infrastructure and available premises and facilities. In schools that remain operational, poor water, sanitation and hygiene conditions negatively impact education, child health and girls’ dignity, in turn directly impacting on enrolment and retention rates.
WHOSD has recently conducted a needs assessment report to better understand the current response and existing gaps and needs. The assessment was conducted in December 2019 in 4 communities in Hajin sub-district; Hajin, Bahra, Abu Hasan, Abu Alkhatir communities. An overall number of 21 schools were screened and assessed including the premises, WaSH facilities, and source of drinking water. Moreover, the availability of protection services in these areas was investigated. The key informants were the community key persons like teachers, local authorities, and some female leaders of women-supporting entities. Data collected about vulnerable people were separately analyzed to make sure that the project design will take their demands in consideration.
The importance of this project lies behind its impact to increase the school attendance rate, enhance knowledge and behaviors regarding several hygiene-related topics .
The delivered services will be WaSH to children in 17 schools in line with education, WASH standards, to raise the level of education and encourage return to schools. WHOSD will contract with contractor/s will execute maintenance and rehabilitation works to the WaSH facilities (latrines and Water sources). WHOSD will carry out hygiene promotion campaigns and capacity building to the education and administrative staff so they will be able to deliver this service even after the end of the project.
The program will take place in Deir Ez Zor governorate, Abu Kamal district, Hajin sub-district in the communities mentioned above. The project will last for ten months, starting from March 2020 until December 2020.
The project will directly target the following groups:
1- 5-17 aged children.
2- Their parents and care givers.
3- Administrative and education staff in the schools.
A focus will be given to the vulnerable groups to improve their access to the available services.
The implementation of this project will be reflected on commuaties by increasing student enrollment in schools after securing water and sanitation services and will improve the health status by securing access to water and sanitation facilities, hygiene campaigns, distribution of student hygiene kits, and dignity kits for teenage girls.

The assessment was conducted in 4 communities in Hajin sub-district; Hajin, Bahra, Abu Hasan, Abu Alkhatir communities. 5 - Implementation of activities

The activities of the project are divided into four categories:I- Coordination:- Coordinate with the humanitarian workers through the coordination platforms and mechanisms.- Coordinate with the NGO forum, WaSH cluster, education cluster, education cluster, and related working groups.- Finalize and sign an MoU with the relevant authorities in the region without jeopardizing WHOSD mandate to the humanitarian principles.- Hold introductive meetings about the project plan and its cruciality to the target area.II- Operational activities:- Draft a technical study and book of terms for tendering the equipment and supplies.- Prepare procurement and recruitment plans.- Recruit the staff.- Procure the required equipment and suppliesIII- M&E activities:- Undertake a baseline KAP survey.- Develop and implement the M&E plan.- Undertake an end line KAP survey to be compared with the results of the baseline KAP survey in order to measure the impact of the awareness-raising component of the project.IV- Program Activities:- WaSH:1- Rehabilitation, maintenance, and expansion of WaSH facilities in 17 schools in DEZ – Hajin.2- Provision of water containers to the schools and TECs.3- Provision of chlorinated water tracking to the schools and TECs.4- Distribute mini hygiene kits to the students in schools and TECs.5- Raising-awareness and hygiene promotion campaigns in the schools, TECs, and community.6- Distribution of dignity kits for teenage girls.

6 - Protection Risk Analysis

-Prioritize safety and security and avoid harm: WHOSD assessed the potential risks that might implicit into program activities, and the risk mitigation and prevention plan was developed cross cutting the program plan. WaSH facilities will be separated for males and females, education facilities will be rehabilitated to be completely safely suitable for the children. Protection services will be based on the fully consent of the beneficiaries and WHOSD team will not force the cases to join the program, rather, they will give them all information to be able to make their own decisions.-Meaningful access: The vulnerable groups are identified as follows: children, orphans, girls, people with disabilities, and women headed households. The services delivery plan took into account these groups and the facilities will be equipped with tools to improve and ease access of vulnerable people to services. Additionally, the services delivery plan includes methodology of reaching out the beneficiaries to inform them about available services and meaningful means of accessing the services. M&E team will monitor the vulnerable groups access rate comparing to their percentage in target communities, and if there is low access, the reasons will be identified and addressed through the program team.-Accountability: through the accountability mechanism that will be applied in the project which includes complaint and feedback tools. Moreover, the raising-awareness sessions will be suitable platform through which the beneficiaries will express their concerns to the service providers. The design of the project was developed basing on the beneficiaries demands and concerns which were identified through the need assessment report development process.-Participation and empowerment: The key persons in target communities will be targeted through FGD, and they will receive capacity building and raising-awareness regarding several topics, including their rights to claim their communities’ rights.
