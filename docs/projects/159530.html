Project Summary

The proposed water supply, sanitation and hygiene promotion interventions purpose to ensure that vulnerable crises-affected populations (affected by severe and chronic food insecurity famine, drought, floods and conflict) in Lower Juba (especially Kismayo and Afmadhow districts) in Bari region (Puntland) and in Awdal region of Somaliland gain access to basic WASH facilities and services. Besides ensuring the establishment, rehabilitation and improvement of water supply utilities, environmental sanitation and hygiene promotion by focusing functional public utilities and facilities and by promoting community led total sanitation (CLTS) to eradicate open defecation while enhancing hygiene promotional activities, this action will place emphasis on strengthening/ improving quality of effective WASH service delivery. By strengthening systems, this action will ensure sustainability of established facilities, public utilities and services. The action will target to develop and build the technical knowhow and capacities of the water operators, management committees and local authorities to effectively operate, manage, oversee and enforce policy applications in service delivery and effectually sustain the operations of existing functional systems. Further, this action will endeavor to engage the youth in WASH service delivery opportunities as a way of training and employment creation; where services will be required in the provision of basic O&M, construction of WASH facilities, installation of solar and pumps, garbage collection and waste recycling and management and any other appropriate technologies that would bring value addition at the different levels of WASH service provision. Further, capacity building in disaster risk reduction (DRR), emergencies preparedness and response planning of the WASH sector will be enhanced beneficiaries by particularly strengthening the coping strategies and mechanism for the disaster-affected communities. This intervention is therefore designed to meet the needs of vulnerable girls, women, boys and men, IDPs and host communities in targeted districts through a participatory process.

Project Activities

Activity 1 - Rehabilitate /upgrade strategic wells/ boreholes into community piped water supply schemesActivity 2 - Support rehabilitation of hand dug wells and install with hand pumps Activity 3 - Provide water supply and storage facilities to schools and health care facilities Activity 4 - Conduct water treatment at waterpoint and household level in disease hotspot areasActivity 5 - Provide disaster-affected households with emergency water supply through vouchers -This approach will be used where vulnerable communities are in serious water scarcity crisis or in emergency. In such situations, the affected households will be supported with water vouchers to help them buy water from local water vendors in their villages. An arrangement will be made with the vendors to provide selected households with a quantified amount of water and using a check-off system, the agency will offset the bills of water delivered to each household based on quantities delivered and signed for. Where water voucher system proves cumbersome, then cash transfer for a speficic amount of money to cater for household water bills could be considered where the money is transferred directly to the beneficiaries mobile phones through (EVC). The challenge with this is that sometimes the households may not use the money for the intended purpose given that they have the freedom. however, considering the dire water situation in most situations in Bari region and its neighborhoods, it is unlikely that the money would not support this cause. Activity 6 - Implement a WINS program in schools to ensure 3-Star certification (using the 3-Star Approach approved by UNICEF).Activity 7 - Implement CLTS in remote rural villages to ensure ODF settlements.Activity 8 - Rehabilitate/construct public toilets / latrines fitted with HWFs in schools and MCHs.Activity 9 - Desludge filled-up latrines in Cholera hotspot areas.Activity 10- Conduct hygiene promotion and sanitation campaigns in main villages including in urban areas. Activity 11- Train water management committees on management, conflict mitigation and DRR.Activity 12- Train water supply service providers, the government and water management committees on public private partnerships (PPP) approaches to management of public utilities.Activity 13- Train youth and engage youth groups in provision of WASH related services.Activity 14- Provide refresher training for hygiene promoters and outreach workers on hygiene promotion. Activity 15- Train community workers, religious/community leaders on CLTS.Activity 16-Train teachers/ CEC members on WINS and MHM in schools.

Mitigation Measures

To ensure sustainability and continuity of the program and subsequently the durable solutions process, the intervention has proposed to work with a local NGO whose capacity will be built to be able to sustain the program. ADRA will provide consistent guidance throughout the intervention to ensure that all key stakeholders and partners maintain a common understanding of durable solutions in the project’s areas of implementation. A protective environment will be ensured through prioritizing safety and dignity by among other issues, ensuring the location of facilities is within a safe and accessible area. The intervention has been designed to link to the WASH sector strategic plans and objectives for Somalia and Somaliland which aim to ensure that all vulnerable populations have access to sustained water sources, appropriate sanitation and adequate knowledge on hygiene. The following are among activities that will be ensure towards protection: 1. Support based on real-time needs assessments, capacity evaluation and appropriate interventions.2. Participatory community-led approaches to site selection for infrasctructure development and beneficiary selection.3. Provide required services in the disaster-affected locations to prevent populations from out-migration.4. Consider priorities of the affected communities in the prescribed interventions.5. Include women, girls and boys in decision making at different appropriate levels.6. Put in place mechanisms and structures that ensure protection of displaced communities.7. Apply an approved child protection policy in all activities.8. Ensure adherence with agency's approved gender-based policy and strict implementation of the same.9. Ensure adoption and the applicability of the do-no-harm principle.10. Conduct environmental impact assessment and analysis and apply in sensitive intervention areas.11. Provide a proper complaints mechanism well informed to the beneficiaries and stakeholders and instill confidence in information provision.12. Provide a plausible whistle-blowing mechanism to beneficiaries and stakeholders.13. Ensure equitable distribution of resources to all affected populations (where possible).

Please provide further explanation

This action will engage local authorities, water user management committees and local youth in the quest to develop the most ideal and sustainable solutions to maintain the WASH service delivery in the IDP camps and host communities. Only sustainable solutions will be chosen and implemented. Capacity building will not only emphasize technical operations but simple business management procedures. If properly executed, these WASH services will be operated as be income-generating enterprises and therefore self-sustaining. Local governance structures will be closely involved to ensure support and ownership. Working in collaboration with relevant local and state authorities, youths provided with tailor-made short-term vocational training courses which will make them useful in the society and crucial in essential WASH services provision, which is the missing link in sustainability equation. Linkage with suitable supply chain with be supported to ensure that established enterprises remain relevant in the market and can address emerging needs in good time. Besides life-saving interventions, other water supply utilities that will be developed and supported will be strategic ones that offer durable solutions for the local communities. The project will also support social and economic inclusion and engagement that are important for consolidating state-building efforts while supporting sustainable peace and development. If fully supported, a great number of adolescents and youth are poised to benefit if these initiatives that are not only cost-effective but also relevant to basic local livelihood needs of the community. Efforts will also be made to the state authorities to grout the achievements in policy and ensure proper enforcement within the legal frameworks to ensure sustainability of such programs. The project will be registered with MOPIC with whom all the details including budget, activities and outputs will be shared. Activities will have to be aligned to the different States Strategic Development Plans. ADRA will work closely with Ministry of Water, Education and Health and involve them in different aspects of the project where relevant to ensure ADRA accountability. MOPIC will also be involved wherever a survey is to be conducted. Working with respective ADSRA will alert health practitioners where heavily contaminated water is encountered and the organization will work closely with the health department where cholera cases are confirmed. ADRA will also present the projects progress periodically at the cluster meetings of the different relevant ministries. Further, ADRA will be open to peer-monitoring and monitoring by OCHA. At these monitoring meetings, the relevant authorities can always be invited to provide their opinion also about ADRA work which will enhance their involvement and accountability on ADRA side.
