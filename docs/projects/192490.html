Project Summary

The purposed intervention takes a holistic approach responding to the economic crisis affecting the learning of children all over Lebanon. Plan will collaborate with LOST to address the educational needs of children by targeting the most vulnerable Leb children in North Beqaa with a focus on out of school, children that dropped out or shifted to public schools to access or remain in formal education. The intervention will be adapted in line with covid-19 measures to ensure children are safe and protected. Caregivers will be supported by addressing both their financial and emotional burden. Also Plan and LOST will be working on supporting the formal education system, to ensure that students are provided with an inclusive and quality education while ensuring schools have the needed basic conditions and resources to adhere to the influx of students transitioning from private education. The project two main objectives are: Obj 1: Direct community support to support the most vulnerable children to ensure continued learning. Plan’s programs are based on a needs bases with a strong emphasis on learning component to ensure that the most vulnerable children educational needs are met. Plan will coordinate with all educational stakeholders in the area to fully map out the most vulnerable areas in North Beqaa. Plan and LOST will be working on addressing both financial and social emotional burden. Plan has already a strong cash component under its programming and has all the developed guidelines and SOPs established to support families in need. Also, awareness sessions will be provided for caregivers to increase their soft skills while also focusing on decreasing their social emotional burdens using an internally developed curriculum “Parenting skills for protection”. In addition to that, Plan and LOST will support in addressing other educational challenges through a strong referral pathway internally and externally to other service providers such as Basic Assistance and FSS, Protection, WaSH and Health. The Second Obj 2: Improving Formal Education of most vulnerable Lebanese and migrant children through schools. This objective will be focusing on schools and ensuring that all aspects such human and operational resources are fully functional to ensure a protective and inclusive environment for all children. Plan under the Beirut blast response was able to distribute close to 1000 education kits and 735 Laptops to children affected by the Beirut blast. Under this project, Plan will distribute 16700 education kit and 37 school kits in September 2021 to the identified schools and student in BML in coordination with the Beirut District. Finally, schools that have seen major influx from private to public schools are in need of cash assistance to support the transition, PLAN and LOST will coordinate with both MEHE, UNICEF and the Education regions in the areas on the number of schools that are in need of cash assistance. The project target group: 1)Leb and migrant students at risk of dropping out of school 2)Students with high level of learning loss in the last 2 years due to inequitable access to remote learning opportunities 3)OOS children and adolescents and at risk of negative coping mechanism 4)Students transitioning from private to public schools. 5)Schools with the least capacity to support transferring students from private schools based on the total number of existing students and teacher to student ratio. 6)Teachers living and working in identified most vulnerable area. 7)Teachers working in public schools receiving significant numbers of students transferring from private schools.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

Obj 1: The service mapping (3Ws) will be updated to include all sectors service providers and geographical coverage, also recruitment and training of local volunteers and staff will also take place, in addition to conducting awareness session to identified parents and caregivers. Families eligible for cash assistance will be mapped out and registered, also awareness on the purpose of the cash assistance will be provided ahead of the distribution.On the remote learning support, recruiting and training volunteers on the remote learning, and providing awareness session to identified parents and caregivers on positive parenting, SEL and making referrals to parents/caregiver to basic need services.Obj 2: Consulting with students/families and school principals on the needs of the kits and its content then procuring the needed kits and distributing them. Mapping out schools that need of customized learning support programs. Provide tablets and rechargeable cards to children in need and provide catch up session for children. On Supporting teaching availability and capacity to manage the large increase in Lebanese students transferred from the private sector to the public sector, Plan will coordinate with UNICEF on schools with the biggest influx, map out these schools and assess their capacity from the influx and support identified teachers and schools with cash assistance.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Plan International integrates PSEA and child safeguarding in programs through policies which provide staff and partners mandatory requirements, awareness and trainings for the protection of children, staff and program participants from violence, abuse and sexual exploitation. Plan also adopts a gender transformative approach to ensure gender equality and inclusion and aims to reshape unequal gender and power relations to realize girls’ rights and equality across children, youth and adults. The intervention approach includes gender and inclusion analysis, using Gender Transformative Marker to identify areas to be strengthened tackling gender inequality & exclusion. Project activities ensure PwD accessibility, sensitization of staff, partners, and community committees on Plan’s Disability Inclusion training package including identification and mitigation of barriers to physical and psychological inclusion. Data disaggregation include disability, gender, age, and diversity considerations.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

To ensure Accountability to Affected Population, Plan prepared an age- and gender- appropriate feedback and complaint mechanism (FCM) toolbox. All Plan International partners are trained on Plan's FCM. At the beginning of project implementation, FCM is adapted, through consultations with partners, children and project participants to select appropriate channels, to fit the purpose and context of the project (geographic location, COVID-19 situation), and to be aligned with existing mechanisms within the partner organization. In case of remote implementation (following covid-19 restrictions), the main channels used include hotline, WhatsApp and surveys. Following consultations, a process will be set to ensure tracking feedback and complaints, referring to relevant focal persons and closing the feedback loop in a timely manner. Additionally, Plan will conduct a FCM survey with all the project beneficiaries to assess their satisfaction with the services and assistance provided
