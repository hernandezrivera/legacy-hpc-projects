Project Summary

Under this project, NCA will provide multi-purpose cash assistance to the crisesand earthquake affected population and to the most vulnerable groups in Aleppo, Rural Aleppo, Idlib, Hama and Rural Damascus to helps people to prepare, prioritize and take care of their families based on their own preferences and decisions. The main objectives of the assistance will be (1) meet the immediate food needs of the most vulnerable households thereby stabilizing the situation and preventing households from resorting to negative coping strategies, (2) restore household productive capacity so that households can gradually move from dependency to self-sufficiency. (3) support the most vulnerable households in maintaining safe accommodation and house rent. And to (4) restore and rehabilitate their houses affected by the earthquake.
Households affected by crises or earthquake are provided with MPCA resources as intended, they will reduce negative coping mechanisms as they will have an increased ability to meet basic needs. The latter will lead to a reduction in protection risks while enabling them to pursue income generating activities to live with safety and dignity
The assistance will be provided through identified financial service providers in line with Cash Working Group recommendations for transfer value and frequency of transfers.
