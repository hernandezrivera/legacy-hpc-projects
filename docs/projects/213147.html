Project Summary

This Action is built on activities implemented in 2023, the Protection Sector strategy and priorities, and in complementarity with WWGVC education intervention, as protection risks are one of the main factors preventing access and retention in school. According to HNO2024, 15.3 million Syrians are need of protection support, out of which 45% are children! 13 years of a multi-faceted crisis including last year's Earthquake, and the worsening financial crisis, is causing 36% of the households to feel unsafe. This goes along with increased rates of violations against children and adolescents. The compounded crisis in Syria is endangering the mental and physical health of children and youth, who are more likely to show early signs of psychological distress (31% of boys, and 34% of girls according to the 2024 HNO). In this context, protection risks are even more pronounced in CwD and with special needs, who incur the greatest risk of exclusion from social life. The project aims at developing and strengthening a Protection response in Aleppo, Deir-Ez-Zor and Raqqa governorates. Interventions will be conducted in accessible areas under GoS where WWGVC is already present and implementing Education interventions. The foreseen activities will include the training of educational staff, and community-based CP activities focusing on a diversified range of protection needs and tailored to different groups based on age, gender, social role, and responsibilities.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

The project aims at developing and strengthening a Protection response mainly targeting accessible subdistricts of Aleppo, Deir-Ez-Zor, and Raqqa governorates (under GoS) - where WWGVC is already present and implementing Education activities - which fall under severity score 3 to 5. The action foresees community-based CP activities focusing on a diversified range of protection needs and tailored to different groups based on age, gender, social role, and responsibilities. WWGVC will carry out training, events, and awareness initiatives (in schools and public safe spaces) as follows:- Training of 500 teachers on PSS, Inclusive Education, Early Marriage, Violence, etc.. This activity is integrated within WWGVC Capacity Building component for Educators under the Education program.- Group counseling/PSS support for 500 teachers and education personnel, in which they will be able to learn and experiment with strategies to manage stress and strengthen resilience and learn methodologies to support their pupils in managing stress and trauma.- School awareness campaigns on CP and social inclusion of CWDs: teachers trained in CP will conduct a campaign targeting 25,000 students; facilitated by WWGVC staff, SARC. and/or DoE volunteers.- Community-based PSS events: 10,000 vulnerable children at risk of school drop-out will be reached with tailored PSS events implemented with trained SARC volunteers‘ support.- Community-based awareness sessions on CP and social inclusion of CWDs: 5000 community members (mainly educators and parents) will be reached by awareness-raising activities facilitated by WWGVC staff, SARC and/or DoE volunteers.Activities will be implemented in close cooperation with the MoE, Doe, and SARC. Training for teachers on PSS, IE, and other relevant topics, will be conducted with the support of DoE. School-based campaigns will be mainly delivered by trained teachers, who will share the contents with the students through a cascade effect. All the activities will be structured in a way to adapt to different target groups of beneficiaries and to the specific context (e.g. rural/urban) and social dynamics of the community where they take place. Outreach activities at community level will be conducted through door to door visits by trained volunteers from the DoE. All project activities will consider the needs of males and females to ensure at least a 50/50 gender participation.
