Project Summary

The projects interventions shall include: advocacy and campaigns to promote girl child education, involvement of men in advocacy to end violence against women and girls. Raising awareness, develop and mainstream, monitor with the community referral systems, lobby for the support of legislature towards revising policies in line with the protection of marginalized groups, conducting dialogues with communities as a means to mitigate these social challenges, identify and train the identified Psychosocial Focal Point (PFP) persons to support with life skills education and psychosocial support among communities at all times and following up referral cases (case management) within the community.

The Project intended impact in the community shall include transformed community’s knowledge on SGBV, reporting SGBV incidences, importance of reporting SGBV, referral path ways of SGBV, mitigation in GBV incidences, Legal implications of perpetrating SGBV, negative implications of not reporting SGBV on the survivors and the perpetrators. Building local leaders capacity in early detection of S/GBV, case management and case referrals, promotion of better participation of women in community net works
Furthermore, changes in gender division of labor within the household, changes in social attitudes to women in positions of leadership, Influence of women/men in community decision-making, opinions of women/men consulted in setting peace-building priorities & strategies, peace-building initiatives address the needs of men/women, implementing staff/CBO partners/local authorities shall be trained on social norms, gender equity, equality, discrimination and human rights. Ratio of women/men in decision-making in peace processes, Women’s participation in decision-making structures, Implementation of international agreements on women’s rights by the local authorities and states legislatures drafting policies.

1 - Needs

Assistance Mission for Africa (AMA) is proposing to work with women and girls in Rumbek East, to restore their dignity. It will be working in establishing women and girls friendly spaces. These will serve as neutral forums where they can confidently share their stories and they can as well get psychosocial first aid (PFA) among other services. Women and girls have been disproportionately affected by GBV, including domestic violence, community members, gang rape by uniformed personnel, un-ascertained militants and armed groups. AMA’s operational presence in the target areas and trends analysis indicates that rape and sexual violence have been increasing threats and that intimate partner violence and sexual exploitation are becoming more acute as the insecurity continues. Women reported having developed secondary GBV complications as a result of untimely response to GBV occasioned by lack of GBV services within Project Proposal’s intervention areas. The increasing protection needs far outweigh the available and limited resources for GBV programming despite genuine efforts of Country Humanitarian Team. The project is expected to provide direct primary and secondary GBV programming services and activities to local communities (see activity list 2). The proposed project will focus on GBV response through establishment of WGFS where women and girls will be supported with PSS, and Case management for SGBV survivors.Another component of our project is to carry out community outreach activities on GBV services to promote referrals from the community, in lieu with functional referral pathways to existing multi-sectoral and variety of service provisions among partner agencies existing in the area. AMA will conduct GBV protection assessments, gender responsive vulnerability profiling, safety audits and enhance GBV protection, prevention and mitigation of risks.With these interventions, AMA will be contributing to national Protection Cluster Objectives, GBV Sub Cluster Strategy and Humanitarian Response Plans Strategic Objectives.AMA will build on lessons learnt and promote a multi-sectoral response strategy, and cooperation with other partners in proposed target areas to ensure the rights, dignity and wellbeing of conflict affected women and girls are preserved through;a) provision of safe, accessible live saving case management and psychosocial services; b) Increasing the participation of women, girls, men and boys in prevention programming to mitigate risks of gender based violence in their communities; c) Do continuous GBV protection assessments and monitoring to inform and enhance women and girls protection and the overall humanitarian response.

2 - Activities

AMA shall carry out the following activities to address the needs identified above under this project and these are; 1. Raising awareness on Gender Based Violence in coordination with GBV Sub-Cluster. AMA shall identify GBV Sub-Cluster partners’ organization that will coordinate and provide information and activities of GBV to the people in need. 2. Develop Women and Girls Friendly Spaces: This project seeks to provide psychosocial support to GBV survivors in term of counseling in a Women and Girls Friendly Space. 3. Training Gatekeepers: Coaching and mentoring women leaders on gender equality on gender equality, equity and peace building. AMA shall facilitate training of gatekeepers on gender equality; equity and peace build to change their perspective on harmful cultural practices. 4. Women Monthly Forum: AMA shall be conduct and facilitate women monthly forum to address issues affecting women in the communities in support of eliminating gender base violence in the project location. 5. Gender Mainstreaming: Women empowerment into the leadership roles, lobbying local authorities support towards women’s empowerment in social and gender norms, AMA shall facilitate training to enable women have full participation in awareness raising and leadership roles in their communities.6. Training and Mentoring: Already instituted Peace Committees will be trained and mentored on gender norms, women’s roles in peace building and security. AMA shall conduct community dialogue, form women peace committees where there is no one in the project locations to increase women participation in peace building for effective community dialogue with women in the bordering communities. 7. Advocacy for Respect of GBV/SGBV Survivors and the rights and promotion of human rights at local level government, enactment of laws/policies that shall provide awareness on GBV to people at the grassroots and at state. 8. Radio Talkshows on GBV and Human Rights: AMA shall conduct nine (9) Radio talkshows in the first nine months of the implementation phase of the project. Questions and feedbacks from the talkshows shall be used for advocacy. 9. Training of Social Workers: AMA shall identify, select and train social workers. The social works shall be able to manage S/GBV case management, continue with awareness raising, provide counseling services to survivors. The documented cases or concerns will inform humanitarian respond and legal intervention. 10. Provision of GBV Case Management: AMA will conduct early detection cases monitoring and case ascertainment, case to case needs based handling (e.g. individual case consultations, case management, case referrals to existing local international, national and government services providers). Data will be compiled from case intake forms and analyzed to guide program implementation and harmonize the referral pathways, where and when needed; All field based staff as well as community mobilizers will be daily technically supported, and trained continuously. As part of the GBV case management, AMA will inform existing women’s groups on the activities designed and available through this component of activities.11. Provision of Individual and Group Psychosocial Support: AMA will organize and provide age appropriate and case based individual and group psycho social counseling’s and community based support activities. AMA will do regular training and mentoring to field based staff on psycho-social support (based on IASC MHPSS Guidelines), support for vulnerable groups (including persons with disabilities).12. Activity on distributing dignity kits: AMA will distribute dignity kits to women of reproductive age and adolescent

6.5 - Includes perspectives of displacement-affected people?

The project location being bordering to affected communities in the 2013 civil war and frequent cattle raiding in their home locations, other communities fled their homes. Thus IDP's from Panyijira (Unity State), Malek, Rumbek Center, Rumbek North are settled in Amongpiny for their safety. Thus the project shall have consideration of displaced -affected people.

6.2 - Is the project linked to government/community systems?

Yes. the project is linked to the government/community systems. This being the community base project, it shall work with the community structure like the local courts, community police, national police, Local Government systems ministry of local government for the involvement of women in local government leadership structures in the grass roots, Ministry of gender and States Courts to ensure all legal recognisation of beneficiaries rights, rightful measures to the perpetrators of S/GBV to accomplish it's intended impact.

6.3 - Is the project aligned with national objectives?

AMA in the last three years implementation of NAP 1325 (National Action Plan) under the UNSCR (United Nations Security Council Resolutions got strength from the different policies Child protection policy, gender equality and diversity policy, AMA code of conduct and formulated within humantarian country's team paper's response for 2019. Thus project is aligned with national objectives.

6.1 - Is the protection environment sufficiently safe/stable?

Being a locked up community due to internal and bordering conflicts, the environment is not safe. There are frequent attacks of cattle raiding, road ambushes. This hinders free movement to conduct any economic activity with Rumbek center, limited access to social services, un employment to educated youths, lots of violations of human rights due to cultural beliefs and limited knowledge on human rights. The beauty is internally the population are hospitable.

6.4 - Does the project include sustainability/handover?

The project is community's initative, therefore trainings shall all be to empower community for ownership/ sustainability of the project. In all the implementation phases the local population shall take lead and equally AMA field officer is a native and so shall the social workers and community volunteers. The communities asked have always been how to access S/GBV services, so the information they shall acquire will help project owned ownership. All Monitoring and evaluation shall jointly be done to prepare community of what shall be required of them to sustain this project, hence the inclusion of sustainability is in plan.

8.18 - Please indicate how the risks selected above will be mitigated.

This project will work with gate keepers to increase girls and women involvement . It will partner with media especially community radios to mount advocacy towards women involvement and representation. It will mount an awareness campaign within the community to raise awareness on referral pathways to enlighten the community on the existence of such initiatives and advocate for their use. It will make outreach using existing structures of communities to advocate and to involve women and girls on the project and all activities planned will ensure women and girls are involved and participate meaningfully. It will also build the capacity of women, girls, youths and engage men in advocacy in awareness raising and women empowerment. Campaign against harmful cultural practices. It will train the local authorities and women leaders on existing protections policies eg child protection policy, gender equality and diversity policy and AMA code of conduct. The project will also conduct civic education on importance of inclusion of women and girls in communities matters, incorporating into communities leaderships structures, teach them life skills, engage them in theater art, sports and incorporating parental guided and support to adolescence.

9.14 - How will you project ensure feedback from the most vulnerable community members?

Before the development of this project AMA did an assessment and also used information from secondary data. AMA will organize a launching of this project where stakeholders will be brought together to discus the project and how it will be implemented. There will be a clear feedback mechanisms for the communities to give there views on the project during the launch and after every activity is undertaken. AMA will ensure post activity assessment to get the feedback of the community. The local government authorities and all beneficiaries will have their say in an planning and review the activities after they are undertaken. This project is going to engage all gender (male & female) and across all age as community mobilizers and community volunteers. AMA will conduct assessment of persons living with disabilities and do regularly beneficiaries' satisfaction survey. AMA will communicate with persons living with disability in mode of their interest and receive prompt feedback. AMA's senior staffs will time to time guide and advise other project staffs in programming that encompass persons living with disabilities. AMA planned to pass all communication about this project in a simplified local dialects of the local communities in Rumbek East. During implementation, AMA will lay down clear selection criteria for project beneficiaries, ensure women and girls are actively participating in all sessions and ensuring that men and boys do not dominate discussion. AMA will create women and girls friendly space that will encourage women and girls to share, interact and discuss their issues without fear.
