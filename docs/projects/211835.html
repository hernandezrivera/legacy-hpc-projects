Project Summary


The main goal of the project (saving lives) is to reduce , as much as possible, people’s suffering from the costs of medical examinations and the costs of very high prices of medicins , as it will target vulnerable groups who are unable. The Association owns a medical center that includes many clinics.
Therefore, providing the basic package of services and healthcare including primary, secondary care, trauma and mental health services to an estimated number of vulnerable and displaced beneficiaries in Al Tal City will help these groups by supporting the Primary Health Care Center and the Physiotherapy Centre. That is, providing humanitarian aid to most people who are urgent need of it. There is also a mobile clinic where field visits are made twice a week to specific areas, providing primary health care to vulnerable population groups from those areas, and distributing appropriate free medicines to them.
As for secondary care cases, they are studied through a medical committee formed and approved by the Association from its members for the patient cases presented. The meeting takes place one day every week.
As for trauma cases, they are treated immediately because they are emergency and life-saving, by the director of the medical center
All referrals from the above (secondary care or trauma care) are referred to hospitals, medical centers, laboratories, contracting with the Association.
The Association has a physical therapy center where trauma care management and rehabilitation services are provided to patients with traumatic injuries
The Association provides services for minor emergency surgeries and burn treatment.
• Provision of assistive devices, including wheelchairs for children and adults, hearing aids, air pressure mattresses, crutches, and walkers.
Standards used to provide medical device service
• The beneficiary must be a live in Al-Tall City ( IDPs- host community)
• The financial situation does not allow the purchase of medical devices / their cost is very high
Needs: In recent years, due to the economic crisis that the Syrian country is going through, many people have become unable to pay the costs of primary or secondary care, in the country in general and here in the Syrian city in particular. The Altal. Most people need medical and nutritional assistance, as life has become very difficult. The family cannot cover even 20% of its various needs, but because medical needs are basic and necessary, and the family’s inability or inability to monitor its health condition will lead to poor health conditions and the spread of diseases, in addition to the need for various services. Necessary operations.
The Association has different accountability systems, through which affected populations can increasingly influence the type, delivery and quality of assistance. The Association maintains accountability and engages people through several ways, such as social media. Direct and transparent dialogue with the community, and responded promptly to their needs. Inquire and listen to their suggestions, follow up on their complaints, and take the necessary amendment measures if necessary. There are also complaint boxes and help office. As for the Association’s center, it is not difficult to reach within the city of Al-altal. It is also available for areas surrounding the city. Patients are received while waiting, through a room prepared for patients. We organize their appointments to preserve their dignity and safety and avoid harm. There is also safe and unobstructed access for people with special needs(disabilities).
Risks
People's lack of awareness of diseases or the spread of epidemics, and it is supposed to be solved by holding awareness sessions and hanging posters on the wall to educate them about this.
The increase in the prices of medicines and surgeries due to the change in the exchange rate, which in turn will affect the budget. This is resolved by focusing on the most urgent cases.
