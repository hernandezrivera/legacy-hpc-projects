Project Summary

The mass displacement experienced in the 3 BAY states for the past 12 years has become worse with the global pandemic of CVID-19. Again from January 2022, the situation will take a different dimension as the Borno state government is committed to the closure of all the IDP camps in Maiduguri, thus transferring the IDPs and associated pressure to already limited resources to adjourning LGAs such as Konduga, Jere, and most of the LGAs headquarters.
Understanding the pressure on emergency shelters and non-food items will have resulting from the mass closure of IDPs in Maiduguri, GREENCODE through this project is positioned to provide emergency shelters and non-food items to IDPs that will be relocating into Konduga, Jere, and outskirts of Maiduguri.
The project will focus on the identification of new camp-like settlements in Jere, Konduga, and the outskirt of Maiduguri, organize community mobilization by engaging both the host community and IDP leaders, training enumerators for registration of households with Shelter and NFI needs, procurement of NFI kits, emergency shelter land site clearing and demarcation, repair/re-enforcement old and construction of new emergency shelter, allocation of new shelters, conduct community feedback sessions, organize joint monitoring visits, conduct PDMs and project closeout.
The project will be implemented for 10 months directly by GREENCODE technical team.

2 - Activities

Since 2018 GREENCODE has been an active partner in the Shelter/NFI sector and has contributed its quarter to closing some of the gaps in MMC and Jere LGAs of Borno state. The proposed project is justified by the fact that all Shelter/NFIs assessment reports by sector, OCHA, GREENCODE clearly indicate shelter and NFI gaps.Similarly, understanding the pressure on emergency shelters and non-food items will experience resulting from the mass closure of IDPs in Maiduguri by the Borno state government. It is estimated that from January 2022, the situation will take a different dimension as the Borno state government is committed to the closure of all the IDP camps in Maiduguri, thus transferring the IDPs and associated pressure to already limited resources to adjourning LGAs such as Konduga, Jere, and most of the LGAs headquarters.GREENCODE through this project is positioned to provide emergency shelters and non-food items to IDPs that will be relocating into Konduga, Jere, and the outskirts of Maiduguri.This proposed project is aligning with the objectives of the Shelter/NFIs Sector and the NHF 1st Standard Allocation and in response to the continued assistance to new arrivals and old (displacement) in Borno. According to NHF 1st Standard Allocation paper, it is stated that the exacerbated vulnerabilities in 2020 as a result of forced displacement and movement, restricted movements and access, global health pandemic, congestion in the camp, and harsh weather conditions especially in the rainy season have led to increasing needs for basic Sector services both in the camps and host communities. Furthermore, the approaching rainy season projects flooded campsites, shelters and NFI damaged or completely destroyed and worsened living conditions. In order to alleviate human suffering and through the provision of humanitarian assistance, the CCCM, Shelter and NFI sector and partners aim to mitigate the impact of the rainy season and identify encumbrances, reduce vulnerability and address the accumulated and projected needs for shelter solutions and NFI needs for IDPs, including persons with special needs in the category of IDPs, returnees and most vulnerable members of the host community. The Sector approach and strategy of the response aim to in turn complement the COVID 19 preparedness and response by incorporating the health and non-health activities in the daily activities and providing services in the most applicable and appropriate way and considering challenges resulting from the global health pandemic.These project shall restore dignity to the total affected population though the provision of basic necessities meeting to the need of the beneficiaries.
