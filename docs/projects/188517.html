Project Summary

The project will provide integrated training programs for young people aged 18-25 who are not enrolled in schools and universities to invest their skills and engage them in society.
Section I: includes 3 training programs:
1. Vocational training 3 months (ICDL-designing - mobile maintenance-hairdressing)
2. Management training 2 months: (Project Management- MEAL - English – Secretarial -
Writing Reports - Principles of Humanitarian work) for male 60% and females 40%.
3. Community awareness and democracy workshops 2 months in parallel with
Management training, two awareness sessions every month for 15 beneficiaries in the awareness session.
Section2:
- First is to provide specialized kits in the field of training for 50 % of participants to open a workshop in which he could work and we will follow up for 3 months to ensure the success of the work and provide consultancy.
- Secondly: Provide opportunities to volunteer for 50 % of trainees in the management training in organizations and pay incentives for 3 months, ensuring that they have a good income and practical experience that will enable them to continue.

4 - Needs

Over 10 years of conflict, infrastructure degradation, and persistent displacement in Syria have exposed millions to ongoing economic shocks and deprivation. Forty-two percent of all individuals in northwest Syria (NWS) aged 18 and above have worked over the past 3 months. While nearly 4 out of 5 (79 percent) males have worked in the past 3 months, only 7 percent of females reported being in some form of employment. In addition to the multidimensional barriers that females face when accessing work, females are far more likely than their male counterparts to rely on short-term, or daily work (43 compared to 35 percent of males). Daily labor by definition is inherently less-stable and may also imply a lack of regulation, potentially exposing females to increased risk of exploitation.Of the 7 percent of females who are in work, there is less representation in business ownership; however, there is a higher proportion of females in short-term or regular work than males. These two work types represent both the highest and lowest of individual employment risks, respectively.The problem we are trying to solve is: A whole generation is vulnerable to lose and delinquency, especially among young (males and females) the reasons are: 1- The lack of secondary schools. 2- The di¨culty of returning to school due to the long dropout of education. 3- The lack of voluntary Courses that are concerned with young (males and females.
