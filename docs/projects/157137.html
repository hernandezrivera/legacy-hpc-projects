Project Summary

This project envisages to serve the educational needs of conflict affected boys and girls in five localities in East Darfur state. The major aim of the project is to increase access to protective quality basic education. The project will sustain local capacities and response strategies for the rights of education among children detected by (HNO 2019 targeting 4,200 conflict affected children & adults (girls 2158, boys 1822 (5-13 year ), teachers & PTAs and preschool mothers). The project targets children ( 5-13 years ) in mixed & girls primary schools and pre-classrooms in Adilla, Assalaya, Abu Jabra, Sheria and Bahr Alarab localities of East Darfur reaching 54% female, 46% males and 70 % IDP students. The proposed project is in line with sector objective of increase access to inclusive and protective lifesaving education (formal and non-formal) for children and youth affected by emergencies. Vulnerable children, especially children with disability will be provided priority of services and friendly learning spaces. This will be achieved through construction of semi-permanent class rooms to provide child-friendly education facilities to children & preschool children. The schools will be constructed based on standard design and specification of the MOE. They will be provided with furniture, teaching & learning materials, gender-sensitive sanitation facilities with hand washing stands and teachers’ offices.
UMCOR will organize five community mobilization sessions to enhance school enrollment. The project strives to improve the nutritional status of the most vulnerable children preschool and primary school age children and adolescents. Through training of preschool mothers on nutritional skills and nutrition sensitive activities, such as referral of malnourished children to nutrition centers, the project will work to reduce malnutrition rates & child mortality. In addition the project will support the establishment of child clubs focusing on health, hygiene, and peaceful coexistence. In collaboration with the State Ministry of Education (SMOE) and a NNGO partner, the project will build the capacities of teachers & PTA members by training them on teaching methodologies, school psycho-social support and school management. Furthermore the project will ensure the participation of the population in the targeted area in all stages of the project cycle. On the other hand the project will ensure accountability to the affected population and establish the necessary feedback mechanism.

Needs

Education sector response strategy in 2019 intended to focus on improving access to preschool, and primary education and to ensures that vulnerable children can gain access and retention to quality formal and non-formal education in appropriate learning environments. According to the education sector people in need (PiN) there are 90, 827 people in need of education services in East Darfur. Of these 57,216 are IDPs and 2,795 are vulnerable residents. The IDPs represent 96.6%, while the host community represent 3.4%. UMCOR Sudan draws this plan to respond to the most severely affected localities hosting IDPs and vulnerable host communities. According to the education sector PiN (people in need), the five target localities (Abu Jabra, Adilla, Assalaya, Sheria & Bahar Al Arab) host 39,451 school age children. According to HNO, the current the school aged children of these groups are at high risk of being out of school, which in turn puts them at higher risk of violence, abuse and other protection risks including banditry and forcible illegal migration. Providing education for IDPs and affected host communities is essential for mitigating these risks and ensuring the right to education observed. SMoE expressed alarming statement reflecting drop out for both boys and girls due to the economic degradation and poor pasture this year, which revealed that protection concerns will result in early marriage and drop out. The Emergency Department in SMoE, reported that all IDP schools are not connected to water sources. 46 % of the schools have no access to essential education supplies, and lack sanitation facilities, school furniture and teaching materials. In addition IDP schools the 5 localities are overcrowded. In almost all schools, there is one latrine for every 358 boys and one for every 300 girls. Poor water and sanitation facilities in schools have a negative impact on education, contributing to higher drop out and lower\ completion rates for girls. Teachers in these schools are lacking of the teaching techniques, life skills and psycho-social support. Moreover these localities are in need of strengthening the role of the PTA's in school management and EiE and psycho social support.

Activities

Activity 1.1.1 Construct 40 semi-permanent classrooms (8 at each school), 10 offices (2 at each school) and 10 preschool classrooms (2 at each school) at 5 Mixed & girls Primary Schools in Abu Jabra ,Adilla , Assalaya,Sheria and Bahar Al Arab,localities Activity 1.1.2 Construct 5 VIP latrines (10 stances each) 10 hand washing facilities (1 for each gender in mixed schools) Activity 1.1.3 Provide each targeted school with seats & desks, teachers’ office furniture , one set of teaching / learning materials( geometric tools/ colored chalks), and sport equipment Activity 1.1.4 provide 500 sanitary kits to adolescents girls in the targeted schools Activity 2.2.1: Conduct training workshops (in coordination with MOE) on teaching methodologies, school core subject, and ALP for 105 teachers . Activity 2.2.2: Organize training workshops on children’ rights and psycho social support Activity 2.2.3: Organize training workshops on school management, life skills & increase enrollments for 55 PTAs members in the targeted schools Activity 2.2.4: Support the establishment of child clubs focusing on health, environment, hygiene, and peaceful coexistence Activity 2.2.5 Undertake 12 school hygiene campaigns in schools involving the surrounding communities Activity 2.2.6: Training of Hygiene clubs members ( 50 )on conflict resolution and disaster risk reduction Activity 3.2.1: training 60 preschool children mothers on nutritional skills. Indicators and targetsActivity 3.2.2: installation of 5 millet machines 1 per each school to meet IGA activities
