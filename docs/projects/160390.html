Project Summary

ALSI a National NGO, currently operating in Yirol Ayod County , Jonglei State and in line with the HRP for 2020 ALSI wishes to seek support for the proposed project to support the food insecure populations Ayod County for the period starting from January to December, 2020.
The key objective of the project is in line with the HRP strategic objective and is in support of FSL cluster objectives to increase Vulnerable household access to food. The project aims support to increased food production and to reduce the level of vulnerability amongst malnourished children through linkage of nutrition and Food security activities.
The project will target mainly IDPs, returnees and vulnerable host population in these areas. A total of 20000 people will be targeted.

This will be accomplished through direct distribution of inputs or services. The key activities will be distribution of inputs, training in resilience building and vaccination and treatment of animals.

1 - Needs

The crisis and conflicts that has been prevailing in south Sudan for the last several years has continued to drastically affect the living conditions of many south Sudanese. In Jonglei State the resultant effect of displacements and conflicts has negatively compromised the community resilience to mitigate their ability on food security. The resultant effect has had a ripple effect almost on all sectors of livelihoods and if interventions are not done, the situation could deteriorate further. Chronic food deficits in the Jonglei states region have aggravated since the July 2016 conflict and the resulting state in the 4 year period has resulted in many families in dire need of humanitarian support. These counties are suffering a major food security and nutrition crisis that is driven by market failure and the depletion of livelihood assets. The in Jonglei state, the Global acute malnutrition exceeds the emergency threshold. These continuing food and nutrition insecurity is likely to result in increased chronic malnutrition and micronutrient deficiencies among nutritionally vulnerable groups. However in the absence of political and economic changes, the trends identified by the 2019 Integrated Food Security Phase Classification (IPC) are projected to continue. In the Ayod county, Jonglei State, the communities of Ayod , Kuach-deng, Kurwai, Mogok, Pagil, Pajiek, and Wau payams has continued to experience diverse and serious natural and human catastrophic effects. This has weakened their resilience in the mitigation of their livelihoods as many households are displaced, their means of livelihoods destabilized, markets have been disrupted, supply corridors have remained insecure and many have no financial capacity to purchase their basic needs such as foods or mitigate on their livelihoods . Majority of the HH are food insecure and many result to coping mechanism for survival. These IDPs and their Host communities and all the vulnerable people need livelihood support in order to cope with the existing situation as depicted by the 2019-2020 South Sudan IPC findings.Latest IPC report In August 2019, an estimated 6.35 million people (54% of the population) are classified in Crisis (IPC Phase 3) or worse acute food insecurity, among whom an estimated 1.7 million people are facing Emergency (IPC Phase 4) acute food insecurity and 10,000 people are in Catastrophe (IPC phase 5). Compared to the same period in 2018, there is a slight reduction in the proportion of people facing Crisis (IPC Phase 3) or worse acute food insecurity by an estimated 5%. However, high levels of acute food insecurity still persist in the country. The main drivers of this conflict and insecurity mainly by cattle raiding, displacement, economic decline, flooding , fall army worm in these areas.In Ayod County, Jonglei state, the latest IPC phase classification report from FSLC projection for 2020 Jan to April, documents a population of 82000 in IPC3 and 18000 in IPC4 and 0% in IPC5. However with 77% targeted for support in IPC 3 intervention is needed for 63140 , and 100% targeted for support in IPC4 & IPC5, i.e. a total of 81140 vulnerable women, men, girls and boys are in dire need of support for food assistance. This project will contribute to improved food security, income and dietary diversification combating malnutrition, disease and destitution by improving access of vulnerable farmers and fishermen to quality seeds through distribution of emergency kits and voucher based seed fairs (SFs). The programme will target county in Ayod, Jonglei State and will cover a one year timeframe (January 2020‐December 2020) and address the herein under defined activities

2 - Activities

Cluster Objective 2Provide food assistance to prevent famine and improve food consumption, dietary diversity and coping strategies for vulnerable populations (in IPC 5, 4 & some 3)Activities • To distribute seeds & tool to support emergency food production for main season agriculture to the vulnerable IDPs and host communities desegregated by gender, age and and vulnerability • To conduct emergency food livelihood support through distribution of fishing kits/ rapid response kits (fishing kits/ cow pea & three variety of fast maturing vegetable seed) to the vulnerable IDPs and host communities desegregated by gender, age and vulnerability • To conduct livestock disease vaccination prevention & treatment safeguarding the livelihoods of the vulnerable IDPs and Host communities desegregated by gender, age and vulnerability • To conduct surveillance, detection and prevention and eradication crop pest and diseases for the vulnerable IDPs and Host communities • To target Women & girls at least 50% of targeted beneficiaries;• To conduct training on improved nutrition/ land conservation/ reducing post-harvest losses/ improved farming practices. Cluster Objective 3, To Reduce dependency on food and agricultural inputs to support and strengthen households’ ability to absorb shocks: Activities • To conduct conditional food assistance through food for assets (FFA) to the IDPs and host communities disaggregated by gender age and vulnerability• To restore, maintain and build assets (eg communal land cultivation, training in improved farming practices, feeder road repair, flood protecting dykes construction etc.) for the vulnerable IDPs and Host communities • To conduct and support seed fairs using locally sourced seed to promote seed security &multiplication strengthening and market functionality• To train and capacity build both in adaptive and absorptive on-farm production through farmer field schools, demonstration plots, income generation business skills, and vocational training skills.

8.18 - Please indicate how the risks selected above will be mitigated.

To address protection of the vulnerable populations especially the women Children and the elderly, a risk analysis is usually conducted to inform and to weigh in the best options of inclusiveness. Targeting of the beneficiaries and site selection for field fairs are carefully considered so that food & livelihood support assistance given mitigates risks and ‘Does No Harm’ to these populations. Special messages with warnings addressing forced recruitment of children or gender-based violence are communicated to ensure that these marginalized and vulnerable groups access assistance; with an understanding that food or support provided does not exacerbate tensions between different social & ethnic groupsIn addition, helpdesks shall be established at each food/cash distribution point to allow for targeted households and/or other community members to raise complaints, ask questions or provide feedback.Optimization of inclusive participation – as part of the targeting and beneficiary selection process, communities will be consulted through community forums to ensure that all the vulnerable and food insecure families are included in the programInformation regarding entitlements will be clearly disseminated to ensure that the whole communities including vulnerable groups of women, men, girls and boys, elderly and disabled have a clear understanding of targeting, and selection criteria, ration entitlements and utilization standards. This will iron out dissatisfactions which could arise from simple misunderstandings. Distributions will start early in the day and the disabled, female headed households, the elderly and he children will be given special priority during distributions so that they receive their portions early to enable them travel home in time before dusk.

9.14 - How will you project ensure feedback from the most vulnerable community members?

AAP Accountability to Affected Populations ( NEEDS ASSESMENT , MONITORING AND EVALUATION and FEEDBACK )A needs assessment will be carried out before the project starts . All affected populations will be party and will be included in the PMC and awareness will be created and information given to the beneficiaries while at the same time gathering their expectations on what and how and when as they want for the projects. This will comprise of: how they will access their needs,  the type assistance to be provided,  how they prefer to receive assistance Translation of languages into the local dialect shall be undertaken as well as pictures and images with signs to guide especially those who cant read and write. To monitor the project implementation an M&E, Framework and tools and an inbuilt feedback mechanism (help desks for feedback) to evaluate the progress of the project implementation and impacts for all activities will be undertaken . This will be carried out together with the affected populations and in close collaboration with other partners, to gather information on relevance and satisfaction of beneficiaries. The activity will be desegregated by gender, age and disability i.e, women, men, boys and girls, the disabled and the elderly and will evaluate in terms of timeliness, input distribution, relevance of the inputs and services offered and distributed, quality and quantity of services offered and overall organization and modalities of service provision including security and protection issues. The feedback from the findings will then be used to streamline and restructure the project to align with the needs and preferences of the beneficiaries. The set inbuilt mechanism will be confidential, accessible and responsive and will give information to the beneficiaries about the existing complaint and feedback mechanism. The feedback gotten will be used to adapt, align and redesign project assistance to meet the needs of the affected population
