Project Summary

As the Anglophone crises continue to deteriorate, more people are being displaced leading to an increasing population in need of assistance. According to OCHA reports of 30 September 2019 1.3 million people are in need, 530 ,000 internally displaced , 376 ,000 members of host community. The current assistance provided is at very small scale compared to the fast increasing needs. WASH needs are most critical affecting a majority of the populations in the South west and intensifying in the North West especially as the crisis is intensifying. Ngoketunjia, Menchum Donga Mantung, Momo Bui and Boyo Divisions are the Divisions with critical need of WASH assistance as indicated in the WASH sectorial framework.
These divisions equally have a high population of IDPS living in hard to reach areas. Examples are in Ngoketunjia in Nkechi and Mbefru Islands, hosting about 1500 IDPS, Atui forest in Benakuma in Menchum hosting above 1000 IDPS , Terendem forest in Batibo in Momo Division hosting more 3000 people, Nkah and Nkuv c farm settlements in Bui and Ndu Sub Division . All these places have no potable water, limited toilets and no access to soap, women and girls are particularly affected as they lack sanitary facilities to use during their periods. They use leaves of trees as pad causing serious infections.
WASH cluster statistics of September 2019 indicates a total of 55,009 beneficiaries reached out of 102,780 people targeted with different type of WASH activities
It is within this frame that we intend to provide an integrated WASH package in hard to reach IDP settlements in Ngoketunjia, Menchum, Momo, Bui, and Donga Mantung Division.
The project aims to ;
Improve sustainable access to basic sanitation and safe drinking water to 2000 vulnerable people in Ngoketunjia, Menchum Momo, Bui and Donga Mantung Divisions through;
-The construction of 2 boreholes in 2 Nkenchi and Mbefru Islands in Ngoketinjia Division
-Provide WASH kits to 400 families to improve their access to WASH facilities in
-Extend and rehabilitate water by gravity to 2 IDP settlements in Ndu
-Train 400 families on water purification using Biosand filters in Momo. Ngoketunjia, Menchum, Momo Bui and Donga Mantung Divisions
Reduce the risk of poor hygiene-related morbidity and mortality for 2 000 IDPs in Benakuma, Momo , Bui , Ngoketunia and Donga Mantung Divisions by;
- construct 5 gender sensitive toilets/showers with hand washing facilities,
Ngoketunjia, Menchum Momo ,Bui, and Donga Mantung Divisions
- train 400 women on how to make soap using local materials and produce user-friendly, hygienic, reusable, menstrual pads
- Train and sensitize the affected population on hygiene and sanitation through community dialogue structures and focused group discussion.
Monitoring will be continuous while evaluation will be at each stage of the project.

Psychosocial will be provided, considering the nature of beneficiaries, majority being people who left the domicile and lost their livelihoods and are depressed.
Social cohesion through joint participation in project implementation will be mainstreamed considering that the targeted communities are made of IDPs from different localities with different behaviors and cultures.
It is expected that at the end of the project ;
- 2000 IDPs and host have sustainable access to basic sanitation and safe drinking in Ngoketunjia , momo, menchum Bui and Donga Mantung Divisions
- 2,000 people know and engage in good hygiene practices and have access to WASH facilities
- 2,000 beneficiaries regain their confidence are fully participating in community activities
- Number of conflicts in beneficiating communities reduced in all 5 communities by half within 6 months.
60% of the project target will be women. Reports will be written monthly

Besoins / Needs

SHUMAS conducted a need assessment in Menchum, Ngoketunjia , Momo , Bui and Donga on water hygiene and sanitation amongst other needs. Key needs identified included the following;1) Potable water There is no access to potable water in Mbefru and Nkechi Islands in Ndop, Terendem Forest in Botibo, Atui forest in Benkuma, Nkah and Nkuv in Bui , Mbiye and Palace site in Ndu . These communities host huge numbers of IDPS . Their main source of water are the streams and lake for the case of the islands. 2 in every 3 people suffer from water borne disease. The situation of those leaving in the forest is worst as they have no health centers and rely on traditional medication which are often used based on assumption. Activities to address these needs include;-The construction of 2 boreholes in 2 Nkenchi and Mbefru Islands in Ngoketinjia Division-Rehabilitate and extend water by gravity to Mbiyeh and Nkah in Bui-Train 400 families on water purification using Biosand filters in Momo, Bui, Ngoketunjia, Menchum and Donga Mantung- Provide WASH kits 400 families in to improve their access to WASH facilities in Ngoketunjia, Menchum Momo, Bui and Donga Mantung Divisions 2) Hygiene and sanitation facilities There are no toilets in IDPS these IDP forest settlements of Atui /Terendem and very few toilets in Nkechi and Mbefru Islands. Excreta is littered in open space with high risk of communicable and vector diseases. These communities have no functional markets where they can access to soap for hygiene purpose. They do not wash hands with soap after using the toilet, cannot bath nor wash their cloths . Their hygiene situation is deplorable women and girls are particularly affected as they lack sanitary facilities to use during their periods. No safe space to clean themselves. They use leaves of trees as pad causing serious infections. Activities to address the problems - Constructing 5 gender sensitive toilets/showers with hand washing facilities,. 1 in each targeted Division - Train 400 women on how to make soap using local materials and to produce user-friendly, hygienic, reusable, menstrual pads- Train and sensitize the affected population on hygiene and sanitation through community dialogue structures and focused group discussion.- Train and support women and girls on how make reusable sanitary pads, and making of soap with locally available materials3) Psychosocial support IDPS in the targeted communities are in serious trauma and depression as athey have completely lost the entire livelihoods and most of them have lost family members. Their living conditions are very deplorable. Absence of basic needs makes their mental health worst.Activities - Engage a psychosocial specialist - Carryout group counseling sessions - Carryout case by case counseling sessions - Refer extremely bad cases. 4) Promote social cohesionThe Targeted beneficiaries live in continuous conflicts as most of them came from different areas with different backgrounds and culture. The limited resources also courses them to have conflicts. Theft is common as some desperate people turn to steal to survive from their neighbors Activities - Sensitize beneficiaries on social cohesion - Engage the beneficiaries participate in the project by providing unskilled labor.- Train and install community management committee made of both IDPs and host to maintain and organize the use of the facilities provided
