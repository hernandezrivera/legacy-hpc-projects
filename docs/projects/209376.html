Project Summary

The World Vision implementation for 2024 is majorly guided by the Humanitarian Needs overview focusing on the current Sudan Crisis that has generated. World Vision will continue its operations in South Darfur, South Kordofan, East Darfur and Blue Nile. Due to the conflict that is persistently looming, World Vision has for now had presence in Al Gezira state and is open to extend its emergency and fragile Health interventions in any other state that turns out to be in need due to a population explosion due to IPDs and returnees.
World Vision is operating on the guidance of its current Sudan Crisis management framework that was develop to ensure World Vision advocates for more funding from a number of donors to focus on the looming current highest displacement population on the content. World Vision has set to prioritise Health interventions that are globally approved as lifesaving in IPDS, returnees and Vulnerable host communities in Sudan.
This FY2024, World Vision affirms its commitment to invest in Health information management system through the timely provision of 4Ws and other Health data to the Cluster. Further still strengthening the Community health systems that promote early warning and response system for key outbreaks and infectious diseases in the states where WVI operate. This is in response to the current WHO senital sites interventions that cumulates into EWARS for Sudan.
World Vision Current operational sites are struggling with the effects of war including and hence medical supplies, medicines and consumables are very inadequately procured. World Vision further appreciates the role played by the current inflation that has hiked all prices of medical supplies, further affecting the Human resources for Health. And hence is factoring all these in the current suggested lifesaving interventions.
World Vision will therefore accelerated implementation offering general clinical services through the general OPD, ensuring that these services are readily available. This will be through static PHUs coupled with the establishment of mobile clinics in those areas designated as heard to reach. These will be offering all services to children under 5 years, adolescents and adults. The sites will be organized in such a way that they become entry points for health education on Key Social and behavioural change communication topics as suggested by the Federal and state Ministry of Health authorities. The focus will also be on communicable and non-communicable diseases and hence ensuring Human resources for Health and facilitated to be readily available.
Both at community and Health facility level, World Vision will promote the maternal, newborn and child health services through the SRH track for Sudan. The PHUs will have a running MCH clinic that offers all continuum of care services. World Vision appreciates all the PSEA complications around the current fragile context and hence will work to integrate child and adult safeguarding services like clinical management of rape/GBV to ensure all cases are cared for in the shortest time possible.
Implementation of cutting edge but innovative human resources capacity building and training will be facilitated. There will be a reduction in workshop led capacity building and more of mentorship and supervision will be done. This will be aimed to ensure that the current staffing norms at the PHUs and mobile clinics are not recalled and hence living the population unattended to.
