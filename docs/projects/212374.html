Project Summary

In response to urgent needs, this project aims to enhance access to equitable lifesaving services through an integrated approach. It closely aligns with the objectives of General Protection (GP), Child Protection (CP) and (GBV) with a focus on providing critical assistance to the most vulnerable individuals, particularly those residing in high-need areas.
As part of Human Appeal’s commitment to creating a safe environment, this project directly targets a total of 32,753 individuals, including 8,264 women, 8,309 girls, 8,122 men, and 8,058 boys across seven areas in Idleb and Aleppo. These areas include Azaz, Ehtemlat, Ariha, Ehsem, Mhambal, Qourqeena, and Atareb, as well as Maaret Tamsrin/Kelly (HA AlZohoor camp).
The planned interventions by Human Appeal encompass;
Child Protection (CP), (GBV) and General Protection (GP), ensuring a comprehensive and effective response to the crisis. The project seeks to achieve the following:
HA GP Planned Intervention:
-Specialized and Tailored Protection Services: This project will provide specialized protection services specifically designed for population groups at heightened risk. These services aim to address immediate protection needs and vulnerabilities.
-Immediate Protection Assistance (IPA): As part of in-kind assistance, the project will offer immediate protection services to individuals and those most vulnerable. This includes timely support to mitigate risks and enhance safety.
-Community-Based Response: The project will strengthen protection capacity and response within frontline communities and high-risk areas. By engaging with local communities, it aims to create a protective environment and improve overall safety.
-Community Networks Strengthening: Efforts will be made to enhance community networks that address protection risks. Mechanisms for raising awareness, risk mitigation, and addressing existing impacts will be developed collaboratively.
-Protection Interventions for IDPs: IDPs will receive community-based protection interventions through awareness-raising sessions, psychosocial support (PSS), legal services, and referrals.
HA CP Planned Intervention:
• Increased and more equitable access for boys and girls to quality CP interventions through specialized services within the targeted communities. 
• Identification, information sharing sessions, develop and conduct sessions on child protection risks, including child labour, marriage, family separation, violence against children.
• Provision of PSS , and Child Protection case management to children in need.
• Conduct parenting programmes to strengthen the caregiving environment for children.
HA GBV Planned Intervention:
-Women and Girls Safe Spaces (WGSS): Human Appeal will establish and maintain WGSS in underserved areas. This includes reopening closed spaces and sustaining existing ones. Notably, HA Al-Zohour Camp already has WGSS and a center that were recently established and activated by Human Appeal. Additionally, efforts will be made to reactivate other WGSSs in different camps.
-Quality GBV Services: Within WGSS, the project will prioritize vulnerable groups and survivors. It will deliver quality GBV services, including case management, psychosocial support, and legal counseling.
-GBV Awareness Campaigns: Mobile and outreach teams will conduct awareness campaigns on GBV risks and related legal issues. These efforts aim to empower individuals with knowledge and promote prevention.
-Training for Humanitarian Actors: Both GBV and non-GBV humanitarian actors will receive training to enhance their capacity in addressing gender-based violence effectively.
This project’s comprehensive activities integrate protection, specifically focusing on Child Protection (CP) and Gender-Based Violence (GBV). Human Appeal is committed to ensuring safe and equitable access for displaced individuals.

0 - Project Summary (extended)

During the implementation of this program, Human Appeal (HA) is committed to delivering well-designed activities to ensure high-quality implementation, through;Psychosocial Support (PSS) Activities:HA will implement PSS activities to promote psychological well-being among beneficiaries.Child Protection (CP) Case Management and Specialized Care:HA’s assigned staff will identify and support Child Protection cases. These cases involve prevalent issues such as child labor, recruitment, survivors of violence, children at risk of abuse and neglect, early marriage, and extreme stress.Case management services, PSS, and referrals to specialized services will be provided to address the unique needs of each child.Coordination with Child Protection AoR:HA will closely coordinate with the Child Protection and GBV Area of Responsibility (AoRs). This collaboration ensures an effective referral system when specialized services are required.Regular updates to the service map will enhance the referral process.Awareness and Information Sharing Sessions:HA will develop and conduct sessions on child protection risks. Topics covered include child labor, early marriage, family separation, and violence against children.Emergency Cash Assistance for High-Risk Children:HA will provide cash assistance to 60 children (30 boys and 30 girls) who are at high risk.The emergency case fund will be used for referrals, direct support, transportation, medical needs, and essential kits.Support for Children with Disabilities (CWD):Out of the 40 children, 5 have disabilities. HA will tailor interventions to meet their specific needs.This comprehensive program aims to create a safer environment for children, address vulnerabilities, and promote their well-being. Parenting Programs for Caregivers (200 Beneficiaries):Human Appeal (HA) will conduct parenting programs for 200 caregivers (160 women and 40 men). HA will provide refresher training to staff (37 women and 91 men) in line with protection minimum standards.The training will cover areas related to Protection, GBV & CP Psychological First Aid (PFA), and Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (PSEA).Response Services for Women/Girls at Risk:The project will provide response services to women and girls at risk. This includes reaching out to GBV survivors through case management and referrals to specialized services.The services will benefit women, girls, persons with disabilities (PWDs), and elderly women.GBV Case Fund: A total of 200 GBV cases will receive case management services during the project period. The GBV Case Fund will be utilized for referrals, direct support, transportation, medical needs, and essential kits.Legal Services: HA will coordinate, advocate, and facilitate access to legal aid services for GBV survivors and those at risk. This comprehensive approach aims to create a protective environment, empower beneficiaries, and enhance overall well-being.
