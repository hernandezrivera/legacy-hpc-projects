Project Summary

Mutawinat main goal is(Advocacy for protection and solutions). To achieve this,we shall address three levels,refugees, host community and local authorities.The objectives are 1) Access to legal assistance and remidies improvement. 2) Raising awareness on SGBV and harrassement and Human Trafficking 3) Inclusion with the host community. Mutawinat shall provide legal assistant for( 600) POC 400 Eritrean 150 in Kassala 150 in Gaderf
200 Ethiopians 100 in Kassala and 100 in Gaderf ,shall also provide legal assistant for( 100) to SGBV from Ethiopians and Eritrean.in Khartoum state Mutawinat will provide legal assistance for (300) For survivors of sexual violence and victims of human trafficking from Eritrean ,Ethiopians ,Congolese .Also Mutawinat will carry out awareness-raising sessions for domestic workers females refugees from Eritrean, Ethiopian and Congolese about their rights and the labor law on domestic workers as protection of their rights in general and prevention of sexual violence in particular.On the other level Mutawinat will target local authorities to increase their knowledge about migration laws and international agreements ratified and also to increase thier knowledge about human trafficking in Khartoum, Gadreef and Khassla . Mutawinat shall target host community in Jabal Aulia , Karari and Sharq El Nile in Khartoum state and select 25 from this localities from active youth and community leaders by Capacity building workshop in peace, conflict resolution and peaceful coexistence, with negotiation and mediation skills . Graduates of this workshop will implement three forums in thier communities To discuss three topics refugee rights the concept of asylum , concept of peaceful coexistence in society and environmental and services issues in Jabal Aulia , Karari and Sharq El Nile. In kassla and Gadreef Mutawinat shall organize a selection committee panel consisting mainly of community leaders,a member of Mutawinat group members from UNHCR and Core.The selected target groups are 30 refugees(males and females) from Kassala and 30 from Gadaref . These refugees shall receive training capacity building package for protection and SGBV, international agreement and national laws and referral system act as protection network and paralegals after graduation . The should also each paralegal will implement 6 outreach sessions in his/her community to raise awareness for (1800) POC .Also in Kassla , Gadreef Mutawinat shall target host community by three discussion forums in will coordinated by paralegals to sensitize society to the concepts of asylum, protect refugees and increase peaceful coexistence.
