Project Summary

Ethiopia is experiencing a prolonged drought following three consecutive failed rainy seasons since late 2020 affecting 5.1 million people living in Oromia and Somali. More than 2.2 and 2.9 million people in Somali and region respectively need water assistance. Large-scale food and WASH assistance will be needed through urgent humanitarian responses including water trucking, provision of food and nonfood items to reduce morbidity and mortality among target population. ASDEPO is intending to provide life-saving interventions in sectors of Emergency WASH response to 81,820 (36,474 IDPs and 45,346 Host communities) of the most vulnerable population affected by drought in Hudet and Moyale woredas of Dawa Zone, Somali Region and Dilo and Dirre woredas of Borena zone, Oromiya region.
ASDEPO has strong presence in Dawa and Borena zones through implementing Emergency WASH and Nutrition Responses for drought affected IDPs and Host communities in Moyale, Hudet, Qadaduma and Mubarak Woredas of Dawa Zone , Somali Regional State of ERM VII funded projects in partnership with NCA and Prevention and Response to Sexual and Gender Based Violence (SGBV) project for Refugees and Host Communities in Megado and Dillo Refugee Camps, Borena Zone Oromiya Regional State.
The proposed project links with the main strategic objectives of the HRP 2022, to deliver conflict-sensitive essential life-saving WASH services to affected people and enhance vulnerable households’ ability to withstand recurrent and future shocks in Ethiopia through early recovery and resilience interventions through delivering water, sanitation and hygiene promotion assistance. The project intervention will ensure the project implementation in line with humanitarian standards, protection, and accountability principles in emergency settings. Vulnerable, especially women, girls, the elderly, and people with disability will be highly considered for participation and consultation during all the project cycles for their maximum benefit from the project.

1. - Projects activities

1. Provide access to safe water for 34,248 beneficiaries through the provision of Water trucking. ASDEPO will rent a water trucks for targeted woredas for a period of two months for communities critically affected by water shortage. Water trucks are designed to transport and distribute water from the potable water source to IDPs or community residential areas to benefit 34,248 individuals through provision of minimum 7.5 litters of water per person per day for their drinking and basic hygiene needs. 2. Improve access for safe water supply through rehabilitation of non-functional water schemes. ASDEPO will assist the community with the provision of safe water through the rehabilitation and maintenance of Eight non- functional water scheme (two per woreda)3. Provision WASH NFIs to 11,682 IDPs and host communities to enable safe practices of proper handwashing and personal hygiene based on the national WASH Cluster standards. ASDEPO will mobilize and procure WASH Non-food items (NFIs) to assist households/individuals under emergency circumstance/IDPs in the four targeted woredas. The WASH NFI includes Jerrican(1 per HHs), HH water treatment chemicals, bucket, Dignity kits containing sanitary pad, under wear, laundry soap, bathing soap and flashlight will be provided for 2000 women. 4.Promote improvements in hygiene and sanitation practices for 81,820 individuals. ASDEPO will provide capacity building/training to 120 HEW, Religious / Community leaders, community volunteers, health/hygiene workers and they will reach about 81,820 individuals through the distribution of key Hygiene & COVID messaging using routine health education and IEC materials/posters. 5.Establish and train eight WASH Committees. ASDEPO will strengthen and train men and women including youths from the IDPs and host communities on the operation and maintenance of WASH facilities for three days, to ensure the proper management and sustainability of the services. A total of 40 WASHCO members will be trained on management, operation and maintenance of the schemes. 6. Construction/rehabilitation of 40 basic sanitation facilities (semi-permanent latrines, hand washing stations) one latrine for 50 person in the target IDP sites. 7.Construction of 40 temporary bathing facilities at IDP collection center to alleviate the sanitation facility needs at IDP sites. 10. Improve COVID-19 prevention through provision of key preventions messages and essential facilities
