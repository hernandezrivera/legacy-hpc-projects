Project Summary

In light of the current economic and fuel crises, the intended intervention will aim to support existing infrastructure for water supply system through procurement and supply of chlorine and other consumables, emergency repair for pumping stations with provision of fuel to maintain minimum operational level and providing the most vulnerable communities with water supply. This approach is deemed to take the most cost-effective pathway to reach a high number of people in need in a short time period. The only way to avoid the complete shutdown of all or part of the water systems in Lebanon is through this intervention and support to pumping stations.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

1.1.1 Provision of fuel support and general maintenance: supporting water access through the existing water infrastructure system by maintaining minimum operational level to ensure continuity of water supply system to deliver minimum of 35l/c/d. 1.1.2 Provision of chlorine and consumables: procurement of chlorine and other consumables to ensure the safety of distributed water pumped in the water system service lines. 1.1.3 Emergency repairs: repairs on the water infrastructure systems and provision of spare parts to maintain services at pumping stations.1.1.4 Cholera Preparedness and Response: to immediately respond to a potential cholera outbreak through the supply of water trucking, desludging and chlorination at household and water establishment level.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

Gender and youth considerations are mainstreamed mainly at programming level in order to ensure the distinct needs and realities of women, men, girls and boys are reflected throughout the response. UNICEF promotes the incorporation of the new global gender-based violence (GBV) guideline, through considering GBV in new assessments, and incorporating GBV risk-related questions in questionnaires and focus group discussions. UNICEF is committed to collect and use sex and gender disaggregated data during assessment, monitoring and evaluation. In addition, UNICEF builds the capacity of the staff that are engaged in outreach activities on WASH-related GBV risks and referrals.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

Monitoring and Evaluation; The main Monitoring and evaluation foreseen mechanisms are listed below: ● Reporting through activity info and ERP reporting systems using sex and age disaggregated data. ● Real time monitoring of water station interventions and repairs through an online dashboard. ● Complaint and feedback mechanisms for women, girls, men, and boys and PWSN will be integrated into existing referral systems already in use (if technically feasible). ● Impact indicators will be presented in project final reports to evaluate the impact of the implementation. Referral mechanism Referrals are shared from water establishments, municipalities, and communities directly to UNICEF, the needs are field validated before intervention. Considering strengthen available systems in country with RIMS referrals platform.
