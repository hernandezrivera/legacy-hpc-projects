Project Summary

General objective:
Strengthen the right to Mental Health for Palestinian population living under occupation and continuous violence.
Specific Objective:
Reinforce the availability, accessibility, acceptability and quality of MH Services in oPt.
Outcomes:
1. Ensure mainstreaming of quality Mental Health services into the primary Health care
package provided by MoH and other PHC providers in oPt.
2. Ensure proper case management of MH cases for children, teenagers
and adults at PHC level by reinforcing quality procedures and protocols, as well as the adequate referral pathway among different levels (community, PHC and CMHC)
3. Strengthen accessibility and acceptability by fighting against the stigma and misconceptions of community about MH.
With this action, MDM contributes to achieve the Objective 1 of the Cluster:
“Ensure the availability, accessibility, acceptability and quality
of essential lifesaving health services to the most vulnerable communities in Gaza and West Bank/EJ, including through health system strengthening, preparedness and community resilience building”
MdM wants to highlight the fact that MH services are a key component of essential health intervention and they include lifesaving activities when global health is threatened by crisis and emergencies; and MH needs even a more compelling attention when vulnerable communities are impacted by protracted periods of violence, displacement and refuge.

Needs

Mental Health and Psychosocial Support in contexts of conflict is a fundamental component of humanitarian assistance. In times of conflict, fear, violence and uncertainty can cause significant psychological and psychosocial distress among affected populations. Pre existing psychological disorders can be exacerbated while other disorders are directly induced by situations of war or conflict (psychological trauma, anxiety and depression disorders to name a few).MDM is currently implementing a direct MHPSS emergency response program in the NWB including Qalqilya , The last analysis conducted by MDM in 2017 showed that 58 % of the adult including men and women who received Psychological first aid report severe signs of acute stress and are at risk of developing PTSD; In Gaza, a study of WHO in 2014, estimated that 20% of Gaza’s population was suffering from a range of mental health challengers with significant levels of depression and post-traumatic stress. End of 2016 , MDM and PCC just finalized a Mental health integration project targeting 25 PHC centers in Nablus governorate in WB, by the end of the project , an external evaluation was conducted , the external evaluation strongly recommended MDM and PCC to extend this program to cover other governorates as it was effective , sustainable and under the main priorities of the MOH MH strategy for 2015-2019 . MDM is implementing a MHPSS Emergency Response Program since 2014, through its continuous intervention in the area , MDM identified the following needs :1) High stigma from the MH problems and disorders preventing community members in need for MH services to access it, around 50% from MDM beneficiaries (23% out of them were females, 25% males, 27% boys and 25% girls) who were in need to be referred to advanced MH services refused these referrals for stigma, lack of services providers at community level and economic reasons. Having MH services at community level together with the awareness campaign will increase the number of clients seeking for the MH services , especially women as women visits by their own to the PHCCs are accepted at community level , the nurses of the PHCCs usually build a trust relation with women in the villages and they will be able to detect and deal with the women MH problems as the majority of the visitors of these clinics are women. Said that, we need also to highlight that stigma is quite heavy among men and they could even have less mechanisms than woman to access MH at primary level as their attendance to PHCs are less and to speak out about mental problems is perceived as a weakness sign and "improper" for strong men in a conflict context. This high level of stress at homes also is related to high incidence and prevalence of GBV, especially intimate partner violence, what it contributes to deepen the impact of mental health disorders. 2) A rising level of violence in a deteriorated economic context: consequences on mental health: The last analysis conducted by MDM in 2017 showed that 58 % of the adult population who received Psychological first aid report severe signs of acute stress and are at risk of developing further problems3) Lack of MH services providers, especially at detection and management of MH cases: Qalqilya governorate is counting only with one MOH MH clinic (CMHC) serving all the governorate (part time male psychiatrist, 1 male social worker and 1 female psychiatrist nurse). Therefore, the integration of MH within a comprehensive Primary Health Concept is key in the availability and accessibility of MH services.In Gaza, data of an MHPSS project implemented by CFTA show similar needs. Positive coping mechanisms and higha daptive behaviour responses have allowed people to challenge stress. However, more and more people are reporting mild to moderate symptoms which impede every-day life activities. MDM -CFTA data show a clear predominance of behavioural problems (50% of the cases) followed by psychological problems.

Activities

Output 1: Increased access to MH services at PHCCs level. The objective of this activity is to reinforce the ability of the PHCC staff to be able to receive, detect, provide adequate support and properly manage the cases suffering from MH problems and disorders.The main activity will be the training of the staff of 24 PHCCs in WB (Qualquiliya governorate: 3 level 4 PHCCs in the main city : 1 MOH , 1 HWC and 1 PMRS , 4 PHCCs level 3 in towns around Qalqilya ,17 PHCCs level 2 ) and 9 PHCs in Gaza ( 2 level 4 ,1 level 3 and 6 level 2 in Khanyounis) on providing MH services at PHC level which includes:(1) training of staff including training modules about interviewing skills, common MH disorders, children psychology, teenagers psychology, adult psychology, child abuse and provision of emergency MH services. The training modules will be based on WHO guidelines including the MH Gap and the PM+, MDM will work with the MH unit / directorate in Gaza and WB for the additional training modules which are completing the WHO modules by focusing on specific vulnerable groups as children , teenagers , elderly. In the case of Khanyounis, clinics have already received training in mhGAP so the support will focus more in the 2 following points.(2) technical and administrative follow up of the trained PHC staff by the staff of the MOH MH clinic in Qualquilia and the staff of the CMHC of Khanyounis supported by MDM. When talking about follow up, MDM means supervision, technical in the sense of correct screening (use of the GHQ 12), treatment and referral to CMHC for specialized services, as well as administrative in the sense of registration, data collection, etc.(3) integration of data collection and reporting tools to be used to report MH problems.To maintain the sustainability of the activities, the MOH mental health clinic staff will be from the beginning of the action. Moreover, MDM will coordinate with WHO to use same tools and technical modules and to avoid overlapping in trained staff and geographical areas. MDM and WHO are already coordinating for the integration of MH at hospital level in Gaza in regular bases.Output 2: Ensured continuum of care for the patients identified at PHC centers through case management and referral system combating gender stereotypes. End of 2015, MOH together with MDM launched the national mental health referral system in West Bank and in September 2018 the referral system was presented in Gaza . Within this action, MDM will integrate the case management approach to the MH services provided at PHC centers and will facilitate the referrals and the referrals feedback among the MHPSS actors.Activities:(1) mapping of the MHPSS providers within the governorate, (2) training of the MHPSS actors on the use of the referral system , (3) supervision , follow up and reporting of the use of the referral system .The use of the MH referral system will facilitate the pathway of the patients identified at PHC and will ensure their arrival to a comprehensive cycle of MHPSS services. Using evidence based figures, success stories and case studies, MDM and implementing partners will advocate towards MoH to ensure the effective implementation of the MHPSS National Strategy.Output 3: Reduced stigma towards MH problems /disorders and its services among the population of Qaqlilya and Khan Younis through MH awareness with gender balance approach.The main aim is to make the Communities aware about their MH wellbeing, impact of traumatic incidents on their physical health and behaviour and the resources available to alleviate it and reducing the stigma associated with MH.The activities includes: (1) Awareness workshops about MH problems. The topics of will be identified following an assessment with the community themselves, (2) printed materials including (3) provision of the PHCCs with the needed multimedia materials (4) Mass media campaigns on MH services and against stigma

Indicators

The indicator of the specific objective of MDM “# of PHC staff capable to provide MH care at PHC centers “ provides clear impact on the O1 indicators of the health cluster: (1) # of people with access to Primary Health Care services in the West Bank. (2)#of patients who receive essential secondary care services in Gaza. (3)# of acutely vulnerable boys, girls, men and women receiving mental health and PSS. To ensure proper monitoring of the project , MDM will use specific data bases which are already created and functional: 1) MH integration data base where doctors and nurses training and supervision results are integrated , to complete this specific focus groups will be conducted with the trainees before and after the trainings where its qualitative results will be analyzed and reflected accordingly . 2) MH cases at PHC data base , where cases detected and referred will be registered , the qualitative dealing with patients will be reflected through analytical case studies . (3)The awareness attendance , pre ad post test will be registered with the awareness data base . Output 1 indicators:• at least 80% of targeted PHC staff complete training and formation. • at least 80 % of PHC staff completing training scores an average of 80% or more in their training post-tests per each training module.• at least 10% of users (men, women, boys and girls) of PHCs at the area of the intervention are attending MH services.• at least 70% oh MH users at PHC level (men, women, boys and girls) express satisfaction with services provided.• among MH services users at PHC level (men, women, boys and girls)there is a rate below 10% for defaulters.• at least 50% of MH service users(men, women, boys and girls) at PHC level express an improvement in their MH condition at the end of the year. Output 2 indicators:• at least 80% of targeted MHPSS service providers are trained on MH referral system.• at least 70% of cases in need to be referred to CMHC by PHC staff are properly and timely referred (men, women, boys and girls).• at least 70% of cases (men, women, boys and girls) referred by PHC to PHC are coming back to PHC for follow-up after being discharged by CMHC.• # of advocacy meetings conducted with MOH decision makers about the implementation of the national MH strategy and use of the referral system.Output 3 indicators:• A survey in the areas of the intervention reflects that at least 50% of the catchment population has been reached with awareness campaign key messages.• At least 50% of awareness sessions targeted people reflect a positive change in the knowledge and attitude towards MH problems and services• # At least 10% of people seeking for MH services at PHC level changed their mind on consulting MH services because they were reached by awareness and anti-stigma campaign.
