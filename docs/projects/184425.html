Project Summary

According to nutrition cluster and FSNAU recently released nutrition overview in Somalia indicate critical acute malnutrition in Lower Juba and Gedo region this due to a combination of factors, including food insecurity, high morbidity, low immunization and vitamin-A supplementation and poor care practices. At the national level, the median prevalence of Global Acute Malnutrition (GAM) has remained Serious (10–14.9%) over the past three seasons. A Critical (15-29.9%) prevalence of GAM was observed in 10 out of the 33 population groups surveyed, with more areas likely to deteriorate from Serious to Critical between September 2021. A Critical (4-5.6%) prevalence of Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM) was observed in the pastoral and agropastoral livelihood of Afmadow and Badhadhe district of Lower Juba region while 14.9% GAM and 2.5% SAM caseload were observed in Gedo region. Morbidity among children was high (≥20%) in 15 out 33 population groups surveyed. However, Crude Death Rate and Under-Five Death Rate were relatively low in most areas. Urgent treatment and nutrition support is required for an estimated
1.2 M children under the age of five years (total acute malnutrition burden), who will likely face acute malnutrition in 2022, including 295,515 who are likely to be severely malnourished while a total of 245,909 pregnant and lactating women will be moderately malnourished hence this require an urgent Integrated nutrition interventions to support recovery and prevent deterioration in the nutrition situation.
Gedo and Lower Juba region were considered hotspot areas with a projected Critical GAM prevalence, indicated by a weight-for-height z-score of 15-29.9% or by a Mid-Upper Arm Circumference below 125 mm of ≥10%, IDP areas of concern of concern for acute malnutrition and need of urgent nutrition and health support interventions
This project therefore target to reach a total 4,200 vulnerable boys, girls and pregnant and lactating women through an integrated response around Nutrition. It is intended to mitigate the physical and protection consequences of drought on populations and communities directly affected in Lower Juba and Gedo region, in particular, Badhaadhe,Bardheere ,Kismayo and Afmadow town. The main objective of this project is to provide integrated lifesaving emergency Nutrition services to 4,200 drought affected direct beneﬁciaries and intends to achieve the following, capacity building of nutrition staff on IMAM. Referral of malnourished children with complicated cases will be done to the Outpatient Therapeutic Programme (OTP) centers and directly linked with primary health care services managed by SORDES and other actors in the program areas thus providing more holistic opportunity to the target beneﬁciaries SORDES will closely coordinate with Local MOH as well as Federal level MOH and social mobilizers, elders all involved in activities on mobilizing communities
