1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

UNICEF has been championing emergency preparedness for natural disasters in Mozambique, particularly by supporting capacity building in Disaster Risk Reduction through PEBE – School based Emergency Preparedness. Additionally, has been very actively supporting the sector preparedness and education local authorities and implementing partners to respond the natural disaster events addressing EiE immediate needs in its cluster coordination role. Within this current proposal UNICEF will implement an approach that will focus on one hand on the mitigation of risks associated to natural through preparedness and anticipatory actions, as on the other hand the immediate response to an event related to natural hazards impact on the education through an integrated approach. The project will target children, teachers and communities affected by or vulnerable to natural disaster across the country including children with disabilities. The project geographical scope includes all provincies but the focus will be the provinces with higher likelihood of being affected by natural disaster considering the last couple of years and rainy season forecast for 2025. The monitoring process will be jointly done with different DPE and partners to ensure quality implementation, participation and accountability on the outcomes planned, aligned with Cluster reporting and monitoring tools and guidance.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The activities package designed under the project is divided in three groups: 1) preparedness, 2) anticipatory actions and 3) immediate response. The decision on the exact geographical areas of implementation will be informed by the map of affected areas in previous years, combined with the forecast for rainy season 2025. Particularly the activities for response will follow the exact location of a particular natural disaster event. Preparedness activities include: Strengthen the capacity of local Authorities at central, provincial and district level on contingency planning and response to natural disaster related emergency; Support to the design of school based emergency plans - PEBE - as preparedness and risk mitigation mechanisms - practical training for school staff, including director, teachers, parents and students on how to implement this basic emergency plan with the distribution of Kits for small repairs. In addition to that School Disaster Management Committee will be created at school level with the involvement of Conselho da Escola [School Management Council]. To achieve this, there are several manuals approved by MINEDH via UNICEF support and kits that must be distributed in each school; Set up an education specific alert mechanism on immediate Natural disaster forecast to prepare the schools to the upcoming events.Anticipatory Actions include: Training on Anticipatory Actions to Cluster Members; Reinforcing learning facilities to mitigate the impact of the natural hazard; Sharing live-saving messages right before the event. 2. Response Activities include: activation of PEBE to ensure efficient management of school, including protection of staff and materials; Delivery of School based MHPSS to children with signs of stress following the events; Set up of temporary learning spaces (TLS) and latrines to enable to respond to the influx of IDP children to existing school. Conducting emergency repairs on education facilities and latrines to meet INEE and Sphere Minimum Standards and ensuring the reopening of schools and/or classrooms, particularly those used as shelter; Provision of teaching and learning materials for students and teachers. The distribution of materials includes Learner´s and School-in-a -box kits, assistance devices for children with disabilities and Menstrual Hygiene Management Kitts (MHM); Conducting back to school campaigns for children, parents and communities; Sectoral and intersectoral dialogue and collaboration with other clusters and partners is a crucial crosscutting component throughout the implementation of the project.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Consultations and Complain mechanism will be in place so that beneficiaries can influence the project design and implementation. Necessarily the proposal will engage teachers, parents and caregivers, throughout Parents and Teacher Associations and School Management Committee engagement, holding UNICEF accountable for the outcomes committed with. In the monitoring of the process, UNICEF ensures that children are comfortable and engaged listened to, collect information that can inform reprogramming so the activities aim also to address their psychosocial and mental well-being needs rather than fostering learning and studying alone. In this monitoring, the communities, parents or caretakers of these children will be as much as involved as possible through creation of reference groups, discussion groups of different sex and age and also using the complaint boxes that will help us improve our implementation. From an organizational point of view, UNICEF is deeply committed to PSEA across all programs and initiatives. The commitment is demanded from all governmental counterparts and all implementing partners supported by UNICEF.There is a PSEA dedicated team in the country office, supporting PSEA operationalization, monitoring and evaluation and a focal point is each field office. From a proposal point of view, all teachers will benefit from training in PSEA and following that they will be able to support identifying and referring and preventing SEA in and around schools.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people specifically women and elderly person?

Significant measures will be put in place to prevent discrimination and promote inclusion of disadvantaged groups, and indicators are disaggregated by sex and age. Through activities implementation, flagging and addressing access gender roles related barriers, including referring to other clusters for an integrated approach to specific groups needs such as like girls’ displaced or on the move or with severe psychosocial well-being needs.. Special attention will be put on sensitization on girls’ education and GBV which can seriously affect the attendance and retention of young girls in schools.. Boys have also been considered in their vulnerability: young boys who are excluded from accessing learning opportunities due to traditional social structures, families often require boys to take up works so that they cannot attend school regularly. The activities want to address the different obstacles equally preventing boys and girls from attending school.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

UNICEF will work with NGOs and staff experienced in delivery specific services and support for children with disabilities (CWD) so that they can fully participate in the activities. Therefore as explained above, these activities are organized in good collaboration with the protection, WASH and health cluster and their selected NGOs in order to provide an intersectoral service for all these children. Parents, caregivers and local authorities and community will be fully involved in the promotion of inclusive activities and education. In the long run, facing and overcoming challenges and reducing the prejudices from the general public and increasing their chances to actively participate in society will help them to grow stronger and more resilient.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The selected location of education facilities will be preceded by an evaluation of the location in collaboration with the local authorities, so that the location can be considered safe with materials that are safe for children to use, free of natural hazards (floods, storms) and clean with basic WASH conditions, and with confirmation on responsibilities for maintaining security, WASH and protection. At the same time the activities will aim to raise environmental awareness and protection of natural resources and avoiding erosion for instance by planting fruit and shade trees, creating living fences, grass planting and directing and harvesting rainwater.Additionally, procurement processes include criteria related to environmental impact, prioritizing supplies with a longer lifetime expected.
