Project Summary

Emergency WASH and GBV activities to COVID 19 epidemic in the South West region
Measures of prevention and control of infection in the community and health facilities will included the following activities:
• The IRC will distribute a total of 8,300 handwashing kits for 49,800 beneficiaries. Distribution of culturally appropriate handwashing. The kits will be composed of (i) a three-month supply of soap, (ii) plastic watering cans (known as lota, sakhannes or various local names) with a collection container (basin or bucket), and (iii) an IEC flyer reminding the key messages of hand hygiene, respiratory etiquette, social distancing and what to do in case of sickness..
• The IRC will implement a package of infection prevention and control in 8 health facilities in the Southwest in order to ensure access to water, personal protective equipment, handwashing facilities, cleaning and disinfection materials, and waste disposal facilities. Activities are estimated to support 30,000 beneficiaries.
Communication of risks and community engagement:
• Training and equipping of 80 community health workers to disseminate the adequate prevention messages of the RCCE strategy in their own neighborhoods and communities.
• Mass sensitization through radio and public announcements.
