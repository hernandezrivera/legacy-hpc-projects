Project Summary

This project seeks to create an inclusive path whereby Persons with disabilities ( PWDs) will be protected and feel a sense of community inclusiveness. It will be done through the community base rehabilitation approach with focus on the community volunteers engaging with the community who have proximity to PWDs. Education and sensitization will be done at its best targeting persons with disabilities in the North West region. As a monitoring and response project, we will equally assist 4800 PWDs with core relief items.
The purpose of this intervention is to prevent and limit the negative effects of the ongoing political crisis and improve on quality of life for persons with disabilities in the North West region of Cameroon. The project targets reaching out to a total of 4800 PWDs.
