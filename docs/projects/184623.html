Project Summary

Even though sustained assistance is preventing worse food security there are still areas that are still classi¦ed as stressed and others are in crisis (IPC 3) such as Hiraan, Galgaduud and Jubas. Further reports show that many households are still recovering from drought or have been affected by conflict, and 2.6 million people remain displaced. Most of the displaced people are in crisis (IPC phase 3 or Stressed (IPC Phase 2). Up to 2.1 million people across Somalia are expected to face food consumption gaps, high acute malnutrition, or depletion of assets indicative of Crisis (IPC Phase 3)1 or worse outcomes. In this Case Yme has designed a project that will respond to the existing food security gaps.
The project will ensure that it averts the deterioration of the situation further. Yme will offer timely and appropriate assistance to the affected population in the southern and central regions of Somalia especially in Mudug. The target bene¦ciaries are from different livelihoods that have been affected by the drought and are in need of assistance. The interventions that we will offer will address the immediate needs of the people such as lack of food and other household needs. This will be done through providing food aid through vouchers to 450 Households (2700 bene¦ciaries) to the most vulnerable households. Also we will provide temporary employment as well as income by having cash for work activities before the rains in Lower Shabelle. The cash for work activities, will target 377 households and we providing 5USD daily rate to the laborers which will increase their income for two months. The infrastructures will contribute to provision of water and also facilitate transaction of businesses through feeder roads. Since most of these infrastructures such as canals, water catchments and feeder roads and not well maintained we will train the existing committees and if they are not there we will organize new committees to be in place. These activities will address the immediate food security needs and support the communities to create assets that will strengthen their livelihoods. The advantage of Cash for Work is that it empowers the bene¦ciaries to buy food or other necessities for immediate use and according to the priorities of the individual. Yme will further work with the communities to build their resilience by supporting the farmers in the targeted regions. Yme will provide agricultural inputs that is crop and vegetable seed to 520 households as well as land preparation of 1 hectare per farmer before the rains. The farmers will receive improved farming techniques training on how they can improve their crop production. In most of these activities we will target at least 60% women headed households as they are the most vulnerable groups in the community. Yme has a technical vocational training centre in Galkacyo which has graduated over 600 students and over 80 percent are estimated to be in gainful employment. Young unemployed men and Women in Galkacyo will be targetted to be trained on selected vocational training to help them start their own businesses. By supporting the young people we will be empowering them to become self-reliant and responsible men and women in the future

1. - Projects activities

The Somalia Food Security Cluster, showed that there has been a significant scale up of emergency food assistance. Even though the sustained assistance is preventing worse food security there are still areas that are still classified as stressed and others are in crisis (IPC 3) such as Hiraan, Galgaduud and Jubas. Further reports show that many households are still recovering from several natural disasters or have been affected by conflict, and 2.6 million people remain displaced. Most of the displaced people are in crisis (IPC phase 3 or Stressed (IPC Phase 2).In order to assist, Yme will ensure community mobilization will be conducted at the start to create awareness on the project including selection criteria involved. The activities that will be carried out will be provision of food aid through vouchers which includes sugar, oil, rice and pulses to 400 households (2400 beneficiaries) with over 50% going to women headed households. Other selection critieria will be households with members who are disabled andd elderly. We will ensure that 25% of the target beneficiaries are in these selection critieria. - indicator: 400 households will have recived food assistance through vouchers for 3 months consisting of suffcient calories and nutritionally ballanced. We will target 300 Households (1800 beneficiaries) in the riverine area with agricultural inputs of crop, legume and vegetable seeds together with urea. Then each farmer will receive 1 hectare land preparation and selected few will be trained on Good Farming Practices. - indicator: 300 households receive seeds and land preparation of 1 hectare as well as training. Creating temporary job opportunities for 300 (1800 hh members) households through rehabilitation of irrigation canals, water catchments and road rehabilitation. Unemployed youths in IDP areas will be selected to be trained vocational skills. Indicator: 300 hh will receive labor income for one member for 2 months through cash for work, 20 days per month, 5 dollar per day.

2.1.1 - What is the justification for using cash or vouchers instead of in kind?

Cash for work is more efficient since the people affected by a disaster are the ones who know best how to counter this, they know their needs and can this way tailer their needs instead of getting something they may not need or something that is less urgent.

2.1.2 - Explain how the project design considered market functionality?

A market assessment will be done beforehand where people are asked on their priority followed by a market assessment. If need be traders will be informed about the cash for work and the potential for additional trade.

4.6 - Mitigation Measures

At the onset of the project a comprehensive community mobilization and sensitization will be undertaken. This will allow all target groups including the marginalized to be fully aware of the project being implemented in their community. In beneficiary selection Yme will target those marginalized groups that are mostly excluded, the IDPs and the returnees since most of the times their needs are overlooked. During displacements they lose their livelihoods and lack access to basic necessities. In ensuring these marginalized groups are included Yme will work closely with the key stakeholder from the different target groups to be certain that all are involved in the project design. By involving all the groups in the target area that is IDPs and even host communities will reduce social tensions that might arise and build cohesion between the two groups. A continues monitoring in the area will be necessary to adapt activities and identify main barriers to access services or discrimination against particular marginalized groups. We will build direct contacts with the marginalized groups to ensure effective representation and avoid discrimination.Different genders and ages are affected differently by the conflicts and displacements. So Yme will have a gender and age sensitive participatory approach in all stages of the project to ensure that adequate and efficient services are provided to the different groups involved in terms of gender and age. You find that people are marginalized and excluded in regards to their ages and gender in the decision making processes in community. After conflicts and displacement many children and women are left as the heads of households however they not involved in many of the decision making process and as a result most of the needs are overlooked. Most of these groups due to neglect, abuses it’s hard for them to access the basic needs and even lack resources and support to manage a household. In ensuring these groups’ needs are met Yme will ensure when collecting data they include them and even have disaggregated data by age and sex. Yme will employ participatory approach in all the programme stages to incorporate the capacities of the different age groups and gender. During community engagements and even forming committees different age groups and gender will participate equally.
