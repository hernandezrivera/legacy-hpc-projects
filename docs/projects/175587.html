Project Summary

The project will focus on two components, food security and livelihoods. In the Food security component the project will consist of the distribution of food for 3 months, pending the harvest season. In the Livelihoods component the project will consist of the acquisition and distribution of seeds and agricultural equipment. The total number of beneficiaries for both components of the project will be 8000 families, equivalent to 40,000 people, resettled in the districts of Namuno (1000 HH), Balama (1000 HH), Mecufi (1000 HH), Ancuabe (3000 HH) and Chiure (2000 HH)

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

Assuming that the project will serve two components, food security and livelihoods, in five districts of Cabo Delgado, it aims to serve 8000 families in both components, Namuno (1000HH), Balama (1000HH), Mecufi (1000HH), Ancuabe (3000HH) and Chiure (2000HH), equivalent to a total of 40,000 people, which means that all families will benefit from food assistance, on the one hand, while they wait for the harvest season, on the other side. The food security will consist of he distribution of food kits (25kg of flour, 25 kg of rice, 5 liters of oil, 10kg of beans and 2kg of salt). As a way of curbing food addiction from humanitarian organizations, the project will create conditions for agricultural practice for all resettled displaced people, consisting of the purchase of agricultural inputs and distribute to all displaced people, allowing them to produce within 4 months and harvest the food. The livelihoods component will consist of purchase and distribution of seeds (corn 5 kg, beans Nhemba - 5 kg, pumpkin - 0.5 kg) and agricultural instruments (1 machete, 2 hoes and 1 ax)

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

The priority activities to be implemented in the areas selected for the execution of this project, specifically Chiure, Ancuabe, Mecufi, Balama and Namuno will be the following:• The first activity in making this project feasible is to contact the government and local authorities to get lists of IPDs in each district. The activities will take place during the period of 4 months that coincides with the agricultural season, to allow families that receive the seeds to wait for the harvest. The roll of activities to be cared out are as the follow:• Physical verification of the lists of beneficiaries to be provided by the government in all districts involved - this activity will consist of the validation of the lists by the Caritas’ technicians to certify the eligibility of the beneficiaries.• Procurement process for food and agricultural inputs in the local market or in Nampula - This activity will consist of seeking quotations and proceeding with the acquisition and purchase process.• Reception and preparation of kits for distribution - after receiving the inputs, these will be prepared in kits (placed in a bag, to facilitate the handling process). The food will be distributed according to the quantities that Caritas has been distributing: 25 kg of flour, 25 kg of rice, 2 kg of salt, 10 kg of beans and 5 liters of cooking oil.• Distribution of kits to beneficiaries. The kits will be transported in rented trucks to the distribution sites that will be cared out following the list previously prepared for this purpose. It should be noted that the agricultural inputs will be distributed only once, while food will be distributed 3 times (monthly) • Post-distribution monitoring - Ensure that there was no deviation in the application of the seed as well as assess the level of satisfaction of the beneficiaries. • Reports. Two financial and descriptive reports will be presented in the middle and at the end of the project.Note: the activities will be implemented simultaneously, as Caritas has a large team capable of covering the implementation areas.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

The identification of risks will be done through involvement and dialogue with local leaders, it is known that the target group belongs to several ethnic groups. To seek to know, together with the leaders, what are the main priorities and immediate needs for the beneficiaries. All target groups will be included to avoid possible confusion between them.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

Caritas as the humanitarian arm of the church, serves all the needy without any discrimination. If there are people qualified to support the distribution, Caritas involves them in their work and in return, they gain experience in the work of assisting the needy, which they can use in the future.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

People with disabilities will be one of the priorities during the distribution, reason why within the community consultation, the field officers will be trained to understand the modality of targeting for the most vulnerable beneficiaries such as people with disabilities with leadership character and ability to do some sort of work, they will be included in the team. Distribution points will be conveniently located ensuring ease of access by community members particularly people with disabilities and women.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

During the distribution of food assistance, the field officers will be trained also to explain the targeted local communities on how to keep the environment clean by avoiding to throw garbage in inappropriate places, promote the production of crops for nutrition and fertilize of the land, as well as to grow some tree fruits and respect the environment by avoiding opening big holes, duping rubbish at any place and promoting hygiene debates. Some sessions of individual and collective hygiene will be carried out by the activists and good use of latrines, water sources.
