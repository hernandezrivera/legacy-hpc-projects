Project Summary

Please provide a short summary of the intervention, describing its purpose and target. Max length: 3000 characters
The designed project seeks to empower the child protection system within the current Nutrition Centres of Northern Western Syria, by ensuring that CP concerns and considerations are taken into the Nutrition sector of the humanitarian response to maximize the impact of the child protection services in these communities and minimize the risks to children which may exacerbate from programming without children’s safety and will be taken into consideration.
Mercy-USA will work on the following activities:
1-Integrating Child-friendly spaces with 10 Nutrition centres: Mercy-USA will target to engage 12,000 children in recreational, sustained child protection programmers.
2- 100 children will receive case management services through the Nutrition centres: Mercy-USA will employ a professional team in each centre who can handle 100 cases(1 case worker per center).
3-A specialized Child protection &PSS trainings to PSS workers; to ensure adequate programming: 30 PSS workers (3 from each centre).
4- A specialized Case Management training will be conducted to 10 case workers.
5- 4000 parents will receive specialized PSS training using SHLS curricula. (400 parents in each center).

3 - Needs

According to HNO2023, almost seven out of ten people in Syria need assistance. The overall PIN has increased from 14.6 million in 2022 to 15.3 million in 2023.Illegal activities and begging remain widespread, while child labor, child recruitment and child marriage also continue to have a devastating impact. Signs of psychological distress in CU18 are reported by more than 27%. Based on MUSA’s experience in working with children under 5 through conducting Nutrition activities in addition to CP referral in NWS since 2015, MUSA has more than 340 registered cases that need specialized CM services which meet the vulnerability criteria applied by the CP sub-cluster.Moreover, Mercy-USA has conducted an RNA in all its Nutrition centers in Idleb and Aleppo, the findings as follows: Mothers preferred to have a Child-Friendly Space inside the Nutrition centers that provide safe spaces for their children through receiving Nutrition services.Mothers are very interested in receiving training on Parenting skills that support them in having better knowledge in dealing with their children.Children have no access to play and PSS opportunities: Which could add a burden on their parents and the host families, and they lose the opportunity to engage in safe, fun, and child-friendly activities that are much needed after they were removed from their original social environment and experience the horrors of indiscriminate attacks.

4 - Implementation of activities

MUSA will use its current Nutrition centers in Idleb& Aleppo that target CU5 and PLWs to integrate CP into Nutrition activities which ensures the provision of integrated safe services to the vulnerable people in the same area and to secure safe access to the same target groups within the PP.The PSS Recreational Activities: is an outreach activity,will target 7,500 children between 2-8 years in MUSA’s nutrition centers,will provide PSS recreational activities in addition to storytelling.2 PSS facilitators will be responsible for conducting the PSS activities for children in each center and 1 PSS worker will be responsible for conducting parenting skills sessions, The Parenting Skills intervention will promote the well-being of children through improving parents’ stress management, fostering positive parenting practices, and providing parents with strategies to support children’s and adolescents’ psychosocial needs. The safe healing and learning spaces/SHLS will implement 12-session parenting skills for 2000 parents who have children under 12 years of old.30 PSS workers (3 in each center) will receive CPIE, CSG and COC trainings in addition to refresher training on PSS/SHLS curriculum. 1 CW in each center will receive technical training on CM and will be responsible for following the identified cases until closing.in total, MUSA will target 500 children through CM services (MUSA will follow the agreed CM vulnerability criteria agreed by the cluster for children under18).

7 - Protection Risk Analysis

Specialized child protection services (case management) for girls and boys are available in targeted locations - Protection risk: Risk of creating or perpetuating stigma for girls, boys, and their families if staff are not appropriately trained or if client data is not managed appropriately. - Likelihood: Medium. Impact: High, mitigating measures: 1. ensure training packages are in line with child protection minimum standards and consider remote managed context 2. Services should not be provided by any staff who have not received anddemonstrated proficiency in training modules. 3. include a practice element to training modules that can be overseen through remote mentorship arrangements to allow for practice prior to service provision. 4. Service mappings should only include services that have been determined to provide safe and quality services. 5. Establish consultation with communities and ensure feedback mechanisms are in place. 6. Child Protection messaging to non-Protection actors.
