Project Summary

Women Aid Vision proposes an Education in Emergencies project that will respond and provide improved access to safe, inclusive and lifesaving education among 3,753 vulnerable displaced and host population 1753 male and 2000 female in Rumbek East (Pacong payam) and Rumbek Centre ( Amongpiny payams) by improving the quality and equitable access to education to ensure continuity of relevant education services among targeted children/ youth, strengthening the response capacity of communities and education actors to mitigate the impact of emergencies on children and youth.
The project will be in line with the following 2 cluster objectives:
Objective 1: Improve equitable access to safe and protective education services for 866,000 emergency affected IDPs ,returnees, spontaneous refugees and host communities children with disabilities and youth (boys and girls).
Objective 2: Improve the quality of gender and disability inclusive education services and teachers capacity to protect the well being of all children and prevent negative impact of emergencies on learning environment.
To achieve this WAV proposes scaling up already achieved activities through current UNICEF and NORAD education funding and sustaining the momentum of activities by establishment/ rehabilitation of 10 learning spaces/ classrooms, provision of school meals, establishment/ rehabilitation of 10 gender disaggregated latrine facilities equipped with 12 hand washing facilities, prepositioning through UNICEF/ Cluster pipeline and provision of scholastic learning and teaching materials/ EiE kits, capacity building and trainings of 60 teachers and volunteers, trainings which will encompass community focal points and authorities on psychosocial support, trainings and equipping of SMCs, PTAs on school governance and management..
WAV has an office in Rumbek town with adequate and experienced staff, with existing strong grassroot networks, that will scale up current UNICEF EiE programming and sustain momentum of activities.

1. Needs

IPC indicates the effects of Covid-19,Inter and Intra communal conflicts, cattle rustling, food insecurity levels, and the impact of flooding on livelihoods as key drivers in exacerbating an already poor economic environment with 9.3 million people in need in South Sudan comprising of 64% or 5.4 million children (0-17 years). Rumbek Centre county in (Pacong payams) and in Rumbek East county (Amongpiny payams) of Lake state now have an extra burden; with many schools in the Counties affected and damaged by the floods and violence which has been recurrent and some of them occupied by IDPs. This has delayed the re-opening of schools in many areas is limited and much of the service infrastructure was damaged, destroyed or closed in 2022.During an inter-agency IRNA in Rumbek East and Rumbek Centre Counties in November 2023 found out that the floods had affected upwards with 12000 people being displaced in Rumbek Centre and 9840 in Rumbek East counties . Schools were found to be occupied by the displaced population as well as livestock. Most Teaching and Learning materials were completely damaged and Learning infrastructure including Temporary learning Spaces and latrines had collapsed. WAV also conducted a rapid school-based WASH assessment in January 2023 and established that 5030 children (53% girls) in 8 schools of Rumbek Centre and 4,730 children (40% girls) in 6 schools of Rumbek East counties lack adequate TLS and WASH structures for schools to reopen. WAV basic assessments on Children living with disabilities found out that of the forms of disabilities existing in schools, 30% of the disabled children are physically disabled; 20% said the children are blind while the smallest proportion of 10% of the teachers identified children as deaf. WAV Humanitarian Response Plan (HRP) for 2023, therefore, seeks to increase equitable access to quality education through participation of 10000 vulnerable children and youth aged 3-17 years (4500 boys & 5500 girls) in Rumbek Centre and Rumbek East counties improve quality of teaching and learning; bridge the gender gap in access to education; improve access to quality education for 2000 (1500 girls and 500 boys) children living with disabilities; and improve management of education service delivery in Rumbek Centre and Rumbek East. This targets 10000 of Education Cluster’s 2024.

2. Project activities

• Establishment/ rehabilitation of 10 learning spaces/ classrooms • Provision of school meals targeting 4500 girls, 5500 boys • Establishment/ rehabilitation of 10 gender disaggregated latrine facilities equipped with 10 hand washing facilities • Prepositioning through UNICEF/ Cluster pipeline and provision of scholastic learning and teaching materials/ EiE kits among 4500 girls, 5500 boys. • Capacity building and trainings of 40 teachers and volunteers.• Capacity building and training of 100 community focal points and authorities on psychosocial support. • Capacity building, trainings and equipping of 100 SMCs, PTAs on school governance and management. • Hygiene promotion among 4500 girls, 5500 boys.• Establishment/ rehabilitation of 10 water points in the targeted schools.
