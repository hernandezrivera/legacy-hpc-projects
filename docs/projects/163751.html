Project Summary

Assessing the situation in Libya, this ‘protection project’ notion will take a slightly altered path that makes protection a more psychological and a practical need than physical. Beneficiaries of this project are all the Libyan and non-Libyan students in 9 targeted schools (6 in Misratah, 2 in Zleetin, 1 in Tawegha) aged from 4 – 15 forming three age groups each targeted separately (4-6, 7-11, 12-15), estimating a number of 9000 boys and girls. This project aims to raise awareness and get the targeted children out of war mood and psychology to avoid some of the possible “after war” mental and psychological consequences distracting their brains not to construct more of war idea. This project will target the social caseworkers at schools by providing the needed training to make a longer lasting effect of the protection. For this project, we are in agreements and cooperation with numbers of specialized centres, and specialized and experienced people. As an investment, Alemdad will select a number of special and talented students at the end of the project for additional care and education as a pay off of the project. Alemdad has a good and solid operational capacity as being a relatively old association working for the humanitarian responses since 2011, it has very good connections with crises committees, ministries and municipalities, which provides us a solid availability for meetings. It also has a staff (managerial) consisting of project manager, financial assistant, reporting and monitoring staff, and a psychological support team. More importantly, Alemdad is able to provide more to its capacity as long as the project requires.

1 - Needs

According to the congested situation in the country and regardless the fact that there is no direct physical exposure to was in our 3 targeted cities, children are severely exposed to war, they are terrified of the unknown and the possible drone attacks, losing relatives dying at fire lines and the a lot of talking happening around them. It started to be so of an acquisition when it comes to the rumors and news analysis by the public and so forth. Even youngest children speak so fluently just as old people do. about all happening. They think of it and construct idea, which is harmful. This project is mainly about the reduction of this idea by making and engaging this number of children to think of different things and tries to some extent control some of the negative psychological results of the happening on them.

2 - Activities

Psychological support is given priority for this child protection proposal based on an assessment of the situation in Libya, which takes different forms held by specialists and experts from different volunteering human improvement and investment centres. There are going to be multiple activities tackling the psychology of students. There is also awareness raising lectures for children and parents separately and together. This part of the proposal also contains training of all the social caseworkers at the schools in order to make a longer lasting effect on the tackled. This psychological aid will work as basic and direct steps into the problem.. This part will mostly focus to enhance and reinforce the spirit of the group, team work and cooperation. There is painting and patch work events and workshops which will contain a talent discovery section, and a gallery at the end. This will teach the children how to organize a gallery. It will also tackle the importance of team work in different ways.There is a more educational part in this proposal which contains of a library and occasional talks and lectures. Activities at the library will contain several mental activities as well as dictation and skills like reading (narrative reading). The lectures will be about astronomy, health and nutrition, nature and hygiene. This will make the child beneficiaries get to know about basic and the most important actual topics from experienced people.

4 - Protection risk analysis

There are safety and security constraints anticipated during the implementation of the project in the targeted locations. For example, if there is a fragility in the security situation, the matter is addressed by contacting the security authorities to help secure the of the activity in order to create an appropriate environment and be more safe for children.

5 - Mitigation measures

With regard to the safety and security of children during the establishment of the activity, the necessary standards will be taken. Then, if the activity is in a closed room, emergency exits are created that ensure the children leave in record time. Primary safety equipment is available as well as rapid intervention medical staff، As for the activities in the open places, the necessary measures will be taken in terms of providing first aid equipment as well as a medical staff accompanying the activity. This will be made by the help and cooperation with the schools, the Ministry of Education, the Municipal Councils, Crisis Committees and by reaching the beneficiaries where they exactly are.
