Project Summary

The intervention is located in Northern Bahr el Ghazal and Western Bahr el Ghazal states. An estimated 5.2 million people urgently need WASH support services in 2020 and only 3 million people are targeted for humanitarian intervention support in the same year. The WASH cluster figures for women and girls in need of WASH related gender based violence are 2.5 million only 1.1 million are targeted in 2020 interventions. This project is targeting to provide 33,500 people with timely, sustainable, equitable access to safe water, sanitation and hygiene for IDP’s, host communities and returnees. 16,851 women and girls are targeted for WASH related gender based violence.
Years of conflict have increased the vulnerability of women and girls, with significant cases of Gender Based Violence. Some of these abuses occur when women and girls undertake domestic chores, such as fetching water or firewood. The needs are closely related to conflict (political conflict and inter-communal conflict) as well as natural shocks. These have contributed to internal displacements and economic downturn, which has affected the macroeconomic situation further straining the limited WASH services. In some cases WASH infrastructures have been deliberately targeted by armed actors during conflicts, further aggravating the WASH situation. Many years of war in South Sudan have indirectly affected Northern Bahr el Ghazal, especially community resilience and coping mechanisms. The current economic crisis has also impacted negatively on the ability of the communities to sustain/maintain WASH services. The region has experienced flooding which resulted in displacement of people who have integrated with host communities, putting stress on the limited WASH services. The scattered settlement pattern in this region makes it difficult to mitigate the distance to the services e.g. access to water at sphere standard of 500m. Western Bahr El Ghazal (Raga County) has been severely affected by the war that has been going on. A lot of displacement has taken place in Raga, according to WASH Cluster 2019 severity analysis matrix, 5,907 people are IDPs, 601 are returnees while 7,586 are host the community. Raga is ranked among the most severely affected Counties in terms of access to safe water and faced with Gender Based Violence by the WASH Cluster.
The project targets the host communities, IDPs and Returnees. The total beneficiary figure is 33,500 people. The targeted recipients of this response are disaggregated as follows: 6,700 Women, 10,151 Girls, 6,599 Men and 10,050 Boys. The beneficiary figures are inclusive of the persons with disabilities in the communities. The intervention aims at increasing access to WASH services through drilling of boreholes, rehabilitation of non-functional hand pumps and solar powered Mini Water Yards. Hygiene and sanitation will be improved through formation and training of water management committees, CLTS triggering, sensitization and awareness campaigns in cross cutting issues like GBV, human rights, conflict mitigation and peace building.

1 - Needs

The project aims to address the following needs; Inaccessibility to safe water sources for drinking and household use: Most communities lack access to safe drinking water. They depend on contaminated sources of water which include rivers, shallow wells, streams, ponds and hafirs. This project will provide safe drinking water from boreholes and mini water yards.• Risks encountered by girls and women in accessing water Women and girls face challenges and risks when accessing water. Some water points are far from households and this increases the risks of abduction, physical assault and sexual abuse. This project will minimize risks by conducting Gender risks analysis, and drill boreholes in locations deemed safe by the communities and creating awareness about GBV through sensitization campaigns..• Household hygiene and sanitation practicesMost communities lack hygiene and sanitation facilities like latrines, hand washing facilities, bathing shelters and rubbish pits. They lack knowledge on good practices of both household and personal hygiene practices. This project will promote hygiene and sanitation through CLTS triggering and conducting hygiene and sanitation campaigns.• Gender inequalityIn most cultural settings, women and girls have no rights over resources, the girl child’s education is not prioritized, and they spend most of the time doing house chores like fetching water, washing clothes, cooking food, looking for firewood and taking care of the young ones. This project will promote gender equality through sensitization and awareness campaigns.

2 - Activities

GBV RISK ANALYSIS FOR EVERY COMMUNITY RECEIVING WATER POINT (55 VILLAGES)GBV Risk Analysis identifies potential risks of the Project on GBV, gaps in service delivery, gaps in security, access, distance covered, privacy, lighting, segregation, help desk, special needs, and first aid services. The analysis checks for inclusion of Gender related issues in WASH Programming, throughout the Program Cycle (Abuse of power for sex, monitoring tools, reporting mechanisms, linking services to beneficiaries and how women are involved). Findings of GBV Analysis will help decision makers in risk mitigation/ averting risks related to GBV and promote gender equity. DRILLING OF NEW BOREHOLES (16 BOREHOLES)Drilling of new a borehole involves geo-physical survey, drilling with either mud or air rotary method, installation of casings (plain and screen casings), well development, pump testing, construction of platform, installation of hand pump and water quality testing. REHABILITATION OF BOREHOLES (35 BOREHOLES)This involves assessment of the broken down borehole to determine the spares required and local materials needed. Major repairs involve complete change of the borehole parts including construction of a new platform whereas minor repairs involve change of damaged parts. Water Quality Analysis is conducted after completion of the rehabilitation. REHABILITATION OF MINI WATER YARDS (4 MINI WATER YARDS)This involves assessment and replacement of damaged parts such as solar panels, control boxes, submersible pumps, tower, cables, water tanks, water collection points, and fence and drainage channel. Water Quality Analysis is conducted after completion of the rehabilitation. WATER MANAGEMENT COMMITTEE TRAINING (55 WATER COMMITTEES)The Water Management Committees are established and trained. They are equipped with knowledge to operate and maintain the borehole. CLTS TRIGGERING (10 VILLAGES)Community Led Total Sanitation is the approach that will be used to combat open defecation. Households will be triggered with the CLTS Tool, which ignites a sense of disgust and shame among the community which drives them to construct their own latrines using locally available materials without any external support.

6.5 - Includes perspectives of displacement-affected people?

• The project caters for displacement-affected people. WASH services are provided for host communities, IDP's and returnees. In Raga County, Western Bahr El Ghazal State, this project targets mostly IDPs whose figure stands at 5,907 people according to the SSD-WC-2019 Severity Analysis Matrix NCT.

6.2 - Is the project linked to government/community systems?

Yes, the project is linked with government/community systems. The project is filling gaps in WASH identified by government bodies and IRNA (Initial Rapid Needs Assessment) by various humanitarian actors. Initially the government is supposed to deliver services to her citizens, however given the current situation of South Sudan, the government is unable to deliver the needed services to all the citizens. Therefore partners like NGOs and UN agencies step in to fill the gaps. IAS starts inception meetings with all stake holders who include line ministries at state level, authorities at County, Payam and villages (Including beneficiary communities). They are involved right from the inception of the project throughout its implementation and closure. They participate in decision making, i.e. site allocation and implementation.

6.3 - Is the project aligned with national objectives?

Yes, the project fits into nationals objectives of the WASH Cluster. Objectives 3 of the WASH cluster is to Provide timely/sustainable, equitable access to safe water, sanitation and hygiene for IDPs, host communities and returnees and objective 4 is to Mitigate WASH related gender based violence.

6.1 - Is the protection environment sufficiently safe/stable?

Northern Bahr el Ghazal is relatively peaceful with fairly free movement across all the 5 Counties of Aweil Centre, Aweil East, Aweil South, Aweil West and Aweil North. Raga in Western Bahr El Ghazal has undergone heavy wars in the past years, however the rebel and government forces ceased hostilities following the signing of the revitalized peace agreement in September, 2018. Currently there is relative peace which provides an enabling environment for humanitarian intervention.

6.4 - Does the project include sustainability/handover?

• When the water points are constructed and water quality tested. Water committees are trained and empowered to oversee the operation, maintenance, and sustainability of the water points. The committee is encouraged to collect water user fees which they will use to repair the water point in case of any breakdown.• The water management committee receives a certificate of ownership and sustainability.
