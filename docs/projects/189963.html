Project Summary

Overall the project aims to achieve “Improved living conditions of IDP communities as a result of sustainable and inclusive access to WASH”. The project Objective is: To improve access to safe water and influence the adoption of good hygiene and sanitation practices in the IDP communities in the Administrative Post of Ocua-Sede and Mueda-Sede (MPEME)

1 - Briefly summarize your project, including key target groups

The project will provide direct assistance to 1,960 IDPs people, This includes 60% women who will be assisted through WASH interventions in two districts, namely Mueda and Chiure Districts, in the resettlement camps of MPEME, Administrative Post of Mueda Sede and Samilala A and B, in the Administrative Post of Ocua (Chiure). Dorcas will provide clean water to 1960 IDP people through constructing of one solar powered elevated water supply system with taps in the resettlement center of Mpeme, including water network connection. In Chiure side, 3 water boreholes, equipped by Afridev pumps will be built to assist IDPs. Additionally, on Sanitation and Hygiene interventions, Dorcas will promote the construction of household latrines and provide latrine slabs. The project will also promote hygiene at household levels and will enhance measures on Covid-19 prevention. The interventions in Mueda and Chiure will complement activities being implemented by the Provincial and District Government institutions, UN Agencies and other local and international NGO’s. We will also be working in coordination with the INGD (National Institute of Disaster Reduction) for planning as they are the responsible entity for Disaster Management in the country. Dorcas will implement directly the project interventions through continued linkages with partners.

2 - Describe the priority activities your project will undertake, please detail priority activities by geographical areas you’re targeting?

Key activitiies to be imp-lemented under this project are:• Mobilize, raise awareness and organize beneficiaries;• Construction of one water supply system equipped with reservoir and 6 taps.;• Construction of 3 water boreholes, equipped with Afridev pumps.• Distribution of "Certeza" chlorine solution for water treatment in the communities where there is no water pumps;• Training, mobilization and monitoring of proper use of water treatment products;• Promotion activities for self-construction of household latrines;• Distribution of latrine slabs for 1000 families;• Capacity building of IDP activists to promote hygiene and MHM messages;• Community and household level hygiene promotion activities;.

5 - Protection Risk Analysis
5.1 - Describe how the proposed project identifies potential risks, and avoid causing harm?

Through direct face-to-face meetings with project staff and volunteers (steering committee members and sub-units), in writing, and also virtually (directly or through steering committee members). The steering committee will consider and choose from such alternative complaint handling methods as mobile hotline, complaint box, and posters (with information how to fill out a complaint). Vulnerability criteria will be applied to identify and select the most vulnerable and needy beneficiaries and avoid inclusion and/or exclusion errors. A participatory process will be applied involving community leaders and local government authorities. The final master beneficiary list will be endorsed by community leaders. Host community members will be explained the benefits of the programme and especially the interventions that extend benefits to the community at large in Livelihood Restoration and mainstreamed interventions.

5.2 - How does the proposed project encourage meaningful access to assistance and there is participation and empowerment of people?

In collaboration with concerned government offices and the community at large, the project will put in place appropriate complaint mechanisms to make sure that the activities will implemented according to plan. A committee will formed, and will have sub-committees whose members are composed of multiple stakeholders (religious institutions, schools, administrations/villages chiefs). Each sub-committee will be in charge of response actions in their respective areas. Using the sub-units, the committee system will orient the beneficiaries concerning the details of the planned benefits/distributions/activities, their roles in beneficiary targeting, ways of filing their complaints on the response program, and other related details. For any complaints, the beneficiaries will first report to their respective sub-committees, and then to the main steering committee. Issues not addressed at committee level will be reported to Response Task Force, in which organization is also a member.

6 - Disability Inclusion
6.1 - Explain how the project will identify people with disabilities and remove barriers to their access to assistance ?

The preferences of the target population, that includes disabilities, will be adequately considered and decisions concerning which methods/channels to use will be jointly decided, considering international standards and safety issues.The targeted IDPs are all poor and include vulnerable women, children, the elderly and people living with disability.

7 - Environmental considerations
7.1 - How does your project impact on the environment, is there mitigation measures or recommended actions such as environment assessments that are planned for?

The project will be implemented in such way that will not harm the environment. All staff and partners of this project will be mobilized to respect and fulfill local culture and protect environment. The type of tolls and machineries to be used in drilling will environmental friendly and will not harm local environment.
