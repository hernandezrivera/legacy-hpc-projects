Project Summary

Four stations have been equipped in Al-Hasakah Governorate. Each station has a capacity of 10 cubic meters per hour. The station operates at least 8 working hours, meaning 80 cubic meters of drinking water are produced per day for each station. The production of the four stations is estimated at 320 cubic meters, which is equivalent to 320,000 liters per day. There are ten Small auxiliary stations, the capacity of the station is 1.5 cubic meters per hour, but the four stations are considered the basic ones in the region because the ten small stations do not operate continuously and have a specific regulation as they do not work more than 5 hours a day. The production of the ten stations is estimated at 75 cubic meters per day for the entire stations at their maximum capacity, i.e. The equivalent of 75,000 liters per day, and it is considered the main artery for the entire city center. According to an official written source for the Water Corporation in Al-Hasakah, these stations serve 100,000 people of different ages, genders, and different situations (women with breadwinners - people with disabilities and their families), meaning about 20,000 families, but these stations need To equipment and desalination materials to operate them, bearing in mind that these stations are not sufficient to fully consume water for all families, but the majority of families consume them for drinking only, as they depend on the rest of their needs from filling tank water, since its source is unreliable for them, in addition to that they cannot bear the costs of main dependence on These tanks
