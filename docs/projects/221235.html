Project Summary

Islamic Relief Worldwide- South Sudan is a Humanitarian International Organization that has been responding the humanitarian crisis in South Sudan since 2003 providing assistance to crisis affected population through key thematic areas of WASH, Food Security and Livelihoods, Health, Nutrition, Gender Protection and Inclusion. Amidst the humanitarian looming crisis, Islamic Relief South Sudan continues to responds to emergency crisis with lifesaving intervention, promotes resilience and development approaches that offers long-term and durable solutions to the challenges communities are facing. The key drivers of Humanitarian crisis in South Sudan that has recurrently been causing havoc includes manmade disasters such as conflict and effects of civil wars, and natural disasters (mainly flood & drought). Islamic Relief’s WASH interventions is aligned with the WASH cluster Strategic Operational Framework 2023, which aims at contributing to achieving Goal 6 of the Sustainable Development Goal (Target 1- 8). Through this HRP, Islamic Relief South Sudan intend to respond to and address human suffering at the targeted counties (Tonj North, Gogrial East and Kapoeta East Counties) through provision of climate smart water infrastructure to provide clean and safe water, improved sanitation and promote good hygiene services while integrating interventions and approaches with Nutrition & Health, Gender and protection sector. This HRP will be implemented in Tonj North, Gogrial East Counties of Warrap state and Kapoeta East of Eastern Equatoria state that falls under priority area 2. Therefore, this project “Enhancing equitable and sustainable WASH services for Internally Displaced Persons (IDPs), Returnees and Host Communities in Warrap and Eastern Equatoria states of South Sudan intends to achieve the following objectives:
i. Contribute to reducing WASH-related morbidity and mortality in priority counties through WASH integrated programming with Nutrition, Gender and protection.
ii. Provide access to basic WASH services in emergencies.
iii. WASH specific activities for GBV mitigation
iv. Community members’ exposure to environmental hazards is reduced through the construction of disaster-resistant structures.
The proposed action intended to provide lifesaving inclusive WASH services targeting 28,000 people (Men 7,000, women 7,840 Boys 6,440 Girls 7,720 & PWD 1,400) people in need in Warrap state (Tonj north and Gogrial East Counties).This project is anticipated to positively impact in the lives of returnees/refugees from Sudan, IDPs and Host Communities through increasing access to improved water supplies schemes, restoring dignity in women and girls through provision of gender sensitive water and sanitation infrastructure and improved hygiene products for women and girls, boys and men, elderly people, people with disability including people suffering with chronic illnesses
To achieve the project objectives, Islamic Relief South Sudan will be employ the Right Based Approach linked with the Accountability to the Affected Population (AAP) to address the pressing WASH needs and gender related concerns to minimize and mitigate the common causes of vulnerabilities.
This project strives to provide timely and equitable access to clean and safe water supply, gender & vulnerability sensitive water and sanitation infrastructures and hygiene services/products through construction of new and rehabilitation/upgrading of water facilities/sources (Hand pumped boreholes, and solarized water yards and sanitation facilities as well as hand washing facilities for improving hygiene. The project shall integrate the aspect Gender, Protection and Inclusion through involvement of the right holders in project implementation and monitoring processes through AAP mechanisms. Community led innovations and community capacity enhancement especially for WASH structures.

1. Needs

South Sudan continuous to experience multiple humanitarian crisis as result of numerous drivers which include disease outbreaks, environmental shocks caused by the adverse impact of climate change, prolonged flood that affected over 710,000 people across the country as of September 2024, insecurity, poor macroeconomic conditions, and widespread violence which is exacerbated by weak economic conditions and political differences, that has led to repeated displacement of at-risk populations. These challenges have led to access constraints and operational Interferences to project locations, as well as public health challenges and climate change resulted in to the dual phenomena of extraordinary flooding and predominant drought especially in eastern equatoria state (Kapoeta East county) and Warrap state (Tonj North and Gogrial East Counties) which have created severe impact on people’s livelihoods, impedes access to nutritional needs, limits access to education for school Children, Water Sanitation and Hygiene (WASH) and Health services. in the proposed project intervention areas. People’s access to safe and clean water and improved sanitation remains very low due to the highlighted challenges. According to UNOCHA, projected People in Need are 5.01 Million, out of the 5.01 Million PiN, over 2.25 million continues to lack access to basic WASH services. This clearly depicts that majority of the population still battles with waterborne diseases, malnutrition and very low Household production capacities thus, low income levels and recurrent sufferings amongst Households within the country. The widespread and prolonged floods which is linked to the effect of climate change, and high level of food insecurity including the effect of civil war Sudan that triggered massive influx of refugees and returnees exacerbated humanitarian situation in the targeted locations thus grossly increased gaps in Water, Sanitation and Hygiene sector especially areas where Islamic Reliefs Worldwide South Sudan is operating such as Tonj North, Gogrial East and Kapoeta East respectively. According to the September 2024, IRNA report conducted in the six Counties of Warrap state, 184,618 people were affected by flood and conflict (30,000 Tonj North, Gogrial West 48,828, and Gogrial East 25,218, Twic 24,876, Tonj South 15,600, Tonj East 15000).Under the WASH component, the IRNA report recommended the following urgent actions that which are incorporated in the proposal: Provision of life saving household water treatment tabs/PUR sachets, and filter cloths, awareness creation on sanitation and hygiene practices through trained community-based hygiene promoters and repair and rehabilitation of water points in phase 2 of the response.Furthermore, according to the IRNA findings conducted in Kauto Payam in Kapoeta East County, Kauto plateau is a residence to approximately 29,400 people, a figure provided by the local authorities and RRC. However, 6,566 people are reported to be affected population during the Murle cattle-raiding crisis. Key findings of the assessment included: limited access to safe water people, people access water from the surface water sources mainly streams are highlighted: There are no clean and safe water sources in the two villages visited, population depend on seasonal water from the ponds (during rainy season) and streams in the valleys (dry season), women and girls travel about 2 to 3 hours to reach water points during dry season (to & fro), some four water sources with hand pumps needs major rehabilitation, lack of water treatment tablets for Households, rampant open defecation across all the villages visited, no sanitary facilities installed at Household, institution or public places and very limited access to soap for practicing appropriate hygiene.

2. Project activities

This project intends to provide timely and equitable access to safe and clean water supply, safe sanitation and improved hygiene services to 35,000 people (Men 8,750, women 9,800 Boys 8,050, Girls 8,400 & PWD 1,750) vulnerable people affected by the conflicts, flooding and drought in the targeted project locations which covers; IDPs, returnees and host communities. Based on the needs highlighted, IRSS will undertake the following activities: - Drill 10 new flood resilient hand pumps, upgrade 4 high yielding hand pumps to solar powered water supply systems and rehabilitate 16 flood resilient water sources- hand pumps (10 Tonj North, 10 Kapoeta East, and 10 Gogrial East). Construction of 20 stances of latrines with flood-resilient design-Eco san/ Fossa alterna design (6 Tonj North, 6 Kapoeta East, and 8 Gogrial East). Four water sources shall be allocated to nutrition sites. Construct 5 blocks of latrines with disability inclusive design (inclusive of supportive rails, sitting pan and rump) that will support some nutrition sites, Train 30 water management committee (WMCs) consisting of 60% women to ensure proper Operation and Maintenance, provide hygiene awareness supported with soap to 35,000 people, Train of 30 Community Based Hygiene Promoters (10 Tonj North, 10 Kapoeta East, and 10 Gogrial East), Train and equip of 30 Community Hand pump Mechanics to ensure effective O&M, Provide standard WASH NFIs kits inclusive of menstrual pad to 5,833 Households covering IDPs, returnees and host communities, provide 2000 standard kits paired with hygiene promotion to children/caretakers, Establish and provide training for 10 School Health Clubs to promote good hygiene amongst school going children. Design assorted hygiene & Sanitation IEC materials printed in multiple languages, support the cluster to conduct KAP surveys at County levels that covers the three-targeted counties, and organize bi-annual WASH program review workshops.
