Project Summary

SoP intending to continue its provision of integrated community-based protection interventions and provision of life-saving protection activities to conflict-affected populations across Northwest Syria.
The Project aims to enhance the prevention and mitigation of protection risks, and respond to protection needs providing GBV,CP services to most vulnerable PiN with a focus on Women, Girls,Boys, and youth(14-24) (including PwDs), prioritizing critical gaps in life-saving protection services, and designed to be people-centered project supports self-sustainable protective environment for the most vulnerable IDPs,in targeted Community and camps.
SoP will Establish WGSS and CFS (in the same community center) centers will be in Dana, Ariha, Qourqeena and Darkosh Subdistrict (accessible for PwDs) . surrounding communities, camps and informal settlements of each location will be targeted by outreach teams.
SoP aiming to provide the following activities:
GBV (Structures and non-structured) :
1-Case Management for the most vulnerable Women and Girls , case fund will be allocated on the budget to support the needs of the most critical cases as per Protection cluster and SCs relevant SOP and guidance.
2-Awareness raising sessions through the mobile team within the community and nearby camps/informal settlements on the key GBV-related topics ( Including PSEA / COVID-19 sessions ).
3-Life skills sessions for Women and Girls.
4- Income-generating vocational training for the Women and Girls.
5-Periodical Safety Audits in the camps by the outreach team.
6-Establishment and support of community Women network.
CP:
1-Case Management for Children , case fund will be allocated on the budget to support the needs of the most critical cases as per Protection cluster and SCs relevant SOP and guidance (Inter-Agency Guidelines for Case Management & Child Protection will be applied).
2- Structured PSS sessions for children in camps and the CFS.
3- Awareness raising activities for children, parents, humanitarian workers, community members and camps management on key child protection concerns, focusing on following topics (Child Labour, FTR, Basic CP, Child Rights) Using UNICEF IEC materials.
4-Center-Based and outreach parenting skills sessions, using IRC Parenting Skills Manual.
5- PSS activities for children.
PM:
Protection monitoring surveys in coordination with PMTF.
Each center will be acting as primary portal to Share information and enroll/refer beneficiaries to the available services.
Information/Data will be collected under each activity as per the SoP’s internal reporting mechanism while taking into consideration proper data disaggregation and MoVs, Monthly figures will be shared with Clusters and SCs through 4Ws , Case Management tool and other reporting channels.
CB and technical support:
SOP have experienced technical staff and been implementing similar interventions since 2016 , Prior to the implementation the technical supervisors will carry-out mini training / refreshers to the staff , and comprehensive coaching and shadowing process will be implemented to ensure high-quality implementation of the proposed intervention.
Coordination:
Coordination with all protection actors will take a place prior and throughout the project lifetime in order to ensure smooth delivery of the proposed services especially referrals.
MEAL:
M&E plan will be in place in the beginning of the project including all the periodic visits and the expected reports from the field. The M&E team in the field; who will monitor all project’s activities, including ongoing assessment and review of the progress being made against the project's logical framework and budget (GBV M&E toolkit will be considered).

4 - Needs

various forms of GBV are experienced primarily by women and girls including restriction of freedoms, denial of services, domestic violence, early marriage, and GBV. Early marriage is common in those areas . SOP has a presence In the targeted areas and conducted a rapid needs assessment in the proposed locations, it has been confirmed the needs to provide life-saving specialized quality GBV services, including case management, psychosocial support and establish referral pathways. The large number of women-headed households, children, older persons and women with disabilities, are particularly exposed to heightened risks and forced unsafe means of survival. early/forced marriage and domestic violence continue to pervade the lives of women and girls Covid-19 situation is significantly impacting children’s and caregivers’ psychosocial well-being and increasing the risks of many CP-related risks as Child labor and separation from usual care givers. children are in need for focused non-specialized services. children are at risk of many protection concerns such as child recruitment, child marriage and worst forms of child labor. SOP will work on improving the quality of community based child protection through supporting community structures and psycho-social support interventions, providing case management to the most vulnerable children and raising the community awareness. The project will ensure inclusiveness, and meaningful access. Sources: Voices 2022/MSNA 2022/HNO

9 - Protection Risk Analysis

The project will contribute significantly to the gender marker as it specifically addresses services to vulnerable population. Child protection seeks to prevent and respond to abuse, neglect, exploitation of and violence against children in emergencies. Providing CP specialized Services to including case management and PSS services risks are: The lack of quality services exposes beneficiaries to harm, enhancing risk of stigmatization reprisal and further trauma. The mitigation measures for this risk is the services mapping including those that can provide CP centered services in coordination with CP sub-cluster. Enhance strategies to empower women and girls and prevent GBV The security situation remains stable and the center remains accessible. The acceptance of SOP and its center by the local community and the local authorities is sustained. SOP will leverage the presence of its staff in the proposed locations. The need for GBV program in the area persists. Risk 1: the confidentiality of the GBV is breached. mitigation 1: strict disciplinary measures will be enforced and train the staff on the code of conduct .Risk 2: beneficiaries will not attend the vocational training regularly. mitigation 2:beneficiaries will be consulted on the topics, the pace of the course and they will be able to give feedback. Another risk is the Privacy concern which might prevent boys and girls to access CP services. Mitigation: ensure confidentiality principles are in line with global standards


10 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

Risk of exploitation or abuse by staff members/service providers if access to services or information are linked to a fee or limited to/excluded for specific persons/groups. SOP will provide regular training to staff and service providers for code of conduct and PSEA Policy, and will conduct induction sessions for new staff to ensure that they fully understand the content of CoC and PSEA policy. Strict disciplinary measures will be applied on any breach of the CoC. Affected population will be consulted on the convenient reporting channels to ensure effective and timely response for any compliance. SOP is a member of PSEA network (Adopting network's CoC and Policy) and regularly coordinating on the relevant issues and disseminating the central hotline numbers during the provided activities along with the organizational own reporting channels and hotlines. SOP periodically assessing the targeted communities on the suitable reporting channels for them and feed the information into threatening the existing CRM and deploying new channels when needed.


11 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

SoP reviewed couple of Risks/assumption and mitigation methodologies for each assumption as follows: Assumption/1: Targeted communities welcome our activities and don't interfere with our work. Risk: The local authorities decide to prevent us from implementing the project or part of it. Therefore, we will not be able to access high-risk GBV cases and we will not be able to follow up on the GBV survivors’ cases whose ﬁles are open. Mitigation: We will coordinate with the technical local councils and raise their awareness on the importance of the project and the impact of it, and will coordinate also with the Access Group. Assumption (2): The COVID-19 pandemic is stable and no positive cases are recorded in Northwest Syria. Risk: COVID-19 outbreak with positive cases recorded and restriction of movements, and different complicated implications. Mitigation:All Protective measures will be taken by the project team, PPE materials will be secured for all staff members. Activities will be continued as possible considering the remote sessions through WhatsApp and e-platforms until the situation is stable again so we can re-activate the in-person sessions taking the precautionary measures in line with WHO/Guidance and recommendations. Assumption (3): The CP intervention welcomed and accepted by community. Risk: that existing traditional practices and community structures/ Cultural norms conﬂict with child protection principles.Mitigation: Liaise with "neutral" parties a sensitize info
