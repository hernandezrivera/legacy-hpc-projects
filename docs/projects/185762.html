Project Summary

The AHP Bangladesh Consortium is designed to contribute to the overarching DFAT Bangladesh Rohingya and Host Community Humanitarian Package (2020–2022). Partners will work towards all DFAT package high-level outcomes: basic needs, self-reliance, resilience and reform.
To meet the immediate life-saving needs of Rohingya refugees and host community members including persons with disabilities residing in Cox’s Bazar district through provision of education, emergency Water Sanitation & Hygiene (WASH) and protection support with a specific focus on child protection and Gender-Based Violence (GBV) prevention. Project covers WASH in camps 10, 13 to meet beneficiary WASH needs with the coordination of clusters, Camp authority, local Government and existing AHP consortium partners (WVI, CBM, CDD and BGS) will be involved in equal efforts. The project targets 27000 individuals in camp 10 & 13.

1 - Identified Needs

Total 27000 population in the 4 allocated blocks of camp 10 & 13 will receive basic WASH services from the this project. There will be no new construction of WASH facilities under this project rather it will focus on operation and maintenance of existing WASH facilities. Due to regular use, heavy rainfall, land sliding there will be need for regular repairing of latirne, bathing and tubewell. Excreta management and solid waste management is the most critical need in the area which World Vision will be implementing. Here are the basic needs identified in the respective area;1. Supplying of safe drinking water through regular repair & maintenance of deep tubewell.2. Regular water quality monitoring of deep tubewell 3. Access to improved latrine facility 4. Access to improved bathing facility5. Fecal sludge management for the latrine facility in the blocks6. Solid waste management of household and community7. MHM kit for adolescent girl and woman8. Hygiene knowledge for the community 9. Complain and feedback mechanism system for the community people.
