Project Summary

The main objective of the project is to contribute to the resilience and cohesion of societies and families through housing improvement in targeted communities through rehabilitation of damaged apartments in Rural Damascus , Rural Latakia, Homs and Deir ez-Zur by rehabilitation of 500 apartments for 500 H.H ( 2,500 individual) in 2022. And 265 apartments on 2023.
The project aims to support the sustainable rehabilitation of the homes of the affected and the returnees families in the most vulnerable areas.
The response modalities used will be direct assistance to carry out rehabilitation work on a small scale for the private apartments.
Since the conflict in Syria before ten years most of the population has been forced to the flee their homes often multiple times which affected the quality of life and exposed them to multiple risks and violations of their rights, there have been countless deaths and devastating injuries, illness and no humanitarian dignity.
The results of the crisis variables and the secured situations of many areas and cities in Syria have led to various needs. These results are obvious particularly in areas that are receiving newly returnee families.
Despite the large destruction and damage to facilities and buildings, an active returning movement of IDPs has been noticed to , Deir ez-Zor, Homs, rural Damascus, and rural Latakia .
Through this project, 500 families will be targeted and encouraged to return and settle in their original areas of residence by rehabilitee their own homes.
Criteria of selecting beneficiaries in this project will be as following:
1- Poor IDPs returnees are in need of rehabilitation support to make their houses habitable
2- Families with disabled member, elderly or sick member’s (chronic diseases)
3- The vulnerable families with widows, divorced, orphaned children and extreme poverty
4- Household without or non-sustainable financial income to cover the life needs such as expenditures for utilities, rent, food and daily expenses
5- women –headed household families and had no job.
6- families have children specially large number (more 4).
- Criteria of selecting apartment in this project will be as following
1- The apartment should be structurally sound
2- A title-deed should be a formal confirmation for beneficiary ownership of the apartment
3- The apartment should be safely accessible in the building to the common works.
In 2023 project, the targeted areas will be as following:
1- Deir-ez-Zor 50 Apartments.
2- Homs city 50 Apartments.
3- Homs/ Tadmor 50 Apartments.
4- Lattakia/ Rabee'a 50 Apartments.
5- Rural Damascus/ Nashabiyeh 40 Apartments.
6- Rural Damascus/ Babella 25 Apartments.
Thus, The total numbers of individual beneficiaries will be 1325.

3 - Needs

These rural areas witnessed armed conflicts between several parties of the Syria conflict, and as a result of these conflicts, families were forced to flee from their original areas to safer areas and live in shelters or with host communities.Currently, these areas have witnessed several waves of return for displaced families, after they became safe areas.But unfortunately, most families were not able to settle in their homes due to the damage and loss of their property, and some settled partially in their homes, even if some parts of their house were damaged GOPA relies mainly on humanitarian reports such as the HNO and HRP recommendation reports as well as its own Beneficiary Registration Management System (BRMS) that reflects the needs assessment carried out by GOPA-DERD mobile teams in all 13 out of 14 governorates in Syria. This conducted needs assessment aims to provide an overview of the situation in the areas based on beneficiary input in one-on-one interviews with beneficiaries who visit GOPA 56 service centers scattered across the country.Also HNO 2023 data shows number of PiN (5,916,585) comparing with HNO 2022 PiN (5,916,585). Which indicate nearly same needs.Regarding the targeting of the Palmyra area and others areas through this proposal, it is due to the fact that the GOPA is currently implementing rehabilitation projects for returnees apartments in these areas, and it has a database for the number of displaced people who wish to return to the area.

4 - Implementation of activities

The rehabilitation works for target apartments will be achieved through the following procedures . 1- Getting the needed approval from the authorities about project and target beneficiaries. 2- Arrange FGD with communities to explain the project objective and beneficiaries’ selection criteria's. 3- Technical assessment of the buildings with observation to the structural soundness and inclusion of common areas such as the entrance, staircase, roof and water and wastewater pipes, plumbing work 4- Preparing BoQ for each apartment. The scope of work will be clearly discussed with and explained to the beneficiary household. (BoQ) with detailed specifications should be shared with the governorate once ready.5- Technical follow-up (Follow up - supervision).6- Once the implementation is over GOPA will hand-over the project to the beneficiary household and to the handover committee that is formed by the concerned Governorate/Municipality/ and will receive a signed handing-over certificate. 7- GOPA will monitor implementation of rehabilitation works regularly to ensure that the scope of work and quality of materials and workmanship follow the set BoQ, drawings and specifications and the timely completion of the planned work.

8 - PSEA (Protection from Sexual Exploitation and Abuse) strategy

At the hiring phase of new volunteers, and within the scope of GOPA-DERD’s Recruitment policy and whilst developing the capacities of existing volunteers, GOPA-DERD makes sure that all new volunteers sign the PSEA, Code of Conduct and Whistleblowing policies after conducting training on it. Also the contractor should sign the Code of Conduct before starting the implementation of rehabilitation works.All our activities use a rights-based approach to provide a protective environment that promotes equity and non-discrimination, reinforcing the basic social and human rights of participants while taking into consideration the cultural, historical and social specification of targeted populations.Also, all our volunteers have already received a package of training including: psychological first aid, non-violent communication, protection induction, basics of SGBV, first aid, PSEA and risk management in case of any emergency, as well as other basic information that enables them to act towards any person in need with total respect and most importantly to deliver assistance while protecting the dignity of the person.GOPA-DERD uses various channels through which beneficiaries can submit their concerns, complaints, needs, or suggestions. A complaints mechanism is established and communicated to beneficiaries via flyers and in person to ensure beneficiary feedback and timely responsiveness by GOPA-DERD to any issue that may arise.


9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Operation Risk: Risk of hurts to families community or owners due to unsound buildings or from ERW during preparing BOQ and implementation. likelihood: Medium, Impact: High. Mitigating Measure: 1- Structural assessment for building by qualiﬁed engineers and ERW experts. 2- repair the building after assuring the soundness and safety of building. 3- clarify for workers about the dangers of ERW and to inform about any strange materials (GOPA will seek the support from the security authority in the target areas and coordination with the UNMASS) Operation Risk: Inflation of exchange rate of US$ in the market (Medium risk). Impact: Medium. Mitigating measures: GOPA will coordinate with shelter sector and OCHA in order to increase or decrease the number of targeted apartments according the budget. Level of risk: Acceptable.Operation Risk: Spread of COVID-19. Impact: Medium. Mitigating measures: in this project the beneficiaries will be contacted individually thus, there is no gathering of beneficiaries in one place. In addition, GOPA is keen to follow all the virus protection measures followed for such projects
