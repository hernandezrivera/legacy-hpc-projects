Project Summary

In the midst of increasing IDP caseload in Somalia, AVORD is submitting this proposal with the aim of enhancing the living conditions of the Internally Displaced People (IDPs) through enhanced coordination, access to information and multi-sectoral services at site level, site maintenance, and durable solutions. Proposed interventions will covers IDPs in need in Banadir, Bay, Lower Shabelle, Hiiraan, Gedo and Galgaduud. Combined, these regions host 1,337,000 IDPs, or 51% of the entire IDP population in Somalia (OCHA HNO 2019).

Project Activities

To achieve cluster objectives, AVORD will undertake a number of interrelated and complementary activities including setting up and strengthening of camp governance structures where necessary to enhance information-sharing and foster system-wide coordination among service providers. Besides, AVORD will support community-led site maintenance activities through cash for work schemes to ensure upkeep of sites, enhance hygiene and sanitation levels and contribute to overall reduction in communicable diseases. Where necessary, AVORD will implement emergency site improvement projects to minimize protection risks and ensure safety.Key program activities will also include service mapping and site monitoring processes that will be carried out in close coordination with the CCCM cluster and other partners to effectively address existing gaps and maximize efficiency of the humanitarian response. Compiling of a master list of sites and mapping of both accessible infrastructure and partners operating in sites will be prioritized to enhance service delivery and coordination. In the selection of beneficiaries to participate in program activities, AVORD will adapt a systematic approach with a clear criterion to prioritize most vulnerable households including the marginalized, single headed-households, large families and/or families with children under the age of 5, women at risk as identified by protection officers and those with special needs. Data collected to inform beneficiary selection will be segregated by gender to ensure that no group is left-out. The effectiveness of the aid package will be evaluated through monitoring of activities thus ensuring that beneficiaries access their services safely and in a dignified manner. Protection will be mainstreamed throughout project duration by closely coordinating with other partners operating Sexual and Gender Based Violence (SGBV) crisis centers and facilitating principled referrals where necessary. An elaborate feedback and complain mechanism will be setup to ensure accountability to the affected population.

Mitigation Measures

Mitigation measures to exclusion and marginalization based on status (2a):1) Undertake direct identification and registration of beneficiaries without use of middle men or gate keepers. 2) Develop and enforce an elaborate selection criteria with priority accorded to most vulnerable households including those from marginalized and minority groups.3) Adapt the highest levels of transparency in beneficiary selection and provide target communities with timely information on the selection criteria, organizational procedures and processes thataffect them. 4) Establish Joint project implementation committees involving different fabrics of target communities thus creating a platform where marginalized communities can share their grievances, if any.5) Operationalize a project-level hotline where target beneficiaries can share views and/or concerns. Mitigation measures for exclusion and marginalization based on gender and age:1) Collect and use data dis-aggregated by sex, age and diversity to inform beneficiary selection and maintain gender parity. 2) Ensure that activities are designed in a manner that address the unique needs of women, men, boys, girls and those with special needs.3) Establish Joint project implementation committees involving youth and women groups from target communities thus creating a platform where youth and women can share their grievances, if any.4) Undertake direct identification and registration of beneficiaries without use of middle men or gate keepers to ensure inclusivity regardless of age and gender.5) Develop and enforce an elaborate selection criteria with priority accorded to most vulnerable groups such as women, boys and girls. Mitigation measures for exposure of beneficiaries to violence:1) Continuously assess local dynamics and make plans accordingly. 2) Ensure that access routes to distribution points and services are safe with enough space fororderly and safe distribution. 3) Undertake program activities during daylight hours, ensuring ample time for beneficiaries to go back home before nightfall. 4) Adapt and observe the Do-No-Harm principles at all times to prevent unintended negative consequences.5) Provide maximum protection to beneficiary data and household information to prevent misuse and exposure of target persons to risk.

Please provide further explanation

This proposal is humanitarian in nature focusing on the coordination of life-saving assistance to IDPs in humanitarian crisis. However, it also contributes to building the resilience of targeted IDPs in a number of ways. On the one hand, strengthening of information sharing, camp management and coordination among humanitarian actors contributes to effective service delivery at site level which will translate into overall improvement in the living conditions of IDPs and their chances to access livelihoods. Multi-sectoral interventions are often more effective when coordinated, leaving IDPs more resilience to future shocks and able to cope with the grim reality of displacement. On the other hand, the project advocates the local integration of IDPs and enhancing living conditions in their places of origin, for those willing to return, thus providing durable solutions to the protracted IDP crisis in Somalia. AVORD will also support communities in IDP sites towards self-reliance in locations where prospects of obtaining land rights is achievable while advocating with local authorities for public land for displaced people. To maximize impact and sustain project outcome, AVORD will seek integration with other clusters providing response in areas where proposed activities will be implemented. Details pertaining to project activities will be shared through relevant clusters and 4W reporting matrix to avoid duplication of effort. Local communities will play a key role in different phases of the project to create sense of ownership which will contribute to sustaining of project outcomes. AVORD will strive to build local capacities thus ensuring that various community leaders and committees are able to sustainably maintain resources following project completion and handover.
