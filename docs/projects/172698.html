Project Summary

The project objective is to protect people from violence, neglect, and exploitation by providing access to life-saving child protection and GBV response services to targeted communities in White Nile, North Kordofan, and North Darfur. In order to accomplish this, Plan will provide high quality protection services and contribute to community stabilization and resilience. Protection services and capacity building activities will include: CP and GBV case management, distribution of dignity kits to vulnerable girls, multi-purpose cash transfer to vulnerable households and hosting UASC, and referrals mechanisms including to Family and Child Protection Units, training of social workers and partner staff on GBV, CP, and referral pathways, and psychosocial support to conflict affected children and youth. The provision of protection services must be paired with activities to increase knowledge of and access to those services. Therefore, Plan will also establish/strengthen and equip Community Based Child Protection Networks (CBCPNs), conduct community awareness session of CP, FGM, SEA and child marriage risks, and conduct sensitization on GBV prevention and response. Plan will foster communal stability and resilience by providing life skills training, parenting sessions, conducting community awareness sessions on peacebuilding and conflict transformation, and training duty bearers and community leaders on peacebuilding. Additionally, Plan will establish child-friendly community feedback and response mechanisms so that all project beneficiaries are able to provide feedback and file complaints.

1 - Needs

According to the Humanitarian Needs Overview 2021, 2.6 million children are projected to be in need protection assistance of which 1 million are IDPs (51 percent girls), 285,000 returnees (55 percent female), and 1.2 million vulnerable children in host communities. Further, of the 16,864 COVID-19 infections documented in Sudan, 74% are in Khartoum (Federal Ministry of Health). Throughout Sudan, the COVID-19 pandemic and the restrictions put in place to slow and prevent its spread have caused an increase in CP and GBV cases—particularly, there is increased sexual violence and abuse towards adolescent girls and women. Some reason for the increase in cases are loss of employment and living in close quarters under frustrating circumstances. There is also an increase in boys engaging in criminal activities and drug and alcohol use. As school are closed, boys are often vulnerable to recruitment into armed groups (particularly in North Darfur) and girls are exposed to sexual abuse and exploitation in farming areas and during collection of firewood. Girls and boys as well as youth and adolescents are also in need of social and economic support as the pandemic, high inflation, and rising prices have exacerbated the situation. Plan will need to support adolescent boys and youth who are engaged in child labor. Girls face challenges of Female Genital Mutilation/cutting, Child Marriage and child labor as the marrying of girls, who are generally subjected to FGM before marriage, is often a negative coping mechanism employed by families when economically stressed in order to reduce cost and acquire income through the dowry provided. Plan will seek to combat this effect by tackling issues around parental or adult care, social discrimination, disability, and the weaknesses of the formal and informal CP systems. In all states, there is a need to establish, strengthen, and equip CBCPNs and support systems, link them with stakeholders and government institution, build capacity to prevent and respond to CP issues. There are also specific protection floods related needs identified such as psychological distress due collapse of houses and loss of assets; raising awareness on risks of younger children drowning in the water or in collapsed latrines, exposure of children to Snake and Scorpion bites while playing, and on the increased child protection and GBV risks. In South Kordofan and North Darfur, UASC and children recently released from armed groups need fostering in host families, economic support, and family reunification and tracing services.

6.2 - Explain how your project aims to ensure protection mainstreaming during its design and implementation

Safety and dignity will be prioritized to avoid causation of further harm and prevention and minimising unintended negative effects or risks during the intervention; meaning access will be ensured through supporting people’s access to assistance and services in proportion to needy groups and without barriers of any discrimination; more attention will be given to individuals and groups who may be particularly vulnerable or have difficulties to access assistance and services; existing CRFM will utilized to have two way communication and acting upon concerns which could be measured by the affected population in regard to its adequacy and addressing raised concerns and complaints; diverse capacity building will be provided to communities to assist them claim their rights, including those related to other services. So, the project focuses on enabling factors to mainstream protection through conduction of safety audit to ensure do no harm and participation of different groups of female and male and empower women to be seen and voice out their issues. In this project the most vulnerable children including disabled children will be prioritized and supported including UASCs to reduce the associated risks and discrimination

7.2 - Please outline how the project or your agency will inform and interact with inter-agency approaches to accountability to affected people and feedback mechanisms, including SEA and abuse complaints?

Plan International is a member of several platforms through which it will ensure it connects to its own child safeguarding and child-friendly feedback and complaints mechanism. The organization will maintain consistent attendance and participation in the AAP/CEA Working Group. Plan International continues to support and will feed into the GBV hotline working with CVAW, and make use of the UNCT/ UNOPS collective call center to ensure issues of accountability to affected populations are dealt with. Within the project locations, Plan will ensure contribution to mainstreaming initiatives led by UNOCHA. Furthermore, the project will contribute to COVID-19 the pillar on RCCE (Risk Communication and Community Engagement). Plan will collect information from beneficiaries through CFRM currently operational in some targeted communities and will continue strengthening the systems that ensure do-no-harm principals, thus including provision of capacity to staff, partners and community segments and structures and filling gaps on PSEA focal point in all operational area.
