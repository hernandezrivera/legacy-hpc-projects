Project Summary

The one (1) year Camp Coordination Camp Management (CCCM), Shelter and Non-Food Items (NFI) project aims at providing assistance to 20,157 conflicts affected IDPs, living in formal camps and informal settlements in Adamawa, Borno and Yobe State through dedicated static & mobile CCCM teams, one shelter technical officer, and NFI officers and Assistant. Almost half of IDPs Population lives in camps or camp like settings. Despite this, low resources for Shelter/NFI and CCCM, leading to below SPHERE standard living in camps/host communities, lack of coordination on camp level and lack of an existing referral system for beneficiaries to accommodate especially the most vulnerable protection cases.
The proposed project is part of SHI’s wider programming. SHI currently implements a CCCM project in MMC at Cheazcoan, Shuwari5, Doron Baga, Tausayi and Umara Bolori.
The proposed project will cover Shelter and NFI gaps and strengthen camp coordination mechanisms and site facilitation in Adamawa, Borno and Yobe state through the activation of CCCM, Shelter/NFI activities which will provide a clear referral pathway for beneficiaries in order to ensure that sensitive cases are being referred to the correct partner in a swift and respectable manner, it will improve the camp infrastructure through urgently needed site upgrades and the establishment of shelter/NFI camp committees; and lastly it will build capacity of communities and local authorities on CCCM with good inclusion of community on shelter maintenance and NFI gaps identification. SHI will deploy dedicated camp management teams to the proposed locations. SHI is proposing to implement this project in Hong, Madagali, Fofore, Maiduguri, Konduga, Pulka, Bade, and Gujiba, LGAs of Adamawa, Borno and Yobe respectively targeting together 20,157 beneficiaries. The locations have been identified in close consultation with other CCCM/shelter/NFI/DMS sector and complement SHI’s ongoing operations in MMC.

1 - Needs

Large number of displaced populations owing to high flood which causes severe destructions of lives and properties in Adamawa, Yobe and Borno state, with the irregular economic effects on community by the lasting of the striking of the covid19 pandemic. Series of camps-camp like setting/host communities are housing displaced population as a result of increase in the trend of displacement, reintegration, and relocation to various communities even with the long insurgent in the northeast continues to evolve. with DTM figures of above 55,000 individuals in Rann (Kala/Balge) displaced/cut off, with little or no access to humanitarian assistance, and over 25,000 influxes into MMC, Konduga, Jere and above 25,000 displaced in Fika and Buratai environs, as a result of military operation to retake the affected LGAs affected and also chronic poor living condition which is rated below sphere standard. SHI will Establish CCCM activities and provide shelter and NFI assistance to displaced population in MMC Host Communities, Hong, Madagali, Fofore, Numan, Maiduguri, Ran, Konduga, Bama, Pulka, Bade, Gujiba, Fika and Potiskum. These activities are aim at complementing the Government, UN-Agencies and INGOs activities in these locations.

2 - Activities

SHI is targeting 20,157 participant in 2022/2023 CCCM Activities.Activity 1.0 Reception Centre in Ran: SHI will ensure adequate reception Centre management is in place and comprehensive first contact response to new arrivals is done, in close collaboration with IOM DTM SHI will assist in the registration of new arrivals during and after biometric registration/verification and also take vulnerability assessments and effective referrals for further humanitarian assistance as available ti needs. activity 1.1 SHI will directly and in close consultation Stakeholders (NEMA/SEMA, IOM) , IDPs leaders take gap analysis and need assessments to identify critical/urgent needs and share results with Sector. Activity 1.2 SHI will establish effective coordination and prompt information sharing mechanism: this is achievable by setting up of coordination mechanism, and maximum usage of CCCM Sector formal camp monitoring tool and monthly reporting to the sector; also establish bi-monthly camp coordination meeting. With once in a month meeting of different camp committee established, this is to give the community a lead in all intervention. Activity 1.3 Camp improvement, SHI will ensure the improvement and upgrade of site after site assessment in consultation with NEMA/SEMA, IOM and Community leaders, priorities will be placed base on urgent/critical needs identified, the upgrade will basically to reduce environmental risk such as flooding mitigation, fire mitigation and other associated environmental risks. Activity 1.4 Establishment of Complain Feedback Mechanism (CFM): SHI will maintain a good/confidential CFM to enable beneficiaries make inquiry about interventions, be a guide to planning and make necessary complain and get response from partners involve, this will include the creation of Complain desk, suggestion box, resolves issues desk and unresolved issues desk for proper accountability to partners Activity 1.5 Provision of camp closure activitities. Activity 1.6 provision of Shelter Assistance by taking in accurate level of assessment to ensure proper prot ection and dignifying the lives of the IDPs. Activity 1.6 Provision of Non-Food Items to Household with low income and little access to NFI/livelihood, to ensure Children and women are not expose to exploitation of any kind sue to hardship, with the help of our NFI committees we will identify gaps and household with disable people that required special attention.
