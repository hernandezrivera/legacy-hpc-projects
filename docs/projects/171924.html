Project Summary

The response capacity of communities living on NGCA to the threat of COVID-19 is reduced due to the humanitarian crisis, that has been ongoing for seventh year. The fighting, capital outflow and economic blockade of the region led to the desolation of WASH infrastructure. Protecting human health during outbreaks of infectious diseases, including coronavirus infection (COVID-19), requires the creation of safe sanitary and hygienic conditions and the supply of water. A significant contribution to the prevention of human-to-human transmission of COVID-19 virus infection will be made through consistent and evidence-based implementation of WASH and waste management practices at the community, household, and social institution levels.
The purpose of this project is, firstly, to provide sewer cleaning systems and personal protective equipment (protective suits, gloves, boots, glasses \ face shields and masks \ respirators) to housing and maintenance offices of the city of Yenakievo. This will allow the community to better service the sewer systems of their city, thereby reducing the likelihood of accidents, as well as significantly speed up the pace of their elimination, which directly affects the preservation of the city's sanitary and hygienic system.
Secondly, it is planned to provide access to water supply to paramedic and midwifery centers that provide basic medical services to rural residents. These institutions are extremely important for the population of villages remote from regional centers, they provide uninterrupted access to medical services. It is also planned to provide these points, their staff and visitors with disinfection products and PPEs in order to reduce the risk of the spread of COVID-19.
The project's beneficiaries will be 25,600 people, and USD will be required to meet these needs.

1 - Needs

The social infrastructure of the city of Yenakiyevo was damaged during the military conflict in the Donbas during active hostilities in the summer of 2014. However, at the moment, the locality is located at a relatively safe distance from the line of contact, which allows for humanitarian interventions aimed at resolving long-term problems. One of them is the obsolescence of sewer systems and their clogging, which can lead to accidents, and those in turn – to a sanitary disaster. Due to the lack of funding, as well as the imposed economic blockade, the community of the city of Yenakiyevo has faced with the constant problem of clogging the sewer system and its leaks, that are creating a real threat to the sanitary and epidemiological situation in the city. In this critical situation community has only a narrow range of ineffective methods of servicing the sewer system. Donbass Development Сenter accepted requests concerning the problem with sewerage from community of the city of Yenakiyevo at the end of 2019. In response to the request, a monitoring visit was carried out to confirm the needs and consultations with representatives of the city's utilities were held, the results of which were compiled based on the field report. The needs of the city community were confirmed. Also, during 2020, we registered 7 personal appeals from residents of Yenakiyevo regarding the situation with sanitation. Due to the fact that the sewerage system is used by all residents of the city (at the time of September 2020 – about 118,000 people), including social, educational and medical institutions, the implementation of this project is of critical importance for the city community. The impact of sanitation and hygiene systems on the share of mortality reduction is proportional to or even greater than the share of direct medical intervention. Therefore, it is impossible to overestimate the importance of maintaining a viable sewer system in the city. In case of malfunctions with it, residents of Yenakiyevo will find themselves without access to hygiene, the number of diseases will increase and additional epidemics may occur. Thus, the relevance of the project increases during the COVID-19 pandemic. According to the COVID-19 emergency response guide on water, sanitation and hygiene measures, prevention and control of infectious diseases in health care facilities, dated March 10, 2020, the need for proper disposal of fecal sludge that is generated in health care facilities is emphasized. The WHO recommendation of 23 April also highlights the need to adhere to health practices for health care workers. Employees should use the necessary PPEs, which includes: protective suits, household gloves, boots, glasses or face shields and masks. Delivery of sewer cleaning systems for the community of the city of Yenakievo will allow it to increase the efficiency of servicing sewers and sewers comunications through the use of mechanized methods. In the conditions of obsolescence of sewer comunications and the inability to replace elements of these comunications due to economic factors, such methods of cleaning and prevention of breakouts will increase the service life of existing sewer comunications. Despite the fact that sewer networks need to be replaced, such work is not possible in conditions of conflict and economic distress. Aware of this, community representatives develop a set of measures to extend the life of available resources and ask for assistance in purchasing the missing equipment. Speaking about the needs of paramedic and midwifery centers, according to information from open sources, there are 129 FAPs and paramedic centers in NGCA of Donetsk oblast. These institutions suffer from a lack of allocated funds, including for the purchase of PPEs, disinfectants and water supply.

2 - Activities & Outcomes

As part of the project, it is planned to: 1) Identification and verification of 10 paramedic and midwifery centers that need water supply.2) Implementation of field events and preparation of design and estimate documentation to assess the necessary work and to draw up defective acts.3) Implementation of works (construction of new wells or restoration of old ones, restoration of the water supply system, installation of pumping equipment, etc. depending on the needs identified during the assessment) to provide 10 paramedic and midwifery points with water supply. Restoration work will be carried out in compliance with State Building Codes, State Construction and Technical conditions, Building codes and labor laws.4) Purchase and distribution of PPEs, disinfectants and cleaning kits for 10 paramedic and midwifery centers5) Verification of the needs of the Enakievo housing and communal offices in equipment for servicing the sewer system and PPEs.6) Purchase and provision of equipment for servicing the sewer system and PPEs. In the course of consultations with employees of municipal utilities, based on the technical characteristics of the sewer system (current condition, pipe diameter, large area), Poseidon B40S channel washing machine (35/40 horsepower) was previously selected as the optimal one) (link: https://z-t.com.ua/product/seriya-b40s/). The equipment will be transferred to the municipal utilities of the city of Yenakievo and used for cleaning sewer networks. In turn, this will reduce the risks of sewer breakthroughs, flooding of housing stock with sewage, outbreaks of dysentery and other diseases, and forced displacement. All work will be carried out in cooperation with representatives of the local community based on their needs. All mechanisms of accountability to the affected population will be observed, a hotline, e-mail will function for receiving complaints and suggestions, and messages will also be received through the organization's social networks and an anonymous mailbox in the organization's office.
