Project Summary

UPO will focus primary to support 35,000 families and ensuring that the most vulnerable Women, men, boys and girls are the center of UPO ESNFI response. ESNFI Needs assessment will continuously be conducted with support from other partners to understanding the gaps and social issues affecting vulnerable communities and triangulate into programmes addressing these needs. the Communities in the target areas are in desperate need of NFI and shelter materials as a step of support improving their lives to cope in managing recurrent shock and build resilience. In order to have effective and efficient response to the needs, UPO will continue with its ESNFI response in Darfur, Khartoum, Gadaref, and South Kordofan. UPO will target the localities with it ESNFI interventions that scored 4/5 in the HNOs 2021. UPO will ensure close coordination with all partners throughout its interventions. UPO activities in target locations will include coordination of needs assessments, IES distribution, training, awareness raising on fire prevention, gender and environment protection, SSB shelter and NFIs distribution, mobilization of resources, sensitization of communities and coordination of responding to the immediate needs of the people.
The project will also focus on the awareness on the prevention of the flood , COVID-19 spread as well as preparedness and hazard mitigation messages including Fire and droughts in the vulnerable areas.

1 - Needs

An estimated 750,000 people have been affected by the flood in 2020 and over 150,000 people affected in 2021, and more 130,000 displaced by conflicts with majority in Darfur, Including some 30,000 people within South Darfur who have fled to Central Darfur with trends indicating continued displacement as armed violence Continues. Hundreds of thousands have lost their livelihoods and access to basic Services. The Jebel Marra massive, straddling Sudan’s North Darfur, Central Darfur and South Darfur states, has since mid-January seen significant civilian displacement as a result of increased fighting between the tribe and the factions of rebel in Jebel Mara localities. Ongoing conflicts in SK has resulted in an increased influx in 2020. The majority of people, in particular displaced, women headed households and those with no or limited productive assets, are living in a state of extreme poverty. humanitarian agencies made efforts to rescue people from appalling humanitarians’ conditions however, due to limited resources and immense needs across the country; it is far from reaching milestone of improving lives of all affected people. Amongst other basic human needs, one of the most immediate needs remains getting access to NFI and emergency shelter for vulnerable people in particular children, lactating and pregnant women, women headed household, newly displaced people, elderly and people with special vulnerabilities. Needs-based assessment will continuously be conducted in coordination with other partners to understanding the gaps and social issues affecting vulnerable communities and triangulate into programmes addressing these needs. Communities are in desperate need of NFI and shelter materials as a step of support improving their lives to cope in managing recurrent shock as result of the conflict, COVID-19 pandemic and flood disaster and build resilience. In order to have effective and efficient response to the needs, UPO will priorities localities that scored 4/5 in the HNOs 2020 will be prioritized to reach many people that are mostly in need of NFI and emergency shelter. UPO will ensure close coordination with partners throughout its interventions. UPO activities in target locations will include needs assessments, IES distribution, training, awareness raising on fire prevention, COVID-19, Flood,gender and environment protection, SSB and NFIs distribution, mobilization of resources, sensitization and coordination of responding to the immediate needs of the people
