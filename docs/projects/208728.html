Project Summary

In recent years, Adamawa State has been affected by outbreak of Cholera, Lassa-fever and other epidemic-prone related diseases, which resulted in Cholera and Lassa-fever related deaths in local communities.
This is due to poor hygiene practices, lack of access to portable water, conflict related displacement, lack of social development and poor household pests control, e.g rodent.
The project will focus on promoting awareness on cholera and lassa-fever early signs and symptoms, structured community response and access to health facilities, preventive measures, hygiene awareness, water preservation and community pests control.
To implement the project, Goggoji Zumunchi Development Initiative will collaborate with Adamawa State Ministry of Health, Adamawa State Health Services Management Board and Primary Health Care Authority to strengthen work-force capacity, support primary health care facilities, promote access to treatment and establish and/or strengthen cholera awareness centres for community members during clinical visit and women during antenatal appointments.
The project will further collaborate with relevant line ministries and agencies, to set up a mobile team of health workers, who will provide assisted referral and mobility access to health facilities for affected individuals, in an event of outbreak within communities.
The project will set-up and/or strengthen existing community structures and action groups (peer health educators and hygiene promoters), towards promoting awareness in local languages, for effectiveness in prevention and community awareness on, health education, good hygiene practices, pest control, water preservation, open defecation, general sanitation, community response and access to facilities for prompt case reporting.

1 - Needs

The project is designed to address vulnerability of local communities to outbreak of epidemic-prone diseases, including but not limited to cholera and lassa-fever.Resulting from lack of awareness, poor hygiene practices, poor pests control, lack of access to portable water, open defecation and other public health concerns.

2 - Activities

The project will target 60,000 individuals across Mubi South, Michika and Madagali LGAs, with awareness on cholera, lassa-fever and other epidemic-prone related diseases within local communities, through local community action groups (health education peer educators and hygiene promoters.The project will train 180 hygiene promoters and health education peer educators within communities of Michika, Mubi South and Madagali LGAs, Adamawa State. the project will further train and support 100 health facility work-force, towards effectiveness in preventive and responsive measures within health facilities, including community mobile teams.
