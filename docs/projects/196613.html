Project Summary

In line with the priorities of the health sector for the year 2023 to support the provision of the basic package of health care and mental health services on a large scale, and therefore the Charitable Youth Association will enhance the provision of the basic package of
Health care and health services in the eastern countryside of Homs (Tel Zubaida) through a medical point that includes three clinics (internal - women's - children's). Health services will support women - children - youth, the disabled and the elderly.
The village is about 16 km away from the city of Homs. Residents drink well water, and there is no sanitation in the village, which leads to the risk of disease spreading. In addition, there is no health center or primary healthcare services. Residents are forced to travel to the city of Homs when they need primary health services, and this is difficult for pregnant women, especially since the village does not have a gynecological clinic. It is also difficult for children and the elderly because there is no pediatric clinic or internal medicine. There is only a dentist and a pharmacy in the village of Tal Zubaydah, and it needs to provide primary and secondary health services. The population of Tal Zubaydah is 2,700 people, of whom 1,150 are displaced, and the medical point will support the village of Tal Zubaydah and - neighboring villages such as Al-Fahila, which includes 3,000 people - Al-Jamiliya, which includes 3,000 Nasma - Tal Al-Shih includes 2000 people - Tal Al-Naqra includes 5000 people. The medical point will support primary health care services including medical consultations, laboratory tests, x-rays, ultrasounds, and medication distribution. This point will support secondary services (C.T Scan - M.R.I - Eye Surgery - Orthopedics). Trauma care services (fractures surgeries - simple emergency surgeries and treatment) will be supported through this point based on the medical referral to a hospital in Homs city and assistive devices (medical glasses - hearing aids - disabled chairs) will be supported through this point base on medical referral to A hospital in Homs.
The medical point will work for 6 hours per day within the framework of the project
The charity studied health needs and reached out to villagers

Furthermore it; 85% of the population lives in poverty and the deteriorating economic situation means that the majority cannot afford basic needs such as food, shelter and health.
