Project Summary

This project will provide access to safe water and sanitation in targeted locations in Rubkona and Guit counties in Unity State, Aweil North in NBeG State. In Rubkona and Guit, this project will continue supporting routine operation & maintenance (O&M) of existing water systems/boreholes in Bentiu IDP camp, SWAT in Site E, Site D-2, Mankui and Bentiu Water Treatment Plant, Rubkona SWAT and existing Solar Water Yards in Guit. Concern is currently exploring funding from UNICEF to drill new boreholes in IDPs sites (E, D-2) to transit from continued O&M of SWAT in IDP sties to community managed solar water boreholes. Similarly, Concern has worked on its hand over plan for transfer of Bentiu Water Treatment Plant to State Ministry that will be done toward the end of this project. The plan for linking UNICEF support to reduce O&M cost of water systems and transit to community management of water sources is a new intervention; Similarly, implementation of transition plan for Bentiu Water Treatment plant will be a new initiative under this project.
The project will support routine O&M of communal latrines in Bentiu IDP camp, and Concern proposes few blocks of new latrine design (piloted by Concern in Sector-5) to address the challenges related to frequent repair, desludging and decommissioning of latrines. (The latrine with new design in Bentiu IDP camp will be a new intervention in this project). Based on recent discussion with USAID WASH advisor, Concern will conduct a cost analysis study for old and new latrine design, including various cost and non-cost parameters (life span/sustainability and local adaptability) for learning and sharing with other partners for adoption if validated by the study. Concern will also collaborate with other WASH partners piloting different design of sustainable latrines in Bentiu and through WASH cluster the partners will agree on suitable design for scale up. In flood displaced site (Site E, D2 and Mankui), Concern will support routine O&M of latrines and monitoring the cleaning of family shared latrines which is done by the household and cleaning materials are provided on need basis.
In Aweil North, the WASH services will be only linked to health/nutrition facilities and catchment population where Concern will conduct supervision and training of existing hand pump mechanics (provided with tools under Irish Aid Program) to conduct repair of any dysfunctional hand pumps in target areas. Similarly, the health and nutrition beneficiaries including mother and father groups will be supported with targeted messages and sensitization on adoption of behaviour/practices to construct their own latrines using local materials. (The WASH approach in Aweil North will only focus on linking to health/nutrition beneficiaries). The restoration of water supply will also be linked to nutrition outcomes including access to water for vegetable production at household level.
Concern will review the approach of hygiene promotion across all locations to integrate the health, hygiene and nutrition under a single team (Community Health Promoters) where appropriate. In IDP settings, Concern will focus investment on practices like cleaning materials, cleaning campaigns, hand washing, and hygiene, and support the eventual handover of projects to communities.

1. Needs

The WASH vulnerabilities across all proposed target location including Guit, Rubkona, and Leer counties are compounded by flooding. Areas hosting displaced people due to floods and Sudan crisis returnees induced settlements in Rubkona and Guit are facing increased risks of water-borne diseases due to inadequate access to water and sanitation far below SPHERE standards. The downward trends in humanitarian funding for South Sudan is likely to further complicate the resources available for WASH services. It is therefore critical to improve WASH services and strengthen system and enhance government and community capacities, to reduce the prevalence and impact of WASH-related diseases and malnutrition in locations with high GAM rates, WASH related vulnerabilities and protection risks. Source of water for site D2 and E is flood water which is used as a source for water treatment but due to less rain in the area, the flood water is receding. Since the usage is high i.e. 13,000 individuals are accessing water here, it seems that in 3-4 months, there will not enough water available for the treatment plants. The people will remain there for many years, since their native areas are still under heavy floods. There is need to develop deep boreholes wells (production wells) in these 2 sites and solarize therefore energy conservation and cost effectiveness and sustainability. Concern has taken this up with other stakeholders mainly UNICEF, OCHA and local authorities. Given the recurrent flooding there is need for resilient WASH facilities to improve sustainability and promote durable solution. Furthermore, it is critical to integrate WASH across health and nutrition centers to improve infection prevention and promote cross sectoral integration. The elevated level of food insecurity and poor WASH conditions continue to contribute to high levels of acute malnutrition. Given the already high GAM prevalence and poor access to WASH services, the proposed project is critical to improve community access to safe water and sanitation to avoid any further escalation of malnutrition crisis, and to reduce morbidity and mortality.

2. Project activities

CO1: The prevalence of high level of malnutrition is often linked to poor access to water and sanitation services. The WASH activities will be implemented in areas with high GAM prevalence and will be integrated and linked to Concern nutrition and health center. Concern will support access to safe water, sanitation and promote safe hygiene in communities with high malnutrition and food insecurity. Based on assessed needs, Concern will also repair and improve water and sanitation in nutrition and health centers. Concern will also implement Baby WASH activities integrated within nutrition programme. The SAM children form nutrition sites will receive hygiene kits (cluster stock). Through established network of the Mother/father groups and CNVs, the care takers will be sensitized on the hygiene including awareness on epidemics e.g. malaria cholera, measles etc.CO2: Concern will continue to support routine operations and maintenance (O&M) of an existing borehole in Sector-5 of Bentiu IDP camp. The storage tanks and pumping hours will be increased to meet minimum sphere standards (15lpd). In Bentiu and Rubkona town, the state authorities will continue to receive O&M support for existing water treatment plants, with continued efforts for gradual hand over of the water systems to state authorities. In Rubkona and Guit Counties, Concern will support construction and repair of boreholes/hand pumps to improve access to safe water while consider necessary DRR and environmental measures. Concern will develop deep boreholes wells (production wells) in sites D2 and E and solarize for energy conservation and cost effectiveness and sustainability. Concern has taken this up with other stakeholders mainly UNICEF, OCHA and local authorities. Concern will convert the Rubkona SWAT to Solar Water Yard along with supply pipeline to Rubkona Town. Concern has collaborated with UNICEF. This is one of the building block in our exit strategy from the management of Water Supply System in Bentiu and Rubkona. Sex-segregated semi-permanent latrines and bathing shelters will be constructed in flood induced displacement sites (E & D), Rotriak and at health facilities. Concern will continue implementing subsidized CLTS approach in target areas. In addition, Concern will continue to support waste management services. The project will support WASH cluster transition strategy on the gradual transfer of sanitation management to the community and government. Context specific hygiene promotion will be conducted to sensitize HHs on hygiene practices. The main focus will be on hand washing practices, correct and consistent use of latrines for all defecations, safe water chain including HH water transport, storage and consumption.CO3: Concern will conduct routine protection monitoring, gender+ and safety audits to adapt program design and approach accordingly. Prior to the construction of water points and latrines, Concern will conduct safety audit and use the WASH Cluster Protection Mainstreaming tip to ensure WASH specific GBV mitigations. The safety audit will analyses different needs, roles and dynamics to improve WASH interventions sensitivity to GBV risks.CO4: In flood prone areas, Concern has redesigned the apron and drainage, approved and adopted by the state ministry. Due to recurrent flooding and its impact on WASH facilities, Concern will engage with the community to ensure that WASH facilities to be built are not located in flood-prone corridors and design will incorporate DRR measures. The waste water of the hand pumps has no surface drainage available which creates environmental issues at most of the places, this water can be used for kitchen gardening etc. Concern will mobilize the communities to use this water by providing them kitchen gardening resources to muse this water which will minimize its hazardous effects and mosquitos, due to water ponding.
