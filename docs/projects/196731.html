Project Summary

Vulnerable households (IDPs, host communities) affected by the war in Syria in the selected locations have secured their survival with dignity by equal and just access to selected basic needs.
The following project activities will be done:
- Water trucking in Hassake city on daily basis to 50 communal tanks (4,500 HHs)
- Distribution of food parcels to IDPs and families in host communities for 6 months continuously to cover basic food needs for about 30 USD per parcel (rice, oil, sugar, lentils, etc. according to the need and beneficiary feedback) in Hassake city and Deir ez Zor (Al Sawa and Al Harmushiye) (4,800 HHs)
- Distribution of dignity kits to IDPs and families in host communities (women) accompanied with female hygiene promotion sessions in Hassake city collective centers (10,000 females)
- Distribution of three stage water filters (plus one UV stage) for water filtering to households in Deir ez Zor (Al Sawa and Al Harmushiye) (1,700 HHs)

3 - Needs

The project activities will assist IDPs and host communities in the governorates of Al Hassake and Deir ez Zor. According to the 2023 HNO, The overall People in Need (PiN) has increased from 14.6 million in 2022 to 15.3 million in 2023, including 5.3 million IDPs, 9.9 million residents and 52,700 returnees. In Al Hassake Governorate, the total population is considered as in Need (1.2 m), of which are 0.9 m in extreme and catastrophic need. In Deir ez Zor governorate the PiN stayed the same to 2022: 0.9 of 1.1 m people are in need and 0.3 m in extreme and catastrophic need. In Hassake the highest need per sector is in WASH with 1.2 m PiN, in Food Security more than 0.7 m people are in need. In Deir ez Zor there are 0.8 m PiN in the sector of Food Security, and 0.68 in WASH. The WFP remote food security monitoring and recentfood security and livelihood assessment in Syria (FSA,FSLA) estimates that seven in ten Syrian familiesis food insecure with the highest concentration within others in Deir-ez-Zor (56 per cent) and Al Hassake (53 per cent) Governorates. The high foodinsecurity rate is heavily impacted by the worseningagricultural situation which the people in thesegovernorates heavily depend on for their livelihoods. Critically bad sanitation conditions, poor hygienepractices and a deterioration in water provision and quality are increasing public health risks, incl. COVID and cholera outbreak in Al Hassake.

9 - Risks, Assumptions and Mitigating measures

Security situation in project region is getting worse due to military/political interventions. MM: Constant monitoring of the security situation, building up security management and back up structures. Establishing a contingency plan. Security trainings for staff. Local militant and/or extremist groups/local councils attempt to influence distribution of aid. MM: monitor the situation and ensure strict adherence to and lobbying for the humanitarian principles of neutrality and independence. If the principles are violated, the distribution/activities are discontinued.Communities or households not included in beneficiary lists may demand access to distributions to fill their needs. MM: The recent growing needs and lack of organisations’ capacities make this scenario realistic. To avoid violent conflicts on distribution points, the selection of locations has to be done in coordination with other aid actors or in another balanced way. Transparent information in preparation of distributions as well as information on the selection criteria is also required.Security situation will get worse and thus the distribution of humanitarian goods will be impaired. MM: Distribution of humanitarian goods is done by our local staff who are in regular contact with the local authorities in order to assess the risks/security situation on site. assess the security situation on a daily basis and give recommendations/orders to staff members.
