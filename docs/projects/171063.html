Project Summary

According to JMCNA 2020, Shelter is the first priority need of more than half of the population. 3.15 million People are in need of shelter and NFI assistance mainly due to inadequate shelter conditions, overcrowding, economic hardship and lack of security of tenure. Lower Shabelle is among the hotspot areas for shelter needs due to its being areas with most frequent and high risks of newly displacement and IDPs living in a high risk sites because of the floods, conflicts, droughts and evictions. In 2020, Lower Shabelle was badly affected by floods which drove more 85000 people out of their houses, destroyed shelter and washed away NFIs particularly in Afgooye, Wanla Weyn, Qoryooley and Kurtunwaarey, most of the needs were not addressed due to fund insufficient, these needs along with the growing needs due to the upcoming seasonal and security related conflicts are to be responded in 2021. Therefore, this project will deliver emergency shelter and NFIs service to 3000 HH (18,000 people 4200 male, 4200 Female 4800 Girls and 4800 Boys) to the people of newly displaced population and people of IDPs living in high risk sites in Lower Shabelle region by distributing 3000 ESK Shelter kit and 3000 NFI kit due to the unavailability of the NFI kits in the local markets and surroundings in the target area, alternatively, AYUUB will precure basic Non Food Items through Tender process in Bakara and Suuq Ba’ad Market. . During the project implementation, the partner will strongly consider site planning/decongestion activities in the IDPs sites to mitigate the risks of diseases including WASH and the Covid19 epidemic and other social risks, such fire outbreaks and to facilitate the easy passage of the IDPs in the sites.
By ensuring to meet with the Shelter Cluster two objectives in 2021 which are to Ensure that persons affected by conflict and natural disasters have protection from the weather and privacy through provision of shelter and NFIs and to Improve the quality of shelter and NFI assistance and ensure accountability through effective complaint and feedback mechanisms, the project will closely collaborate with the community key leaders, local authorities in a better design of the modality of the project through giving an open ear to the beneficiaries in community feedback mechanism that AYUUB has already put in place via the hotline number 2288, enabling the beneficiaries to channel their inquiries and complaints for a better accountability to the affected population
AYUUB will prioritize the Most vulnerable people in the Targeted areas through employing AYUUB Selection criteria Procedure to meet with the most needy and appropriate households by the project, with special consideration to the disable people, minority groups, female headed house hold and families with orphans.

Project Activities

In the move to accomplish the cluster objectives of to ensure that persons affected by conflict and natural disasters have protection from the weather and privacy through provision of shelter and NFIs and to improve the quality of shelter and NFI assistance and ensure accountability through effective complaint and feedback mechanisms, the project will involve in the following activities:-Activity 1.1: NFIs Through In Kind distribution: AYUUB will distribute NFIs through in Kind distribution to 3000 households (18,000 people 4200 male, 4200 Female 4800 Girls and 4800 Boys) due to the unavailability of the NFI kits in the local markets and surroundings in the target area, Cash is Impossible alternatively, AYUUB will precure basic Non Food Items through Tender process in Bakara and Suuq Ba’ad Market. The age and gender disaggregation is based on the demographic profile in Somalia and prioritization of women and girls who are the most affected by displacements. The actual gender and age breakdown will be reported once beneficiary registration is completed. AYUUB will promote the dignity of the beneficiaries by placing the decision of the content of priority NFIs in their best interests. The approach will empower displaced people in prioritizing needs and give them the power of making decisions for their needs.Activity 1.2: .ESK Shelter Kit though in-kind distribution by targeting the same 3000 HH (18000) in Afgooye, Wanla Weyn, Qoryooley and Kurtunwaarey districts and due to the unavailability of the Shelter kits in the local markets and surroundings in the target area, alternatively, AYUUB will precure basic Shelter Kit through Tender process.Activity 1.3. During the ESK Construction, Decongestion will be considered where by the Shelter Spacing in between 2-3 Meter, due to Space in the targeted areas and each distribution will be followed with a follow up monitoring.Activity 1.4. AYUUB will publicly mobilize to the Targeted community through microphones and meetings and placing the AYUUB Complaint and Feedback Mechanisms Hotline number / Free charge (2288) in the public gather areas for feedback and suggestions. Beneficiaries will be targeted based on vulnerability criteria that will prioritize women headed households, the elderly and children headed households especially girls. The beneficiaries will be distributed equally with each beneficiary getting the Standard NFI and Shelter kit approved by Cluster. AYUUB will closely coordinate with Other Agencies distributing NFI's in the areas and start the project implementation with community consultations and coordination with Local Authority to identify vulnerable people displaced by the drought.

Mitigation Measures

1- Risk of no access to or awareness of protection assistance or referral services for survivors of protection incidents (i.e. GBV, child protection, evictions, explosive hazards, etc.) is likelihood and to mitigate this risk, AYUUB will integrate the protection Project service with this Shelter project service for complimentary purpose and achieve the objectives of the two projects, enabling the two projects to work in a parallels. 2- Risk that the project does not have sufficient internal mechanisms to combat and prevent child safeguarding and PSEA. This risk is likelihood, but AYUUB will assign the PSEA staff to be involved in the project and give PSEA awareness to both project and beneficiaries prior the start of the project activities implementation and link all of them to the organization’s complaint and feedback department through the Hotline Number: 2288 which is free of charge to held the involved individuals with accountability to the concerned party. 3- Risk of women, girls and boys exposed to sexual violence, including sexual exploitation and abuse (SEA), when accessing humanitarian assistance.AYUUB will apply the same risk mitigation measures of point 2 ( above)4- Risk of interference from external stakeholders in the impartial and needs-based selection of beneficiaries thus negatively affecting social cohesion and sparking intercommunity conflict over access to resources is likelihood to occurred, AYUUB will first mobilize the Local authority on the service of the project and ask the government to support AYUUB to implement the project through provision of security and directive insights, in terms of accessibility and vulnerable members.
