Project Summary

The project is aimed at providing comprehensive Gender based violence services to 1000 survivors as wells protection services to 550 boys and 980 girls in Baidoa and Wajid Districts, Bay Region, Somalia . The services include Medical, Psycho-social and legal support. The Gender based violence survivors conditions will be improved through group therapy and hand craft skills training to ensure early recovery and formation of community support groups for smooth community reintegration. The project will in addition focus on the following child protection activities: Recreational activities and counseling as well as medical child support, building capacity of case workers, provision of material support and access to essential services for child survivors, Prevention and Response services for unaccompanied and Separated Children(IDTR ), Child Protection assessment and Monitoring of Child Protection in the targeted settlements as well as conducting awareness and community engagement and coordination with other partners and sectors.
