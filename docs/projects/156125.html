Project Summary

The collapse of the health system in northern Syria ( Manbej – Al Raqqa ) has pervaded every component of the healthcare continuum and has resulted in the breakdown of the availability of human resources, pharmaceuticals, vaccinations, and medical supplies and equipment. Availability of health services in northern Syria is ensured mostly by NGOs. Despite this, health continues to have acute problems including lack of health systems structures, poor articulation of emergency and referral systems and poor articulation of efforts among primary and secondary levels.
The situation is even more constrained due to the limited number of humanitarian organizations that can operate in the area. the severe gaps in healthcare provision and the limited response of agencies operating in the Northern East Syria ( Manbej – Al Raqqa )areas calls for an improved response based on PHC strengthening as well as the secondary health care services.
The Proposed project aims to respond to immediate emergency requirements for most vulnerable communities as well as cover the gaps in the health system to avoid any future collapsing in the system.
The proposed project will cover the salaries and running costs of the following facilities in the following target locations in order to improve access to health care services.
Primary health care based on the updated Essential Health Service Package agreed in the health cluster for northern Syria
IDA 10 PHC Level 2 in each of Manbej and Al-Raqqa district.
The proposed project will cover health and protection needs of both male and female, with a special focus on children, women and elderly.

Protection Risk Analysis

This project proposal has been designed on the basis of needs assessments consisting ofinterviews with key informants within the target population. The project staff will be hiredwhenever possible from the target area itself, ensuring intimate knowledge of the contextand good acceptability of the staff by the community. IDA has an incorporatedbeneficiaries feedback into their existing feedback system, through suggestion boxes ateach PHC, which are collected and reviewed by the M&E field officer on monthly basis.The M&E Officer will obtain direct patient feedback during the monitoring process.
