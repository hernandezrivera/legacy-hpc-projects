Project Summary

Beit Lahia Development Association (BLDA) proposed response is planned to be implemented in 2023 in the Beit Lahia locality is aligned with Strategic Objectives 3 as well as the WASH Cluster Objectives 3.
In close coordination with PWA and CMWU, the BLDA will complement the ongoing construction stormwater by MoLG through an extension of construct stormwater drainage systems in Beit Lahia to protect vulnerable women, men, girls, and boys from being in contact with flooded stormwater or wastewater during the winter season due to the accumulation of stormwater in the cesspits flooding in the streets and in schools.
The proposed intervention aims to support 5000 individuals including 1,343 boys, 1,281 girls, 1,147 adult males, 1,110 adults female, 55 elders males, and 64 elders females are identified as the most vulnerable to flooding risks through the construction of a stormwater system by establishing a 1-kilometer-long carrier line as the main line with all stormwater inlets & manholes in Aslan neighborhood/ Abu Obaida school area to the Aslan basin.
The collected rainwater collected through the system will be dumped in an existing Aslan infiltration basin.
The project is supported by the mainstreaming of an Integrated Protection Approach (IPA)with a specific focus on the most vulnerable groups, including women, girls, boys, the disabled, and the elderly. Gender principles are mainstreamed throughout the entire action and CBPCs are trained on referral mechanisms for protection cases such as GBV with a particular focus on women and children. Finally, Accountability to Affected Populations (AAP) is core to the proposed Action.

1 - Needs

Needs risk of flooding is one of the four main key issues generating people’s need for WASH assistance as it is found according to WASH vulnerability Mapping 2021 and updated recently in August 2022 that almost 94,000 Palestinians reside in low-lying areas at risk of flooding events during winter due to heavy rains and over ow of stormwater facilities and sewage pumping stations. The report stated that recently and due to climate change, flooding became more frequent and has caused significant losses of assets, displacement, disrupted access to livelihoods and services, and exacerbated the risk of waterborne diseases. According to Gaza Flooding Hot Spots 2022; the Aslan community located in Beit Lahia has been identified as a red zone/high-risk zone in urgent flooding, the target community consists of a population exceeding 5,000 citizens at risk of flooding, and assets loss is considered. To ensure community engagement during the preliminary design phase of the intervention, on December 2021, the Municipality of Beit Lahia conducted a meeting with representatives of al Aslan (Abu Obaida school area & Al Nabi Yousef), discussed the current situations, solutions, and the proposed interventions and positive feedbacks and also appreciations were found. This will ensure that the proposed the interventions are meeting the needs of the targeted beneficiaries and that they will support the success of the project. BLDA at the early beginnings of the project will consult and communicate with varied groups of people in terms of Age, Gender and Diversity (AGD), to obtain greater and deep understanding on their needs, how the outlined issues affect them and their life, and the different ways they would like to be supported to prevent and mitigate those risks

2 - Activities

Through this intervention, the following activities will be implemented: 1) Constructing storm water system through establishing: (1.1) 1 kilometer stormwater carrier line as the main line with all fittings including manholes in Aslan neighborhood. which will deliver collected water to an existing Aslan in filtration basin. (1.2) A storm water collection network including all fittings (e.g., manholes, gullies, …etc.). Rain water collected through the system will be dumped in the existing Aslan in filtration basin The existing in filtration basin. Preliminary designs for the proposed interventions are available and they will be reviewed by the Beit Lahia Development Association technical staff and tender documents as well. Ensure well coordination with the local authorities including PWA, CMWU and Beit Lahia Municipality developmental plans for the targeted area. BLDA will consult and communicate with varied groups of people in terms of Age, Gender and Diversity (AGD), to obtain greater and deep understanding on how the outlined issues affect them and their life, and the different ways they would like to be supported to prevent and mitigate those risks. As such, planned activities will be tailored, to the extent that it is feasible to their points of view and suggestions. Ensuring this level of diverse community engagement is maintained, BLDA will focus on ensuring adequate protection, age, gender, diversity and PSEA mainstreaming by mapping risks and opportunities for different groups of people, mitigating and/or strengthening them appropriately. To ensure community engagement during implementation phase of the proposed intervention, meetings are planned to take place with representatives of Aslan area in the presence of the Municipality of Beit Lahia and all related authorities (i.e., PWA and CMWU) in order to keep beneficiaries’ updated and to gather their feedbacks and recommendations that will be used for enhancing the intervention and thus its impacts. It is worth mentioning that the municipality of Beit Lahia is ready to start pavement works at the targeted area pending completion of infrastructure works, which will be concluded once the storm system has been established. This ensures that the intervention will contribute to the development of the targeted area.

The intervention proposed here will in fact help minimize therisk of floods, thus reducing the risk of transmitting diseases and in turn contributingto lower harm of the population.
