Project Summary

Sindh province has historically suffered from different natural disasters. The high level of risk is mainly from floods, heavy rains, cyclones in coastal area, sea intrusion, droughts, earthquakes, epidemics etc. In such a situation the most vulnerable population i.e., children (girls and boys), women, persons with disabilities and elderly people mainly suffer due to their vulnerabilities. The proposed project aims to safeguard the protection rights of children (girls and boys), adolescent girls & boys from violence, abuse, exploitation and neglect due to the humanitarian situation. The project will provide psychosocial support through recreational activities, a safe and protective environment and referral to basic services (SWA) for children (girls and boys) in the targeted flood affected areas.The proposed project aims to safeguard the protection rights of children (girls and boys), adolescent girls & boys from violence, abuse, exploitation and neglect due to the humanitarian situation. The project will provide psychosocial support through recreational activities, a safe and protective environment and referral to basic services (SWA) for children (girls and boys) in the targeted flood affected areas.
PLaCES (Protective Learning and Community Emergency Services) will be established in 10 locations with outreach teams to reach the most vulnerable and hard to reach populations. The risk of separation of children from families especially in displaced population will be monitored and awareness on prevention will be included in DRR messages. The children will be reunified with the families with support form the Social Welfare Department. Access to non-discriminatory mainstream services will be provided with the support of other service providers, by establishing an effective referral system at the local level with the Government (Social Welfare Department). The community mechanisms will be strengthened to monitor and report cases of abuse of girls and boys through child protection committees and by strengthening the capacity of parents, teachers and community influencers to identify and report violation of protection rights of children from abuse, exploitation and neglect.Throughout the emergency response Shifa Foundation will ensure
