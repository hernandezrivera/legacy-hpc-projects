Project Summary

In Central Mali, intercommunal conflicts and tensions continue to lead to population displacements. According to DCA’s Rapid Response Mechanism, 5445 IDPs have been registered in April 2018 in Koro Circle/District of Mopti region, due to intercommunal clashes between the Dogons (farmers) and Fulani (pastoralists) groups. In the same month, in Djenne Circle/District of Mopti, 3700 IDPs have been registered. There are predispositions to confrontations related to the economy, history and behavior of the army. The quasi-disappearance of State control over the area is being maximally exploited by violent extremist groups to fuel these intercommunal conflicts. Therefore, social cohesion is of a great need for these populations.
In addition to the above, the food security situation has continuously deteriorated since 2016. The frequency of shocks and aggravating factors (climatic, environmental, economic, security) accentuates the deterioration of livelihoods for poor and very poor households and increases significantly their level of vulnerability, while greatly reducing their resilience. There are few favorable signals to hope that this trend will reverse in 2019. Poor and very poor households need assistance to face the lean season and in long term perspective to improve their resilience to ever recurring shocks.
Finally, the operational capacity of local CSOs for delivering actions to reinforce social cohesion remains a great challenge as many are characterized by institutional and organizational weaknesses.
Targets groups of the projects are underserved and insecurity affected communities, IDPs and returnees, Women headed of households, very poor farmers and pastoralists, CSOs.

Needs

The three targetted circles (Douentza, Djenne and Bankass) are affected by conflicts. In Djenne 8 communes/12 are affected. In Douentza, 13 communes/15 are affected. In Bankass, 7 communes/12 are affected. Chronic and structural factors of needs are the inadequacy of community mechanisms for conflict prevention and management, lack of peace education, weak capacity of local systems in education for peace and protection, dysfunction of social services basic.The intervention area is also affected by drough and climate change consequences. The food security situation has continuously deteriorated since 2016. Beyond drought, insecurity situation has a negative impact on agriculture, fishing and livestock. This leads to problems of protection of livelihoods, people displacement, exodus of young people and food insecurity during the lean season for very poor households.Gender analysis shows that men are actors and targets of conflict. Due to community clashes, women are head of households, are affected by diverse forms of violence, displacement, trauma and prostitution as negativ coping strategy. Boys and girls are facing all forms of violence, child labor and exploitation, begging, rural exodus, separation of from their parents and exposure to IED/ERW consequences. Young children are tempted by the enlistment in armed groups. Children are facing school drop-out and school closure.

Activities

- Organize food assistance for very poor households at risk of food insecurity during the lean season (mobile cash). This activity will target specially women headed of households. - Support agro-pastoral income generating including market gardening and small livestock (seed, small tools, equipment). These livelihood micro projects will be done with men and women associations taking into account their needs during community planning. - Support to livestock for agro-pastoral households vulnerable people who have lost their productive assets through pastoral amenities (watering points, grazing rehabilitation, etc.). It is also about providing support to the prevention & management of conflicts related to natural resources and land. Community safety planning will be organized in communities involving women and men, older adults, middle-aged adults and young adults. - Integration of accountability mechanism and gender analysis in interventions. Complaint handling committees (30% women) and feedback boxes will be available to ensure people can raise a complaint or provide feedback in an anonymous manner. - Strengthen social cohesion between communities and sensitize and / or train humanitarian actors in prevention. Conflict mitigation/safety micro-projects (pastoral tracks, realization of small dams and embankments, etc...) will be realized with men and women associations to reduce specific conflicts. - organize risk education on mines, explosive remnants of war (ERW), improvised explosive devices (IEDs), small arms and light weapons (SALW). Small arms and light weapons education will target mainly men as main holders of arms. Mines and REG risk education will target mainly women and children are most affected people. Messages will be also broadcasted in local languages for all community members.
