Project Summary

The proposed intervention aims at reducing and mitigating the Protection and WaSH risks faced by the most vulnerable persons from refugee and host communities, in Bekaa (Rachaiyya, West Bekaa and Zahle districts) and Southern Lebanon (Nabatieh, Hasbaya, Bintjbeil and Marjayoun districts), by enhancing response and prevention mechanisms.

1. - Brief description of the specific project activities under each sector objective

INTERSOS will implement WaSH Community Support Projects (CSPs) which will improve WaSH conditions of targeted municipalities while enhancing social cohesion. CSPs will support areas hosting large numbers of refugees in maintaining the operation of their systems, ensuring access to water for HHs, factories, agriculture, etc. This will be combined with the distribution of general and menstrual hygiene management kits, coupled with hygiene promotion. For the CSPs, INTERSOS, with the participation of municipalities and communities, will identify key WaSH projects to be implemented based on: 1. total number of affected people who will benefit from the intervention; 2. cost of single project; 3. community’s feedback on the CSP proposed.

2. - What is your protection risk mitigation plan?

INTERSOS will ensure safety for the staff and management of business continuity through its standard operational tool tailored to each area of implementation: the Comprehensive Planning Process (CPP). This dynamic analytical, contingency and risk mitigation tool is complemented in Lebanon by a set of tailored procedures for all the main risks identified (SOPs for cash safety management, movements, Covid-19, centers safety, demonstrations, etc.), while also relying on set-ups that are built on past years lessons learnt (e.g., energy, exchange rate and fuel crisis management) to ensure business continuity through an agile and flexible adaptation to the context.

3. - How will the project ensure accountability for affected populations (AAP)?

This intervention upholds all four Sphere Protection Principles and the 9 Core Humanitarian Standards. INTERSOS strictly adheres to the do no harm imperative and strengthens people’s safety and dignity by adopting a beneficiary-centred approach. AGD Mainstreaming approach will be ensured, taking into consideration communities’ specific needs and risks. Accountability to Affected Populations is ensured in all the project implementation phases, and INTERSOS is committed to the beneficiaries' participation at all stages. To ensure accountability to PoC, complaints and feedback mechanisms will be available through different modalities to receive and respond to beneficiaries’ needs and suggestions. MEAL team will conduct regular monitoring, including field inspections and follow up with the teams, to ensure the activities are conducted properly. Anti-fraud measures and policies in place, as well as CP Safeguarding Policy and PSEA Policy that are signed by all staff, partners and volunteers.
